# Jungle — Biome Asset Package

## Lore Text

Nothing here is still. The canopy seethes — a living ceiling of dark leaves and tangled vine, so thick the sky is a rumor. Below, in the hot wet gloom, everything grows over everything else in a slow war of root and tendril. You can feel the Life threads thrumming through the soil beneath your feet, a vast green pulse connecting every trunk, every fern, every bright-mouthed orchid in a single breathing network. The jungle does not welcome visitors. It simply absorbs them.

## Game Logic

### Sphere Affinities
| Sphere | Affinity | Role |
|--------|----------|------|
| **Life** | 0.95 | Primary — strongest Life affinity of any biome. Growth, healing, transformation through living systems |

### Reach Domain Modifiers
| Reach | Modifier | Rationale |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Flesh | +25% | Abundant biological resources, healing herbs, poisons |
| Shadow | +15% | Dense cover enables concealment and ambush |
| Veil | +10% | Life-magic saturation amplifies ritual power |
| Eye | -10% | Visibility severely limited by canopy and undergrowth |
| Stone | -20% | Terrain resists construction, structures reclaimed by growth |
| Star | -15% | Cannot see the sky for navigation or fate-reading |

### Resource Tendencies
- **High:** Medicinal plants, poisons, exotic materials, animal products, lumber
- **Medium:** Fresh water (rivers/rainfall), food sources (fruit, game)
- **Low:** Metals, stone, open land

### Agent Behavior Effects
- Agents with high **Life** or **Flesh** domain capability thrive here
- Settlements trend toward **temple** and **grove** sub-locations
- Axiological **tradition_innovation** biased toward tradition — old ways work in old forests
- Increased **condition trait** acquisition rate (+30%) from disease, parasites, venomous creatures
- Life magic workings have +20% effectiveness; Entropy magic has -15% effectiveness
- Faction control is difficult — the jungle erodes political boundaries
