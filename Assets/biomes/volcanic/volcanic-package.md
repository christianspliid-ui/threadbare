# Volcanic — Biome Asset Package

## Lore Text

The earth remembers fire. Beneath the blackened crust, rivers of molten fury still pulse — ancient veins of the world's first anger, never cooled, never forgiven. Ash drifts like grey snow across fields of obsidian glass, and the air tastes of sulfur and endings. Those who dwell near the cauldrons speak of the ground's voice: a low, ceaseless murmur that rises to a scream when the threads of Energy burst through the cracks, golden and searing, as if the world itself were trying to split its skin and be reborn.

## Game Logic

### Sphere Affinities
| Sphere | Affinity | Role |
|--------|----------|------|
| **Energy** | 0.80 | Primary — volcanic heat, eruption, transformation through fire |
| **Matter** | 0.75 | Secondary — crystalline formations, mineral wealth, geological structure |
| **Entropy** | 0.50 | Tertiary — destruction, erosion, breakdown of existing forms |

### Reach Domain Modifiers
| Reach | Modifier | Rationale |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Iron | +15% | Forge-ready terrain, weaponsmithing advantage |
| Stone | +20% | Abundant building materials, obsidian and basite |
| Veil | +10% | Volcanic energy amplifies magical workings |
| Gold | -10% | Difficult trade routes, inhospitable to commerce |
| Heart | -15% | Isolation and danger suppress social bonds |

### Resource Tendencies
- **High:** Obsidian, rare metals, gemstones, volcanic glass, sulfur
- **Medium:** Geothermal energy, fertile ash-soil (after eruptions)
- **Low:** Timber, fresh water, arable land

### Agent Behavior Effects
- Agents with high **Force** or **Energy** domain capability are drawn to volcanic hexes
- Settlements here trend toward **fortress** and **forge** sub-locations
- Axiological **courage_prudence** biased toward courage — living here requires it
- Increased **scar trait** acquisition rate (+25%) from environmental hazards
- Agents develop **condition traits** related to heat exposure, ash-lung, tremor-sense
