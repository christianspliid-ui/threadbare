# Glacier — Biome Asset Package

## Lore Text

Time moves differently here. The ice is old — older than memory, older than the names of gods. It creaks and groans in the dark like a sleeping thing, and deep within its blue-black depths you can see shapes: frozen rivers, ancient forests, the bones of creatures that walked the world before the first Unmaking. The Time threads pulse through the glacier in slow concentric rings, and near the brightest nodes the air ripples with afterimages — echoes of moments trapped in the ice, replaying endlessly, whispering of a world that was and may yet be again.

## Game Logic

### Sphere Affinities
| Sphere | Affinity | Role |
|--------|----------|------|
| **Time** | 0.40 | Primary — temporal preservation, frozen moments, chronomantic resonance |

*Note: Glacier has a relatively low affinity ceiling compared to Volcanic (0.80 Energy) or Jungle (0.95 Life). This reflects ice as a preserver rather than a generator — Time magic works here but the glacier doesn't actively produce it. The Obsidian note shows only Time with 0.4, making glaciers subtle rather than magically intense.*

### Reach Domain Modifiers
| Reach | Modifier | Rationale |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| Eye | +15% | Vast sightlines, nothing hidden on open ice, chronomantic visions |
| Star | +10% | Clear skies above glaciers, celestial navigation unobstructed |
| Iron | -10% | Treacherous footing, difficult terrain for armies |
| Gold | -25% | No trade routes, no commerce, no market |
| Stone | -20% | Cannot build on shifting ice — structures calve and collapse |
| Heart | -15% | Extreme isolation suppresses social bonds |
| Flesh | -20% | Hostile to biological processes, disease rare but healing slow |

### Resource Tendencies
- **High:** Ancient preserves (frozen artifacts, extinct specimens, echo-fragments), pure ice-water
- **Medium:** Wind energy, rare crystals in pressure formations
- **Low:** Everything else — food, timber, metals, arable land

### Agent Behavior Effects
- Agents with high **Time** or **Eye** domain capability drawn to glaciers
- Settlements extremely rare — **hermitage** or **observatory** sub-locations only
- Axiological **courage_prudence** biased toward prudence — recklessness kills here
- Condition trait acquisition: frostbite, snow-blindness, temporal displacement (+10% chance of chronomantic visions)
- Time magic workings have +15% effectiveness; Life magic has -25% effectiveness
- Agents linger longer than intended — the temporal distortion subtly slows departure
- Very high scar trait value — surviving a glacier stay is a mark of distinction
