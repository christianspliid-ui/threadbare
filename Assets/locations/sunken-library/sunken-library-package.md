# The Sunken Library — Location Asset Package

## Lore Text

No one remembers who built it, or when it sank. The entrance is a crack in a hillside that opens into flooded halls stretching down and down into the dark. The water is black and still, and beneath its surface, shelves of stone tablets and crystallized scrolls line corridors that have not breathed air in centuries. Mind threads pulse through the murk in electric blue dendrite networks — branching, connecting, flickering with the stored thoughts of a dead civilization. Scholars who dive too deep say they hear voices. That the library *reads them back*.

## Game Logic

### Sphere Biases
| Sphere | Bias | Role |
|--------|------|------|
| **Mind** | 0.80 | Primary — stored knowledge, memory, cognitive resonance |
| **Spirit** | 0.40 | Secondary — the library's own quasi-sentience, lingering presence of dead scholars |
| **Time** | 0.30 | Tertiary — preservation of ancient knowledge, temporal stasis of submerged texts |

### Available Actions
| Action | Reach | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **Research** | Eye | Study tablets/scrolls; gain knowledge traits and lore fragments |
| **Commune** | Veil | Attempt contact with the library's stored intelligences |
| **Dive** | Flesh | Physical descent into deeper flooded levels; risk + reward |
| **Catalogue** | Eye | Map and organize accessible sections; unlocks deeper research |
| **Ward** | Veil | Protect sections from degradation; preservation magic |

### Agent Attraction Rules
- Agents with high **Eye** or **Mind** domain capability are drawn here
- Scholar and mystic archetypes strongly prefer this location
- Agents with low **Flesh** capability avoid — the diving is dangerous
- Factions with knowledge-oriented goals will contest access

### Resource Generation
- **High:** Knowledge fragments, lore, magical formulae, historical records
- **Medium:** Rare magical components (crystallized thought-matter), Mind-sphere catalysts
- **Low:** Physical resources, food, trade goods, military value

### Sub-Location Slots
| Sub-Location | Type | Description |
|-------------|------|-------------|
| **The Upper Stacks** | Research | Accessible dry sections; basic research |
| **The Flooded Halls** | Exploration | Submerged corridors; requires diving; richer finds |
| **The Cognition Chamber** | Ritual | Central node where Mind threads converge; commune actions |
| **The Deepest Vault** | Sealed | Unknown contents; requires significant effort to reach |

### Narrative Hooks
- The library reads visitors back — Mind threads scan anyone who enters (mechanical: agents gain the "Known" condition trait after 3+ visits, making them partially transparent to the library's intelligence)
- Deepest Vault is sealed — breaking the seal may release stored knowledge *or* a cognitive hazard (doom escalation risk)
- Crystallized scrolls can be "thawed" with Mind+Time magic — but the contents may be propaganda, truth, or the desperate final thoughts of drowning scholars
- Rival factions racing to catalogue the library creates a knowledge-war subplot
