# Wraithwood — Location Asset Package

## Lore Text

The trees died long ago but they will not fall. They stand pale and petrified in their thousands, bark stripped to bone-white wood, branches reaching like skeletal fingers into a sky that never fully brightens. Between them, Spirit threads drift upward in violet ribbons — the half-dissolved remnants of every soul that ever died here, unable to move on, unable to fully form. Wraithwood is not haunted in the way of ghost stories. It is a place where the boundary between living and dead has worn thin as old cloth, and things slip through from both sides.

## Game Logic

### Sphere Biases
| Sphere | Bias | Role |
|--------|------|------|
| **Spirit** | 0.75 | Primary — thinned veil between living and dead, persistent soul-fragments |
| **Entropy** | 0.50 | Secondary — decay, dissolution, the slow unraveling of physical matter |
| **Darkness** | 0.30 | Tertiary — absorption, concealment, the void between states of being |

### Available Actions
| Action | Reach | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **Commune with Dead** | Veil | Contact soul-fragments; gain prophetic hints or historical knowledge |
| **Harvest Spirit Essence** | Veil | Collect concentrated Spirit-sphere material; alchemical/magical use |
| **Navigate** | Shadow | Move through Wraithwood unseen; the mist provides concealment |
| **Bind** | Veil | Anchor a soul-fragment to a purpose; risky, ethically fraught |
| **Lay to Rest** | Heart | Help trapped spirits move on; reduces Spirit density, gains moral standing |

### Agent Attraction Rules
- Agents with high **Veil** or **Spirit** domain capability are drawn here
- Hedge-Priest and mystic archetypes strongly prefer this location
- Agents with strong **Heart** domain may come to help the dead
- Agents with high **Iron** or **Gold** domain avoid — nothing here to fight or trade
- Entropy-aligned factions may claim it as sacred ground

### Resource Generation
- **High:** Spirit essence, prophetic visions, soul-fragment lore, Veil-sphere catalysts
- **Medium:** Rare petrified wood (alchemical component), concealment advantages
- **Low:** Food, water, building materials, anything of physical value

### Sub-Location Slots
| Sub-Location | Type | Description |
|-------------|------|-------------|
| **The Pale Circle** | Ritual | Central clearing where Spirit threads are densest; communion site |
| **The Bonewood Path** | Transit | Narrow trail through the densest section; haunted, dangerous |
| **The Roothollow** | Shelter | Beneath a fallen petrified trunk; only safe resting place |
| **The Thinning** | Anomaly | A spot where the veil is nearly gone; things pass through |

### Narrative Hooks
- The Thinning is getting wider — each cycle, more of the boundary dissolves (doom escalation indicator for Spirit-related dooms)
- Soul-fragments have memories of past cycles — they can reveal echo-information but may lie or be confused
- An ancient ritual site (The Pale Circle) can be used to permanently seal OR permanently open the Thinning — faction-defining choice
- Agents who spend too long in Wraithwood acquire the "Ghost-Touched" condition trait — they see things others don't, but struggle to connect with the living (-5% Heart, +10% Veil)
