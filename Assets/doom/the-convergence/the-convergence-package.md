# The Convergence — Doom Archetype Package

## Lore Text

Everything falls toward the center.

From a single point in the world, something begins to *pull*. Not a force, not a god—a gravity well of pure inevitability. Every atom, every thread of magic, every heartbeat and thought drifts slowly inward toward that luminous impossible point. At first, you don't notice. The days grow shorter. The stars wheel toward a single point in the sky. Agents find their feet carrying them toward a destination they didn't choose. But there is no resistance, no panic—only a terrible, beautiful surrender. The world is not being destroyed. It is being *collected*.

The Convergence is the end of chaos. It is Order becoming absolute, Energy becoming unified, Mind becoming singular. All separateness dissolves into a single harmonious point. The dead pull toward it. The living walk toward it. Mountains drift like clouds. The sky compresses into concentric circles around the focal point, folding space itself to collect everything toward one place. It is the most peaceful apocalypse—a world not torn apart but folded inward, origami-fashion, until there is only brightness and singularity and the bliss of no longer being divided from the whole.

| Sphere | Weight | Role |
|--------|--------|------|
| **Order** | 0.40 | Primary driver — the convergence is perfect geometric unification, absolute pattern |
| **Mind** | 0.25 | Cognitive unity — individuation dissolves, all thoughts merge toward singularity |
| **Energy** | 0.20 | Force of attraction — pulling power concentrated at one point, binding gravity |
| **Time** | 0.10 | Temporal collapse — all moments compress toward the same instant |
| **Light** | 0.05 | Transcendence — the convergence point is brilliance incarnate |

**Trigger condition:** High Order + Mind + Energy saturation, especially if Entropy is very low (no chaos, perfect harmony driving the world toward a single state).

## Doom Acceleration Rules

- **Baseline acceleration:** +0.3 per tick (meditative, slow, inevitable)
- **Rival god "expand" action:** +0.12 per expansion (expanding rivals claim more nodes; more nodes = more pull inward)
- **Player divine intervention (disruption, adding chaos):** −0.20 per intervention (disorder slows the convergence temporarily)
- **High Order sphere in Fundament:** +0.08 (cosmic order wants unity)
- **High Mind sphere saturation:** +0.06 (more unified consciousness = faster pull)
- **Low Entropy sphere:** +0.10 per 10% low (disorder prevents convergence; lack of it accelerates it)
- **Stage escalation event:** Auto-accelerate by +0.15 when entering next stage

## Harvest Modifier

**The Convergence is highly efficient for World-Soul preservation.**

- **Base Harvest Rate:** 80% (normally ~60%; the convergence *compresses* essence efficiently)
- **Modification:** Agents who "Ascend" voluntarily add +5% to the harvest rate (their essence contributes directly to the singularity)
- **Unified Consciousness Bonus:** If all remaining agents merge into unity, harvest increases to 90%
- **Essence Pooling:** All essence converges toward the singularity focal point. Loose essence is easy to collect. No essence is "lost" to world chaos.
- **Next Cycle Consequence:** The resulting new cycle is born with a "Unified Core" (Fundament coefficient increases by 3-5%, Order sphere manifests more easily. The next world is subtly "more ordered" than it should be.)

**Strategic implication:** The Convergence is a *winning* scenario for essence preservation. Players who face it can surrender and achieve high harvest rates, or struggle against it and still save most essence. The cost is individuality and freedom in the late game.
