# The Lantern of Stars — Artifact Asset Package

## Lore Text

It should not exist. The Lantern is a cage of dark iron containing a fragment of something that was never meant to be contained — a sliver of the World-Soul's light, captured during a previous Unmaking by hands unknown. It burns with the gold of Energy and the warm radiance of Light, and where its beam falls, hidden things become visible: buried treasures, concealed enemies, the invisible threads of fate connecting every living soul to every other. But the fragment *wants to return*. The Lantern pulls toward the sky, toward the World-Soul, and its bearer must hold it down with will alone.

|-------|-------------|-----------|

### Rarity
**Legendary** — unique, contains a literal piece of the World-Soul

### Echo Potential
**Extreme** — the fragment *is* an echo; guaranteed to persist, may trigger special echo events

### Bonding Requirements
- Bearer must have **Eye** or **Star** domain capability ≥ 0.30 (must be able to perceive what the lantern reveals)
- Bearer must have **Force** or **Mind** capability ≥ 0.35 (willpower to hold the lantern down)
- Bonding requires a night of sky-gazing with the lantern — takes 1 tick
- If bearer's Force+Mind combined < 0.50, the lantern escapes (flies toward sky, lost for 5-10 ticks until it falls somewhere else)
- Rival gods are *intensely* attracted to the Lantern — holding it paints a target
