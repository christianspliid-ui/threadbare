# Griefender — Artifact Asset Package

## Lore Text

The blade was quenched in the tears of a god. Or so the story goes — what is certain is that Griefender feeds on sorrow. It grows heavier in the hands of the content, lighter for the grieving, and in battle it strikes with the accumulated weight of every loss its bearer has ever suffered. The crimson Force threads that vein the dark steel pulse faster when blood is drawn, and the amber-bronze Matter lattice along the spine grows new crystalline facets after each kill, as though the sword is *building itself* from violence. Those who carry it long enough say it comforts them. That is when they should put it down.

## Game Logic

### Sphere Affinity
| Sphere | Affinity | Role |
|--------|----------|------|
| **Force** | 0.75 | Primary — directed violence, impact, martial power |
| **Matter** | 0.50 | Secondary — self-constructing crystalline growth, physical durability |
| **Entropy** | 0.25 | Tertiary — feeds on loss and grief; destructive emotional resonance |

### Trait Contributions (Sigmoid Pool)
| Trait | Contribution | Mechanism |
|-------|-------------|-----------|
| **Grief-Weight** | +0.40 Iron | Striking power scales with bearer's accumulated losses |
| **Sorrow Bond** | -0.20 Heart | Emotional isolation; the sword becomes the bearer's closest companion |
| **Crystal Growth** | +0.15 Stone | Blade self-repairs and strengthens through combat |
| **Violent Comfort** | -0.10 Flesh | Bearer becomes physically dependent on combat for emotional regulation |

### Rarity
**Legendary** — unique weapon, highly contested, attracts violent agents

### Echo Potential
**High** — strong narrative significance; likely to persist as a Relic Echo with "bloodthirst" behavioral modifier

### Bonding Requirements
- Bearer must have **Iron** domain capability ≥ 0.35 (must be able to wield it)
- Bearer must have at least one "loss" event in their history (death of ally, defeat, exile)
- Bonding is instant for the grieving — the sword *wants* them
- Content or joyful agents cannot bond; the sword feels dead in their hands
- After 10+ ticks bonded, bearer acquires "Grief-Dependent" condition trait (withdrawal symptoms if separated)
