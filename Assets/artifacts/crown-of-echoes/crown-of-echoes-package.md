# The Crown of Echoes — Artifact Asset Package

## Lore Text

It was not forged — it *remembered itself into being*. The Crown of Echoes is a circlet of dark metal wound with orange Time threads that pulse in slow concentric waves, and those who wear it hear the voices of every previous bearer, stretching back through cycles uncounted. Some whisper strategy. Some scream warnings. Some simply weep. The crown does not grant power so much as *perspective* — a terrible, layered awareness of how this has all happened before, and what it cost.

## Game Logic

### Sphere Affinity
| Sphere | Affinity | Role |
|--------|----------|------|
| **Time** | 0.85 | Primary — temporal awareness, echo-memory, cycle knowledge |
| **Mind** | 0.40 | Secondary — cognitive enhancement from accumulated wisdom of past bearers |

### Trait Contributions (Sigmoid Pool)
| Trait | Contribution | Mechanism |
|-------|-------------|-----------|
| **Temporal Awareness** | +0.35 Eye | Bearer perceives time-echoes; enhanced foresight |
| **Echo Burden** | -0.15 Heart | Voices of past bearers create social isolation |
| **Cycle Memory** | +0.25 Veil | Access to knowledge from previous world-cycles |
| **Fractured Present** | -0.10 Iron | Difficulty focusing on immediate combat; too many timelines |

### Rarity
**Legendary** — only one exists per cycle, may persist through Unmakings via echo system

### Echo Potential
**Very High** — the crown *is* echoes; near-certain to persist as a Relic Echo if active during Unmaking

### Bonding Requirements
- Bearer must have **Mind** domain capability ≥ 0.40 (need cognitive resilience to handle the voices)
- Bearer must have **Time** affinity or be in a location with Time sphere bias ≥ 0.20
- Bonding takes 3 ticks — during bonding, bearer experiences disorientation (all domain capabilities at -20%)
- If bearer's Mind capability < 0.30, bonding inflicts the "Shattered" condition trait (permanent -0.25 Mind)
