# The Hedge-Priest — Actor Archetype Package

## Lore Text

The Hedge-Priest stands between worlds—not in a grand temple but at the edge of fields, in a cottage heavy with smoke and hung with charms. They are healer and counselor, listener to the dying and namer of the newborn. Their threads sing with the ascending wisps of Spirit and the organic branching of Life—magic that comes not from formal study but from communion with forces older than stone. They know the language of herbs, the weight of grief, the structure of a community's sorrow. Where the Warlord commands and the Scholar explains, the Hedge-Priest *understands*. They mend torn families, guide lost souls, bridge the veil between living and dead. They are beloved by those they touch, feared by those they don't. Yet this gift is also a cage. They lack the power of institutions and formal authority; their magic is personal, not scalable. They cannot command armies or unlock ancient vaults. They watch kingdoms crumble while they tend to one sick child, acutely aware of the tiny sphere of influence their gifts permit. In a world of grand forces, the Hedge-Priest's power is achingly small.
