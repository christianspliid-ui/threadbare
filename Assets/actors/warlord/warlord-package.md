# The Warlord — Actor Archetype Package

## Lore Text

The Warlord is born where iron meets necessity. They rise through battlefields and hardship, commanding not through grace or honeyed words, but through the irrefutable logic of strength. Their threads burn with the red fury of Force—sharp directional streaks that cut through chaos and impose order by sheer will. They are architects of victory, builders of empires carved from bone and blood. Their soldiers follow not out of love but out of respect for the iron hand that turns impossible odds into triumph. Yet this same singular focus blinds them to subtlety; they see every problem as a wall to be breached, every negotiation as a skirmish to be won by force of personality. In the quiet chambers of diplomacy, the Warlord is often lost, treating whispered counsel with the same blunt certainty they use to shatter shields.
