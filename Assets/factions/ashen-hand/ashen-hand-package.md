# The Ashen Hand — Faction Asset Package

## Lore Text

All things burn. The Ashen Hand merely accelerates the inevitable. They are not cruel — cruelty requires anger, and the Ashen Hand has transcended anger into cold philosophical certainty. They are spies, assassins, saboteurs, and scholars of decay. They move through shadows not from shame but from pragmatism; their targets do not see the knife until the end is already written. Their territories are the Blightweald and the Dryrot, places where the world is already breaking, where fungi bloom on corpses and nothing green survives. They see order as delusion and life as a temporary eddy in entropy's current. When the world grows sick, the Ashen Hand helps it die cleanly.

### Recruitment Bias
Favors agents with:
- High **Entropy** or **Mind** domain capability (0.50+)
- Assassin, Spy, Sage, Corruptor archetypes
- Traits: "Cunning," "Patient," "Detached," "Analytical," "Accepting"

Actively rejects:
- Life or Order affinities above 0.65 (will not recruit)
- Paladin, Crusader, Healer, Builder archetypes
- Traits: "Righteous," "Protective," "Creative," "Hopeful," "Idealistic"
