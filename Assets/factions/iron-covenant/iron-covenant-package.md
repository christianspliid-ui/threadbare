# The Iron Covenant — Faction Asset Package

## Lore Text

They came first to the broken places — shattered temples, war-torn steppes, cities drowning in Chaos. The Iron Covenant believes salvation lives in discipline: perfect geometry, crystalline hierarchy, the hammer-stroke of coordinated will. Their strongholds are wonders of Euclidean precision, basalt walls fused with golden Order threads that sing when enemies approach. They despise the Entropy cults and the wild green things that grow without permission. To them, a world not bound by law is a world in freefall. Some call them tyrants. They call themselves the only ones holding back the dark.

### Recruitment Bias
Favors agents with:
- High **Iron** or **Matter** domain capability (0.50+)
- Soldier, Warlord, Strategist, Engineer archetypes
- Traits: "Disciplined," "Loyal," "Duty-Bound," "Practical"

Actively rejects:
- Chaos or Entropy affinities (will not recruit; will actively oppose)
- Trickster, Wanderer, Mystic archetypes
- Traits: "Unpredictable," "Wild," "Chaotic," "Spiritual"
