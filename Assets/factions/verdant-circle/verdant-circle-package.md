# The Verdant Circle — Faction Asset Package

## Lore Text

They do not build; they tend. The Verdant Circle moves through the world as gardeners move through a neglected plot — patient, necessary, inevitable. Where Matter is broken, they restore. Where Life has been strangled, they breathe it back. Their druids speak to the growing things; their shamans walk the boundary between flesh and spirit, between the living and the honored dead. They have no strongholds, only Deepthickets where the old-growth is so thick that sunlight never touches ground, where the border between life and death blurs into something sacred. They oppose the hammer and the furnace alike. To them, violence and industry are two faces of the same crime.

### Faction Traits
| Trait | Domain Impact | Benefit | Drawback |
|-------|---------------|---------|----------|
| **Living Bond** | Life +0.25, Matter -0.20 | All agents gain +0.25 Life domain; can commune with natural entities | Matter domain capped at 0.35 max; agents struggle with constructs, artifice, and industrial reasoning |
| **Spiritual Communion** | Spirit +0.20, Order -0.25 | Agents can commune with ancestral spirits; +0.20 Star domain | Order domain reduced by 0.25; agents struggle with hierarchy and rule-bound structures |

| **Circle Symbiosis** | Life/Spirit aura +0.25 (passive) | Nearby allied agents gain +0.15 Life and Spirit affinity in natural terrain | In built-up/industrial areas, agents suffer -0.20 domain penalties; faction is weakest in cities and fortresses |

### Recruitment Bias
Favors agents with:
- High **Life** or **Spirit** domain capability (0.50+)
- Druid, Shaman, Healer, Naturalist archetypes
- Traits: "Nurturing," "Spiritual," "Rooted," "Communal," "Patient"

Actively rejects:
- Force or Matter affinities above 0.60 (will not recruit)
- Warlord, Engineer, Industrialist archetypes
- Traits: "Violent," "Domineering," "Extractive," "Rigid," "Expedient"

### Rival Factions
