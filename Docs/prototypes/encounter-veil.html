The torchlight gutters as another caravan approaches the checkpoint. Vasara's hand rests on the seal of office, the weight of it familiar now after three months at this post. The merchant at the head of the line carries papers from House Venn — the faction that controls the river crossing and, by extension, most of the grain that feeds the western settlements.

But the papers are wrong. Not forged — worse than forged. They're genuine documents issued for a different shipment, reused with a clumsy alteration to the date. Someone in House Venn's bureaucracy either made an honest mistake or is running a quiet side operation. Either way, the caravan cannot pass legally.

The merchant's eyes are steady. They know something is wrong.
