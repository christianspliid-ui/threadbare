# 2026-09-08 — THR-1442: the grid's `control:claim` producer, measured rather than re-gated

**Verdict: no gate lands.** Both halves of THR-1286's deleted `evaluateControlClaimGate`
are already covered — one by construction, one by a guard that was never recognised as
load-bearing. What shipped is the measurement, the name, and two falsifiable assertions.

## What was asked

THR-1303 deleted `evaluateControlClaimGate` correctly: its first line was
`template.verb !== 'control'`, and no template has carried that verb since the six
ambition-driven control templates were retired. But the *live* producer of control
stances is the undertaking grid's `cell.control_claim.location`, which never carried
that verb either — so it was never covered by the gate, and the deletion made a
pre-existing gap visible rather than creating one. The ticket asked which of the two
guarded failure modes the grid's claim path can actually reach, measured on a real run,
before writing any gate.

## Measured (medium map, 300 ticks, **one seed per process**)

| seed | stances | collapses | re-claims | min gap | `< 24` | `[24, 30)` |
|------|--------:|----------:|----------:|--------:|-------:|-----------:|
| 42   | 52      | 40        | 12        | 27      | 0      | 3          |
| 99   | 8       | 8         | 0         | —       | 0      | 0          |
| 7    | 76      | 54        | 8         | 24      | 0      | 1          |

Every stance in all three worlds comes from one cell: `cell.control_claim.location`.

**Q1 — `already_held`: unreachable.** 3425 live-stance ownership readings, every one
`own`. `claimControl` writes a `controls` edge, `LOCATION.ownedVia` includes `controls`,
so a held target reads `own` and the cell's `unowned` ownership rule excludes it before
any gate could see it. Re-adding the guard would add a branch nothing can reach — the
exact defect that made the original dead.

**Q2 — `reclaim_refused`: reachable, and already guarded.** Zero re-claims inside 24
ticks across 102 collapses, and seed 7's minimum gap of *exactly 24* is the generic
`recent_duplicate` variety guard releasing. That guard matches the collapse record
`retireControl` writes (actor + template + target), which is the THR-1286 fix that
survived the deletion. The deleted 30-tick cooldown's entire marginal effect over it is
deferring 4 re-claims out of 102 collapses by ≤ 6 ticks — noise, not churn.

## What shipped

- `STRATEGIC_RECENT_DUPLICATE_WINDOW_TICKS = 24` — the literal `24` in the variety guard
  was a bare magic number (NFP #1) that had quietly become the only thing standing
  between a collapsed stance and its immediate re-claim. Named, documented with the
  table above, and pinned.
- Two falsifiable assertions in `strategicControlChurn.test.ts`, plus a third pinning the
  window inside the history window it reads from. Both were falsified on the closeout:
  window → 0 turns the re-claim test red; widening `control:claim` ownership to `any`
  turns the held-target test red. Dropping the belt-and-braces
  `undertakingVerb === 'control' && own` skip *alone* stays green — the two Q1 guards are
  genuinely redundant and the ownership rule is the load-bearing half, which is now
  recorded rather than assumed.
- The stale file header promising two tests THR-1303 had deleted, corrected.

No new gate, no new trace category, no re-introduced `verb === 'control'` predicate.
