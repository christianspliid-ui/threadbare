# Prose Doctrine v2 remediation sweep — the guidance surfaces stopped teaching the retired mode (THR-1324)

**2026-08-29** · Content / Improvement · docs-only diff (10 repo files + 3 vault pages)

The first triggered guidance audit (retro 2026-08-28) found ten operative surfaces still
teaching Prose Doctrine v1 as live instruction, three days after the THR-1252 conversion
sweep had supposedly cleared them — and **four of the five `prose@2` stamps over-claiming**,
which is a validation marker reporting success on files carrying contradictions. This
closes all ten, corrects the stamps, and records the two rulings the audit asked for.

## What changed

**The four contradictory skills.** `encounter-pipeline` no longer teaches "one short flavor
quote" in the orientation paragraph every factory run reads first (its own REVISE trigger 16
banned it). `template-encounter-rewrite` no longer instructs authors to write the retired
`fiction` field 150 lines above the gate that rejects it, and its editorial Q3 no longer
teaches show-don't-tell with a model answer opening on the vagueness lexicon's flagship
word. `prose-content-systems` READ-FIRST item 3 no longer demands "a moment, not a label"
with a camera-work exemplar three lines above the paragraph retiring the workflow that
produced it. `prose-pipeline` gained the narrator-mode section it never had — it taught
register but never *mode* while carrying a doctrine stamp.

**The plan docs.** Supersession banners on the nudge-card repertoire's card-face spec (the
flavor quote as "the card's only prose"), the voice-register doc's peak-surface list (which
still granted encounter climaxes), the nudge-model doc's settled ruling 3 (reversed by the
THR-883 pivot), and the four unmarked sections of the May encounter-experience plan —
whose header called itself an "implementation contract for the executor" while handing over
a retired choice-card model.

**The vault.** `Vision/taste-profile.md` no longer mandates the retired picturable-anchor
rule to every design pass, and its vagueness bullet now reflects the *scoped* lexicon rather
than the flat one THR-899 retired. `Systems/Tonal Bible.md` Blend Principles 1–3 are
amended in the THR-1252 pattern (P1 taught outright in-situ interiority), and its header no
longer routes every content author here first. The Cheat Sheet's stale routing line — sitting
directly under its own correct tombstone, pointing back at the surfaces the tombstone had
just retired — now points at canon.

## Two rulings recorded

1. **Foreshadowing has no narrator-mode exemption, and needs none.** The skill described
   the passage as "written from inside the agent's head," which read as a silent carve-out.
   Reporting a motive *is* narration — a GM says "she thinks the road ends somewhere worth
   standing" without entering her head. What v2 forbids is interior *sensation*.
2. **Artifact lore does not get peak register.** `attachment-pipeline` granted peak to
   marquee artifact lore; canon's peak list is a closed enumeration that does not include it,
   and canon names reaching-for-peak as the exact drift the model exists to stop. Narrowed to
   baseline. Widening the list is a director call, not an executor one — flagged, not taken.

## Stamps

All four over-claiming `prose@2` stamps were re-earned the only way a stamp may be:
a full end-to-end read of each file against the doctrine, not a bump on a file that was
merely edited. Those reads turned up four further contradictions the audit had not named —
`template-encounter-rewrite` Q7's model answer opening on the banned word, and its
worked-example "Problems" list citing "No sensory detail" as a defect; `encounter-pipeline`
asking aftermath prose for what the protagonist "*feels* and *becomes*", and asking Pass 1
for a "fiction-grade" paragraph against its own trigger 29 (*game prose, not novel prose*).
Two files gained a first honest stamp: `template-context-rewrite` (whose entire Pass 2 was
the retired scene-first workflow by name) and `attachment-pipeline`.

## What this does not fix

`src/data/foreshadowing-content.ts` ships four live clauses opening on `Something` — real
evasive-lexicon violations in player-facing prose, filed as THR-1360. They are a code diff
and out of this ticket's docs-only scope. Notably the *doc* taught the pattern via its ✅
exemplar, so the corpus was drafting against guidance that endorsed it.

## The honest limit on this work

`check:guidance-freshness` reads green and always did — the ticket's own Rule 0 evidence is
that the gate "cannot see a touched-but-still-wrong dependent." A green gate is a necessary
condition here and not evidence of correctness. The proof is the per-file quoted amendment,
which is why every change above names the text it replaced.
