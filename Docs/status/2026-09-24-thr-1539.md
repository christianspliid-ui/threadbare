## What shipped

- **Harm** (`src/engine/fights/fightHarm.ts`). `computeFightErosion` uses the band tables (`FIGHT_CLASH_HARM_MULT` / `FIGHT_NERVE_HARM_MULT`) and applies ×2 only when the action's `effectiveTier` is `story_beat`, and ×1.5 when the opponent is berserk. It is floored net of harm already queued this tick. The floor is `MEETING_QUINTESSENCE_FLOOR` for a bonded First. Harm is queued as `fight_harm`, and fight steps skip the generic band quintessence event.
- **Band conditions** land through the new exported **`applyConditionToActor`**, the one condition writer. It was extracted from the aftermath's `apply_condition` case, which delegates to it unchanged. A rout leaves the fighter `terrified`, and a strike-down leaves them `wounded` at `FIGHT_CONDITION_INTENSITY_SEVERE`. Each landed condition is recorded in `fightState.conditionsApplied`.
- **Courage and momentum** are named terms on the fight roll: `FIGHT_NERVE_COURAGE_WEIGHT`, `FIGHT_NERVE_CARRY` and `FIGHT_CLASH_MOMENTUM`. A berserk opponent adds `FIGHT_BERSERK_MIGHT_DELTA` to each clash.
- **The `spell_price` split** in `phaseQuintessence`. A ward covers harm, never a spell's price. This is the one live behaviour change: a warded caster on `use × Power` now pays.
