# 2026-09-28 — THR-1646: the doom clock waits for The First

**Ticket:** [THR-1646](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1646) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1605-the-opening.md` § S2 · **Pillars:** Engine (the wake gate, the Unmaking floor, rival grace, omen gate, clock length); Content (7 doom-wake chronicle lines); UI: N/A in this slice (the top-bar reveal is S5, THR-1648).

## What was wrong

A round-1 cold tester's world ended ("The Unmaking begins") in Summer Year 1, about 10 real minutes in. `DEFAULT_DOOM_TICKS` was 200, the clock ran from tick 0 whether or not the player had met anyone, and nothing stopped an accelerated clock from ending the world early.

## What shipped

- **The clock sleeps until the bond.** A fresh clock carries `wokeAtTick: null`. `phaseDoom` does not advance it until `isFirstBonded` holds. It then records `wokeAtTick`, traces `doom.wake`, and posts one chronicle toast from `DOOM_WAKES_LINES` (`src/data/doom-wake-lines.ts`, one line per archetype). A First who dies later never puts the doom back to sleep.
- **The floor.** `phaseDoomExpiry` will not start the Unmaking until `DOOM_MIN_RUN_TICKS_AFTER_BOND` (720) ticks after the wake, whatever accelerated the clock. Until then an expired clock holds at Culmination. `doom.expiry_held` is traced once.
- **Length.** `DEFAULT_DOOM_TICKS` 200 → 1080.
- **Rivals** take no action before the wake or for `RIVAL_GRACE_TICKS_AFTER_BOND` (48) after it. `rival.grace_hold` is traced once. The inline `8 + floor(rng*5)` is now `RIVAL_ACTION_INTERVAL_BASE` + `RIVAL_ACTION_INTERVAL_JITTER`, with the same draw at the same site.
- **Omens** first activate `OMEN_FIRST_ACTIVATION_TICK` ticks after the wake, not after tick 0.
- **New cycles** start with the clock already awake (`wokeAtTick: 0`). A later cycle has no opening to protect, and a cycle whose First died must not sleep forever. *Executor decision; the plan specified only the surviving-First case.*
- **Old saves:** a clock without the field reads as woken at tick 0, so it behaves as before, floor included.
- **Inspectability:** `getOpeningState()` gains `doomWokeAtTick`, `doomFloorMetAtTick` and `doomCurrentTick`. The interface rows `first-bond-wakes-doom`, `doom-floor-holds-unmaking` and `doom-progress-paces-first-journey` are 🔵 UNVERIFIED-OK. The rulebook § Doom Clock is now tagged [IMPL].

## Verified

- 11 tests in `doomWakesAtBond.test.ts` cover: no advance before the bond; the wake on the first bonded tick (trace, line, once); no re-sleep; expiry held under `doom_rate_multiplier` 10 until exactly the floor; the never-woke clock; the legacy clock; rival silence before the bond and through the grace window, then action; the omen gate; the new-cycle wake; and a line for every archetype.
- Headless CLI, seed 42 medium, first run: at `tick 30`, doom `currentTick` is 0, `wokeAtTick` is null, and the world has 504 agents. Second run: after 10 ticks a thread is bonded via `eval`, and at tick 15 the clock shows `wokeAtTick` 11, `currentTick` 5, with `doom.wake` traced at t11.
- Fixtures that model a world in play now state that the bond (and, where relevant, the grace window) is already behind them. The doom-milestone smoke pins the 200-tick clock its invariants were written against. The appointment suite's missed arm gives its mortal the `death_prevented` ward: under the new pacing, seed 42's picked mortal died on an unrelated `agent_death` before the window closed.
