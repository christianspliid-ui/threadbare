# 2026-09-26 — THR-1625: condition loss-guards now guard

**Ticket:** [THR-1625](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1625) · **Project:** Powers & Item Generation · **Plan:** none (the ticket carries the fix; deferral of THR-1569) · **Pillars:** Engine. Content N/A (no catalog entry changed; the fix makes the two shipped guards true). UI N/A (`Browser-verify exempt: engine only`, per the Done-when).

## What was wrong

`prevent_loss` has two channels, and only `quintessence` had a reader. Nothing asked the `condition` channel, so every ward against conditions was inert: Sap-Blessed's `#wound` guard let Wounded through, and The Silent Testament's untagged guard blocked nothing.

## What shipped

- **One check at the condition writer.** `applyConditionToActor` asks `refuseByConditionGuard` right after tag immunity and refuses with `reason: 'prevent_loss'` (guard id, name, spent-or-not on the result). The aftermath's `condition_attachment` path, which writes its own edge, runs the same check.
- **Rules.** Only a harmful (`#negative`) condition is guarded — a ward never stops a blessing. A tagged guard matches any of its tags (`#`-normalised). An untagged guard covers every harmful condition. `amount` has no meaning on this channel.
- **Consuming guards are spent after one block.** An artifact or agreement guard is removed. A trait guard loses only that bearer's `has_trait` edge, because the trait node is a shared definition.
- **Traces.** Aftermath refusals trace `failReason: 'prevent_loss'` naming the guard. A warded Scarred reads `scarSkipped: 'warded'` on the fight-ending trace. `inflict_condition` already traced the reason.
- **Docs.** Wiring guide (the primitives list now has four entries) and the encounters manual page.

## Worth a look

The Silent Testament's guard is untagged and non-consuming, so its bearer is now permanently immune to every harmful condition. Its summary line reads "prevents 1 condition loss", which suggests a one-shot was meant. The fix honours the content as authored; retuning the item is a content call.

## Evidence

`conditionLossGuards.test.ts` (7 tests, real catalog nodes): a Sap-Blessed bearer does not take Wounded and keeps the power, while the unguarded control does. The `#wound` guard lets Terrified through. The Testament blocks Wounded and Terrified but not Blessed. A consuming artifact guard blocks once, is removed, and the next wound lands. A consuming trait guard spends only one bearer's edge, and the other bearer stays warded.
