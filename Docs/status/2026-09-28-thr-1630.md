# 2026-09-28 — THR-1630: people start tied to each other (the people web, slice S1)

**Ticket:** [THR-1630](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1630) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1630-notables-and-ties.md` slice S1 · **Pillars:** Engine (worldgen passes, graduation filter, bond readers); Content (bond-word vocabulary, a `kin` row in both backstory bond tables); UI: N/A for S1 (the bond word on the sheet ships in [THR-1655](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1655)).

## What was wrong

A new world opened with no two people who live in the same place tied to each other. The only tie pass was a random draw between named heroes anywhere on the map. It made no mutual ties and none between neighbours, and nothing ever wrote a family tie at all. So every ambition that reads `lineage`, `heir` or `kin` was dead, and grief had no heir to pass to.

## What shipped

- **Ties** (`seedLivingWorld.seedTies`): each named hero gets one kin, one friend and one rival among the people of their home settlement. A hero living at a tower, road or ruin ties to the nearest settlement of their culture within 6 hexes. Ties are written both ways, stamped `origin: 'worldgen'`, and drawn from a new reserved stream (prime 60089). A deep seeded rivalry still becomes an old quarrel.
- **The old random pass keeps its dice.** The block in `seedWorld` still rolls every draw, so the rest of the world is byte-identical, and only the write is off (`WORLDGEN_RANDOM_PROTAGONIST_TIES_ENABLED = false`). Its three inline numbers are now named constants. `seededTies-streamIntact.test.ts` proves it: with the flag on and off, the world's nodes and non-tie edges are identical.
- **Home standing** (`seedHomeStanding`): a hero's starting Realm membership (THR-1620's standing) moves to the Realm that holds their home, or, failing that, their tie-pool settlement.
- **Favours** (`seedFavors`): each hero with a faction owes a favour to its highest-standing fellow member. A friendship pair is added when the two had no tie.
- **Secrets per hero**: `seedMarks` now counts 0.33 per hero, taken in rounds across cultures. It used to be one per culture. The per-culture ceiling rose from 1 to 6.
- **Seeded ties never make a decider**: `phaseNpcGraduation` counts only edges earned in play toward `SPOTLIGHT_MIN_EDGES`.
- **One word for kin** (`src/data/bond-basis.ts`): `bondBasisMatches` folds `lineage`, `heir`, `exile_kin` and `kinship` onto `kin`, `enemy` onto `rivalry`, and `spouse` onto `romantic`. Ambition `bondModifiers`, `agent_has_bonds`, the backstory resolver and the Return's spouse ripple all read through it. The ambition assignment trace gains `bondsMatched` / `bondBases`.
- **Found and fixed on the way: a secret about you hid favours owed to you.** On the undertaking board, an Agreement is placed at its edge's target, and two on one node collapsed to the first by edge id. A favour owed *to* a mortal and a secret someone holds *about* them both sit at that mortal, so a mortal who was the subject of any mark could never redeem a favour owed to them. Seeding secrets per hero made that common, and `yieldBandCells.test.ts` caught it. `findValidTargets` now keeps the handle the actor is eligible to act on when two objects share a place.
- **Content**: a `kin` row in both backstory bond tables (the `lineage` lines, reworded to read with "kin"), plus `BOND_BASIS_WORDS` for S4.
- **Docs**: UL term **Kin** (`Agents.md`), interface rows `worldgen-ties-reach-ambition-and-grief` and `seeded-ties-never-graduate` (both LIVE), the extended `worldgen-seeds-the-living-world` row, wiki pages, and a wiring-guide note.
