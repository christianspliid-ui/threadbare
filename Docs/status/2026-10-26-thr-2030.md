# THR-2030 — A seeded relic lies at its place

**Shipped 2026-10-26.** Engine and view models (`src/types/graph.ts`, `src/types/edgeSchema.ts`, `src/engine/seedEchoes.ts`, `src/engine/dominion.ts`, `src/engine/views/subjects/subjectPage.ts`, `src/engine/views/connections.ts`). No content change.

**Why.** The relic an earlier god leaves in a new world (THR-1843) kept its site as `properties.locationId`, an id in a property bag that no reader looked at. Its Inspector page had no "where" line, `placeOf` could not find it, and the site's Connections did not list it.

**What changed.** A new narrow edge, `lies_at` (artifact → place, many-to-one, `sinceTick`), decided by the design lane on 2026-10-25 instead of widening `located_at` (about 39 readers treat its sources as residents) or `contains` (pathfinding walks it). `seedRelic` writes it and no longer sets `locationId`. `placeOf` and the subject page's `whereOf` fall back to it for a node with no `located_at`. The two Connections rows that read artifacts through `located_at` (a place's artifacts, an artifact's "where") now read `lies_at`; no artifact ever had `located_at`, so neither row lost anything. The relic still reads Foreign until someone takes it up. Taking it up (THR-2031) must remove the edge.

**Verified.** `seedEchoes.test.ts`: on seeds 42 / 99 / 7 with the sample account the relic has exactly one `lies_at` to its host, no `locationId`, `placeOf` returns the host, its page's where line names the host, and the host's Connections list the relic (the artifact's own Connections "where" row keeps the knowledge floor and stays hidden for an undiscovered site, as on seeds 99 and 7). A schema arm refuses `lies_at` from an actor. A jsdom render of the real `SubjectHeader` shows the site in the relic's where line. The wiring: interface contract `unborne-artifact-lies-at-its-place` and an anchor-catalog row.
