# 2026-09-26 — THR-1569: fear wards, wound cures and curse wards reach the real conditions

**Ticket:** [THR-1569](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1569) · **Project:** Powers & Item Generation · **Plan:** none (the ticket carries the fix) · **Pillars:** Content (three condition tags). Engine N/A (no engine code changed; the shipped readers were already correct). UI N/A (`Browser-verify exempt: content data only`, per the Done-when).

## What was wrong

Wards (`tag_immunity`) and cures (`condition_remove` by tag) find a condition by its family tag. Terrified, Wounded and Cursed did not carry theirs. As a result, the three shipped fear wards blocked nothing on a mortal, the wound cures left the Wounded that fights give, and a curse ward let Cursed through. Each time, the trace showed the check running.

## What shipped

- `src/data/condition-trait-content.ts`: Terrified gains `#fear`, Wounded `#wound`, Cursed `#curse`. All three are seated family tags. The Condition kind requires *a* family and a polarity, and a second family beside `#combat` / `#mystical` is accepted.
- **Corpus test** `src/data/__tests__/wardTagCoverage.test.ts`: every tag named by a `tag_immunity`, a `condition_remove`, or a `prevent_loss` on the `condition` channel must be carried by a condition a bearer can hold, across every registered content kind, walked deep. Place conditions (`#location`) do not count. The allowlist fails both ways.
- **Behaviour test** `src/engine/effects/__tests__/wardsReachCoreConditions.test.ts`: the real catalog wards block Terrified and Cursed through the one condition writer, and the real wound cures lift Wounded, each with a control. On the pre-fix data, 9 of 14 assertions go red.

## Misses reported (allowlisted with reasons)

11 ward tags name no bearer condition: `#intimidation`, `#bruise`, `#tracked`, `#marked`, `#corruption`, `#blight`, `#poison`, `#illusion`, `#cold`, `#frostbite`, `#dissonance`. None of them is seated in the closed tag vocabulary, and the vocabulary ratchet forbids a condition authoring an unseated tag. Covering one means seating it or retargeting the ward, which is content design and not a tag fix. The allowlist gives the reason for each tag.
