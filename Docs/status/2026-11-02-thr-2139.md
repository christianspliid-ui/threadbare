# THR-2139 — the Aftermath and the mortal's sheet agree

**Shipped 2026-11-02.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience. Found by cold playtest round 6 (2/3 testers; the veteran's worst moment was "the numbers under the prose" not matching the sheet).

Five places where the Aftermath and the sheet told one fact two ways:

1. **Reputation word at `known`.** `getAgentInfoCard` set `reputationWord` only at intimate+, but the sheet's reputation row counts as revealed at `known` (or by gossip depth). The row then said "No one has spoken of Elara's reputation to you" beside an Aftermath that had just raised it. The card now sets the word at every level, and the sheet resolver (`personRows.ts` `reputation`) is its one gate. The Disposition panel keeps its own gate (`dispositionRevealed` or intimate).
2. **"Passed" on a lost guard trial.** `cg.join`'s fallback had one reaction, `join_accept` ("Passed."), shown on any outcome. The fallback now has `byOutcome` bands for `failure`, `critical_failure` and `contested_lost`: a "sent away, come back later" overview and reaction, and no chip, because nothing is written (Law 56, `chipBacking.test.ts`).
3. **"Won, at a price" with no price.** `computeFinalActionOutcome` reads any failed or near-missed step as `success_at_cost`. `priceRowFor` named a price only when a step itself landed `success_at_cost`. It now takes the resolved steps (optional, NFP #6) and names what the worst missed step cost, by its consequence band: a near miss learns less, a failure loses the step's gain, and a critical failure also costs quintessence. The words come from `outcomeConsequences.ts`, with no amounts (Law IV).
4. **"Faced it alone" after the god paid.** The cause line said "alone" whenever the deciding step had no hand. When another step of the same chapter carried the hand, it now reads "your cards were on another step, not this one" (`CAUSE_GOD_ELSEWHERE`).
5. **Two-step chapters listed twice in Story.** One event node is minted per step. Each node now carries the action's `actionId` (an additive property), and the Story group folds nodes with the same `actionId` into one row, keeping the latest step's row. Older nodes without the id behave as before.

**Tests.** `thr2139-aftermath-sheet-agree.test.ts`, one block per item. `aftermathCauseLine.test.ts` expected "alone" in its many-step case, where the other step held the hand. That was the defect, so the test now expects the new clause.
