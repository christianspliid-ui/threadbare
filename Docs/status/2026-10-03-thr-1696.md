### 2026-10-03 — THR-1696: a ruin visit follows the ruin it was arranged for

**The bug.** The review-gate backtest on PR #2151 (THR-1664) found two faults in the ruin visit. (1) `claimLeadVisit` stamped `pendingVisitDueTick` on the lead *before* `plantAppointmentPromise` ran. When the planter then refused (`over_max`: three appointments already held; `place_unresolved`), no visit was planted, but the stamp stayed. The lead was spared decay, and every repeat survey was refused `visit_pending` for about 72 ticks. (2) `sharpen_clue` did not know which ruin its visit was for. It picked the stamped lead on the mortal's hex, else the soonest stamp, and lapsed stamps counted too. A missed visit to ruin A could therefore cool the lead on ruin B, where the mortal happened to stand.

**The fix.** The claim now also stamps `pendingVisitSeedId`, the seed the visit rides. When the planter refuses, the new `releaseLeadVisit` clears the stamp, but only the stamp that claim wrote, matched by due tick. A missed seed keeps its seed id, so `sharpen_clue` passes the action's `spawnedFromSeedId` into `resolveVisitLead`, which resolves the lead stamped with that seed wherever the mortal stands. The fallback, used for `?spawn=` reviews and older stamps, now considers only *live* stamps (`isLeadVisitPending`), so a lapsed stamp never wins the pick. These changes are additive: one new optional edge property and one new optional parameter.

**Evidence.** `leadVisit.test.ts` grows from 11 to 16 tests, all passing. The new tests cover: an `over_max` refusal plants nothing and leaves no stamp, and the next survey once a slot frees is admitted; release clears only its own stamp; a plant stamps its seed id; the two-ruin scenario (A due T+48, B due T+60, mortal on B, A's missed sequel at T+72), where A goes cold and B stays narrowed and stamped; and an expired stamp loses to the lead underfoot. `npm run gate`: test PASS (1447 files / 22844 tests), typecheck ratchet OK at 2800 (unchanged), build PASS, encounter PASS, 30-tick CLI smoke PASS. In the gate's test-heavy lane, two files timed out (`debugTickBatch`, `chapterArchive`) because a second `test:heavy` run was going at the same time. Re-run alone, both pass 7/7.
