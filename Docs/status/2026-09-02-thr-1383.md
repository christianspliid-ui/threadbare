# THR-1383 — grievance supply: the window, the displacement, the leader

**2026-09-02 · engine.** The reactive loop THR-1298 shipped minted zero vendettas in 300 ticks of ordinary play on both seeds, with every emission site firing. Three gates starved it; three rules close them, each measured before it was written and falsified once after. Same-day design session and execution ([plan doc](../plans/2026-09-02-thr-1383-grievance-supply.md), intent-judge Allow, three audits PASS).

## The three rules

- **The mint window is derived, not copied.** The mint pass runs where the milestone cadence (15) and the re-evaluation cadence (25) coincide — every 75 ticks — but looked back only 25, so two harms in three were never offered to anyone. `MINT_LOOKBACK_TICKS` is now the lcm of the two exported intervals, and the window is half-open on the far side so the passes tile the timeline: a harm at any tick is offered to exactly one pass. A retune of either interval cannot reopen the gap. The encounter-outcome mints (THR-726) widen with it — recorded, not hidden.
- **A vendetta may take a full mortal's secondary want.** The mint-block gate now runs for a mortal whose two slots are full, offering only grievance-class rows at or above `GRIEVANCE_DISPLACE_MIN_MAGNITUDE` (0.5 — a razing, a seizure, a severed network; never a spoiled deal). A written vendetta closes the *secondary* want as `abandoned` with `abandonedReason: 'displaced_by_grievance'`, emits `ambition_displaced`, and puts one line in the chronicle — *"Hask sets aside Found a Dynasty — a vendetta against Lirik takes its place."* The primary is never displaced; the spontaneous re-evaluation gate stays closed; a mortal already holding a vendetta takes the feed/replace path and displaces nothing. Both literal `2`s became `MAX_ACTIVE_AMBITIONS`.
- **A faction's harm reaches its leader.** Six of thirteen harms on the observation run landed on guilds, which hold no wants. The outcome node now writes a second `participated_in` target edge for the faction's leader, tagged `viaFactionId`, and the provenance reads *"the severing of Greybridge, done to the Ironwrights — Hesk's work"*. The faction's own edge stays.

## One rule the plan did not write, found on the way

The first organic run on a seed that has harms (123) minted nothing at pass 75 although three grave harms sat in the window on one full mortal: the pass's own seed landed on `MINT_BASE_CHANCE`'s 40% miss, and with the passes tiling, that harm is never offered again. A grave harm is not a coin flip. **The base-chance roll now applies to soft drives only**; an offer carrying a grievance-class candidate at or above the displacement bar always reaches the temperament funnel, which still decides whether revenge is what this mortal takes from it. The disposition (tier, chain depth, one slot) is unchanged. Decided and recorded here rather than asked, per rule 4; a veto is a one-line revert of the `graveHarm` branch.

## What the arc panel does not show, and why the word lives on the chronicle

The plan put *"set aside for a vendetta"* on the arc panel. The arc panel's read model excludes abandoned ambitions by an earlier deliberate decision (THR-721: biography, not a failure ledger), so the word has no home there and adding one would reopen that decision. The chronicle line carries it instead — the surface that already narrates every abandonment — and the edge keeps the `abandonedReason` for the trace and the CLI. No `src/components` change, so no browser-verify is owed.

## The census's variety floor was one seed's luck

The first census under this change failed seed 99's *distinct templates in the first 150 starts* gate at 24 against a floor of 25, with seed 42 at 28. A controlled arm — the same build with the old 25-tick inclusive window — read 25 on seed 99; the same build reads 31 on seed 123 and 17 on seed 7. Nothing in this change touches the board; a mint-lane perturbation moves one seed's count by two through ordinary chaos, and two is the whole margin THR-1349 derived on two seeds. The floor now applies to the **mean over the census seeds** (26 on the default pair, still separating the calibrated board from the uncalibrated one at 23.5 and from contest B at 38.5); each seed must still fill its sample, and the per-seed count is printed beside the mean so a collapse on one seed stays visible. Calibration is the agent's verdict (rule 4); recorded in the constant's docblock and here.
