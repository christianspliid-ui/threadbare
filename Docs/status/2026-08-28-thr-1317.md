# Seven chips that named someone and pointed nowhere (THR-1317)

**The whole population was one misunderstanding, written down three times.** Seven declared chip anchors carried an `entityId` and no `visualKind`, so `fromConceptRef` — `if (!ref.entityId || !ref.visualKind) return undefined` — dropped the reference before it ever reached a resolver. The chip rendered as plain text carrying a `$actor` sentinel nothing would read. Not a dead link (Law 21 is satisfied: no affordance is drawn) and not a `check:chip-anchors` violation (the declaration is well-formed) — an anchor authored into a field its own surface ignores.

What made it worth reading rather than pattern-fixing is that **every one of the three sites said out loud that it wanted the actor**. The drowned-archive header recorded a package-critic re-anchor from `$target`/`location` to `$actor` "so the chip's click lands where the state actually changed"; toll-of-blades cited the anchor catalog's "bearer entityId plus stat tooltipId"; the broken seal kept `$actor` as "the carrier route the anchor catalog names for an ambition (seen on the pursuing actor's sheet)". All three then dropped or omitted the `visualKind`, believing the id alone carried the route. It does not — dropping the kind removes the route itself. So all seven gained `visualKind: 'agent'`: the intent was never in doubt, only its compilation.

**The ticket's second disposition turned out not to exist, and measuring that is what settled the hard case.** The Done-when offered "or drops the `entityId`, making the chip honestly anchorless" — attractive for `seal.crit_fail.the_wanting`, whose correction is explicit that the sentence is about the ambition and *not* the person. Tried, and refused by `check:chip-anchors`: Law 56 clause 2 requires a `stateNoun` to carry an `entityId` **or** a resolving `tooltipId`, no tooltip concept names an ambition, and the `visualKind` union has no `ambition` member. So anchorlessness was not the narrower option it read as — it was a new gate violation, and the carrier route the comment already asked for was the only shape available. (Dropping the id would not even have entered the `chipsWithoutReferent` ratchet the ticket routed it to: that predicate counts chips with *neither* a `stateNoun` nor `concepts`, and this chip keeps both.)

**The pinned list is emptied and kept.** `ANCHORS_WITHOUT_VISUAL_KIND` in `worldRefNoOpGate.contract.test.ts` is now `[]`, which is the live assertion rather than a leftover — the `toEqual` fails the moment any new chip declares an `entityId` its surface will ignore. Falsified rather than assumed: removing one `visualKind` again fails both gate assertions and names the anchor (`toll_of_blades::toll.iron_tested::$actor ... resolved to nothing`), then passes on restore. Six characterization tests across two encounter files that pinned the broken shape were inverted with the reason recorded, not deleted — including two "carries exactly one individual-anchored chip" counts that were true only while the other chips were dead.
