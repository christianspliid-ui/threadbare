# THR-2265 — The Mandate arrives with its first mention

**Shipped 2026-11-20.** In cold playtest round 8, two of three testers read "Seren's deed raised your Mandate in Time." and "Counts toward your Mandate." long before the Mandate row reached the rail: the row waited for the Seat gift and then queued behind Doom and Rivals. Built from the design lane's decision on THR-2262 (no plan doc).

1. **One predicate,** `mandateNamed(state)` (`src/engine/mandateNamed.ts`): true when the run is not staged, the Seat gift has resolved, or a closed chapter has credited the Mandate — a deed or a god's cast. The first credit writes a durable latch, `GameState.mandateFirstCreditedTick` (in `phaseUnifiedActionProgress`), so the Mandate stays named after the resolved action is pruned; the live action's `mandateDeedCredits` and `mandateState.recentDeeds` (older saves) are read too. The review gate's round 1 found the latch was needed: a cast credit leaves no deed record, so without it the card line went away again 20 ticks later. It also reads true with no beat state or a finished spine (the reveal's existing fallbacks), and on a throw.
2. **The row's trigger is that predicate, and the row jumps the queue.** New trigger `mandate_named` and row flag `jumpsQueue` (`src/data/opening-reveal-table.ts`). In `releaseOpeningArrivals` a ready flagged row arrives on the render it becomes ready, alone, and counts as that step's arrival, so the next row still waits one closed chapter.
3. **The card hint waits for the row.** `buildNudgePhaseModel` sets `mandateHint` only when `mandateNamed` holds; before that the card shows no Mandate line.
4. The "New beside the map: Mandates." toast and caption are unchanged; the Aftermath's deed row is unchanged.

Executor note: past the 200-tick ceiling the row can arrive before the Mandate is named; the card line then still waits for the first credit or the Seat.

**Evidence.** Unit: `src/engine/__tests__/mandateNamed.thr2265.test.ts` (Done-when a–e), `openingReveal.test.ts` updated for the jumping row. Browser-verify substitution: jsdom-render — `src/components/Game/GameView/__tests__/mandateArrivesWithMention.thr2265.test.tsx` renders the real `useOpeningReveal` hook (the Mandate shows and toasts once on the credited render, with nothing else arriving) and the real `NudgePhaseShell` (no Mandate line before it is named, the line after). A live capture would have needed a full Meet-The-First playthrough to reach a staged run's first credited Aftermath.
