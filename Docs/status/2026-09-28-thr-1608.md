# 2026-09-28 — THR-1608: the Stellaris clock

**Ticket:** [THR-1608](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1608) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1605-the-opening.md` § S3 · **Pillars:** UI (interrupt registry, resume policy, popup queue); Content (store page and two wiki pages rewritten from "turn-based"); Engine N/A (the plan says the engine part is nil).

## What was wrong

Round-1 cold testers read "turn-based" on the store page and got a real-time game. Resolving any popup restarted time, even over a manual pause, through three separate paths:

- the THR-668 force-resume ref;
- the choice-set modal's direct `setRunning(true)`;
- the notification hook's own pause pair.

Doom-stage popups rendered beside open beats. The Chapter Ledger let time run while the player read it.

## What shipped

- **One registry.** `src/components/Game/interruptRegistry.ts` declares `INTERRUPT_SURFACES`: the ten THR-668 surfaces plus the Chapter Ledger and the popup channel. `resolveInterrupts` is pure. The auto-pause, `getOpenModals()` and the new `getInterruptState()` all read its result.
- **One at a time.** The moment card and popups yield to every other interrupt. A doom stage that fires under an open beat or ledger waits in the queue, then shows.
- **Resume to prior state.** `useInterruptAutoPause` records the clock state when the first interrupt opens and restores it when the last one closes. The three always-resume paths are gone.
- **Copy.** `the-game.html` (title, eyebrow, footer), `turn-structure-reference.html` and `run-lifecycle-reference.html` now describe the Stellaris model. Rulebook §3 and the quick reference are flipped DESIGN → IMPL.

## Evidence

- 18 new or rewritten tests (registry 10, hook 8). Full suite: 1375 files, 22076 tests green. Typecheck ratchet unchanged at 2818. `vite build` green.

  - paused → open ledger → close → still paused;

  - a doom-stage popup queued behind the open ledger (`queuedPopups: 1`, `open: ['ChapterLedger']`), then rendered alone after close (`open: ['EventPopup']`), and the clock returned to paused on Acknowledge.
  - Screenshots are in `Docs/evidence/thr-1608/`.

## Contested line (plan § Notes)

Encounter commit-and-continue used to force a resume. It now returns to the prior state: a player who paused, then committed a card, stays paused. The PR records this and invites a veto rather than re-adding "always resume".
