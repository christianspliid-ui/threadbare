# 2026-10-09 — THR-1779: template seams stop reaching the player

**Ticket:** [THR-1779](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1779) · **Found by:** warm playtest round 1 (veteran and the dry-run story tester)

The Chapter Ledger, work names and ambition lines no longer show template seams.

- **Empty cast slot.** `buildChapterRecord` (`src/engine/chapterArchive.ts`) now passes the template's `supportBundle`, the action's `supportBindings` and its `targetId` into the chapter context, the same way the live stage does. "His only kin, , claims" now names the heir.
- **Literal tokens.** The archive now enriches the aftermath overview and each change's title and detail before it stores them (`enrichAftermath`). Before, `{location}`, `{actor}` and `{cast:*}` reached the ledger as written.
- **Doubled noun.** Christening stamps `christenedAtTick` on the work. `resolveAnchorName` skips a christened node, and the namer drops an anchor that already contains the work's noun (`anchorRepeatsNoun`). A quarter inside a quarter is no longer "The Quarter of Quarter of Heart of the Barrow".
- **Pronoun flips.** The 46 gendered lines in `ambition-templates.ts` now use pronoun tokens. `resolveAmbitionProseFor` (`src/engine/ambitionProse.ts`) fills them from the actor's gender at the three event sites in `ambitionTick.ts` and on the sheet's intent line. The Codex uses they/them.

**Evidence.** `thr1779-template-seams.test.ts` has 10 tests, one block per cause. `thr1779-chapterSeamSweep.test.ts` (heavy lane) runs seed 42 on a small map for 300 ticks and finds no `{` and no ", ," in any archived chapter text.
