# THR-1959 — The restless road (firing rhythm S2)

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** Slice 2 of the encounter firing rhythm plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-16-thr-1218-encounter-firing-rhythm.md`, D6 / E5). Suppose a paused (followed) mortal has gone `STORY_DROUGHT_TICKS` (72, six days) without a story chapter, and no story is among the candidates she can actually draw. She then sets out for the nearest place within `RESTLESS_ROAD_MAX_HEXES` (12) whose encounters hold a story she may still draw. The Chronicle says *"{name} grows restless and takes the road to {place}."*, with both names as links.

**What changed.**
- **Engine.** New `src/engine/restlessRoad.ts` holds the due check, the destination search (nearest by hex distance, ties to the lower location id, no PRNG), the thread-edge writes, the `agent.restless_road` trace and the Chronicle event. Forced travel's path code is extracted into `startTravelTo` in `phaseAgentDecision.ts`, and both callers share it. The trigger runs after the tellings cap and the cooldown pass, on the real candidate list. A mortal with an appointment keeps it.
- **Chronicle links.** `TickEvent.segments` and `ChronicleEntry.segments` are new, both optional. `phaseNarrative` copies the segments onto the entry, and `ChronicleEntryCard` renders each segment that carries a ref through `EntityLink` (Laws 2, 21). Before this, the Chronicle rendered plain text only.
- **Constants.** `STORY_DROUGHT_TICKS` is now live and listed in the CMS. `RESTLESS_ROAD_COOLDOWN_TICKS` (48) and `RESTLESS_ROAD_MAX_HEXES` (12) are new, each with `@range` and a CMS entry.
- **Debug.** `getAttentionCadence()` now returns the real `lastRestlessRoadTick` and the new `restlessRoadCheckTick`.
- **Census.** `npm run census:rhythm` lists the drought windows, the restless-road checks inside each, the longest window, and which template prefixes set the anchors. It adds a PASS/FAIL predicate: every drought that runs `RESTLESS_ROAD_COOLDOWN_TICKS` past the threshold must hold a check.
- **Docs.** Rulebook §7 gains *A mortal with no story nearby goes looking for one* `[IMPL]`. New interface contract `restless-road-reaches-chronicle`.

**Deviation from the plan.** The plan has three outcomes; the cooldown now runs after each of them, not only after a set-out. A second thread-edge property, `restlessRoadCheckTick`, records the last check. Without it, a `no_destination` or `story_at_home` result would scan the whole encounter cache and emit a trace on every decision of a drought. The plan's performance note assumes "at most once per four days". In the plan, "story at home" also meant a cheap check on her own location's cache entries; here it reads the decision's own candidate list, which is the stronger predicate.

**The review gate's two confirmed findings changed three more things.**
- **She keeps her road.** The journey now records the target story's `questPriority` as `motivationPull` and names it in `targetEncounterId`, the THR-1639 shape. With no pull, the moving path's re-route check would have dropped the road at its first re-check.
- **"May still draw" runs the real filter pipeline from the destination**, and her own checks (retirement, the tellings cap, cooldown) run first so the pipeline's candidate cap never cuts them. This differs from the plan, which said not to. It runs for the nearest few places at most, and at most once per four days. Without it, a story inside her awareness that a prerequisite or gate had already rejected could be the place she walks to.
- **The road waits out her story breath.** During the breath every story is held back on purpose, at home and at any destination, so "no story in reach" would only be the breath talking. Writing the heavy test surfaced this.

On `story_at_home` the trace's `storyTemplateIds` now lists the story candidates she already has.

**Evidence.**
- **Headless.** `restlessRoad.heavy.test.ts` uses the seeded medium world with the drought aged. With a story at home, she is checked once in 40 ticks and stays. With every story in her awareness told out, she sets out, `lastRestlessRoadTick` is stamped, and the Chronicle entry carries agent + location refs.
- **Census.** `census:rhythm` on seeds 7 and 42 PASSes all four predicates each. Neither seed reaches a 72-tick drought in 600 ticks: social and other non-routine starts move her drought anchor, while the census gap list counts `encounter.*` chapters only. So natural set-outs are 0, and the census now prints that split.
- **Browser.** bash-vite on 5247, Playwright at 1920×1080. The line was rendered through the served `buildRestlessRoadEvent` → `phaseNarrative` → `ChronicleEntryCard`, with both names as routed links. The console showed 0 errors. `getAttentionCadence()` returned the new fields live.
- **Gate.** `npm run gate` passed every check.

A stack trace inside `recordStepEventNode` printed during the long seed-7 census. That code path is untouched here.
