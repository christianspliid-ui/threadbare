### 2026-09-10 — THR-1424 (UI · Game Design): a proportion reads as the bar it already had

**The ruling, implemented rather than re-decided.** THR-1424 sat blocked on a creative fork with no agreed outcome to test against: Law 13 bans percentages on player-facing surfaces outright, and unlike a raw tick count a proportion has **no sanctioned numeric alternative**. `durationLabel` — the off-the-shelf fix THR-1423 used eight times — works only because it converts a duration into a unit the player already reasons in (days); `62% of something` has no such unit. And banding it to a word is closed off from the other side: the Law 13 amendment of 2026-08-12 rules an adverb the wrong answer to *"how much?"*, Christian's verdict on `grew steadily` being *"how can a player use that word to gage anything"*.

An attended design session recorded the answer on the ticket this morning, and this run implemented it:

> **The sanctioned reading for a player-facing unitless proportion is the surface's existing non-numeric rendering, and where none exists the number is dropped rather than translated.**

That is the Law 15 question — *"a third magnitude language needs a ruling"* — answered **no**. No new language enters the design system. `Docs/design-system/laws.md` carries it under Law 13/15 with the reasoning against a word ladder restated in place, so the next executor does not re-derive `steadily`.

**What shipped: the progress family, everywhere it renders.** The ticket named two sites; both had same-quantity siblings, and shipping a ruling to one of three renderings of the same number would have left the surface disagreeing with itself.

- `DoomClockDetail` — the named site. The ring centre read `${pct}% / elapsed`, a **third** rendering of a quantity the surface already states twice (the ring arc, and `Current Chapter N of 5`). Dropped; the centre now carries only `∞ / UNMADE`, which names a terminal state rather than answering "how much?". Two siblings went with it: the next-beat label (`Tremors @ 40%` → `Tremors`) and the per-stage threshold column, whose reading is the stage list's own order and its past/current/future styling.
- `AgentInfoCard` — the other named site. The active-effect tooltip dropped `${strengthPct}% strength`; the strength bar beside the label is the reading. THR-1423 had fixed the duration half of this same string and left this half deliberately, with a comment pointing here.
- `DoomBar`, `JourneyVignetteModal` — the same doom proportion rendered in persistent chrome and on the journey badge.
- `MandateTracker`, `MandateDetail` — mandate progress (pips + `ProgressBar` are the reading) and the checkpoint doom thresholds. `FULFILLED` / `NEW` / `In Progress` survive: each names a **state**, not a magnitude.

`JourneyVignetteModal` is the one site exercising the ruling's second limb. Its badge names the journey *phase*, not the doom, so there was no non-numeric rendering of the doom to fall back to — and the honest move there is to drop rather than invent a fourth language.

**Deliberately not swept, and why it is a scoping call rather than a shortfall.** The ruling's membership sweep is far wider than the progress family, and reading every match showed it is not one class but **four**. Filed as THR-1451 with each site classified: **deltas** (`+7%` on a sphere row) already have a sanctioned language in Law 15's delta cluster, so they are a *conversion*, not a drop; **odds and probabilities** already have pips, likewise; **plain proportions** elsewhere (faction reputation, domain score) take this ruling directly and are mechanical; and the settings volume slider is app chrome reading its own control, which wants ruling explicitly out of membership so it stops resurfacing. Applying "drop it" uniformly across all four would have deleted readings that Law 15 says should be *converted* — which is why the residue is a ticket and not a bigger diff.

**Two tests were caught passing for the wrong reason, both by the same discipline.** Every negative arm here is paired with a positive assertion on the surviving reading, because `not.toMatch(/%/)` alone passes just as happily on a surface that rendered nothing at all.

1. **The vacuous-modal trap.** `Modal` uses `createPortal`, so RTL's `container` is **empty** for `MandateDetail`, `DoomClockDetail` and `JourneyVignetteModal`. The first draft asserted `container.textContent).not.toMatch(/%/)` and went green on three modals it had never actually looked at. The paired positive arm is what failed and exposed it; all modal arms now assert on `baseElement`.
2. **The invented fixture.** The first `DoomClockDetail` fixture omitted three **required** `DoomClockState` fields and crashed the component at its real `.filter` — and the first `AgentInfoCard` fixture invented an `InterventionType` and a `SphereName` that do not exist, caught by the typecheck ratchet at `+2`. Both were fixed against the real types rather than papered over with `as unknown as`, which is what hides invented values behind a green test.

The arms were then falsified from both directions: injecting a `99%` row into `DoomClockDetail` reds exactly the two surface-wide arms and nothing else, and the change itself red four **pre-existing** tests that asserted the percentages were present (`DoomBar`'s `25%`/`75%`, `MandateTracker`'s `50%`, `GameView`'s `\d+%`, and `TickReadoutLaw1314`'s deliberate `toMatch(/%/)` pin) — that last one carrying the comment *"when THR-1424 lands it should fail and be tightened"*, which is exactly what happened.

**One real bug of my own, caught by the ratchet rather than the suite:** removing `MandateDetail`'s `pct` left line 218 still reading it, so the modal threw on open. `npm test` surfaced it only through an unrelated Law-13 sweep test; `tsc -b` named it directly. It now reads `state.progress > 0`.

**Verification.** Suite green (**1204 files, 19819 tests**), ratchet **2912 unchanged and baseline not refreshed**, `vite build` green. No engine files touched, so no CLI smoke. **Browser-verify substitution: `jsdom-render`** — this was an unattended scheduled run and `preview_start` is refused outright in one (impediments #546/#574), which shuts the Playwright route with it. The substitution is jsdom render assertions on the real components covering every face the change produces plus absence where an element should no longer render: 14 arms across `ProportionReadoutLaw1315.test.tsx` and `DoomClockDetailNoPercentage.test.tsx`, with the rendered surface captured verbatim — `DoomBar` now reads `Doom · Signs · The First awaits · Next: Tremors`, and `DoomClockDetail` reads `Current Chapter 2 of 5 · Elapsed two days of eight days · Next Beat Tremors · Whispers Signs Tremors Crisis Culmination`, no percentages in either.

**UI Laws judgment (Laws 1, 13, 14, 15, 17, 21, 33, 37):** compliant. Law 13 is the subject and is now satisfied on all six surfaces; Law 15 is satisfied by *not* minting a third magnitude language; Law 21 holds because nothing became a control that does nothing — the dropped numerals were readouts, and each surface keeps the bar, ring, pip row or name that carried the quantity. Laws 1/17/33/37 are untouched: no layout, affordance or vocabulary changed, only text removed from surfaces that already rendered the same quantity another way.
