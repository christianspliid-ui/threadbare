# THR-1483 — three location conditions had a state nothing read

**Date:** 2026-09-13 · **Pillars:** Engine · Content · UI-N/A (no component, hook, context or CSS file in the diff)
**Issue:** [THR-1483](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1483/three-location-conditions-have-no-live-mechanical-effect-under-watch)

## What was wrong

THR-1475 built the surface that says what a condition *does* — on the hover and on the sheet — and in doing so had to ask each of the fifteen shipped `trait.condition.*` definitions what it actually did. Twelve answered. Three answered *nothing*: `under_watch`, `tended_shrine` and `standing_welcome` appeared only as something content **wrote** and as a chip `stateNoun` that **named** them. No engine reader existed for any of the three.

Their own definitions claimed readers they did not have. `under_watch`'s comment said *"the readers are the movement tax and the gate"* — it carries no tax by design, and every shipped `requiredTargetTraits` gate in the corpus names a `trait.reputation.*` trait. So the claim was false twice over, and the interface-map row for location conditions had already caught it one layer out.

THR-1475 could not fix it by writing a sentence, because the sentence would have been a lie. *"Quiet work here is likelier to be seen"* is the obvious line for `under_watch` and nothing read the trait, so nothing was likelier. A chip may not promise what the engine cannot enact (director ruling, 2026-09-12) — and that rule does not loosen when the unenactable promise would have made a gate pass. The three were parked in `CONDITION_IDS_WITHOUT_EFFECT` and rendered no effect line at all, with a both-directions test making the list a record of a real gap rather than a way to pass the file.

## What shipped

Per condition, one of the two outcomes the ticket allowed — never a third.

### `under_watch` and `tended_shrine` — a real reader

A third substrate, `LOCATION_CONDITION_STEP_MODIFIER`, the movement tax's sibling answering the other half of the question: not what a place costs to **reach** but what it costs to **work in**.

| condition | term | why that reach |
|---|---|---|
| `under_watch` | `{ shadow: -0.06 }` | What the condition has always said it does — *"quiet work here is harder and more likely to be seen"* — and what its own `#shadow` tag declares |
| `tended_shrine` | `{ veil: +0.05 }` | Veil is the ritual reach, and a kept shrine is the one place-state that says a rite here has been answered lately (`#sacred`) |

Read by **`collectLocationConditionContributions`** (`src/engine/resolutionModifiers.ts:634`), wired into `computeResolutionModifiers` at `:713` and `:748`. That site was chosen because `locationId` is **already a parameter there** and `collectTerrainContributions` is already a place-derived term sitting beside it — so a location condition reaches the roll through the one channel every other contextual modifier uses, and surfaces a named factor line by construction.

Four decisions inside it worth stating:

- **One reach, not a spread.** Being watched does not make you worse at lifting a beam. A condition that dusted a penalty over every reach would be weather, not a mechanism — and would draw a factor line on steps it has no business touching.
- **It resolves up one position tier.** Both callers pass the actor's `located_at` target, which under the three-tier model is the *most specific* node they occupy — a Place as often as a Location, while conditions land on either tier. Reading only the exact node would have shipped a reader that fires sometimes and misses silently otherwise: the original defect one tier along. A Place now also collects its enclosing Location's conditions, which is the honest reading in game words too — in the tavern of a watched town, you are in a watched town. It stops there rather than resolving to the hex: a hex is terrain, and a neighbouring settlement's watch is not your problem.
- **Terms add, then clamp** at `LOCATION_CONDITION_STEP_MODIFIER_CAP` (0.10), unlike movement taxes, which multiply. A step is one roll against one threshold; a place may lean on it hard without being allowed to decide it by accumulating bad seasons.
- **Not `domainContributions`.** That bag is a node's own capability, walked by `computeRawScore` for the *actor*. A place has no capability to move (THR-1143); what it has is an effect on work done there. Keeping them separate is what lets a test assert the two are **disjoint** rather than hope so — which matters, because `conditionEffectLine` now reads both bags together.

The effect line then comes free: `conditionEffectLine` reads the new table as a third substrate, so both conditions now render a sentence **derived from the term that moves the roll**. `under_watch` reads *"Shadow slightly lower. Lasts about seven days."* The sentence cannot drift from the mechanism, which is the whole point.

`tended_shrine` also needed the `CONDITION_DURATIONS` row it shipped without — and that gap was worse than untidy. The fallback is not a default but **permanence**: `CONDITION_DEFAULT_DURATION_TICKS` is `0`, and `0` omits `ticksRemaining`, the only field `decayConditions` counts down. Every gift left at a shrine kept it tended forever. Now 144 ticks (12 game days), between `under_watch`'s seven and `plague_scare`'s fourteen: the noun is *lately*, so neglect has to show inside a season, and a shrine that stayed kept on one offering would make the second offering meaningless.

### `standing_welcome` — deleted

THR-1206 retired it on the director's reputation ruling and left the definition registered under the THR-1177/1183 read-tolerance pattern, so worlds saved before it could render their live edges in words rather than as a raw id. **That window has closed.** Three weeks with zero writers against a duration of 120 ticks (10 game days) — every edge minted under the old noun lapsed twenty times over, so the tolerance protected nothing while costing two separate sweeps a special case.

The zero-writers claim survives the trap the coordination block flagged, and it is worth recording: a naive grep for the bare word finds **14 hits** in `encounters/vertical-slice.ts` and every one is a comment or a **change id** (`slice.kin.a_standing_welcome`, `…_well`, `…_dearly`) that merely shares the noun. The trait id itself has none. An executor who greps the word reaches the opposite conclusion in about ten seconds.

Three fixtures were re-pointed at `tended_shrine`, which makes the same claims about a place-carried positive state, and the `vertical-slice` assertion that the gate does not name the retired trait got *stronger* — it now guards against naming a trait that does not exist at all.

### `CONDITION_IDS_WITHOUT_EFFECT` is now empty

The export stays, and it is not dead. An empty list is what makes the **first** direction of the gate cover every condition with no exceptions, and a future condition that genuinely ships ahead of its reader has a documented place to sit for exactly as long as that is true — which re-arms the falsification arm automatically.

## The factor-line trap, avoided deliberately

`deriveContributionLines` **silently drops** any contribution whose `kind` has no sentence pair in `DERIVED_FACTOR_SENTENCES`. A new `ModifierSourceKind` without its pair therefore produces exactly the defect the test panel exists to prevent: a number that changes the roll while no line says why. The `aura` entry's own comment warns about this in as many words. So `condition` was added to both the kind union and the sentence table in the same change, with a test arm that fails if the pair goes missing.

### Guards, and which ones bite

Eleven new arms in `locationConditions.test.ts` plus four in `conditionEffectLine.test.ts`, each written to fail on absence rather than to pass on presence:

- **The reach-filter arm is the falsification of "mechanism, not mood":** a watched place returns a Shadow contribution and returns **nothing** for Iron and Heart.
- **The with/without pair** — the same place carrying no condition tilts nothing — because a lone absolute number can be produced by an unrelated location term.
- **Both polarities**, so the substrate is shown to report *direction* and not merely presence.
- **The tier arm was falsified explicitly.** Reverting `tierIds` to `[locationId]` reds exactly 1 of 32, naming *"a step taken in a Place feels the enclosing Location's condition"*. This is the arm that matters most in a live world.
- **The sideways arm** proves the walk resolves *up*, not *across*: a sibling Place's tended shrine stays its own, so "read the parent too" could not have been implemented as "read everything nearby".
- **The disjointness arm** pins the invariant the merged read depends on, rather than trusting "by construction".
- **The clamp arm** compares against the uncapped sum (six watchers would be −0.36), so it measures the clamp rather than restating the constant.
- **Two population pins moved deliberately, in opposite directions**: the corpus floor 15 → 14, and the condition-polarity sweep 62 → 61. A retirement now has to be *stated* in the sweep that counts conditions, not absorbed silently — which is the same discipline the additions had.

Three existing arms inverted, because their premise was the defect: two in `AttachmentDetailView.test.ts` asserted `under_watch` renders no effect line, and they now assert the sentence its reader earns. The **absence** arm was kept alive rather than deleted with its last shipped example — it is driven by an unshipped id now, because the branch it guards (no substrate → no sentence) is still live code and still the thing that keeps a future condition from being given an invented line.
