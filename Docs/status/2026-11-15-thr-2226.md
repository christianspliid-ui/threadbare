- **The Ordeal is a roll the god leans on (Law 10).** The nine Ordeal choices are now leans: "Pour everything into the thread" (+0.30, 5), "Steady the thread" / "Share the burden" (+0.12, 2 / 3), "Step back: this must be {theirs}" (0). None names an outcome. A legacy choice's `ordealOutcome` is ignored.
