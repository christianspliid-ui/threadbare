# 2026-09-29 — THR-1662: "lead", "clue" and "delve" enter the Ubiquitous Language

**Ticket:** [THR-1662](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1662) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** docs-only (UL-proposal). Engine, Content and UI: N/A. The words already ship; this seats them.

## What was wrong

The rulebook (§10) and the agent sheet ("knows where it lies") put **lead** and **delve** in front of the player, but no UL shard defined **clue**, **lead** or **delve**. The intent judge flagged the gap while reviewing the THR-1636 plan, which makes *hear → survey → visit → delve* a climb the player can watch.

## What changed

- `Docs/ubiquitous-language/Encounters.md`: three entries, seated by delegation 2026-09-29. **Lead** is the player word. It is a `knows_clue_of` edge with three rungs: *vague* (has heard of it), *narrowed* (has a lead on it), *located* (knows where it lies). A survey sharpens it, and it goes stale by precision-scaled age. **Clue** is the code word, used only in code and traces. **Delve** is admitted by `phaseDelveAdmission` for a located-lead holder on the ruin's hex. "Goes cold on a missed visit" is recorded as THR-1664's and not yet shipped.
- `Docs/ubiquitous-language/README.md`: three index lines.
- `Docs/canon/rulebook.md` §10 *Watching earns something*: *observe → clue → delve* becomes *survey → lead → delve*, and the paragraph gains the three rungs and the THR-1663 sharpening. THR-1664 will rewrite this paragraph again when the visit ships.
