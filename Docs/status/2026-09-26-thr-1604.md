# 2026-09-26 — THR-1604: five small interface faults from the cold playtest

**Ticket:** [THR-1604](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1604) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience (Cold playtest · round 1) · **Plan:** none (self-scoped bug) · **Pillars:** UI only. Engine N/A (no simulation change). Content N/A (the only new words are the Settings confirm).

## What shipped

1. **Cast no longer slides.** The drawer's "Choose a card." hint stays mounted and goes `visibility: hidden` once a card is armed. The centred footer row keeps its width, so Cast stays under the cursor.
2. **Enter submits the name.** `OriginBeat`'s name field calls `handleContinue` on Enter (ignored mid-IME composition).
3. **"Rivals" / "Notables" words open their panels.** Each label toggles its dropdown. Found while verifying: **every** shared `Dropdown` panel opened off-screen, one viewport below the page. The enter animation ends on `transform: translateY(0)` with `forwards` fill, and a transform makes its element the containing block for a `position: fixed` child. The panel was placed relative to a wrapper at the foot of `<body>` (y ≈ 1146 at 1920×1080). The fixed position now sits on a wrapper outside the animated element. This was the likelier reason testers called the panel broken: even the icon opened it invisibly.
4. **Chapter Ledger badge agrees with its list.** New `countThreadedChapters` counts what the ledger's default view lists: threaded chapters, archived and in progress. It no longer counts every archived encounter in the world.
5. **Return to title.** Settings has a new "Game" section. The first press asks "Return to the title screen? This world will be lost." with *Return to title* / *Stay*. Confirming calls `onExitToTitle` (App → GameView → top bar → panel). App strips the dev query flags and sets phase `start`, so the next New World starts fresh.

Tests: `coldPlaytestUiFaults.test.tsx` (8), `OriginBeat.enter.test.tsx` (2), one new arm in `ActionDrawer.test.tsx`. The Dropdown placement test fails with the fix reverted.

Not reproduced: "map hexes drawn on top of pop-up cards". Dropdowns (z 9999) and the Settings panel (z 50) render above the canvas at 1920×1080. The off-screen dropdown above is the closest confirmed fault.
