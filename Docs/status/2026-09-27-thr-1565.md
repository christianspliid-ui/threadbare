# 2026-09-27 — THR-1565: three seeds that told the wrong story are retired

**Ticket:** [THR-1565](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1565) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** none (bug ticket; found by the THR-1526 design research) · **Pillars:** Content (three templates). Engine N/A (no engine change). UI N/A (`Browser-verify exempt: content only`).

## What was wrong

Three `encounter_seed` effects planted a sequel whose opening was false for the parent that planted it:

1. **The Healer at the Ward-Gate** seeded the Grateful Kin. The Kin's opening says she is kin to *the family from the fen road*, which is the Swindled Family. From the Healer it thanked the mortal for something they never did.
2. **The Swindled Family** seeded *itself* from its decline ending, its haunted `critical_failure` band and its fallback. The label promised news of the family. The scene replayed the first meeting.
3. **`encounter.shrine_offering`** planted a seed with no `templateId`, no `query` and no `encounterFamily`. `evaluateEncounterSeeds` threw it away every time it matured.

## What shipped

All three seeds are **retired**. No authored sequel is true for a Thornwall household, for "news of the family", or for a heading from the stones, so there was nothing to re-point them at.

- **Healer:** the seed is gone, and so is the `child_recovers` `future_hook` chip it backed. Without a seed, that chip promised a return nothing brings. The child's fever breaking stays in the overview, which is prose. `HEALER_GRATITUDE_DELAY_TICKS` is removed.
- **Swindled Family:** all three self-seeds are removed, along with `SLICE_FAMILY_NEWS_DELAY_TICKS`. The 'East Is Theirs' chip's noun changes from `seed` to `knowledge` (`ui.knowledge`). The `intelligence` record in the same reaction backs it, the way 'Two Roads' on the fallback already worked. The family still seeds the Swindler Found and the Grateful Kin from the endings where the mortal helped.
- **Shrine offering:** the empty seed and the chip it backed ('A Heading') are removed from `critical_success`. The heading stays in that band's overview.

## Evidence

- New `src/data/__tests__/seed-target-honesty.test.ts` (5 tests). No seed in the corpus lacks a template, a query and a family. `shrine_offering` plants no seed. The Swindled Family never seeds itself. The Grateful Kin's only parent is the Swindled Family, whose family its opening names. A non-vacuity guard confirms the Healer is in the walked corpus.
- The Healer's own test adds the same pin and drops its assertions on the retired chip.
- `check:encounter`: `encounter.shrine_offering` and `encounter.slice.swindled_family` are clean.

## Found on the way

- `healer.quest.wandering_healer_shrine_access` fails the full Composition Contract, and did before this change. It has no `settings`, no outcome bands and no nudge-bearing step, and it registers at no location subtype. This change removes one of those failures (the unanchored `child_recovers` chip). The rest would take a full re-author, which is out of scope here.
- A "news of the family" sequel for the Swindled Family's decline path is still worth writing someday. It would put back the "out of sight is not out of the world" beat the self-seed was trying to deliver.
