# THR-1485 — the content-object registry: one place that says what an author may write

**Shipped 2026-09-12.** Slice 1 of five for [THR-1481](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1481) (content model). **No behaviour change** — nothing in the engine reads the registry yet. This slice is a vocabulary plus its guards, and the guards are the deliverable: the corpus is now pinned against the catalogue, so the next four slices build on something that fails by name rather than drifting quietly.

## Why a second registry

`world-objects.ts` says what the engine **mints**. Nothing said what an author **writes**. That asymmetry is why content references content by literal id — and literal ids rot: 67 of 115 reveal families matched zero templates before [THR-844](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-844) aliased them; undertaking `catalystEncounterIds` spell `encounter_` where the corpus spells `encounter.`, so none can resolve. THR-1481's fix is to name content by *kind and tags*; that needs a place that says what the kinds are. This is it, and `instantiatesAs` is the one-way join to its sibling.

## What changed

**Engine/data — the registry (`src/data/content-objects.ts`) and its loader (`contentCatalogs.ts`).** Twelve kinds, one row each: game word, UL term, id prefixes, catalogs, required tag axes (empty until slice 2), projections, the world object a granted entry becomes, the machine gate, `owningSystem` verbatim from the subsystems registry, status and a note recording the decision. The loader is the other half — the static imports that turn `{module, export}` strings into entries, because esbuild cannot build an import from a string and a dynamic `import()` would make the registry unreadable to the generator. **Both lists are pinned against each other in both directions**, so neither can grow an orphan.

**The plan's "one prefix, one row" rule does not survive the corpus, and the honest version is better.** `anomaly_spore_*` names an item, a power **and** a condition; `anomaly_crystal_*` names two. The ids were never built to discriminate. So: the **catalog** decides where the prefix cannot, the **prefix** decides where the catalog is pooled, and *one of the two must always decide* — which is now guard B, and it is strictly stronger than the rule it replaces, because it also catches the case where neither decides. The three genuinely shared prefixes (`reward_`, `starter_`, `anomaly_`) are declared with reasons; an **undeclared** collision fails.

That reframing paid immediately. The first version forbade catalog sharing outright, which made the Action row under-count its own kind by **141 entries** — `510 + 46 ≠ 697`. Under the corrected guard both rows read the pooled array and the prefix sets partition it: `510 + 187 = 697`, pinned by a test that fails on an id claimed by *both* or by *neither*.

**Three guards caught real errors in my own work on their first run, which is the argument for writing them before believing the registry:**

1. **Items declare `sphereAffinity` and no catalog entry carries it** — 0 of 134. The plan expected to project the item sphere tag from that field and retype it in slice 2; there is nothing to retype. Recorded as a seam: items' sphere tags stay authored until the field has bearers, the verdict [THR-477](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-477) reached for their reach.
2. **An omen's sphere is nested and conditional** (`sphereTrigger.sphere`, 6 of 44 tracks), not the flat field a projection reads. A projection firing for a seventh of a kind reads as "no tags authored".
3. **The encounter row pointed at a *retired* UL term.** `Encounters.md#encountertemplate` resolves — to a retirement notice telling the reader the format no longer exists. The plan's harness rule ("the registry's `ulTerm` must resolve") became guard E, and enforcing it is what surfaced this; repointed to `#encounter`.

**The generator (`scripts/generate-content-objects.ts`).** Renders `Docs/canon/content-objects.generated.md` and the served `public/content-objects-reference.html`; `--check` under `check:generated-freshness`. Fatal on an **UNCLAIMED** id or an **EMPTY** kind; **ungated kinds are counted, not fatal** — 10 of 12 today, and closing that is what the later slices are judged by. Unlike its world-object sibling it boots **no world** (~1s vs ~40s): content is authored, never minted, so the census is a static read — which is why it can afford to be blocking. Falsified unpiped: exit **1** on a broken prefix, exit **0** restored.

**Content/docs.** New canon page `Docs/canon/content-objects.md` (79 lines) with the one-PR rule, the known seams and four rejected approaches; index rows in `Docs/canon/README.md` and the CLAUDE.md canon table. UL: **Content Object**, **Content Tag**, **Content Query** seated in `Encounters.md` beside `EncounterTemplate`/`UnifiedActionTemplate` (2026-09-11 blanket delegation on UL seating) — **plus `Omen`**, which no shard had at all and which guard E forced rather than let me point a row at nothing.

**Inspection.** CLI `content [kind]` (sibling of `objects`) and `await window.__DEBUG.getContentObjects()`. Both read the catalogs and not the world, so the numbers are identical on every seed and tick — stated in the JSDoc and the CLI's own header, because a stable number otherwise reads as a stale read.

**Harness.** Systems-inventory `aliases` on the Encounters and Attachments rows gain `content object`, `content tag`, `content query`, `tag vocabulary` — the search keys, deliberately not `activityKeywords`, which are measured against runtime activity and would have flipped badges. Interface-map contract `content-objects-registry` added and regenerated (127 contracts).

## Deviations from the plan, and why

- **`catalog: {module, exports}` → `catalogs: [{module, export}]`.** Items come from four modules, conditions from three; a single-module field does not survive the corpus. The *how* of an agreed design (CLAUDE.md § User review interface, rule 4).
- **The prefix-ownership rule**, above — replaced with a strictly stronger claim that is true.
- **Two projections dropped** and recorded as seams rather than authored against fields with no bearers.
