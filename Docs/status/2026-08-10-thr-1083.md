# THR-1083 — the aftermath is inside the prose gate

**Shipped 2026-08-10.** `collectClassedTemplateProse` — the single walk every prose
detector reads through — now covers `aftermathConfig`. The last screen the player
reads was outside every gate we own: **~59k authored words across 295 templates,
36% of the corpus, never once scored.**

The proof was one shipped line. `encounter.slice.unsafe_bridge` authored *"The
bridge spent something on this crossing that it will not get back."* — `something`
is the first term in `EVASIVE_VAGUENESS_TERMS` — and it passed THR-973's
re-authoring, THR-883's format lock, and production, into the director's review of
the vertical slice, with every gate reporting clean.

## What changed

- **The walk** reaches variant prose, `changes[].title`/`.detail`, `reactionPrompt`,
  reaction labels and intents, and every `byOutcome` band. Appended last, so a
  template without an aftermath scores byte-identically and prior WS5 batch
  evidence stays comparable.
- **The vertical slice** lost its four evasive terms (`something` ×2, `somehow`,
  `thing`). No other slice prose was touched.
- **Field classes**: `detail` → `outcome`, `overview` → `scene`, labels →
  `interactive`.
- **11 assertions** in `src/data/__tests__/aftermath-prose-coverage.test.ts`,
  falsified against the un-widened walk (5 of 8 core tests fail without it).

## One instruction deliberately not followed

The ticket specified `overview` as `outcome`. It shipped as `scene`, after
measuring. The `outcome` lexicon adds the natural indefinites, justified on the
ground that after the roll the player *has no other source* for the consequence.
An `overview` sits directly above the change chips, which name every consequence
explicitly — so the player does have another source. Read strictly it flags **165
fields on indefinites against 57 genuinely evasive**, and in the slice every one of
those was prose like "Nothing was promised. Nothing was taken." — the contortion
THR-899 split the lexicon to end. Evasive terms are still enforced at zero there.

`detail` stayed strict, and that is what makes "spent something" catchable.

## The finding that matters more than the fix

**Widening coverage made the gate weaker.** Vagueness is a density, so +59k words
grew the denominator and three templates crossed the threshold downward with their
defects completely untouched:

| id | words | hits | density | verdict |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| `cg.social.citizen_petition` | 218 → 312 | 5 → **5** | 2.29 → 1.92 | FAIL → pass |
| `social.rob` | 275 → 367 | 3 → **3** | 2.18 → 1.63 | FAIL → pass |
| `tavern.the_warning` | 317 → 422 | 6 → **6** | 2.52 → 1.90 | FAIL → pass |

Population `vagueness` failures read 21 → 18, which looks like progress and is the
opposite. These are THR-1067's population, and its body tells its executor to
re-derive rather than trust its filed list — so the honest procedure would have
returned 15 and dropped three real defects.

They are pinned in `DILUTED_BUT_STILL_DEFECTIVE` as *defect* assertions: they pass
while the defect exists, fail when someone fixes the prose, and the list may only
shrink. THR-1067 carries the table. Logged as impediment #532; the general problem
(a whole-template denominator cannot tell "prose improved" from "more prose
arrived") is deliberately not ticketed, per the 2026-08-10 process-work throttle.
