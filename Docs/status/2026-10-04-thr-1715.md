### 2026-10-04 — THR-1715: The First asks — her story waits on her god; her chores stay out of the Ledger

**What changed for the player.** The First is born asking. Her story chapters stop the world and open the encounter veil, from the first step. After one ends she gets a two-day **story breath** of ordinary life before the next can begin. Her chores (authored `threatRating: 'trivial'`, now **Daily life**) never notify, never fill the Chapter Ledger's default view or badge, and sit behind a *Daily life* chip. The Threads row's toggle reads **Asks you** / **Lives on**, repaints on click, reaches pause for The First at tier 1, is free, and shakes with a reason when it refuses.

**Engine.** `engine/attentionCadence.ts` (new): `isRoutineTemplate`, `resolveAttentionMode`, `storyBreathRemaining`, `recordStoryChapterEnd`. `toUnifiedTemplate` carries `routine`. The visibility phase skips routine, and a pause-mode thread's `shaping` step needs no tug. New filter stage `filterByStoryBreath` (3b). The breath anchor `lastStoryChapterEndTick` is written at the chapter-archive transition. `toggleAttentionMode` returns `{ok, reason}`. **Executor decision:** a new action's step 0 was never notified for *anyone* (the order is progress → visibility → agent decision), so a pause-only, dedup-keyed visibility pass runs at 2b.2. Auto-mode threads are unchanged.
