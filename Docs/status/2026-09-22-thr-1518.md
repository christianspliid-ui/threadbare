## What the row said

**UNREACHED, on every seed tried.** Seed 42 / 200 ticks: the Crossroads fired once and the mortal refused (positive `tradition_novelty`). Seeds 99 and 7: never fired. At 1000 ticks: the same single firing on 42, none on 99. The mechanics are proven live; a mortal choosing to plant one on the live board is not. That is exactly the THR-1497 shape, caught by the census before the flip instead of after it — so the two interface contracts **stay 🔴 LEAKED-with-ticket**, repointed from this issue to [THR-1524](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1524), which closes on a census HIT. Die B's floor is the remedy already in place: the next batch must author a second appointment-bearing encounter, and it should carry broad settings and a planting path not gated behind one pole of a value axis.

The plan's Done-when *"the two interface contracts are 🟢 with evidence"* is therefore **deliberately not met**. Flipping on live-proof evidence alone would be the wired-but-unreached pathology the row exists to prevent.
