# THR-1208 — 18 cast-fate chips stop wearing the reputation label

**Shipped 2026-08-24.** Deferred from THR-1206 (plan: `Docs/plans/2026-08-23-thr-1206-reputation-unification.md`, § The two content sweeps).

Eighteen aftermath chips across four nudge-era encounters (`road-ambush`, `soul-ferryman`, `the-brink-rescue`, `the-courtyard-duel`) declared `kind: 'reputation'` in files containing **zero** reputation-family effects — cast-fate outcomes wearing the reputation label, telling the player a system had moved that nothing had written. One even claimed standing in so many words (*"Her standing survives, shaken but intact"*) while no reputation mechanism changed.

**Both sanctioned resolutions were used, chip by chip.** Fifteen chips are cast fate and re-kinded to `shell_state` — alive, wounded, retreated, freed, diminished — with the handful of `Disposition: X` key-value sentences rewritten as plain prose and the courtyard duel's explicit standing claims trimmed to the formal-finding fiction that is actually true. Three road-ambush chips whose fiction genuinely **is** pairwise standing with a person (*"Disposition toward the god: positive and specific"*) went the other way: THR-1206 landed first, so they keep `kind: 'reputation'` and gained real `reputation_with` writes — Soraya's gratitude (+0.08) and Dragan's wary resentment (−0.05) on the shield branch, Soraya's wariness (−0.04) on the chaos branch, each duplicated across both reactions of its band because the feeling comes from the rescue itself, not from which thread the god keeps afterward. Named constants, all inside `REPUTATION_WITH_MAX_DELTA_PER_OUTCOME`.

**The predicate returns zero matches repo-wide.** A side effect worth naming: `check:encounter`'s systems-count had been crediting these files with a *reputation* connection off the mislabeled chips — soul-ferryman drops 2→1 systems and brink-rescue's under-quota becomes visible now that the phantom credit is gone. Pre-existing thinness, exposed rather than introduced; THR-1130's Composition Contract retrofit owns it.
