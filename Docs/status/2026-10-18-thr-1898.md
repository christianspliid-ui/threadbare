The map now has four lenses, one at a time, keys 1–4. Both new lenses are rows on Lenses I's list and generic layer; neither wrote layer code beyond three new draw kinds.

- **Trouble (3)** — `views/lenses/troubleLens.ts`. Lights a hex for **Unrest** (a Location carries the Lawless chip, "Unrest: Ferris is lawless.", or the hex survey's mood bucket is agitated or boiling), **Strife** (the conflict pulse, tense or volatile), battles and sieges (lit, no badge, their sprites and words already on the plain map), rival **schemes and drains** (the rival is named), undefeated **lairs** by tier, and a local **omen's** reach (a dotted ring). The legend head names the doom stage ("Trouble · Doom: Strange Whispers") with the doom bar's tooltip.
- **Trade (4)** — `views/lenses/tradeLens.ts`. Lights every hex on a known road's line (`hexLine`, ends included), draws the road in caravan gold or threat red with a mid-road badge, and tells the road words that used to sit on the plain tooltip as sentences, then each end's prosperity and scarce goods in words.
- **The plain map sheds** trade roads, the unrest pulse and rival rings. `HexPulseMesh` and `RivalInfluenceMesh` are deleted with their last caller (and the `hex_pulse` zoom-matrix row and render slots); route words left the plain hex tooltip.

- **`hexLine`** hoisted into `src/lib/hexMath.ts`; world-gen pass09 calls it, its output pinned unchanged.
- **Interface:** contracts `local-omen-on-trouble-lens`, `unrest-on-trouble-lens`, `prosperity-on-trade-lens`.
