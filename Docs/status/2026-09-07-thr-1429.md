# The dormant kinds I — powers and conditions (THR-1429)

**Shipped 2026-09-07.** Second plan doc off the wayfinder map [THR-1396](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1396), first half of the *dormant kinds* band. Three wanted cells woke two dormant seams, each with its reader in the same commit (the map's rule).

**A scholar learns a spell (`create × Power`).** The Power kind got the node shape the world-object registry deferred in its own note: a **trait node whose subcategory names its class** — `bestowed` for a god's gift, `spell` for one a mortal learned. One shared definition node per spell, minted at seeding from `SPELL_TEMPLATES`, per-bearer state on the edge (THR-1395). `knows_spell` is *known* — the biography, unlimited — and `has_trait` is *wielded*, capped by `SLOT_CAPS.spell`; both point at the same node, so learning past the cap leaves a spell known and not carried, which the sheet states rather than treating as a failure. First writer of `knows_spell`, whose schema row was retargeted from `action_template` (a guess made when spells were not nodes) to `trait`.

**A zealot blesses, a witch curses (`create × Condition`).** One **signed** cell, and the first in the game whose object is made against a person. The sign is read from the actor's relation to the target and is itself the gate: self or an ally is a blessing, un-gated; a mortal the actor holds a motive against is a curse with a band-scaled duration; a stranger is refused, with no neutral third outcome. It mints through the catalog's own `instantiateReward`, so every live reader — decay, the predicates, the walkers, the cure — reads an inflicted condition exactly as it reads an encounter reward. A curse registers the new `afflicted` harm class and reaches the grievance funnel; a blessing registers none.

**A rival's art is sealed (`destroy × Power`).** Mints the catalog's existing Null-Touched rather than authoring a duplicate suppressing condition — two edits, not a new entry. The power is *not* removed: it stays known and wielded and will not answer, which makes curing it the counter-play, free.

## What the substrate said, against what the plan assumed

Three corrections were made from measurement rather than from the plan, and each is recorded where the next session will look.

- **The caster roles the plan named do not exist.** `mage`, `hedge_witch` and `sage` match nothing in a seeded world. The eleven that do — `priest`, `healer`, `acolyte`, `monk`, `oracle`, `enchanter`, `warmage`, `alchemist`, `scholar`, `chaplain`, `herald` — supply 65 casters of 331 mortals on seed 42 small.
- **`domainCapabilities` is a raw 0–100 scale, not 0–1.** The plan's `0.35` Veil floor would have passed every mortal carrying the field while reading in a trace exactly like a working gate. It is `40`, and it is a deliberately thin limb: only 11 of 331 mortals carry the field at all, so the role limb is what supplies the population.
- **The tradition shelf would have been empty for the whole world.** At tick 30 on seed 42, **3 of 337** mortals carry any non-zero sphere score and **1** carries an alignment. Read literally, the shelf offers nothing to ~99% of mortals and the cell can never fire. The shape is kept — aligned mortals draw from their tradition first, sorted, first unknown — and `LEARN_SPELL_UNALIGNED_SHELF_OPEN` decides what an *unaligned* mortal sees. This is the plan's own kill criterion ("the constants move, not the shape"), reached at build time from a measurement instead of after two census seeds.

## Three defects found by building it

- **The cells were unreachable.** A cell listed in no ambition profile's `cells` gets zero ambition ids from `profiledAmbitionIdsFor`, so the board never walks it — and neither can the review lever, which bypasses the ambition-profile *gate* but still needs the ids. All three cells are now listed on the profiles whose meaning they match.
- **`destroy × Power` enumerated zero targets, always.** `POWER.ownedVia` was empty — correct while `use` was the only verb, since it is an `own` cell and never had to ask who holds a power. `destroy` is an `other` cell, so with no holder edge the ownership filter matched nobody and the motive gate found no owner to hold a motive against. `has_trait` is now the Power kind's holder edge.
- **Learning a spell renamed it for the whole world.** `christenCompletedWork` renames the first created node an op reports, and `learn_spell` reported the *shared* definition node: one priest's study renamed Crystal Gate to "The Standing Quarter of Morthane" for every mortal alive. Fixed twice over — the cell reports the **edge** it actually created, and christening now skips `trait` nodes outright, because a power and a condition are catalog content with authored names and christening is for works.

## The shared-node hazard, and why the seal is read off the bearer

`effectStates` is keyed by attachment **node id**, which is exact while every attachment is a per-bearer instance — and every one was, until the Power kind got a shape. A spell definition is now shared by every mortal who learned it, so a suppression flag written against that node id would say "this spell is sealed" for the whole world rather than for the one person somebody cursed. Two wielders of one spell is the common case, not the exotic one, so the leak would have fired early and read as the seal working.

The definition node therefore carries **no `effects` array** (so the suppression pass never flags it), and "is this mortal sealed?" is answered by `isSpellSuppressedFor` off the bearer's **own** conditions — through `collectAttachmentEffects`, the same walker the suppression pass uses, not a hand-rolled second resolution. A test pins the bystander case directly: cursing one wielder must not seal another.
