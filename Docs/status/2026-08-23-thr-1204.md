# THR-1204 — the ruthless agent stops being called patient

**Shipped 2026-08-23.** Three of the six `mercy_ruthlessness_negative` bodies in `FEAR_PROSE` rewritten, their `POLE_MANIFEST` entries re-read off the replacement text. Player-visible prose: `FEAR_PROSE` renders into the backstory strand.

## The mismatch

`mercy_ruthlessness_negative` resolves `{value}` to **"ruthlessness"** (`VALUE_NOUNS.mercy_ruthlessness[1]`) and carries the fear **"Fears becoming heartless"** (`FEAR_DESCRIPTIONS.mercy_ruthlessness[1]`). Bodies 1 and 3 carried that fear correctly — restraint exhausting into someone who cannot feel — but named the agent's disposition *"the patience"*, the mercy pole's word, in the same sentence that renders "ruthlessness":

> What Mira will never admit is that the **ruthlessness** they display contains within it the fear of what happens when it runs out — the moment when **the patience** finally exhausts itself in an unrecoverable way.

"it" is the ruthlessness; the next clause renames it "the patience".

**A class no existing check covers.** Not a pole swap (THR-1187) and not an off-axis body (THR-1202), so neither of those readings would have surfaced it — and `POLE_MANIFEST`, even widened to all 108 bodies by THR-1203, cannot: a fragment witnesses which pole a body sits *on*, not whether the body's own nouns agree with what `{value}` resolves to. It was found by the 108-body read THR-1203 performed, which is the only thing that would have found it.

## What shipped

Bodies 1 and 3 name the disposition with ruthlessness-side language while staying on the "becoming heartless" axis. Composed with `{name}` = Mira:

- **Body 1** — *"…the ruthlessness they display has never once been stopped by what was in front of it. The fear is of the day something arrives that should stop it and nothing does."* Keeps the original's unrecoverable-moment shape; the moment is now the discovery of heartlessness rather than the exhaustion of patience.
- **Body 3** — *"…covers is not simply anger but a fear of what it is wearing down. Each hard call comes easier than the last, and Mira has noticed."* Erosion of the capacity to be moved, which is what this pole's fear names.

**Body 2 was rewritten too, taking the ticket's open judgement call.** It read *"a fear of their own anger … that it is larger than they have admitted"* — a *restrained* agent's fear, near-synonymous with `mercy_ruthlessness_positive` body 2 (*"beneath the restraint is something that, once released, would not stop"*), which is why THR-1203 classified it `witness: 'edit'`: two bodies saying the same thing on opposite poles cannot witness a swap between them. Leaving a body that reads as the opposite pole's fear on this key is the same defect this ticket exists to fix, one degree softer. It now reads *"a fear that has nothing to do with anger. Anger would at least be feeling, and what Mira watches for is the absence of it"* — which only the ruthlessness pole fears, so its `witness` flips `'edit'` → `'pole'` and the pinned pole-agnostic count drops **11 → 10**.

Bodies 4–6 were already coherent and are untouched. Body 4 works precisely because it makes the calm an exterior over something else.
