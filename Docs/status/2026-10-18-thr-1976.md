# THR-1976 — a chapter's Play button answers, once

**Shipped 2026-10-18.** Cold playtest round 4: on *The Page Beneath the Saint* the Play button took the choice and nothing moved. The veil was wired to `handleEncounterIntervene`, which charges, records and resolves but never closes the veil; the open veil kept holding the clock, so the step could not resolve until Escape. `handleEncounterCommitAndContinue` (the THR-1812 handler with the answering card) had no callers. `StageHost` now hands the veil that handler, which claims the step once (`claimAuthoredChoiceCommit`, the THR-1730 committed set, released if the pool rejects the spend), then shows the answering card on a paused clock or closes onto the aftermath on a running one. A second press charges nothing and cannot overwrite the recorded choice. The answering line no longer ends ".." on a sentence-shaped choice label.

Browser (bash-vite, 1920×1080, `?spawn=veil.truth.page_beneath_saint`): the free option plus Play shows the answering card with no Escape (`getChapterAnswer().state === 'answering_card'`); a paid option pressed twice logs one intervention and takes essence 38 → 36.
