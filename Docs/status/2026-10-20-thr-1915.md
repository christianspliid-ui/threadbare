# THR-1915 — Find anything (Inspector S7)

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** Ctrl+K (or `/` when no input has focus) puts the cursor in the Find field, which now fills the top bar's centre slot. The box drops under the bar (`--find-box-w`: 560 px at 1080p, 630 px at 1440p, capped at 60vh). It never dims the map and never pauses time.

**What a result does.** A world result flies the camera to its hex and opens through the router (`via: 'find'`) in the Inspector: a person or place docked, a power wide. It replaces a pinned subject, and focus lands on the page heading. A card result arms the card (`origin: 'find'`). A Codex result opens the Codex at that entry. The eye on a row flies without opening. Esc closes the box first, above the Inspector.

**The seen floor.** `createSeenFloor` (`src/engine/views/knowledge.ts`) builds THR-1822 § D5's table. A place is nameable when its hex is visible or remembered. A person must be known at `recognised`, threaded or in sight. A power needs a nameable member or a held place. An army must be in sight. An artifact must be held by the god's people or lie at a nameable place. `buildFindIndex` (`src/engine/views/find.ts`, `getView('findIndex')`) lists nothing else. So "Corran" (unseen in the seeded world) and "qxjvwk" both read "Nothing you know of by that name." A remembered place's fact is "Last seen … ago". One branch is not read: "in a holder's revealed possessions", because no store records which possessions the god has seen. Leaving it out can only hide a thing, never leak one.

**Ranking.** Whole name, then word start, then inside a word; then yours; then recent (Inspector history); then name. Accents and case fold. Six rows per group, then "Show all N". An empty box lists Recent and Your people.

**Evidence.** 16 Node tests in `views/__tests__/find.test.ts` cover the leak, ranking, accent and empty-box rules. Playwright on a worktree Vite, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440. "Shutash" is a plan-fixture name with no counterpart in the seeded world, so the Enter flow ran on Kael Thornweaver (docked, heading focused) and The Thieves Guild (wide).
