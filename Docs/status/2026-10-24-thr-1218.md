# THR-1218 S1 — Encounter firing rhythm: a followed mortal rarely tells the same story twice

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** Slice S1 of `Docs/plans/2026-10-16-thr-1218-encounter-firing-rhythm.md` (D4, D5, D7, D8). The restless road (D6) is S2, [THR-1959](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1959).

**What changed.**
- **One paused-mortal predicate** (`isPausedThreadActor`, `attentionCadence.ts`): an inbound thread, not dormant, in `pause` mode. The story breath now reads through it, with no change in behaviour.
- **Repeat floor for every paused mortal (D4).** `PAUSED_STORY_REPEAT_FLOOR_TICKS` (60). The old `FIRST_CHAPTER_REPEAT_FLOOR_TICKS` stays as a deprecated alias. `phaseAgentDecision` now passes the floor for any paused mortal, not only the bonded First.
- **Tellings cap (D5).** `applyStoryTellingsCap` runs after retirement and before cooldown. It drops a paused mortal's story candidates that she has already attempted `PAUSED_STORY_MAX_TELLINGS` (2) times. Chores are never dropped. The cap relaxes once her drought reaches `STORY_DROUGHT_RELAX_TICKS`. The drought is ticks since `lastStoryChapterStartTick`, a new thread-edge prop written when she starts a story chapter. Each drop emits trace `encounter.tellings_capped`. Grey zone resolved: `attemptCount` is incremented at selection, so failed tellings count.
- **Lever hygiene (D7).** The curator weights are named `CURATOR_W_*` in `attention-constants.ts`, with values unchanged. `STORY_BEAT_COOLDOWN`'s comment now says the cooldown is global. `STORY_BEAT_QUEUE_OVERFLOW_DEMOTE` is read at the `phaseAttention` demote site. `ATTEND_COOLDOWN` is marked unused.
- **Census (D8).** `npm run census:rhythm` (`scripts/firing-rhythm-census.ts`). The debug accessor `__DEBUG.getAttentionCadence` gains `storyDroughtTicks`, `storyTellings`, `tellingsCapRelaxed`, `maxTellings`, `lastStoryChapterStartTick` and `lastRestlessRoadTick` (null until S2).

**Tuning by the kill criterion.** The plan set `STORY_DROUGHT_RELAX_TICKS` = 144. The design-time baseline at `617802fb` (18 / 10 / 13 own-draw story chapters on seeds 42 / 7 / 1234) no longer describes main. The same census run on `origin/main` `cf6fb7de`, without S1, gives **6 / 19 / 12**, so the script is re-anchored to those numbers. With the relax at 144, seed 7 fell 19 → 11. The plan's kill criterion says "lower STORY_DROUGHT_RELAX_TICKS first". 96 and 72 both measured **10 / 18 / 12**, every predicate PASS. 96 (eight days) ships because it keeps the relax after S2's 72-tick restless road. Without the cap, main breaches the two-tellings bound on seeds 7 (`inheritance_wake` ×4) and 1234 (`barter_with_travelers` ×4).

**The aftermath-only split is a harness artifact.** It is not a defect. Every aftermath-only story chapter on all three seeds had a **compulsion pending for The First** on some tick while it was live (census `blockers while live`: seed 42 compulsion ×4 of 4, seed 7 ×12 of 12, seed 1234 ×11 of 11). Most halted chapters had none. `phaseEncounterVisibility` skips an agent with a pending compulsion (`encounterVisibility.ts:628`) because the compulsion modal is that moment's interrupt. Headless, nobody answers the compulsion, so it sits for its whole `eligibleUntilTick` window, and a 2–3-tick chapter resolves under it. In the browser the compulsion modal halts the clock, so the chapter cannot resolve before the player answers. The design-time guess (a veil nobody dismisses) does not fit the data: `other-veil-open` mostly appears on the *halted* side.

**Evidence.** `npm run census:rhythm` on the final code, seeds 42 / 7 / 1234 × 600 ticks, medium: own-draw story chapters 10 / 18 / 12, no story told more than twice outside a relaxed window, family repeats 20.0% / 10.5% / 7.1% (tripwire > 20%). The census measures the Done-when's "storyTellings ≤ 2" check headlessly. The browser half rests on that and on the accessor unit test (`firingRhythm.test.ts`). `npm run gate`, code track: test, typecheck (ratchet 2691), build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt and the 30-tick CLI smoke all PASS. `test:heavy` runs on CI after merge.
