# THR-1421 — one companion, one description, wherever the player meets them

**2026-09-07 · UI · Bug · Encounter Experience**

`AttachmentsTab` rendered a companion's always-on bonus as `+2 stone · +1 eye` — a raw magnitude on a player-facing surface (**Law 13**) in raw internal reach keys (**Law 14**). `AscendantSheet` rendered the same companion as `Steadies your Stone and Eye.` The defect was not really either line: it was that one companion had two descriptions depending on which surface you opened.

## What shipped

**The fix is a shared function, not a copied one.** `companionReachLine` moved out of `AscendantSheet` into `src/components/Game/companionWords.ts`, and both surfaces now call it. Copying the treatment across would have satisfied the Done-when and ended the divergence for exactly as long as the two copies stayed in step — which is the state the ticket existed to end, re-created with a longer fuse. Sharing makes parity structural: a future change to the wording reaches both surfaces or neither.

The old `contributionLine` is gone rather than deprecated. It had one caller.

**The row had a second numeral, and it was in scope.** The Done-when reads *"no numeral reaches the companions row"* — and `⏳ 4 ticks remaining` sat three lines below the contribution line. A tick is an engine unit with no player-facing display name anywhere in the game, so it fails Law 14 as squarely as the integer fails Law 13. This did **not** need a ruling: `durationLabel` (`src/engine/aftermathWords.ts:226`) is the sanctioned reading, already ships, converts by the game's own `TICKS_PER_DAY`, and **spells its count out** — so the row now reads `⏳ one day remaining` and the `not.toMatch(/\d/)` arm covers the whole row rather than only its permanent variant. `durationLabel`'s own docstring names this exact violation, which is what made the call an implementation choice rather than a Law 15 magnitude ruling.

The five sibling surfaces still rendering raw tick counts — `AgentInfoCard`, `OmenDetail`, `ProwessTab`, `ReadTheThreadsPanel`, and a *different row* of `AttachmentsTab` itself — are filed as [THR-1423](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1423) with its coordination block, not swept into this diff. `AgentInfoCard` additionally renders a percentage, which Law 13 bans outright with no sanctioned numeric alternative; that half is flagged there as a Law 15 ruling to be split out, not decided in a bug fix.

## What the ticket asked for and did not get

Nothing. All three Done-whens are met. The third — *"the mortal row and the ascendant row describe the same companion the same way"* — is met structurally rather than by assertion, which is stronger than what was asked.

## The verification worth recording

**The first falsification of the new guard was a false pass, and finding that out is the useful part.** The `not.toMatch(/[+-]?\d/)` arm exists to make the fix falsifiable rather than eyeballed, so it was falsified: a numeral was deliberately leaked back into `companionReachLine` and the tests re-run. Three unit tests went red — and **the component test stayed green**. The mutation had hit the single-Reach branch, and the component fixture carries two Reaches, so the renderer was demonstrably broken while its guard reported success. Re-running the mutation against the two-Reach branch reddened the component test as designed.

Both guards are now confirmed to bite on the branch their own fixture exercises. A guard falsified in the wrong branch is not a falsified guard — it is a second thing to believe.

The unit sweep over the sanctioned contribution range (`COMPANION_CONTRIBUTION_RANGE` 1–3 × all eight reaches) exists for the same reason: a numeral leaking at one magnitude but not another is exactly the shape one fixture misses.
