# 2026-10-16 — THR-1932: the crown — oaths of fealty and tax, how a faction wins them, keeps them and loses them

**Ticket:** [THR-1932](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1932). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-15-thr-1932-crown.md`. Engine, Content and UI pillars.

Realms can now win other factions' fealty, tax them, and lose them. A realm holding two or more oaths is a **Crown**. Before this there was no relationship of fealty in the game at all.

- **Engine.** One new edge, `sworn_to` (vassal → liege realm; `graph.ts` + `edgeSchema.ts`), written only by `src/engine/crown.ts`. A seventh faction action, `seek_fealty` (`can_court` prerequisite, weight 6, cooldown 12), coerces a faction whose town the realm besieged for two days or took within five days, else persuades or buys (a gift from its purse) the richest candidate whose offer reaches 0.85, else cools the best refuser. A daily inline `crown` phase right after `faction_purse` moves loyalty (six reasons, on the reputation ladder), breaks an oath at Distrusted (a `relates_to` grudge and a 20-day cooldown; a big realm breaking away rebels with `hostile_to`), then moves 0.3 of each vassal's day income to its liege after it paid its own soldiers, never into debt. Conquest stamps `takenFrom` on the moved `controls` edge. `crown/crownView.ts` derives the Crown and exports `protectedHoldingsOf` for war aims (THR-1933). `CROWN_ENABLED` is the rollback.
- **Content.** `src/data/crown-constants.ts` (every number and the never-swear lists). Five chronicle triggers in `crown-chronicle-content.ts`: `crown_formed`, `crown_lost`, `rebellion` (world-wide), `oath_sworn` (line by how it was won) and `oath_broken` (line by the reason), the last two inside attention only.
- **UI.** Faction sheet: a Crown chip and line under Leadership ("Waisai wears the crown; two factions owe it fealty."), a Fealty section (a vassal's liege, how the oath was won and its standing word, "one step from breaking" at Unknown; a liege's vassals folding past eight), a Purse sentence ("It pays a share of all it earns to …"). The map draws a crown outline around a Crown's and its sworn realms' land; the Held-by line adds "which owes fealty to …, a Crown". Debug: Economy-tab Crowns and Oaths tables, `__DEBUG.getOaths` / `getCrown` / `swearFealty`.

**Measured (`npm run measure:crown`, seeds 42/99/7, 1080 ticks):** a Crown forms on every seed (first on days 4, 8, 9); oath income is 15–34% of each Crown's full income at day 60; 1 of 8 coerced oaths broke and 0 of 3 persuaded; 35–69% of swear-eligible factions stay independent at day 90; no one-layer violation on any tick; no negative wealth. **Missed:** the courtship rate is 68–73% of days against a 70% floor; the 12-tick cooldown on an 8-tick action cadence caps it at 75%. Filed as [THR-1961](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1961). Breaks are rare (lieges stay rich on the live purse), so "force breaks first" passes on one break.
