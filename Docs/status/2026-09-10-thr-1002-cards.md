# THR-1002 — one card face, and a cast that tells the truth about its odds

**2026-09-10 · `tb-opus-pickup` · worktree `clever-euler-8f76e2` · [THR-1002](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1002)**

The second and final pass. The foundation slice (PR #1865) extracted `shared/CardFace` from the nudge card and shipped the readout the action card would consume; this pass rewrote the action card onto that face, replaced the drawer around it, authored the content the face prints, and retired what the face replaced.

## The shape of it

Christian's directive of 2026-08-06 was that *"players would expect the same type of syntax and rough layout and language for all 'cards' in the game"*. There is now **one face**: `shared/CardFace`, rendered by both cards, with each kind of card reduced to a function from its own data into that face's model — `NudgeCard` for a nudge, `actionCardModel(slot)` for a cast. The two cannot drift, because there is nothing left to drift.

The action card had been two cards — a 160×110 art tile in the fan and a 400×560 MTG-style frame at screen centre — sharing no zones, so the card you chose from was never the card you read. Everything that told you what an action *did* lived in the overlay. Both are gone. The face is complete, so nothing expands: the drawer arms a card on click and fires it from one **Cast** button in the footer (Law 48), which is the nudge stage's own *Let fate decide* shape.

## The defect this pass actually found

The card's odds word is classified from the probability the roll will use. The foundation slice pinned that probability against `computeResolutionThreshold`, described in the test as *"the resolver's own single source of truth for P"*. It is not the *last* word on P, and two divergences had shipped green underneath that pin:

- `stepResolutionCore` lifts a below-floor probability to the **scale** floor outright. Measured on a fresh god (capability 0.354), the card said 0.354 → `perilous` where the roll gives 0.65 → `favorable` — on every `local` and `personal` cast in the game, which is most of what a young god can reach.
- `resolveUncontestedStep` returns `probability: 1` for a difficulty-0 step from an early return *above* every scale adjustment. So cosmic's `+0.10` offset never touches an unpriced step, every divine verb is `fated` at every scale, and impediment #996's note to the contrary was wrong — it reasoned about `applyScaleDifficultyAdjust` in isolation and never checked whether the resolver reaches it. That row now carries the correction.

Both are the exact class of lie THR-998 existed to remove, and both were invisible to the sweep because the readout and its pin agreed with each other about the wrong layer. The pins now run through `resolveUncontestedStep` driven for real — the only side of that comparison that cannot be wrong about what the resolver does. Logged as impediment **#998**.

## What the face carries now

Every word comes from a table, and no numeral reaches it (Law 13). The verb chip reads the declared `crudType` through `ACTION_VERB_WORDS` — a *sustained* template files under **Control** whatever it changes, because to the player that is a different kind of card. The scale chip replaced `{n} hex`. The upkeep channel replaced `0.5 force/tick`. The framed pips replaced the numeral cost badge *and* the `" Costs 3 essence."` suffix every effect line used to carry. The `IRON · CREATE` type line — recovered by splitting the slot id on dots — is gone with the string surgery that built it.

A dimmed card's reason is now guarded on **shape** rather than phrasing: three of the four `lockedReason` producers interpolate a number, so `actionBlockedReason()` maps the known cases to words and replaces any remaining reason containing a digit with a vaguer true sentence. A future producer inventing a new numeral phrasing costs the player a little precision; it cannot re-open Law 13.

**32 effect lines were authored** — every id `collectGrantedActionIds()` returns and every actor-targeted agent-hand template. Before this they fell through to the composed fallback, which prints one sentence for every `update`-on-agent card in the game: eleven cards, no way to tell Embolden from Intimidate on the face. `actionEffectsProse.test.ts` pins both predicates from their own sources, so the gap cannot silently reopen.

## Two calibration calls

- **The plan's 14-word cap on effect lines was dropped**, not implemented. Measured against the shipped corpus, 36 authored lines run 15–21 words while every one sits inside the existing 140-character cap — so it was a guardrail the content could not meet, picked without measuring, and answering the same question as a cap that already exists. What constrains the line is the card's fixed width, which characters measure directly and words only proxy for.
- **`playerCastBalance.test.ts` was re-pointed, not deleted.** Its purpose was *the card's word is not decorative* — a spread across the corpus, no word dominant. The new invariant is *the card's word equals the roll's tier*, which is strictly stronger: a spread can be healthy while every individual card lies. The spread assertion is kept beneath it as the anti-degenerate guard.
