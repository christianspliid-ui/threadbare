# 2026-09-28 — THR-1633 S1: a fair shortlist, shipped dark

**Ticket:** [THR-1633](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1633) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1633-written-encounters-land.md` § S1 · **Pillars:** Engine; Content N/A (no templates or prose); UI N/A (evidence is headless, THR-688 rule C).

## What was wrong

The 40-slot shortlist cap filled its free slots by walking the candidate list from index 0. The list arrives in cache insertion order, so a template registered late was never looked at. On `main` before this change, 76 · 60 templates per seed (42 · 99) had the cap as their first gate.

## What shipped

- `capWithDiversity`'s Phase 2 fill can now (a) take one entry per template before any repeats and (b) start at `hashString(agentId + ':' + tick) mod n` and wrap. Both sit behind `CAP_FILL_DISTINCT_FIRST` and `CAP_FILL_ROTATE`. The hash is pure, so no PRNG stream moves. Reserve phases 1a–1d are untouched.
- The `encounter_filter` trace carries `capCutTemplates`: distinct templates entering the cap minus distinct templates leaving it. A positional cut now shows up as a count.
- Interface row `shortlist-reaches-every-template` is registered as 🟠 PARTIAL, deferred to THR-1633.

## Why the rotation ships OFF

Measured with `readers/reach.ts 42,99 200` (medium), one arm per switch setting on the same tree:

| Arm | Cap-first-gate templates (42 · 99) | Drawable fired | Total firings (42 + 99) | `selected_not_spawned` (42 · 99) | Top-10 share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Old fill (both off) | 76 · 60 | 117 | 872 + 626 = 1,498 | 15 · 19 | 0.258 |
| Distinct-first only | 76 · 60 | 117 | 1,498 (byte-identical) | 15 · 19 | 0.258 |
| Rotate only | 7 · 11 | 109 | 564 | 55 · 59 | 0.270 |
| Both (plan) | 7 · 5 | 99 | 515 | 54 · 73 | 0.282 |
| Per-agent offset (no tick) | 4 · 8 | 100 | 475 | 61 · 64 | 0.303 |
| 25-tick offset | 17 · 3 | 102 | 529 | 64 · 63 | 0.285 |

Rotation does what it was designed to do: the cap stops being a first gate. But it cuts total firings by about two-thirds. The wider shortlist hands mortals encounters at other places, which they choose and then lose on the way (`selected_not_spawned` 34 → 127). That is the reroute defect S2 fixes ([THR-1639](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1639)). The plan's fallback, distinct-first alone, measured as a no-op, so it would not have met the gate either.

So the mechanism ships dark. The distinct-first switch stays on, since it is harmless and byte-identical. `CAP_FILL_ROTATE = false` keeps today's behaviour exactly, which is the clean baseline S2 needs. **Next:** after THR-1639 lands, flip `CAP_FILL_ROTATE`, re-run `reach.ts`, and close THR-1633 against its original gate.
