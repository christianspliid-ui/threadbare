### 2026-10-02 — THR-1294: `requiresLocation` is authored, not defaulted

**The flag now lives where a content author looks.** Undertaking checkpoints used to read `template.requiresLocation ?? UNDERTAKING_DEFAULT_REQUIRES_LOCATION`. The constant said `false`, the type comment said "absent ⇒ `true`", and two comments said the binder had not shipped (it shipped 2026-08-27). The constant is deleted. The gate reads `template?.requiresLocation === true`, so an absent flag stays `false` (fail-soft, same as before).

**Authored everywhere:** 28 hand-written multi-tick templates across the builder, court, merchant, scholar, warlord and zealot packs now say `requiresLocation: false`. The synthesised undertaking cells author it in `synthesiseCell`, and factory overrides inherit it through `applyCellOverride`.

**Pinned:** `undertakingRequiresLocation.test.ts` walks the live registries (packs plus cells) and fails on any multi-tick template that leaves the flag out. Falsified by deleting the flag from `strategic_found_shrine`: the test names it.

**Behaviour-neutral, measured:** the 150-tick census (`npm run census:undertakings`, seeds 42 and 99) is byte-identical to `origin/main`. Rolled share is 100% (97/97) on seed 42 and 98.4% (182/185) on seed 99. The census's start-volume and variety gates fail on `main` as well; they are not about this flag.

**Still not built:** nothing moves an agent toward its undertaking's stage. Every shipped value is `false` until a design wants a stage mover.
