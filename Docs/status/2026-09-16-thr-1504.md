# THR-1504 — the breadcrumb's crumbs become controls the detail panel's focus trap can reach

*Deferral from [THR-1024](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1024), filed under the
browser-verify contract's rule that a pre-existing UI-Law violation is filed with its law
number rather than fixed in passing. THR-1024 gave the detail page a real Law 50 focus
trap; the one control that trap could not reach was the breadcrumb.*

## The gap

`DetailBreadcrumb` rendered every navigable crumb as a bare `<span onClick>` — no
`role="button"`, no `tabIndex`, no keyboard handler. Law 23 says an interactive `<div>`
carries `role="button"` plus keyboard handlers, and a click-only span is the same defect
in a different tag.

It mattered more after THR-1024 than before it, because the trap is built on
`FOCUSABLE_SELECTOR`, whose last clause is `[tabindex]:not([tabindex="-1"])`. A crumb with
no `tabindex` at all is outside the cycle *by construction*: a keyboard player could enter
a stacked detail page and Tab between close, back and the footer CTA, but never reach the
control that jumps back several levels at once. At depth four or five the trail collapses
to `…` plus the last three crumbs and becomes the primary way back; `←` still worked, one
level per press, so nothing was unreachable — only slow, and only for keyboard players.

## What shipped

**`DetailBreadcrumb.tsx`** — a crumb the `clickable` branch renders now carries
`role="button"`, `tabIndex={0}`, the sanctioned `.focus-ring` class (Law 23: the ring shows
on `:focus-visible` and is never suppressed) and a key handler that answers **Enter and
Space** — both are promised by the role, and Space is also page-scroll by default, so it is
prevented once the crumb claims it. The `tabIndex` is what puts the crumb inside
`FOCUSABLE_SELECTOR`, so the Law 50 trap cycles through it with no change to the trap.

**Both directions hold**, as the Done-when demanded. The last crumb and the collapsed `…`
gain nothing — no role, no tab stop, no class, no handler — so the trail is no noisier to
Tab through than it is to use (Law 25: a control that does nothing does not render as a
control). The affordances are spread from one conditional object, the same shape
`HooksBlock`'s chips use, so a crumb is either fully a control or fully inert and cannot
be half of one.

## The guard fails on the old shape, by construction

`DetailBreadcrumb.test.tsx` (new, 12 arms) renders the real component and asserts: a
navigable crumb carries the role and a zero tab stop, takes focus, matches
`FOCUSABLE_SELECTOR`, activates on Enter and on Space (with the default prevented) with the
right stack index, ignores other keys, still clicks, and wears `.focus-ring` with no
inline outline. Against it: the last crumb, the `…` crumb and a one-crumb trail carry no
role and no tab stop and fire nothing on click, Enter or Space; over the collapse threshold
exactly the visible non-last crumbs are controls.

`DetailModal.test.tsx` gains the arm that ties this to THR-1024's contract: inside the
topmost `detail-panel-N` the two navigable crumbs are in the trap's focusable set and come
**first** in document order; Shift+Tab from the first crumb wraps to the footer button and
Tab from that button wraps back to the crumb; Enter on a crumb pops the stack to that level;
the last crumb is not a stop.

**Falsified explicitly:** with the component reverted to its committed shape and the new
test kept, exactly 7 of 12 arms go red — and the 5 that stay green are the inert-direction
arms plus plain click, which is the pre-existing behaviour and should not move. Two existing
Law 50 wrap arms had collected `button` elements only and now collect through
`FOCUSABLE_SELECTOR` instead, because the trap's "first" is the `ENCOUNTER` crumb and not
the close button any more. Five snapshots updated; the diff was read and is purely
additive — `class`, `role` and `tabindex` on the navigable crumbs, nothing removed.
