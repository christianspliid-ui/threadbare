# 2026-10-09 — THR-1788: The First's story moments name their stage in words

**Ticket:** [THR-1788](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1788) · **Found by:** warm playtest round 1, where testers quoted "Beat 1 — Call" as evidence that the onboarding was replaying

- **The footer names the stage.** The journey vignette footer (`src/components/Game/JourneyVignetteModal.tsx`) now reads "The Call", "The Road of Trials", "The Crisis", "The Ordeal" or "The Return". The words come from the new `JOURNEY_STAGE_LABELS` map in `src/data/ui-content.ts`, keyed by `CampbellianPhase` (Law 17). The mortal's name stays on the right.
- **No beat index on the player surface.** The engine's 0-based `beatIndex` no longer shows to the player (Law 13). The debug panel keeps it. The word "chapter" is avoided on purpose, because in the glossary a Chapter is an encounter's record.
- **Evidence.** `JourneyVignetteStageFooter.test.tsx` renders the real modal. It checks that the footer reads exactly "The Call" for `beatIndex: 0, phase: 'call'`, with no digit and no "Beat". It checks that a later beat index never surfaces, and that every stage carries its article. This is the jsdom-render substitution, used because the unattended run could not start a dev server.
