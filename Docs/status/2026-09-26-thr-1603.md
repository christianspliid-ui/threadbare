# 2026-09-26 — THR-1603: a cast names its card and its target

**Ticket:** [THR-1603](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1603) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience (Cold playtest · round 1) · **Plan:** none (self-scoped bug) · **Pillars:** UI (the dispatch-time toast and its chronicle line, built in `useAgentInteraction`). Content: no template prose changed; the authored `initiation` predicates are reused, now given a subject. Engine N/A.

## What was wrong

Casting Oneiric Sending on the First read *"Mind — Action Invoked. reaches into the sleeping mind"*: the sphere as the title, a predicate with no subject, no card name and no target. Cards with no sphere read *"Action — Action Invoked."* The chronicle got the same string.

## What shipped

- New pure builder `src/components/Game/hooks/castReceipt.ts` (`buildCastReceipt`). The title is the card's player-facing name (`spellName ?? name`, Law 14). The body is a full sentence with the player as subject and the target named: *"Oneiric Sending. You set it upon Kael Thornweaver: it reaches into the sleeping mind."* A self-cast reads "yourself". A template with no initiation falls back to its success line, then to a stock line, and the target is still named.
- `useAgentInteraction` uses it for both the toast and the chronicle entry. The sphere stays as the toast's tint.
- `castReceipt.test.ts` (5 tests) covers a Mind card, a sphere-less card, the fallbacks and the self-cast.

Browser check (`?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, 1920×1080): the toast and the latest `recentEvents` entry carry the same sentence.

What this does not fix: the player still does not see what changed afterwards. That is the design ticket filed alongside this one in the same milestone.
