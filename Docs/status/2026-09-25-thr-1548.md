## What shipped

- **`fighterEndingBranch`** (new, `src/engine/fights/fightEnding.ts`), first in `FIGHT_END_BRANCHES`, records a `FightEndingRecord` on `fightState.ending` for every fight and emits one `fight.ending` trace:
  - `yielded` to a mortal → **humiliation** (`reputation_with` −`FIGHT_HUMILIATION_REPUTATION` toward the residence settlement) + drift toward prudence; `routed` → the drift; `yielded` to a beast or no opponent, `broke_off` → nothing.
  - `struck_down` → guards first (The First via the `thread` edge's `courtPosition`, any `avatar_of`), then — monster victors only — one draw at `FIGHT_KILL_CHANCE_BY_TEMPER`. **Slain** goes through `markMortalDead` (`cause 'fight'`, retained, ward honoured) and writes a `named_death` outcome node in the plot's shape. **Mauled** writes `trait.scar.scarred` through `applyConditionToActor` (once per mortal) and a `blood_drawn` grudge, upgrading `old_quarrel`.
  - Victories record their face only; the yields are D2 (THR-1549).
- `createUndertakingOutcomeNode` takes an optional `source: { kind: 'fight', … }` in place of a project; node `evt_und_fight_<actionId>_<tick>`, `source: 'fight'`.
- `blood_drawn` joins `GrudgeCause`, `INJURY_CAUSES`, `GRUDGE_PROVENANCE` and `GRUDGE_CAUSE_CLAUSES`.
- UL: **Struck down** and **Scarred** seated by delegation 2026-09-25; the `scar` contract names fight endings; Grudge's provenance list brought up to the gate's seven. The Narrative Event sentence the plan asked to correct was already fixed by THR-1564.
- Both calibration harnesses reset the fighter's death, scar, grudges and drift between fights (the `blood_drawn` grudge otherwise lent every later fight the Old-wound advantage).
