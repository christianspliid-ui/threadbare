# THR-1219 — the slice prose rewritten to the 2026-08-15 standard

**Date:** 2026-08-24 · **Ticket:** [THR-1219](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1219/rewrite-the-slice-prose-to-the-2026-08-15-standard-direct-pass) · **Project:** Encounter Experience

Christian decoupled the slice prose rewrite from the Encounter Factory retrofit on 2026-08-24 so the
integrated checkpoint session does not wait for the factory to be approved and built. This is that
direct pass over `src/data/encounters/vertical-slice.ts` — a register pass only, judged against the
three plainness moves (`Docs/canon/prose.md`) and calibrated against the 10 hand-edits recorded in
[THR-974](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-974/consequence-verdict-session-christian-rules-on-world-graph-consequence).

## What the pickup found, and how it narrowed the job

The ticket's predicate is *every prose field in any template whose prose fails the standard at
pickup* — a predicate, not a count, so the first task was measuring which templates actually fail
rather than rewriting all 788 authored strings on principle. Two findings moved the scope, and both
are worth recording because a later pass would otherwise re-derive them:

- **Seven of the nine templates had already been through the factory line** (`content(thr-1131):
  Retrofit Batch 1 — the slice six through the factory line`). `retrofitPending.ts` still lists
  exactly two: `encounter.slice.riders_behind_caravan` and `encounter.slice.snow_on_the_pass` — and
  those two are precisely the two the detector sweep flagged. The remaining seven needed a read
  against the three moves, not a rewrite.
- **`fiction` is not drift, it is the locked format.** The 61 aphoristic card lines ("What holds,
  holds", "Rot cannot argue with daylight") read as exactly the "cut the aphorisms" target until
  the THR-883 spec is checked: *Flavor quote · `fiction` · one short generic line, the card's only
  prose*. The aphoristic register **is** the specified form for a card face, and Christian's ruling
  was aimed at the aftermath endings and chips, not at card flavour. Rewriting them would have been
  a format change wearing a register fix's clothes. They were left alone except where one tripped a
  detector on other grounds.

## The measurement

The gate the Done-when names is `auditTemplate` in `nudgeAuditDetectors.ts`, run over all nine
slice templates before and after. Baseline vs. shipped:

| | vagueness (o/s/i) | not-X-but-Y | divine | 2nd person | intensifiers | **failures** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Before** | 3 / 1 / 1 | 1 | 0 | 7 | 3 | **1** |
| **After** | 0 / 0 / 0 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 0 | **0** |

**The baseline carried a live gating failure nobody had surfaced:** `riders_behind_caravan` tripped
`second person x3 on mortal-drawn (>= 2)`. All three hits were one line of quoted dialogue — the
caravan master addressing the traveler as "you" — which is character voice and not the defect the
rule exists to catch (narration addressing the mortal). Rather than argue the detector, the step
narrative was given a subject-first frame and the master's line reworded to third person, which is
the better prose independently: it no longer opens on a bare quote.

The two surviving second-person hits are single quoted lines ("The moon will find you", "You kept
it"), each below the 2-hit threshold and each correct character voice. They are left as written.

## What changed

**The two un-retrofitted templates**, in full — `snow_on_the_pass` and `riders_behind_caravan`:
fallback and per-band overviews, chip `causeClause`/`detail`, reaction `intent`, and the band
fragments carrying maxims. Representative: *"the mountain keeps the toll it took"* → *"Whatever the
climb took out of them, they are not getting it back on the way down"* (abstract noun doing
concrete work); *"What they wanted, they took. What stood between them and it got moved."* →
*"They took what they came for and put down what stood in the way."* (fronted-object inversions,
move 1); *"Everyone on a long road carries one item above their station"* → *"Half the column owned
one item too good for them"* (a general maxim narrating a specific event).

**The seven retrofitted templates**, where the factory's critic left drift the three moves catch:
stacked ironic closers (*"They came off it in the end. Less of them came off than went up."*),
aphoristic tags (*"which was its own quiet answer about the man"*, *"A lockup is slower than anger,
and it holds"*), and abstract nouns standing in for events (*"the family's faith in strangers
thinned with the light"* → *"by dusk the family had stopped asking the traveler for directions"*).

One fix is Christian's own sentence: the Grateful Kin chip read *"It opens less wide than it was
going to"*, which trips the not-X-but-Y detector. His calibration #9 is *"It's a narrower door than
it was going to be"* — the same beat, phrased so the gate does not fire. Matching him cleared the
last structural hit.

## What was deliberately not done

**`retrofitPending.ts` is unchanged.** Removing the two slice entries would claim a factory retrofit
that did not happen: THR-1130 retrofits to the full **Composition Contract**, which is broader than
prose register, and THR-1219 explicitly supersedes the "rides the retrofit" line *for the slice
prose only* while leaving the factory to retrofit the wider corpus. Their prose is now at standard;
their composition-contract status is THR-1130's to close.

**Two of my own edits were reverted by a gate and one by judgment.** `checkNudgeHand` caps carryover
factor lines at 12 words; two rewrites came in at 13 and turned the suite red. One was a real
move-1 fix (a participial opener, *"Caught short of the saddle, they make camp…"*) and was reworded
inside the cap. The other — de-eliding *"the wood dry, the storm outside"* into full clauses — was
over-reach: the terse elliptical form is the field's word budget, not drift, and it was put back.
