### 2026-10-02 — THR-964: the choice-commit pipeline is retired

**Nothing ever wrote to it.** `pendingChoiceCommits` had readers and clearers but no producer. So `phaseChoiceResolution` returned early every tick, and the detection phase looped over zero commits. The authored-choice layer it served was replaced by the nudge model. That makes it a rejected mechanic (`Docs/canon/encounters.md`, THR-772), not an unwired one, so it was deleted rather than wired.

**Deleted together:**
- **Engine:** the phase, `choiceResolution.ts` and `itemConsumption.ts`.
- **Types:** the `PendingChoiceCommit` type and its GameState field.
- **Constants:** `CHOICE_PROBABILITY_TILT_*`, `CHOICE_DRIFT_MAGNITUDE_*` and `CHOICE_ESSENCE_COST_*`.
- **Helpers:** `resolveChoiceDrift` / `heuristicDriftMagnitude`.
- **Traces:** `choice_resolved` and `item_consumed_by_choice`.
- **Debug UI:** the debug-only Choices tab.
- **Unmounted UI:** the never-mounted `SceneStatePanel` cluster.
- **Tests:** the tests that asserted all of the above.

**Kept and extracted:** the threshold-crossing and rival-seed block is now `recordDetectionCrossings` in `phaseDetectionPressure.ts`, with its own unit tests. `phaseDetectionPressure` itself now only decays. Nudge cards are the live pressure writer, but they do not call the helper yet, so nudge pressure never crosses a band. THR-1690 wires that.
