**The factory now rolls for its premise as well as its ending, and the numbered hook catalog the ticket asked me to fold in turned out not to exist.** THR-1145 fixed the *back* of an encounter — what happens to someone when it ends — by rolling a consequence hand instead of offering a menu. THR-1147 is the same correction applied to the *front*: asked to invent a premise under a deadline an author writes the nearest one, and a corpus grown that way is all roadside trouble and shrine errands. `npm run draw:hooks -- <briefSlug> --reach <reach>` now offers three story seeds from the project's own inspiration corpus, and the author takes one or blends two.

**The catalog is 66 hooks, well past the ticket's ≥40 bar, and every one is sourced rather than invented.** They come from the three places the ticket named: the Obsidian `Archetypes/` pages (Adventure & Quest, Event, Ordeal — the curated Malazan/Mystara-derived corpus, 61 hooks), the four recoverable numbered seeds, and the one encounter draft that never shipped (`pick-pocket-skill-test.md`). Each carries a one-line premise compressed to the situation and its fork, a closed-vocabulary theme tag set, reach affinities, and a `source` string that is the way back to the long form — the vault page stays the essay, the catalog is what an author needs at brief time.

**The numbered `Hook #NNN` list is gone, and the Done-when anticipated that.** `vertical-slice.ts` cites `#204`–`#207`, so a numbered catalog existed in the THR-883 session. Searched 2026-08-17 across all three sources the ticket named: `Hook #` appears in the repo only in the slice's own headers, in the THR-1078 records that removed those citations from player-facing prose, and in the plan text commissioning this ticket; the vault holds no numbered list under `Archetypes/` or `Brainstorms/`; no `Docs/plans/` doc carries one. So the four knowable numbers are preserved on their hooks and **nothing was invented for the rest** — a fabricated #1–#200 would be worse than the absence, because it would read as recovered provenance. The reasoning is recorded in the module as `PLOT_HOOK_NUMBERING_NOTE` and printed by the CLI, so the next person to look does not repeat the search.

**The one hard difference from the consequence draw is that this hook is not binding, and the design follows from that.** The consequence hand is a claim `check:encounter` audits: it recomputes the hand from the template id and fails a family nothing wires. A premise has no such handle — there is nothing about a finished encounter a machine could honestly compare to a starting point, and creative drift is what a starting point is *for*. So this ticket deliberately ships **no template field and no gate rule**, which is also why `Engine N/A` in the ticket held: the only new code is authoring-time content-eval, and nothing under `src/engine/**` imports it. What *is* mandatory is that the brief record `plotHookRolled` / `plotHookTaken`, because that record is the only trace the hook leaves and it is what makes coverage measurable across batches.

**Reuse damping is the variety guarantee, and it is wired end to end rather than declared.** Each hook carries `usedBy`; weight decays by `PLOT_HOOK_REUSE_DAMPING` (0.45) per recorded use, so a well-used hook keeps appearing but rarely and the long tail gets its turn. Decay rather than exclusion, because a good hook is worth a second encounter under a different reach — habit is the thing being corrected, not repetition. The four recovered hooks ship already stamped against their slice encounters, which is what makes the damping demonstrably live instead of a field nothing reads: the CLI prints `weight 0.45 · used 1×` for `hook.unsafe_crossing` against `6.00` for an unused affinity hook. Affinity flavors without restricting — 6 in a tagged reach, 1 elsewhere — the same non-zero-floor law the consequence matrix is pinned to, so a siege can still surface in a `heart` brief and the interesting question becomes what a siege is about read through `heart`.

**Two of the twenty-two tests measure rather than assert a shape, because the two things most likely to be silently dead here are distributional.** A reach affinity that does not shift what gets drawn and a damping that decays a number nothing reads would both pass every structural check while the table behaved as a uniform shuffle — which is the whole failure this ticket exists to prevent. So the suite samples 400 seeds per reach and pins that a tagged hook draws >2× more often in its own reach than off it, that a used hook draws less than a comparable unused one, that >75% of the catalog surfaces across 400 briefs (a table circling its top-weighted handful fails here and passes everything else), and that the eight reaches do not all share a top hook. Thresholds are written as **literals, not re-derived from the constants under test** — a test computing its expectation from the same constant passes for any value of it. **All three guards were falsified before being trusted**: `PLOT_HOOK_AFFINITY_WEIGHT` 6→1 reds 3 tests, `PLOT_HOOK_REUSE_DAMPING` 0.45→1 reds 2, and pointing a `usedBy` at a non-existent template id reds the rot guard — the THR-844 class one level down, where a back-reference to a template nobody can open would damp a hook forever on the strength of nothing.

**Wired at three surfaces, not one.** The authoring spec gains § The Plot-Hook Draw ahead of § The Consequence Draw (hook first, ending second — the order an author works in); the pipeline SKILL gains Step 0a ahead of Step 0b, with the roll explicitly upstream of the brief because a brief written first has nothing left to roll for; and the batch report renders a **Plot hooks taken** table with a theme spread, read from the brief file it already receives via `--brief` — the only place the roll exists, which is precisely why recording it there is not optional. The report also surfaces a `usedBy` count above 0 as a prompt to stamp, since nothing can stamp it automatically. Verified end to end against a two-hook fixture brief: the section renders, resolves both hooks, tallies themes, and flags an unknown id rather than silently dropping it.
