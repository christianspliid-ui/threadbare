## Found along the way

- **A reputation chip about a person names the town in its tag on board-drawn encounters.** Filed as [THR-1685](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1685). On a board draw `{target}` is the settlement, so `reputation with {target}` anchored on `$cast:<key>` reads as the town. This affects batch 1, this batch and older content. The fix belongs in the renderer, once for the corpus.
- **Twelve chips with no concepts (Law 2)** in four of the six, caught by the pinned live sweep and fixed before the commit.
- Impediments #1113 (the live proof's failure-band false negative, second recurrence), #1114 (the dry-run compile misses the gates `check:encounter` runs) and #1115 (no `$companion` sentinel; relocation chips borrow the `seed` noun).

## Decisions made in-lane (veto welcome)

- **Slots.** The ticket named Stone, Iron, Shadow and Veil plus two flex. The live gauge showed only Iron and Shadow under the floor, so the flex slots went to them.
- **The appointment sequels are short seed-only templates outside the factory catalog**, as the hunt's `hunt.trail_cold` is: one step each, no hand, with `town.` ids so `check:encounter --all` does not hold them to the full contract. They exist to tell a promise already made. Making them full factory encounters would double the batch.
- **Parallel drafting.** The six were drafted in parallel, not in sequence, with the brief's variance table as the guard against six copies of one hand. No two share a card-type composition.
