# THR-1967 — A trade run pays, and the economic ambitions can be met by their own work

**Shipped 2026-10-21.** Undertakings in the economy, slice S3 (plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-17-thr-1937-undertakings-in-the-economy.md` § Engine 5–7).

- **A trade run pays.** `use × route` pays the trader 2 Means, plus 1 per unit the lane carried yesterday, up to 6. It pays once per trader per day, through `bankWealth` with reason `trade_success`. The build found that conduct trade never worked through the cell: the executor picked the lane from where the trader *stood*, so every run on seed 42 failed with "no route at anchor". The cell now trades on the lane its Route names (`conductTradeOnLane`, the THR-1883 shape).
- **Four ambitions are met by their own undertakings.** Dominate Regional Trade adds *holds two lanes* and *Means Wealthy* and completes on 3 of 5. Found Something New asks for a town the mortal founded, Rebuild from Ashes for any town held, and Protect the Home for walls in the home town. The estate, camp and castle milestones no undertaking could make are gone.
- **A regional monopoly meets a faction's `resource_acquisition`.** The faction then turns to `defensive_consolidation`.
- **The guild founding cost row** (`create×faction`, 30) is in the cost table; costs stay off.

**Kill line (`npm run measure:founding-starvation`, 600 ticks).** Foundings with costs off / on: seed 42 5 / 0, seed 99 4 / 2, seed 7 5 / 1. The floor (half) fails on 42 and 7. Trade pay is not the cause: it paid one run (2 Means) across all six runs. Costs alone starve founding, as THR-1865 found. Costs stay off (the comfort-pace gate reads 514 ticks against 120). The switch's own problem is filed as THR-2006.
