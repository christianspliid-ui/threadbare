# 2026-09-28 — THR-1609: who am I

**Ticket:** [THR-1609](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1609) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1605-the-opening.md` § S6 · **Pillars:** UI (hex-map avatar marker, hex tooltip, avatar sheet header); Content (one Beat 0 line, the framing copy); Engine: N/A (the avatar stays an `avatar_of` edge; no new node type, no rename field).

## What was wrong

All three round-1 cold testers mistook the avatar for a mortal to follow. It carries the past-life name the player typed in the remembrance, and nothing on screen said the figure was the player's own shape. One tester listed three names that might each be them.

## What shipped

- **Avatar marker.** The avatar keeps its sphere-tint ring and pulse, and now also carries the god's sigil: a sphere-tint disc at its lower right holding the primary sphere's glyph (`drawAvatarSigilBadge`). It starts god-marked before its portrait loads, and stays god-marked if the portrait fails (`buildAvatarSigilDotTexture`). A mortal's marker never carries the badge. The badge's size, offset and ink are named constants in `agentSpriteTypes.ts`.
- **Hover.** The hex tooltip on the avatar's hex leads with *"You walk here as {name}."* next to a sphere-tint ring.
- **Sheet header.** The avatar's sheet reads *"{name} — your mortal shape"*, with the god's title under it: *"You are Vara, the Witness"*. The divine name comes first, then the archetype title, or the hunger when the title only repeats the name. The sheet shows the origin portrait rather than a silhouette, and it drops the "stranger" knowledge badge, because the player's own shape is never a stranger to them.
- **Beat 0.** "Reach Down" now opens with *"You walk the world again as {name} — your own shape, not the soul you seek."* Spine prose gains one narrow placeholder, `SPINE_AVATAR_NAME_TOKEN`, filled by `fillSpineAvatarName`. The raw token never renders.
- Copy lives in `src/data/avatar-framing.ts`.
