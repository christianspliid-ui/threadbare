# 2026-10-12 — THR-1836: the picker vignette stays above the fold

**Ticket:** [THR-1836](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1836), filed by THR-1815's browser verify. UI only. No rule or content changed.

- **Cause.** In `SensingBeat.tsx` the vignette block sits inside the portrait button, anchored by its bottom edge 12vh below the portrait's foot, and grows upward. Its bottom edge is pure geometry: `50vh + (portraitHeight/2 + 12vh) × FOCUS_SCALE`. At 1920×1080 with scale 1.1 that is 1085.9px, which matches the browser reading of 1085.88.
- **Fix.** `FOCUS_SCALE` goes from 1.1 to 1.05, so the bottom edge is now 1061.1px. Lowering the scale was chosen over shrinking the 12vh drop. Everything inside the button scales together, so the name line keeps its place against the portrait's foot (12.1px above it, the same relation as before). A shorter drop would have pushed the name further into the portrait art. The magic numbers are now named constants (`VIGNETTE_DROP_VH`, `PORTRAIT_MAX_WIDTH_PX`, `PORTRAIT_WIDTH_VW`), and `focusedVignetteBottomPx(w, h)` states the geometry.
- **Evidence.**
  - Bash-vite on port 5307 plus the in-app browser at 1920×1080, on `?view=game&firstunmet&size=medium` after "Reach Down", with `getMeetingState().beat === 'sensing'`. Each candidate was focused and measured after its transition settled. Solenne, Beatrix and Liora (all four-line vignettes) each ended at a block bottom of 1061.1 (was 1085.9). The name line sat 12.1px above the portrait foot. `scrollHeight` was 1080 and the console had 0 errors.
  - New test `sensingVignetteFoldThr1836.test.ts` pins the bottom edge at or below 1068 at 1920×1080. It also checks that the geometry model reproduces the old measured overflow.
  - `npm run gate` passed on test, typecheck, build and encounter.
- **UI Laws on the composed picker.** 1 holds (one focused subject). 13/14 hold (same type tokens). 17 holds (the name stays tied to its portrait). 21 holds (the transition is unchanged). 33 is now satisfied (nothing below the fold at 1920×1080). 37 holds (the vignette is the only prose block).
