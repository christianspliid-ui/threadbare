# 2026-09-25 — THR-1545: the named-beast hunt fights the lair's own monster (Monsters M2)

**Ticket:** [THR-1545](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1545) · **Project:** Physical Conflict · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-23-monsters-as-opponents.md` (slice M2) · **Pillars:** Engine (cast binding, liveness filter, draw gate, `{target:family}`) · Content (the hunt rewrite, the family opening) · UI N/A (plan doc 4 owns the monster's surface; `Browser-verify exempt: engine + content only`).

## What was wrong

After M1, every lair's beast carried a fighting card, but no scene could reach it. *The Named Beast* hunt bound no cast and fought three flat difficulty rolls, so the card was never read. The hunt was also offered at any lair, including one whose beast was dead or had never been minted. Separately, every actor cast spec could bind a **dead** body: a retained death keeps its `located_at` edge, so a band casualty could be cast as a scene's guard.

## What shipped

- **`matchProperty` on actor cast specs** (`src/types/encounter.ts`, `src/engine/encounterSupportBundle.ts`). It binds the living actor at the placement whose property matches, and it is checked before role matching. It never materializes, and it always takes the legacy route, even on a `useScoredBinder` template.
- **The dead are never cast.** Every actor spec's candidates now exclude a deceased node (`isAgentGone`), and the role branches skip monsters.
- **`requiresLiveMonster`** (`src/types/unifiedAction.ts`) is gated on both draw paths: `filterByPrerequisites` (which also carries the Adventurers' Guild's quest entries) and `generateUnifiedCandidates`. The check is `liveLairMonsterAt` (new, `src/engine/monsters/liveMonster.ts`): the lair itself, or a place inside it, must be a lair whose `namedEliteId` names a living monster.
- **The hunt rewrite** (`MONSTER_HUNT_NAMED_ELITE`, `src/data/monster-encounter-content.ts`):
  - a tracking step, then a fight block against the cast `beast`;
  - the aftermath is keyed on `fight:<result>`, with the existing reactions kept;
  - the return seed carries `inheritContext: true` and is planted only by results that leave the beast alive, plus the no-fight fallback. `fight:overcome` plants nothing;
  - the old step reward pools are gone, because a fight step carries none.
- **`{target:family}`** (`src/engine/proseEnrichment.ts`) resolves to a monster target's family card line and strips for any other target. The new `{?target_has_family}` conditional removes the whole sentence for a non-monster. *The Beast in Its Den* now opens with the family line.
- **Docs:**
  - the wiring guide (`matchProperty`, `requiresLiveMonster`, `{target:family}`);
  - the new rulebook §7 paragraph (`[IMPL]`) and its quick-reference line;
  - the wiring checklist;
  - three wiki pages;
  - the interface contract `lair-monster-gates-the-hunt`, plus the death-funnel contract's two new read sites.
