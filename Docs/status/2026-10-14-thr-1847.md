# 2026-10-14 — THR-1847: a siege ring and a battle sprite say what they are

**Ticket:** [THR-1847](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1847), deferred from THR-1846. UI + Content (two registry words); Engine N/A (the engine already writes `battleType`, `attackerArmyId` and `defenderArmyId`).

- **What the player sees.** Hovering a besieged hex now says, in words, *"Under siege by sovereignty of Stone-Set Hollow"*. A field-battle hex says *"Battle between X and Y"*. The lead word is in the colour of its map marker (the orange ring or the red swords), so the word reads as the marker's name.
- **How.** `ui.siege` and `ui.battle` are registered in `ui-content.ts`. Both resolve through `resolveTooltip` (Law 17). `battleIndicatorForNode` now carries the attacker's faction name, and on a field battle the defender's, read through `getFactionMembershipEdges` the same way the army sprite reads them. The new pure module `HexMapV2/interaction/battleHexLine.ts` builds one line per battle on the hovered hex. `HexTooltip` renders that line under the title. When a side's name is missing, the line falls back to the word alone, never an id.
