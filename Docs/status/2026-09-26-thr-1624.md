# 2026-09-26 — THR-1624: reactions on `blessed` and `cursed` now fire

**Ticket:** [THR-1624](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1624) · **Project:** Powers & Item Generation · **Plan:** none (the ticket carries the fix; deferral of THR-1568) · **Pillars:** Engine. Content N/A (no catalog entry changed; the fix makes the two shipped entries true). UI N/A (`Browser-verify exempt: engine only`, per the Done-when).

## What was wrong

`ReactiveTrigger` named `blessed` and `cursed`, but no `EffectEvent` mapped to either, so a reaction keyed on them never fired. Two shipped items promised one: the Ember Sigil (Star +0.03 for six ticks when blessed) and the Whispering Eye (Heart −0.03 for six ticks when cursed).

## What shipped

- **Two events, two trigger arms.** `EffectEvent` gains `blessed` / `cursed`; `getReactiveTrigger` maps each to its trigger.
- **One landing hook.** `raiseConditionLanded` (`conditionProxyEvents.ts`) raises `damaged` for a harmful condition (unchanged) and then `blessed` for a `#blessing`-tagged condition and `cursed` for a `#curse`-tagged one — the two family tags `content-tags.ts` defines. People only. Landing only; lifting or expiry raises nothing new.
- **Every landing site calls it:** `applyConditionToActor`, the aftermath `condition_attachment` write, an item's `condition_grant` action trigger, and the bless/curse undertaking verb (`inflictCondition`, which previously raised nothing at all — not even `damaged` for a curse).
- **Fix found on the way:** the action-trigger payload helper dropped its threaded states when a raise was gated out (a boon grant), and the end-of-batch hand-back then replaced the caller's effect states with an empty map. It now keeps the map.
- **Wiring guide:** Capability 23 item 5 now says what raises the two triggers.

## Evidence

`reactiveWindow.test.ts`: the census runs every shipped reactive-with-modifier entry with no skip branch; two fixture-world tests land a real `#blessing` / `#curse` catalog condition through `applyConditionToActor` and read the resolver's burst as a control-world delta (+0.03 Star, −0.03 Heart); a plain condition raises neither; a threading caller keeps its states when nothing raises.
