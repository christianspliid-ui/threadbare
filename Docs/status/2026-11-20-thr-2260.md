# THR-2260 — After a chapter closes, the map comes back to the middle

**Shipped 2026-11-20.** Cold playtest round 8 (3/3 testers): after a chapter closed, the map sat off to one side with half the screen empty parchment. The Stage frames a place in its right-hand window, and since THR-2221 the close never moved the camera back.

- **Close re-centre, restored with its guard.** `useStageCamera`'s close waits `STAGE_LAYOUT_SETTLE_FRAMES` for the rails, then `chooseCloseRecentre` (pure): a lit place outside the visible map's middle third (`STAGE_CLOSE_CENTRE_BAND_FRACTION`) is eased to the visible map's centre, once. A camera the player moved after the frame (`cameraMovedSince`, THR-2166) stays. THR-2221's open rule is unchanged: a place already inside the Stage window does not move the camera on open.
- **Pan clamp.** New `src/components/HexMapV2/camera/panClamp.ts` (pure): the grid must cover at least `PAN_MIN_GRID_COVER` (½) of the canvas per axis, or the whole grid when it is smaller. `setupD3Zoom` applies it as d3's `constrain` (drags) and in the custom wheel handler. Programmatic jumps (`zoom.transform`) bypass it. HexMapV2 turns it off while the Stage holds the map, because the Stage may frame an edge place in its right-hand window. On close the camera goes back inside the bound: through `centerHexesAt(…, { clamp: true })` when it re-centres, or `settlePanClamp` when it does not.
- **Browser, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium` at 1920×1080.** Three story-beat chapters opened and closed in a row. Each close reported `afterClose.recentred: true, inMiddleThird: true`, with the lit hex at x 950 on a 320–1580 canvas. One of the three was hex 12,0 on the map's top edge. Console: 0 errors.
- **"Centre on my First" button.** The button does move the camera: hex 14,16 went from screen y 564 to y 910 on the first click. A second click is a no-op because Kael is already centred. The `ui_map_camera_jump` trace did not show up in `__DEBUG.getTraces()`, which reads the simulation trace buffer, so the button-versus-Home difference the skimmer reported could not be reproduced.
- **Trade-off (THR-2221).** On the full layout at 1920 the Stage window (its centre measured at x 1574) and the middle third do not overlap. So a run of chapters at a place under the panel frames on open and re-centres on close, every chapter. Cold r8 judged the off-side map worse than that. A camera the player has moved still never moves on close.
