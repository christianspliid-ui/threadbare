# THR-1995 — Heavy lane stops failing on runner speed

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** `heavy-tests.yml` went red on main at `b0f9a26e` (THR-1895). The one failure was `armedTargets.budget.heavy.test.ts`: the worst hand card's median was 17.2 ms, and the test asserted it was under the 16 ms frame budget (`INSPECTOR_VIEW_BUDGET_MS`).

**Cause: runner noise, not a regression.** `b0f9a26e` only adds files (+7931/−0) and does not touch `armedTargets`, `hexContents` or `godRail`. On the same code, the previous green run measured the worst card at 10.5 ms. On the red run, the hex-contents index also took 7.6 ms instead of 4.5 ms (×1.7). Nothing changed in the index, so the whole runner was slower.

**Fix.** Two new constants in `src/engine/views/viewConstants.ts`: `SHARED_RUNNER_SLOWDOWN` (2), and `INSPECTOR_VIEW_CI_CEILING_MS` = budget × slowdown (32 ms). Both Inspector budget tests (`armedTargets` from THR-1914, `actOnCounts` from THR-1913) now assert against the ceiling. They still log the 16 ms product budget next to the measured number, so a change that eats the frame stays visible. A genuine regression above two frames still fails.
