# THR-2191 — A Story row says when the deed was done to the subject

**Shipped 2026-11-18.** UI Visual Overhaul — Design System v1. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-11-17-thr-2191-story-rows-say-whose-deed.md`.

- **The change.** The person, faction and relic Story steps (`participated_in`) gain a `storyRole` fact rule (`src/engine/views/connections.ts`, `storyRoleWord`). It reads `participated_in.role` through `INSPECTOR_WORDS.storyRole` (`src/data/view-content.ts`): `target` → "done to them", `opponent` → "set upon", `defender` → "defending", `fallen` → "fell in it". Every other role, a missing role or a non-string role says nothing, so own deeds stay unmarked.
- **The fold.** The THR-2223 repeat fold keys on chapter plus role word, so a subject's own runs and the runs done to them are two rows. A folded row carries both words: "done to them · twice". The role word survives the two-step-chapter instance replace.
- **The test.** `src/engine/__tests__/thr2191-story-role-words.test.ts` (20 cases): every role word, the unmarked roles, prototype keys, the split fold, the joined fact, the two-step chapter, faction and relic subjects, no underscore or raw key on any fact. The THR-2223 and THR-2145 suites pass unchanged.
- **Browser.** Bash-Vite on port 5287 + Playwright DOM at 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, 200 ticks through `__DEBUG.tick`: mortal `born_lc_1` (Yroror) shows "Seize an item · done to them" and "Destroy an item · done to them" beside unmarked own deeds ("Tend to Wounds · twice").
- **Seen in passing.** Undertaking-outcome rows done to a subject (for example three "Consecrate a pilgrim way · done to them" on `ind_9`) do not fold, by THR-2223's design: only encounter chapters fold.
