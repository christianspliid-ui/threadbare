# THR-2142 — six small faults from cold playtest round 6

**Shipped 2026-11-02.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience. Found by cold playtest round 6.

1. **Setup screens that move on with only "Choose again".** New `ChoiceHoldCue` in `remembranceChoice.tsx`: "Moving on" over a thin bar that fills across the hold (`remembranceHoldFill` keyframe, `index.css`), keyed on the chosen item so a new choice restarts it. Shown above "Choose again" on the Stirring and Drive screens. Copy: `REMEMBRANCE_MOVING_ON`.
2. **A black second before the sphere pour.** `SpheresStep` now fades in on the first beat (`textVisible`, 200 ms) instead of the content beat (800 ms), so the black gap after the court's Continue shrinks from 0.8–1.8 s to about 0.2 s before the fade starts.
3. **"What she is to him".** The bond row's heading is `whatTheyAreToYouLabel(name)` — "What Kael is to you" — on both resolver paths.
4. **Observe sold as Piercing Gaze.** The Act-on row prints the slot's `spellName` (Law 14), so it matches the hand and the receipt. The Chronicle line is now `observeRevealMessage` ("Piercing Gaze reads Kael: their Iron reach now shows on their sheet"), not the raw domain id. `observeHasNothingLeft` locks the card ("You already see all it would show", `NOTHING_TO_REVEAL_REASON`) once every Reach is known, through the new `observeNothingLeft` input on `getTargetActionSlots` (passed by `actOn.ts` and `useAgentInteraction.ts`). A cast already in flight that finds nothing left still answers with `observeNothingLeftMessage`.
5. **Read the Threads counting hidden pools.** New `readThreadsPools.ts`: the read charges the god's primary pool, then its secondary, and nothing else; Available shows those two by name ("Energy 120 · Iron 80"). It used to sum and drain all twelve spheres, the folded Foundation pools too.
6. **A Needs-you pill with nothing waiting.** When only News is in the list the pill's word is "News" (`NEEDS_YOU_NEWS_ONLY_WORD`). The tier-promotion row still opens the mortal's sheet: a promotion unlocks no chapter of its own, so the ticket's "points to the chapter it unlocked, if one did" has nothing to point at.
