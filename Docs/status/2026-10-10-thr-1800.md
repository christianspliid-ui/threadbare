# 2026-10-10 — THR-1800: a chapter with unpriced choices can be played again

**Ticket:** [THR-1800](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1800) · **Found by:** cold playtest round 3, story persona (quit point) — "I couldn't pick either option, couldn't press play, and couldn't tell why."

- **Content — every authored card is priced.** Five pole-choice chapters (*The Page Beneath the Saint*, *The Comet at the Turning*, *The Wall of the Mason Lord*, *The Verdict That Burns*, *The Granaries in the Famine Year*) shipped both cards without `essenceCost`. The god's push now costs 2; "Stay your hand" costs 0 — giving nothing is free.
- **UI — a missing price reads as free.** `choiceEssenceCost` in `buildUnifiedEncounterStageModel.ts` turns a missing or non-finite cost into 0, so an unpriced card can never grey out a chapter again. `encounterVisibility.ts` reads it the same way, so the intervention tally never sums `NaN`.
- **UI — no empty, captioned image panel.** `/concept-art/encounters/placeholder.jpg` never shipped. The 9 templates that pointed at it, plus 3 more that pointed at missing art, lose their `illustrationUrl`. `renderableIllustrationUrl` (new `encounterIllustration.ts`) drops the placeholder in both stage adapters, because the contract builders still write it as their `painting` fallback.
- **Guards.** `assertAuthoredChoicesPriced` checks every template catalog-wide, with a negative control. A second catalog test checks that every `illustrationUrl` resolves to a file under `public/`. The adapter test proves that an unpriced card comes out `affordable: true` and that the placeholder renders no panel.
