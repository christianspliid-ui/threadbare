- **The readers.** Five sites, one family: `armySpawning` (`isEligibleForArmySpawn` faction Gold tier ≥ 3, `selectCommander` member Iron tier ≥ 4 and highest wins, `spawnArmy` Gold tier → warband / regiment ≥ 5 / host ≥ 7) and the campaign mirror in `notableAgendas.raiseCampaignArmy` (the same Gold gate, and the notable commands in person at Iron tier ≥ 4). These are standing requirements and size bands, not d100 reads. They read the pre-refit curve (midpoint 10, k 0.4).
- **Measured** (`npm run measure:army-scales`, every living faction, medium, tick 0, seeds 42 · 99 · 7). Faction Gold sits at raw 60–80 (p10 25–27, min 12), so the Gold gate passes ~100% on every scale. Army size: pre-refit made every faction a host (52/59/58 of 52/59/58). Reach share gives warband/regiment/host 1/3/48 · 1/2/56 · 1/2/55, and the dice 4/8/40 · 4/6/49 · 4/5/49. - **A tie bug, fixed.** `selectCommander` ranked on the tier. On every saturating curve the strongest candidates tie at tier 10 (raw 40 protagonists beside the raw 60 garrison captain), and the first edge wins. Pre-refit picked the strongest Iron member in only 4/6 · 5/8 · 4/6 factions. It now gates on the tier and ranks on the raw Iron score, and picks the strongest in 5/5 · 6/6 · 6/6.
- **Verdict: reach share** (`armyReachTier` = `computeTier(computeReachShare(...))`, exported for the campaign mirror). The Gold gate opens past raw 8, a regiment past 16, a host past 24, and the commander gate past raw 12. Thresholds are unchanged. The `ARMY_SPAWN_*_TIER_MIN` constants and `determineSizeCategory`'s bands remain the levers if armies should vary more in size.

Two more encounter lines use `{are}`, which only `ambitionProse` resolves. They are filed as [THR-1810](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1810).
