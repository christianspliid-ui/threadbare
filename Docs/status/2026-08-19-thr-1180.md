# THR-1180 — Sphere attunement: the essence-earned counter and the fourth unlock channel

**2026-08-19 · shipped**

Workstream D of the nudge-library plan. A god's repertoire could deepen three ways — starting cards, milestone grants, and the still-inert THR-791 god-trait hook. This adds a fourth, keyed on *practice*: how much essence you have ever drawn **through** a sphere, counted for the life of the run and never decremented by spending.

## What the ticket asked, and where the answer diverged

The task named "the three divine-economy GraphOp paths" as the sites to instrument. There are more than three, and the set grows: capped cosmic generation in `phaseEssence`, `perTickIncome` in `phaseControlEffects`, the effect delta in `unifiedActionResolution`, the place-of-power streams, the elder-site reward in `ruinTransformation`, the delve refund. Instrumenting a list means the counter is right until somebody adds the seventh site — and a counter that quietly stops counting is worse than none, because the unlock it gates simply stops arriving with nothing red anywhere.

So the accrual sits at the seam every one of those must pass through: the **phase merge**. `applyEssenceEarned` is hooked into `runInlinePhase` (`orchestrator.ts`) and `runRegisteredPhases` (`phaseRegistry.ts`), diffs the pool the phase returned against the one it was handed, and banks the positive movement. A grant site written tomorrow is counted without being told to. Both funnels are hooked because which one a phase runs through is a THR-238 migration detail, not a property of what the phase does.

The price is stated in the module and on the wiki rather than left to be discovered: accrual is **net movement within one phase**, so a phase that both grants and spends banks the difference. Exactly one shipped phase does both, and there netting is arguably the honest number — a control effect costing more upkeep than it yields has earned the god nothing. Across phases nothing nets; maintenance and cast debits live in their own phases and cannot eat `phaseEssence`'s income. The fast path is a reference compare, so ~40 phases a tick pay one pointer comparison.

## The rule that keeps this deepening rather than a re-key

THR-870's score-keyed *access* pivot stays parked, and the code enforces that rather than promising it. `memberAccess` runs **before** `isMemberUnlocked` in `buildRepertoire`, so a god attuned to a sphere they do not hold crosses the mark and gains nothing; a god who holds the sphere but has not worked it sees the family without its attunement members. The sphere identity is still the door; attunement is only the depth behind it. The falsification arm for that ordering is a real test, not a comment: a god attuned to all twelve spheres at 10,000 each holds no attunement member outside their own two, and loosening the access gate reds it by name.

Three members exercise the channel — **The Wider Swing** (chaos, mark 20), **Nothing To Find** (darkness, mark 20), **The Whole Shape** (light, mark 60) — with two at the first mark and one at the second, so both rows of `SPHERE_ATTUNEMENT_THRESHOLDS` have something waiting on them. That is not padding. The `god_trait` stub is tolerated inert because THR-791 will come and claim it; this channel has no future owner, so shipping the kind with nothing gated on it would mean nobody notices the day it stops resolving. A liveness sweep additionally catches a member naming a mark the table does not contain — `threshold` is a plain `number`, so nothing else stands between a typo and a card that can never unlock.

## The kill criterion was unreachable, and that mattered more than the tuning

The plan said: if attunement never fires in a 100-tick seeded playtest with active essence income, retune the thresholds. It never fired — and the thresholds were not the reason. `INITIAL_ESSENCE_PER_SPHERE` is 50 and `BASE_MAX_ESSENCE` is 50, and the ceiling only rises per held thread, of which the CLI world has none. So the pool starts *at* its cap and `generateEssence` credits zero every tick forever: after 100 ticks at seed 42 medium every sphere read exactly 50.0 and the counter was `undefined`. Reading that as "the marks are too high" would have lowered `[20, 60]` and changed nothing, because the income is not small, it is identically zero.

Drained to 1 per sphere, the same seed reached chaos **19.250000000000007** at tick 55 and **21.000000000000014** at tick 60 — crossing mark 20, identical to the last digit across repeat runs, so *same seed ⇒ same unlock tick* holds. The marks stand unchanged. What the measurement actually established is a design fact worth keeping: **nothing is earned until something is spent**, which makes attunement a record of *use* rather than of age — the reading the design wanted anyway. Logged as impediment #685, because a default fixture sitting at a clamp is the same family of trap as an empty population: the lever runs, reports success, and cannot move.

## Evidence

Every guard was falsified before it was trusted, each reverted individually and confirmed red: the access gate (off-sphere members appear), the threshold comparison (locked cards deal), monotonicity (spending decrements the counter), and the adapter thread-through (the card never reaches the rendered hand). The trace is pinned in both directions — one emission per crossing, and silence on a phase that vaults a mark already behind, which is what makes it a progression log rather than a per-tick level reading.

**Browser-verify substitution: jsdom-render — unattended run, no startable dev server** (impediment #683, 13th occurrence, and the first that cost nothing: the route was decided before the ticket was claimed rather than at capture time). The render arm asserts the real `NudgePhaseShell` DOM in both directions — the authored face on screen at the mark, absent one below it.

## Not done, and named

The plan asked to "extend the card-row explain surface with `sphere_attunement`". **There is no such surface to extend** — `RepertoireEntry.source` has had no consumer in `src/components/` since THR-887 shipped it, and `buildNudgePhaseModel` reads only `entry.member.id`. So the union gained the value and `sourceFor` reports it ahead of the sphere check (an attunement member is both sphere-signed and attunement-gated; the provenance a surface must explain is *how it was earned*), and the new `window.__DEBUG.getRepertoire()` is where it is legible today. Building the player-facing explain surface is genuinely unshipped work rather than a line this ticket skipped.

Three wiki pages took a `Wiki-freshness-exempt`: `turn-structure` and `tick-cycle` matched on `orchestrator.ts` though no phase was added or reordered, and `threads-court` matched on `types/influence.ts` where only a type declaration landed. `nudge-cards` and `essence-control` were updated in full.
