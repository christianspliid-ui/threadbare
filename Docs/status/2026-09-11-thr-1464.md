# THR-1464 — the chip row that painted over its own price

**2026-09-11 · UI · Bug · High · Encounter Experience**

Found by the [THR-1463](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1463) slice pre-flight on the deployed build (`c3ef6c78`): **The First Thread**, the first divine choice a new player makes, offered two cards whose badge strips read `✳ CR▣TE▸✦◈◇S▮◆G⋯` and `◉ FIND ▣ ()9▶M◈✦✦`. Both illegible.

## What it actually was

Not the sphere label, and not a font or a glyph problem. **The scale chip** (`COSMIC`), painted straight through the price badge.

`CardFace`'s chip row is a two-group flex row: the kind chips on the left, the reach mark + sphere mark + framed price on the right. The left group carries `min-width: 0` — which lets a flex item shrink **below its own content**, and is the right call when the content can absorb it. Here it cannot: `CardKeywordChip` is `inline-flex` with `white-space: nowrap` and visible overflow, so a group squeezed under its contents does not truncate. It keeps drawing, past its own edge, over whatever is beside it.

Measured on the deployed build at a verified 1920×1080:

| card | content needed | row width | group shrank to | group overflow | scale chip × price overlap |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| `Agent Thread` (`bind_thread_agent`) | 315px | 184px | 44px | 131px | **77px** — the chip's entire width |
| `Piercing Gaze` (`observe_agent`) | 266px | 184px | 73px | 81px | **53px** |

The 315px breaks down as keyword 94 + gap 4 + scale 77 + gap 6 + right group 134. The right group is 134px because a 10-essence price renders six pips plus the overflow glyph inside its frame — so the defect needed *both* a two-chip card and an expensive one, which is why the nudge hand (cost 1, one chip, no grouping span) never showed it and the shared face still looked healthy.

**`Place Thread` on the Seat beat is the same component and the same shape** — the ticket's third repro, fixed by the same change.

## The fix, and why not truncation

The row wraps. Three declarations on the shared face:

- `flex-wrap: wrap` + `row-gap` on the chip row — content that will not fit takes a second line instead of overflowing onto its neighbour.
- `flex-wrap: wrap` on the grouping span — `min-width: 0` may still shrink it, but it may no longer do so while forbidding its chips to wrap.
- `margin-left: auto` on the right-hand group — `justify-content: space-between` does not right-align a line holding a single item, so without this the price would pack left the moment it wrapped.

The gap is now one constant, `CARD_CHIP_ROW_GAP_PX`, used on both axes: the space between the chips and the price below them is the same separation that sat between them side by side, so the two lines read as one strip rather than two.

**Truncation was the alternative and was rejected.** All three readings are load-bearing — the verb, the scale and the price are three separate vocabularies (Law 9), and ellipsising any of them is a Law 13/14 regression that trades an unreadable row for a lying one. So the ticket's open question — *show the six-pip-plus-ellipsis render in full, or truncate it deliberately?* — resolves to **shown in full**. `✦✦✦✦✦✦⋯` is what the price is; wrapping is what buys the room for it. The `⋯` already carries "more than shown" and is the price's own vocabulary, not a layout apology.

A card that fits is laid out exactly as before. Only one that cannot takes the second line.

## Evidence

`preview_start` is refused in unattended runs (impediments #546/#574), so the local-dev-server Playwright route was shut. The route was decided at claim, not at the capture, and split in two:

- **Cause and before-state:** Playwright against the **deployed build** — a public URL needs no local server, and it is the only place the `getBoundingClientRect` overlap the ticket asks for is measurable at all. Screenshot at a verified `innerWidth/innerHeight` of 1920×1080.
- **After-state:** the three declarations injected into the live composed surface and re-measured. Every overlap went to **zero area** (`scale×cost`, `keyword×cost`, `keyword×scale`), group overflow to **0px**, and the price stayed inside the row and right-aligned (`costRightAligned: true`) with its full `✦✦✦✦✦✦⋯` intact. Then `Browser-verify substitution: jsdom-render` for the committed code, pinning the declarations that proof depended on.

Console: 0 errors, 4 warnings — two `THREE.Clock` deprecations and two WebGL `texImage3D` notices, all pre-existing and unrelated to the card row.

## The snapshot pin, moved once and deliberately

`NudgeCard.snapshot.test.tsx` exists to prove THR-1002's extraction did not move the nudge face, and its header says updating it "is never the right fix". Eight snapshots failed here on three changed style strings.

The pin was moved, and the bar it had to clear is now written into that header so it does not quietly become updateable on an argument. **No rendered geometry changed, and that was established by measurement rather than by reasoning:** the three declarations were injected into five live nudge cards on the deployed build, and the row, the right-hand group and the price badge all came back byte-identical on `getBoundingClientRect` to sub-pixel precision — `GEOMETRY_UNCHANGED: true` ×5. Each declaration is inert on a row that fits, and the snapshot diff is exactly 2 lines per snapshot with no DOM structure change. Absent that measurement, a failing snapshot still means the primitive is wrong.

## Guard

Five arms in `ActionCard.test.tsx`, against the failing shape (two chips, cost 10 — the only shape with a grouping span and an overflowing price). jsdom has no layout engine, so these pin the *declarations*, not the pixels; the docblock says so and points at where the pixel proof lives.

The first arm is a falsification guard: if the fixture ever stops producing both chips, every other arm would pass vacuously against a row that cannot overflow. **The suite was confirmed falsifiable** — reverting `flex-wrap` to `nowrap` and dropping `margin-left: auto` turns it red (2 failed / 29 passed), green again on restore.
