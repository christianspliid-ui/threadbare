# THR-1302 — the ambition boost that was true by construction

**2026-08-29 · Engine · Thematic Pressure & Living World**

## What was wrong

The unified decision board scored an undertaking's desire as
`max(axiological × W + ambitionBoost, MINIMUM_DESIRE) ^ EXP`, and it read
`ambitionBoost` from the encounter path's `getAmbitionBoost`:

```ts
return agentPursuesReach(graph, agentId, reachPrimary) ? AMBITION_REACH_BOOST : 0;
```

That question — *does the agent pursue **any** ambition with positive affinity for
this reach?* — genuinely discriminates for an encounter, whose candidates come off
the hex and may have nothing to do with what the agent wants. For an undertaking it
cannot: `generateStrategicCandidates` only ever walks an active ambition's own
`strategicProfile.templateIds`, and the board takes `reach = pickPrimaryReach(template)`
from that same template. The agent pursues it *because the candidate exists*. Measured
over 150 ticks on seeds 42 and 99, the undertaking desire multiplier sat at `0.354` for
p25, p50 **and** p75 — identical on both seeds.

This is the same shape as the vacuity the temperament bracket caught one term over
(impediment #829): a predicate whose population is generated by the very thing it tests.

**The sibling assertion could not have caught it.** `decisionBoardLiveness.test.ts`
already pinned "the undertaking desire multiplier is not frozen", and that test went
green when THR-1297 authored `motivations` — over a still-frozen boost. A product
varies as soon as one factor does. Nothing on the trace carried the factor.

## What changed

`computeAmbitionCentralityBoost` (`decisionBoard.ts`) replaces the shared term on the
board side only. Two changes make it discriminate:

1. **It asks the proposing ambition**, not the agent's whole pursued set. The board
   knows which ambition generated the candidate, so it need not ask the diffuse
   question. On the shipped corpus **19 of 77** real (ambition, template) pairs sit on
   a reach the proposing ambition has no affinity for at all — permitted by its
   template list, but not what it is about. All were paid in full before.
2. **It reads the magnitude.** `reachAffinity` is authored as a graded map (0.2–0.9)
   and was being collapsed to a boolean.

Normalised by the ambition's *own* strongest affinity, times a new
`UNDERTAKING_AMBITION_CENTRALITY_BOOST = 0.5`. The normalisation is load-bearing rather
than cosmetic: no ambition authors a `1.0`, so raw magnitudes would cap every
undertaking at `0.4` — a uniform level cut dressed as a spread (measured raw: max
`0.400`, mean `0.258`, 6 distinct; normalised: max `0.500`, mean `0.331`, 8 distinct).
Normalised, an undertaking on its ambition's most-preferred reach is paid exactly what
the flat term paid, and only peripheral ones are paid less.

The constant is deliberately **separate from** `AMBITION_REACH_BOOST` despite holding the
same number. The encounter path keeps the flat term, `getAmbitionBoostForEntry` is no
longer exported, and the two knobs turn independently — the coordination block's
explicit requirement, since a naive shared fix would have re-ranked encounters too.

`BoardEntry.ambitionBoost` and its trace field are new. A multiplier's *inputs* are
where this defect class hides, so the input is now on the record (NFP #2).

envelope itself is right remains THR-1301's question, unchanged.
