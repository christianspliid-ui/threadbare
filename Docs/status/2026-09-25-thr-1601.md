# 2026-09-25 — THR-1601: the title menu no longer leaks dev surfaces

**Ticket:** [THR-1601](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1601) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Pillars:** UI only — Engine / Content N/A (menu and link targets).

## What was wrong

Cold playtest round 1: all three testers read "Ubiquitous Language" on the title menu as a programmer term, and one clicked through it, hit **Back to Game**, and played the whole session in the dev quick-start world ("The Dev Oracle") without knowing. Both the Codex and the glossary linked "Back to Game" to `?view=game`, which routes to `quickStartPhase(42)`.

## What changed

- `StartPage.tsx` renders the "Ubiquitous Language" item only when `import.meta.env.DEV`. `?view=ul` stays reachable by URL in every build.
- The Codex and glossary back links now read **Back to Title** and point at `/`, the title screen. (Relabelled as well as repointed: a link that says "Game" and lands on the menu would be its own small lie.)
- Regression test in `UbiquitousLanguageDashboard.test.tsx` pins the href and label.

Verified on a production `vite build` + `vite preview` at 1920×1080: menu reads New World · Codex · Settings · Credits; Codex → Back to Title lands on `/`; `?view=ul` loads and its back link lands on `/`; console empty.
