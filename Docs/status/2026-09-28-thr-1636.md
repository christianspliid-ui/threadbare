# 2026-09-28 — THR-1636: trade lanes carry while their towns stand (seeded things that stay alive, slice 1)

**Ticket:** [THR-1636](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1636) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-28-thr-1636-seeded-things-stay-alive.md` § S1 · **Pillars:** Engine (the lane-traffic step); Content: N/A (lane traffic is state and writes no prose; the existing "A Road Falls Silent" line and the route-event encounters carry lanes into the story); UI (the hex tooltip's *blockaded* / *fading* words).

## What was wrong

Every trade lane in the world died on tick 36. Nothing in the simulation ever marked a seeded lane as traded, so each one decayed 1 volume per tick from volume 1 and dissolved. Founded lanes lasted 36 ticks after founding (their grace window), then died the same way.

## What shipped

- **`laneTraffic(graph, edge, tick)`** in `tradeRoute.ts` sorts each lane into one of three classes. **`carrying`**: both ends resolve to Locations in the registry's settlement class, and neither end has cursed roads. **`suspended`**: the lane is blockaded and still threatened. **`idle`**: anything else. `isLocationRoutesCursed` is now exported from `phaseProsperity.ts` rather than copied.
- **`phaseTradeRouteDecay`** runs this check first. A carrying or suspended lane is marked traded every tick, so it never goes stale, and a suspended lane is never dissolved. Once a day (every 12 ticks) volume steps one toward a **traffic level**: 2 as a base, up to 4 when the two ends' surpluses and shortages match, and 1 less while threatened. A suspended lane steps toward 1. Volume that a merchant adds on top falls back one step a day. Only an idle lane reaches the old decay rule.
- **Traces:** `trade_route_volume_change` has a new cause, `traffic`. A `trade_route_upkeep` summary is written once per settle tick, never once per lane.
- **Kill switch:** `LANE_TRAFFIC_ENABLED`. Readers use `setLaneTrafficEnabledOverride` to run A/B arms.
- **UI:** each `RouteTooltipEntry` carries `traffic`. `HexTooltip` shows ` ·blockaded` for a suspended lane and ` ·fading` for an idle one, next to ` ·threatened`. **Debug:** `window.__DEBUG.getTradeLanes()` returns each lane's class, volume, freshness and endpoint hexes. The plan named a trade block in the debug tab, but that block does not exist, so the accessor stands in for it.

## Measured

- **A/B census, `readers/upkeep-ab.ts`** (seeds 42 and 99, medium map, 300 ticks, switch off → on):
  - Worldgen lanes standing at t300 went from **0 → 6 of 6** on both seeds.
  - With the switch off, every worldgen lane still dies at t36, which reproduces today's behaviour.
  - With the switch on, one lane died: a founded lane on seed 99, at t176, whose town had become `ruins`. The lane was `idle` and its death is traced.
  - Lanes standing at t300 were 7 on seed 42 and 6 on seed 99, which is no more than the seeded plus founded count.
  - Route-event scans that planted a seed after t36 went from 0 → 9 on seed 99. Seed 42 had none in either arm.
  - Seed 42 settlement prosperity sum at t300 went from 866.5 → 871.3 (+0.6%). There is no runaway.
  - Output: `output/upkeep-ab-2026-09-28.json`.
- **Budget, THR-1592 harness** (new `lanes-off` arm; 3 interleaved reps; median ms per tick, t21–200):
  - Seed 42: 127.9 → 130.2 (+1.8%).
  - Seed 99: 171.6 → 173.8 (+1.3%).
  - Deciders at t200: 19 → 20 and 20 → 19.
  - Both seeds are inside the +10% line. Output: `output/lanes-cost-2026-09-28.json(l)`.
- **Browser, 1920×1080:** at tick 50 all six seeded lanes are `carrying` at volume 2–3, and the gold lane lines are still drawn. The hover tooltip on Great Silverhold lists both of its lanes.

## Recalibrated

`worldContextPast.live.test.ts` (heavy) asserted that every found core grows in the seed-42 world at t160. Once lanes stand, that world keeps no dead who belonged to a faction, so `oath_object`, whose every line names a faction, has nothing to name. The test now holds a core the world cannot host to the review world instead, so a core that grows nowhere still fails. It also caps unhosted cores at a quarter of the catalogue.
