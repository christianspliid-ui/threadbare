# 2026-09-28 — THR-1636: trade lanes carry while their towns stand (seeded things that stay alive, slice 1)

**Ticket:** [THR-1636](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1636) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-28-thr-1636-seeded-things-stay-alive.md` § S1 · **Pillars:** Engine (the lane-traffic step); Content: N/A (lane traffic is state and writes no prose; the existing "A Road Falls Silent" line and the route-event encounters carry lanes into the story); UI (the hex tooltip's *blockaded* / *fading* words).

## What was wrong

Every trade lane in the world died on tick 36. Nothing in the simulation ever marked a seeded lane as traded, so each one decayed 1 volume per tick from volume 1 and dissolved. Founded lanes lasted 36 ticks after founding (their grace window) and then died the same way.

## What shipped

- **`laneTraffic(graph, edge, tick)`** in `tradeRoute.ts` puts each lane in one of three classes:
  - **`carrying`**: both ends resolve to Locations in the registry's settlement class, and neither end has cursed roads.
  - **`suspended`**: the lane is blockaded and still threatened.
  - **`idle`**: anything else.

  `isLocationRoutesCursed` is now exported from `phaseProsperity.ts` rather than copied.
- **`phaseTradeRouteDecay`** runs this check first. A carrying or suspended lane is marked traded every tick, so it never goes stale. A suspended lane is never dissolved. Once a day (every 12 ticks), volume steps one toward a **traffic level**:
  - The level is 2 at base.
  - It rises to as much as 4 when one town's surpluses answer the other's shortages.
  - It is 1 lower while the lane is threatened.
  - A suspended lane steps toward 1 instead.
  - Volume a merchant adds on top of the level sinks back one step a day.

  Only an idle lane reaches the old decay rule.
- **Traces:** `trade_route_volume_change` has a new cause, `traffic`. A `trade_route_upkeep` summary is emitted once per settle tick, never once per lane.
- **Kill switch:** `LANE_TRAFFIC_ENABLED`. Readers use `setLaneTrafficEnabledOverride` to run their A/B arms.
- **UI:** `RouteTooltipEntry` carries `traffic`. `HexTooltip` appends ` ·blockaded` for a suspended lane and ` ·fading` for an idle one, beside ` ·threatened`.
- **Debug:** `window.__DEBUG.getTradeLanes()` returns each lane's class, volume, freshness and endpoint hexes. The plan called for a trade block in the debug tab, but that block does not exist, so the accessor stands in for it.

## Measured

- **A/B census, `readers/upkeep-ab.ts`** (seeds 42 and 99, medium map, 300 ticks, one process per arm, on `main` after THR-1632 merged):
  - Worldgen lanes standing at t300 went from **0 → 4 of 6** on seed 42 and **0 → 6 of 6** on seed 99.
  - Seed 42 lost two lanes at t100, when their capital was razed to `ruins`. Both lanes were `idle`, and both deaths are traced. No lane died without a visible cause.
  - With the switch off, every worldgen lane still dies at t36, and founded lanes die 36 ticks after founding. That is today's behaviour, reproduced.
  - Lanes standing at t300: 6 on each seed. That does not exceed seeded plus founded (seed 42: 4 worldgen and 2 founded).
  - Route-event scans that planted a seed after t36 went from 0 → 9 on seed 99. Seed 42 had none in either arm.
  - Seed 42 settlement prosperity sum at t300 went from 822.0 → 871.4, with the highest single settlement at 48.9. There is no runaway.
  - Output: `output/upkeep-ab-{off,on}-2026-09-28.json`.
  - The reader now finds deaths by diffing the lane set each tick. The rolling trace buffer evicts phase-6.62 dissolution traces on busy ticks, and a first run read three traced deaths as none.
- **Budget, THR-1592 harness** (new `lanes-off` arm; 5 interleaved reps; median ms per tick over t21–200, off → on):
  - Seed 42: 132.3 → 138.6 (+4.8%).
  - Seed 99: 185.9 → 189.1 (+1.7%).
  - Both are inside the +10% line.
  - Deciders at t200: 19 → 19 and 20 → 18.
  - The lane step itself profiles at about 0.005 ms per tick (`trade_route_decay`). The remaining delta sits in unrelated phases and flips sign between seeds, which is machine noise on a loaded box: some single reps ran 30% slow.
  - Output: `output/lanes-cost-2026-09-28.json(l)`.
- **Browser, 1920×1080:**

  - The hover tooltip on Great Silverhold lists both of its lanes.
  - The two new words are covered by the jsdom render test, because no debug lever can blockade or raze a live town.
