# 2026-10-16 — THR-1931: the faction purse — the land fills it, war empties it, and a broke faction's armies desert

**Ticket:** [THR-1931](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1931). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-15-thr-1931-faction-purse.md`. Engine, Content and UI pillars.

Every faction now has a purse. Before this, no code added to a faction's `wealth`, so every faction sheet read Destitute, raising an army was free, and the quest, bounty, warehouse and counting-house gates never opened.

- **Engine.** A daily `faction_purse` phase (`src/engine/factionPurse.ts`, pure helpers in `src/engine/factionPurse/`) runs right after holding income. It pays five sources: town tax to one tax holder per town (the border realm, else the highest influence), place yield, a realm's land rent per border hex (from the cached realm projection), tolls, and guild work (the town's tax holder takes a fifth). Then it pays army upkeep (warband 1.5, regiment 2.5, host 3.5 a day) in army id order, whole armies only, with no debt. It writes `meansBand`, `purseLastDay` and each paid army's `paidThroughTick`. `checkUnderfunded` reads `paidThroughTick`, so an unpaid army loses cohesion and deserts at 0. Ambition and campaign raises cost 50 and need the raise plus a first day in hand. Worldgen gives every faction 10 coin plus 20 days of its armies' upkeep. A faction Destitute for 5 days adds 0.15 to schism pressure. `FACTION_PURSE_ENABLED = false` restores the old behaviour.
- **Content.** `src/data/faction-purse-constants.ts` holds the rate tables, including `GUILD_PLACE_AFFINITY`, which THR-1923 will read. Four chronicle triggers (`purse_broke`, `purse_broke_bare`, `army_unpaid_desertion`, `purse_recovered`) have three variants each. They fire only for factions inside the player's attention, with a 10-day cooldown.
- **UI.** The faction sheet's Means row is now a **Purse** panel: the Means chip, the two largest sources by name (linked), and the spending line. It shows the broke line when an army goes unpaid. *What They Control* gains "pays tax" / "yields salt" clauses. Debug: an Economy-tab Purses table, an Armies-tab "Paid through" row, `__DEBUG.getFactionPurse` / `getPurseLedger`.

**Measured (seed 42 medium, headless):** faction_0 holds 121 coin by day 10 and 187 by day 20; every guild grows above its opening purse; no worldgen army goes unpaid. Worldgen armies still die within days, but from march attrition while paid. That is not the purse, and it was already so on main. Browser: faction_0 reads *Wealthy*, *"Most of its coin comes from its lands and the city of Silverborough."*, *"It keeps no soldiers."* (`Docs/audits/2026-10-16-thr-1931/faction-purse-1920x1080.png`).
