# 2026-10-14 — THR-1854: the First's journey choices land, answer, and never repeat

**Ticket:** [THR-1854](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1854). Warm playtest round 2: 3/3 testers met the same "Road of Trials" moment twice, and 2/3 never understood whether a journey choice did anything (one WOULD QUIT). Engine + Content + UI.

- **Engine.** New `applyBeatChoiceEffects` (`src/engine/journeyEngine.ts`) writes a choice's `axiologicalShifts` to the First's `axiologicalProfile` (clamped to [-1, 1]) and its `reachChanges` to her raw `domainCapabilities` at the meeting's ×100 (`JOURNEY_REACH_CHANGE_RAW_SCALE`), with a new `journey.choice_applied` trace. `selectTemplate` takes the beats already played and drops their templates, then their variants, and repeats only if nothing else remains.
- **Content.** Five value-pair keys the engine never had (`courage_cowardice`, `honesty_deception`, `sacrifice_selfishness`) renamed to `courage_prudence`, `honesty_cunning`, `sacrifice_survival`; `BeatChoiceEffects.axiologicalShifts` is now keyed by `ValuePair`, so a stray key fails the build.
- **UI.** `JourneyVignetteModal` answers the click with the choice's `consequenceProse` and a Continue button (any close then commits the answer), opens with focus on the scene instead of the first choice, and disables a choice the paying pool cannot cover. `handleJourneyChoice` charges `essenceCost` all-or-nothing against the god's primary sphere (the THR-1720 rule). The cost was shown and never charged before.
- **Evidence.** Seed `?seeded` medium in a browser: the "Guide the awakening" choice showed its line, and Continue raised Kael's veil from 35 to 40 raw, with the beat history reading `call_fallback`, then `trials_discovery`.
