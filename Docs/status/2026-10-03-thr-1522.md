### 2026-10-03 — THR-1522: a place's traits reach its prose

**A haunted place now reads haunted.** Places earn five traits from their own fortunes (*Welcoming*, *Lawless*, *Veil-thin*, *Haunted*, *Blood-soaked*, written by `phaseLocationTraits`). Until now they moved travel cost, step odds and the encounter pool, and showed as a chip on the location page, but the place's prose never mentioned them.
- **New resolver.** `locationTraitResolver` (`src/engine/proseResolvers.ts`) reads the place's live `has_trait` edges and adds one line per trait from the new `LOCATION_TRAIT_PROSE` table (`src/data/prose-layer-content.ts`). There are three game-master lines per trait, and each one says the trait's word.
- **Caps.** At most `LOCATION_TRAIT_PROSE_MAX` (2) lines, at `LOCATION_TRAIT_PROSE_PRIORITY` (80). The category is `tension`, so a trait line never pushes the settlement genome or culture out of their `character` slots. It is registered right after `settlementGenomeResolver`.
- **Fail-soft.** An unmarked place, or a trait with no row, adds nothing.

**Evidence (CLI, seed 99 medium, tick 150).** *Ardenmor Keep* (Welcoming): "Ardenmor Keep is welcoming these days. Good years have made it generous, and strangers are fed before anyone asks their business." *Murkwick* (Lawless) and *The Bone Coast* (Blood-soaked) each say their word. Two unmarked places say nothing.

**Declined, with the reason recorded in the plan addendum** (`Docs/plans/2026-09-21-thr-790-traits-wave-2.md` § Slice 4 addendum). Slice 1's census reads the trait pool term as live but **flat**: gold-reach encounters resolve 0 of 122 times at Welcoming towns, and still 0 of 83 at the cap. The limit is eligibility, not weight, so trait-weighted merchant routing and a Broken-state drift bonus were not built.

**Noticed, not acted on.** On seed 99, *Crystalspire* is both Welcoming and Lawless at tick 150. The two rules read different scalars (prosperity and unrest), so a rich, restless city can hold both. The prose now says both lines, side by side.
