# 2026-09-29 — THR-1654: one notable in every settlement

**Ticket:** [THR-1654](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1654) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1630-notables-and-ties.md` § S2 · **Pillars:** Engine (the worldgen pass and the local roster); Content: N/A (local agendas reuse the existing feud, claim and rite prose; no new lines); UI: N/A in this slice (the settlement page's notable line and the Notables panel's Local section are S4, [THR-1655](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1655)).

## What was wrong

A new world opened with most of its settlements holding nobody who wanted anything. After S1 (the people web), 37 of 47 settlements on seed 42 and 53 of 67 on seed 99 still had no resident with an ambition, a quarrel, a secret or a favour at tick 0. The notable-agenda system could only give a want to faction leaders, so a town far from a court stayed quiet.

## What shipped

- **`seedNotables`**, a new `seedLivingWorld` pass after marks and before garrisons. Each settlement's wealthiest-role ambient resident (`ROLE_WEALTH`, id tie-break, no draw) is promoted to the notable tier through `hydrateToTier` on its own reserved stream (prime 60091) and stamped `notableOrigin: 'worldgen'`. The package:
  - **a holding**: an unheld Place in their own settlement, commerce then authority then any, through `grantHolding`;
  - **an old quarrel**: `writeGrudge(..., 'old_quarrel')` with the nearest decider within 8 hexes, else the nearest other settlement's notable. All notables are promoted before any package is written, so the first settlement in id order also has a neighbour to quarrel with;
  - **a secret or favour** with the nearest decider within 12 hexes (preferring someone other than the quarrel partner): even-indexed settlements' notables know something about the hero, odd-indexed ones are owed a favour;
  - **membership** of the faction that holds their settlement.
  - No ambition and no `relates_to`, so importance stays 0 and seeding graduates nobody.
- **Local agendas.** `listLocalNotables` (seeded notables still at the notable tier, excluding faction leaders) launches on the same 12-tick roster scan under its own cap, `MAX_ACTIVE_LOCAL_AGENDAS` (3), counted apart from the leaders' 7. Only feud, claim and rite apply, each aimed at the notable's own story (`selectLocalAgendaTarget`): feud → the quarrel partner; claim → a Place the partner holds, else an unheld Place in the nearest other settlement; rite → their own settlement. The local scan runs after the leader scan on the same stream, so the leaders draw exactly what they drew before. A world flag (`agenda.<id>.local`) marks a local agenda; the launch trace carries `local` and `targetSource`, and the roster-scan trace carries the local counts.
- **Constants:** `NOTABLES_PER_SETTLEMENT` (per class, 1 each), `WORLDGEN_NOTABLE_QUARREL_MAX_HEXES` (8), `WORLDGEN_NOTABLE_TIE_MAX_HEXES` (12), `WORLDGEN_NOTABLE_MARK_MAGNITUDE` / `WORLDGEN_NOTABLE_FAVOR_MAGNITUDE` (0.5), `WORLDGEN_NOTABLE_HOLDING_PLACE_CLASSES`, `NOTABLE_ORIGIN_WORLDGEN`, `MAX_ACTIVE_LOCAL_AGENDAS`, `LOCAL_AGENDA_FAMILY_IDS`.
- **Executor call:** the plan's `mintLeverageMark(..., 'worldgen', ...)` put the favour writer's `context` word in the `secretType` slot. The seeded secret uses the undertaking default `'indiscretion'`, the type every mark reader already knows.
