# 2026-10-05 — THR-1730: a minimised moment waits for its god

**Ticket:** [THR-1730](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1730) · **Project:** Encounter Experience · **Pillars:** Engine (per-step hold, Phase 1 skip) · Content (`ui.encounter_waiting`, rulebook §3 sentence) · UI (minimise sets, commit/dismiss clear, badge "waiting for you", `__DEBUG.getPlayerHolds()`).

## Why

THR-1724 made Escape / "Show on map" minimise the encounter veil instead of deciding it. The engine still advanced the step, so a set-down pause-tier step played out on the mortal's terms once the clock ran. That was a second, accidental "let fate decide", against Christian's approved THR-1724 text and the THR-1715 promise that The First's story chapters stop the world.

## What shipped

- **Engine:** `UnifiedAction.playerHold?: { stepIndex, sinceTick }` and the new pure module `src/engine/playerStepHold.ts`. Phase 1 of `phaseUnifiedActionProgress` now runs `progressActionsWithPlayerHolds`. A live hold is passed through un-progressed. A dead hold (`step_changed`, `thread_not_pause`, `resolved`, `max_ticks` or `disabled`) is released, traced, and progressed on the same tick. `advanceStep` and `completeUnifiedAction` strip the hold, so it never carries into the next step. Two constants are added: `PLAYER_HOLD_ENABLED` (a kill switch) and `PLAYER_HOLD_MAX_TICKS = Infinity`.
- **UI:** `handleEncounterMinimize` writes the hold, but only for a pause-mode (`autoResolveTick === null`) unified-action encounter notification. The nudge commit (cards or an empty hand), the authored-choice commit and the dismiss path all release it. A held step's thread badge reads "… — waiting for you" and adds the registered `ui.encounter_waiting` tooltip sentence.
- **Traces:** `encounter.player_hold_set` and `encounter.player_hold_released` (with a reason).
- **Debug:** `window.__DEBUG.getPlayerHolds()`.
- **Canon:** the rulebook §3 clock paragraph and the quick-reference clock line each gain the `[IMPL — THR-1730]` sentence. The interface contract `minimised-step-hold` is added, along with a wiring-checklist section.
- **Out of scope:** legacy `encounterProgress` notifications and auto-mode notifications keep today's play-out behaviour. A held action is not "completing", so an action contesting it resolves uncontested.

## Verification

Browser check (worktree build, 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium&spawn=encounter.slice.unsafe_bridge`):
1. Escape, then `tick(10)`: `getPlayerHolds()` returned `live: true` with `heldTicks: 10`, and the badge read "The Unsafe Bridge, step 1 — waiting for you".
2. Reopened the badge and played "Stay silent, let fate answer". Holds were then empty. After `tick(10)` the badge read "The Unsafe Bridge concluded — open aftermath".

Tests: 16 unit tests in `playerStepHold.test.ts` (including a phase test) and 3 badge-model tests. `npm run gate` passed.
