The first click still only looks closer and the second chooses (UI Law 48).

**Keyboard.** Tab looks closer at a portrait, the same as a first click; Enter chooses. Only a Tab press counts as intent: the shell focuses the first portrait on mount, and a mouse press focuses the button before its click lands, so plain focus would have looked closer by itself (seen in the browser, then fixed).

**Layout.** The third portrait's rest position moved from 18vh to 11vh so its name line ends at ~1012px on a 1080px viewport (Law 33). The hint sits under the opening line, not under the cluster: the cluster's foot is near the fold. The resting line is drawn outside the dimmed portrait so it reads at full contrast. Two of the 24 portraits (eye-scout, heart-counselor) paint a title at their foot; the stale "every portrait paints its title" comment now says so.
