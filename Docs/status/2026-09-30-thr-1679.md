## Decisions made in-lane (veto welcome)

- **Reaches: eye ×2, iron ×1, shadow, star, veil**, not the ticket's literal "iron ×2". Plan D3 counts iron's existing expert toward its floor; a second iron would have taken it to 3 while six reaches sat at 1. The three flex slots went to the reaches at 1 with the thinnest band below.
- **The oath rite's decline arm is priced at expert odds** (veil 0.62); no exit in an expert-only batch can be cheap in its odds.
- **The toll gate's Sentinel arm tests Heart** on an Eye-first mortal (forecast *perilous* unaided), kept for reach variance.
- **`encounter.black_market_deal` now declares `drawable: true`**: the pawned box seeds it, and every seed target must say whether it stands alone on the board (THR-1526). It does.
