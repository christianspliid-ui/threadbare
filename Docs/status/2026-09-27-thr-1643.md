# 2026-09-27 — THR-1643: a threaded mortal is seen by the attention system

**Ticket:** [THR-1643](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1643) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Found by:** onboarding design session · **Pillars:** Engine + Content (template data; UI: N/A — the map already drew these threads as `watched`).

## What was wrong

`bind_thread_agent` ("Agent Thread") and `bind_thread_agent_strong` wrote the `thread` edge with `courtPosition: null`. `resolveEffectiveTier` and `phaseAttention` both read a missing position as "skip", so every encounter a player-threaded mortal walked into resolved `invisible` and could never become a tug — the essence bought nothing, while the map drew the same thread as `watched`.

## What shipped

- Both templates now write `courtPosition: 'watched'` — the attention model's entry tier, matching the map and spine Beat 0's "Watch this one".
- **Why `watched`, not `retinue`:** the reader audit found no reader that treats `watched` as invisible. `TIER_MATRIX`, `VISIBILITY_BY_POSITION`, the curator and the attention-cost table all carry a `watched` row. Two readers deliberately give `watched` *less* than `retinue`: premonition compulsion (the First and retinue only), and default-follow in `followedAgents.ts`, where a watched thread is still explicitly followable. That is the tier working as designed, not a skip.
- `peopleThingsCells.test.ts`: its two fresh-world cases get an explicit `FRESH_WORLD_CASE_TIMEOUT_MS` (60 s). This is the heavy-lane timeout flake's fifth recurrence (impediment rows 1073/1081/1084/1088 and this run).

## Evidence

- `src/engine/__tests__/bindThreadAgentCourtPosition.thr1643.test.ts` runs both templates' real ops through `executeGraphOps`. It asserts `courtPosition === 'watched'` and that no intrinsic tier resolves `invisible`.
- CLI, seed 42 medium: 40 mortals threaded with the template's edge properties, then `tick 30`. With `null`: 29 of 30 of their actions `invisible`, 0 tugs. With `watched`: 25 `background`, 4 `shaping`, 3 active tugs.
