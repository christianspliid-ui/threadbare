# 2026-09-27 — THR-1570: a masterwork is made with an idea

**Ticket:** [THR-1570](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1570) · **Project:** Powers & Item Generation · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-26-thr-1570-seeded-item-generator.md` · **Pillars:** Engine, Content, UI.

## What was wrong

A mortal who finished a masterwork made an object that did nothing: `mintMasterwork` wrote `effects: []`, a stock name and *"Made well, by someone who meant it."* The world then fought over these things (rivals seize them) although mechanically they were nothing. Every masterwork was also carved as a reward *template*, so a reward draw could hand a stranger a clone of somebody's masterwork.

## What shipped

- **The item generator** (`src/engine/itemGenerator/`): `generateItem` grows an item around an authored trope core, drawing every world table on its own seeded stream (`drawFromTable`, lifted to `src/lib/drawTable.ts`). `validateGeneratedItem` is the honest-vocabulary gate. `describeItem` gives the words. `mintGeneratedItem` is the one writer. `buildItemWorldContext` reads the maker, their faction, place and culture from the live graph.
- **Nineteen trope cores** (`src/data/item-generator-cores.ts`), ported from the THR-1236 prototype, each with **two signatures** that do different things. Eleven can be a masterwork and tell their maker; eight are found things. The two Mundane-only cores are dropped.
- **`mintMasterwork` makes a `gen_masterwork_*` item** when given the world seed: Storied, or Mythic on a `critical_success` finish, never Legendary. Both mint paths pass the seed and the band. Christening leaves a generated item's name alone and reports it.
- **Instances are not templates.** A node with `craftedBy` or `origin: 'generated'` is no longer carved as an `item_template`.
- **Storied at birth.** `assignArtifactTrait` takes an optional starting level. The UL **Rarity Band** entry carries the *Storied* ruling.
- **The artifact sheet** shows *Made by* (a link to the maker), *What it does* and *The catch* for generated items, in words.
- **Review path:** CLI `generate items [N] [--band] [--origin] [--seed] [--live]`; `__DEBUG.getGeneratedItems`, `previewGeneratedItem`, `mintGeneratedItem`.

## Result

- Gate test: seeds 7/42/99 × bands × origins × 12 items, plus every signature of every core at every legal band: **zero validator problems, zero read-back failures, zero rerolls, all 19 cores fire**. All eight dishonest control items fail.
- Seed 99 medium: four generated masterworks by tick 150 (*Bell of the Unburied*, *Solara's Staff*, *The Soft Picks*, *The Last Almanac*). Seed 42 made none by 150 (two were still in progress) and two by tick 220 (*Wardhorn*, *Emberfind*). Zero fallbacks on both.
- Generator on vs off, seed 99 at tick 62 (just after the first mint): the world fingerprint outside the masterwork itself is identical. The generator shifts no other roll.

## Deferred

- [THR-1637](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1637) — the live world context reads the past (dead notables, events, monster hosts) for found things.
- [THR-1626](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1626) — reward draws carry generated items at the band share.

## Note on the plan's Done-when

"`ITEM_GEN_MASTERWORK_ENABLED = false` reproduces today's run exactly" holds for everything the generator touches, but the carve fix is independent of the switch. With the switch off, a plain masterwork is still no longer dealt as a reward template. That is the leak fix the plan asked for, not a regression.
