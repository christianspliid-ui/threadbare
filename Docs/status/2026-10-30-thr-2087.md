# THR-2087 — A hex card grows the sphere of the place it falls on

**Shipped 2026-10-30.** Thematic Pressure & Living World. A hex card targets the synthetic `hex_COL_ROW`, which no graph node carries, so `phaseSpherePressure` dropped every hex-aimed push as an unknown entity. Seed Life, Bestow Vision and the other sphere-tagged hex cards grew no sphere anywhere, and the Mandate never heard of them.

**Fix** (plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-28-thr-2087-hex-sphere-pressure.md`):

- New pure `src/engine/hexPressureTarget.ts` → `resolveHexPressureTargets`, called at the head of `phaseSpherePressure` (try/catch falls back to today's list). A hex push lands at full strength on the lowest-id place on the hex, else the nearest place within `HEX_PRESSURE_FALLBACK_RADIUS` (2, `src/types/sphereAffinity.ts`), ties by id, else it is dropped. Each case leaves a `sphere_pressure.hex_resolved` or `sphere_pressure.hex_unanchored` trace (`src/types/traces/sphere-traces.ts`).
- The four energy-priced flows cards (`hex.attune_leyline`, `hex.amplify_flow`, `hex.anchor_sphere`, `hex.channel_current`) now carry `sphereAffinity: 'energy'`. Their price does not change. Their technical lines, plus Seed Life's and Bestow Vision's, name the push.

**Executor notes.** THR-2081 (god-caused Mandate) and THR-2086 (energy cards) had both merged, so the mutexes were moot. The resolver copies each event whole, so the Mandate's `causedBy` rides through to the place. Because pushes are rewritten before grouping, the Mandate ledger credits the place at its aggregate weight. No per-tick place-list cache: the list is built once per call, and only on ticks that carry a hex push.

**Evidence.** `src/engine/__tests__/hexPressureTarget.test.ts` has 12 tests: lower id on a two-place hex at full magnitude; an empty hex lands on the nearest place within 2, ties to the lower id; beyond 2 it is dropped with a trace; a non-hex push comes back as the same object; sublocations are ignored. Through `phaseSpherePressure`: Seed Life moves life on the hex's place and on the nearest place from an empty hex; Bestow Vision moves mind; each flows card carries energy and lands energy on a place. `npm run gate`: PASS (test, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, cli-smoke).

**Not done here.** Sustained hex controls still leave `targetNodeId` unset, so their per-tick hold pressure does not fire (plan § Not in this plan). The kill-criteria 600-tick reading was not run.
