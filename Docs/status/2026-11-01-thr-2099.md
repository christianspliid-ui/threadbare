# THR-2099 — the art-led god rail and top bar (slice 3)

**Shipped 2026-11-01.** UI Visual Overhaul — Design System v1. This slice dresses the chrome that is always on screen, the god rail and the top bar, and adds nothing new to either (Law 53). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-11-01-thr-2099-art-led-god-rail.md`.

**What changed.**
- Rail headings draw their carved shapes from a new required `icon` column on `GOD_RAIL_SECTIONS`: Essence, Your cards, Mandate, Signatures and Hooks. Reaches and Covenants have no shape yet; they are listed in `GOD_RAIL_NO_IDEA_ROWS` and wait for THR-2104.
- Named mortals show their face, all through one `mortalFace` → `resolveEntityVisual` call, gated by what the god knows of them as their page header is: a stranger draws the silhouette (Law 8, PC-6):
  - The First is an identity chip on the Your First line.
  - A vow with a mortal counterparty leads with that mortal's face.
  - A Needs-you row that points at a mortal shows the face after the row's icon.
- The Seat line draws its town's place shape. Clicking it still centres the map.
- Reaches' empty line sits in a `QuietSlot`.
- The doom bar opens with the `doom` shape. The omen draws no second icon.

**Executor decisions.** Covenants show no face. The plan asked for one on a covenant held on a mortal, but the review gate confirmed none can exist: `spawnControlEffect` (`controlEffectSpawn.ts`) refuses any target that is not a place or a land, so the path was dropped rather than shipped as a reader with no writer.

**Open-row cap.** An opened folded row's body is now capped at `GOD_RAIL_OPEN_ROW_MAX_VH` (30vh) and scrolls inside itself. Opening Hooks on the seeded world already ran 8px past the fold at 1920×1080. With faces on its four vows it ran 46px past, because a faced vow chip no longer shares a line. With the cap, the rail ends at y=1066. The vow chips themselves keep their 32px height.

**Evidence.** `railDressingThr2099.test.tsx` has 11 tests:
- the no-idea registry walk
- faces on a mortal and none on a place, a faction, the god or a missing node
- a stranger's face is the silhouette even when their portrait exists
- vow faces, and none on a covenant (it is held on a place)
- the Seat shape and its click
- the doom and omen icons
- Needs-you faces for agent and journey targets, none for a hex or a stale id

`godRail.test.ts` pins The First's face. `npm run gate` passes.
