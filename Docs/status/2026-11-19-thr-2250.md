# THR-2250 — The Twilight says what the Mandate came to

**Shipped 2026-11-19.** Ticket 3 of 3 of `Docs/plans/2026-11-19-thr-2245-a-well-played-age-can-be-won.md` (Decision 6).

- **Engine.** `src/engine/twilight.ts`: `AgeEnding` gains an optional `reckon` line. A Mandate ending names the entity with the largest `godHeld` share (`topGodHeldEntity`: summed over both Mandate spheres, ties on the id) through `mandateWhere`: a place reads "Most of it was raised at {place}."; a mortal (an `actor` with `actorType: 'individual'`) reads "Most of it was raised through {mortal}'s chapters." A gone place drops the line; a gone mortal keeps the name its deed record captured, else "the chapters of a mortal bound to you". A Doom ending reads `mandateState.progress` into "{Mandate} stood within reach / stood half-raised / had barely begun." No Mandate state, no line. Fallback rows go to `twilight.beats_built.fallbacks`.
- **Content.** `src/data/twilight-content.ts`: `TWILIGHT_MANDATE_WITHIN_REACH` (0.75), `TWILIGHT_MANDATE_HALF_RAISED` (0.40), `mandateWherePlace`, `mandateWhereMortal`, `doomMandateReckon`.
- **UI.** `AgeTwilightScreen.tsx` renders the reckon line under the ending line, same type step: the named place or mortal is an `EntityLink`; a Doom ending's Mandate name carries the `ui.mandate` tooltip. No layout change.
- **Debug.** `__DEBUG.setMandateProgress(fraction, heldAt?)` writes the god ledger to that share of both targets (the configured targets before the settle), the progress, and `completed` at 1; `heldAt` puts the whole ledger on one entity so the Mandate ending has something to name.
- **Contract.** `mandate-ledger-reckons-twilight` (scripts/interface-contracts.ts + interface map).

(linked); 0.8 → "Witness Ascendancy stood within reach."; 0.2 → "Witness Ascendancy had barely begun."; `getTwilightBeats().ending.reckon` matched the DOM each time; 0 console errors; no digits on the ceremony; panel inside the viewport.
