# 2026-09-28 — THR-1631 S1: the world starts with a past (the past on the graph)

**Ticket:** [THR-1631](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1631) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-28-thr-1631-world-with-a-past.md` § S1 · **Pillars:** Engine; Content N/A for S1 (the facts carry no prose, and the line tables are S2); UI N/A for S1 (the chronicle section and page lines are S2, [THR-1656](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1656)).

## What was wrong

A new world held two to four dead empires and about a hundred of their ruins, and said nothing about any of them. No settlement had a founding date, there were no wars before t0 and no dead. `backstoryStrata`, which clue scoring reads to favour a ruin's descendants, had no writer at all.

## What shipped

- **`src/engine/worldPast.ts`, `seedWorldPast`.** Called once from `initializeGameState` after latent sources and before `loc.start`, inside a try/catch, on its own stream (`mulberry32(seed + 101363)`) with a fixed draw order. It writes graph only:
  - **An elder war** (`past_elder_war`) between the two empires with the most battlefield ruins. It carries a generated name (`pastName`, e.g. "the Breaking") and an `occurred_at` edge to each of those battlefields.
  - **A founding age** (`foundedYearsAgo`) on every settlement and plain ruin, always younger than the elder war. Each Realm's seat is raised to be its Realm's oldest place, and gets a **named dead founder** through `constructed_by { structureType: 'founding' }`. That edge carries no `tick`, so Builder's Legacy does not count it.
  - **Two or three wars in living memory** (`past_war`) between neighbouring Realms (`realmNeighbourPairs`, 8 hexes). The winner and loser live on the Realms' `participated_in` edges (`role`). Each war has a fallen commander (dead, `member_of` the losing Realm at rank 0.6) and, where a plain ruin lies within 10 hexes of the seats' midpoint, a burned town.
  - **Descent** on about 25% of mortals whose home region an empire held: `backstoryStrata: [{ cultureId, relation: 'descent' }]` plus `originCultureId`. The new type `WorldPastDescentStratum` replaces the inline cast in `clueLifecycle`.
  - **The dead**, capped at ten: commanders, then founders, then up to four wonder finders. They are written in exactly the run-time `retain` shape (`deceased`, a negative `deceasedTick`, `deathCause`), plus `pastRole` / `pastOrigin: 'worldgen'`, at the ambient tier.
- **Readers.** `readWorldPast` (the whole view), `getPlacePast` (one place; `fellInEventId` only where an `occurred_at` backs it), `isSeededDead`, and `window.__DEBUG.getWorldPast()`. The trace `world_past_seeded` records every miss by id, and a console line reports it at worldgen.
- **`pastSpanLabel(years)`** in `aftermathWords.ts`: "nine years", "two decades", "about five centuries", "many centuries", never a numeral.
- **Four readers now skip the dead** (the kill criterion's "fix the reader, not the dead"): `graphQueries.getAgentsAtLocation`, `hexZoom.getAgentsAtLocation`, `buildHexActorIndex` and `routeEvents.pickTargetAgent`. The 200-tick census on seed 99 caught a seeded founder being handed a scarcity quest (`mct.quest.settle_dispute`) from tick 27. The run-time dead had the same leak: they were counted by the birth-density gate, heard rumours and joined mentorship and strategic pools.
- Three interface rows: `world-past-reaches-the-chronicle` (PARTIAL until THR-1656), `world-past-descent-feeds-clue-scoring` and `seeded-dead-stay-dead`. Also the census reader `Docs/audits/2026-09-25-living-world-data/readers/past.ts` and wiring-guide capability 36.

## Tick cost (kill line: +4%), same session, sequential, 200 ticks, steady state

- Seed 42: 180.7 / 178.7 → 198.0 / 199.1 ms/tick (**+10.5%**).
- Seed 99: 219.5 / 220.0 → 218.4 / 220.6 ms/tick (0%).
- Two more seeds: 7 goes 226.7 → 229.4 (+1.2%) and 13 goes 203.8 → 196.7 (−3.5%). The mean over four seeds is about +2%.

**Verdict: seed 42 changes its course of play; the pass adds no per-tick cost.** I isolated it by ablation on seed 42. With the dead off, or the events off, cost returns to 181 ms. With the full pass, the world fires 125 more `encounter_outcome` events by t200 (2009 → 2134), about +6% encounters. Dropping only the fallen commanders' `member_of` edge returns it to baseline (186 ms, 2034 events). So one dead Realm member shifts a membership-reading draw, and seed 42 plays out a busier game. The other three seeds carry the same writes at no cost. This is logged in the impediment log. The member_of readers that do not skip the dead are a pre-existing leak shared with the run-time dead, and they are not fixed here.
