# 2026-09-25 — THR-1587: action-drawer cards get a solid body

**Ticket:** [THR-1587](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1587) · **Project:** Encounter Experience · **Pillars:** UI (`shared/CardFace`) · Engine N/A (no state) · Content N/A (strings unchanged).

## What was wrong

The card face's background was a 2% white tint, carried over when THR-1002 extracted the nudge card's face into `shared/CardFace`. The nudge hand always sits on the encounter stage's opaque backdrop, so there the tint looked like a surface. The ActionDrawer is a fixed strip with no backdrop of its own, so the tint let the map, the location page, and whatever else was behind it show through every card body.

## What shipped

- **An opaque floor under the tint.** `background: linear-gradient(tint, tint), var(--bg-surface)`. The selected gold tint and the unselected 2% tint are unchanged and now sit on `--bg-surface`. A gradient does not interpolate, so the tint snaps on arm. The gold border and glow still animate.
- **Dimmed cards darken rather than fade** (found while fixing this). `opacity: 0.45` made a blocked card 55% see-through again. It is now `filter: brightness(0.5) saturate(0.6)` (`CARD_DIMMED_FILTER`), so the card stays opaque and still reads as dimmed.
- The nudge card snapshots moved on purpose. The only change in them is the background shorthand and the dimmed filter.

## Evidence

`Docs/evidence/thr-1587/` has the drawer open over the map with the location page up, the armed card, and the nudge hand (`?spawn=encounter.slice.unsafe_bridge`), all at 1920×1080. `getComputedStyle` on `action-card-*` gives `backgroundColor rgb(26, 26, 31)` (alpha 1) under the tint gradient.
