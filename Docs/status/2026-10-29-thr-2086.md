# THR-2086 — Energy gets cards a god can cast

**Shipped 2026-10-29.** Thematic Pressure & Living World. None of the 141 ascendant-castable cards carried `sphereAffinity: 'energy'`. So a Kindle, Wander or Illuminate god earned energy essence and had no card that grew energy. `check:god-card-progression` warned `hole: no energy market card at tier 1`.

**Two tier-1 market cards on existing ops** (plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-28-thr-2086-energy-cards.md`):

- **Stoke the Forges** (`loc.stoke_forges`, spell *The Banked Coal Wakes*, a town, 3 essence). Prosperity +`LOC_STOKE_FORGES_PROSPERITY_DELTA` (6), unrest +`LOC_STOKE_FORGES_UNREST_DELTA` (5). The +6 crosses `ECON_SHOCK_DELTA`, so the boom plants scenes on the town's mortals.
- **Kindle a Spark** (`divine.kindle_spark`, spell *The Struck Match*, a mortal, `KINDLE_SPARK_ESSENCE_COST` 2). An energy `inspire_intervention` influence on Inspire's decay curve, with an `own_lean` drift of `KINDLE_SPARK_VALUE_DRIFT` (0.12). That sits between Inspire (0.10) and the compulsion (0.15). A mortal at 0 on the pair holds nothing and the `influence.no_lean` trace fires.

Both push energy pressure on their target through the existing resolution push. `pricedIn` is unset, so both are priced in energy. Two card images are in `public/assets/actions/`.

**Executor decisions.** THR-2088 merged first. It gave Inspire its own drift and its own `inspire_intervention` receipt lines, so plan item 3 (widen the receipt key, author Kindle's lines) was already covered. Kindle a Spark shares Inspire's intervention type, as the plan intended for the "Inspired" chip. It therefore reads Inspire's receipt lines ("A sudden purpose kindles in {target}…"), which fit a spark. No `receipt-content.ts` or `playerReceipts.ts` change. Stoke's toast is the template's success line through the existing overview path; not re-captured in a live cast. The interface contract text for cast value drift now names `divine.kindle_spark`.
