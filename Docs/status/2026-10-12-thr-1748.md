# 2026-10-12 — THR-1748: Dominion core — how much of the world is the god's

**Ticket:** [THR-1748](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1748). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-11-thr-1748-dominion-core.md`. Engine, Content and the debug half of the UI. The map, sheet and card chip are THR-1750.

- **The read.** `src/engine/dominion.ts` holds one pure read, `dominionOf(graph, ascendantId, objectId)`. It takes the object's sphere scores against the god's bought points, with each opposite pole weighted by its pair strength. The god's power widens friendly ground only, capped at 2.0. The result is one of five bands: Hostile, Foreign, Touched, Held or Sovereign. It reads places, sublocations, mortals, cultures and factions (on the unrounded member mean), armies (through their faction), companies, artifacts (through their bearer) and hexes (through the terrain table, read-only). Every missing input reads Foreign. All constants, band words and lines live in `src/data/dominion-content.ts`.
- **Consumers.**
  - Card cost: the preview (`getTargetActionSlots`) and the charge (`preparePlayerCast`) use the same pieces, so preview equals charge.
  - Cast odds: the player-only `sphereFactor` feeds the resolver and the card forecast.
  - Both cost and odds ship behind `DOMINION_PRICES_LIVE = false`. THR-1750 turns them on with its card chip.
  - Live now: thread yield is tier × band (0.1 Unaware, then 0.15 / 0.30 / 0.45 / 0.60). Source income is × host band, and a Hostile host pays nothing.
- **Writers.** `phaseDominion` runs after control effects. It applies Claim, seat and threaded-mortal pushes (carry is capped per place; a bestowed gift is worth one tier), and neglect. Neglect builds a debt against the current level, so an untended place drops a point only when the debt reaches that level's whole cost. `afterSpherePressure` keeps the seat at Touched or better, traces band changes, and once a day writes the chronicle lines.
- **Other changes.**
  - Claim and Shift Dominion now target places, with the same ids. Claim's upkeep comes from the primary sphere, and Shift breaks the opposing pole.
  - Other holds push at 0.03 a tick (was 1).
  - Rivals take the opposites of the buy. The probe lands on real turf at 4, throttled, and a tended place absorbs one burst.
  - Attunement marks raise the god's own sphere score.
  - Faction aggregates now exclude the dead and run at init.
- **Debug.** `__DEBUG.getDominion`, `getDominionCensus` and `getGodPower`, plus a Dominion row in the Sphere State tab.
- **Finding (§ Decided 6).** THR-1747's divine economy ledger Arm C asserted that two tier-4 threads with no ground go unpaid. Under the decided Model B yields that is no longer true: they pay their own way on Foreign ground. The arm now pins that Hostile ground costs income instead. No constant was retuned.
