# THR-1085 — action cards stop naming the graph and the backlog

**Shipped 2026-08-13.** Two `UnifiedActionTemplate` descriptions spoke to the player in our vocabulary rather than the world's. `divine.relay.compose_a_clue` said the agent "gains a `knows_clue_of` edge" — the graph shape, not the knowledge. `loc.awaken_spirit` was worse than a Law 14 breach: past the raw `place_spirit` identifier it narrated its own backlog, *"**v1** raises divine presence sharply and marks the place; spawning the embodied place_spirit actor **is filed as a follow-up**"*. A player reading an action card was handed a release note.

These render on the action card and in the Codex. This is the third surface in one chain — THR-1051 fixed the aftermath chip, THR-1075 the technical-effect map, and both scoped narrowly enough that the next surface had to be filed separately. THR-1085's own body says as much, and asked for a predicate sweep rather than its two-item list.

## The sweep found three, not two

| Template | Field | Leak |
|---|---|---|
| `divine.relay.compose_a_clue` | `description` | `knows_clue_of` — a graph edge type |
| `loc.awaken_spirit` | `description` | `place_spirit` + `v1` + `is filed as a follow-up` |
| `action.faction.recover_doctrine` | `description` | `recovered_doctrine` — a clue type id |

The third is the return on writing the predicate before the fix, and it is the same shape as the other two: *"A **recovered_doctrine** clue you gathered from delving the ruins now lands inside the faction that lost it."* The sentence before it already says "a forgotten teaching surfaces" — the prose had the world word and reached for the key anyway.

Per the ticket's fix shape, `awaken_spirit`'s backlog note is **deleted rather than rephrased**. What replaces it describes what the action actually does — the `divinePresence` delta and the mark — in world terms: *"Your presence settles hard into the streets, and the ground keeps the mark of it."* No renderer-side scrub was added; per Law 14 the vocabulary resolves keys and the surface prints what the template holds (THR-1051's ruling, reaffirmed here).

## The predicate had to be narrowed twice, and both narrowings are the finding

Run literally, the ticket's predicate — *"a graph edge type, node/actor type id, effect kind, or bare `snake_case` enum"* plus *"`deferred` or similar backlog language"* — reports **five false positives**, and each one teaches something about writing this class of gate.

**Enrichment syntax is not a leak.** `{?has_faction}…{/has_faction}`, `{cast:reference_contact}`, `{name}` — these carry `snake_case` by design and the player never sees a character of them, because `enrichProse()` resolves the whole span first (systemic wiring guide, Capability 1). Four of the five hits were this. The guard **strips** placeholder spans before scanning rather than exempting the hits afterwards, because a genuine leak can sit *inside* an enriched string, right next to a legitimate placeholder — and an after-the-fact exemption list would swallow it.

**The world says "deferred" too.** `uk.elite.shadow_coup`'s failure prose reads *"What is owed is deferred — not forgiven."* That is a good sentence about a debt. A flat backlog lexicon flags it, and the next reader who sees this gate fire on obviously-correct prose learns to silence the gate. So `DEV_STATUS` holds only phrasings that can *only* be about our progress: version markers, `TODO`, `as a follow-up`, `placeholder`, `unimplemented`, `not yet wired`. Bare "not yet" and "for now" are **not** members — the catalog uses both in good world prose (*"the merchants have not yet learned to listen"*).

This is the same lesson THR-899 recorded for the vagueness detectors, on a different detector family: the flat version damaged the prose it was protecting. Scope the lexicon or it costs more than it catches.

## What is deliberately not swept

`technicalEffect` is excluded. It is the design-reference field `public/action-catalog.html` renders **for us**, and naming constants and graph-ops is its entire job — `bless_company`, `BLESS_COMPANY_DURATION_TICKS`, `draw_together` all appear there correctly. THR-1085's predicate names the player-facing fields only. Sweeping it would report ~15 entries doing exactly what they should, which is the fastest way to get a gate ignored.

## Guard

`src/data/__tests__/action-template-player-vocabulary.test.ts` — four tests over every player-facing string in the catalog (`description`, `name`, `spellName`, `narrativeTemplates.{success,failure,initiation}`, `controlSpec.narrativeTemplates.{established,active,lapsed,destroyed,usurped}`).

Two of the four exist to stop the guard passing vacuously, which is the failure mode a predicate sweep is most prone to:

- **Population guard** — asserts >400 strings across >100 templates. A predicate that finds nothing because it collected nothing is not a green gate.
- **Strip falsification** — plants `{name} gains a knows_clue_of edge in {location}.` and asserts the stripper still surfaces `knows_clue_of`. If `ENRICHMENT_SYNTAX` ever widened to swallow whole strings, both leak assertions would pass on any content at all, and nothing else in the file would notice.

**The guard is catalog-wide, which is wider than the ticket's Done-when.** THR-1085 scopes to `src/data/unified-action-templates.ts`; the test imports `UNIFIED_ACTION_TEMPLATES`, which aggregates the faction and encounter content files too. Those turned out to be **clean** under the corrected predicate, so the wider scope cost nothing and is worth more — it closes the "class spans surfaces" gap the ticket itself flagged, instead of filing a fourth ticket for the next surface to discover the same way. That also discharges the ticket's instruction that whoever runs second re-run THR-1075's predicate: the dev-status arm covers the same ground and found only `awaken_spirit`.
