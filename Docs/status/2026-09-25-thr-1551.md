# 2026-09-25 — THR-1551: the opponent header (Fight on screen F2)

**Ticket:** [THR-1551](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1551) · **Project:** Physical Conflict · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-23-fight-on-screen.md` (slice F2) · **Pillars:** UI (the header, the watched line, StepDots, the navigator) · Content (clock words, card phrases, step titles, `fight.*` tooltips) · Engine N/A (reads only).

## What was wrong

A fight rendered as four unlabelled encounter steps. The player could not see who the mortal was facing or how close it was to falling. The step whisper said "Moderate threat" next to the card's own difficulty, which is two magnitude languages for one quantity (Law 10).

## What shipped

- **`buildOpponentHeaderModel(state, action, template)`** (new, `encounter-stage/adapters/`). It builds on fight steps only. The opponent is `fightState.opponentId`, or before that the step's `opponentRef` through the cast, else the target, using the same `resolveFightOpponent` as the roll. So on `monster.hunt.named_elite` the header names the beast, not the lair. The clock reads `fightState.clockNow`; before the fight exists it reads the card's recovered clock, which is the value FB2 records as `clockAtStart`.
- **`OpponentHeader`** (new) sits under `ContextStrip` in one wrapping row: the chip portrait, the step title, the name as a link, one card sentence ("A walking rot that spreads where it goes. Fearsome to face, a fair match."), square clock pips at 14 px and the clock word. A duel shows both clocks.
- **Law 33 in practice:** the first draft was a stacked block (~125 px). At 1920×1080 it pushed the hand under the footer, which is the plan's kill criterion. The single row costs ~48 px, and the hand and "Let fate decide" stay fully visible with no scroll.
- **The threat whisper is gone on fight steps** in the full veil and in the watched view. The watched view shows one line instead: "Facing Ryx — bloodied". GameView now passes the live unified action to the watched fallback adapter. `threatLabel` is optional.
- **Step titles:** fight steps are "Facing it", "First exchange" … in the header, the step-navigator dots and the replay (`history.stepTitle`).
- **`StepDots`** gets `shape` (`square`) and `ariaLabel`. Existing uses render exactly as before. A styleguide sample was added.
- **`fight.*` tooltips** (Dread, Might, Temper, the clock, each clock word) are routed in `tooltipResolver.ts`, and `fight.*` is added to Law 17's prefix list.
- **`window.__DEBUG.getOpponentHeaderModel(actionId)`** is async.

**Deviation from the plan:** the temper clause's tooltip is `fight.temper`, not `attachment.trait.temper.*`. The registry does not resolve those derived ids (a temper is not an attachment subcategory), and a dead id is a Law 21 link that explains nothing.
