## What shipped

**`has_trait` admits things.** The edge schema's sources gain `artifact` / `artifact_legendary`. `src/engine/artifactTraits.ts` is the one writer for those edges and holds the three rules in one place: a holding face (`holdings.ts` mints freeholds as `type: 'artifact'`, `attachmentCategory: 'holding'`) is never a bearer; only a `trait.artifact.*` definition goes on a thing; a missing definition is seeded on demand, so every older save and fixture graph is safe.

**Two state-carrying definitions, and only two.** *Storied* is stamped at level 1 by `mintMasterwork` and climbs in words every `ARTIFACT_STORIED_ENCOUNTERS_PER_LEVEL` encounter steps its bearer resolves carrying it — on both growth roads (`encounter.ts`, `unifiedActionResolution.ts`). Presence never mints the trait. *Cursed* is the readable form of THR-661's untyped `properties.cursed`: `curse_artifact` writes the edge beside the flag, `nullify_artifact` removes it, the monster return mirrors it — so `resolveTraitPredicate` now sees a cursed blade by tag, id or name, where the flag alone satisfied no predicate. `#masterwork` / `#heirloom` / `#stolen` stay family tags.

**The artifact sheet shows them.** A Traits chip row — name, level in words, polarity as sentiment, the definition's hover — appears only when the thing carries one. Tags now render through `contentTagChips`, the builder the attachment sheet shares, ending the two sheets' disagreement. The sheet takes `worldVersion`, so a trait stamped or climbed while it is open appears on the next render (the graph is mutated in place; the memoised sheet had kept painting what it read at open — found in this run's browser check, pinned by a falsifying rerender arm).

**Registry, UL, canon in one PR.** The `trait` world-object row's `via` is now a list (mortal, location, item) and the reachability pin walks each member; UL **Artifact Trait**; rulebook §4 *A thing remembers where it has been*; interface contract `artifact-traits-on-the-edge`; systemic wiring guide Capability 33. `artifactTraits.ts` homes under Personality & Emergent Traits in the subsystems registry.

**Inspectable.** `artifact_trait` traces on stamp / climb / removal; `window.__DEBUG.getArtifactTraits` / `stampArtifactTrait`; CLI `traits`; `?debug.openModal=artifact`.
