## What shipped

All 9 token-carrying `encounterType: 'steal'` templates authored out of the `{adj}`/`{verb}`/`{noun}`/`{action}` shape — 71 token lines. **The family is drained**, which is the predicate this campaign states in place of a count that rots.

`shadow_hunt` · `pickpocket` · `vault_heist` · `grave_robbery` · `smuggle_goods` · `steal_secrets` · `black_market_deal` · `the_fence` · `shadow_in_the_night`

Register: **theft is bookkeeping with consequences — a job is measured by what it leaves behind, and the bill is presented later.** A Thieves-Guild voice per the skill's own guide: short declarative sentences, risk counted in minutes and sightlines and who remembers a face. It covers a market lift, a counting-house vault, a barrow, a salt run, a tower's correspondence and a back-room purchase because each one is the same arithmetic at a different scale. Calibrated against `encounter.trial_by_combat`, the THR-1036 exemplar.

Predicate re-derived at pickup: **48** of 168 carrying, matching batch 8's stated figure exactly, family breakdown line-for-line. Seven consecutive runs of a stable scan.

`shadow_hunt` also carried the **retired lyrical register** alongside its tokens — *"glides through shadow like water"*, *"becomes shadow itself"*, *"vanish like smoke"*. That is the register rejected game-wide on 2026-07-30, and it went out with the tokens rather than being left beside authored prose. Worth noting as a second, independent reason a template can need this treatment: the token scan finds the mad-libs, and finds the lyrical residue only by accident of co-location.

## The ratio landed furthest to the mad-lib side yet, and confirms it has no trend

`steal` came in **6 full mad-libs to 3 wedged-token**, against `acquire` 8/5, `create` 6/7, `build` 7/9, `trade` 7/8, `assist` 6/9 and `lead` 5/9.

At 2:1 this is the most mad-lib-heavy family the campaign has seen — wider than `acquire`'s 8/5, which held that record. Seven families in, the spread now runs from `lead`'s 5/9 to `steal`'s 6/3, and batch 7's conclusion is the one that survives: **the ratio chooses between two candidate families and does not replace reading a sample.** It is a per-family property, cheap to measure, not worth extrapolating. Recorded here so that whoever scopes `explore` (30) reads a sample rather than assuming half.

The cost tracked the split as expected: the six mad-libs were full rewrites, the three wedged were surgical.

## Three pronoun/referent defects, all pre-existing, all found by rendering

Eighth consecutive run the render pass has paid for itself. Every line of all 9 templates was rendered through `enrichProse` — the function the game actually calls — in **two pronoun arms**, and read back.

- **`shadow_hunt.patience` carried the `{they}`-plus-bare-verb class** — `{They} move too soon` rendered *"she move too soon"*. The settled remedy since batch 3: drop the pronoun subject. `{s}` cannot fix it, and this instance is notable only for being invisible to the token scan — the line carried **no** `{adj}`/`{verb}` token at all, so nothing but the render pass would have surfaced it.
- **`steal_secrets.discovery` carried the actor-bound referent class** (impediment #563, occurrence 3) — *"interpret `{their}` meaning"*, where the referent is the secrets and the token renders the **actor's** pronoun. Batch 7 established the tell: any `{their}`/`{them}`/`{they}` whose nearest preceding noun is not the actor. Rewritten to name the referent with a noun.
- **`black_market_deal.purchase` carried `the situation`**, a *nominalised placeholder* in the **evasive** set — enforced at zero in every field class, scope-independent. Batch 6 met the same trap as `the moment`. It went out with the tokens.

**The durable point this batch adds:** two of those three sat in lines the token scan does not flag. A family's token count measures the rewrite cost, **not** the defect count — and the defects that survive a drained family are exactly the ones no scan can see. The render pass is not a quality nicety layered on the batch; on this family it was the only instrument that found anything.

## Vagueness 6 → 0, and half of it was inherited

Swept at the strictest applicable scope (`outcome`) across every touched line.

- **Three were this batch's own**, all `way` in outcome prose, all rephrased at no cost (*"by the way the frost sits"* → *"by its frost, which lies thinner…"*).
- **Three were pre-existing**, all in `shadow_in_the_night`'s two crit-band afterimages — lines this batch did not otherwise touch: `something` (evasive, every field class) plus `thing` and `someone` (natural indefinites, and an afterimage is outcome prose). Fixed rather than left beside authored prose, per the batch-5 rule that the pass covers every line a batch touches. *"a dropped thing"* became *"a dropped pick"*, which is also simply better — the vague noun was hiding a concrete one.

**One deliberate non-change:** `grave_robbery` retains *"The sound goes down into the ground rather than out of it"*. `rather` trips the intensifier substring in a **comparative** construction, not an intensifying one; intensifiers are warn-only in every field class and count toward no failure. Left, exactly as batches 4–7 ruled on the same construction.
