# 2026-09-13 — THR-1454: the Realm gets a court a mortal can be called to

THR-1155 gave the map a Realm the player could *see* and never *meet*: a faction minted
per world, a six-rung court ladder, a `$realm` sentinel, and — deliberately — no prose.
This lands the first content that spends it. Three encounters, one family, each a
different relationship a mortal can have with a crown: **The Crown's Summons** (the
Realm calls you), **The Border Levy** (it takes from you at its edge), **The Tithe
Demanded** (it is owed by you where you live). A mortal who meets all three has been
summoned, taxed on the road, and taxed at home, which is roughly what belonging to a
realm feels like from underneath.

All three are nudge-native and composed rather than fully authored: four authored
specials across the batch plus a declared `deal` fill on every nudge-bearing step, so
most of what the player is offered is cards they already hold. Every one carries a
setting envelope (`urban`+`stronghold`, `wayside`+`rural`, `rural`+`urban`), a cast
binding class-honest across both of its classes, and the family's spine —
`faction_reputation_gain` on `$realm`, positive on the success side and negative on the
failure side, so a crown that is displeased *withdraws* rather than jails.

**Registration is the whole of realm spawn scoping**, per the plan doc's ruling that
realm content is faction content: three class-scoped rows in `FACTION_ENCOUNTER_META`
with `factionClass: 'realm'`, which `metaBelongsToDefinitionId` resolves against every
per-world Realm. No new axis, no `?spawn=` special case.

**Gates.** `check:encounter` — 3 clean, 0 failing, **0 warnings**, five systems each
(cast · rewards · conditions · reputation · factions), one over the contract's floor.
`check:encounter-live` — **3 proved, 0 vacuous**. 20,310 tests pass; typecheck ratchet
unchanged at 2872; heavy lane 212 pass; build clean.

**The ticket's third Done-when is about a sheet, so the proof ends at a string, not at
the engine.** `realm-court-family.test.ts` walks the chain the game walks — the template
authors `faction_reputation_gain` naming `$realm`; the sentinel is legal on `factionId`
and refused on an agent field; the real binder resolves it on a *generated* world to the
Realm whose projection claims the actor's hex; applying it moves a rung; and
`getFactionNetworkSummary` — the one producer of the `rankLabel` the faction sheet's
member row draws — reports the new court word. Two controls: the sentinel is refused on
a field of the wrong kind, and survives unbound when no projection is supplied. Ending
at the engine would have been the two-halves-each-green failure this project keeps
meeting, because `rankLabel` falls back to the edge's stored `role`, so an unreachable
ladder renders a plausible word.

**One vocabulary decision, flagged for veto.** The family needed a word, and the family
axis carries one `#<body>_errand` tag per work-setting faction. `#court_errand` is the
Underking's, so the Realm got `#crown_errand` — the thirteenth, seated on the bearer arm
with three bearers the day it landed, recorded in `Docs/canon/content-objects.md`.
Seating a tag is nominally a design-session act; the alternative was `#court`, which is
honest for the summons and wrong for the levy and the tithe, and a family word that fits
a third of its family is the rot the errand axis exists to prevent.

**Three things found on the way**, all logged as impediment rows rather than tickets per
the process-work throttle: `compile:encounter` emitted a generated test that did not
parse because the encounter's name contains an apostrophe (#1034, **fixed in this PR** —
the `describe` title is `JSON.stringify`d now); `trait.condition.debt-laden` is fully
authored and grantable by nothing (#1035); and `assign_ambition` accepts only one of the
three shipped ambition catalogs (#1036). The last two are the same shape — the id exists,
in the wrong array, and only a gate will tell you.

**Deferral:** [THR-1499](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1499) — `$realm` binds
effect fields but is not a chip anchor, so nine standing chips report a move they cannot
link. Sibling of THR-1462, which is the same gap for `$here`.

**Not verified in a browser, and this is the honest limit of the run.** `preview_start`
is refused in unattended sessions, so the `?spawn=` route was proved to its precondition
— all three resolve in `UNIFIED_ACTION_TEMPLATES` and `LOCATION_BRANCHING_ENCOUNTER_TEMPLATES`,
with subtypes derived from their envelopes — and not clicked. The review link is
`https://threadbearer.co/?view=game&seeded&size=medium&spawn=encounter.realm.court_summons`.
