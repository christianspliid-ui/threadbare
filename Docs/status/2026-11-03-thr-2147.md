# THR-2147 — Every card says what it buys

**Shipped 2026-11-03.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience (cold playtest round 6). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-11-02-thr-2146-every-card-says-what-it-buys.md`.

1. **E1 — `no_target`.** `nudgeHasNoTarget` (`nudges.ts`) withholds a card whose whole purchase is `remove_condition` on the actor when the actor holds none of those conditions (no odds, rider, cost channel or other grant). Checked before the price, so the Balm on an unhurt mortal is withheld, not dimmed as too dear. Designer reason: "Nothing here for it to end".
2. **E2 + C1 — the result line.** `nudgeResultLine` (`buildNudgePhaseModel.ts`) fills `resultLine` on a card with `forecastDelta` 0 and a grant, from `NUDGE_GRANT_RESULT_LINES` (`nudge-card-display.ts`, keyed on `ResultLineGrantKind`). Nouns come from the world: the condition node's name, `ARTIFACT_CATEGORY_RESULT_NOUNS`, the ambition's `displayName`, `COMPULSION_URGE_NOUNS`. A missing noun takes the fallback sentence and warns once. The spell a `spell_grant` teaches is picked at play, so that card always reads "{Name} learns a working."
3. **E3 — `nudgeWrites`.** `dispatchNudgeCommitments` now applies each card's grants as its own reaction (one failing card no longer drops the others' grants), probes the mortal before and after, and returns one `NudgeWriteRecord` per grant that wrote. `autonomous_aftermath` stores them on the new optional `UnifiedAction.nudgeWrites`. `buildNudgeWriteChanges` draws each as an Aftermath chip, merged beside the cast chips. A removal that found nothing and a hidden mark record nothing.
4. **U1–U3.** `CardFace` draws `resultLine` in the odds slot in primary text with the `ui.card.result` hover. The price row drops the sphere mark (✦ and the pool word only). The reach mark moves beside the kind chip with its word. The action card drops its scale chip, sets `costSphere`, and its verb chip hovers `ui.card.verb.<crud>.<scale>` (composed in `ui-content.ts`, first sentence of each row).

**Decision taken here.** Rider cards (Mercy, Insurance) also do not move the odds, but their purchase is the rider, which their effect line states. They get no result line; the coverage test skips them.
