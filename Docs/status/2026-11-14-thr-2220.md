# THR-2220 — a dream that found no hold no longer says "held" or "Heart grew"

**Shipped 2026-11-14.** Thematic Pressure & Living World · Warm playtest round 4.

- **Diagnosis.** `tookNoHold` was right. `resolveCastValueDrift` reads the target's *base* `axiologicalProfile`, which no cast writes, so the receipt's re-run sees the same `no_lean` the executor did. The cast truly moved no one. The contradicting chip was the **caster's** practice growth ("Heart grew", from `applyEncounterGrowth`), and the band word was the shared lexicon's "held", which is also a Dominion tier.
- **Engine.** New `castStepFoundNoHold` (`castInfluenceDrift.ts`) runs the same resolver over the step's `apply_influence` drift rule. `executeStepResult` withholds the caster's growth on a landed step that found no hold, so the sheet and the receipt agree. Hiding the chip instead would have left a silent Heart change, against the aftermath visibility-parity rule. The executor's existing `influence.no_lean` trace records the cause. Every other step (no drift rule) is unaffected.
- **Receipt words.** New `receiptBandWord` (`receipt-content.ts`): a no-hold cast reads "found no hold"; the neutral band reads "landed" and the at-cost band "landed at a cost" (`RECEIPT_BAND_WORD_OVERRIDES`). The shared `OUTCOME_BAND_WORDS` lexicon is unchanged for the encounter surfaces. `DivineReceiptModal` and the toast fallback in `playerReceipts.ts` read it.
- **Tests.** `dreamNoHoldReceipt.thr2220.test.ts` runs the real `divine.dream` through `executeStepResult` and `processPlayerReceipts`: on a zero-lean mortal the receipt is no-hold with no change chips; on a leaning mortal (control) growth lands. No receipt band word is a Dominion tier word. `DivineReceiptModalNoHold.thr2220.test.tsx` renders the dialog.
- **Browser-verify substitution: jsdom-render.** The Done-when is text-shaped (what the receipt says), so the dialog is rendered in jsdom.
