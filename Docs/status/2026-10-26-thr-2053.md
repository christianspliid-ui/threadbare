# THR-2053 — A chapter the world opens waits for the player's click

**Shipped 2026-10-26.** Cold playtest round 5, PC-5 (recurring). Two of three testers clicked a tab, the Ledger or a card and got a chapter instead: THR-1853's arrival holds covered premonitions, visions and popups, never encounters.

**What changed.**

- **The auto-open scan holds.** Before `runEncounterAutoOpenScan` opens a chapter on its own, GameView asks `worldOpenedEncounterHeldBy` (interruptRegistry). Inside `INTERRUPT_QUIET_AFTER_INPUT_MS` (2.5 s) of the last click or key, or while a player surface is open, nothing opens; a timer or the surface closing re-runs the scan.
- **The Ledger and the court hold it too.** `encounterHoldingSurfaces` (giftDelivery) is `arrivalHoldingSurfaces` without the ledger/court carve-out, because an encounter shares the screen with dialogs ("clicked Ledger and got step 2"). The Full Inspector still steps down rather than holding.
- **Badge-, row- and Needs-you-opened chapters stay immediate.** The `encounter` home entry is still not an `arrival`; only the scan waits.
- **Needs you.** Held rows were already non-buttons (`opens: 'none'` renders a disabled div). The "jumped to a different item" report was the scan opening the next chapter under that click, which the hold now prevents.
- **Debug.** `__DEBUG.getInterruptState().heldEncounter` names the held notification and why.

**Evidence.** `npm run gate`: test, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt and the CLI smoke PASS; typecheck at baseline (2662) once the debug fallback carried the new field. Four new registry tests. Browser (bash-vite :5287, Playwright, 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`): after a pointerdown and `tick(2)`, `heldEncounter` = `{ notificationId: "enc_notif_ind_dev_the_first_encounter.healers_oath_1", heldBy: ["recent_input"] }` with `open: []`; 3.3 s later `heldEncounter: null`, `open: ["EncounterVeil"]`. The only console error is the absent TTS server.
