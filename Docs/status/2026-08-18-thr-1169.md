# THR-1169 — the exerciser block, sorted into proofs and items

**Shipped 2026-08-18.** THR-745 migrated 57 capability artifacts and stopped at a stated
seam: everything past `// ─── Effect Primitive Exercisers` was held back whole, on the
rationale that each entry there demonstrates exactly **one** effect primitive and bolting a
second onto a single-primitive proof weakens what it proves. That rationale is sound. It
just does not describe every entry that ended up in the block. This pass makes the
per-entry call, migrates **9**, and writes the split into the file so it is not re-argued.

## The test used, and why it is not "tier ≥ 3"

An entry is a **proof** when its identity *is* its primitive: remove the primitive and
nothing remains, and the name describes a mechanic rather than an object. An entry is an
**item** when it is composed — several interacting effects expressing one design idea,
borne rather than consumed, with flavor describing a thing that has a history.

Tier correlates but does not decide, and the counter-example is load-bearing:
**Double-Edged Blade is T3 and stays a proof** — it is `tradeoff` alone, named after its
own mechanic. Had the rule been "T3/T4 migrates" it would have been swept up wrongly.

## What moved (9), and what the magnitudes are for

Values sit on the rungs THR-745 established rather than a parallel scale — T3 primary
0.7–0.8 / secondary 0.3–0.35, T4 primary 1.2–1.3 / secondary 0.6 — because a second scale
in the same catalog is the failure this migration exists to end.

**One penalty was deliberately *not* written.** The Quiet Cup carries
`action_gate: { mode: 'block', reach: 'iron' }`. Pricing that a second time as lost Iron
capability would double-count a single authored cost — the gate already forbids the
actions outright, and a capability penalty on top would tax a channel the item has already
closed.

## Three T4 "provisions" that are not consumables

`The Quiet Cup`, `The Last Harvest` and `The Black Mead` sit under the `provisions`
subcategory, which the predicate excludes as consumables. Every mechanical signal says they
are not: `lossCondition` is `permanent`/`cursed`, there is no `consumable_charge`, and
nothing carries `destroyOnEmpty`. They are borne, so the consumable exclusion never reached
them.

This is an **entry-level reading, not a revision of the predicate** — `provisions` remains
excluded as a category, and the ordinary provisions in the block (Ward Incense, Spring Water
Vial, Meditation Stones, Battle Salve, Salvage Kit) stay excluded on exactly that ground,
each carrying `lossCondition: 'consumable'` and charges that destroy them.

## The three individually excluded entries — all three confirmed

- `letters_of_introduction` — **confirmed.** Shown once and spent; nothing is borne, so
  there is no interval over which it could shape capability.
- `hearthglass_ward` — **confirmed.** `consumeOnPrevent`; it shatters the one time it
  matters.
- `duelists_luck_token` — **confirmed, and the reasoning is recorded on the entry** because
  it was the closest call. Unlike the other two it *is* borne (`stealable`), so the
  consumable argument never applied. It is excluded on the predicate's other half: its sole
  channel is a `test_shaper`, and the flavor is explicit that it catches a stumble rather
  than teaching skill. Granting it Iron capability would quietly convert a luck charm into
  a competence item — a different object than the one authored.

## The anomaly catalog needed nothing, checked rather than assumed

Ten artifacts, eight already migrated under THR-745. The two holdouts — `anomaly_amber_phial`
and `anomaly_herb_bundle` — are genuine consumables (`lossCondition: 'consumable'`,
`consumable_charge`, `destroyOnEmpty: true`), correctly excluded by the predicate. That
catalog is complete.

## The guard was falsified against *these* entries, not merely observed green

`item-stat-bands.contract.test.ts` was green before this change and after it, which alone
proves nothing — a ceiling test passes happily against entries it never reaches, and the
entries in question are new. Pushing The Anvilbone to `stone: 2.9` fails it **by name**
(`reward_tools_instruments_the_anvilbone: stone=2.9 (> 2)`); reverting returns it to green.
The double-dip half is satisfied by construction — no migrated entry carries a legacy
`domainContributions` bag.

## The split is recorded in the file, not only here

A block-header comment now names every entry on both sides of the line and states the test,
so the next pass inherits the decision instead of re-deriving it — which is what THR-745
asked for, and the reason that ticket filed this one rather than absorbing the call.
