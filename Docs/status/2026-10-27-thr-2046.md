# THR-2046 — A road's encounters write to that road

**Shipped 2026-10-27.** M3: Dynamic Economy. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-25-thr-2046-encounter-lane-writes.md`.

**What changed.** A route-event seed now carries its lane (`laneEdgeId`). `resolveSeedLane` re-checks the lane when the seed spawns and puts it on the action. Every lane row in the outcome table lands there through `resolveLaneAnchor` (`economyShift.ts`), and falls back to the town's busiest lane only for an organic draw. `economy_shift` gains `lane_threat` (mark or clear `threatened`, never a blockade; an encounter-set mark clears after `ENCOUNTER_THREAT_CLEAR_TICKS`) and `lane_toll` (`controlledBy` goes to the faction holding the town, else to the acting mortal; it never strips a mortal holder). Seven templates carry the rows: the ambush, the toll dispute, the embargo, the toll bridge, the pirate raid, the overdue caravan and the caravan escort. A struck toll adds an aftermath row: *A toll is set* / *"The road from {town} is tolled by {holder}."*, with the holder linked. The lane events, and four other templates that gated on the non-existent `village` subtype, now reach hamlets. Rollback: `ENCOUNTER_LANE_WRITES_ENABLED`.

**Evidence.** `encounterLaneWrites.test.ts` (12 tests) and `encounterLaneWrites.switchOff.test.ts`; content invariants in `outcomeEffects.test.ts`. `npm run census:lanes -- --seeds 42,99,7 --ticks 360`: 25 / 36 / 21 seeded-lane writes, 0 of them on another lane; encounter tolls 4 / 5 / 3 and threats 4 / 5 / 10, so all three seeds have both.

**Next.** The kill criteria were not compared against main: at 4–10 encounter threats a month across ~130 towns, a tier drop is implausible. If one is seen, halve `ENCOUNTER_THREAT_CLEAR_TICKS`.
