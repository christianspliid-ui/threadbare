# 2026-09-24 — THR-1536: a killing's grief reaches the living, not the corpse

**Ticket:** [THR-1536](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1536) · **Project:** Physical Conflict · **Pillars:** Engine. Content is N/A because the existing `named_death` minting rows are reused. UI is N/A because the provenance label already renders wherever a minted drive's label does. **Plan:** none. The outcome was decided on THR-1266 ("the dead's bonds take grievances"), and this ticket is the missing wiring.

## What shipped

Since THR-1430 a retained death leaves the victim's `participated_in role:'target'` edge on a corpse.

- **The dead do not pursue.** `phaseAmbitionProgress` (`src/engine/ambitionTick.ts`) filters `isAgentGone` actors out of its all-actor walk. A retained body is never minted a drive and never has its residence observed.
- **Grief reaches the living.** `createUndertakingOutcomeNode` (`src/engine/grievance/undertakingOutcomeNode.ts`) applies `routeGriefToBonds` when the harm has a culprit and the individual victim is already gone. The victim's warmest living bonds each get their own target edge tagged `viaBondOf`. A bond is a `relates_to` edge in either direction with `sentiment ≥ GRIEF_BOND_MIN_SENTIMENT` (0.25); only the top `GRIEF_BOND_MAX` (3) are taken, and the culprit and the dead are never included. The tuple builder carries `viaBondOfName`, so the label reads *"the killing of Thornhall, done to Oswen — Hesk's work"*. The outcome trace gains `griefBondIds`.
- **A culprit is required.** The first census wrote eight bond edges, and all eight were self-facing `undertaking_abandoned` nodes: an owner died and their unfinished work collapsed. That wrongs nobody, so culprit-less outcomes never route.

**Known gap, stated rather than fixed.** Succession in `grievanceLifecycle.ts` used to reach a dead victim's heir by minting on the corpse's walk. Deaths are now covered at write time instead. The one case left uncovered is a victim harmed while alive who then dies before the next mint pass (a window of at most 75 ticks). Broken victims still go through succession unchanged.
