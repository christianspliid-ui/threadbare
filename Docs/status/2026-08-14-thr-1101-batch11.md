# THR-1101 batch 11 — the explore family's survey half stops reading as mad-libs

**Date:** 2026-08-14 · **Issue:** [THR-1101](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1101) · **Lane:** `tb-opus-pickup`

## What shipped

The **survey sub-theme of `explore` is drained** — 12 of the family's 30 token-carrying templates authored out of the `{adj}`/`{verb}`/`{noun}`/`{action}` shape, 84 token lines across 93 prose fields:

`deep_descent` · `delve_into_depths` · `prospecting_expedition` · `mineral_vein_discovery` · `prospect_the_seam` · `map_the_passages` · `catalogue_the_tower` · `inspect_the_armoury` · `case_the_joint` · `study_surroundings` · `forage_provisions` · `forage_the_land`

Register: **survey work is measurement under conditions that do not care about you — the place answers or it does not, and the only question is whether the reading was right before the light or the weather turned.** Success is a number that can be defended; failure is a number that cannot. That premise carries a ruin descent, three kinds of prospecting, a passage survey, a library inventory, an armoury audit, a burglar casing a house, and two foraging runs, because all nine are the same act at different stakes: go to a place, count what is there, and be answerable for the count. Calibrated against `encounter.trial_by_combat`, the THR-1036 exemplar.

Predicate re-derived at pickup: **30** of 168, matching batch 10's hand-off figure and its family breakdown line-for-line (`explore` 30/194). Nine consecutive runs of a stable scan. Post-batch: **18 templates / 110 token lines**, one family.

## Why this split, and why not by count

Batch 10 required `explore` be split **by sub-theme rather than by count**, on the grounds that halving a family arbitrarily is the one thing the per-family register discipline cannot absorb. Three sub-themes are legible in the 30:

- **Survey** (12) — a mortal physically in a place, reading what it holds. *This batch.*
- **Divination / arcane reading** (9) — `spirit_walk`, `starborn_vigil`, `read_the_stars`, `trace_ley_lines`, `investigate_anomaly`, `arcane_resonance_study`, `read_the_wards`, `test_the_seal`, `festival_of_spheres`.
- **Scholarship and hearsay** (9) — `knowledge_test`, `sacred_text_study`, `decipher_ancient_inscriptions`, `decipher_old_markings`, `hermits_wisdom`, `pilgrimage_trial`, `listen_for_rumors`, `local_gossip`, `garrison_gossip`.

The remaining 18 are **two coherent registers, not one**, which is the correction to batch 10's "two-run job" estimate: what a god learns from the sky and what a mortal learns from a drunk in a garrison do not share a voice. Sizing says one run of 110 lines is possible; the register discipline says it should be two of 9. Recorded so the next run does not read "two-run job" as licence to take all 18.

## The ratio

`explore`'s survey half came in **4 full mad-libs to 8 wedged-token** — the furthest to the *wedged* side any slice has landed, against `lead` 5/9, `assist` 6/9, `build` 7/9, `trade` 7/8, `create` 6/7, `acquire` 8/5, `steal` 6/3, `hire` 6/3.

This is the second batch where the split had a **visible cause in the source**, and it corroborates batch 10's finding rather than merely repeating it: the eight wedged-token templates are almost all under the `LOCATION-GAP FILLS` and universal-template headers — written later, individually, as real prose with tokens pushed into working sentences — while the full mad-libs sit among the older shaping-tier entries. Authoring era predicts the ratio better than family does.

## Findings

**A verbatim duplicate line across two templates** (impediment #571). `study_surroundings` and `trace_ley_lines` carried character-identical prose — *"Nothing stirs. The `{adj}` silence holds, and `{actor}` learns only that some places guard their secrets well."* — surfaced only because an `Edit` refused on two matches. Both are `explore`-family, both `veil`-reach second steps, and a player drawing both reads the same failure twice. This is the "single-variant content" failure the prose canon rejects, arriving by copy-paste rather than by design, and no instrument in this campaign looks for it: the token scan counts lines, the render pass reads each line once, and the suite asserts on tokens. Only the one in this batch's slice was rewritten; `trace_ley_lines` keeps the original text and belongs to the divination slice, where it will be authored under that register.

**Two probe false positives, both adjudicated rather than absorbed into the predicate.** Batch 10's generic bare-verb rule (a `she`/`he` subject followed by a word neither auxiliary nor `-s`-carrying) flagged *"she dug"* — a correct irregular past tense, exactly the 31-of-52 class THR-1107 quantified. A separate strictest-scope sweep flagged `way` inside the phrasal verb *"gave way"*. Neither is a defect. The first was listed in the probe as an explicit adjudication rather than silently added to the skip-set, so the ruling stays visible; the second was rephrased because *"a floor that has fallen through"* is at least as plain, and arguing a lexicon exception costs more than the rewrite.

**One real catch, and it is batch 10's own trap re-sprung.** This batch wrote *"tries the edge, the balance, the temper"* in `inspect_the_armoury` — and **`the balance` is a nominalised placeholder in the evasive set, enforced at zero in every field class**, not an outcome-only indefinite. A blade's balance is literal here, which is precisely why it slipped past: the detector cannot distinguish the physical noun from the abstraction, and batch 10 logged the identical word one batch earlier. *"the weight in the hand"* is the concrete noun the vague one was standing in front of, so the fix improved the line. Worth inheriting: the evasive set's nominalised placeholders are the ones that read as innocent domain vocabulary.

## Verification

**1009 files / 16,085 tests green, exit 0**, on the tree being pushed. Exit code read via `PIPESTATUS`, not the pipeline tail (the #562 trap).

The first run of the suite — taken before this batch's last two prose edits — reported one failure, and the disposition is recorded because the *method* is the reusable part. `gateDutyAftermathProgression.test.ts` timed out at 5000 ms under full-suite parallel load, then passed in isolation at **552 ms**, a 9× margin against its own limit: a timeout rather than an assertion, in an engine test, against a diff of prose strings in a data module with no engine coupling. That was enough to call it contention (the `impediment-id-allocation.test.ts` class, #562) but not enough to *ship* on, so the suite was re-run on the final tree — which is the green result above. A flake diagnosed from an isolation pass is a hypothesis; a clean full run on the shipped tree is the evidence.

Ratchet **OK at 3447, unchanged**. `vite build` clean. Generated-freshness, wiki-freshness and impediment-ids run **last**, against the committed tree being pushed — not at their numbered position. Engine smoke run although not owed.

**Verification went past the gate deliberately, because the gate cannot see prose quality.** All 93 lines rendered through the real `enrichProse` in two pronoun arms, with vagueness scored in the same pass by the real `countVagueness`, and every predicate carrying a falsification case proving it can report non-zero (batch 10's rule). Final: **0 raw tokens, 0 unresolved braces, 0 agreement errors, 0 vagueness — including at the strictest `outcome` scope applied to every line**, scene fields included, which is stricter than those fields owe. Probe deleted before commit.

Four intensifier hits remain and are **warn-level in every field class, deliberately left**: three are the comparative `rather than`, ruled on identically by batches 4–9, and one is *"does not quite sit in its frame"*, where `quite` is the precise word for a door that almost closes. The substring matcher cannot distinguish either from an intensifier.

**Browser-verify exempt:** prose strings in a data module fire no UI-pillar path trigger.

## The lock

`encounterWordPlaceholders.test.ts` asserts `withTokens.length > 0` since batch 8 lowered it to its contract. At 30 → 18 it holds with nothing owed, for the third batch running.

**The retirement is now next batch's, not this one's.** Batch 10 assigned it to "the final `explore` batch"; because `explore` splits into two register-coherent runs rather than one, that is the batch after next. The guard goes red exactly once, when the population lands on zero, and is retired alongside the corpus it guards.
