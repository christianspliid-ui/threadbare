### 2026-10-04 — THR-1698: an apex monster's card names the apex

**The bug.** THR-1682 lets a legendary golem or behemoth grow into its apex (`monsterState.apex`, severe / severe). Only prose read it, through `monsterCardLine`. The fight header and the lair card both build their sentence through `familyLineFor` (`buildOpponentHeaderModel.ts`), which read only the family's `cardLine`. So a colossus golem's card said "A thing of stone that does not tire" next to severe ratings while the scene called it a colossus. `listMonsters` dropped `apex` too.

**The fix.** `familyLineFor` reads `monsterCardLine(bag)`, the same lookup the prose uses, with the beast line as the fail-soft floor. Both card surfaces follow. `ListedMonster.apex` is set when the card carries one (extra to the pinned shape); the bridge JSDoc says so.

**Evidence.** New `apexCardLine.test.ts`: family line before hardening, apex line after; unknown apex/family fail soft; the lair card's sentence opens on the apex line; `listMonsters` carries `apex` only after hardening. Live capture on `?view=game&seeded&size=medium` after `tick(200)`: 14 apex monsters listed, `spawnFight('elite_lair_2_50')` shows Turvane's header reading "An ancient behemoth the land has fed for a hundred years", matching the scene prose (`Docs/evidence/thr-1698/`).
