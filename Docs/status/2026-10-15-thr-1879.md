# 2026-10-15 — THR-1879 S1: Alegreya and the type tokens

**Ticket:** [THR-1879](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1879) (S1 of the type and icons rollout, plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1879-type-and-icons-rollout.md`). Engine: N/A. Content: the font files, `OFL.txt` and the Fonts credit line. UI: the tokens, the label rule, the prose sort, monospace off player surfaces.

- **Story is set in Alegreya.** `public/fonts/` gains Alegreya roman (variable 400–900), Alegreya italic 400 and Alegreya Sans 800 (latin subsets from Google Fonts' css2 API, 90 KB), plus `OFL.txt` for all three families (no Reserved Font Name). `--font-prose` is `'Alegreya', Georgia, …`; `--type-body-prose` and `--type-flavor` now read it. Measured: story prose is Alegreya at 20 px / 32 px at 1920×1080 and 22.5 px / 36 px at 2560×1440, nothing below the fold.
- **The label rule.** `--type-section-label` is Alegreya Sans 700. `.section-heading` (now tertiary), `.section-heading-gold`, `.topbar-section-label`, `.quote-attr`, `.subloc-badge` and `.avail-label` drop the transform and the tracking; the literal heading strings they carry are sentence case ("Rival gods", "Full account"). Cinzel titles track at `0.02em`.
- **The prose sort.** 23 of the 60 `--font-prose` sites moved: 17 story lines to the prose shorthands (chronicle cards, Meet the First vignettes and dilemmas, Remembrance fragments, the lair sentence), 6 non-story sites to the body face (the World-Soul line, a candidate's name caption, the dilemma counter, the chronicle's empty line, the lair clock word and killer link). The ceremony display prompts and the Stage / ceremony `FONT_PROSE` constants keep their sizes and take the face through the token.
- **No monospace on player surfaces.** 19 sites in 14 files move to the body face with tabular figures (`--figures-tabular`, `.figures-tabular`); what remains is World Pulse (retires) and the map tooltip (THR-1878).
- **Ratchet.** New row `display-face-small` (Cinzel below `--text-lg`), baseline measured at landing; `monospace`, `uppercase` and the others re-baselined down.
- **Docs:** `Docs/design-system/typography.md` rewritten from THR-1833, § Type Scale kept as THR-1819 wrote it. Evidence: `Docs/audits/2026-10-15-thr-1879/`.
