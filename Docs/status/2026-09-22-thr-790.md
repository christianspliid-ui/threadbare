**Ticket:** [THR-790](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-790) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** Engine, Content, UI. **Plan:** [`Docs/plans/2026-09-21-thr-790-traits-wave-2.md`](../plans/2026-09-21-thr-790-traits-wave-2.md). Slices 2–4 are children (THR-1520, THR-1521, THR-1522).

## What shipped

**The first producer of location traits from the world's own state.** Every `trait.condition.location.*` edge used to be planted by an encounter aftermath. `phaseLocationTraits` (orchestrator `6.6385`, after magical saturation) now reads `prosperity`, `unrest`, `magicalSaturation` and `deathCount` off the place tier each tick and mints four new definitions with the settlement-promotion hysteresis — enter / release thresholds, a sustain counter persisted on the node, the dead band holding the counter — releasing each when its reading recovers: *Welcoming*, *Lawless*, *Veil-thin*, *Haunted* (*Haunted* needs the dead and supersedes *Veil-thin*). A minted edge carries no term; the phase is its lifecycle. Every constant is in `src/data/location-trait-constants.ts`; a chronicle line on mint, none on release; one aggregate `location_trait` trace per moving tick; `__DEBUG.getLocationTraits`, CLI `traits`.

**The pool reads them.** `scoreAndSelect` adds `locationTraitBonus` beside the economic-context term — trait × *content tag* (`LOCATION_TRAIT_ENCOUNTER_BONUS`), summed and capped — reusing the location node it had already resolved. Keyed on seated tags of any axis because the corpus carries family words on 16 of 236 encounters and projected reach tags on all of them; a family-only table would have moved nothing. The Decision Breakdown panel shows the term per candidate.

**The carve landed first.** `condition_template` excluded ids under `LOCATION_CONDITION_ID_PREFIX` unless the query says `classes: ['location']` — the pre-fix arm proves all ten location ids were in the set an untagged `condition` recipe could draw from (45 shipped recipes). No new field; the id prefix is the declaration.

**The page says what the state does.** `LocationProfileModal`'s condition rows gain the effect line `AttachmentDetailView` already had (*"Travel through here costs less."* for *Welcoming*; `conditionEffectLine` learned to read a sub-unity movement multiplier). No `#blood-soaked` — no per-place battle record exists; the dead enter *Haunted*.

## Two calibrations, both measured

**Thresholds.** The plan's draft took the promotion phase's 70 for *Welcoming* and 70 for *Lawless*. Measured on medium × 150 ticks, seeds 42 / 99: settlement prosperity p50 14–17, p90 27–30, busiest cities averaging 28–38; exactly one place in two worlds ever held ≥ 70, and no mortal visited it. At 70 the census read `UNMINTED` on seed 42. Recalibrated to *Welcoming* 40 / 25 and *Lawless* 50 / 30 (unrest spikes to 88–99 on one or two places per world, a handful sit at 50–60); saturation bands unchanged. After: seed 42 mints one *Welcoming* (t36) and one *Lawless* (t147); seed 99 mints five *Welcoming* (peak 5, one released) and two *Lawless*. The rationale sits in the constants file.

**The kill criterion, read honestly.** The parent plan's bet: *if location traits ship and headless runs show no shift in pool composition at marked vs unmarked places, the react-verb integrations need rework.* `npm run census:location-traits` is that instrument (verdicts `UNMINTED` / `UNDERSAMPLED` / `MOVES` / `FLAT`, `MOVES` requiring a five-point share rise on ≥ 3 hits over ≥ 20 marked encounters). **The term is live**: a scoring-trace harvest on seed 99 found 104 candidates carrying the bonus and 43 of them selected, against a 20% base selection rate. **The composition does not yet move**: at *Welcoming* towns on seed 99 (122 encounters over ~110 ticks) gold-reach encounters were 0 of 122 against 6.5% elsewhere and `#heart` 18.0% vs 17.3% — `FLAT`; *Lawless* and seed 42 `UNDERSAMPLED`. Re-run with every row at the cap (0.15): still 0 gold at *Welcoming* places. So the limit is **eligibility, not weight** — the gold / social / tavern templates the rows name do not become candidates at the marked towns (capitals and cities whose live pools are `#veil` / `#star` / `#iron` stories), and no bonus reweights a candidate that is not there. Rows kept at the plan's sizes. *Veil-thin* / *Haunted* read `UNMINTED` on every headless run by construction — saturation is written only by divine action and spellwork.

**What this means for THR-1522** (slice 4, gated on "slice 1's census showing the pool term moves"): the gate does not read `MOVES` today. The honest next lever is content — encounters at the `#social` / `#tavern_night` / `#trade` families registering at the settlement classes that mint *Welcoming* — or a design decision to let a location trait widen *eligibility* rather than only score. Recorded for the orchestrator to read at promotion time; not decided here.
