# THR-1894 — Screen frame slice 2: the god rail

**Shipped 2026-10-18.** Slice 2 of the screen-frame plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1875-screen-frame.md` § F1 slice 2, § F6). The left rail is now drawn from one list. It shows who the god is, what it holds and what it can cast. Attention moved there from the top bar.

- **One list per kind (Law 58).** `GOD_RAIL_SECTIONS` (`src/data/frame-registry.ts`) lists identity and essence (pinned), the hand (fill), then Reaches, Mandate, Covenants, Signatures and Hooks (folded). `GOD_RAIL_COMPONENTS` (`frame/godRailComponents.tsx`) is the `Record`-keyed body map. The god rail's part of `NOT_YET_LISTED` is gone: essence claims `attention`, and the hand claims `investiture`.
- **The view model.** `views/godRail.ts` (selector `godRail`) composes the god's sheet selectors. It builds the hand: every revealed god card, priced at par through `dominionScaledEssenceCost(…, undefined)`, with `paySphere = sphereAffinity ?? primary`. `payable` is measured at the sovereign price. The VM also carries Attention and the folded-row summaries (≤ `GOD_RAIL_SUMMARY_MAX_CH`).
- **The hand ("Your cards").** Move, then Investiture (once revealed), then the cards, tinted by the pool that pays them. Line 2 reads "Life · 2 essence" or "Free". The price carries the `ui.hand_price` tooltip, built from the Foreign band's line and `DOMINION_CAST_CLAUSE`. The fit rule (`frame/handFit.ts`): the hand is whole while it fits; at the rail's limit it groups by paying sphere, keeping the armed card's group open, else the remembered group (`GOD_RAIL_HAND_GROUP_KEY`). While a moment is open the cards rest under a line that says why.
- **The press seam.** `HandPressContext` / `useHandPress()`. With no provider above it, the god rail mounts the default: a press opens the card's Codex entry. THR-1914 replaces it with arming.
- **One price label.** `essencePriceLabel` ("Free" for 0) is shared by the Codex and the hand. The essence formatters moved to `src/data/essence-format.ts` (`components/shared/formatEssence.ts` re-exports them), because `engine/views/` may not import from `components/`.
- **Restyled to the type system.** `ascendant-bar/*` no longer has a literal font size, uppercase label or tracking. The ratchet fell 172 → 118 literal sizes and 96 → 82 uppercase. Essence shows two spheres to a row. The god's name is Cinzel at 22 px.
- **Retired:** `ActionsBlock.tsx`, `BarSection.tsx`, `TRAY_EMPTY_COPY`, `ASCENDANT_BAR_SECTION_DEFAULT_OPEN`, and Attention in the top bar.
- **Debug:** `getFrameState().godRail` (sections drawn and open; the hand's cards, mode, open group, armed card, rest state, free height, overflowing rows), `getView('godRail')`.

Evidence: worktree Vite with Playwright on `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`. At 1920×1080 the rail is 320 px; at 2560×1440 it is 400 px. The page does not scroll at either size. Attention is in the rail and absent from the top bar. With 5 cards the hand is `whole`. With 14 cards it is `grouped`, the Mind group is open, and no row overflows. With the Chapter Ledger open the hand reads `rest: 'moment'` with the quoted line. Pressing a card opened the Codex. Console: 0 errors.

**Open:** capacity is below the plan's estimate in this seeded world. The 1080p hand region is 346 px and holds 5 cards whole; at 1440p it is 642 px and holds 9. Three things eat the height. The dev world holds 8 spheres, which make 4 essence rows. All 4 folded rows render. The top bar wraps to 97 / 109 px, which slice 3 (THR-1875) fixes. In the plan's reference state (4 spheres, 3 rows, a 48 px bar) the region gains about 155 px, which fits 8 whole cards. The full card-face hover on a row is deferred to THR-1975.
