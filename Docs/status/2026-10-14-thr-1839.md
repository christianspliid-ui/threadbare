# 2026-10-14 — THR-1839: places founded during play take their people

**Ticket:** [THR-1839](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1839), found while planning THR-1793. Engine + Content (one fringe subtype); UI N/A (the hex culture panel already reads fringe links).

- **What the player sees.** A hamlet founded mid-game now belongs to the nearest people, the same way a town that stood there from the start does. Its people are born to that culture and its encounter openings read that culture's custom. A worked mine takes a people too.
- **How.** The worldgen fringe rule (nearest heartland by hex distance, ties by culture id then place id, no draws) is now one function, `pickNearestHeartland`, in `cultureGenerator.ts`. Worldgen uses it unchanged. A new tick pass, `assignFringeCultureToFoundedPlaces`, runs right after settlement promotion: it links any cultureless settlement-type place within the scenario's reach, writes nothing on a quiet tick, re-indexes each linked place in the encounter cache, and emits one `founded_place_culture` trace. Today's-world scenario (reach 0) keeps it off. The god's starting shrine stays cultureless, as THR-1793's plan decided.
- **Mining decision.** `mining` joins `CULTURE_FRINGE_SUBTYPES`: people live and work at a worked mine, so it belongs to the nearest people. Unworked deposits (gem deposits, seeps, shoals) stay out. Veto welcome.
