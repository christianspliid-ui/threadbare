# 2026-10-14 — THR-1839: a hamlet founded in play belongs to a people

**Ticket:** [THR-1839](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1839), found while planning THR-1793. Engine + Content (one subtype added to a data set); UI N/A (the hex culture panel and the genome already read whatever `belongs_to` a place carries).

- **What changes in play.** A hamlet a mortal founds at tick 200 now takes the nearest heartland's culture as its fringe, the same way a far town does at worldgen. A worked mine now takes one too. Ruins, lairs, wonders and anomalies stay cultureless.
- **How.** The draw-free nearest-heartland rule moved into `src/engine/cultureFringe.ts` (`pickNearestHeartland`). The worldgen pass calls it, and its output is unchanged. Both founding paths call `takeFringeCulture` after `createLocation`: the strategic `create_location` mutation and the Place object's `create` verb. They read the world's own reach through `fringeReachOf(state)`. A fringe town is never an anchor, so a fringe cannot chain outward. `mining` joined `CULTURE_FRINGE_SUBTYPES`.
- **Mining decision (agent call, veto welcome).** A worked mine is a place where people live and work, like a camp. The nine mortals standing at one on seed 42 belonged to no people.
- **Evidence.** Seed 42 / medium, CLI `eval`. Cultureless settlement places went 2/57 → 1/57 at t0 and 4/58 → 1/58 at t240. Both founded hamlets (`loc_hamlet_29_22_74`, `loc_hamlet_24_20_190`) now carry `culture_1` as a fringe. The one left is `loc.start`, the First's own Sacred Grove, which is out of scope. 10 new tests in `cultureFringe.test.ts`. The undertaking grid drops Culture's "untouched by design" reason, because founding now reaches it.
