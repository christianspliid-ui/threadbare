**2026-09-04 — THR-1411 (UI, Bug): the authored hand reaches the attended veil carrying its stance.**

`?spawn=crafting.quest.flawed_steel` rendered three choice cards with no stance word — `document.querySelectorAll('[data-testid=choice-stance]')` returned 0, hovered or not — although every one of the template's authored cards carries an `interventionType`. The ticket's hypothesis was that the field is lost somewhere between `encounterVisibility` and the model, and pointed at `debugEncounterTools` as the likely culprit on the `?spawn` route.

**It is one line earlier than that, and not on the spawn route at all.** `buildUnifiedEncounterStageModel`'s authored branch builds its choice models straight from the card — `id`, `label`, `intent`, `targetLabel`, `essenceCost`, `likelyBurden` — and never copies `interventionType`. The generic-notification fallback immediately below it *does* set both `interventionType` and `stanceLabel`, but that branch runs only when the step has no authored choices, so it can never see an authored card. `debugEncounterTools` contains no reference to `interventionType` at all; it was never in the path.

The reason this looked route-specific is an **adapter asymmetry**: `buildSimpleEncounterStageModel` reads the authored hand out of `notification.choices`, which `phaseEncounterVisibility` fills with `card.interventionType` — so the very same template rendered its stance at `watched` tier and dropped it at attended. `flawed_steel` is a unified template, and `GameView` routes every non-`watched`, non-gate-duty unified template to the unified adapter. One tier showed the stance; the other never could.

**One dropped field, three dark reads.** `interventionType` is not only the stance word's source: `ChoiceBlock` also derives the card's top glow line from `TYPE_COLORS[interventionType]` and the withdrawn card's `fate decides` meta label from it. All three were absent from the attended veil for the same reason, and all three return with the copy.

**The Law 45 measurement the ticket asked for found a real failure.** The ticket said *measure, do not assume*, and the measurement is why this is not a one-line change. Composited on the ground the word actually sits on (`--veil-void` under the card's own gold wash at 0.03 hovered / 0.06 selected), the shipped label alphas gave **6.89:1 supportive, 3.79:1 coercive, 2.69:1 withdrawn** — two of the three below the 4.5:1 floor for text. They had read as passing because the attended veil never rendered the word, so the only surface showing them was the `watched`-tier one nobody had measured. Fixing the producer would have shipped a legibility failure to a surface that previously showed nothing, so the alphas moved with it: coercive `0.7 → 0.85`, withdrawn `0.5 → 0.8`, supportive unchanged. That lands at 7.12 / 5.30 / 5.19 on `--veil-void` (6.89 / 5.06 / 4.98 on the stricter card ground), and preserves the hierarchy — the grey is still the quietest of the three. `TYPE_COLORS`, the decorative glow line, is untouched: Law 45 binds text, not edges.

**Three gates, each falsified before being trusted.**

- A **producer-path sweep** over every shipped template carrying authored choices — 32 of them — asserting each card reaches the model with its enum and its banded word. `notification.choices` is passed **empty** on purpose: the generic fallback is the adapter's only other stance producer, so emptying it means a pass can only have come from the authored card. Restoring the pre-fix adapter turns 33 of its 34 cases red; the one that stays green is the corpus-population guard, which correctly does not depend on the fix.
- The **Law 45 case list** added to `encounterVeilLaws.test.ts`, in that file's existing polarity pattern — plus the anti-vacuity half it insists on: a source assertion that `TYPE_LABEL_COLORS` actually ships the three alphas the table measures. Without it the table would assert that three numbers typed into a test clear AA while the component shipped others, which is the exact shape the file's loss-red gate exists to prevent.
- A **rendered-veil file** running the real template through the real adapter into the real `EncounterVeil`, including the hover reveal, the two sibling reads, the Law 14 negative, and an absence arm for a choice with no stance. Reverting the producer turns 4 of its 8 red; the 4 that stay green are correctly stance-independent.

**Browser-verify substitution: jsdom-render.** `preview_start` is refused in unattended runs, which shuts the Playwright route with it. The route was decided before implementing, per the standing rule.

**What that does and does not discharge.** The ticket's wrap Done-when — *the withdrawn card's `stand back · ◆ 0 essence · fate decides` row does not wrap or push the 540px card* — is **half answered here and half not**. The decisive half is structural rather than geometric: that meta row sets no `flexWrap`, so it is `nowrap` by CSS default and cannot wrap whatever it holds; the card keeps `maxWidth: 540`, so a long row would overflow rather than widen the column. Both are now pinned from the rendered DOM. What remains an argument rather than a measurement is overflow: at `--text-xs` (16px) the three items plus two 16px gaps come to roughly 300px against 496px of content width inside the card's 22px padding, so there is about 40% headroom — comfortable, but arithmetic, not a pixel. Filed as **THR-1419** for the attended pass, alongside the same ticket's stance-hue confirmation, rather than either waived or left to block a fix the surface needs now.

**Not touched, deliberately.** `buildSimpleEncounterStageModel` already carried the field correctly and is left alone. The type doc on `stanceLabel` did need correcting: it read *"Absent means the producer tagged no stance (authored choice cards carry their own prose instead)"*, which described the one producer that dropped the field as though it had chosen to — and `AuthoredChoiceCard.interventionType` is a required field, so an authored card always has a stance to band. A comment that makes an omission read as intent is how this survived a year of veil audits.
