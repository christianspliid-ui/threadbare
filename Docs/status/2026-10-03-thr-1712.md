### 2026-10-03 — THR-1712: Meet The First tells one story about one person

**The bug.** Cold playtest round 2: all three testers hit a contradiction in the scene they rated best. A woman picked by her face was named "Aldric". Her path and binding cards showed the catalog's stock men. Test 2's backdrop was always the burning village, even for a story about storm-forecasting and seeds. Mortals were named "Decay" and "Corrode".

**The causes and fixes.**
- **Name ignored gender.** Each of the 24 vignettes now declares `gender`, matching its prose pronouns. The candidate's name is drawn from the new gendered pools (`FOUNDATION_NAMES_FEMALE` / `_MALE`, gendered generic fallbacks, `pickGenderedName`). The created First carries `gender` on its node, which the prose resolvers already read for pronouns.
- **Path cards ignored the mortal.** `bindSparkVisionsToCandidate` gives every path card the chosen candidate's own portrait. The card keeps its own scene backdrop and prose. This is also image doctrine ruling 10: the Sensing portrait is the only likeness the flow shows. Three vision texts that said "she" about the subject are now neutral.
- **Scene art was picked by index.** The dilemma mapper dropped `resonance`, so every scene tied at 0. It now passes `resonance` through. `selectSceneForDilemma` scores on the emotional register plus literal imagery found in the prose (`SCENE_CONTENT_KEYWORDS`: fire, storm, sea, riot, battle, plague). A scene that shows one of those (burning village, burning palace, harbor storm, market riot, battlefield, plague ward) can only be drawn when the prose names it. Ties break on a hash of the template id, not the test's position.
- **Abstract-noun names.** 76 entries in the person-name pools that read as things ("Decay", "Codex", "Requiem", "Hourglass"…) were replaced one for one with names that read as people. Array lengths are unchanged, so seeded draws of the other names do not move.

**Evidence.** `src/data/__tests__/meetingCandidateCoherence.test.ts`, 15 tests: vignette gender vs pronouns, names drawn from the matching pool across 5 foundations × 40 seeds, gender landing on the node, path cards bound, resonance surviving the mapper, distinct registers picking distinct scenes, a storm story never drawing fire across 50 seeds, a library-wide check on imagery scenes, and an abstract-noun stop-list. Browser check with Bash-started worktree Vite on `?view=game&firstunmet&size=medium` at 1920×1080: three women at Sensing; the chosen crossroads seer was named "Beatrix"; test 1 drew `misty-graveyard`, not index-0 `crossroads`; all three path cards showed her portrait.
