# THR-1181 — Sphere Attunement enters the glossary

**Shipped 2026-08-19.** The mechanic THR-1180 built the day before now has a name the UL will defend.

## What the entry had to settle

Three distinctions, each of which the code makes and no prose did:

- **Earned, not held.** `essenceEarnedBySphere` sits beside the spendable pool and never decrements. A sphere going 10 → 4 has not un-earned six, it has spent six. Because a run starts at the essence ceiling (`INITIAL_ESSENCE_PER_SPHERE` equals `BASE_MAX_ESSENCE`), nothing is attuned until essence has actually been spent and re-earned — attunement is a record of *use*, not of age.
- **Depth, not a key.** `memberAccess` runs before `isMemberUnlocked`, so a god who attunes to a sphere they do not hold crosses the mark and gains nothing. The entry says this in the same breath as naming THR-870's parked re-key, because "sphere score unlocks cards" is exactly the sentence that would be misread as the pivot having landed.
- **Accrual is net movement across one phase merge.** The plan specified instrumenting "the three divine-economy GraphOp paths"; the implementation banked at the phase-merge seam instead, and the entry documents the shipped semantics including the stated cost — a phase that both grants and spends banks only the difference.

## The `Repertoire` fork, and why both halves were taken

The handoff comment flagged that the proposed definition's core sentence — *"deepens Repertoire card families"* — defines a new term using one that has **zero hits across all nine shards**, which is the drift the UL exists to prevent. It offered two routes: define against `[[Nudge]]` and concrete nouns, or file a companion proposal.

Both, deliberately. The entry avoids the word entirely (it says "the nudge card library" and names the four unlock kinds), so it stands alone today. But *Repertoire* is not incidental in the tree — it names a module, `RepertoireEntry`, `RepertoireContext`, `buildRepertoire`, `repertoireCardCost` and a liveness report — so leaving it undefined is a real gap rather than a stylistic one. Filed as **THR-1186** with the distinctions it owes (repertoire is per-run and library-level, a hand is per-step; access is not unlock; echo cards bypass access entirely). Taking only the first route would have shipped a clean entry and quietly dropped the finding.

## A stale premise, corrected in the taker's favour

The promotion comment recorded that `essenceEarnedBySphere` "does not yet exist in `src/`", consistent with THR-1180 being unbuilt at promotion time. THR-1180 merged eight hours later (`782c8a40`). So the entry documents **shipped code** rather than a design ahead of its field, and every claim in it was read off the implementation — the `[20, 60]` marks off `SPHERE_ATTUNEMENT_THRESHOLDS`, the net-movement semantics off `applyEssenceEarned`'s own rationale, the access-before-unlock ordering off `buildRepertoire`. Three attunement members exist (`card.gambit.attunement.chaos` @20, `card.veil.attunement.darkness` @20, `card.whisper.attunement.light` @60), so the channel is exercised and the entry is not describing an inert path.

## The Cosmology half

The ticket asked for a cross-reference from `Sphere`. It got a sentence rather than a bare See-Also link, because the two readings are genuinely confusable and now sit one line apart: **`[[Sphere Alignment]]` is an actor's affinity for a Sphere (an `aligned_with` edge); `[[Sphere Attunement]]` is the lifetime essence a god has drawn through it. Alignment is who you are; attunement is what you have practised.** A See-Also alone would have put the two names beside each other with nothing saying they are not synonyms.
