# 2026-10-13 — THR-1837: the god's screen fills one gift at a time

**Ticket:** [THR-1837](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1837), the implementation of [THR-1808](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1808) (round-3 PC-7, "no direction"). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-12-thr-1808-post-bond-reveal.md`. Engine + Content + UI.

- **What the player sees.** After the bond the screen shows the time controls, the god's identity, essence, Attention, the hand with Move, the Threads panel, the Chronicle, Read the Threads and Settings. Every other panel arrives with the gift whose words introduce it. The Seat brings Doom, Mandates and Investiture, and its prose now names the Doom and the mandates. The First Word brings Notables. A Path Opens brings the Reaches, World Pulse, the World-Soul and Omens, and its prose now names omens. The First's first chapter brings the Chapter Ledger. All panels that arrive on one render share one toast ("New beside the map: Doom, Mandates and Investiture.") and get a 1px gold ring for 6 s. A line in the top bar says "Watch over Liora. A chapter of her story is waiting →" (the arrow opens the chapter), or "…Let time run…", until her first post-bond chapter resolves. Settings → "Show every panel" turns the staging off.
- **How.** `OPENING_REVEAL_TABLE` (18 rows, `src/data/opening-reveal-table.ts`) and a pure selector `selectOpeningReveal` (`src/engine/openingReveal.ts`) read the bond edge (new `getFirstId`), the doom wake tick, the beat history and The First's chapters. A 200-tick ceiling reveals everything if the spine stalls. `useOpeningReveal` latches each panel and owns the toast, the ring and the setting. Only a session that arrives with The First unbonded is staged (`GameState.openingRevealStaged`, set in the `useSimulation` arrival memo). The CLI, fixtures, `?seeded`, `?spawn=` and warm starts keep the full screen.
- **Executor decisions.** (1) Before the bond, the bond-row panels stay on screen (reason `pre_bond`), not hidden as the plan's E1 reads. The hand holds the card that makes the bond, so hiding it would stall the opening, and the pre-bond screen is exactly THR-1648's. (2) The direction arrow opens The First's unread encounter notification (the Threads-badge path). If that cannot open, it falls back to the Chapter Ledger. (3) The dangling `getAscendantBeats()` pointer in `debug-bridge.d.ts` now points at `beatSchedule().pending`, because `listBeats()` is a catalogue, not pending state. (4) Table order puts Investiture after Mandates, so the toast reads in the plan's own wording.
- **Evidence.** `npm run gate` PASS (test, typecheck, build, encounter, cli-smoke, test-heavy). 24 new unit tests in `openingReveal.test.ts`. Browser (bash-vite :5719 + Playwright, 1920×1080, `?view=game&firstunmet&size=medium`): at the bond, `getOpeningReveal()` gave the bond rows `at_bond`, Doom/Mandates/Investiture `waiting:beat.spine.the_seat`, Notables `waiting:beat.spine.the_first_word` and the four Path rows `waiting:beat.spine.a_path_opens`. The direction line read "Watch over Liora. A chapter of her story is waiting →". After The Seat: Doom, Mandate and Investiture were on screen with `data-newly-revealed`, the toast appeared once, and the direction line was gone after her chapter resolved. "Show every panel" revealed all 18 rows with no toast and no ring. `?seeded`: `staged: false`, full screen. Console: one error only, the local TTS server being off (pre-existing).
- **Noted, not fixed.** The imperative toast channel never prunes expired toasts while the clock is paused, so the reveal toast (like the "A seat is raised" toast beside it) stays until the next push. This is pre-existing behaviour.
