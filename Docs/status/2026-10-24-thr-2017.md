# THR-2017 — "Oaths pay" is judged on the leading Crown

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** Deferred from THR-1961. The crown plan's predicate read "on each seed with a crown, its oath income is 15–35% of its full income at day 60". Once realms court every day, a second Crown forms on some seeds, and that Crown fell outside the band.

**What the re-measure showed** (1080 ticks, medium, on main after THR-1974's wages; `measure:crown` now prints each Crown's vassal count and age):
- seed 42: hold of Witness Skyfield 30% (13 vassals, crowned 38 days), march of Shadow-Kept Dawn 11% (3 vassals, crowned 58 days).
- seed 99: hold of Witness Iron 28% (12 vassals), domain of Open Riverlands 19% (2 vassals).
- seed 7: kingdom of Shade Wooded Ridges 33% (14 vassals). The 36% reading in the ticket is gone since THR-1974.

The low Crown is not young. It is the oldest Crown on seed 42 and holds only three small vassals. The share follows the vassal count. `FEALTY_TAX_SHARE` scales every Crown's oath-to-own odds by the same factor. On seed 42 the two Crowns' odds differ ×3.5, and the band allows only ×3.1, so no tuning value of the share fits both.

**What changed.**
- **Plan doc.** The predicate now reads the leading Crown, the one holding the most oaths. Smaller Crowns are reported, not judged. The re-rule note carries the evidence.
- **Measure.** `measure:crown` reports vassals and Crown age per Crown and prints a `leading crown at day 60 … in 15–35%` verdict line.
- **Constants.** `FEALTY_TAX_SHARE` (0.3, unchanged) carries a comment that cites the measurement.

**Every other predicate on all three seeds:** a Crown forms in days 15–45, force breaks first, 62–79% of factions stay independent, no faction serves two layers, no faction goes into debt, and courtship runs on 87–98% of days.
