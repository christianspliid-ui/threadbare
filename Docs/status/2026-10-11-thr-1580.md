# 2026-10-11 — THR-1580 (slice 1): social leverage compares standing on the reach share

**Ticket:** [THR-1580](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1580) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-24-thr-1575-forecast-window.md` § Systems design 4 · multi-PR deferral, ticket stays open.

- **The reader.** `computeInitialLeverage` (`src/engine/socialLeverage.ts`) gives an actor a starting edge in a social scene when its Gold is ≥ 1.5× the target's (wealth) or its Iron is ≥ 0.20 above (power). Neither is a d100 chance: they compare two agents' standing. They read the pre-refit curve (midpoint 10, k 0.4), which saturates at raw ~20.
- **Measured** (`npm run measure:leverage-scales`, all ordered pairs of living individuals, medium, tick 0). When both agents are past the old knee (raw ≥ 20 in the compared reach), the pre-refit curve fired **0%** of the time on all three seeds (42 · 99 · 7): two seasoned merchants could never out-bid each other. On the reach share, wealth fires 11.0 · 13.4 · 0.0% between two raw-≥20 Gold holders (on seed 7 those holders sit within 1.5× of each other on the capped share) and power fires 15.7 · 13.1 · 17.0% between two raw-≥20 Iron holders. Population-wide it stays close to today's numbers: non-ambient pairs wealth 36.1 · 33.1 · 34.2% → 33.6 · 34.3 · 35.6%, power 27.1 · 33.2 · 28.7% → 25.4 · 23.5 · 23.1% (seeds 42 · 99 · 7). The dice curve would roughly halve power (13–19%), so it was not chosen.
- **Change.** Both comparisons read `computeReachShare` (THR-1562's scale for comparisons of standing); thresholds unchanged. Four `TODO(THR-1580)` sites are gone. The contract `capability-thresholds-read-the-reach-share` gains `socialLeverage.ts` as a read site. Three new tests in `socialLeverage.test.ts` (seasoned pair fires both, near-equals fire neither, Gold vs no Gold fires wealth); before this there were no wealth/power tests.
