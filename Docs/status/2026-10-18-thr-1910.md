# THR-1910 — Inspector S2b: the place, faction, army, artifact and attachment sheets' fields move into the page-layout table

**Shipped 2026-10-18.** Slice S2b of the Inspector plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1876-the-inspector.md` § D7.2, D7.4–D7.7, § D7.11). Every field `LocationProfileModal`, `LocationView`, HexSidebar's Area facts, `FactionSheet`, `ArmySheet`, `ArtifactSheet` and `AttachmentDetailView` show now has a home: a row of `DETAIL_PAGE_REGISTRY`, a header field, a Connections row, the land page, or a stated retirement.

- **Place** (`views/subjects/placeRows.ts`): one place prose (`generateEntityProse`), past line, fortunes, prayer, livelihood, contested, walls, conditions from `has_trait` edges (the old row read a property nothing writes), standing, Place of Power, Area facts (size, feature, danger, sphere strengths as words and bars), guild postings, encounters here. `what_this_place_wants` is hover-only.
- **Faction** (`factionRows.ts`): description and motto, venerates, crown, fealty, agenda (status worded), conclave, means, purse, strongest reaches (word and bar, no score), Realm customs, ranks (no slot counts), recent actions from `factionActionHistory` (keys and outcomes worded). A Realm's header says "Realm". Liege and vassals join Connections (`sworn_to`).
- **Army**: `what_they_are_doing` reads `objective.targetNodeId` (the army type's field — the old read never named the target) and carries the war-aim line with links.
- **Artifact and attachment** (`itemRows.ts`): made by (links), what it does and the catch, traits, tags; the attachment card composes through the table with effect, duration (`CONDITION_DURATIONS`), triggers (payload worded, "(Nx)" gone) and source. `mechanicalSummary` is never shown.
- `allied_with`, `opposed`, `who_leads_them`, `who_gave_it` are hover-only (Connections carries them in the Inspector).

Three pure readers moved out of retiring components so both read one source: `engine/placeConditions.ts`, `engine/ruins/guildPostings.ts`, `engine/views/contentTagChips.ts`.

Evidence: `?view=inspector-lab` (now with an attachment pick) at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440 — 45 cells, `overflowX: false` and no numeral leaks in every cell, console 0 errors. Parity test `src/engine/views/__tests__/sheetParity.s2b.test.ts` green, including the army-objective test. Review gate: two rounds, two findings fixed (condition terms read the writers' fields).
