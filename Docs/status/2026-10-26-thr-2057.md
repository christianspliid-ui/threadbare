# THR-2057 — Six small faults from cold playtest round 5

**Shipped 2026-10-26.** Cold playtest round 5, PC-3 (items 1, 2), PC-6 (items 5, 6), PC-8 (item 4), plus one layout fault (item 3).

**What changed.**

1. **The whisper says what it draws toward.** The confirmation and trace read a noun phrase (`WHISPER_NUDGE_DIRECTIONS` / `describeWhisperDirection`, `premonition-content.ts`): "Elara is subtly drawn toward the way of Veil", not "… toward The way of Veil calls". The button labels are unchanged.
2. **The bond test names The First and keeps her pronouns.** `meeting-bond-test.ts` now carries `{name}` and pronoun tokens in the setup, purpose line, factor lines, card names and effect lines, and the five receptions. `personalizeBondTest` (new `src/engine/bondTestVoice.ts`, reusing `ambitionPronounsFor` / `resolveAmbitionPronouns`) fills them per mortal. `BondBeat` (meeting and short/full rites) and the bond-only rite pass the test through it. Unknown gender reads "they", as before.
3. **The chapter footer wraps instead of crushing.** The hand bar row in `NudgePhaseShell` wraps, and the essence count and the commit button keep one line (`whiteSpace: nowrap`, `flexShrink: 0`), so in a narrow column the legend drops to its own line.
4. **A tooltip no longer opens on mouse focus.** `Tooltip` opens on focus only when it is `:focus-visible` (keyboard). Focus handed back by a closing panel no longer leaves a popup over the next control, eating its first click. Synthetic focus events and browsers without the selector keep the old behaviour.
5. **A God's Will compulsion reads "Compelled", with a cause.** `applyCompulsionChoice` writes `interventionType: 'compel'` (new `DivineInfluenceKind`, kept out of the `InterventionType` toolkit tables) and `cause` (the encounter's name). The chip shows "Compelled", with the hover "Your will drives them toward {cause}, fading in {duration}." (`INFLUENCE_CHIP_HOVER_CAUSE`).
6. **The Seat names its place before the press.** `previewHomeSeat` (`ascendantBeatSeeding.ts`) shares one resolver with `seedHomeSeat`. The beat prose now reads "{place}, where {first} stands, becomes your seat" (or "the nearest town to {first}"), replacing "Bind a place to yourself".

**Evidence.** `npm run gate`: PASS. New `coldPlaytestRound5Faults.test.ts` (9 tests: items 1, 2, 5, 6, and preview = seeded seat), plus 2 Tooltip tests (mouse focus stays closed, keyboard focus opens). Browser (bash-vite :5343, Playwright, 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`): The Seat beat read "The Bone Coast, where Kael Thornweaver stands, becomes your seat". The chapter hand bar at 600 px kept the button (52 px) and essence count (16 px) on one line each.
