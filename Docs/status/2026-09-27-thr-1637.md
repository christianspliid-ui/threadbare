# 2026-09-27 — THR-1637: found things are dressed by the world's own past

**Ticket:** [THR-1637](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1637) · **Project:** Powers & Item Generation · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-26-thr-1570-seeded-item-generator.md` § Systems design (`worldContext.ts`) · **Pillars:** Engine (Content: the wording tables; UI: N/A — no surface changed).

## What was wrong

`buildItemWorldContext` read a maker, their faction, place and culture — enough for a masterwork — and left the past empty. The nine found cores (the weapon that chose its bearer, a saint's relic, salvage from a disaster, a monster's trophy, the heirloom, …) tell their story through a dead person, a named disaster or a monster host, so a found thing could only ever be dressed from the review world's hand-made people and places.

## What shipped

- **The live world context reads the past** (`src/engine/itemGenerator/worldContext.ts`), by default whenever there is no maker (`past` option):
  - **the dead** — retained `deceased` mortals (band, plot, fight, battle and commission deaths; never the living or a monster), most recently fallen first. Their **deed** is the battle they fought in (`participated_in` → a `battle_fought` record), else their trade at their home; their **fate** is their `deathCause` at the place they fell, and names `slainBy` when that mortal is still in the graph;
  - **the disasters** — `battle_fought` records: a siege, or a field battle told as a last stand, worded from `resolutionType` alone; one per place and kind, the most recent kept;
  - **the monster hosts** — `isMonsterFaction` actors at a lair that is not cleared, with a one-of ("a golem of the Blighted Golem Cluster") and the sphere's ward.
  The factions, cultures and places the past names join the tables, at the settlement tier.
- **Every word is a named row** in `src/data/item-generator-tables.ts` § *The live world's past* (fates by cause, deeds by trade, battle wording by type and outcome, monster words by sphere, three caps).
- **Coherence fix in `generateItem`:** a line naming a person *and* an event is only told at that person's own battle — a live world's dead mostly fought in none. Review-world output is unchanged (every review hero has an event).
- **Places read mid-sentence:** "lived at the Shattered Sanctum", not "The Shattered Sanctum".
- **The salvage core's last-stand name word** `Ford` → `Unbroken` (a live field battle is not at a ford).
- **Review levers:** CLI `generate items N --origin found --live` and `__DEBUG.mintGeneratedItem({ origin: 'found' })` dress found things from this world's past, and fall back to the review world only while the world has none (the CLI says so).

## Result

- Seed 42 medium, tick 160 (heavy test `worldContextPast.live.test.ts`): the past holds 10 dead, 6 battles, 12 monster hosts. **129 found items across every found core × every legal band, zero validator problems on the first draw, zero read-back failures, every found core fires**, and no hero is told at a battle they were not at.
- Seed 42 small has no retained dead by tick 160 — the cores that tell only of a dead person are then ineligible, which is the world's truth, not the generator's.

## Not changed

- The found origin still has no production minting point — that is [THR-1626](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1626) (reward draws), which this unblocks. THR-1626 owns the interface-map contract for the production read.
