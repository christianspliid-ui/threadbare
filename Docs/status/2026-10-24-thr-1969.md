# THR-1969 — God's stake S3: the market cards and the two kept promises

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** Slice S3 of `Docs/plans/2026-10-17-thr-1935-the-gods-stake-in-the-economy.md` (§ Engine 7, § Content S3, § Done when S3).

**What changed.**
- **Fortune's Favour / Ruin** (`action.means.fortunes_favour`, `action.means.ruin`; tier-2 market, Matter / Entropy). The outcome table runs an `economy_shift` with the new `bandStep` (`1` / `-1`). `meansBandStepAmount` (`mortalMeans.ts`) computes the amount from the mortal's live wealth, so the card moves exactly one word: 45 → 60 (Wealthy), 45 → 39 (Getting by). The receipt is the existing Means change with the band named. Sheet reasons `divine_favour` / `divine_ruin`. **Id deviation from the plan:** the plan named them `mortal.*`. `divine.*` is reserved for the eight decaying-influence interventions, and a new prefix needs two catalog registrations. `action.*` is the existing family of god cards aimed at a mortal.
- **Bless the Holding** (`loc.bless_holding`; tier-2 market, Order). The new `bless_holding` op writes the god's `blessed` edge (kind `holding_yield`, `untilTick` a year on, `mult` 1.5) on a place a faction controls or a mortal owns. One edge per god and place: a recast extends the year and never stacks. An unheld place is refused. `computePurseIncome` (town tax) and `collectHoldingPayments` (freehold, tithe) multiply that place's term while it lives. The purse day sweeps it at `untilTick`. The receipt line reads "{holder}'s yield from {place} swells this year. The blessing fades at the year's end."
- **Claim Resource pays what it claims.** `ControlSpec.incomeFrom: 'claimed_good'`. `spawnControlEffect` binds the most abundant deposit, and `phaseControlEffects` credits the good's sphere × 0.5 × its stock tier (0.5 / 1 / 1.5) and carries the resolved `perTickIncome` for the forecast. A good with no class pays the old 0.5 Matter.
- **Open the Markets' thieves' guild.** The card's new `mark_markets_opened` op stamps `marketsOpenedTick`. From 8 ticks after, for 10 days, while the town holds prosperity 75, `scoreUnifiedBoard` multiplies a Shadow-led resident's `cell.create.faction` by 1.5 (`thievesGuildOffer` on the entry and the board trace). The founded guild's calling (Shadow → thieves) keeps the promise. `LOC_OPEN_MARKETS_TRADE_RANGE_HEXES` had no reader and is deleted.
- **Content and canon.** Effect lines and technical effects for the four cards, the rulebook paragraph *Your hand in the world's purse*, three interface contracts, ledger rows for the two ops, the wiring checklist section, seven reference pages. `effectSourceFor` now counts an outcome table as a wired aftermath effect.

**Evidence.** `godsStake.test.ts` (18 tests) binds every S3 Done-when predicate at fixture level. The tests include the next `economy.purse.day` trace's tax ×1.5 and its end at `untilTick`, and 0.75 / 0.25 Life on surplus / scarce grain. Live CLI (seed 42, medium): Fortune's Favour took Nesrin 11 → 20 (Destitute → Getting by), and Ruin took Solenne 14 → 7. Solenne was already Destitute, so the poor-loss scale softened the fall. **Not live-proven:** the 600-tick predicate for the thieves' guild. Seed 42 has one Shadow-led resident (Eidra @ Wraithwood). With her town stamped at prosperity 80, nobody founded a guild in 120 ticks, so the board boost is asserted at fixture level only.
