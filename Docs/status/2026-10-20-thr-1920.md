# THR-1920 — Encounter debts count

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** A debt granted by an encounter now counts as a debt.

**Cause.** Two writers put agreements on `relates_to` edges in two shapes. The reward writer (`instantiateAgreementReward`, the path every encounter `attachment_grant` of an `agreement.*` template takes) writes `agreement: true` with `agreementType`, `tier`, `terms` and `ticksRemaining` beside it. The dev fixture writes `agreement: { type, tier, terms, … }`. The debt counter, the attachments list and the ascendant bar's vows row all read `agreement.type`, which is undefined on `true`. So the three authored debt grants (`the_garrisons_price` ×2, `cathedral_loan`) never counted toward debt-laden, and every reward agreement was labelled and coloured a pact.

**Fix.** One reader, `readAgreement` (`src/engine/agreementShape.ts`), accepts both shapes and returns the nested one. `countDebts` (now exported), `getAgentAttachments` and the bar's `extractChips` go through it. Neither writer changed, so saved games of both shapes keep reading.

**Direction.** The reward writer always puts the debtor at the edge's source. A flat-shape debt therefore counts only for its source, so the encounter's creditor (the dean's factor in `cathedral_loan`) does not turn debt-laden too. Nested-shape debts keep their old either-direction count.

**Term.** Nothing on the graph expires an agreement edge: no pass counts its `ticksRemaining` down or removes it when the term ends. Counting flat debts with no term check would have made debt-laden permanent (the review gate caught this). `agreementLapsed` reads the term instead: a flat debt stops counting once `tick` reaches `acquiredTick + ticksRemaining`, or when its edge is `active: false`. The edges themselves still linger after their term, and the sheet still shows the full term without counting it down. That gap predates this change and is filed as THR-1999.

**Evidence.** `src/engine/__tests__/agreementShape.test.ts` grants `agreement.debt.minor` through the real reward path: `countDebts` returns 1 for the debtor and 0 for the creditor; two debts make the debtor debt-laden on the next economic-traits pass, and it stays; a debt stops counting when its term runs out and debt-laden clears; the attachments list files it as a debt; the bar paints it with the debt swatch.

**Not in scope.** `socialOutcome.createOrUpdateAgreement` writes a third shape (`agreementType` with no `agreement` flag). No agreement reader sees those edges at all; that is a separate question for the social-agreement system.
