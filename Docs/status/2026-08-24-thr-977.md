# THR-977 — the skill line's pips are gone; capability was never an odds effect

**Date:** 2026-08-24 · **Ticket:** [THR-977](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-977/factor-line-delta-mixes-two-scales-the-skill-line-carries-absolute) · **Project:** Encounter Experience

Filed while shipping [THR-970](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-970/factor-lines-carry-a-signed-delta-for-the-pip-row-and-nothing-renders),
which rendered the factor-line pip row that [THR-892](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-892/derived-factor-lines-agenthexglobalcarryover-modifiers-on-the-test)'s
`delta` field was added for. Rendering it made a pre-existing model inconsistency visible: two
different quantities were reaching one pip vocabulary.

## The verdict: option 3, and it is Law 10 rather than a design call

**`deriveSkillLine` no longer sets `delta`, so the skill line draws no pip row.** The ticket
offered four options and said *"this needs a design call, not a fix"*. It does not, for two
reasons that both post-date the filing:

1. **THR-972 shipped two hours and twenty minutes later** (`016fec4e`, PR #1268) and ruled that
   *"pips mean only 'effect on the odds' everywhere"* — a director-reviewed verdict. That rules
   out option 4 (accept it) outright, and it discharged the `Mutex with: THR-972` the ticket was
   parked behind. Nobody re-read the ticket after, which is why it sat in `Idea` for 22 days.
2. **The rule is now written down as binding law.** `Docs/design-system/laws.md` **Law 10** — [E],
   sourced to THR-972 §5 — reads: *"Distinct meanings get visually distinct vocabularies … pips
   mean only 'effect on the odds', everywhere. Never reuse a glyph row for an unrelated
   quantity."* The skill line was a Law 10 violation, not an open question. Under
   `Docs/canon/process.md` § User review interface **rule 4** the remaining fork is only *how*,
   so this was the executor's call to make and record.

## What was actually wrong

`DerivedFactorLine.delta` was documented *"Raw 0–1 magnitude, for the pip row"*, and the ticket
described two producers. There are **four**, and the skill line is the lone outlier:

| Producer | `delta` | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| `deriveSkillLine` | `safeCapability` | **absolute capability** — the outlier |
| modifier loop | `value` | signed forecast contribution |
| carryover | `forecastDelta ?? 0` | signed forecast contribution |
| Whisper reveal | `0`, with a comment saying a reveal shifts no odds | contribution, measurably zero |

Three of four were contribution-shaped. The reveal is also the precedent for the fix: the adapter
already maps `delta === 0` to `undefined`, and the model contract already says *absent means draw
no pips, never draw zero pips*.

## Reproducing the premise, then fixing it

The ticket asked that the 2026-08-02 reading be reproduced rather than assumed. It reproduces,
more starkly than filed — rendering the real `NudgePhaseShell` against a `legendary`-tier actor:

```
BEFORE   id=skill:iron                 delta = 0.9999863259909154
         "Sera Vance is legendary in iron.★★★★★"    pips: "Fated, 5 of 5"  tier=fated
         id=equipment:item.rusted_key  delta = 0.06
         "Sera Vance carries the Rusted Key.●○○○○"  pips: "Faint, 1 of 5"  tier=faint

AFTER    id=skill:iron                 delta = undefined (no pips)
         "Sera Vance is legendary in iron."         pips: ABSENT
         id=equipment:item.rusted_key  delta = 0.06
         "Sera Vance carries the Rusted Key.●○○○○"  pips: "Faint, 1 of 5"  tier=faint
```

Two adjacent rows in one list: *"Fated, 5 of 5"* meaning **"she is very good at iron"**, beside
*"Faint, 1 of 5"* meaning **"this moves the odds a little"**. Same vocabulary, unrelated
quantities, indistinguishable to the player. **Nothing is lost by removing the row** — the word
`legendary` survives and is where the magnitude was always meant to live (Law 13, words never
numerals); `deriveSkillLine`'s own contract already said so, which is why its polarity never
flips.

## Where the fix landed, and one place it deliberately did not

`delta` is now optional on `DerivedFactorLine` and documented as a contribution and nothing else.
The three docstrings that carried the ambiguity are tightened — but **not** the way the
coordination block suggested. It asked that `OddsPips`' *"a `forecastDelta` **or** a success
probability"* be narrowed "to the one meaning that survives". Both meanings survive:
`NudgePhaseShell:590` passes `hand.forecast.probability`, deliberately, because THR-890 put the
forecast into the card row's language so the player reads one vocabulary across the surface.
Narrowing that doc would have made it false.

The sharper statement — and the guard rail that actually prevents the next occurrence — is that
both legitimate values live in **odds-space**: either a movement of the odds, or where they
stand. Capability is in neither. `OddsPipsProps.value` now says that, and names this ticket as
the precedent.
