# THR-2072 — A bounty is collected in a duel

**Shipped 2026-10-27.** M3: Dynamic Economy; plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-26-thr-2072-bounty-collected-in-a-duel.md`, decided by the design lane under delegation.

**What changed.** A faction's bounty used to be a promise nobody kept: its coin left the world and `hunted` was never lifted. Issue Bounty now posts only on a living individual mortal, at most one open per faction per target, and holds its 10 coin on the bounty as `escrow`. While it is open, a member of the issuing faction who shares a place with the target may come for them: the grudge trigger gained a bounty licence (`runGrudgeDuels`, own stream, `BOUNTY_DUEL_CHANCE_BASE` 0.01 × courage) that spawns `fight.duel.bounty` ("The Price on Their Head", hunter acts) or `fight.duel.bounty_defence` ("Hunted", when only the target is threaded). If the hunter beats the target (yielded, spared, mauled or slain), `bountyCollectBranch` (a new `FIGHT_END_BRANCHES` entry) calls `collectBounty` (`src/engine/factionBounties.ts`): escrow paid out, a decider hunter +1 Means step, a new `collected` edge (hunter → bounty), `hunted` lifted, and a chronicle line "Edda Vane collected the price on Joss Harl's head at Millbrook." A target who runs gets a routine "got away" line. `closeBounties` (every tick) lapses an uncollected bounty and refunds its escrow. The target's sheet Blood row reads "The Adventurers Guild has put a price on their head." Trace `economy.bounty`; debug `__DEBUG.getBounties(factionId?)`; CLI `spawn duel <hunter> --with <target> --bounty`; switch `FACTION_BOUNTIES_ENABLED`.

**Tuning.** The plan's 0.02 measured 25 / 17 / 27 bounty duels a month on seeds 42 / 99 / 7 (27 is above the plan's 25 ceiling), so the kill criterion halved it once to 0.01: 22 / 11 / 25. Most of the bound comes from the pair's 80-tick fight cooldown, not the chance. If a later change lifts the count past 25, the next lever is `BOUNTY_EXPIRY` or the cooldown, not the chance.

**Evidence.** `factionBounties.test.ts` (20), `factionBountiesSwitch.test.ts` (1). `npm run census:bounties` (medium, 360 ticks, one seed per process): collected 7 / 3 / 4, no bounty-duel deaths, every ledger balances. Browser: the Blood row on a live hunted mortal at 1920×1080 (Playwright, worktree Vite).

**Deviation from the plan.** The hunter's stakes `won` / `lost` say "an old comrade's head" instead of "{target}'s head": the stakes builder never fills a token inside `won`, so `{target}` would have shown as literal text.
