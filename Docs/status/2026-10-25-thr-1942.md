# THR-1942 — the hand bar names the cards below it

**Shipped 2026-10-25.** A hand of five to eight cards wraps to two rows (four per row, Law 33), and at 1920×1080 the second row scrolls under the stuck hand bar. The design lane (2026-10-24) kept the cards as they are and gave the bar one line: while cards sit under it, it says **"2 more cards below"** ("1 more card below" for one), and clicking the line scrolls the column until the last card clears the bar.

**Change.**
- `NudgePhaseShell.tsx`: `nudge-hand-more`, a quiet ghost `Button` above the commit row, after the miss line when both show. It shows only while the column overflows and the count is above 0.
- `countCardsBelowBar` (pure): a card counts as below when part of it is under the bar's solid base and less than `HAND_MORE_VISIBLE_FRACTION` (0.6) of it shows above that base. The bar's top `HAND_BAR_FADE_PX` is a see-through fade, so it does not hide a card.
- The count re-measures on scroll, resize, card intersection and animation/transition end. The veil's entrance moves the cards by transform, which fires no scroll or resize event, so without the intersection trigger the first count was stale (6 of 6 on the oath rite).
- Words: `NUDGE_HAND_MORE_ONE` / `NUDGE_HAND_MORE_MANY` / `nudgeHandMoreLine` beside `NUDGE_EMPTY_HAND_LINE`.
- Debug: `getStage().handCardsBelowBar` reads the bar's `data-cards-below`.

**Evidence.** Playwright at 1920×1080, bash-vite on port 5297. `spawn=encounter.town.oath_breaker_rite` (six cards): the line reads "2 more cards below", `handCardsBelowBar` 2, commit bottom 1022 px. After the click: scrollTop 453, every card's bottom above the bar, line gone, count 0, `scrollingBelowCap` []. `spawn=encounter.slice.unsafe_bridge` (five cards): "1 more card below", count 1; after the click: scrollTop 357 (the column's maximum), line gone, count 0. Unit tests (`nudgeHandMore.test.tsx`, 15) cover the words, the hidden test on stubbed geometry, the click's scroll delta, the four-card and fitting hands, and the miss-line order. `npm run gate` code track: 6/6 PASS.
