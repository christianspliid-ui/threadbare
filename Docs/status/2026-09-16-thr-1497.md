# THR-1497 — the catalyst reaches the live board, and two things it needed on the way

*Deferral from [THR-1488](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1488) (content model slice 4).
That slice activated `catalystQuery`, migrated all 35 pack carriers, and then measured that
under `UNDERTAKING_MODEL: 'cells'` a profile's `templateIds` are never walked — so the field
resolved correctly and no mortal on a default seed could reach it. This ticket held the decision.*

## The decision

**The catalyst belongs on the cell.** `UNDERTAKING_CELL_CATALYSTS` in
`src/data/undertaking-cells.ts` is a bounded authored table by cell id — the THR-1438
`UNDERTAKING_CELL_PHRASES` pattern — read once at synthesis into `catalystQuery`, and
overridable per package through `UndertakingCellOverride.catalystQuery`. Not the registry's
verb semantic: those entries are graph-op functions, and a family is content, not an op. Not
the legacy pack arm: starting the dead model to reach one field would be the wrong kind of
reachable.

Eleven cells carry one, and **the family is chosen for what the work disturbs, never for who
does it**: building or raising a settlement stirs the Builders' Fellowship whether a zealot or a
merchant laid the stone (`#fellowship_errand`); a lane opened, widened or blockaded is the
Consortium's problem whoever raised it (`#consortium_errand`); an army raised or reinforced
draws the Company (`#company_errand`); a masterwork brings the next commission
(`#craft_commission`); a ring founded is the Thieves' Guild's competition (`#thieves_errand`); a
settlement razed is the Watch's (`#watch_errand`). Every row is on a cell a shipped profile
walks, and every family has an individual-performable, settlement-accepting member — asserted
in `undertakingCellCatalysts.test.ts`, because the seeding site's eligibility filter runs *after*
the query and a family that resolves but has no such member seeds nothing.

## Two defects the live claim exposed, both in scope

**The site was registered and never emitted.** `resolveSeedByQuery` traced every seed as
`site: 'encounter_seed'`. `undertaking_catalyst` sat in `CONTENT_QUERY_SITES`, the census
watched it, the `catalyst_seeded` live claim filtered on it — and no `traceContentQuery` call
ever named it. Even with a cell carrying the query the claim could not pass. The site is now
derived from the seed's provenance (`seedQuerySite`, off `STRATEGIC_CATALYST_REACTION_ID`, one
constant for the planter and the reader). Falsified: reverting to the literal fails exactly the
two provenance tests.

**A mortal inside a Place had no subtype.** The eligibility filter read `locationSubtype` off
the raw `located_at` node. A Place — a market district, an inn, a palace keep — is
`type: 'location'` with `parentLocationId` and no subtype of its own, so the read returned
`undefined` there and every gated family withered for a target who was, by the position
model's own rule, exactly where the family wanted them. The 200-tick probe showed three of ten
catalyst seeds firing from inside a Place of a settlement the family accepted, all three empty.
`seedTargetSubtype` now resolves through `resolveToParentLocation`. This is the aftermath
path's filter too, so `encounter_seed` sequels for a mortal at an inn stop withering as well.
Falsified: the raw read fails the Place test alone.

And one the proof needed: `check:undertaking-live` stopped ticking the instant the project left
`active`, while the seed comes due `STRATEGIC_CATALYST_SEED_DELAY_TICKS` later. It now settles
up to `CATALYST_SETTLE_TICKS` when a catalyst was rolled, reads the roll off the history entry's
`catalystSeeded` (a missed roll is `not_declared`, not a site that failed), and takes the spawned
action's `spawnedFromSeedId` as durable evidence — a medium world emits more traces per tick than
the 2000-entry ring holds, so a mid-tick `content.query_resolved` is often gone before the
tick's harvest. That field is now stamped on **every** seed spawn, not only one carrying a
causation source (the THR-143 test that pinned the old behaviour is updated to say why).

## The live claim

Seed 42 / medium / 200 ticks, a per-tick probe over `state.unifiedActions`: **14 catalyst seeds
planted, 6 spawned an errand** — `tg.quest.pocket_run` ×2, `tg.quest.warehouse_raid`,
`bf.quest.lay_foundation`, `bf.quest.repair_wall`, `mc.quest.siege_work` — three of them from
inside a Place. `check:undertaking-live cell.create.network --seed 3` passes `catalyst_seeded`
(`tg.quest.fence_goods`). The withered runs now say where the actor stood (`loc_68 (fort)`,
`loc_37 (ruins)`): the review lever's first actor is a garrison mortal, and a Fellowship errand
does not come to a fort. That is the THR-1488 asymmetry, by design and now legible.

The census row for the site still reads 0/0 at 200 ticks — the ring — which is why the durable
record matters and why the canon page says to lower `--ticks` before calling a site silent.

**Closing predicate:** "a pack template's `catalystQuery` is unreachable while
`UNDERTAKING_MODEL === 'cells'` and no cell declares one." Asserted false as a conjunction, off
the live flag and the profiles the board reads through `profileWorkIds`, in
`undertakingCellCatalysts.test.ts`. Emptying the table fails it.
