# THR-1888 — Design requests close when their Done-when is met

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** The design lane may now close a design-request ticket itself when its Done-when names only filed tickets or a recorded decision, provided the closing comment quotes the Done-when and links every output. No close keyword is involved. This matches how the lane already worked: it closed THR-1782, THR-1784 and THR-1805–1807 that way. It also stops the opposite case. THR-1785 was left in `Todo` "for a one-click close", waited ~17.5 h for Christian, and kept warm playtest round 2's gate shut for that time.

**What changed.**
- `.claude/skills/design-lane/SKILL.md` § The boundary: a new "Design-request carve-out" bullet next to the wayfinder carve-out.
- `Docs/canon/process.md` § Merge-gated Done: the rule now lists both carve-outs (wayfinder and design request), so the canon and the skill say the same thing.
- `.claude/skills/wayfinder/SKILL.md`: its "nothing else" scope line now points to the second carve-out.
- `keep-work-flowing-cc` is unchanged. It only scans parks that are `In Dev`, and these tickets are in `Todo`.

**Evidence.** This change is docs-only. The output of `npm run gate` is recorded in the PR.
