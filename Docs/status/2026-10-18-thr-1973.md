# THR-1973 — armies last, and realms raise again

**Shipped 2026-10-18.** Every army used to bleed out within a week: a paid, supplied host still lost about 15 cohesion a day and nothing restored it, and seed 42 fielded no army by day 30. Two rules fix it. **Garrison:** an army that is paid, supplied, out of battle and standing in a settlement its own faction holds pays no attrition and heals `ARMY_GARRISON_RECOVERY` (0.5 a tick) up to its maximum; the march, an empty purse or an empty larder still wear it down. **Standing armies:** realms and free companies raise again whenever they field none and can pay, whatever their ambition. The refusal that kept them armyless was `not_military` — a faction ambition never ends on its own, so a realm or free company that drew a trade ambition never passed the military-ambition gate again. `armyRefusal` names the failing gate; `npm run measure:army-census` counts refusals per faction per day.

Evidence: fixture (`armyAttrition.test.ts`) — a paid, supplied host at rest in its own town keeps cohesion 100 for 30 days; heavy (`armyLongevity.heavy.test.ts`) — seed 42 medium over 90 days meets the army-days and re-raise predicates.
