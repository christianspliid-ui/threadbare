# THR-1461 — Kael's whisper opens Kael's sheet

The premonition's name control named one mortal and opened another's profile: click *Kael Thornweaver* on the whisper, get Thorne's sheet, `aria-label="Close profile for Thorne"`. Laws 1 and 33 — a control whose effect does not match its label.

## The fix is two lines, and the interesting part is which two

**Not** the one the ticket's own *Likely cause* prescribed. That version — "pass `subjectId` to the sheet opener explicitly" — was tried on the live surface by [THR-1477](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1477) the day before and **reproduces the symptom rather than fixing it**. The sheet has two inputs and only one of them is the one the caller names:

> `GameView` renders `<AgentProfileModal card={agentInfoCard} />` gated on `profileModalAgentId && agentInfoCard` — and `agentInfoCard` is memoised on **`selectedAgentId`**, not on `profileModalAgentId`.

So `openAgentProfileForId`, a bare `setProfileModalAgentId`, produces a sheet whose *id* is the subject and whose *content* is whoever was selected before. Nothing throws. A sheet opens. It is the wrong person's, and it looks like a rendering fault rather than a routing one.

The two lines: `PremonitionModal`'s `onViewAgent` becomes `(agentId: string) => void` and passes `premonition.agentId` from both the name and the portrait; `GameView` wires it to `openAgentSheetForId` — the both-ids primitive THR-1477 built for the encounter veil and deliberately left the premonition to adopt.

## The coordination instruction that was reversed, and why

The handoff's mutex line said: *if THR-1490 has landed, repoint through the router rather than the raw opener.* [THR-1490](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1490) **had** landed. The instruction is still declined, on a measured ground the author could not have had:

**the router's `agent` sheet arm is `handleAgentSelect`** (`useSheetOpeners`, `useNotificationNavigation.ts:56`), which opens the **ActionDrawer at z 40**. The premonition holds z 60. Routing through it would have swapped a wrong-mortal sheet for an invisible drawer — the exact Law 21 dead click THR-1477 removed from the veil. That is correct behaviour for a notification click-through and wrong for any caller behind a full-screen interrupt, which is the distinction `openAgentSheetForId` exists to draw. Recorded per THR-688 rule B: a mutex or routing instruction may be reversed when its stated reason is verifiably inapplicable.

## Why three test files and not one

Two of them would both have passed while the bug shipped. The component called the handler it was given; the handler set the id it was given; the defect lived in the **seam** — which opener the host reached for — and a unit test on either side cannot see a seam.

So the third file, `premonitionOpensSubjectSheet.test.tsx`, renders the seam: the real hook, the real premonition, the real sheet, behind a copy of GameView's own `profileModalAgentId && agentInfoCard` gate. The gate is copied deliberately rather than simplified — mounting the modal on the profile id alone would test a surface the game does not have, and is precisely why the wrong sheet rendered instead of no sheet.

Each file carries a falsification arm, and the composed one's arm is the strongest evidence here: the same harness with `onViewAgent={() => openAgentProfileForId(premonition.agentId)}` restored — the shipped wiring, verbatim — reproduces *Kael's whisper, Thorne's sheet* and is asserted to do so.

## The four that were left

`openAgentProfileForId` has four other reachable callers, all in `GameView`: the champion chip (5063), `AgentProfileModal`'s own cast links (5165), the `ChapterLedger` (5188), the authored-moment overlay (5421). Same defect, measured, filed as [THR-1500](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1500) with the membership predicate and two judgement calls written out — 5165 is not a pure swap, because navigating between sheets would also move the world's selection behind the modal.

`__DEBUG.openAgentSheet` (2022) is **not** a member and the ticket says so: it calls `gotoAgent` first, which ends in `handleAgentSelect`, so its selection is already right. Right by ordering rather than by design — but not the bug, and counting it would have inflated the sweep.
