### THR-1215 — the encounter portfolio, measured (2026-08-24)

Step 1 of the director's authoring order had no reference to read, so every factory run
would have re-derived "what does the game need" from scratch — or skipped it. Now there is
one: `Docs/audits/2026-08-24-encounter-portfolio-assessment.md`, census plus gap list plus
a ranked target mix, pointed at from the spec's § Authoring order step 1.

**Measured, not read.** The census bundles the live `UNIFIED_ACTION_TEMPLATES` array and
counts it. **687 templates; 466 are mortal-facing encounters; `check:encounter` sees 195 of
them** — it scopes on the literal `encounter.` prefix, so the 184 guild-family templates and
87 borderland/social/tavern/old-branching ones are checked by nothing. Of the 195 in scope,
**185 sit on `RETROFIT_PENDING`** and **10 are composition-complete** (6 slice, 4 company).

**The director's flagged skew, stated exactly.** Thirteen templates in the entire corpus
declare a setting envelope, all nudge-era. **Six of the eight slice encounters open on a
roadside.** The four company-drama scenes are the only content reaching `ruin`,
`stronghold`, `arcane` or `battlefield` — one opening each — and both epic classes belong to
templates whose scenes are a held gate and a recruitment conversation. **`battlefield` has
one authored opening in the game and nothing fights in it.**

**Three gaps the spec's list did not have.** Zero `{cast:*}` tokens across all 195
`encounter.*` — every scene person is inline prose. **One** template applies or removes a
condition, though `conditions` is a mature target-freely system and the traveling-agent core.
One omen emitter. Genre bands are not empty on paper (23 ruin-and-delve, 20 war-adjacent, 13
deep-magic) but **every one of them is legacy** — nudge-native content is 7 camp chores, 8
roadside vignettes, 4 company scenes and one ascension. The game's new voice has only ever
spoken slice-of-life.

**The tension named and resolved on the engineering side.** The genre bar (war, sieges,
monsters, ruins, prophecy, deep magic, factional collapse) maps almost exactly onto systems
the catalog marks deferred, which reads as a contradiction. It is not one: catalog §7 says
deferred systems *"may appear as flavor, never as required mechanics"* — the tier governs
what an encounter is **built on**, not what it is **about**. A siege runs on movement +
conditions + carryover + forks + items, all mature, with the siege as the scene. Nothing
blocks epic subject matter today except that nobody authored it. That reading is the agent's
call; the mix is not.

**Recommended mix, awaiting the director's ruling:** siege and war-band ×6, ruins and delve
×5, monsters and hunt ×4, prophecy ×3, deep magic ×3, factional collapse ×3 — siege first
because it is the largest gap, opens the two emptiest setting classes, and forces
`conditions` into a corpus where one template uses it. Trade (32 legacy), camp chores (33)
and building (10) are deliberately excluded — not gaps.

**The 1,200-hook corpus was unavailable** (`.gitignore:73` hides `Design/research/`), so the
genre gap list is built from the director's stated bar plus the spec's recorded mechanical
gaps. Said so in the doc rather than implying it was consulted. Docs-only diff — classified
empty; no tests, typecheck or build owed or run.
