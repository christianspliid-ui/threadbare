# 2026-10-14 — THR-1793: every town has a people

**Ticket:** [THR-1793](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1793). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-13-thr-1793-every-town-has-a-people.md`. Engine only (Content N/A: existing culture readers reach the newly linked towns; UI N/A: the shipped "{culture} fringe" label renders on more towns).

- **What the player sees.** A town far from any capital now belongs to the nearest people as its fringe (*Varn fringe* in the hex panel), and the NPCs minted there inherit that culture. Before this change two in five mortals on seed 42 belonged to no people.
- **Root cause.** NPCs already copy their town's culture. 24 settlements on seed 42 had no culture themselves, because the fringe pass stopped at 4 hexes from a heartland, a speed cut made during THR-1632.
- **How.** `DEFAULT_WORLD_SCENARIO.cultureFringeMaxHexes` now references `CULTURE_FRINGE_MAX_HEXES_MAX` (24), declared above the default so the reference is not a TDZ read. `WORLD_SCENARIO_TODAY` is unchanged (still 0). No code path changed.
- **Tests.** Three new predicates in `worldScenario.test.ts` on a generated world: the default equals the ceiling; no fringe-subtype place other than `loc.start` is cultureless within 24 hexes of a heartland; every `npc_*` whose home settlement has a culture carries one. The first two fail on the old default (checked).
- **Evidence.** `npm run gate` and `--final` verdicts plus the `measure:tick-cost` pairs are in the PR body.
