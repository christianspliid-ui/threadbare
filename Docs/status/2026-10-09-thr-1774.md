# 2026-10-09 — THR-1774: every world draws its doom

**Ticket:** [THR-1774](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1774) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-08-thr-1774-doom-archetype-draw.md`

Every run used to play Breach. Now each world draws one of the seven dooms when it is made, and the player is told which one.

- **The draw.** `selectDoomArchetype(seed, identityKey)` (`src/engine/doomArchetypeSelection.ts`) makes one `mulberry32` draw at even odds (`DOOM_ARCHETYPE_DRAW_WEIGHTS`, all 1). The draw is keyed on the seed plus `DOOM_ARCHETYPE_DRAW_SEED_OFFSET` (194087) and the god's stored hunger id. `initializeGameState` draws whenever no archetype is passed. An explicit archetype always wins. The draw is traced as `doom.archetype_drawn` and can be read through `getOpeningState().doomArchetype`. At seed 42 the twelve hungers draw 6 distinct dooms, and each doom lands near 1/7 over 6,000 draws (tested).
- **Showcase and lever.** `?seeded` and `?firstunmet` pin Breach (`DEV_SHOWCASE_DOOM_ARCHETYPE`). `?doom=<archetype>` overrides the doom on any route.
- **Named.** The wake toast now adds "The Age of the {Name} has begun.", and the same line is a new Chronicle entry. Stage pop-ups and the doom bar use the display name, never the lowercase key.
- **The sigil agrees with the cards.** `DOOM_ARCHETYPE_SPHERE` lives in `src/data/doom-archetype-presentation.ts`, and a test holds it equal to `getDoomCardSpheres`. The sigils are: Convergence Force, Changing Chaos, Ascension Spirit, Reckoning Mind. Breach, Sundering and Failing show their glyph. The sigil has a `doom.<archetype>` tooltip; there are seven, each ≤ 200 characters.
- **Tooltip stacking.** The shared `Tooltip` now hides itself while the pointer is over a nested trigger ("innermost trigger wins"). Without that, the doom bar's own tooltip opened on top of the sigil's.

**Evidence.** In the browser at 1920×1080, `?seeded&doom=reckoning` showed a Mind sigil with the tooltip "Reckoning … It presses Mind." The Chronicle card "The Age of the Reckoning" appeared after the wake. `?seeded` and `?firstunmet` both read `breach`.
