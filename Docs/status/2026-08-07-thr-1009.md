# THR-1009 — Older panels adopt the Law primitives

**Shipped 2026-08-07.** Three pre-nudge-era surfaces now render game entities through the shared primitives instead of as bare text, sub-floor icons, and registry-bypassing tooltips.

## What changed

**`HexDetailView`** — the ticket called this the highest player-visible value per effort, and it was the worst offender: locations and agents were `names.join(', ')`, a comma-separated string with no image, no tooltip, and no link. They are now `ListRow` + `EntityVisual chip` rows with kind-routed clicks (agent → agent surface, location → location surface), fail-open to plain styled text when no handler is passed. The same pass caught three further Law violations on the surface *as composed*, which is the standard the Laws bind at:

- `Region: region_0` — the raw internal key. The region node carries the authored name ("The Expanse", "Ashhollow"); the key was never meant to reach a screen (Law 14). An id that does not resolve now omits the field rather than printing itself.
- `Elevation: 12% / Temperature: 55% / Moisture: 91%` — three raw percentages, which Law 13 bars outright. Banded to words (`Lowland`, `Temperate`, `Drenched`) via named tables; the numbers stay in `tile.geoParams` and the designer view.
- The sphere resonance row's 13px glyphs sat below Law 11's 14px floor and carried no tooltip. Now 14px with `sphere.*` registry tooltips and an `aria-label` stating the pip row's reading in words.

**`ArtifactSheet`** — was a stub reading "Full artifact sheet coming in a future update", which made every artifact link in the game a live-looking link to an empty page. That is Law 21's dead-link clause, and the ticket judged it *worse than no link*. It now renders the artifact-representation pattern in full: canonical visual (bespoke plate → category plate → designed glyph tile, all through `resolveEntityVisual`), identity block (rarity + subcategory in words), and the item's own flavour prose, with a designed absence line when the graph holds none.

The interesting decision here is what is **not** rendered. Artifact nodes carry `mechanicalSummary` — "+0.05 Iron roll · Iron capability +0.3 while borne, rescues near-miss combat rolls (+1 step, within 1 margin)" — which is an authoring aid, not player copy. Surfacing it would have been the easy way to make the sheet look substantial and a straight Law 13 violation. It stays in the data.

**`HexSidebar`** — the collapsed rail's 8px colour-only sphere dots carried `title={sphere}` raw-key tooltips: below the icon floor (Law 11), outside the tooltip registry (Law 17), and with colour as the sole meaning channel (Law 31). Replaced with `SphereIcon` at 16px inside a 24px hit area (Law 46), registry tooltips, and worded `aria-label`s. The expanded rail's `Danger: 73%` became `Deadly country`.

## Deliberately not done

The ticket's fourth named instance — *"RetinuePanel — agent rows without portrait chips; `EntityVisual chip` adoption is a small diff"* — was skipped. `RetinuePanel` has **zero renderers**: it was replaced by `ThreadsPanel` in Phase 16-01 and left behind, so the diff would have been small and also pointless, and would have left the component looking maintained while staying unreachable. Filed as THR-1022, which also covers the sharper half of that finding: `src/data/ia-manifest.ts` still names `RetinuePanel` as the `reader` of two GameState fields, so the IA commitment doc asserts a surface that does not exist.

`ArmySheet` and `LocationProfileModal` are the same "coming in a future update" stub `ArtifactSheet` was, and are the same Law 21 dead link. They were not fixed in this pass because each needs its own info source rather than a copy of the artifact fix: a location's hero art needs a terrain hint plumbed through the caller, and a seeded medium world holds **zero** army nodes at tick 12, so there was no live subject to design or verify an army sheet against. Filed as THR-1023.

## Verification

967 test files / 15137 tests green; typecheck ratchet unchanged at 3477; `vite build` clean. Twelve new regression tests across `HexDetailView` and `ArtifactSheet`, ten of which were confirmed red against the pre-fix build by stashing the source and re-running (the two that passed are negative assertions the empty stub trivially satisfied — they guard the new content rather than proving the baseline).

Browser evidence at a measured 1920×1080, 0 console errors: the hex panel rendering chips (Kael Thornweaver resolves to real portrait art), "Region: Ashhollow", and worded geo bands; a click on an agent row routing to `selectedAgentId: ind_dev_the_first`; the artifact sheet loading `/assets/items/categories/arms.jpg` with `naturalWidth > 0` above "Mundane · Arms"; and the collapsed rail reporting three sphere glyphs, zero raw `title` attributes, and 24px hit areas.
