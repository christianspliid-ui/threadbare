# THR-2031 — A relic left lying can be taken up

**Shipped 2026-10-27.** Dominion — Player Power Progression. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-26-thr-2031-take-up-a-lying-relic.md`.

**What changed.** The Item kind gains `control:claim` (`claim_item`). It removes the relic's `lies_at` edge and writes the claimant's `possesses { via: 'claimed', takenFrom }`. It is refused `ineligible:not_lying_at_a_place` at proposal and `already_held` at completion. A past god's relic speaks its own completion line: *"… takes up the Hollow Bell, left by a god of the last age, and carries it out of the Long Barrow."* The cell's words are *Took up* and *Take up a relic left lying*, plus its own activity, completion and narration lines. *Uncover Secrets* and *Chase the Wonder* walk the cell, ahead of `create.item`. `resolveTargetHex` reads an artifact through its bearer or its `lies_at` place, so item targets order by distance and no longer sort last. `{place}` names where the relic lay, never the relic. Christening leaves an item that only changed hands (`claim_item`, `seize_item`) under its own name: before this, a seized item was renamed to a work-name. `check:undertaking-live` gains `--account sample`. The review gate found four defects, all fixed:
- A mortal who arrives after another has carried the relic off gets the `taken` line (*"… reaches the Long Barrow too late. Someone else has carried the Hollow Bell away."*), no deed, and *"… came to nothing."* on their arc. The same arc rule now covers `no_ground` / `no_room`.
- An item target's travel penalty prices the trip to where the relic lies.
- The echoes chronicle names the relic's bearer once it is taken up (*"The Hollow Bell is carried now by …"*).

**Evidence.** `claimItem.test.ts` (7 tests). `check:undertaking-live -- cell.control_claim.item --seed 42 --seed 99 --account sample` proved on both seeds. A 240-tick sample-account run took the Hollow Bell on day 10.8 (seed 42) and day 4.6 (seed 99). The kill criterion (under a day) did not fire, so both ambitions keep the cell. In a world with no account, the cell never starts in 60 ticks on either seed. Browser (1920×1080, Playwright): after the claim, the relic's Inspector page shows *Who: Kael Thornweaver — holds it* and no *where* row.

**Deviation.** The plan expected a no-account world to refuse the cell as `no_object_in_range:item`, with one `undertaking_cell_unreachable` trace. An ordinary world holds about 120 loose unborne artifacts (123 on seed 42), so the cell finds objects and refuses each one as `ineligible:not_lying_at_a_place`. It never starts, which is what the bullet guards. `preferredVerbs` on *Chase the Wonder* does not suppress the cell; it only lowers its alignment, so no `control` was added.
