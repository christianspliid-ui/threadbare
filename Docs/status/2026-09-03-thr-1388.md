# THR-1388 — the covet rivalry: what a mortal cannot take, they come to hate

**2026-09-03 · engine + content.** The reactive loop's raw supply on the default seeds was zero because the motive gate asked every destroy for a quarrel and the world never wrote one. Four probes on the real tick pipeline located the starvation: every `no_motive` refusal on seeds 42 and 99 was a conqueror aiming at a faction-owned holding whose faction was simply not their rival, and mortal-to-mortal `hostile_to` had exactly two writers — mentorship breaks and the grievance lane itself. The loop's seed was its own output. Leader-as-owner, the one retune that mirrors THR-1383's victim routing on the read side, was run as a controlled arm and would have passed 0 / 2 / 9 refusals across the three seeds; the variety penalty and the cap were measured as not the lever. The retune that shipped lets coveting write the quarrel: a mortal pursuing a destroy-heavy ambition who is refused the same holding `COVET_RIVALRY_THRESHOLD` boards running gains a `hostile_to` edge with cause `covets`, which the gate reads as **rivalry** and never as a grudge — it licenses that mortal's destroys against that owner but mints no vendetta by itself; only the harm it licenses does, through the existing lane. One live covet per mortal, never the ascendant's holdings or the mortal's own faction, no decay (no `hostile_to` is removed anywhere; the plan dropped a TTL the judge found rode a sweep that did not exist). The same PR moves `ambition_conquer_territory`'s three destroys ahead of its always-available verbs: at positions 9–10 of 10 two of them had been proposed zero times in 300 ticks.
