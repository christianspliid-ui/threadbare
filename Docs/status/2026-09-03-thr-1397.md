## What was decided

Every one of the 36 open cells now carries a verdict, and the grid gained two states to hold them: **wanted** (decided yes; the note names the operation the cell needs) and **later** (decided, waiting on a named precondition). **Open** is empty. Nine live cells also record what they **owe** — a decided consequence nothing reads yet, the THR-1400 finding.

**The three forks.** Killing a mortal is **the plot** — a premeditated killing with observe, positioning and the strike as its stages; motive-gated harder than any cell, heaviest harm, writes `deceased` (never a node removal), and surfaces as a moment before it resolves when the target is threaded. The duel is an encounter off a quarrel; slayings belong to battle and the delve. Cursing is **one signed cell**: a curse against another (motive-gated, cured by the live destroy × Condition) or a blessing for oneself; stripping a power inherits it. Usurping is **a forced succession, never a coronation**: the succession phase runs early with the usurper as a weighted candidate; three outcomes fall out of what exists (leads moves, a quarrel and a standing loss, or the live schism); seize × Army is the same one rank down.

**Standing riders** (on the grid): a completed work grows capability in the Reach it leaned on; wealth (the 0–100 score every mortal and faction carries, invisible on every sheet today) appears on the mortal sheet and the faction view as a banded word before any yield cell ships; holding a thing yields passively through the unpaid `WEALTH_*_INCOME` constants, and use × Location is the active harvest on top.

**Fog cleared on the map:** yield is a verb (both halves); the favour class of Agreement gets its life cycle from use × Standing plus the two live Agreement cells. Still fog: whether Places need a stock before change × Place means anything — now a *later* cell rather than a question.

## What this did not do

Nothing here ships an operation. The `wanted` cells are the supply for the plan docs the map hands off; the census prototype (THR-1402) runs on the cells that exist. Mortal moved from `NOT_AN_OBJECT` to a full row so that destroy × Mortal could carry its verdict; the other seven verbs stay `no` with sovereignty as the reason.
