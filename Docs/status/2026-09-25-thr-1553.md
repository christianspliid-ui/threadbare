# 2026-09-25 — THR-1553: the fight chips (Fight on screen F3)

**Ticket:** [THR-1553](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1553) · **Project:** Physical Conflict · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-23-fight-on-screen.md` (slice F3) · **Pillars:** UI (the aftermath's chip block, the ◆ marker's word) · Content (chip nouns and sentences, `fight.chip.*` tooltips) · Engine N/A (reads only).

## What was wrong

A fight's ending recorded everything it did on the resolved action's `fightState` (plan docs 1–3): the beast felled, the den cleared, the fighter scarred or slain, the conditions the bands left, a storied weapon, a trophy, a town's gratitude, a grudge. The aftermath showed none of it. The engine's derived-chip channel emits nothing for a condition applied mid-step, and nothing read `fightState`, so the chip block was silent about the fight itself.

## What shipped

- **`buildFightChanges`** (new, pure): one chip kind per `fightState` field, each in one of the four existing categories. PATH covers the clock worn down, the opponent slain and the lair cleared. SCAR covers the fighter scarred or slain, and loss conditions. BOON covers gain conditions, Storied and the trophy. BOND covers standing and the grudge. A chip whose field is absent, or whose anchor node is gone, never renders (Law 56).
- **Precedence:** slain (opponent) suppresses the clock chip, and slain (fighter) suppresses scarred. Only a persistent clock gets a chip; a per-fight clock is not a world object.
- **Whose record:** the fighter-anchored chips read `fightState.ending` only. So a duel winner who spares the loser shows none of the loser's marks (`opponentEnding` is THR-1561's).
- **The ◆ marker's word:** an `opens` change may carry `deltaLabel`, and `DeltaCluster` draws it beside the ◆ ("slain", "cleared", the clock word). Every existing PATH chip keeps "a way opens". There is a styleguide sample.
- **Content:** `FIGHT_CHIP_COPY`, `FIGHT_CHIP_CATEGORY`, `FIGHT_CONDITION_CHIP_COPY`, and `fight.chip.*` tooltip ids declared as literals in `fight-screen-content.ts`. A condition chip's noun uses the condition's own `attachment.*` tooltip.
- **`window.__DEBUG.getFightChips(actionId)`** builds the aftermath through the veil's own adapter and returns the fight chips.

## Evidence

- `buildFightChanges.test.ts` has 28 tests. They cover:
  - absent-field and present-field for each chip;
  - anchors resolving, and a missing anchor dropping its chip;
  - both precedences;
  - inspired as BOON and wounded as SCAR;
  - the grudge;
  - no chip for a per-fight clock;
  - the agent-mode spare fixture;
  - no `{`;

  - the standing noun as the settlement's name;
  - tooltips resolving;
  - the unified adapter end to end.
- `DeltaCluster.test.tsx` has 2 more tests.
- Live check on seed 42 medium: `spawnFight(Krenn, { clockFilled: 3, outcome: 'critical_success' })` at a major lair, stepped through the veil. The aftermath drew *PATH · KRENN — Krenn was slain. ◆ slain* and *PATH · THE KINDLED WARREN — ◆ cleared*, plus BOON inspired and BOON Prayer Scroll. Every `getFightChips('ua_227')` sentence was in the DOM (`Docs/evidence/thr-1553/`).
- The standing chip did not appear live because Krenn's lair has no settlement within `FIGHT_GRATITUDE_RADIUS_HEXES`, which the Done-when allows ("when `ending.reputation` is present"). The unit and adapter tests pin standing.
