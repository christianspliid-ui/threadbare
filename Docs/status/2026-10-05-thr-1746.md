# 2026-10-05 — THR-1746: "Dominion" is seated, and the labels that collided with it are renamed

**Ticket:** [THR-1746](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1746) · **Project:** Action System & Unlocks · **Pillars:**
- **Content:** UL entries.
- **UI:** two label renames.
- **Engine:** N/A. The read itself is THR-1748.

## Why

Christian ruled on THR-1745 that **Dominion** is the god's graded hold on a world object, and that it is the player's power loop. Two player-visible labels already used the word for something else. The god sheet headed its Reach rows "Dominion", and the mortal ambition category was shown as "Dominion". If they stayed, the band word would arrive on surfaces that already meant other things.

## What shipped

- **UL, Cosmology shard:** three new entries, *Dominion*, *Dominion Band* (Hostile · Foreign · Touched · Held · Sovereign) and *Opposing Dominion*, plus the README index rows and a regenerated UL dashboard. The Dominion entry records how every other use of the word was reconciled.
- **God sheet:** the Reach section heading now reads **"Reaches"**.
- **Mortal ambition category:** the player word is now **Supremacy**, in both label tables. The engine literal `dominion` is unchanged. This is the Freehold pattern.
- **Kept as they are:**
  - **Claim Dominion and Shift Dominion** are the plain Dominion verbs. Re-keying their effects is THR-1748's job.
  - **Proper nouns:** *Dominion of Stone*, *Word of Dominion* and the remembrance title word.
  - **Ordinary-English prose** that happens to use the word.

## Verification

- A new jsdom render test asserts the sheet has a "Reaches" heading and no heading matching /dominion/. It was falsified by restoring the old heading, and the test failed.
- The Codex rail test now expects "Supremacy".
- `npm run gate` passed: test, typecheck, build and encounter.
