# THR-1166 — the god sways, never decides

**Shipped 2026-08-17** · [THR-1166](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1166/content-sweep-the-god-decides-the-god-sways-odds-and-influences) · project *Encounter Experience*

Christian, reading The Grateful Kin's description in an attended session: *"the concept
that the god decides anything is wrong. the god does not decide, but sways the odds and
influences the outcomes."* Not a new rule — the nudge model's founding charter (THR-772)
says the god nudges, fate rolls, and the god never picks endings. It was violated in
shipped prose at scale and nothing could see it.

## What the measurement changed about the sweep

The ticket sized the job by grep: 21 occurrences of `god decides`. Re-running that grep
at pickup reproduced it. The sweep was nevertheless **not** that population, and the
difference is the substance of this ticket.

Grep matches a string; the canon rule is about a *grammatical role*. The god choosing its
own action is not merely tolerable but is the entire game — the player-god picks which
card to play — so "The god perceived the threads of the forge and chose how to pull"
(`crafting.quest.flawed_steel`) is correct prose that a string sweep flags and a careless
pass would have "fixed". Two such lines surfaced the moment the detector was measured
against the corpus instead of the source files, and two more shapes were found that are
canon-*correct* and would have been destroyed by a looser pattern: `the-star-pilgrim.ts`
ends a choice with *"is not the god's to decide"* — a line asserting the rule — and
`the-veiled-consultation.ts` has *"a cost the god had decided not to pay"*, the god's own
ledger.

So the gate is scoped to the god as author of a **result**, and the infinitive is the
whole distinction:

| Sentence | Verdict |
|---|---|
| "the god decides whether **the debt is paid**" | fails — a result |
| "the god decides whether **to** press them" | passes — its own act |
| "the god chose **how to** pull" | passes — its own act |
| "is not the god's to decide" | passes — states the rule |

An earlier draft also admitted `how` and `between`; the corpus sweep returned the two
false positives above and neither word survived. Final measurement: **14 sentences across
11 templates**, every one genuine, zero false positives.

## What shipped

**Content** — 27 rewrites across 12 files (10 encounters, 2 mentorship). All 14 gated
violations, plus the ungated
`whether to` / `chooses between` instances the ticket listed, which read better fixed even
though the detector correctly leaves them alone. The replacement vocabulary was not
invented: *"the god leans them toward"* already sat in the `success` line of each regional
encounter, directly beside the violation in its `initiation`. The house verbs are *sways,
tips, presses, leans, steadies, steers*. The Grateful Kin line Christian read becomes "A
god can steady the thanks, or let it fumble."

**Engine** — `DIVINE_DECISION_PATTERNS` + `countDivineDecision` in
`nudgeAuditDetectors.ts`, gating at zero in every field class, reported as
`NudgeAuditScores.divineDecision`. It joins `auditTemplate().failures`, which is what
`check:encounter` already reads, so no wiring in the script was needed beyond its header.
Enforced in *all three* field classes deliberately — unlike vagueness (THR-899) or second
person (THR-1045), there is no slot where the claim is true, so there is no class to carve
out.

**Content/docs** — rule 5b in `nudge-authoring-spec.md` § prose rubric with the verbatim
ruling, and a detector-spec entry carrying the verdict table above.

**UI** — N/A, no component changes.

## Evidence

Falsification ran both directions on the director's own sentence: reintroduced into
`vertical-slice.ts`, `check:encounter -- encounter.slice.grateful_kin` exits **1** with
`[register] divine outcome-authorship x1`; reverted, exits **0**. Corpus sweep goes 14 → 0.
`check:encounter -- --all` green (unchanged from the pre-change baseline), 16 new unit
tests, typecheck unchanged at 3178, build green.

Two full-suite runs each showed timeout-only failures in `debug-bridge.test.ts` and
`scripts/__tests__/impediment-id-allocation.test.ts` — both pass in isolation (1435ms and
1.95s against a 5000ms limit), both unreachable from a diff of content prose and one
content-eval module. Worker-contention flakes, triaged per CLAUDE.md § Known Sandbox
Limitations, not shipped on.

## Note for the next author

The gate covers what `check:encounter` covers, which is the `encounter.*` prefix plus the
golden exemplar — not every template in `src/data/**`. The sweep went wider than the gate:
`mentorship-templates/`, `road-ambush.ts` and `soul-ferryman.ts` were fixed by hand and are
not protected against reintroduction by this detector. That is a scoping fact about
`check:encounter`, not a gap this ticket opened, but it is the thing to know before
assuming the corpus is now self-policing.
