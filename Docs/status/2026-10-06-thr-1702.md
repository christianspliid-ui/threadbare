# 2026-10-06 — THR-1702: a found lead ends the climb

**Ticket:** [THR-1702](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1702) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-05-thr-1702-found-leads-end-the-climb.md` · **Pillars:** Engine + Content (one chip line); UI N/A for components (the sheet's "found it" is an engine selector string, pinned by a unit test).

## Why

A `located` lead on a site no delve can ever enter — any wonder, or a plain worldgen ruin — had no last rung. Its holder was pulled back to survey it every decision (`leadPull`), each survey refreshed the lead so it never aged out, and every visit it tried to arrange was refused `lead_visit_not_narrowed`. On seed 99 one mortal re-surveyed a glowcap hollow 9 times in 300 ticks.

## What shipped

- `delveRoadOf(props, tick)` → `now | later | never` (leaf module `ruins/delveRoad.ts`, re-exported from `delveVariant.ts`); `isDelvableRuin` is now `=== 'now'`.
- `isLeadSpent` — an unconsumed `located` lead on a `never` site — behind `CLUE_SPENT_LEAD_ENDS_CLIMB` (default `true`).
- The lead pass (`heldLeadRuinIds`, now taking `tick`) leaves a spent lead out; the survey reader refuses `already_found` for a spent lead or a found never-site.
- `phaseClueDecay` settles a spent lead: `recordPlaceFound` writes `knows_of.foundTick`, the lead is removed, `ruins.lead_found` is emitted. The sheet's known places read it as "found it".
- Chip `ruin_lead.located` no longer promises "can go down into it".
- Interface rows `found-lead-becomes-known-place`, `lead-pass-reads-delve-road`; UL § Lead extended.

## Measured (census `readers/lead-dead-ends.ts`, medium, 300 ticks, current tree, switch on vs off)

- Loop gone: no re-survey of a located never-site and no live located never-site lead at t300 on any of seeds 42 · 99 · 4 · 8 · 1 · 2 · 3 · 5. Seed 99 `not_narrowed` refusals 9 → 0. `ruins.lead_found` fires once on each seed that locates a never-site (42, 99, 2), never twice for one (knower, site).
- Delves: 13 on vs 15 off over eight seeds; over the plan's four seeds 5 vs 7. Five seeds are identical arm to arm; 42 and 2 keep their delves. The whole difference is seed 99, the looping seed: off, one of its two delves is by the looping holder `ind_9` and the other follows divergence after `ind_9` is freed at t60. Judged divergence on the one affected seed, not a starved rung, so the switch stays on (kill-criterion finding reported on the ticket).
- The plan's 10-05 baseline (8 delves) predates THR-1686's round-2 merge, so the comparison above is the controlled one.
