# THR-2180 — A good-odds step that misses says so

**Shipped 2026-11-06.** Warm playtest · round 3. Decided by the design lane on THR-2171 (veto window closed 2026-11-06T05:30Z).

1. **The odds word is frozen at the roll.** `StepProseRecord.forecastTier` (optional) is set in `executeStepResult` from `classifyForecastTier(resolutionStats.probability)`. It is set only when the step rolled: there are roll stats and no `fightEnd`. The `encounter_step_prose_recorded` trace carries it. Old saves and no-roll steps have no tier, so no line shows.
2. **One helper.** `oddsMissedLine(tier, band)` in `data/nudge-stage-content.ts` returns "It looked {Fated|Favorable}, but fate rolled against it." for a `near_miss`, `failure` or `critical_failure`, and null otherwise (`ODDS_MISSED_TIERS`, `ODDS_MISSED_BANDS`, `ODDS_MISSED_LINE`).
3. **Where the player learns it.** The Stage history entry gets `oddsMissedLine`. The last-step answer adds it after the choice suffix ("Step 1 broke. You: Steady Hand. It looked Fated, but fate rolled against it."). The step navigator's replay shows it under the band word. A chapter's last step has no "Step n" answer, so `causeLineSteps` carries the line and `buildAftermathCauseLine` adds it only when the deciding step is the last one. The line never shows twice for one step.
4. **One step of several.** If a chapter is won, its deciding step failed, and it had more than one step, the cause line reads: "… at a price. One step went wrong. The work on Heart failed, and your cards could not save that step." It uses `CAUSE_ONE_STEP_WENT_WRONG` and `CAUSE_GOD_COULD_NOT_SAVE_STEP`. Lost endings and the alone/elsewhere clauses are unchanged.
5. **Legend and hover.** The legend now ends "… Favorable, Fated. Even Fated can miss." (`NUDGE_LEGEND_EVEN_FATED_MISSES`). The `ui.forecast.fated` hover keeps Christian's ruled opener ("The best word on the ladder: a sure thing needs no god.", pinned by `essencePoolLimit.test.ts`) and then gives the plan's sentence: "Nearly sure, never certain: fate still rolls, and now and then even a Fated step misses." The plan's version had dropped the opener. Keeping it honours the earlier ruling, and the new meaning is unchanged.
