# 2026-09-26 — THR-1623: Place pages and mortal cards describe culture again

**Ticket:** [THR-1623](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1623) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** none (self-scoped bug, found by the design lane while prototyping THR-1599) · **Pillars:** Engine + Content. The UI change is a one-call swap in `HexChronicle`; no layout changed.

## What shipped

1. **One derived key for culture prose.** `CULTURE_LOCATION_PROSE` is keyed by a foundation pair (`order_light`, `chaos_darkness`, …), but no culture stores one. `cultureResolver` and `agentCultureResolver` read a `foundationPair` field that no worldgen writer ever set, so they returned nothing for every place and every mortal. The new `getCultureFoundationPairKey` (`src/engine/cultureFoundationPair.ts`) derives the key from `foundationBias` plus `veneratedSpheres`. The bias fills its own axis, the first venerated pole of the other axis fills the other, and a named default covers a culture that venerates neither pole.
2. **Light- and Darkness-foundation cultures resolve.** The chronicle's old inline rule built `light_light` and `darkness_light`, which no table entry answers, so those cultures fell back to the generic "claim this land" line. The helper gives them an Order/Chaos pole instead (`DEFAULT_ORDER_CHAOS_POLE = 'order'` when they venerate neither).
3. **All three readers share the helper:** both resolvers and `HexChronicle`. The resolvers also prefer a current `belongs_to` culture over a `cultureLayer: 'historical'` one. The legacy `foundationPair` field is still honoured, so older fixtures keep resolving.

## Evidence

- Generated medium worlds (`cultureProse-generatedWorld.test.ts`): seed 42 has places 40/40 and mortals 235/235, seed 99 has 60/60 and 325/325. On the unfixed resolvers the same test reads 0/40, 0/235, 0/60 and 0/325, matching the ticket's measurement.
- Every foundation × one or two venerated spheres resolves to an authored key (`cultureFoundationPair.test.ts`).
- jsdom render (`HexChronicle.test.tsx`): Darkness- and Light-foundation cultures render their table line, not the fallback. Both cases fail on the pre-fix chronicle.
