# THR-831 — the off-reach ceiling stands; the retune option was arithmetically backwards

**Date:** 2026-08-24 · **Ticket:** [THR-831](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-831/nudge-off-reach-max-difficulty045-is-calibrated-against-a-partial-hand) · **Project:** Encounter Experience

A tuning call spun out of [THR-827](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-827/resolutioninputactionmodifiers-is-documented-capped-at-020-but-nothing).
`NUDGE_OFF_REACH_MAX_DIFFICULTY = 0.45` was justified by a measured claim that an off-reach
`notable` mortal stays pinned to `PROBABILITY_FLOOR` *through the whole authored hand*, citing
`+0.37`. THR-827 found `0.37` was one ascendant's sphere-gated subset, not the hand's ceiling —
the five non-trait-gated cards sum to `0.55`, and at that total the whole cohort **clears** the
floor the rationale said it stayed pinned to. The ticket asked which reading was intended and
offered two.

## The verdict: reading 1, and reading 2 does not exist

**`NUDGE_OFF_REACH_MAX_DIFFICULTY` stays at 0.45. No constant changed.**

Reading 2 was *"the ceiling is mis-set and wants **lowering** until 0.55 no longer clears the
floor."* Re-derived from `computeResolutionThreshold`, that is backwards:

```
p = capability − difficulty + modifiers,  clamped to [0.05, 0.95]
floored  ⟺  difficulty ≥ capability + modifiers − 0.05
```

Difficulty sits on the **same side of the equation as the floor**. Lowering the ceiling makes a
hand clear the floor *more* easily, never less. To keep even the strongest off-reach notable
(capability 0.119) pinned against a 0.55 hand you would have to **raise** the ceiling to
**0.6192** — past the `severe` band floor (0.60), the band this rule exists to keep open-draw
content out of. So the option that read as the conservative one would have inverted the
constant's entire purpose.

That settles the direction without needing a game-feel argument: the ceiling was always right and
only its *rationale* was wrong. The measurement described the pathology the ceiling prevents,
observed at the boundary — not an invariant to preserve.

## The game-feel answer, which falls out of the same arithmetic

Measured at the ceiling (d = 0.45), across the notable off-reach cohort (raws 1–5):

| hand played | raw 1 | raw 2 | raw 3 | raw 4 | raw 5 | reading |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| unaided (0) | 0.050 | 0.050 | 0.050 | 0.050 | 0.050 | all floored |
| +2 cards (0.22) | 0.050 | 0.050 | 0.050 | 0.050 | 0.050 | all floored |
| measured subset (0.37) | 0.050 | 0.050 | 0.050 | 0.050 | 0.050 | all floored |
| Swollen Ford step 0 (0.46) | 0.050 | 0.050 | **0.067** | **0.093** | **0.129** | graded |
| full non-trait hand (0.55) | **0.127** | **0.139** | **0.157** | **0.183** | **0.219** | all clear |
| rubric maximum (0.70) | **0.277** | **0.289** | **0.307** | **0.333** | **0.369** | all clear |

**A god with essence across many spheres lifting an off-reach mortal off the floor is the feature
working, not a leak.** You are still bending a mortal's odds rather than substituting your
competence for theirs (`rulebook.md` § *The nudge*) — but a wide pool bends them further than a
narrow one, and the cohort's own spread still decides who benefits. A ceiling tuned to keep the
cohort floored *through* a full hand would make broad sphere access buy nothing, which is the
decorative-hand failure in its other form.

The graded row matters: the crossover sits between 0.3808 (strongest floored below this) and
0.4734 (weakest floored below this), so **the current shipped exemplar's own 0.46 step-0 hand
already lands inside the graded band.** This is not a hypothetical about the retired Darkhollow
fixture — re-derived from The Swollen Ford's live `forecastDelta` values, per the impediment #258
rule that a constant is the thing that rots and the fixture is what you re-derive from.

## What shipped

- **The verdict, on the constant itself** (`nudgeAuthoringConstants.ts`) — with the arithmetic
  spelled out, so the next reader who notices the 0.37/0.55 gap finds the answer rather than
  re-filing the question.
- **The boundary pinned in both directions** (`nudgeModel.test.ts`, +2 tests). The existing test
  asserted only the floored side, *which is how the rationale came to claim more than it had
  measured*. Now: the whole cohort clearing at a full non-trait hand with probabilities as
  **literals** (not re-derived from the same capability/difficulty expressions under test — that
  would assert only that arithmetic is arithmetic), plus the monotonicity of the difficulty term
  and the 0.6192 crossover, so the retune question cannot be re-derived from silence.
- **The authoring spec corrected** (`nudge-authoring-spec.md`) — the surface *both* authoring
  skills load still read "stays floored through the entire hand", the exact overstated claim. It
  now says what the number actually bounds: the worst case an open-draw step presents to a
  *narrow* god.
- **Audit § B answered** with the verdict, the measured table, and the actions taken. Fixed a
  stale `NUDGE_HAND_MAX_TOTAL_DELTA (0.65)` in the same section — the committed value is 0.70,
  because 0.65 failed its own conformance test on the first run.
