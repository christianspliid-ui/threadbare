# 2026-10-05 — THR-1728: stakes on every encounter

**Ticket:** [THR-1728](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1728) · **Project:** Encounter Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-04-thr-1727-encounter-stakes-line.md` § Content pillar · **Pillars:** Content (`stakes` on every encounter template); Engine (validator in required mode, a Composition Contract block, the trace's no-stakes label); UI (the veil's fallback only, covered by a unit test, no browser route owed per the Done-when).

## Why

Christian, on The Granary Riot (2026-10-05): the veil's subtitle read *"A two-step master Heart job in a town: … plants a town-watch errand"* — *"looks more like a prompt than an actual prose text."* Only the five slice encounters authored `stakes`, so every other encounter fell back to its designer-voice `description`.

## What shipped

- **546 templates gained `stakes`**:
  - the 328 in the validator's old predicate;
  - 18 `encounter.*` templates it missed (company drama, the ten anomalies, the sprung trap, three shell proofs);
  - 200 encounter-shaped registry members that open on the veil when a threaded mortal runs them (the ten guilds' quests, tavern, social, faction scenes, the appointment sequels, the ruin leads).
  - Each has `goal` / `risk` / `won` / `lost`, written from that template's own failure and critical-failure prose.
  - `lostBadly` appears where the crit ending differs. `arms` covers forks whose arms end differently.
  - Authored in sixteen parallel batches from per-template prose briefs and validated by a mirror of the rules, then spliced inline after each `id:` line, so the data files stay the one source.
- **The validator is in required mode.** Its predicate is widened to `isEncounterAction(id) || id.startsWith('encounter.') || isEncounterShapedTemplate(t)`. Only the verbs stay out: `action.*`, `npc_*` and ascendant-castable templates (THR-1635's own classification). The headless sweep reports 0 templates without stakes.
- **One rule set.** `src/data/content-eval/encounterStakesRules.ts` holds it, shared by the validator and a new Composition Contract `stakes` block. So `check:encounter`, including `--package`, fails a template or package without stakes.
- **The veil never shows `description`.** Without usable stakes it shows the scene's opening prose. Pinned by `buildUnifiedEncounterStageModel.noDescription.test.ts`. The trace's fallback label is now `no_stakes_initiation_used`.
- **The factory authors stakes from now on:**
  - format card § Stakes;
  - the implementation prompt line;
  - draft-prompt section 9b;
  - the nudge-authoring spec's contract table and register table;
  - all 43 factory package JSONs carry `stakes`;
  - the golden exemplar too.

## Judgement calls (veto welcome)

- **Swindler Found's top-level `risk`** names the ending both arms share (the swindler gone to another town). The knife in the alley lives only in the corner-him arm's `lostBadly`, because the opening line shows before an arm is chosen.
- **Prose inconsistencies found, not fixed here:**
  - **Tend to Wounds:** the final step's crit text (the arm set crooked) disagrees with its aftermath (the patient dies). The stakes follow the death.
  - **Old Blood (`fight.duel.grudge`):** the `narrativeTemplates` success and failure read swapped. The stakes follow the step prose.
- **The three shell-proof templates** are test shells with one-line prose, so their stakes are generic.
