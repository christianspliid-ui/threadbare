# THR-1977 — a priced card is charged from the pool it names

**Shipped 2026-10-18.** Cold playtest round 4: Observe and Agent Thread were priced "Mind · 5 / 10 essence" in the hand, but casting them took nothing and Agent Thread said nothing until the Rite opened. Neither card has a `sphereAffinity`; the price fell back to the god's primary sphere, while the drawer slot carried `sphere: null` and `commitPlayerCast` charges no pool for a null sphere. `payingSphere(template, primarySphere)` (`targetActions.ts`) is now the one resolver: the drawer slot's `sphere`, the god rail's hand price and the moment card's act all read it, so the pool the price names is the pool charged. A cast that opens the Rite of the Thread now shows one line at once — `buildRitePendingReceipt`: "Agent Thread. You reach for Corran. The thread is forming. It takes hold in a moment." — and the Rite still tells the bind.

Regression: `playerCastPayingSphere.thr1977.test.ts` sweeps every priced drawer card on a mortal (charged its shown price from its shown pool) and every priced template in the catalogue (paying sphere never null; the hand prices god cards in the pool they pay from).
