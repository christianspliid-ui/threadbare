# 2026-10-17 — THR-1933: war aims S1 — armies fight enemies, defend first, and march for what a town is worth

**Ticket:** [THR-1933](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1933). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-15-thr-1933-war-aims.md` (slice S1; S2 is THR-1955). Engine, Content (sheet words) and UI (army sheet, debug tab) all ship here.

- **One enemy rule.** `isEnemyFaction` / `enemyArm` (`src/engine/warAims/enemy.ts`, hoisted from THR-1922) now also gates the field-battle start (`areHostile`), adds a monster arm, and lets an alliance quiet the sentiment arm. Two armies that are not enemies share a hex in peace.
- **Defence first** (`warAims/defence.ts`): an enemy army marching on an own or sworn holding (`protectedHoldingsOf`) within 8 hexes turns the army to `defend` it. The holder is the defender at that place (prepared-defence bonus), not on the road there.
- **Value targeting** (`warAims/score.ts`, `townValue.ts`): the richest enemy town in reach, scored with the purse's own helpers (town tax + place yields + a share of the unheld fields), +0.5 for a staple the attacker's towns are short of, ÷ (1 + hexes/6). A revenge army leans ×1.5 toward the enemy it resents most. No target → `holdReason`.
- **Kill criterion fired: supply reach dropped.** Seed 42 at tick 160: the one army had 7 enemy towns within 16 hexes and 0 in supply reach. Reach is now a march path (`marchableFrom`, the same start movement paths from) plus the 16-hex backstop. `WAR_AIM_REQUIRE_SUPPLY_REACH` stays as an off lever.
- **Daily re-aim** (`warAims.ts`, first in `phaseArmyMovement`): a threat wins, a valid march is kept, else it re-picks. Campaign armies (`source: 'agenda'`) and armies in battle are skipped. A territorial/revenge ambition takes the conquer target.

Lines are built by `warAims/sheetView.ts` (no graph reads in the component). The Crown tooltip now adds "its armies ride to their defence" (the crown's Law 45 guard lifts).
