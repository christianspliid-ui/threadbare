**Cold read and kill criterion 1.** First read: 13 findings (P2/P3 lines, an arrival class with no wall, a cloth step that contradicted its own failure line, two `stakes` mismatches). All were fixed. Then a fresh critic read the revised text. It found two P3 lines and one stakes mismatch, which were fixed without a further read. It also found 19 findings of a new kind: **step-to-step continuity**. A step-0 failure or critical line ("feels no pull at all", "the walk ends there") contradicts the next step's fixed narrative ("{actor} can feel the line"). The board format writes step 1's narrative once, for every step-0 band. So this class sits in every two-step board template, slice 1's and the exemplar's included, and is not something the wording of one wave can fix. Under kill criterion 1 the rewrite stopped there. The finding goes to the design lane on THR-2108 before slice 2b (THR-2128).
