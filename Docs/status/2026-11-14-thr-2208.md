# THR-2208 — a bound mortal's deeds fill the Mandate

**Shipped 2026-11-14.** Thematic Pressure & Living World. Plan doc: `Docs/plans/2026-11-13-thr-2202-give-both-clocks-a-verb.md` (slice 1). Answers THR-2199 (the Mandate bar promised deeds that never counted). Complaint classes PC-1, PC-6, PC-8.

- **Deed credit.** When a mortal the god threads closes a chapter, `mandateDeedForChapter` (`unifiedActionResolution.ts`, on the resolving step) credits each Mandate sphere `(chapter sphereAffinity is that sphere ? MANDATE_DEED_BASE_POINTS : 0) + essence paid in it on the chapter's cards × MANDATE_DEED_POINTS_PER_ESSENCE`, times `MANDATE_DEED_BAND_FACTOR[band]` (every band > 0). Spend in any other sphere credits nothing (Law 30). The credit rides on the action as `mandateDeedCredits`; `phaseUnifiedActionProgress` queues it on `pendingMandateCredits` with `source: 'deed'`; `phaseMandate` folds it the same tick. Trace `mandate.deed_credit`.
- **Spend split.** `spendNudgeEssence` returns `bySphere`; the nudge commit (`GameView.handleCommitNudges`) sums it onto the chapter's `mandateSphereSpend` through `addMandateSphereSpend`. Authored-choice and meeting spends do not count (not cards on a chapter).
- **Trend word reads the last day.** `phaseMandate` keeps `recentLedgerMoves` / `recentLedgerDelta` over `MANDATE_TREND_WINDOW_TICKS` (12); `selectMandateRow` reads `mandateTrendOf(recentLedgerDelta)` instead of the cumulative 2% gate. `MANDATE_TREND_WORD` is now Rising / Holding / Slipping (failing keeps Drifting), matching `INSPECTOR_WORDS.mandateTrend`.
- **Player-facing.** Card face: "Counts toward your Mandate." under the price, only on a priced card paid in a Mandate sphere for a threaded mortal (`mandateDeedSpheres`). Aftermath: one BOON · MANDATE row, "{mortal}'s deed raised your Mandate in {Sphere}." (or "far in" at ≥ `MANDATE_DEED_STRONG_POINTS`). Mandate detail: "Last deeds", newest three (`mandateState.recentDeeds`). `ui.mandate_tracker` tooltip says what the word means and that cards in its spheres count. Executor decision: the tooltip had to stay under the 200-character registry cap, so it reads "how it moved today" rather than the plan's longer sentence.
- **Debug.** `__DEBUG.getMandateDeeds()` — trend, `recentLedgerDelta`, ledger, last deeds and lines, open chapters' spend.
- **Census.** `npm run census:mandate -- deeds` (and `deeds-none`).

**Evidence.** `npm run gate` PASS (test, typecheck ratchet, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, cli-smoke). Census, seeded route, 300 ticks, the policy paying one Mandate-sphere essence on each threaded chapter: 42 chapters credited, `mandate.deed_credit` points > 0 on all, the word Rising within a day of 42/42, ledger 28.3 / 0.0 (kill criterion 80 by doom 0.4 not approached). No-cards arm: 0 credits — none of the First's chapters in 300 ticks carried a Mind or Spirit `sphereAffinity`, so the base credit is untested live and covered by unit tests (`mandateDeedCredit.thr2208.test.ts`, 18).
