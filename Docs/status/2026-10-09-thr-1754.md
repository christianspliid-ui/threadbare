# 2026-10-09 — THR-1754 S2: the Rite of the Thread on screen

**Ticket:** [THR-1754](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1754) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-06-thr-1644-threading-ceremony.md` (slice S2, decisions D2 and D6)

Every mortal the god threads with the Agent Thread card now plays a rite on screen, showing the mortal's real name and portrait. The 2nd and 3rd threads play one meeting test and then the bond. From the 4th thread on, the rite is a ceremonial card with the bond alone on a two-card hand. Any rite can be waved through with *Bond without a hand*.

- **Engine.**
  - `RITE_SURFACE_ENABLED` is on, and GameView sets `riteSurfaceMounted` on the session, so a pending rite waits for the player. A headless run (CLI, tests) has no surface and still resolves each rite at once without a hand. The review gate caught that the flag alone would have stranded every CLI rite.
  - `threadingRiteQueue.ts` gains `planThreadingRite`. It picks the shape's tests on the rite's seed: the short rite takes one slot-1 converted test for the mortal's primary reach, or degrades to `bond_only` with a `rite.degraded` trace.
  - `closeThreadingRite` covers three paths: played, waved through, and mortal gone. All three land through `applyThreadingRite`, promote the next queued rite and return one chronicle line. `dismissThreadingRite` is now a thin wrapper over it.
  - The drain prints the meeting's own "claimed as The First" line for a card-route First, and an overflow line for a rite it resolves on the spot.
  - `applyThreadingRite` appends the node's `riteHistory`.
  - `castOpensRite` tells the cast path to drop the generic Agent Thread receipt.
  - `getThreadingRite()` now lists every thread with its `riteShape` and `bondReception`. That carries the S1 "DebugPanel Threads rows"; the DebugPanel has no Threads section.
- **Content.**
  - `src/data/threading-rite-prose.ts` adds:
    - the header and ordinal subtitle;
    - 8 reach openings, with pronoun agreement and a no-empty-slot fallback;
    - 5 reception lines;
    - the D3 First line;
    - the rite's own bond god-voice (the meeting's per-Hunger lines speak of the *first* soul, which is false on a later thread);
    - the chronicle lines;
    - the sheet line.
  - A generated wilderness name reads "out in the wilderness" and never shows its hex coordinates.
  - The `riteText` content check covers the 40 slot-1 meeting tests. Only `AX-IRON-01` ("they do not know you are here") needed a rite variant, which is carried through the dilemma mapper's allowlist.
- **UI.**
  - `src/components/ThreadingRite/ThreadingRite.tsx` reuses the meeting's `FormativeTestBeat`, `SparkBeat` and `BondBeat`. BondBeat gains a no-vision mode that ends at fate's answer.
  - The bond-only rite is a `RevealCard`.
  - The interrupt registry's `ThreadingRite` waits until every other interrupt is closed. A thread can land in the same tick an encounter opens, and the first browser pass showed the rite rendering underneath it.
  - The sheet's Identity section shows the rite line.
  - `__DEBUG.openThreadingRite(agentId)` threads a mortal through the real card path and ticks until the rite is due.
- **Executor calls:**
  - The opening's primary is "Reach for them" and the result's is "Return to the world". The plan named only the "Bond" button. The hand's commit keeps the shared nudge-shell label ("Play your hand, let fate answer"), as in every encounter.
  - *Bond without a hand* hides once the bond has rolled, so it cannot discard a played result.

  - The gate is green (test, typecheck ratchet, build, encounter, CLI smoke, test:heavy).

They cover: the short rite's opening (Nesrin, real portrait), its bond stakes, and its result; the 4th thread's bond-only card (Saiwia); and the sheet line "Bound in spring, Year 1 — took your thread in doubt."
