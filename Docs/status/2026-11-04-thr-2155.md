# THR-2155 — The next follower has a path

**Shipped 2026-11-04.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience. Decided by the design lane on THR-2138 (items 2–4). No plan doc; the ticket was self-scoped. Engine: none (reads the existing seen floor). Content: three strings. UI: three components plus the dialog host.

1. **How to bind another (`src/data/first-use-captions.ts`, `ThreadsPanel.tsx`).** New first-use caption `threads_next`: "To bind another, play Agent Thread on a mortal you or {name} can see." The ticket's 19-word line was cut to 14 words because the caption table enforces a 15-word rule (`FirstUseCaption.test.tsx`). The meaning and the targeting rule are unchanged. The panel shows the caption only while the god holds exactly one *mortal* thread (`nextThreadCaption`). A relic threaded by Beat 2 does not count. `{name}` is The First. Binding a second mortal dismisses the caption for good (Law 51), the same way a hover or a press dismisses the other captions. A bare `{name}` with no name now falls back to "The First", never a raw token (`FIRST_USE_CAPTION_BARE_NAME_FALLBACK`).
2. **"Dormant court" → "Untouched lives" (`ReadTheThreadsPanel.tsx`, `view-content.ts`).** The label is authored as `READ_THREADS_UNTOUCHED_LIVES_LABEL`. The list goes through the seen floor: the new `untouchedLives(entries, canNameAgent)` keeps an entry only when `createSeenFloor(state).canName({kind:'agent'})` is true. `DialogHost` passes that predicate while the panel is open (`readThreadsCanName`, which is fail-soft and names nobody on a throw). If no predicate is passed, every entry is kept, as before. An emptied list does not render its section.
3. **The bonding pick says the others leave (`SensingBeat.tsx`, `meeting-narrative-prose.ts`).** A line under the choose hint reads "The two you pass over go their own way. You will not meet them again." It sits under the hint, not under the portrait cluster, because the cluster's foot is near the fold at 1080 (Law 33). It stays up while a portrait is focused and fades on confirm. With two candidates it says "The one you pass over…", and with fewer than two it is absent (`sensingPassedOverLine`).
4. **Tests.** `nextFollowerPath.thr2155.test.tsx` (9 tests) covers: the caption with one thread and not with two; a relic does not count; the second bind retires the caption for good; the name fallback; Untouched lives drops a mortal the floor cannot name; the label; an empty list hides the section; the passed-over line at 3 / 2 / 1 candidates.
