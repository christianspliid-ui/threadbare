# 2026-09-25 — THR-1552: the lair card (Fight on screen F4)

**Ticket:** [THR-1552](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1552) · **Project:** Physical Conflict · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-23-fight-on-screen.md` (slice F4) · **Pillars:** UI (the lair card in both sidebar sections) · Content (reuses F2's words: the card sentence, the clock words, the temper clauses) · Engine N/A (reads only).

## What was wrong

After F1 the lair block named its monster, and nothing more. The sidebar did not show how dangerous the beast was, how close it was to falling, or whether anyone had killed it. A legendary lair keeps its lair when its beast is felled but loses its `namedEliteId`, so the dead monster vanished from the card, and that is the common case (14 of 22 lairs are legendary by tick 100). A cleared major lair showed only "Cleared".

## What shipped

- **`buildLairMonsterCardModel`** now carries the whole card: the sentence (family line, Dread and Might phrases, the temper clause only once `temperShown`), the clock from `readOpponentCard` (recovered at the current tick, so it is the clock the next fight starts from), and `deceased`.
- **The slain reading:** when no living monster holds the lair, the card uses the lair's `namedEliteId` if it points at a retained dead monster. Otherwise it reverse-looks-up the dead monster whose `lairId` names the lair, and the latest `deceasedTick` wins. It reads "slain", full pips, and "slain by {name}" **only** when the monster's own sheet names the killer (`getAgentInfoCard(...).death.by`). The killer's name is a link.
- **`LairMonsterCard`** (new) renders it in the Monster Lair block and, new, in the Cleared Lair Section. It uses square 14 px pips, one clock word, no digits and no `word: value` strip. GameView builds cards for cleared lairs too, memoized on `worldVersion`.
- The opponent header's sentence and clock builders are exported and shared, so the veil and the sidebar say the same sentence.
- `__DEBUG.getLairMonsterCard` passes the ascendant through; its `.d.ts` entry documents the full row.
- Interface contract `monster-card-shows-on-lair`: 🟢 LIVE.
