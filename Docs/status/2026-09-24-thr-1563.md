# 2026-09-24 — THR-1563: a won siege keeps the town it took

**Ticket:** [THR-1563](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1563) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** Engine only. Content is N/A because no authored data changes. UI is N/A because nothing renders differently, only a node that used to vanish now stays (`Browser-verify exempt: engine only`, per the Done-when).

## What was wrong

A siege writes the besieged settlement's own id as `defenderArmyId` (`siegeResolution.ts`: *"Settlement acts as 'defender army'"*). On an attacker victory, `applyAftermath` therefore took the settlement as the losing army, and its unconditional `disbandArmy(loserArmyId)` ran `removeNode` on the town it had just conquered. That deleted the residents' `located_at`, the conquest `controls` edge written a few lines earlier, the trade routes, the Places and the `occurred_at` history. On seed 42 medium, Crystalspire (`loc_6`) vanished at tick 158 and left 12 of its 16 mortals with no position.

## What shipped

- **Only an army is disbanded.** `battleAftermath.ts` disbands the loser only when it carries `armyState`. A sacked settlement keeps its losses (prosperity, tier, Places, routes, conquest) and stays on the map.
- **No one is left with no position.** `relocateOccupantsToParent` moves everyone `located_at` a Place up to its parent Location before the aftermath removes that Place. The parent comes from `resolveToParentLocation`, falling back to the settlement. The function traces `result: 'occupants_displaced'`.
- **Tests with the real siege shape.** Four `battleAftermath.test.ts` arms, all with the defender id set to the settlement:
  - the won siege keeps the settlement, the victor's `controls` edge and the residents' positions;
  - a mortal in a destroyed Place ends up at the parent Location;
  - a defender-won siege still disbands the attacker;
  - a field battle is unchanged.
- **Siege-level test.** A `siegeResolution.test.ts` arm drives a real siege to attacker victory and asserts that the town and its resident survive.
- **Falsified:** with both fixes reverted, 3 of the new arms go red.
- **Wiki:** `armies-battles-reference.html` gains a player-facing line and an IMPL bullet.
