# 2026-10-05 — THR-1732: the commit stays on screen when the hand wraps

**Ticket:** [THR-1732](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1732) · **Project:** Encounter Experience · **Pillars:** UI only. Engine N/A: layout only, no state or tick path. Content N/A: no copy changes. · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-04-thr-1732-five-card-hand-fit.md`

## Why

Hands deal 4–8 cards and wrap at four per row, so a second row is ordinary. With two rows the commit button sat at y = 1276–1325, below the 1080 fold, and the player had to scroll the column to act at all (Law 48's fire beat).

## What shipped

- **The hand bar** (`nudge-hand-bar`, `NudgePhaseShell.tsx`). The essence-left counter, the commit with its cost pips, and the glyph legend now sit in one bar, `position: sticky; bottom: 0`, as the shell's last child. When the hand fits, the bar sits in flow under the cards (the commit is 28px below the last row, against 24px before). When the hand overflows, the cards scroll under it and the commit stays visible.
- The row above the cards that held the counter and the legend is gone, and its ~34px went to the hand. An empty hand keeps the commit and drops the legend.
- New constants: `HAND_BAR_PAD_Y_PX` (12), `HAND_BAR_FADE_PX` (28, the fade to void, which doubles as top padding) and `HAND_BAR_SKIRT_PX` (32). The skirt is a void box-shadow below the bar. Chrome resolves sticky against the column's padding box, so the stuck bar rides 24px above the column edge, and without the skirt the scrolling card showed through that strip. The plan did not name this constant; it is an executor addition. `CARDS_PER_ROW` stays 4, and the card format is untouched.
- `data-hand-overflow` comes from one `ResizeObserver` and is presentation state only. Without an observer it stays `false`.
- Comments at the three mount points (`EncounterVeil.tsx`, `BondBeat.tsx`, `FormativeTestBeat.tsx`) say that no ancestor may set `overflow`. At the veil column's `paddingBottom` a comment says why the bar needs no reservation.
- Tests: `nudgeHandBar.test.tsx` (7 tests).
- Deviation: the legend sits beside the commit rather than flush right. `marginLeft: auto` sits inside the Tooltip wrapper, as it did on the old row.

## Verification

- `npm run gate`: test, typecheck, build and encounter all PASS (track=code, engine=no).

  - **Unsafe Bridge** (5 cards): commit 995–1044 without scrolling; page `scrollHeight` 1080; `data-hand-overflow="true"`. At max scroll every card bottom (657 ×4, 967) is ≤ the bar top (967).
  - **Riders Behind the Caravan** (5 cards): commit bottom 1044. In flow, the gap from cards to commit is 27.5px.
  - **Meet The First** formative test (7 cards): commit bottom 1036.
  - `__DEBUG.getOpenModals()` → `["EncounterVeil"]`.
- No route dealt a one-row (≤4) hand. The in-flow placement was measured at max scroll instead.
