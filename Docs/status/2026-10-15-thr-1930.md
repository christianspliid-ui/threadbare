- **The guard now measures the defect.** `scripts/first-encounter-gate.ts` counts three faults: `no-pull` (a journey to an encounter with no `motivationPull`), `target-lost` (a `better_encounter` reroute that drops `targetEncounterId`) and `goal-cut` (an arrival goal `dropped` because the story breath or the cap cut it, the two stages that must keep it). The arrival verdict comes from a new `movement` trace (`event: 'arrival_goal'`, phaseAgentDecision.ts). It is written however the decision ends. The DECIDE timeline event carries the verdict only when an encounter is picked, so a cut that ended in an undertaking or in idle went unseen there (found by the review gate). The filter pipeline now reports `journeyGoalCutBy` (`story_breath` or `cap`) when one of those two stages removes the flagged goal, and the trace carries it as `arrivalGoalCutBy`. An eligibility drop (threat, reputation, prerequisites) is fair and is not a fault. The encounter cache is static per location, so it cannot tell the two apart (review gate, round 2). The heavy test asserts zero faults on seeds 42, 99 and 11, and that each seed watched at least one journey and one arrival. The first-encounter-by-t30 bound stays. The longest gap is still reported but no longer gated.
- **The guard has teeth.** Removing the pull assignment: 26 `no-pull` faults on seed 42. Removing the cap reserve and the story-breath keep: `goal-cut` on all three seeds (1 · 4 · 3). No decision-phase reroute happens on these seeds, so the `target-lost` check could not be shown to fire here.
