# THR-1121 — the encounter veil stops selling odds

**2026-08-15 · Encounter Experience · Engine + Content + UI**

Christian's THR-974 verdict session found two halves of one legacy surface on Swindler Found step 2: *"the resume/intervene buttons bottom right in step 2, is a legacy UX pattern from the old encounter"*, and of the choice set — `supportive · 1 essence · +3% success / coercive · 5 essence · +15% success / withdrawn · 0 essence · fate decides` — *"this step 2 and the choices seem like legacy encounter design. am I correct"*. He was: both came from the pre-nudge divine-intervention layer, which sold **odds** for essence. That is the exact trade the Nudge Model pivot rejects (THR-773/WS0) — the god nudges the fiction, fate picks the outcome.

## What changed

**The generic stance triple is retired.** `generateInterventionChoices` returns `[]` for every court position, including under the force-full-visibility debug override. It is emptied rather than deleted because `EncounterNotification.choices` is still the channel a template's `authoredChoices` ride on; only the generic producer is gone. Court position now governs prose depth alone.

**Nothing reads `probabilityBoost` any more.** The application site in `unifiedActionResolution` — where a choice's price was converted into a flat addition to the roll, on the same additive channel as push and the company assist — is gone, and `resolveInterveneChoice` no longer synthesises the number into choice memory. An authored choice still costs essence and still keys which authored ending resolves (`aftermathConfig.branchOnStep`, which 30 of 34 variant-carrying templates depend on); it simply no longer moves the roll. The named `nudge:<id>` channel is now the only route from a player decision to a probability, which is the point — it renders as words, and it is authored per card rather than priced per stance.

**A step with nothing authored gets the fate-alone stage.** Retiring the triple left unauthored steps with an empty `choices` array and no nudge phase — a scene with no move on it. So the nudge phase is now built with an empty hand whenever the step authored neither a hand nor choices, giving *"Nothing here answers to you. Let it play out."* over the usual motive/test framing with `Let fate decide` as the only move. That is the model working, not a gap.

**The `Intervene` / `Resume` pair is gone from the veil.** The commit moved into the stage body, under the choices, wearing the nudge stage's own affordance and words (`Let fate decide`, gold-outlined, running cost in `CostPips`) — one pattern for "hand this moment to fate" wherever the player is standing. `Resume` named a simulation control, which is the wrong noun for setting an encounter down; the walk-away now reads `Look away`. The dead `+N% success` label went with the mechanic it reported.

## Two things deliberately not done

**Gate duty keeps its stances, sourced locally** (`gateDutyStanceChoices`, `probabilityBoost: 0`). `cg.quest.gate_duty` is the one encounter that *decorates* the generic triple rather than authoring a hand: roughly eight tables key off `interventionType`, in the adapter and in `GATE_DUTY_BRANCH_CONSEQUENCES`. Retiring the producer would have deleted a fully-authored encounter's move set as a side effect. Converting it to nudge cards is real content work and is [THR-1123](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1123).

**A pre-existing Law 13 violation was filed, not fixed.** `buildSimpleEncounterStageModel`'s Resolution Readout prints `Capability 45% · Projected success 33%` to the player. This change narrows who can reach it — `unifiedTemplateForStage` now admits every unified template, so the simple adapter serves only `watched` tier and the (empty) legacy population — but does not make it legal. [THR-1124](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1124).

## Verification note

`preview_start` is refused in unattended runs, which also shuts the Playwright route. Browser-verify was discharged by the sanctioned jsdom-render substitution: `nudgeStageFateAlone.test.tsx` renders the real `NudgePhaseShell` over a phase built by the real adapter and asserts the empty-hand line, the commit, the absence of `Intervene`/`Resume`, and that no percentage appears anywhere on the stage — each with its falsification twin.

**The 1920×1080 pixel pass is still owed, and is filed rather than waved through** — [THR-1125](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1125). Per the triage split at impediments #586/#593, jsdom is sufficient for a token or label change and is *not* sufficient here: this diff deletes an element, adds one, and makes a whole new screen reachable, so overflow, wrap, z-order and the viewport contract are all in play and none of them is assertable without pixels.
