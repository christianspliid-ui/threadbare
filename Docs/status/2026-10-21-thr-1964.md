# THR-1964 — The aftermath opens with what decided it

**Shipped 2026-10-21.** The aftermath's first line now says what decided the ending before any count or chip: *"Kael Thornweaver won Trade Caravan Escort, at a price. The work on Iron failed, and Kael Thornweaver faced it alone."* It leads both the derived overview and an authored one.

**What changed.**

* **One price word.** `describeActionOutcome` ("completed at great cost") is gone. The overview and the two resolution chronicle lines read `outcomeSentence` / `outcomeClause` in `aftermathWords.ts`, which shares `PRICE_WORDS` with `OUTCOME_PHRASES` ("won, at a price").
* **The cause line** (`buildAftermathCauseLine`). The deciding step is the last worst step on a costly or lost ending and the last best step on a won one. It is named by its own title (a fight step's "First exchange") or "The work on {Reach}", with a band verb (held / nearly broke / failed / broke badly / went well / shone) and a god clause per step ("your cards held it" / "your cards could not save it" / "{name} faced it alone"). A deciding step whose band contradicts the ending (an at-cost win over a step that went well) is not named, so the line falls back to the outcome sentence alone.
* **The price row** (`priceRowFor`). A `success_at_cost` ending with no loss row and no complication gets one "The price" row with no number. THR-978's no-double-report rule holds.

**Evidence.** 46 unit tests in `src/engine/__tests__/aftermathCauseLine.test.ts` (every outcome × band × god-touched/alone × one-step/many-step, and the price row's presence rule). Bash-vite at 1920×1080 with `?spawn=encounter.slice.unsafe_bridge&outcome=success_at_cost`: the pin verdict is `band_rendered`, and the aftermath opens with the cause line. In that run a complication drew ("Hostile Interest"), so no price row was owed.
