Content (two seed-only templates). UI: the encounter veil, unchanged, renders both templates.

## What changed

1. **A survey arranges a visit.** `UNDERTAKING_CELL_APPOINTMENTS['cell.observe.location']` gets two new optional payoff fields. `siteClasses: ['ruin','wonder']` means a survey of a town arranges nothing. `leadVisit` means the survey must have left the surveyor holding a `narrowed` lead, and **one pending visit per holder per ruin** (the re-plan's rule, pinned by a test). The visit falls due 48 ticks later, at the ruin.
2. **A pending visit pauses decay.** `knows_clue_of.pendingVisitDueTick` is spared by `phaseClueDecay` until due + `CLUE_LEAD_VISIT_GRACE_TICKS`. The plan drafted 12. It is **24**, because the missed branch only becomes eligible at due + window (12) + missed delay (12).
3. **The visit's outcome sets the lead.** The new `sharpen_clue` effect acts only on the actor's own lead. It keys on the encounter's **terminal outcome**, not a step band. A new pure `terminalActionOutcome` mirrors `advanceStep` without drawing, and the step-outcome reaction carries it as `actionOutcome`. So the lead always agrees with the ending the player reads: success → `located`, at cost → `narrowed` and fresh, failure or missed → cold.
4. **Two seed-only templates.** `ruins.lead.visit` (Eye then Stone, a dealt hand on each step, all five endings, chips on the ruin via `$here`) and `ruins.lead.cold` (the chip is on the mortal, because `$here` is not the ruin there). The `ruins.` id prefix is claimed for the encounter kind. The tags `#ruin_lead` and `#lead_gone_cold` are seated.
5. Rulebook §10 *Watching earns something* now describes the shipped climb, *hear → survey → visit → delve*.

**Deviations from the plan.** The visit verdict is keyed on the action outcome rather than the step band: a step `near_miss` aggregates to `success_at_cost`, which gives the plan's row. The templates carry no `locationSubtypes`, following the hunt's confront precedent: `requirePlace` already fixes the place, and the appointment-table tests judge the kept member at settlement tiers. The planter reads graph state rather than the `ops` array.
