# THR-1953 — mortal wealth S3: money encounters change the world

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** Slice S3 of the mortal-wealth plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-15-thr-1936-mortal-wealth.md`). `economy_shift` now writes the world as well as a mortal's Means. The new quantities are `prosperity` and `unrest` (a settlement's 0–100 scalar), `stock` (a resource id, every `staple` or every `deposit` at the settlement), `lane_volume` (the settlement's busiest `trades_with` lane, never thinned below `ENCOUNTER_LANE_VOLUME_FLOOR`, so an encounter slows a road and never closes one) and `purse` (a faction's treasury, through `applyTreasuryDelta`). A world row lands where the actor stands (`$here`) unless it names `$target`. A purse row names `$realm`, a cast key or a faction definition id, and definition ids bind through `bindFactionDefinitionIds`. Each quantity has its own named steps in `src/data/economy-shift-constants.ts` (`ECONOMY_SHIFT_STEPS`). Staple rows use `harvestStockAmount(band)` = `HARVEST_ENCOUNTER_BASE` 8 × `HARVEST_BAND_SCALE`, which replaces the god-scale ±25 for encounter use.

Content: world rows on 18 simple encounters (harvest bounty, aid to refugees, the rich vein, labour dispute, pirate raid, the caravan and harbour builds, the toll bridge and more) and on 10 branching ones (granary riot, coiners' mint, crown's reckoning, overdue caravan, the famine granaries on both paths, the warlord's tribute's redistribute reaction, which fills the stores its line already named, THR-1872 defect 11). The seven purse rows are the border levy, the keeper's petition and the tithe demanded (to `$realm`), the sway mercenary, the garrison's price and the toll of blades (to `mercenary_company`), and the guild crisis (in or out of `merchant_consortium`). Debt rows on `attachment_grant` (`agreement.debt.minor`): the tithe demanded on a critical failure (owed to the collector) and the tithe-barn raid at a cost (owed to the reeve). Both count in `countDebts`. Market day festival's success narrative gains its prosperity line.

UI: the outcome table reads the town before and after (`snapshotCastEconomy` / `diffCastEconomy`, the cast receipt's reader), so a watched encounter's aftermath names the move in the town page's own words. A crossed tier comes first, and nothing appears when nothing moved (Law 56). Traces: `economy.world.shift`.

**Not built (left to the Deferral, THR-1954, or not specified by the research):** smuggled salt (no `salt` resource exists), toll writes (`toll_bridge` success, toll dispute), `the_loan`'s debt (a simple encounter has no counterparty to owe), the debt arbitration's promissory note and the toll-gate writ's toll-book debt (path-specific, prose only partly supports them), the cathedral's temple treasury, and every lane-bound or "threatened" write.

Evidence: `npm run gate` PASS (test, typecheck ratchet, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, CLI smoke). `economyShift.test.ts` (9) and `outcomeEffects.test.ts` (10, 3 new) pass. `test:heavy` runs on CI after merge.
