# THR-1895 — The Stage, slice 3: encounters play on the Stage

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** Slice S3 of the encounter Stage plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1877-encounter-stage.md` § D4, D9, D10). An encounter no longer covers the screen with an opaque veil. It plays on the Stage, in the full layout, beside the god rail over the dimmed map with its place lit in the window. The scene art, the cast and *At stake* sit in the left column. The step dots, title, context line, stakes line, scene, factor lines and the hand with its commit bar sit in the right. Every step, the aftermath and the watched view use the same panel. The aftermath's exit now reads **Continue** (was "Return to the world"). Escape and *Show on map* still minimise, and the badge reopens the step.

Engine: the encounter builders (`encounter-stage/adapters/*`, `narrativeLinker`, `buildLairMonsterCardModel`) moved into `src/engine/views/stage/`, with one-line re-exports at their old paths (THR-1822 D16 step 5). The pure modules they read moved too, so `src/engine/views/` still imports nothing from components: `encounter-stage/types`, `encounterNotificationRuntime`, `shared/entityVisualResolver`, `attachmentGlyphs` (to `src/data/attachment-glyphs.ts`) and `DELTA_CLUSTER_MAX` (to `src/data/delta-cluster-content.ts`). The gate-duty adapter now sets the encounter's hex, so the checkpoint lights its place (`litSource: 'beat'`).

UI: `EncounterVeil` keeps its name and props. Its three `document.body` portals, the full-screen inset, the 52% / 65% column, the 220 px side column and every 540 / 500 px cap are gone. It renders two columns into a `renderFrame`, which `StageHost` fills with the Stage (`STAGE_FRAME_KINDS` now lists `encounter`). Prose caps at `--measure-prose`. A wheel inside the Stage panel now scrolls the moment's column (the S2 layer swallowed it). `getStage()` separates `scrollingBelowCap` from `scrollingAtCap`, the panel-height column that Law 57 allows to scroll. Content: the player manual and the IA manifest row name the Stage (new mount kind `stage`).

**Measured in the browser** (bash-vite, Playwright): at 1920×1080 the panel is 1272 × 984 at (344, 72) with a 280 px window; at 2560×1440 it is 1592 × 1336 with a 520 px window. In both, the lit hex is in the window and `scrollingBelowCap` is empty. A five-card hand (*The Unsafe Bridge*) lays out as a row of four and a row of one with the commit bar on screen. At 1080p the column scrolls 279 px at the panel's height to reach the fifth card. That is lawful under Law 57, and fitting it without the scroll is THR-1994. Evidence: `Docs/evidence/thr-1895/`.
