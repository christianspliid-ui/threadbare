# THR-2266 — A quiet day on the map between chapters

**Shipped 2026-11-21.** Cold playtest round 8: testers described the First's chapters as back to back, with no room to cast a card or open a place between them. Built from the design lane's decision on THR-2263 (no plan doc).

1. **The hold.** `ArrivalHoldReason` gains `'breather'`; `worldOpenedEncounterHeldBy` takes an optional `breatherTicksLeft` and pushes it when > 0 (`src/engine/views/interruptRegistry.ts`). `CHAPTER_BREATHER_TICKS` = 12 running ticks (one game day; 12 s at speed 1), with `chapterBreatherTicksLeft(closedAtTick, tick)` and `chapterKeyOf(notif)`. Ticks, not wall-clock, so the day does not run down while paused.
2. **Where it starts.** GameView records the tick when an Aftermath is dismissed ("Return to the world", and leaving it by Escape or the context strip). The auto-open scan passes the ticks left only for a chapter none of whose beats has mounted yet (`openedChapterKeysRef`, filled on every successful open, capped at `OPENED_CHAPTER_MEMORY_MAX` = 200). A later step or Aftermath of an opened chapter, a badge/row/Needs-you open, and the live answer run are never held. `breatherTicksLeft` is a scan dependency, so the scan looks again each running tick of a quiet day.
3. **Needs you.** New producer `chapter_waiting` (group Waiting, Law 58) lists "‹Mortal› — ‹chapter› waits for you" for a `breather` or `card_armed` hold, never for the 2.5 s `recent_input` hold; a press opens the chapter at once (`opens: 'chapter'`). Words in `src/data/frame-content.ts`.
4. **Trace.** `encounter.auto_open_held` (`EncounterAutoOpenHeldTrace`) with `reason: 'breather'` and `ticksLeft`, once per hold. `__DEBUG.getInterruptState().heldEncounter` now carries `ticksLeft` for a breather hold.

**Evidence.** Unit: `src/engine/views/__tests__/chapterBreather.thr2266.test.ts`. jsdom: `src/components/Game/__tests__/chapterWaitingRow.thr2266.test.tsx`. Browser (bash-vite on port 5287, Playwright, 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium&forceencounters`): after an Aftermath closed, a second chapter spawned on Kael Thornweaver read `heldEncounter.heldBy = ['breather']`, `ticksLeft: 12`; after `__DEBUG.tick(11)` still held with 1 left; after one more tick the chapter opened. Screenshot: `Docs/evidence/thr-2266/needs-you-chapter-waiting-1920.png`.

**Measurement (Done-when 4), partial.** A plain `?seeded&size=medium` run auto-played for 162 ticks opened no chapter at all, and the breather held nothing in that span (0 `encounter.auto_open_held` traces), so the 600-tick count was not finished in this unattended run. Bound: the breather delays only the first beat of a new chapter, by at most 12 ticks per chapter boundary, so at 4 chapters in 600 ticks it adds at most 48 ticks.
