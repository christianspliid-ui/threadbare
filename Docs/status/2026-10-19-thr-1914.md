# THR-1914 — Inspector S6: card-first arming from the hand

**Shipped 2026-10-19.** Slice S6 of the Inspector plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1876-the-inspector.md` § D9 way 3). Pressing a card in the god rail's hand now arms it. While armed, every thing it can land on is lit on the map and in the Inspector and the rest dims; links, back and forward stay live. A banner says what the card waits for ("Illuminating Whisper: choose a person. You can keep following links." + Esc). A map click, or a drag of the card onto a row or the map, **chooses** a target: the banner becomes the commit line ("Illuminating Whisper on Conrad. Reaches into a mortal's inner world…" + "Cast on Conrad"). "Cast here" on an Inspector row, or the commit line's button, **casts** through the one cast path, and the existing receipt toast names the same card and target. Esc puts the card down first; a card that leaves the hand clears itself with "… left your hand. Nothing was spent."

Engine: `engine/views/armedTargets.ts` — `evaluateCardOnTarget` (one card on one ref through the Act row's gates, one template), `buildArmedTargets` and the world-keyed `armedTargets` selector (candidates from `hexContents` on hexes the seen floor allows), `refForMapNode`; the `'armedTargets'` `KnowledgeSurface` in `knowledge.ts`; `ARMED_CLEARED_LINE_MS`, `ARMED_LOCKED_LIT`. UI: `inspector/useCardArming.ts` (the only writer of the new `GameScreenState.armed`; sets `lensSuppressed` while armed; Esc layer above the dialog band; `ui_card_arm` traces), `inspector/ArmedCard.tsx` (banner — in the Inspector, or at the bottom centre of the map when it is closed — and the lit / locked / dim rows via a new optional `rowState` on `LinkRowAccessory`), `HexMapV2/scene/ArmedTargetsLayer.ts` on the lens plate builder (`addLensDimPlates`, factored out of `HexLensLayer`, never forked), the hand's `HandPressContext` provider and drag seam. Content: `ARMED_TARGET_WORDS`, `ARMED_WORDS`; `ui.your_cards` takes its arming words and the retired `ui.avatar_wheel` folds into it; the unconsumed `ACTION_CARD_GLYPH_LEGEND` is deleted (§ D14). Debug: `getArmedTargets()`, `armCard`, `chooseArmedTarget`, `castArmed`, `disarmCard`.

**Calls made on the way.** (1) Bare-land cards light only the known hexes the range gate can reach; a hex past it is "Too far from here." by the same gate and the click on it says so, so the build never runs the gates over the whole map (kill criterion 6, applied pre-emptively). (2) Eight cards declare a `'faction'` target category outside `TargetCategory`; they now light factions (their guild halls). (3) The gates decide what a card lands on — Illuminating Whisper's gates accept factions as well as people, so their rows light too. (4) The Done-when's Shutash and Nutat do not exist in today's seeded world; the browser check used The Bone Coast's page (seven people and six factions lit with "Cast here", places and land dimmed) and cast on Conrad.

Measured on seed 42 medium, fog off, every card revealed: the worst of 143 hand cards (`sub.vision`, 770 lit) builds in 2.98 ms — inside the 16 ms frame budget.

**Review gate (two rounds, three confirmed findings, all fixed).** An armed cast now re-reads only the armed card's slot, because the full hand stops at `MAX_SLOTS` (20) and a lit card past the cap would have failed silently; every refusal now answers with a line. The armed-targets view keys on a stamp of what a paused cast changes (pool, held actions, covenants, the avatar's hex), so the map relights. `lensSuppressed` has one writer in GameView — Stage open or card armed — so neither lifts the other's hold, and no armed lighting draws under the Stage.
