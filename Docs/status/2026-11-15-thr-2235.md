# THR-2235 — Read the Threads fades each chapter by its age

**Shipped 2026-11-15.** Thematic Pressure & Living World. Decided by the design lane on THR-2225.

- **The rule.** Each chapter in a Read the Threads result now gets its detail from its own age, not from the length of the window. Up to half a day it is told in full. Up to a day it keeps full detail. Up to two days it shows the mortal, the chapter and the outcome. Older chapters fade. The strained-pool drop still lowers every chapter by one step. A longer read therefore shows every row a shorter read shows, at the same detail, plus older ones. Before, the 3-day read (12 essence) blurred even the last half day to a tally.
- **Faded chapters still tell a story.** Where a reach group has faded chapters, it leads with one story line: the most significant faded chapter, picked with the Story So Far's own significance score and told in its beat words (`pickStoryEntry` and `tellDigestEntry`, exported from `threadDigest.ts`). `DialogHost` resolves the line, because the beat words need the world graph. The tally sits under it, smaller.
- **Notable is never an empty heading.** A notable chapter old enough to fade renders as a mortal / chapter / outcome row.
- **Each window says what it buys.** Four lines under the costs, authored in `src/data/view-content.ts` (`READ_THREADS_WINDOW_LINES`), with no numerals.
- **Evidence.** Six jsdom tests (`ReadTheThreadsPanel-thr2235.test.tsx`) cover the superset, per-age detail, the faded story line, the fallback line without a host teller, the notable row and the window lines. Browser check at 1920×1080 on `?view=game&seeded&size=medium` after `__DEBUG.tick(40)`: the four window lines render under their costs, the 3-day read renders, there are no console errors, and the panel stays in the viewport (`scrollWidth` 1920, dialog bottom 1080). Forty ticks is too short for anything to fade in the seeded world, so the faded path is proved by the jsdom tests.
