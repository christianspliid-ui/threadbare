### 2026-10-03 — THR-1697: a timed condition never lands permanent by omission

**The bug.** *Wolves at the Fold* (`wolf-winter-watch.ts`) applied Under Watch to a village through `apply_condition` with no `durationTicks`. The one condition writer (`applyConditionToActor`) fell back to `CONDITION_DEFAULT_DURATION_TICKS = 0`, wrote no `ticksRemaining`, and `decayConditions` never counted it down, so the village kept the watched-shadow penalty for the rest of the game. Its chip reads `CONDITION_DURATIONS` and promised a week. The content lint added here found the same shape in 10 more sites across 7 other encounters (Exhausted in `the_table_that_holds`, `court_summons`, `crowns_reckoning`; Shaken in `border_levy`, `tithe_demanded`, `keepers_petition`; Harvest Blight in `flood_dyke_mending`).

**The fix.** `resolveConditionDurationTicks` (`src/engine/effects/conditionApplier.ts`) resolves an explicit `durationTicks`, then the condition's `CONDITION_DURATIONS` term, then indefinite. That covers every `apply_condition` and `inflict_condition`. Conditions with no row (Scarred, minted traits) stay indefinite, and `durationTicks: 0` is the explicit spelling for a permanent condition. Wolves at the Fold also authors `CONDITION_UNDER_WATCH_DURATION` explicitly.

**Evidence.** New `conditionTermFallback.test.ts`: writer arms for omitted, explicit and untimed durations, plus a content lint over every registered template. The lint fails any timed `apply_condition` that resolves to a permanent edge and asserts it walked the wolf site. Two tests that pinned the old "omitted = forever" behaviour were updated: `conditionExpiry.test.ts` now expects an omitted duration to take the condition's term and expire, and an explicit `0` to stay; `fightHarm.test.ts` uses explicit `0` for the indefinite case. `npm run gate` and the 30-tick CLI smoke results are in the PR body.
