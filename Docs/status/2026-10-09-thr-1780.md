# 2026-10-09 — THR-1780: Who holds power opens from its lists

**Ticket:** [THR-1780](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1780) · **Found by:** warm playtest round 1, 3/3 testers (and 3/3 in the dry run)

The Notables and Rivals panels were lists you could not open, and the Notables badge disagreed with the list beneath it.

- **Notables, one row per notable.** The panel drew one row per agenda, completed and failed included, so a ruler with two agendas appeared twice ("Rulers (19), with Scorvin in it twice"). `buildNotableEntries` (`src/components/Game/NotablesPanel.tsx`) now groups agendas under their sponsor, active first. The badge reads `countShownNotables`, the same set the panel draws, where it used to count active agendas only.
- **Names open cards.** Every notable and every agenda target is an `EntityLink` through the one ref router (Law 21), kind from `worldRefKindOf`. A sponsor missing from the graph renders as text, never a dead link. Clicking a name closes the dropdown, because the portalled panel (z 9999) otherwise stays lit over the card's backdrop.
- **Rivals, opened in place.** Rival gods are run state (`rivalDefinitions`), not graph nodes, so there is no sheet to route to. The name is a disclosure (`aria-expanded`) that shows the god's spheres, temper and stance toward you. A rival-god card would be a new page kind and was kept out of scope; veto invited on the ticket.
- **Layout.** The family label now rides the progress strip. Beside the target line, it had truncated long place names to "Eyes on …".

**Evidence.** Five new jsdom tests: one row per notable, badge equals distinct notables, both names route, missing sponsor stays text, and the dropdown closes on open. One more test covers the rival disclosure. In the browser at 1920×1080 on `?view=game&seeded&size=medium` after `__DEBUG.tick(200)`: badge 13, 13 rows over 20 agendas. Riasu's and Scorvin's cards open from their names, and the Rivals disclosure reads "God of Life and Time, territorial by temper. Toward you: wrathful." Captures are in `Docs/evidence/thr-1780/`. A pre-existing card defect (raw archetype key subtitle, doubled thread chips) is filed as [THR-1797](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1797).
