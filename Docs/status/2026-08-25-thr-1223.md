### THR-1223 — the shipped nudge corpus rewritten to Prose Doctrine v2 (2026-08-25)

**The ruling.** Playing `encounter.border.the_unclaimed_relic`, the director called the shipped prose *"still borderline unreadable … you are still writing in situ instead of just describing."* The failure was **mode**, not vocabulary: six generations of sentence-level correction had polished prose inside a scene-painting register the old rules themselves commanded. Prose Doctrine v2 (PR #1606) replaced the rules; this ticket brought the fifteen shipped nudge-era templates to them.

**What every template now holds.** Openings in the three-paragraph skeleton — a one-sentence P1 arrival per setting class carrying `{name}`/`{location}`, with the P2+P3 spine (situation with costs already paid, then exactly one Seed Dice stake shape) in the first step's `narrativeTemplate`, composed 51–75 words against the 80 budget. Spines, afterimages, card band prose and aftermath overviews in narrator mode: a game master reporting events, facts stated never encoded, no interior sensation, no camera work. Every card named imperative verb+noun and described like a spell — direct effect, no odds-talk — under the two live-calibration rulings from batch 1 (rewrite from scratch in basic game-master language; a card's effect line never repeats a word from the card's name). Cast members now enter the spines by token (`{cast:survivor}`, `{cast:officer}`, `{cast:serjeant}`, `{cast:keeper}`, `{cast:stranger}`, `{cast:swindler}`, `{cast:bridge_keeper}`, `{cast:caravan_master}`), so the doctrine's "named people over unnamed figures" is real graph names, not authored ones.

**Shipped in five batches, director-gated once.** Batch 1 (PR #1610) was the calibration case, rewritten live with the director; batches 2–5 (PRs #1612, #1613, #1615, and the batch-5 PR) applied the approved register to the remaining border five and the slice nine with no further review ask, per the ticket's Done-when — the integrated slice checkpoint (THR-1220) is where the result is read. Zero mechanical changes throughout: same steps, effects, seeds, bands, hands; the fork template's F1/F1b/F2/F3 band-guarantee semantics and the toll template's five package fixes preserved, one corpus test's pinned prose literal carried forward, and one composition test re-pointed at the new literals. ~90 cards renamed; four genuine imperatives (`count`, `keep`, `thicken`, `warm`) added to the checker lexicon by its own documented extension path.

**The measurable delta.** `check:encounter --all` reported 302 doctrine warnings at THR-1224's ship; after this ticket every member template reports **zero** — the remaining warn population is the camp seven (THR-1222's retrofit, which now binds Doctrine v2) and pre-slice legacy templates. `fiction` strings were left untouched by design; their retirement is THR-1225.
