**Buttons hold still under the player's hand.** Warm playtest round 3 reported three clicks that missed because the target moved. All three are fixed. (1) In the aftermath, the "choose what to carry forward" prompt now hides but keeps its space once a reaction is picked, so Continue stays where it was. (2) The top bar's clock status line now has a fixed width (17.5rem, or 12.5rem when the bar narrows) and no longer sizes to its text, so the items after it do not move as the status changes. Measured at 1920×1080: the line stays 280px wide for both "Time is still…" and "running". (3) When a Stage moment closes after the player has panned or zoomed, the camera stays where the player put it. The Stage snapshots the camera once its frame settles, and on close a move larger than `STAGE_CLOSE_PLAYER_MOVED_PX` (4px) or `STAGE_CLOSE_PLAYER_MOVED_ZOOM_RATIO` (1%) skips the THR-2056 re-centre. Under reduced motion the re-centre still cuts by design; elsewhere it eases. Tests: `EncounterVeil.test.tsx`, `SimulationControlsStatusWidth.test.tsx`, `useStageCamera.test.tsx`.
