# THR-1922 — armies march on and besiege only enemies

**Shipped 2026-10-17.** `selectArmyObjective` now takes only a settlement held by an enemy of the army's faction (`isEnemyFaction` in the new `src/engine/warAims/enemy.ts`: `hostile_to` either way, or `relates_to` with `isRival`, `basis: 'war'` or sentiment below −0.3) and never one its faction keeps a hall or home in. Siege start (`phaseBattleDetection`) asks `mayBesiege`: never the own-hall town, otherwise an enemy's town or the army's own objective (agenda campaigns keep working). An army with no enemy town holds at its muster point.

Seed 42 medium, 600 ticks, after the guard: 30 siege-ticks, 0 on an own-hall town, 0 on a non-enemy holder (the ticket's baseline: 397, of which 200 own-hall). Field-battle start is unchanged; the war-aims S1 ([THR-1933](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1933)) hoists `isEnemyFaction` into it, which this ticket unblocks.
