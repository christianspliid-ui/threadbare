### 2026-10-02 — THR-1586: each kind of dialog moment gets its own colour family

**A dialog's colour now names the kind of moment.**
- **Story** beats (StoryBeat, AscendantBeat, JourneyVignette) sit on a firelit umber ground with a terracotta-amber edge.
- The **elder** dilemma sits on verdigris.
- **Gains** (DivineReceipt, MomentCard) sit on dark gilt, edged in the one game gold.
- The **Premonition's** violet / wine-plum and the **veil's** void-and-gold are untouched.
- Every **reference surface** stays on the neutral ramp, so colour keeps meaning *this is a moment*.

**Mechanism.** `Modal` gained an optional `context` prop, which applies a `.dialog-ctx-*` class.
- That class sets local `--dlg-*` properties, and the panel reads them with today's neutral values as fallbacks. A modal without a context is unchanged.
- The registry lives in `src/components/shared/dialogContext.ts`. `__DEBUG.getDialogContexts()` reports it, plus the context of every open dialog.
- `RevealCard` forwards `context`. `EventPopup`, its only standalone caller, passes none.

**Sphere tint spreads to the card face.** The Premonition's per-option recipe moved to `shared/sphereTint.ts`, and the Premonition's output is unchanged, as pinned by a test.
- `CardFace` now tints the resting edge and ground of any action or nudge card that draws essence from a named sphere.
- Selected, resolved and disabled cards keep their own border. The price is never tinted.
- The nudge-card DOM pin was re-recorded with the reason in its header. Only colour strings moved: tinted and untinted cards measure the same 210×333.75 px.

**Contrast and laws.**
- Every new tone clears AA on both gradient stops, including the neutral text tokens that children keep using. This is locked by `dialogContextTokens.test.ts`.
- The Law 30 amendment and the Law 32 clarification are in `laws.md`, with the chat gate satisfied on 2026-10-02.

**Defects fixed in passing:**
- An undefined `--bg-base` painted gold-button labels at 1.7:1. It is now `--bg-deep`.
- Literal hex values in JourneyVignetteModal.
- Hex fallbacks in EmergenceDilemmaModal.
