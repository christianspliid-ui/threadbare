# Gate Duty authors its own hand (THR-1123)

**2026-08-15** — `cg.quest.gate_duty` was the last encounter running the generic
stance triple. It now deals authored nudge cards like every other encounter.

## What it was

Gate duty never authored its own moves. It *decorated* the engine's generic
supportive/coercive/withdrawn set: roughly eight tables in
`buildGateDutyEncounterStageModel` and one in `unifiedActionResolution` keyed on
`EncounterChoiceMemory.interventionType`, turning three generic stances into
per-step labels, intent paragraphs, aftermath recaps and consequence lines.

THR-1121 retired `generateInterventionChoices`, which would have deleted that
whole move set as a side effect of removing a producer gate duty happened to
lean on. The stances survived as a local stopgap (`gateDutyStanceChoices`, at
`probabilityBoost: 0`), so the encounter stopped selling odds but kept showing
generic stance labels.

## What changed

- **Content** — three authored `StepNudge` cards on each of the three steps,
  carrying the retired stances' fiction. Prices keep the old shape (steady is
  the cheap thread, force the expensive one, withhold free) and the odds move
  through `forecastDelta`, the sanctioned nudge channel, rather than a flat
  addition to the roll.
- **Engine** — `GATE_DUTY_BRANCH_CONSEQUENCES` re-keyed from `interventionType`
  to card id, read off `action.activeNudges` at the step it resolves.
- **UI** — `gateDutyStanceChoices` and `buildChoiceIntent` deleted; the stage
  renders the nudge hand. The specialized adapter survives for what the general
  one cannot produce here: clearance-gate signals, the checkpoint cast, and the
  branch-keyed aftermath prose, all re-keyed to card ids.

`recordUnifiedActionNudgeMemory` is the one piece of new machinery, and it
exists because `activeNudges` names the *current* step's cards and is replaced
when the next step commits. Gate duty's history afterimages and aftermath echoes
ask what was played on step 0 after the encounter has moved on, so the committed
hand is also written to `choiceHistory` — the existing per-step channel, through
the same upsert every other choice writer uses.

## Two defects found on the way

- **Every priced card rendered dimmed.** The adapter never threaded `gameState`
  to `buildNudgePhaseModel`, which reads the per-sphere `essencePool` to decide
  playable-vs-dimmed. The summed `essence` the stage already had cannot answer
  that question. Caught by the jsdom render evidence returning the hand in
  affordability order instead of authored order.
- **An unrecognised branch id crashed the stage.** The keys used to be a closed
  three-value union so a lookup could not miss; card ids are open strings, and a
  save written before this change carries the retired `intervene_*` ids. All five
  branch-table reads now fall back to the unplayed prose (NFP #4).
