**THR-1097 — the consequence content pass: every vertical-slice ending rewritten as cause → change.**
THR-1082 shipped the language (four categories, the structured payload, the causality rule);
this applies it to the eight encounters that already existed. All **55 authored consequences**
now declare `category` / `stateNoun` / `direction` / `causeClause` and lead with a cause drawn
from the scene.

**The defect was subject, not writing quality.** The slice's prose was already authored to the
locked THR-883 format, so nothing here is a style fix. But roughly a third of the chips
reported **world state** where rule 3 asks for a personal outcome — "The bridge is one crossing
older", "The hut is better stocked than they found it", "Thirty went in at the gates". Each is
true; none is a success or failure *for the character*. They are now what the crossing, the
night and the column did to the person who walked through them: what they learned the river is
worth, a debt the road owes them by name, the caravan roads opening to someone thirty people
can identify.

**Palette breadth, the second Done-when.** Christian's ask was *all* the attachment types and
many different conditions, not growth-and-standing on repeat. The slice now writes **eight
consequence kinds**: conditions (`wounded`, `exhausted`, `terrified`, `inspired`), condition
*removal* (the pass's warm night actually gives the climb back), authored quintessence erosion,
hidden marks (`concealed_action`, `secret_knowledge`, `reputation_note`), spawned artifacts,
favors, standing tallies and seeds. Every one of the eight encounters carries at least one
consequence beyond growth/standing — the bar was half.

**Two are new mechanical writes, not relabelled prose.** The caravan's success-at-cost band
mints a `concealed_action` mark for moving a hunted traveler out of a watched column — the
fiction always said a face was remembered and nothing recorded it. And The Grateful Kin mints
**the pressed keepsake its own design block promised in a header comment and never built**.

**Quintessence is authored only where the engine is not already charging.**
`outcomeConsequences.ts` moves it automatically on `critical_success`, `success_at_cost` and
`critical_failure`, so the three authored shifts sit on **`failure` bands only** — the pass's
night in the open, the caravan's unbroken hunt, the family's fen-in-mind evening. Each is the
shape Christian's "loss of confidence" example names; none double-charges a beat.
`SLICE_NERVE_EROSION` (−0.02) is named and sized between the passive-regen floor and the
engine's own encounter-failure erosion (NFP #1).

**The lexicon result beats the baseline, and the baseline is the point.** Outcome-class
vagueness measured **0/0/0 after**, against a baseline carrying **5 pre-existing hits**. Seven
further hits were introduced *by this pass* and caught by diffing against a stashed baseline
rather than by trusting the rewrite — `whatever the climb took`, `the long way round`, `owing
nothing to anyone` are ordinary English and all banned in outcome prose (impediment #575). The
two surviving detector failures are **second-person on card prose** (3 and 4 hits), identical
to baseline and out of scope: the card voice is second person by design.

**Two new gates, both falsified before being trusted.** One pins the four structured fields per
change — deleting a single `category` block fails it by name. The other enforces the lexicon
**per field class**, and that is the finding: its first draft banned both lexicons on
`causeClause` and failed 8 honest lines including "the master said nothing at the gates". A
`causeClause` is the *scene beat*; `detail` is the *change*. So evasive terms are banned on
both and natural indefinites only on `detail` — THR-899's scoped model, not a flat ban.

**One substrate gap filed, not faked.** `agreement` is in the palette and the crossroads promise
literally is one, but agreements mint through `add_node` graph ops and the aftermath effect
vocabulary has no attachment-granting member. The promise rides its `encounter_seed` (the
mechanically real claim) and the chip describes the bond honestly; the missing effect is engine
work, filed as [THR-1110](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1110).
