## What shipped

- **Agent mode is a property of the block.** `fightBlock({ mode: 'agent' })` stamps `fightMode: 'agent'` on every step. The default (`'npc'`) stamps nothing, so every existing block is byte-identical. An agent-mode block against a monster still fights the card in NPC mode.
- **The opposed roll** (`src/engine/fights/opposedRoll.ts`). After the fighter's core, `resolveUncontestedStep` synthesizes the opponent's roll. The opponent is the actor, priced from the fighter's derived card through the same `resolveFightStepInputs`, and resolved by `resolveStepCore` (`agent` / `none`) on its own stream (`DUEL_OPPONENT_STREAM_SALT` 6271, keyed on action, opponent and step). It is never stored in `state.unifiedActions`. In a duel neither side's own modifiers price the other side's step.
- **The opponent side** (`applyDuelStepResult` in `fightState.ts`). Both clocks are per-fight (`FIGHT_MORTAL_CLOCK` 2). Each band advances the other side's clock, and it harms, conditions and carries momentum for its own side. Both sides' effect events fire, and the opponent's draw on a sibling stream. A `fight_clock` effect against the fighter lands in the fighter's mailbox and drains into `fighterClockNow`.
- **The matrix.** A critical failure strikes that side down, and on the nerve step routs it. It ends the fight before any clock is read. Both sides failing at once gives `struck_down` / `routed` with `opponentLoss`. The opponent's clock filling gives `overcome` / `'clock'`. The fighter's clock filling gives `struck_down`. Both clocks in one exchange is a double knockout.
- **Both sides decide.** After a wounding clash each side takes its own concession fork (`runDuelForks`), recorded with its `side`. The god's hand leans only on the fighter. An opponent who yields gives `overcome` / `'yielded'`. Both yielding gives `broke_off` / `'yielded'`. Quarter goes to the side that is behind. Temper is NPC-mode only.
- **Old Blood** (`fight.duel.grudge`). It is registered in the new `FIGHT_ENCOUNTER_TEMPLATES`, spread into `UNIFIED_ACTION_TEMPLATES` and searched by `getAnyEncounterById`, so a duel is archived as a chapter. It is spawn-only until E3.
- **Levers:** `__DEBUG.spawnDuel(a, b)`, the duel fields on `getFightState`, CLI `spawn duel <a> --with <b>`, and `npm run calibrate:duels`.

Duels resolve a little faster than THR-1264's sim.
