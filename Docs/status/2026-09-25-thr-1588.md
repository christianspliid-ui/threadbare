## What changed

- **W4 falls back to the nearest settlement** (`seedFreeholds`, `src/engine/seedLivingWorld.ts`). A qualifying protagonist whose home has no unheld commerce or authority Place holds one in the nearest settlement that has one, nearest first with id tie-breaks, within `WORLDGEN_FREEHOLD_SETTLEMENT_MAX_HEXES = 8` (new, `src/data/worldgen-living-constants.ts`). The reach gate is unchanged. It says *who* plausibly holds property, and that never drifted; *where* the protagonist lives did.
- **Test:** `seedLivingWorld.test.ts` asserts that every qualifying protagonist on a generated small world holds a freehold. With the fallback disabled, 3 of 6 hold nothing, so the test catches the defect. Determinism is still green.
- **Wiki:** `public/run-lifecycle-reference.html` documents the fallback.

## New baselines (seeds 42 · 99, medium)

- `owns` **4 · 3**. Every qualifier holds one; seed 99 has only three qualifiers.
- `hostile_to` **4 · 14**. Re-baselined: seed 99 is within ±25% of closeout, and seed 42's drop is the THR-1155 stream shift. `WORLDGEN_QUARREL_SENTIMENT_MAX` is untouched.
- `census:cells` starts **84 · 83** in 150 ticks (main before this fix: 65 · 91). This is the forecast window working as ruled; the fix's new freeholds give holders cells to start.

## Decided, veto invited

The Done-when asked for `owns ≥ 4` on both seeds. Seed 99 reads 3 because only three of its 22 protagonists lead in gold, stone or heart, and all three now hold a freehold. The only way to reach 4 is to change who qualifies (for example, adding `iron` for authority Places), and that moves a meaning to hit a number. I kept the gate. Widening it is a one-line change to `WORLDGEN_FREEHOLD_LEADING_REACHES` if the world should have more property-holders.
