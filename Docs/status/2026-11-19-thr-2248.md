- **Spend credit.** `handleHeldSteps` now records each held step's card spend per sphere through `addMandateSphereSpend`, as `GameView` does (THR-2208). Every played arm's Mandate reading was too low without it.
- **The `full` arm.** The `answer` arm plus, every tick after Stay's turn, the cheapest place card in each Mandate sphere still short of its target, when the purse can pay. `placeAttempts` / `placeRefused` count the purse's refusals; snapshots carry `placeEssP` / `placeEssS` (the fit projection's input). The proto's `--place-all` is dropped.
- **Flags.** `--arms` picks the driver's arms (default `none,greedy,answer`); `--snap-every` sets the snapshot cadence and is passed to child arms. A run that includes the full arm defaults to `AGENCY_WIN_TICKS` (900). An arm whose file was never written is left out of the compare instead of reading "not measured".
- **The predicate.** `agencyWinPredicate` / `agencyWinLine` in `scripts/agency-verdict.ts`: hands-off finishes on ≤0 seeds; the full player finishes with Doom below `DOOM_CLIMAX_START` on ≥2; chapters-only finishes early on ≤0 and ends ≥`AGENCY_MANDATE_DELTA` above hands-off on ≥2 (`AGENCY_CHAPTERS_MIN_DELTA_SEEDS`); the purse refuses ≥0.25 of the full player's place-cast attempts. Measured seeds only.
