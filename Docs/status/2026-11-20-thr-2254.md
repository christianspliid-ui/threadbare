# THR-2254 — The race names a place to tend

**Shipped 2026-11-20.** Ticket 4 of the race plan (`Docs/plans/2026-11-19-thr-2246-the-player-can-read-the-race.md`, decision 4). Now that a place cast fills the Mandate by its cost (THR-2249), a place is a real lever.

- **Engine.** `raceLever` (`src/engine/raceForecast.ts`) gains a `place` entry between the chapter and Stay: `raceLeverPlaceId` picks the god's place (a live `location` / place-tier node) with the largest `godHeld` share in the Mandate sphere furthest from its target. Mortals, artifacts, gone nodes and non-positive shares are passed over; ties break on the id.
- **Judgment call.** The place lever is offered only when the god already holds points at a place in the short sphere — a place it has tended there. With no held place, Stay stays the lever, so a fresh god is not told to tend places it may have no card for.
- **Words** (`src/data/race-content.ts`): "Tend {Place} in {Sphere} to raise your Mandate." with the place as an `EntityLink` (Law 21) and the sphere hover; an unnamed place falls back to "Tend a place in {Sphere} to raise your Mandate." (no link). The turn alert, `MandateDetail` and the Doom dialog's race block show it.
- **Evidence.** `src/engine/__tests__/racePlaceLever.thr2254.test.ts` (12 tests: pick, order, fallbacks) and a jsdom render of `MandateDetail` with the place lever in `RaceSurfaces.thr2252.test.tsx`. `npm run gate` PASS on the code track.
