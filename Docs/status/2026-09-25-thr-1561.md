# 2026-09-25 — THR-1561: chips for a duel's loser

**Ticket:** [THR-1561](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1561) · **Project:** Physical Conflict · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-23-fight-on-screen.md` § 2 (the *slain (opponent)* row; "Whose record each chip reads") with `Docs/plans/2026-09-23-mortal-duels.md` (E2 writes `opponentEnding`) · **Pillars:** UI (the chip rows) · Content (the chip and tooltip copy) · Engine N/A (the chips read what E2 already writes).

## What shipped

- **slain (opponent) has a second source.** It also renders when `fightState.opponentEnding.face === 'slain'`. It is the same row as the monster case: PATH ◆ *slain*, *{opponent} was slain.*, anchored to the deceased opponent.
- **A new BOND ▼ chip, `grudge_against_fighter`.** It renders when `opponentEnding.grudgeWritten`. The noun is the opponent (the grudge sits in their Blood section), and the sentence is *{opponent} holds a grudge against {fighter}.* It carries tooltip `fight.chip.grudge_against_fighter`. The copy lives in `FIGHT_CHIP_COPY`, `FIGHT_CHIP_TOOLTIP_IDS` and `FIGHT_TOOLTIP_COPY`, beside F3's rows.
- **Not chipped:** the loser's scar and lost standing. Those are the loser's own costs and already show on the loser's sheet.
- **Fight-chip sentences open on a capital.** A faction named "sovereignty of Open Ink" read "sovereignty of Open Ink will remember this." on the live aftermath. The fix applies to sentences only; the ◆ word stays lowercase.
- `getFightChips(actionId)` is unchanged: it renders through the same `buildUnifiedEncounterStageModel`, so it returns the new chips.
