# THR-1495 — six content kinds had no codex category; four are chartered, two are withheld by ruling

**2026-09-13 · Content · UI · Engine-N/A** · Deferral from [THR-1491](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1491) (slice 2 of [THR-1482](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1482), `Docs/plans/2026-09-12-thr-1482-one-card-one-router.md`)

## What the defect actually was

Slice 2 made the codex overlay the Tier-3 sheet for content references, and measured that six of the twelve `ContentObjectKindId`s resolve to **zero** codex entries. Those six render their content card without the "open in codex ↗" footer, because Law 25 forbids a control that does nothing.

Nothing there was broken. The defect is that **all six read as one undifferentiated silence** — a kind deliberately withheld and a kind nobody had got to looked identical to the player and to the next agent. This ticket's product is not four tabs; it is that each of the six now says which it is.

## Re-measured first, and the re-measurement was the point

The coordination block asked for it explicitly (THR-688 rule A: a ticket states a predicate, never a count). Probing every catalog id against `getAllCodexEntries()` on 2026-09-13:

| | 2026-09-12 | 2026-09-13 |
|---|---|---|
| `encounter_template` | 557 | **513** |
| `action_template` | 239 | **187** |

**Every count in the description's table had moved in one day. The predicate — "a kind whose catalog ids resolve to zero codex entries" — picked out exactly the same six.** That is the rule demonstrated rather than cited, and it is why the doc table now carries a date and points at `contentKindCodexCoverage()` as the live reading.

## The six rulings

**Chartered (4)**

- **Legendary artifacts** (3) → `possessions`, under a new `legendary` rail group. The plain gap the ticket predicted: the category existed and never read `ARTIFACT_TEMPLATES`. Three entries are a group, not a tab.
- **Companions** (9) → their own category, **deliberately not** a shelf under Possessions. A companion is a person who walks with a mortal; filing one among things-you-own would be the game saying something it does not mean.
- **Ambitions** (20) → their own category. The deciding argument was not in the ticket: **Undertakings — the *work* — have been catalogued since THR-1434**, and an ambition is the *want* that work serves. Cataloguing one half of a mortal's motives and withholding the other left the vocabulary readable in one half and dark in the other.
- **Cards** (37) → their own category, "The Repertoire". The deck the player plays from. All 37 members are dealable today (`dealableMembers()`, measured), so the tab holds no card a god can never hold — pinned by a test that names the offenders if that stops being true.

**Withheld (2)** — recorded in the row's `note`, quotable, which is the whole point of the field

- **Encounters** (513) — a catalog of every encounter is the answer key. A chapter a mortal meets has to be *met*. The note explicitly records what is **not** being refused: a chronicle of encounters already met is a good feature and a different one.
- **Omens** (44) — an omen track is the run of signs shown before something breaks, read through its beats by design. A catalog converts dread into a lookup table.

## The bug the design pass caught on the way

The first draft of the companion and legendary mappers imported `formatDomainContributions` from `codexRegistry`. Two things were wrong with that, and the second is the interesting one:

1. It is documented *"Exported for direct testing (THR-1113), **not for use outside this module**"*, and importing it created a real import cycle.
2. **It would have been wrong on the merits.** That ladder reads 0–1 sigmoid weights (`commanding` at `0.50`). Companion and legendary contributions are raw sub-agent points — measured `1 ×8, 2 ×7, 3 ×2` and `1 ×3, 1.5 ×3` — so **all 20 values would have banded as "commanding"**, and on the sibling reach-bonus ladder all 20 as "faint". That is precisely the failure `CAPABILITY_CONTRIBUTION_BANDS` documents at its own definition: *a ladder whose entire corpus collapses onto one rung is pinned by nothing.*

So `BESIDE_YOU_CONTRIBUTION_BANDS` is a third ladder with its scale anchor recorded (`COMPANION_CONTRIBUTION_RANGE` is `{1,3}`; agent-scale is 4–16), built from the shared `magnitudeWord` primitive in `engine/aftermathWords.ts` — which also removed the cycle. A test asserts the corpus spreads across more than one rung **and** that the top rung is reachable by real content, so the constant cannot become decoration.

## Anti-rot: the claim is now measured, not asserted

The failure this ticket is a deferral *from* was a table in a comment going stale. Two new contract tests in `surfaceRegistry.test.ts` close that:

- **`claims a codex sheet exactly where the codex actually holds the kind`** — each row's `sheet` against live coverage. Deliberately `inCodex > 0`, not `inCodex === ids`: four covered kinds are covered *partially* (60 of 116 undertakings), and the row is a claim about the *kind* while `codexHasEntry` is the per-entry check the CTA makes.
- **`withholds exactly the two kinds THR-1495 ruled on`** — names the answer rather than deriving it, so a kind *silently losing* its category cannot satisfy the first test by flipping both sides together. The two are only independent because of this.
