# 2026-10-14 — THR-1811: god cards carry their progression as data, and the world offers them

**Ticket:** [THR-1811](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1811). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-13-thr-1811-god-card-progression-and-offerings.md`. Decided on THR-1794 (the ladder) and THR-1770 (Christian's option B). Engine + Content + UI.

- **What the player sees.** About once a day a pill reads "Three powers stir". It opens the beat modal with three card faces, each with a price phrase and a button: *Paid from Spirit*, *From Life, at double* or *Beyond you yet* (button disabled, "Not yet"). *Let them pass* closes it; an offering not entered passes at the next day boundary at least 6 ticks away, with no chronicle line. Taking one up writes "You took up {card}." As attunement grows, rungs open and give key cards one at a time ("Your hand is felt", "A further gift"); the secondary reach signature is rung 2's first key.
- **How.** `CardProgression` on `UnifiedActionTemplate`; 143 blocks copied from the THR-1794 TSV (ids matched the live catalog exactly). `src/engine/godProgression.ts` is pure: rung marks, offerable cards, prices, payment, draw, expiry, readout. Rungs and key gifts run in `phaseAscendantProgression` (`stepGodProgression`); the offering joins the Director's cadence draw (weight 8) and resolves in `resolvePendingBeat`. Spending goes to `offeringSpentBySphere`; the earned counter is never written. The reach gate is one predicate, `isWithinGodReaches`, read by the drawer and the offering. The 13 grant-bearing pool beats moved to `RETIRED_POOL_BEATS`, resolvable by id.
- **Gate and model.** `npm run check:god-card-progression` (in `scripts/gate.ts`): 143 cards, 93 market / 50 gift / 32 generic, tiers 44 / 60 / 28 / 11; warns on 5 spine-tagged cards no spine beat grants and on sphere × tier holes. `npm run model:god-cards` runs the ported THR-1794 model off the templates, with `--sphere-gate` for `OFFERING_IN_SPHERE_ONLY`.
- **Executor decisions.** (1) Rung-2 key order follows the table / catalog order the plan's Decided 7 names (Bestow, Shift Dominion, Attune Thread, Strengthen Thread); the Done-when's list swapped the last two. (2) No `beat.offering` content template: the offering's words live in `god-progression-content.ts` and the beat has no `templateId`, so the pool-template drift test keeps meaning "one template per pool beat". (3) `accruePlayerReachPractice` moved to `reachPractice.ts` (re-exported): the resolver importing the phase closed a module cycle once the phase read the template catalog. (4) The modal eyebrow reads "The World Offers" so the kicker and the title do not repeat. (5) Two THR-1771 tests that measured the retired investment beats were removed; the offering's identity weighting is tested instead.
