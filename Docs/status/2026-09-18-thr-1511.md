# THR-1511 — the wake is offered where the work stands, not where the actor does

*Found by the [THR-1510](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1510) investigation, which
falsified "the seed is dropped" and measured instead that on seed 42 / medium / 200 ticks
**8 of 14 catalyst seeds withered on a family that had eligible members**. An undertaking
completes wherever its actor happens to stand — a route laid from a fort, an army raised in the
field — while every family in `UNDERTAKING_CELL_CATALYSTS` is gated to settlements. The
grooming block resolved the fork to option 1 (plant the wake where the object is) and left the
how to the executor.*

## What shipped

**The seed knows where the work is.** `PendingEncounterSeed.resolutionLocationId`
(`src/types/unifiedAction.ts`) names the Location a query-resolved seed's `locationSubtypes`
gate is judged against *first*. `maybeSeedCatalyst` (`src/engine/strategicActionLifecycle.ts`)
now takes the completion's own ops and stamps it from `catalystAnchorLocationId`
(`src/engine/undertakingCatalystAnchor.ts`, new): created nodes first, then the object the
cell acted on, then the target — a route resolves to the settlement end it reaches (far end
first: "the town your road now reaches"), a Place to its parent Location, a group to where it
stands, a masterwork to nothing. `resolveSeedByQuery` (`src/engine/encounterSeeding.ts`)
judges the gate at that anchor first and the actor's feet second, with one `rng()` draw
whichever place the eligible set came from (NFP #3). A dangling anchor, or one the family
rejects, costs nothing over the old path; an aftermath-planted seed carries no anchor and
takes exactly the pre-change path (NFP #6).

**The trace says where it looked.** `content.query_resolved` / `content.query_empty` now carry
`judgedAtLocationId` and `judgedAt` (`resolution_anchor` / `target_location`), so a wither
reads as "no place accepted it" rather than "the actor stood in the wrong place" (NFP #2).

**One content hole closed on the way.** `#craft_commission`'s only member,
`crafting.quest.flawed_steel`, listed `settlement` (a subtype no location carries) and omitted
`capital`; three of the seven residual withers on the first post-fix sweep were masterworks
made in a capital. `capital` is added, and `undertakingCellCatalysts.test.ts` now asserts every
family has a member for each of town, city and capital.

## Measured

- **Seed-99 `cell.create.route` repro** (the ticket's own): before, `catalyst_seeded: fail —
  withered where the actor stood: loc_68 (fort)`; after, `catalyst_seeded: pass —
  #consortium_errand resolved at undertaking_catalyst → mct.quest.caravan_escort` after 3
  settle ticks.
- **Seed 42 / medium / 200 ticks, engine fix only (same world trajectory as the baseline):**
  14 planted, 7 spawned (was 6), all 7 anchored; 7 withered, of which 3 were the craft/capital
  content hole and 4 had **both** anchor and feet at a hamlet or in wilderness.
- **With the craft fix** the pool at capitals widens and the trajectory diverges, so the seed-42
  counts are no longer comparable (9 planted / 3 spawned / 6 withered). Across seeds 7, 42 and
  99 after both fixes, **every remaining wither had both the anchor and the feet at a hamlet,
  a shrine or in wilderness** — zero withers with a town, city or capital on either side. That
  residue is [THR-1515](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1515) (Deferral): no catalyst
  family has a member accepting `hamlet`, and `cell.create.location` *founds* a hamlet, so the
  thing the work made is rejected by construction — the option-2 follow-up the grooming block
  sanctioned.

Sweep read off `state.tickEvents` per tick (the orchestrator resets it every tick and the
2000-entry trace ring evicts mid-tick), never off the ring — the THR-1510 lesson.

## Observed, not fixed

18 templates list `'settlement'` in `locationSubtypes`; no location producer writes that
value (three test fixtures do). It gates nothing and narrows nothing, but it reads as a real
subtype to an author. Noted on THR-1515.
