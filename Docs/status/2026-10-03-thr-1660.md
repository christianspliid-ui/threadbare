### 2026-10-03 — THR-1660: the faith can move — consecrate a pilgrim way

**Until now, faith in this world was set on the first tick and never moved.** Each congregation got one pilgrim route to its capital at worldgen (THR-1632), and nothing could ever add another. A mortal who wants to spread the faith now has one long work that changes the map: *Consecrate a pilgrim way*.
- **Engine.** `pilgrim_way` is a class of the Route kind in the undertaking registry (`classOf: 'route'`, on the MONSTER precedent), with one verb, `create`. It writes `sacred_route` **from the congregation of the site's own culture** to a town, city or capital, never from the mortal: 0 of 86 faith holders belong to any congregation, so a membership rule would be a dead cell. The write goes through `createRelationEdge` with `origin: 'undertaking'` and the `projectId`. The board refuses by name through the THR-1617 create hook, and the verb re-checks at completion: `consecrator_gone`, `site_gone`, `no_congregation_here`, `already_a_pilgrim_destination`. Shrines and temples are never sites. The existing encounter-cache reader then pools `encounter.pilgrimage_trial` at the town, and the cell arm's pool invalidation makes it live the same tick. A new bounded `UNDERTAKING_CELL_REACH` table gives the cell its Star/Heart lean. The legacy template and its three tests are untouched. There is no unmaking verb.
- **Content.** Phrase, deed word (*Consecrated the way to Brindle* — `{object}` reads *the way to <town>*), a GM-narration line set, the `#temple_errand` catalyst, a grid class-cell note retiring `establish_sacred_route`. `ambition_spread_faith` walks the cell.
- **UI.** `Game/PilgrimWayLine`: the Location sheet's Allegiance section reads *Pilgrims come here — a way of [congregation]*, and the congregation's Faction sheet reads *Pilgrim ways to [town], …*. Every name carries its image and its link, and the concept word has a tooltip (`ui.pilgrim_way`). The lines cover the seeded ways too, which had reached no player surface since THR-1632. `await window.__DEBUG.getPilgrimWays()` reads the same selector.
