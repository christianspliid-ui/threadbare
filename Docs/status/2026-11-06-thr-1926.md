# THR-1926 — The Crossroads, engine and content (slice A)

**Shipped 2026-11-06.** Dominion — Player Power Progression. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1926-the-crossroads.md`. The page row, torn badge and switching God's Will off are slice B, [THR-1928](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1928).

1. **A threaded mortal's next roads are read off the deciding board.** At each full decision `phaseAgentDecision` writes `crossroads` on every mortal the god threads: the board's entries in order, minus the road just taken and secret cells, cut at `CROSSROADS_OPTION_COUNT` (3). Encounters and undertakings alike. Pure module `src/engine/crossroads.ts` (snapshot, settledness set/leaning/torn, `forecastLean`, `leanBaseCost`, `resolveLeanOutcome`, fail-soft readers).
2. **The god can lean on one road.** `src/engine/crossroadsActions.ts` `placeCrossroadsLean` refuses (writing and spending nothing) when unthreaded, unreadable (`canReadIntention`), no reading, stale road, already held, away on a non-leader, or unaffordable; it prices by rank at the mortal's Dominion band and writes the lean without spending (the caller spends via `withEssenceSpend(…, 'crossroads')`). `liftCrossroadsLean` clears it, no refund.
3. **The lean is one more board multiplier.** `scoreUnifiedBoard` takes `crossroadsLean` and multiplies the exact road (family + template + target) ×2 toward / ×0.5 away, on `score` only, recorded as `BoardEntry.crossroadsLean` and on the board trace. An encounter target ranked outside the top five is pulled up (`findHeldEncounter`, now shared with the Compulsion).
4. **It resolves once, aloud.** After the live branch and before the Compulsion Override, `crossroadsDecision.ts` resolves followed / resisted / lapsed, emits `crossroads_lean_outcome`, pushes one toast with the mortal's answer by bond reception (`src/data/crossroads-content.ts`), clears both handles, and sets `leanTurned` so the engagement gauge counts a turned choice as not free.
5. **God's Will unchanged in this slice.** `COMPULSION_PREMONITION_ENABLED = true` gates the emitter (the debug force still bypasses it); THR-1928 flips it.
6. **Wiring:** four traces in `TraceEntry`; essence cause `'crossroads'`; six `ui.crossroads.*` tooltips; `window.__DEBUG.getCrossroads` / `leanCrossroads`; contracts `god-leans-mortal-crossroads` and `decision-board-crossroads-snapshot`; rulebook §4 engine `[IMPL]`.
