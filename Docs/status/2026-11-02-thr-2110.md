# THR-2110 — Flawed Steel and the Mason-Lord rewritten to Prose Doctrine v2 (slice A)

**Shipped 2026-11-02.** Encounter Experience. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-11-02-thr-2110-novel-prose-story-beats.md` § Slice A.

**What changed.** Every player-facing string in `crafting.quest.flawed_steel` and `stone.permanence.mason_lord_wall` was written again from scratch to Prose Doctrine v2: step 0 is the three-paragraph opening skeleton with real names (`{cast:…}` in Flawed Steel; `{actor}` and `{location}` in the Mason-Lord, both resolved by `proseEnrichment.ts`), each step-1 variant says in one clause what the god did and then what the mortal does, and each result line says what happened. No line has the god notice, feel or see. Flawed Steel fell from 3,576 to about 1,800 prose words, and the Mason-Lord from 2,282 to about 1,100. The Mason-Lord gained decision 5's five result lines.

**Budgets (reader over `authoredProse` plus choice and reaction intents).** Opening 73/80 for both. Variants 46–53/60. Overviews 38–52/60. Intents 12–22/25. No budget was raised.

**`check:encounter` by id.** Flawed Steel: `[register]` x2 lines (not-X-but-Y x2, second person x2) → 0, intensifiers 2 → 0. Mason-Lord: `[page]` x2 → 0, intensifiers 5 → 0. Structural lines (decision 9) unchanged: Flawed Steel 26 aftermath + 1 hand + 1 setting; Mason-Lord 13 aftermath + 1 cast + 1 hand + 1 setting, plus the three pre-existing `stone.circle_censure` seed warnings.

**Mechanics unchanged.** A snapshot of both templates with every prose string masked is identical before and after, except for the five new afterimage keys (and the fallback's spread copy of one of them).

**Not done: the Mason-Lord did not join `FIRED_TEMPLATE_COMPLETION`.** With it added, `firedTemplateCompletion.test.ts` fails clause 2 (step 1 offers the god no verb) and clause 3 (no authored losing-band aftermath). Both are structural, so they belong to decision 9 and not to a text slice. The plan made joining conditional, so the append was reverted.

**Cold read.** Round 1 (a cold critic given only the doctrine, the Swollen Ford exemplar and the audit's P1–P4) returned 19 findings. Most were figurative reaction labels, narrator-verdict lines, two variant narratives that told the result before the roll, and a role noun that read as a second person. All 19 were fixed in one rewrite round. Round 2, a fresh cold critic, found **no P1, P2 or P4 residue** and no doctrine-rule violation. It found 7 same-path contradictions. One was a two-readings "she" (P3). Most came from losing bands that fall back to the base ending's chips and overview (the decision-9 structural gap). Each was fixed by making the line true on every band that shows it. The same critic then checked the fixes (one new contradiction found and fixed) and returned **PASS: zero open, zero new**. Under the kill criterion this is one rewrite round plus targeted consistency fixes, not a second rewrite of the register.
