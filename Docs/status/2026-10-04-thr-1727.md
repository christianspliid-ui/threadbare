### 2026-10-04 — THR-1727: one sentence says what an encounter is about

**What changed.** The top of the encounter veil now carries one **stakes line**, `[lead], [actor] must [goal] — or [risk].`, in the subtitle slot under the title. On The Unsafe Bridge it reads *"Passing through Sacred Grove, Vara must cross the rotten toll bridge — or go into the river with the pack."* It replaces both the hand-written `template.description` summary and the THR-972 motive intro line. When the encounter resolves, the same parts become the **result line**, chosen by outcome band (*"Vara turned back to the long ford and lost the day."*, *"Vara went into the river with the pack."*). That result line names the chapter in the Chapter Ledger, the encounter badge tooltip and a new line on the agent's thread row.

**How.** `src/engine/encounters/stakesLine.ts` builds both lines with no rng (the lead variant is a hash of the action id). Phase 2b.1 `stampStakesContexts` freezes why the mortal is here (motive source, errand, place) onto the action on the tick path, so the opening and result lines share one lead. Readers fall back to a live read for an action that was never stamped. `ChapterRecord.stakesLine` is built at archive time. The lead and result tables, length caps and forbidden words are constants in `nudge-stage-content.ts`. Fork arms resolve by `variants` key, so a mortal who struck the crossroads bargain is never credited with refusing it.

**Content.** `stakes` is authored on the five slice parents (bridge, pass, caravan, crossroads, family). The crossroads and the family author `arms.negative` endings. Both raw-entry converters pass `stakes` through, so THR-1728's migration cannot be dropped by the allowlist. The validator runs in report mode: it fails malformed blocks and lists 320 of 325 encounter templates still without stakes.

**Evidence.** 29 unit tests (`stakesLine.test.ts`) plus 5 in the validator. `npm test` 1458 files / 22963 tests green, `test:heavy` 43/43, ratchet 2799 unchanged, `vite build` ✓. CLI seed 42 medium, tick 30: 105 of 105 actions stamped. Browser at 1920×1080 on the bridge and caravan spawn routes: the built line equals the rendered line, and no motive line is drawn. With `&outcome=failure` and `&outcome=critical_failure` (pin `band_rendered`), the ledger row and the thread row show the ford and the river respectively, each matching the band prose on screen.

**Noticed.** A `?spawn=` debug action that resolves inside its first tick is never stamped (it resolves before phase 2b.1 runs); its lines come from the live read, which gives the same lead on the routes checked. The motive explainer lost its entry point with the intro line (`onOpenMotive` is kept but unread).
