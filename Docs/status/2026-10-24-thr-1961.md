# THR-1961 — A realm may court once every calendar day

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** Deferred from the crown (THR-1932). The plan's predicate is "a court-eligible realm executes `seek_fealty` on 70–100% of days". The shipped rolling 12-tick cooldown capped that at 75% and measured 68–73%. A calendar-day window was tried in THR-1932 and measured ~50% for a reason nobody had traced.

**The cause.** It was the measure, not the rule. `measure:crown` checked, at each day's first tick (12d), whether the realm courted in the 12 ticks ending there. That window holds the previous day's ticks plus the first tick of the new day. Faction sweeps run every 8 ticks, so every 24 ticks hold sweeps at 24k and 24k+8 (day 2k) and 24k+16 (day 2k+1). Under the calendar window, a realm that courts at 24k is closed at 24k+8, and the checkpoint at 24k+12 sees only that closed sweep. That is a miss on half the checkpoints, whatever the weight, which is why weight 6 and weight 16 both read ~50%. The trace (`npm run measure:crown -- --seeds 42 --ticks 240 --trace`) shows the pattern sweep by sweep: tick 24 courts, tick 32 "window closed", tick 40 courts again.

**What changed.**
- **Engine.** `seek_fealty` has `cooldownTicks` 0. The `can_court` prerequisite also requires `seekFealtyWindowOpen(history, tick)`: no courtship yet in the day `floor(tick / SEEK_FEALTY_WINDOW_TICKS)`. `SEEK_FEALTY_WINDOW_TICKS` (12) replaces `SEEK_FEALTY_COOLDOWN_TICKS`.
- **Measure.** `measure:crown` counts courtship by calendar day and gains `--trace` / `--trace-ticks`.
- **Docs.** The crown plan doc's template line and constant row, rulebook §crown, and the factions wiki page.

**Evidence (1080 ticks, medium, courted / eligible days per realm).**
- Old rule, calendar count, seed 42: 54/90, 64/90, 31/42 (60–74%).
- New rule: seed 42 76/90, 84/90, 36/38; seed 99 26/28, 85/90, 80/90; seed 7 57/64, 81/90, 86/90. **84–97% on every realm.**
- Crown in days 15–45, force breaks first, 61–77% independent, one layer, no negative wealth: all pass on all three seeds.
- **Not met, filed as [THR-2017](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2017):** with more courtship, a younger second Crown forms on seed 42 and reads 11% oath share at day 60, and seed 7's Crown reads 36%. The band is 15–35%.
