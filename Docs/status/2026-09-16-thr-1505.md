# THR-1505 — the 24 endings that told one fact twice are re-authored

*Deferral from [THR-1474](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1474), which shipped
the whole-page read and named the findings it raised as [THR-1473](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1473)'s
to fix — a ticket that had already closed. This is the drain that ticket pointed at.*

## The predicate

Any `(band, block-pair)` that `npm run check:encounter -- --all` reports under a `[page]`
warning: two blocks on one assembled ending sharing a run of
`CHIP_OVERVIEW_OVERLAP_RUN_WORDS` (4) words. At pickup the live set held the same 24
findings the ticket was filed with, in the same four shapes — 14 overview ↔ reaction,
4 chip ↔ reaction, 6 reaction ↔ reaction (one authored pair in `toll_of_blades`,
repeated across every band its `fallback` reactions are offered on), 0 chip ↔ chip.

## What shipped

Content only: 22 edits across 10 encounter files, no schema, no effect, no id changed.
The rule applied is spec § Consequence Draw 1c — a reaction's job is the stance the
player takes, so the usual fix was **cutting the recap clause**, not rewording it.

- **Overview ↔ reaction (14).** Each reaction that read the overview back now says what
  the god does about it instead. `slice.caravan.walk_in_empty` no longer re-narrates the
  riders coming off the ridge; `apotheosis.unmade.let_them_be` no longer repeats *"at
  their own table by morning"*; `apotheosis.withdrawal.listen` (one block, two bands)
  turns from *"they prayed … and listened afterward"* into the god's choice not to answer
  the listening. Two findings were the **label**, not the intent: `Carry it to the next
  fork` was the overview's own last clause, and `Let them sit down once the cart is gone`
  quoted the tithe overview's first sentence — both labels re-authored, ids kept.
- **Chip ↔ reaction (4).** The reaction offered back what the chip already carried
  (*"the lid came off at the top of the stair"*, *"the records are gone"*, *"they stood in
  front of"*). One was an artefact of the page assembly: `rite.carry_them_out`'s label
  *"Let them be carried out"* joined to its intent *"The reader …"* formed `be carried out
  the`, matching the chip's `had to be carried out — The story …`. The page reads a label
  and its intent as one block, so the label is what changed.
- **Reaction ↔ reaction (6 = 1 pair).** `toll.let_them_rest` and
  `toll.let_the_story_travel` shared the frame *"The god spends the moment on … matters
  more than the …"*. They carry different effects (a quintessence recovery plus a
  conditional exhaustion lift, and a mercenary-company standing gain), so they stay two
  cards — but now phrased as two stances: sit them down versus let the telling go north.
  The success-band overlap on *"at the next halt"* fell out with the same rewrite.

A second pass after **reading the assembled pages** (the ticket's *"read the page, not
the finding"*) tightened four label ↔ intent echoes the n-gram cannot see — `Let them
open it here / Open it in front of…`, `Let them finish it / Do not stop it…`, and two
on the slice — since a label restating its own intent is the same defect one line lower.
