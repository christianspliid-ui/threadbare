# THR-1476 — prose may not bind the mortal to a place and a time the engine does not enforce

**Date:** 2026-09-12 · **Pillars:** Content · Content doctrine · Engine N/A · UI N/A
**Issue:** [THR-1476](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1476)

## What the director saw

Reviewing A Bargain at the Crossroads, Christian read the stranger's offer — *"collect it
here at the next full moon"* — and asked the structural question rather than the prose one:

> creating an encounter that requires the agent to be back at a certain place at a certain
> time … requires an encounter type that makes the agent in the game go back to the same
> place. i dont think we have functionality for that in the game, so we should refrain from
> building encounters with prose that sets constraints … if it can't translate this
> constraint to game behavior.

He is right, and the gap is structural. **`encounter_seed` has no spatial field.** It carries
`delayTicks`, `targetAgentId` and `inheritContext`; `encounterSeeding` ripens it by tick and
fires it on the agent **wherever they stand** — its only location read is the subtype filter
on *family* seeds, and a `templateId` seed skips the eligibility filter besides. No
appointment, rendezvous or return-to drive exists anywhere in the engine; movement comes from
ambition targets, strategic winners and idle drift only.

So the chain promised a place, delivered a tick, and then **congratulated the mortal for
keeping an appointment nobody had made them keep** — the sequel's gift chip read *"They kept
the night they promised"* under an opening that admits *"{name} is on the open road near
{location} when the moon rises full."* The doctrine had the fact and missed the implication:
the spec's own Seeded Sequel row describes the shape as firing *"later, elsewhere."*

## The rule — prose rule 7b

**Prose may not set a constraint on future world behaviour that no effect enacts.**

Rule 7's forward-looking twin, and the pairing is the whole idea: rule 7 governs the past
tense (do not assert state the world never **wrote**); 7b governs the future tense (do not
promise behaviour the world will never **perform**). If a scene tells the mortal to be
somewhere, do something, or refrain from something later, name the effect that makes it true
— a seed, a condition, an agreement with a term, a drive — or say what the engine will
actually do.

Landed on three surfaces, each chosen for who reads it:

- **[`nudge-authoring-spec.md`](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/.claude/skills/encounter-pipeline/reference/nudge-authoring-spec.md)** — rule 7b beside rule 7, with the trap spelled out (no spatial field, no drive, `{location}` enriches to the agent's *current* place) and the truthful shapes that cost nothing; the critic's design-conformance question gains its arm; the Seeded Sequel row now says *"later, elsewhere" is load-bearing*.
- **[`SKILL.md`](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/.claude/skills/encounter-pipeline/SKILL.md)** — REVISE trigger **34**, sited beside trigger 31 (its past-tense twin) so a reviewer meets the pair together.
- **[`systems-prompt.md`](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/.claude/skills/encounter-pipeline/agents/systems-prompt.md)** — the Aftermath Supportability question gains *"does every later-tense promise in the prose name the effect that performs it?"* **That agent is the one holding the effects list**, which is why the check belongs to it and not to the prose critic: deciding whether a sentence is enacted requires reading the effects, and only this pass does.

Each surface tells the author the same thing about scope: until the appointment primitive
ships ([THR-1479](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1479), which Christian has agreed
to), **a scene that needs a placed, timed promise is a scene we do not author.**

## The rewrite — truthful by removal

The stranger's own line was already the answer and was sitting in the accept step unused:
*"The moon will find you," he says. "It is better at that than I am."* Everything else was
rewritten toward it.

| Where | Was | Is |
|---|---|---|
| Crossroads opening | *"asks in payment only a promise: collect it here at the next full moon"* | *"asks in payment only a promise. He will bring it at the full moon and find them himself."* |
| Tally the Days, `critical_success` | *"the gift, the moon, the walk back, the standing still"* | *"the gift, the moon, the debt, the man who comes for it"* |
| Accept reaction intent | *"The road goes on, with an appointment at the end of it."* | *"The road goes on. At the next full moon, he finds them on it."* |
| Accept `success_at_cost` | *"Every evening … has the appointment in it."* | *"Every evening … has the promise sitting in it."* |
| Refuse-path seed label | *"The road bends back toward a dead tree and an offer left unanswered."* | *"An offer left unanswered has a way of finding the road again."* |
| Full Moon step | *"is there before the light finishes arriving"* / `purposeLine: 'Keep the appointment'` | *"finds the road they are on before the light finishes arriving"* / `'Stand the exchange'` |
| Full Moon gift chip | *"They kept the night they promised"* | *"The moon came full and he found them"* |
| Full Moon `success_at_cost` + its seed | *"a second appointment … he keeps his appointments"* | *"a second visit … he is good at finding people"* |
| Full Moon `narrativeTemplates.failure` | *"The appointment was kept…"* | *"The exchange was made…"* |

**Nothing mechanical moved.** The `attachment_grant` of `agreement.bargain.promise_given`
with its `durationOverride`, and the `encounter_seed` at `SLICE_FULL_MOON_DELAY_TICKS`, are
untouched — the prose stopped over-claiming what they do. The opening was then trimmed to 79
words: the first truthful draft ran 87 against an 80-word budget, and `check:encounter`
caught it.

## The corpus sweep

**Predicate:** any opening, band prose, chip sentence (`causeClause` / `detail`), reaction
`intent` or `seedLabel` that names a place and a time the mortal must meet, or an action they
must take or avoid later, with no effect on that path that enacts it.

Swept `src/data/` for return/obligation constructions (`come back`, `return to`, `be here at`,
`by dawn/dusk/nightfall`, `within a week`, `has until`, `must be/come/return`, `promised to
return`, `rendezvous`, `appointment`). Dispositions:

| Hit | Disposition |
|---|---|
| The Crossroads bargain + The Full Moon Collection, 9 sites | **Rewritten** (table above) |
| The Crossroads refuse-path re-seed label | **Rewritten** — the seed re-offers the bargain, it does not bend the road back |
| The Beast in the Granary, `critical_success` — *"There will be a feast in {location} before the week is out, and {name} will be asked to stay for it"* | **Left — an effect enacts it.** `condition_attachment` of `trait.condition.location.festival` on `$target`, and the invitation is the `membership_change` its own reaction writes |
| **The Table That Holds** — *"{name} returns to {location}, the town that keeps a door open for them"* | **Filed as [THR-1493](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1493).** A real hit; the fix is a scene redesign, not a prose edit — see below |
| The Swindler Found — *"be there when he takes it"* | **Not a hit.** Within-scene framing of the step being resolved now, not a later-tense promise |
| ~30 `by morning` / `by dawn` matches across the corpus | **Not hits.** Afterimage prose narrating a scene's own resolution forward, which the resolution performs |

### The one hit not fixed here, and why

The Table That Holds asserts a **return journey** and **standing with the named town**. Three
facts say it cannot: `{location}` enriches to `ctx.currentLocationName` — the agent's *current*
place, never the seed's inherited `targetId`; the seed is spatially blind like every other; and
the template's own `requiredReputationWith` gate is bypassed on the seeded path. So 12 ticks
after The Grateful Kin the scene lands wherever the traveler drifted and calls it the town that
keeps a door open for them.

The honest prose rewrite would delete the premise [THR-1182](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1182)
built the encounter around, which is a design change and not a truthfulness edit — and it is
precisely the case THR-1479 fixes properly, by making *"returns to"* **true** rather than
forcing a rewrite THR-1479 then reverts. Filed with both candidate fixes rather than
half-solved. **This is the one piece of the ticket's Content scope not landed in this PR, and
it is deliberate.**

**What this gate is, stated in the test itself:** a regression pin on the specific sentences

over the corpus, where *appointment* can be an honest word.
