## What shipped

- **The roll reads standing modifiers.** `resolveUncontestedStep` (via `deriveStepRollInputs`) adds `computeStandingModifierTotal` (`resolutionModifiers.ts`) to its modifier sum: items and their `passive`/`conditional` effects, traits, terrain, the place's conditions, sphere alignment, divine attention, auras, altered rules. Read for whoever rolls (the company's acting member when one answered), on the step's authored reach, with live effect states. Fight steps are skipped: their `standing` named term already carries the read in a combat context with the reach swap, so every step is read exactly once. `UNIFIED_ROLL_READS_STANDING_MODIFIERS` (`src/data/standing-modifier-constants.ts`) is the one-flag revert.
- **The forecast carries the step's scale (addendum).** `buildNudgePhaseModel` always sets `forecastScale` (`template.scale ?? 'regional'` for ordinary steps), so a `local` step shows the 0.65 floor it rolls at, and the card deltas are summed before the floor. It also passes live effect states, as the roll does.
- **The planner plans with the same total (THR-1579 follow-up).** `createStandingModifierReader` (`plannerForecast.ts`) memoises one total per (reach, sphere) per decision pass. `scoreAndSelect` builds one and passes it to `estimateCompletionProb` and `forecastEncounterExpectedUtility`, including push and resist.
- **Trace.** `resolution.input` gains `standingModifiers` (absent when 0).
- **The fight-only branch stays.** The ticket asked to delete it once the road-wide read landed. It differs in two ways that matter (combat predicate context; reach swap applied first), so it stays as the fight step's read and the ordinary read skips fight steps. Recorded in `fightStepInputs.ts`'s header.
