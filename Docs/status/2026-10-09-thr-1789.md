# 2026-10-09 — THR-1789: An ending shows one reputation row per mortal, named

**Ticket:** [THR-1789](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1789) · **Found by:** warm playtest round 1. 3/3 testers read "six identical 'Bond · World Standing' rows — bonds with whom?", and asked "▲ / ▲▲ / ▲▲▲ … on what scale?"

- **One row per mortal, carrying the net.** The authored, branch and residual reputation producers in `unifiedActionResolution.ts` no longer push a row each. Their sum folds into one change per actor under the stable id `<actionId>:reputation:<actorId>`. A later step supersedes the earlier row, as capability growth does (THR-1467). Movements that cancel out leave no row. The pure helpers are in the new `src/engine/reputationAftermathFold.ts`.
- **The residual no longer double-counts.** `stepReputationParts` reports the residual as total − authored − branch. It used to report the total, so the same movement showed twice.
- **The sheet's word, with the mortal's name.** The noun is "<Name>'s reputation", and it links to that mortal (portrait tile, opens the sheet). The tooltip is the new `ui.reputation`, which carries `ui.standing`'s copy under the sheet heading "Reputation". Faction standing is untouched.
- **The legend teaches ▲.** The first-contact consequence legend adds "▲ rose · ▼ fell" and "▲ slight · ▲▲ clear · ▲▲▲ great". The size words are derived from `DELTA_CLUSTER_WORDS` through `deltaClusterLegendWord` (one source). The legend keeps the same dismissal key.
- **Evidence.**
  - Unit tests: `reputationChangeFold.test.ts` runs a real 3-step resolution and checks it ends with one row carrying the sum under a stable id. It also checks the residual split (0.05/0.02/0.10 → residual 0.03), the cancel-out case and that rows for different actors stay separate.
  - Adapter test: the noun names Kael and "reputation", and links to him.
  - Render test: the legend shows the three words.
  - Browser at 1920×1080 on `?view=game&seeded&size=medium&warm=120`: the ending shows a single "KAEL THORNWEAVER'S REPUTATION" row and the ▲ legend.
