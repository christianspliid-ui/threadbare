# THR-1503 — the dead encounter-condition route is deleted, not revived

**Shipped 2026-09-19.** `src/engine/phaseEncounterTraits.ts` is gone. Its two
minting functions — `processEncounterConditions` (wounded / inspired / terrified
from an encounter outcome) and `processEncounterMastery` (a per-reach success
counter that minted mastery traits) — never granted anything in a live world, for
two independent reasons, and the fork the ticket raised was settled as **option 2:
fold into aftermath**.

## Why it was dead

1. **The gate matched nothing.** Both functions read the encounter template's
   `category` through a cast (`(template as Record<string, unknown>).category`);
   `UnifiedActionTemplate` declares no such field, and no shipped template in
   `src/data/` carries any of the eight values the tables gated on.
2. **The caller never ran.** The only call site was the orchestrator's loop over
   the legacy `state.encounterProgress` collection, which no production path
   populates — pinned by THR-1069 and `legacyEncounterBranchUnreachable.test.ts`.

## The decision (recorded on the ticket by the grooming lane, veto invited)

Conditions are consequences authored on the aftermath path (`apply_condition`)
and drawn from the reward pool. Those are the live writers. Reviving the route
would have meant a new declared `category` field backfilled across the corpus
plus a second, table-driven condition writer beside the authored one — the exact
shape THR-1484 had just finished reconciling. A wound that traces to the fight is
authored on the band that caused it and stamps its own source.

**`processEncounterMastery` was verified before deletion, not assumed.** Same
sole caller, same dead loop, same cast. The mastery family has one live writer —
`mentorshipOutcomes.grantMasteryTrait` on apprentice graduation — and that path
is untouched.
