# THR-2042 — A faction's commission pays the member who takes it up

**Shipped 2026-10-26.** M3: Dynamic Economy; plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-25-thr-2042-faction-commission-pay.md`, decided by the design lane under delegation.

**What changed.** Commission Quest's coin used to leave the world: nothing read a `faction_quest` posting. The posting now holds its 5 coin as `escrow` (`status: 'open'`). When a mortal who makes choices completes any of the faction's encounters, `processFactionEncounterReputation` returns the faction it credited and `executeStepResult` calls `takeUpCommission` (`src/engine/factionCommissions.ts`): the oldest open posting pays one Means step (`COMMISSION_MEANS_PAY` = `MEANS_STEP`), is marked `taken`, and a new `took_up` edge (agent → posting) records the taker. The aftermath's `economy` change ends "— a commission their faction paid for." and the sheet's Means tooltip reads the same `COMMISSION_CAUSE_LINE`. An untaken posting lapses at `expiryTick` and `lapseCommissions` (every tick) refunds its escrow to the purse. Unseen members never take one. Trace `economy.commission`; debug `__DEBUG.getFactionCommissions(id)`; switch `FACTION_COMMISSIONS_ENABLED`.

**Evidence.** `factionCommissions.test.ts` (13), `factionCommissionsResolution.test.ts` (3, through `executeStepResult`), `factionCommissionsSwitch.test.ts` (1). `npm run census:commissions` (medium, 360 ticks, one seed per process): taken 4 / 8 / 10 of 50 / 42 / 66 posted on seeds 42 / 99 / 7; every ledger balances.
