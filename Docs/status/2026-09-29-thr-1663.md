## What was wrong

On seeds 42 and 99, 300 ticks, every one of 72 leads was `vague`, 65 sat with ambient mortals who never act, and 0 surveys reached a ruin. A held lead was read by nothing that decides where a mortal goes. A survey of a ruin the surveyor already held a lead on was refused (`clue_already_held`), so a lead could never improve.

## What changed

1. **Leads lean toward deciders.** `CLUE_BIAS_DECIDER = 4.0` is a ×5 factor in `clueLifecycle.scoreCandidate`. The breakdown carries `deciderBonus`.
2. **A held lead joins the survey targets.** A lead's ruin rides ahead of `cell.observe.location`'s proximity cap (`CLUE_LEAD_SURVEY_CANDIDATES_MAX = 2`, freshest first). The survey carries `leadPull`, which the unified board folds into its desire term. It is on `decision_board_comparison`'s `boardTop`. A **lead pass** walks the survey cell for a deciding holder's lead ruins even when no active ambition lists it.
3. **A survey sharpens instead of refusing.** `sharpenClue` raises the lead to the better of its precision and the band's, and freshens `discoveredTick`. This emits `ruins.clue_sharpened` (`via: 'survey'`).
4. **Delve admission scans from `located`-lead holders**, not every actor × every location. Order and outcome are pinned by `delveAdmissionEquivalence.test.ts`, whose seven dense worlds' snapshots were recorded on the old scan before it was replaced.

**Tuned:** `CLUE_LEAD_SURVEY_PULL_MULT` went from the plan's 1.5 to **2.5**, the value of `ARRIVAL_GOAL_COMMITMENT_MULTIPLIER`, which the plan says it mirrors. At 1.5, seed 99's lead surveys reached the board's top five 7 times and won none (0.56–0.77 of the encounter winner).

## The kill criterion fired: decider share

The target is ≥ 30%. Even at `CLUE_BIAS_DECIDER = 50`, where a decider wins every draw they are in, the share tops out at **27% and 23%**. About 20 deciders live among 550–750 individuals, so only a quarter of rumour pools hold one. The recipient weight is saturated; the pool is the limit. Per the plan ("stop before S3 and re-plan; the next lever is lead hand-off"), [THR-1675](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1675) is filed to decide between hand-off, a wider pool for deciders, or a new target.
