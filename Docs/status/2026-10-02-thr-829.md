### 2026-10-02 — THR-829: rival schemes take hold again, and the map shows where

**The materialize beat was broken in every real world.** Rivals are state, not graph nodes. The materialize move tried to bind a `sponsors_scheme` edge from the rival, `addEdge` threw on the missing source, the fail-soft catch logged `rival.scheme_move_failed`, and the beat lost its sphere pressure along with the edge. The edge is gone for rival schemes (rivals stay off the graph, as the 2026-10-02 design decided). The beat now emits `rival.scheme_materialized` `{rivalId, targetId, compositionId}`, and its pressure push runs. `sponsors_scheme` stays registered, because notable agendas bind it legitimately.

**The overlay reads state, not an edge.** `buildRivalSchemeTargets(activeCompositions, worldFlags)` returns each non-failed rival scheme whose materialize move-done flag is set. `buildRivalInfluenceMarkers` takes that as a third input and marks the target hex `reason: 'scheme'`. A live essence drain still wins a shared hex.

**Two more defects stood between the fix and the player, and both are fixed here:**
- **Starved schemes.** The composition runner capped at 16 per tick *before* skipping finished entries. Completed notable agendas never leave the ledger and sort ahead of every `rival-scheme-*` id, so on seed 42 all three rival schemes sat at phase 0/4 for 145 ticks. Only `active` compositions now enter the cap. After the fix, 200 ticks on seed 42 medium (doom woken at tick 0) give 9 materializations, 0 move failures, and 0 rival edges.
- **An invisible overlay.** The rival layer's visibility froze at the zoom tier it was built in, because it had no `zoomTier` sync, unlike its sibling layers. Its outline was also depth-tested at z≈0.08 under settlement models standing at z≈6, so it hid on exactly the hexes it marks: zero marker pixels in the capture. Both are fixed. The outline now rings Ardenmor Keep in the rival's green at regional zoom.
