# 2026-09-28 — THR-1639: a journey keeps its goal (The First's quiet stretches)

**Ticket:** [THR-1639](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1639) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1633-written-encounters-land.md` § S2 · **Pillars:** Engine (journey state, cap stage, arrival decision); Content N/A (no templates or prose); UI N/A (no component; the DECIDE timeline event gains a `journeyGoal` field the debug exporter already carries).

## What was wrong

A mortal who picked an encounter in another town set out for it and then lost it, in four ways:

1. **No pull recorded.** The `queue_movement` branch never set `motivationPull`, so the mid-road re-check compared alternatives against zero, and any entry elsewhere won at the first check.
2. **Reroutes dropped the target.** Both reroute paths built a new movement state with no `targetEncounterId`.
3. **The arrival board cut the goal.** On arrival the 40-slot shortlist cap cut the very encounter the trip was for, on 14 of 41 arrivals in 60 ticks (seed 42). The mortal re-decided without it and often walked straight back.
4. **Unified goals read as vanished.** GUARD 5 checked the target with the legacy lookup only, so every unified-template goal (`fa.*`, `veil.*`, `realm.*`, `mc.*`) was flagged "target invalid" every re-check. That was 42 bogus reroute traces per 200 ticks on seed 99.

## What shipped

- **The pull is recorded on the axis the re-check reads.** This is a deviation from the plan. The plan said to record the candidate's `finalScore`. Built that way, the gate failed: seed 11 left The First ping-ponging between two towns for its last 77 ticks. The re-check scores alternatives by the cache entry's `questPriority` (1–9), and `finalScore` sits about 10× lower (0.1–1.4), so a low-scoring pick lost to any default-priority entry. The journey now records the chosen entry's `questPriority`. `REROUTE_SCORE_MULTIPLIER` (1.5) is unchanged and now compares like with like.
- **Both reroutes carry their new target.** The decision-phase reroute carries the cache entry's template and sublocation. The movement-phase reroute carries the candidate's `bestTemplateId`.
- **`JOURNEY_GOAL_CAP_RESERVE = 1`.** The arrival goal is flagged `journeyGoal` on a copy of its cache entry and claims a Phase 1e slot, after every other reserve.
- **`ARRIVAL_GOAL_COMMITMENT_MULTIPLIER = 1.5`.** The first decision after a journey ends scores the travelled-for encounter ×1.5, then the goal is consumed. The DECIDE timeline event records `journeyGoal: kept | dropped`.
- **GUARD 5 checks both catalogs.**
- **`scripts/first-encounter-gate.ts`.** This is the S2 acceptance gate, with the constants `FIRST_FIRST_ENCOUNTER_MAX_TICK` and `FIRST_ENCOUNTER_MAX_GAP_TICKS` (both 30). A heavy-lane test pins seed 42.
- **Contract `journey-keeps-encounter-target`** is registered and LIVE.
