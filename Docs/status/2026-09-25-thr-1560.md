# 2026-09-25 — THR-1560: a mortal can hunt a beast (Hunts H2)

**Ticket:** [THR-1560](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1560) · **Project:** Physical Conflict · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-23-hunts.md` (plan doc 6 of 6), slice H2 · **Pillars:** Engine · Content · UI (the codex and the undertaking grid).

## What shipped

- **The `monster` undertaking object type** — a class of the Mortal kind (`classOf: 'mortal'`), live beasts only (`isLiveMonster`), held by itself (`selfOwned`), tier by lair (major 2, legendary 3), two verbs:
  - **Track** (`cell.observe.monster`): `recordHuntTracking` mints a `hidden_weakness` mark and reveals the temper; one secret per hunter per beast (`already_tracked`); a beast's mark is never pressed into a favour (`no_favour_from_beasts`).
  - **Hunt** (`cell.destroy.monster`): `deferredPayoff` — completion writes no outcome node and closes no grievance; it plants the confront at the lair as an appointment (48 ticks, kept branch `#lair_confront` aimed at the beast, missed branch `#hunt_trail_cold` → the new seed-only `hunt.trail_cold`). No beast is ever the creditor; a refused plant pushes no seed; a lost lair drops the seed after releasing its promise.
- **Who hunts:** three doors — a scar (`blood_drawn` / `grievance_cooled` toward the beast), a grievance whose culprit is the beast, a den within 2 hexes of home — recorded as `gate_exempt:<reason>`. Offered through `KINDS_BY_REACH.iron`, Eye's observe rider, and hand lists on `ambition_seek_revenge`, `ambition_avenge_fallen` (its first profile) and `ambition_conquer_territory`.
- **One fight, not two:** `hunt_confront_pending` refuses a second hunt while the promise stands; a waiting hunter is not offered *The Named Beast*; M4's walk-in skips them (`hunt_appointment`).
- **A beast's grievance closes only on its death** — never by the hunt, never by another project (`grievanceClosesOnCompletion`).
- **UI:** the codex shows *Hunt a monster* / *Track a monster* under *Mortal (monster)* with the reason line *Only with a scar, a grievance, or a den near home*; the grid renders a *(monster)* sub-row (`LIVE_CLASS_CELL_NOTES`).
- **Debug:** `listMonsters().huntedBy`, CLI `hunts`, `npm run census:hunts`, trace `hunt.tracked`.

## Three measured additions beyond the plan's text

1. **`pricedByHex`.** Lairs have no `adjacent` / `road` / `contains` edge, so `computeAppointmentSlack` read every lair `unreachable` — the regime was `lost`, no journey was ever queued, and the first planted confront missed as `unreachable`. `queueAppointmentJourney` already walks an off-graph place by hex A*; the new opt-in payoff flag prices the slack the same way (`APPOINTMENT_HEX_TICKS_PER_HEX` = 3). Opt-in, so THR-1479's "no road is unreachable" rule stands for every other appointment.
2. **The conqueror's hand list.** On seed 42 no deciding mortal held a vengeance or devotion ambition in 300 ticks, and survival holders did not lead with Iron: the plan's routes founded **zero** hunts (the kill criterion). `ambition_conquer_territory`, the commonest Iron-led deciding ambition, now lists both hunt cells; the doors still gate it. Decided under the 2026-09-11 delegation; veto invited on THR-1533.
3. **`HUNT_TARGET_SCAN_CAP` 64 → 128**, because an epic map holds 120 living monsters at 300 ticks (the plan asks the cap to cover every preset).
