## The two that would actually spoil the session

**[THR-1464](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1464) is the worst thing found**, and not because it is hard — it is a layout collision. It is the worst because of *where* it is. The First Thread beat is the game's opening divine choice, and both cards it offers render as `✳ CR▣TE▸✦◈◇S▮◆G⋯`. The DOM is correct: `✸`, `CREATE`, `COSMIC` and `✦✦✦✦✦✦⋯` are four separate, correctly-populated leaves. Only their boxes overlap — measured, the pip row covers the **entire width** of the `Cosmic` label and sits inside its vertical band. It reproduces on a third card (`Place Thread`) in the Seat beat, across two separate page loads, so it is the shared ascendant action-card header rather than one card's data.

**[THR-1465](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1465) is the one that took longest to believe.** Every slice encounter shows the same three chips — `Wayside Keeper / Wayside Keeper`, `Fellow Traveler / Fellow Traveler`, `Road Outrider / Road Outrider` — name line identical to role line, all `disabled`. `EncounterVeil.tsx` is **not** at fault and needs no change: its Law 21 handling (an unbound spec gets a plain name, not a control that does nothing) is exactly right, and where a real cast member exists the same component renders `View The Caravan Master`, enabled, opening the sheet. The bug is upstream — the generic specs never bind to a node.

The sharp edge is The Swindled Family, where the row is **entirely** placeholder: the prose introduces *"a man, a woman, three children"* and the swindler who sold them the deed, and none of them appear, while three strangers who appear nowhere in the prose do. The row is not merely noisy there; it is actively wrong about who is present.

## What was already good

The list above is not the whole picture, and the good half is the larger one:

- **Prose is at standard on all five.** Narrator mode throughout, P1 arrival / P2 costs / P3 stake intact, no in-situ scene-painting. The carryover between steps lands — *"They came up short of the saddle and camped in the scrub."*
- **Zero unresolved `{cast:*}` tokens on screen**, across every step and aftermath walked. The [THR-1459](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1459) defect class does not appear in the slice.
- **Zero legacy stance choices, zero Resume-Intervene** — confirmed both ways. All nine `stance` grep hits in the slice file are substring matches inside *distance* and *instance*; all five `resume` hits are the prose phrase *"the road resumes"*.
- **Seeds plant, and surface.** The Swindled Family's aftermath rendered two `PATH` chips carrying the sequel hooks — *"The man who sold the paper is still working, and his face is known now"* and *"Word of a kindness on the fen road is traveling ahead of the traveler."* Assertion 7 satisfied end to end.
- **Zero console errors** on every route.

So four of the five defects are presentation-layer and none requires re-authoring an encounter.

## Two findings about the protocol itself

**`window.__DEBUG` does not exist on the deployed build.** It is tree-shaken in prod exactly as CLAUDE.md documents — `typeof window.__DEBUG === "undefined"` on `threadbearer.co`. THR-1463 asks for the sweep to run *on the deployed build* and to use `window.__DEBUG.tick(n)` and `getOutcomePinVerdict()`; those two requirements cannot both hold. Consequences, separated because they are not equal:

- **Assertion 2 survives intact.** `?outcome=` is a URL flag and works in prod, and its `[?outcome]` console line reports both the band shown and the full authored inventory — strictly more than `getOutcomePinVerdict()` returns per call. One pin per encounter yielded the whole band table in THR-1468.
- **Assertion 8 does not.** *At least one natural firing in free play* requires `tick(n)`, because `document.hidden` throttles the interval loop to ~1 tick/click in an automated tab. It was **not satisfied**, and no substitution on the deployed build can satisfy it. Recorded as impediment 1019 rather than ticketed, per the process-work throttle.

**The `[?outcome]` line fires at the *final* step's resolution, not the first.** A pin read after step 1 of a two-step encounter looks like silence, which reads as a diverged or absent pin. This cost one false reading during the sweep (a `critical_success` pin on the crossroads that appeared to produce nothing) and is written into THR-1468's coordination block so the next person does not repeat it.
