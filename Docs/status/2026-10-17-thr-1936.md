# THR-1936 — mortal wealth S1: deciders are born with Means, and the money encounters pay or cost it

**Shipped 2026-10-17.** Slice S1 of the mortal-wealth plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-15-thr-1936-mortal-wealth.md`). Every autonomous decider is born with Means at world creation: role wealth plus a home-town shift (`ensureDeciderMeans`, `MEANS_BIRTH_HOME_SHIFT`); The First minted at the meeting (and the dev-seeded First) is born with Means at its mint (`giveBirthMeans`). Templates may carry an **outcome table** (`outcomeEffects`, band → aftermath effects; a resolved variant's table wins), applied once per resolved action, for every mortal, before the summary (`applyOutcomeEffectsTable`, `outcomeEffectsApplied`). One new effect kind, `economy_shift` on `means`, banks through `bankWealth` with a halved loss below Getting by and no write at the floor. One new aftermath change kind, `economy`: "Better off / Worse off — X is now <band>" on a crossed band, "Fortunes turn up / down — X comes away a little richer / poorer" inside one; a BOON or SCAR chip on MEANS with the `ui.econ.means.change` tooltip, words only. Seed row: Merchant's Gambit (±`MEANS_STEP`; stakes name the loss). `__DEBUG.getMortalMeans`; traces `economy.means.birth`, `economy.means.shift`; master switch `MORTAL_MEANS_ENABLED`.
