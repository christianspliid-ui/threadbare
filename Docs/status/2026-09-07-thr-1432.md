# THR-1432 — a mortal's work casts omens

**2026-09-07 · Engine (omen agenda + motive receipt) · project Thematic Pressure & Living World**

Omens & Atmospheric Pressure was the last subsystem on the generated view that a mortal's work could honestly reach and did not: the foreshadowing layer read the doom clock, the spheres and the culture, never what mortals *did*. One reader closes it — no new cell, kind, verb, node or edge.

## What changed

- **The portent.** The omen agenda phase (1.7) gained a last step, `castUndertakingPortent`: every `undertaking_outcome` event node within `OMEN_UNDERTAKING_LOOKBACK_TICKS` (6) is weighed by its own `harmMagnitude` × `OMEN_UNDERTAKING_WEIGHT_BY_HARM` × the attention term (`OMEN_UNDERTAKING_FOLLOWED_WEIGHT` 2 when the god follows the culprit *or* the victim), and the top one becomes an `EmittedOmen` — local scope at the razed town (the thing the work was done to, else where it happened), intensity at the harm's own weight, one day long, category by `OMEN_UNDERTAKING_CATEGORY_BY_HARM` so it tilts the encounter scorer the way the existing affinity table already reads. It carries `provenance` — the outcome node, the harm, the culprit, the victim, the site and **the deed in words** ("the razing of Lower Ravenham — Oswen's work", the grievance lane's own label shape) — so a surface can say what it portends without ever showing a cell id. The node is stamped `portendedTick` and never portends twice; one portent per tick; a tie is one seeded draw and a clear winner costs the phase's PRNG nothing. The chronicle carries the hook (`UNDERTAKING_PORTENT_HOOKS`, one GM line per harm class); the trace is `omen_emitted` with `sourceReactionId: 'undertaking_outcome'` and every candidate's score on it.
- **The receipt.** `MotiveContributionKind` gained `undertaking`: when the ambition pulling a mortal's encounter choice was minted by an undertaking outcome (`mintedByEventId` is an `evt_und_…` node — a harm done to them, or their own work collapsing), the receipt's ambition term reads as `undertaking` with the node as `provenance.nodeId` and the deed as `provenance.detail`. The composer binds a `{deed}` slot; four motive clauses and three stake clauses render it; the classifier counts it as mission. `resolveMintedAmbitionOrigin` keeps the event beside the label; the label-only helper and every THR-726 caller are unchanged.
- **The map.** `SUBSYSTEM_READERS` gains one shared reader (`OMEN_PORTENT_READER`) on all ten LIVE-TOUCHED subsystems, so the grid shows Omens as `READS`; its `UNTOUCHED_BY_DESIGN` "gap, not a design choice" entry is removed — the generator would have failed it as stale. Interface contract `undertaking-outcomes-cast-omens` (Ambitions & Undertakings → Omens) is 🟢 LIVE. The clocks wiki page says mortals cast omens too.

## Measured

- **The Done-when said "tick 12"; the world says tick 40.** A destroy × Location needs 36 progress at 6 per checkpoint, so with the band pinned to `success` it completes 36 ticks after the spawn (seed 42 medium: outcome node written at tick 39, portent at tick 40). The calibration THR-1403 is changing to `[2,3,4]` brings that inside twelve ticks; the live test's horizon is the measurement, not the wish.
- **The reader is falsified against the real writer.** Every fixture node in the unit test is written by `createUndertakingOutcomeNode`; the live test runs `initializeGameState → runTick` and reads `state.emittedOmens` the tick after the node lands. The headless CLI run (seed 42, medium, `spawn undertaking ind_0 cell.destroy.location --band success`, `tick 40`) shows the node, its stamp, and the omen with its deed and hook.
- **A first portent appeared unasked:** a research circle abandoned at tick 31 portended at 32 ("Tools lie where they were dropped at Stone Remnant; the work abandoned at Horsepolis is a thing people step around") — the self-facing harm the grievance lane also mints from, at the lowest weight.
