# THR-2240 — the answer to Doom is where Doom is

**Shipped 2026-11-15.** Thematic Pressure & Living World. No plan doc (the ticket body is the plan); the Doom half of THR-2202, building on THR-2207 (Stay the Unmaking). Answers warm round 4: no tester found an answer to Doom.

- **The Doom panel has an answer line.** `DoomClockDetail` gains an Act line under the current stage (`DoomStayAnswer`), read from `doomStayNotice(state, { includeNotHeld: true })`: not held → "Nothing you hold slows it yet. After its first step, you will be given a way to hold it back." (`DOOM_STAY_NOT_HELD_LINE`); held → THR-2207's hint line plus a **Stay it** button; active → "You are holding it back." The button runs the stage notice's own handler: it closes the panel and opens the god's page, whose Act row holds the card. The line is hidden once the doom has landed. Once Doom is past the stage the first-stage gift fires at but the gift is not yet received, the line reads "Nothing you hold slows it yet. A way to hold it back is waiting for you." (`DOOM_STAY_GIFT_WAITING_LINE`, added at the review gate: the first line promised a step that had already happened). The stage notice itself keeps the silent not-held case (option off).
- **The Doom bar's hover says it too.** `DoomBar` takes `stayLine` and appends it after the next-stage hint. The static `ui.doom_bar` text is unchanged and stays within 200 characters.
- **The gift names the card.** The first-stage milestone prose ends "It is yours now as Stay the Unmaking. You will find it on the Doom panel, and on your own page." The button stays "Receive".
- **The warm recap says Doom moved.** `snapshotWarmRecap` records `doomStage`; `buildWarmRecap` adds a `doom` line, "Doom took a step while you were away: {stage}.", plus "Stay the Unmaking slows it." when the card is held. Kept second by priority, shown last. A stage with no name reads "its next stage" (Law 43). New `ui.warm_recap.doom_step` / `doom_stay_held` entries.

No engine rule, card, price, interrupt, trace or constant changed. Words only.
