**Shipped 2026-11-08.** Dominion — Player Power Progression. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-11-07-thr-2189-an-away-lean-waits-for-the-reach.md`.

1. **An away lean waits for the reach.** `awayLeanWaitsForReach` (`crossroadsDecision.ts`, pure, fail-soft) is OR'd into THR-2188's `leanWaits` in `phaseAgentDecision`. A held, unexpired away lean whose road is on the board, with no override, whose **unleaned** winner (`boardWinnerWithoutLean`) is another road or idle, is kept and traced `crossroads_lean_waiting` (`direction: 'away'`). It resolves at the decision where the mortal reaches for that road. `CROSSROADS_HOLD_MAX_TICKS` still ends it.

4. **Words.** New away held line, a direction-keyed expiry toast ("never reached for {road} again"), the refusal line, the `ui.crossroads.forecast` tooltip and rulebook §4.
