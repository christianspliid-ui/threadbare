# THR-2043 — The faction sheet names its open commission

**Shipped 2026-11-19.** Builds on THR-2042 (a faction's commission pays the member who takes it up).

- **The change.** `src/engine/views/subjects/factionRows.ts`: the faction sheet's Purse row ends with "It has posted a commission: the first member to finish its work is paid." while the faction holds an open posting (`openCommissionsOf` in `src/engine/factionCommissions.ts`). The sentence goes when the commission is taken, lapses, or passes its expiry tick (Law 56: the sentence is the state). With `FACTION_COMMISSIONS_ENABLED` off, nobody is paid, so the sheet says nothing (the review gate caught this in round 1; `factionCommissionsSwitch.test.ts` gains the case).
