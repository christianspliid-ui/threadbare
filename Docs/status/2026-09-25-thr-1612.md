# 2026-09-25 — THR-1612: the Mercenary Company's encounters can be drawn

**Ticket:** [THR-1612](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1612) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** Content = the fix · Engine N/A (the awareness gate is correct) · UI N/A.

## What was wrong

All 15 `mc.*` Mercenary Company templates named their reach only on their steps. `filterByAwareness` skips any entry with no `reachPrimary`, and both entry builders copy that from `template.reach`. So every Company encounter was dropped at the funnel's first stage, even for the Company's own members on the same hex. Measured by THR-1597: awareness gating 100% on seeds 42 and 99, 0 firings. The Company is the one guild with deciding members on both seeds.

## What changed

- `src/data/mercenary-encounter-content.ts`: each template now carries `intrinsicTier`, `reach: 'iron'`, `crudType`, `scale: 'local'`, `apCost: 1` and `motivations`. The values mirror the Civic Guard's matching templates.
- The 31 steps' `failBehavior: 'block'` (not in the type) became `'continue_weakened'`. This is behavior-neutral, because the lifecycle only tests `=== 'fail_action'`.
- New `src/data/__tests__/template-level-reach.test.ts`: it fails if any catalog template has no reach, and it pins every field the draw reads on the `mc.*` set. Confirmed red without the fix and green with it.

## Measured after (THR-1597 reach reader, seeds 42,99 × 200, medium)

Awareness gating on `mc.*` is **0** (was 100%). Firings:

- `collect_bounty`, `siege_work`, `hostile_negotiation` and `extraction_op` fire on both seeds.
- `patrol`, `war_council` and `siege_contract` fire on seed 99.
- `guard_caravan`, `escort_prisoner` and `field_command` now stop at **prerequisites**, which is a separate gate.
- The social templates, `mc.join` and `mc.promotion` stop at rank access or prerequisites.

## Noticed, not fixed

Four catalog templates carry a reach name outside the Eight Reaches: `divine.afflict_bless` (`life`), and the `encounter.shell_proof.*` trio (`resolve`, `combat`). Seven lack `crudType` or `motivations`: `army.aftermath.refugees`, three `reputation.*` templates, and the shell_proof trio. `encounterCache.buildEntryUnified` spreads `tmpl.motivations`, so any of those that reaches the location cache would throw there.
