# THR-1944 — a mortal brokers an oath

**Shipped 2026-10-25.** Two encounters now swear a real oath of fealty. A mortal who wins *The Faction Unification* swears one faction in the room to the realm they serve, persuaded. A mortal who wins *The Warlord's Tribute* by demanding the oath swears the warlord's company to that realm, coerced (it starts disloyal, like a siege oath). Built to the design-lane spec on the ticket (2026-10-18b).

**Change.**
- `src/engine/crown/brokeredOath.ts` (new): the `faction_broker_fealty` resolver. Liege = the mortal's highest-ranked realm membership (ties to the lower id), or that realm's own liege when it is sworn. Vassal = the named agent's faction (`$cast:warlord`), else the faction present at the mortal's town with the best standing toward the liege, then the higher income, then the lower id; never the liege's enemy.
- `src/engine/crown.ts`: `swearBrokeredOath` applies every crown rule (crown on, `maySwear`, an unsworn realm liege, the re-swear cooldown) and writes through `writeOath`. The `oath_sworn` trace, chronicle line and `crown_formed` path moved into one shared `announceOath`; `seek_fealty`'s behaviour is unchanged.
- `sworn_to` gains `brokeredBy`; `forcedBy` widens to `demand`. The sheet reads "won by Maret's word." / "forced by Maret's demand." with the mortal linked. Chronicle: `OATH_BROKERED_LINES`.
- Content: outcome tables on the three winning bands of both encounters. Switch `BROKERED_OATH_ENABLED`.
- Debug: the Economy tab's Oaths table gains *brokered by*; `__DEBUG.getOaths` returns `brokeredBy` / `forcedBy`.
