# 2026-10-09 — THR-1644 S1: one rite writer, and The First is the first

**Ticket:** [THR-1644](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1644) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-06-thr-1644-threading-ceremony.md` (slice S1; S2 = THR-1754, S3 = THR-1755)

Whoever the god threads first is now The First, through the meeting or through the *Agent Thread* card. Every thread a god binds now goes through one rite writer.

- **D3 at the thread write.** `graphOpExecutor`'s thread write calls `resolveThreadWrite` (`src/engine/threadingRite.ts`). If the god holds no First, the card's thread is written at `the_first`, with `storyPhase: 'call'` and `attentionMode: 'pause'` (the meeting's two journey fields). Every First perk follows through the existing `the_first` readers: shaping attention, the journey, the doom wake, the gold thread line, and retiring the meeting. The Return cooldown gates the card exactly as it gates the meeting. The thread also gets the real `establishedTick`; the templates hard-code 0.
- **D1, one writer.** `applyThreadingRite` lands a rite's value-pole shift (× 0.5 for a grown mortal), spark investment, scar (with the meeting's floor) and bond reception on an *existing* mortal. `createAgentFromMeeting` is now create-then-apply. The meeting's output for seeds 42/99/2 is pinned by a golden snapshot recorded on the pre-refactor writer (`meetingWriterGolden.thr1644.test.ts`).
- **D2, the shape ladder.** The ascendant's `threadsBoundCount` counts every thread ever bound and never decreases. Worlds without the count fall back to live individual threads, so a seeded First counts. A new First plays `full_no_sensing`, threads 2–3 play `short`, and later threads play `bond_only`.
- **Pending rite.** The executor has no GameState, so it leaves a `ritePending` marker on the edge. Orchestrator phase 2a.15 (`drainThreadingRites`) moves the rite to `GameState.pendingThreadingRite` / `pendingThreadingRiteQueue`. Overflow past `RITE_QUEUE_MAX` resolves as the bond alone, with no hand. GameState is not persisted, so a reload has no pending rite and the thread survives.
- **No rite surface yet.** `RITE_SURFACE_ENABLED = false` resolves each rite at once as *Bond without a hand* (D6): a seeded no-card bond roll (world × agent × tick) lands the reception. S2 flips the switch when the modal ships.
- **Traces:** `thread.court_position_resolved`, `rite.queued`, `rite.applied`. **Debug:** `await window.__DEBUG.getThreadingRite()`.
- **Executor calls:** a new First always plays the full rite, whatever its ordinal. The Return cooldown gates the card route too. The DebugPanel thread rows (`riteShape` / `bondReception`) move to S2 with the rest of the UI. The THR-1643 test now runs in a world that already holds a First.
