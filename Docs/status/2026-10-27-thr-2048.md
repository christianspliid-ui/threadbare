# THR-2048 — A place held after success

**Shipped 2026-10-27.** M3: Dynamic Economy. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-26-thr-2048-place-held-after-success.md`.

**What changed.** A new aftermath effect kind, `hold_place`, leaves the actor holding one Place of a type at the scene's settlement or wild place. If none stands, it raises one through `createSublocation`, named "{Actor}'s {noun}", and `grantHolding` makes it the actor's freehold (`via: creation`). If an unheld one stands, the actor takes it up (`via: grant`) and it is made permanent, so a conditional mine never dissolves under its holder. If the actor already holds it, nothing is written. If someone else holds it, nothing is raised and the actor is paid `heldElsewhereMeans` as *work that paid*. A scene never takes a holding away. Three money encounters carry it on their success bands: forge construction (a forge, plus a prosperity step; the costly success pays the overrun), the prospecting expedition and prospect the seam (a claim on the mine). The forge assembly's failure line now reads true under a failure: *"Whatever stands here now, it is not yet a forge."* The forge and seam stakes now name the cost, as the outcome-table invariant requires. Trace: `encounter.place.held` with its `result`.

**Evidence.** `holdPlace.test.ts` (10 tests): raised, already_theirs, held_elsewhere (+`MEANS_STEP`), taken_up, no site, and a taken-up conditional mine survives `checkDissolutions` with no ore. Content: every success band holds, no failure band does, and every amount is a named step. 720-tick seed-42 medium census (the world enters twilight at tick 114; ticked on through it): 2 Places raised across 33 settlements, 0 taken up, no site with two, and both still standing at tick 720. The kill criterion (more than one raise per three towns) is not tripped. Browser: 1920×1080 capture of the pinned forge scene showing the new stakes line (`Docs/evidence/thr-2048/`). The Holdings-sheet capture could not be taken: the seeded world reached its end-of-age hold before the pinned scene resolved. No UI code changed. The sheet reads the `owns` edge that the tests assert.
