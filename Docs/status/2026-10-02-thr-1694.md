### 2026-10-02 — THR-1694: a held ticket is skipped, not claimed and released

**The defect.** A ticket handed off under a 24 h veto window sat in Ready for Dev, and its hold lived only in a comment. `pull-work` reads comments after claiming, so the hourly pickup claimed it, read the hold, released it, and exited — six times on THR-1687 on 2026-10-01, while the whole queue was held (impediment row 1121).

**The fix.**
- **Writers:** design-session Step 5 item 2a and design-lane Step 3c write `Claimable from: <ISO-8601 UTC>` into the issue description when a veto window applies.
- **Reader:** pull-work Step 1 drops a candidate whose time is still ahead, before the priority sort. It logs `[pull-work] skipped THR-X — claimable from <time>`, and reports "queue held until <time>" when nothing is claimable. That is a filter on data already read, so claim-before-read is untouched.
- **One definition:** `scripts/claimable-from-predicate.ts`. A missing or malformed line means claimable, so a bad hold can never hide work. The test covers a hold an hour ahead (skipped), an hour behind (claimable), and no line (claimable).
- The `tb-opus-pickup` prompt and its mirror carry the same rule.
