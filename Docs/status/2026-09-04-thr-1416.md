# THR-1416 — the consequence chip's tag ceiling, measured against the corpus

**2026-09-04 · UI · Improvement · [THR-1416](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1416)**

The ticket asked for one constant: raise the consequence chip tag's `maxWidth`
from 190 to 240, because `BOND · REPUTATION WITH SACRED GROVE` wrapped to four
lines. The constant shipped at **210**, and the reason is the interesting half.

## What the measurement changed

The tag column and the sentence beside it live in one flex row inside a
`maxWidth: 540` container (`EncounterVeil.tsx`, the `aftermath-consequences`
block). The sentence is `flex: 1`. So the row's arithmetic is fixed:

```
sentence column = 540 − 2 (border) − 12 (padding) − 40 (tile) − 12 − 12 (gaps) − tag
                = 462 − tag
```

Every pixel the tag gains is a pixel the prose loses. Measured in a real layout
engine at the row's true geometry (Palatino Linotype 16px / 0.16em — both fonts
in this row are system fonts, so the metrics are the shipped ones), over the
four Grateful Kin bands and the six widest authored tags in the corpus:

| `maxWidth` | tag lines | sentence column | row height | tag↔sentence gap |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| 190 (was) | 4 | 270px | 136–163px | 48–75px |
| **210 (shipped)** | **3** | 250px | 136–163px — unchanged in 8/10 | 70–97px |
| 240 (proposed) | 2–3 | 220px | 163–190px — **taller in 9/10** | 119–146px |

**240 makes the row taller and the two columns less even**, which is the
opposite of what the ticket wanted. 210 removes the four-line wrap in 10 of 10
cases at almost no cost to the prose, and satisfies the Done-when as written
(*"renders in ≤ 3 lines"*) exactly.

## Two premises in the ticket that did not hold

Both are recorded because they are the reason the proposed value was wrong, not
to score a point against the ticket — it was authored from a live capture, and
a live capture is normally the stronger evidence.

1. **"The tag towers over its own sentence."** It does not. At 190 the tag is
   88px and its sentence is 136–163px — the *sentence* was always the taller
   column, in all ten measured cases. Shrinking the tag widens the gap.
2. **"No other authored chip noun pushes its sentence column below ~560px."**
   Unattainable by construction: the container is 540px, so the sentence column
   maxes at 462 − 74 = 388px and sits at 270px in the measured case. The clause
   cannot be satisfied at any tag width, so it cannot be the gate.

The one number the ticket and this pass both measured independently — the tag's
**4 lines at 190** — agrees exactly, which is what makes the rest of the model
trustworthy.

## The corpus, not the sample

The ticket sized the ceiling against `BOND · REPUTATION WITH SACRED GROVE` (35
chars). Swept across all **634 authored aftermath changes** (69 distinct
non-compact tags), that string is only the **17th widest**. The real ceiling
case is `SCAR · THE PROMISE THEY GAVE AT THE CROSSROADS` at 46 chars, and the
six widest all sit between 39 and 46. A value tuned to fit the 17th-widest tag
in two lines was always going to be tuned to the wrong thing; 210 is chosen
against the whole distribution instead.

## Shipped

- `CONSEQUENCE_TAG_MIN_PX` (74) and `CONSEQUENCE_TAG_MAX_PX` (210) — both
  literals named, per the ticket's Tunability clause and NFP #1. The measurement
  table lives on the constant, so the next person to retune it starts from the
  numbers rather than from a screenshot.
- `data-testid="consequence-chip-tag-{category}"` on the tag span. It had no
  test id, so the ceiling it carries was not assertable from the DOM at all.
- A guard in the existing Grateful Kin block that builds its model from the
  authored template and asserts the shipped ceiling reaches the DOM, plus that
  the tag is still the element carrying the long noun — a cap on a span the text
  has moved out of would pass while governing nothing.

## Evidence

`Browser-verify substitution: jsdom-render — unattended run, no startable dev
server` (#546). jsdom has no layout engine, so the wrap itself was measured
separately in a real engine driven through a `data:` URL — `file://` is blocked
in both the Browser pane and Playwright, and no dev server can start in an
unattended run. That route is worth keeping: for a question that is purely
font-metric and geometric, it gives a true measurement with no server at all.

The guard was falsified before being trusted — setting the constant to 240 turns
it red, 210 green.
