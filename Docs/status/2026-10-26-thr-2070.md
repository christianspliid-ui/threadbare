# THR-2070 — A town short of stone no longer goes hungry

**Shipped 2026-10-26.** M3: Dynamic Economy; found by the design lane while designing THR-2069 (salt).

**What changed.** The famine/glut chronicle fires for staple and strategic goods, but every line was written for food, so a stone shortage read *"the people go hungry"*, and the good was printed with its registry capital (*"The Stone has run short"*). `resolveEconomicChronicle` now takes an optional `resourceCategory` on its context: on `resource_scarcity` / `resource_glut`, `'strategic'` draws the new `RESOURCE_WANT_CHRONICLE_TEMPLATES` pool (builders wait, the yards are quiet, nothing rots), and anything else keeps the food lines. `phaseResourceStockTiers` passes the class category and fills `{resource}` with the lower-cased good name (`resourceWord`); the trace summary keeps the display name.

**Evidence.** `src/engine/__tests__/resourceScarcityChronicleClass.test.ts`: the phase renders a stone crossing with no hunger word and a lower-case "stone", a grain crossing still draws a famine line, and 64 seeds × both triggers of the strategic pool carry no hunger word.
