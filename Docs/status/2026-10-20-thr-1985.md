# THR-1985 — Show what the pour and the odds do

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** Slice S1 of the "words teach themselves" plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-18-thr-1980-words-teach-themselves.md`, E1–E3, A1–A5, U1–U5). Round-4 cold testers said the sphere pour did nothing ("every essence still showed 50") and could not read the odds legend.

**The pour.** On the pour screen, each sphere shows how fast its pool will refill: "refills fast", "refills steadily" or "refills slowly". The word changes as the player pours. The deepest sphere adds "· pays your upkeep". In the god rail, each essence row has a three-dot rung under its balance. The row's hover opens with the word: "Refills fast." For the deepest sphere it reads "Refills steadily: your threads' upkeep is paid from this pool." One key line under the rows says what the dots mean ("how fast each pool refills"). A rate is never shown as a number (Law 13).

**The band** is a sphere's net income as a share of the god's total gross income (`essenceRefillBand`, fast ≥ 0.2, steady ≥ 0.08, slow > 0, else drains). Because it is a share, the Seat and thread yield do not change any sphere's word except the deepest one's. A unit test pins this. `computeEssenceIncomeBreakdown` returns the ledger mirror's net income plus its gross total. `computeEssenceIncome` is unchanged for its callers.

**The odds.** The stage legend is now one sentence: "The word is how it looks now, worst to best: Doomed, Perilous, Uncertain, Favorable, Fated. Green lines help, red lines hinder." It is two hover targets, so both registry tooltips stay in use (Law 17). A card that moves the odds inside one tier now says so: "▲ your hand lifts the odds — still Uncertain", or "— close to Perilous" when the odds are within `FORECAST_NEAR_NEXT_TIER_MARGIN` (0.05) of the next rung (`forecastMoveDetail`). The card legend shows the whole pip ladder, "● ■ ◆ ★ odds, weakest to strongest", built from `PIP_ODDS_TIERS`. The Fated hover starts with "The best word on the ladder." Fated stays grey: its colour is a decision reserved for Christian.

**Executor decisions (plan grey zones).** The rail's two-column rows are 141 px at 1920 and 181 px at 2560. That leaves no room for the word anywhere, so the rung sits under the balance numeral, where it takes no width from the label. The word is in the rung's accessible name and in the hover, and one key line names the rung (Law 12). At first the rung went inline, which cut the sphere labels to "M…" at 1080p. That was caught in the browser and fixed before shipping. The THR-1791 "one odds word on the stage" test now skips the dismissible legend, because naming the ladder is the point of the legend.

**Measured in the browser** (bash-vite :5297, Playwright, 1920×1080): `getEssenceRefill()` gives the bought spheres Mind and Spirit "fast". Every unbought sphere is "slow", and Mind's row carries the upkeep mark. On the pour screen, moving Spirit from two points to one changed its word from "refills fast" to "refills steadily". The Unsafe Bridge spawn showed "▲ your hand lifts the odds — still Uncertain". The page had no scroll, and the only console error was the known TTS health probe.
