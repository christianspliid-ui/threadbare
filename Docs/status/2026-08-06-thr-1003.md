**The ending of an encounter did not say which encounter it was the ending of (THR-1003, 2026-08-06).** Director review of the shipped THR-971 chip surface: *"there is nothing in the aftermath UI that helps the player understand that it is part of the same encounter. The encounter title is not there at all."* The step view carries full identity chrome — encounter title, agent context strip (portrait, name, reach chip, location, Show on map), clickable step navigator. The aftermath carried a hand-rolled row of flat green dots in the top-left corner reading `resolved`, and nothing else: **it said resolved without ever saying resolved *what*.** Fixed by giving the aftermath the same header the step view renders, from the same components rather than copies — `StepNavigator` gains a `mode="resolved"` reading and `ContextStrip` is reused unchanged, so the two surfaces cannot drift apart the way a forked header would. The corner dot row is deleted; the navigator now shows each step in its own outcome colour (`held` / `wavered` / `held` on the verification run) instead of flattening three different endings into one green. **Two decisions the ticket did not ask for but the reuse forced.** The aftermath path renders no replay view, so every dot is `disabled` in resolved mode — a control that silently does nothing is worse than no control. And the count is derived, not asserted: an encounter can stop before its last step, so the label reads `all 3 resolved` only when it did, and `1 of 2 resolved` when it did not. The `AFTERMATH` marker moves below the divider in the veil's gold, because the title styling it used to share now belongs to the encounter title above it and two identical ghost lines read as two titles. Browser-verified end to end at 1920×1080: `encounter.shrine_offering` played to resolution shows *Leave a Shrine Offering*, Vara's strip (Star, Sacred Grove), and the three-dot navigator complete.
