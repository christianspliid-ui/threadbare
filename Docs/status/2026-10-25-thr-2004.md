# THR-2004 — mandate texts stop promising that mandates slow the Doom or grant essence

**Shipped 2026-10-25.** Four player texts promised effects the engine never applies. A completed mandate pushes sphere pressure and fires the "fulfilled" event. A checkpoint kept well banks counter-omens that `computeEscalationSeverity` (`phaseDoom.ts`) spends to soften the next Doom stage. Nothing a mandate does touches the Doom clock's pace (`tickModifier`) or the essence ledger. The design lane picked option 1 (words, not a rule change) on 2026-10-24; Law 30 keeps the two clocks rivals.

**Change.**
- `ui.mandate_tracker`: "the second clock, racing the Doom Clock. Keep a checkpoint well and you blunt the doom's next blow."
- `ui.mandate_stage_culmination`: "Complete it and the mandate is fulfilled before the Doom Clock closes."
- `ui.essence_panel`: names the income `computeEssenceIncome` pays (threads, Seat, held places of power, aspects), not mandates.
- `beat.spine.the_seat`: "every mandate you keep well blunts its blows" (the same wording THR-1965 used for the doom-echo lines).
- `public/tick-cycle-reference.html`: the Mandate entry no longer says completion grants essence.

**Evidence.** `src/data/__tests__/ui-content.test.ts` pins the four texts: no doom-slowing / hold-back claim, no essence claim on the mandate tooltips, no mandate named as an essence income. `openingReveal.test.ts` carries the new seat line verbatim. `npm run gate` code track: typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, cli-smoke PASS; test 25387 passed, with one unrelated `beforeAll` timeout under load in `scripts/__tests__/worktree-write-guard.test.ts`, which passes 9/9 when run alone. Content strings only, no UI-pillar paths, so no browser evidence is owed.
