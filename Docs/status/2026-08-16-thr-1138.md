# THR-1138 — Faction standing reads as a word, not `62%`

**Shipped 2026-08-16.** A one-span display fix on the character sheet, plus the
render assertions that pin it.

## What was wrong

The character sheet's Faction section (`OverviewTab`, reached through
`AgentProfileModal`) drew a standing bar and then printed the same magnitude
again as a raw numeral beside it:

```tsx
<span className="text-xs tabular-nums" style={{ color: 'var(--text-tertiary)' }}>
  {Math.round(card.factionReputation * 100)}%
</span>
```

Law 13 forbids raw magnitudes on any mortal-facing surface — *"No percentages
anywhere player-facing"* — the same law that removed "auto-resolves in 4 ticks"
(THR-1070). This one predates the ratified laws; Christian found it in passing
during the 2026-08-16 attended session on THR-1136.

## What shipped

**The numeral is gone; the bar stays.** The ticket left the executor the choice
of bar-only or bar-plus-word. I took **bar + word**, for two reasons:

1. The bar alone is a magnitude with no anchor — the reader can see *more* or
   *less* but cannot name where the standing sits. That is the same complaint
   Christian made of `steadily` in THR-1082: a reading the player cannot place.
2. The sheet already speaks this exact vocabulary a few sections down, where
   personal reputation renders `card.reputationWord`. Using a different idiom
   for faction standing on the same surface would have been the inconsistency,
   not the fix.

The word comes from `getReputationWord` in `data/domain-words.ts` — the engine's
existing **absolute** standing ladder (`Distrusted → Unknown → Accepted →
Respected → Revered`), already in service for personal reputation. Deliberately
**not** `REPUTATION_MAGNITUDE_BANDS` from `aftermathWords.ts`, which the ticket
also gestured at: those band a *delta* ("standing deepened markedly") and would
have been the wrong semantics for an absolute 0–1 position.

The bar itself is untouched. A visual magnitude is sanctioned by the THR-1082
amendment (pips/fill), and only the digits were ever the violation. Its `width`
is still a percentage in a `style` attribute — that is a CSS length, not a
rendered numeral, and the tests assert the distinction directly.

## Evidence

Five jsdom render assertions on the real component
(`OverviewTabFactionStandingWord.test.tsx`): the word reaches the DOM; the
Faction section carries **no digit at all** at every knowledge level that renders
it (`recognised`, `known`, `intimate`, `transparent`); the bar fill still renders
with `width: 62%` and empty text content; and two standings that band
differently produce different words, so the surface reads the value rather than
printing one constant.

Expected words are sourced from `getReputationWord` rather than typed as
literals, so the assertions exercise ladder → surface → DOM instead of restating
the ladder somewhere it could drift from.

**Falsified before trusted.** Against the original `62%` span, 4 of the 5 fail,
quoting the defect exactly — `expected 'FactionThe Ashen ConcordWarden62%' not to
match /\d/`. The fifth (the bar-still-renders assertion) passes both ways *by
design*: the bar is what the fix preserves, so a test that failed on it would be
testing the wrong thing.

These stand in for the contractual 1920×1080 capture, which no unattended run can
produce — `preview_start` is refused outright with nobody present to approve it
(impediments #546, #574).

**UI-Laws judgment** (Law 13 primary, plus the standard sweep): **13** — fixed,
the raw percentage is gone and the sanctioned visual magnitude remains. **14** —
holds; the word resolves through the `REPUTATION_WORDS` display vocabulary, not a
raw key. **1** — improved on its magnitude clause. **17/21** — the faction *name*
beside this bar is plain text carrying no tooltip and no link to `FactionSheet`,
which exists. That is a pre-existing violation on the section this ticket
touches, out of scope for a numeral fix, and is filed as **THR-1149** rather than
folded in. **37** — N/A, not an encounter flow.

## Scope note

Deliberately not widened. The other `* 100` occurrences in `OverviewTab` (lines
90, 114, 146, 173) are CSS `left`/`width` positioning for the axis markers —
visual magnitudes, not rendered numerals, and legal under the same amendment.
