# 2026-09-29 — THR-1671: innate powers — each monster is born with a power of its kind

**Ticket:** [THR-1671](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1671) · **Project:** Powers & Item Generation · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-29-thr-1571-power-runtime.md` § S3 · **Pillars:** Engine (the stamp at `createNamedElite`); Content (eight innate powers, UL **Innate Power** anchored); UI (the sheet's *Innate Powers* section and `Innate ·` class word).

## What changed

- **A third Power class, `innate_power`** (`TraitCategory`, `POWER_SUBCATEGORIES`). Eight shared definitions, `power.innate.<family>` (`src/data/innate-powers.ts`): Thick Hide, Stone Body, Crackling Air, Deep Vigour, Wrong Thoughts, Half There, Already Moving, Rot Breath. Fate-woven, stateless carried effects only, every primitive `live`.
- **The stamp.** `createNamedElite` → `stampInnatePower` writes one `has_trait` edge (`source: 'innate'`) to the family's power after the monster card is minted, traced `power.innate_stamped`. Definitions are seeded at world init; a world seeded before the class existed gets its definition on demand.
- **The sheet** shows an *Innate Powers* section (✵), each row `Innate · <tier>`. `__DEBUG.getSpellHolders()` lists elites (`source: 'innate'`); `getAgentPowers` reports `powerClass: 'innate'`.

## Two lane decisions (the *how* of the agreed design)

- **The three auras target `all`, not `enemies`.** An `enemies` aura needs two hostile *factions* (`areFactionsHostile`); a lair's elite has none, so as tabled they could never apply. Radius 0 keeps them to the monster's own hex, which in practice means whoever came to fight it.
- **Thick Hide is a plain `passive`.** `conditional { in_combat }` on Iron is a passive in disguise, and the honest vocabulary refuses it.

## Known limit

Deep Vigour and Already Moving are `test_shaper`s, which shape the *bearer's own* roll. They work when the monster rolls (its lair encounters). A monster fought as a card does not roll, so they do not shape a fighter's step.
