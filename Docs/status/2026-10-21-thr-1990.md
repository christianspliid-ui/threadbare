# THR-1990 — The avatar's page says it is your mortal shape again

**Shipped 2026-10-21.** When the Inspector replaced the character sheet (THR-1911/1912), the player's own avatar opened as an ordinary person page: no "— your mortal shape", no "You are Vara, the Witness", a "Stranger" chip on yourself, and an initial in place of the origin portrait. THR-1609 had made all four part of the sheet, because cold testers mistook the avatar for a mortal to follow.

**Fix.** `SubjectHeaderVM` gains `mortalShape: { godTitleLine } | null`, set only on the person page whose node carries the `avatar_of` edge to the god. `SubjectHeader` renders `AVATAR_SHEET_SUFFIX` in the heading and `avatarGodTitleLine` under it. The avatar's header drops the knowledge chip, never silhouettes, and shows the origin portrait. `sheetParity.test.ts` lists the field with its home; `SubjectHeader.render.test.tsx` checks the avatar shows both lines and a mortal shows neither.

**Evidence.** Worktree Vite at 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, `openRef('agent','avatar.hunger.witness')`: heading "Vara — your mortal shape", title "You are Vara, the Witness", no Stranger chip, origin portrait, no console errors, no horizontal scroll.
