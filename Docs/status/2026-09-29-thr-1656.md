# 2026-09-29 — THR-1656: the player meets the past

**Ticket:** [THR-1656](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1656) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-28-thr-1631-world-with-a-past.md` § S2, § Content pillar, § UI pillar · **Pillars:** Engine (fog-gated selector, placeholders); Content (line tables, five tooltips); UI (chronicle section, place line, dead person's line).

## What was wrong

S1 wrote a thin past onto the graph at worldgen: dead empires and their ruins, an elder war, founding ages, wars in living memory and up to ten dead. The player saw none of it. The chronicle panel did not even mount until the first chronicle entry.

## What shipped

- **`readWorldPastForPlayer`** and **`isPastPlaceKnown`** (`worldPast.ts`) gate the past by fog. The outline shows from minute one: empires, the elder war, which Realms fought, every founding. The specifics wait until a place is found, meaning its hex is `visible` or `remembered`, or a Find/Perceive has revealed its ruins layer. Specifics are which ruins fell in the elder war, which town burned, and where a commander or wonder finder lies. Nothing new is stored.
- **`worldPastWords.ts`** turns the past into sentences made of segments. A name segment carries its `ref`, which the one router links. A concept word carries its `tooltipId`. There are three builders: `buildBeforeYouWoke` (four counted groups), `buildPlacePastLine` and `buildDeadPastLine`. Ages are words from `pastSpanLabel`, ruin counts are word bands, and an unbound token drops with one warning.
- **`world-past-content.ts`** holds the line tables:
  - founding frames;
  - war, burned-town and fallen-commander lines, each with a fogged twin;
  - elder-war and empire lines;
  - two legends for each of the 13 wonder subtypes, assigned so no two wonders share a legend while an unused one remains;
  - ruin lines by kind, where a fall clause renders only when `getPlacePast` holds a `fellInEventId`;
  - dead-role sentences.
- **UI:**
  - The **"Before you woke"** section is pinned in `ChroniclePanel`. Its four groups collapse, and the collapsed state persists in `threadbare.ui.chronicle.beforeYouWokeCollapsed`. The chronicle now mounts at minute one whenever the world has a past.
  - The **place line** sits under `LocationView`'s header.
  - The **dead person's line** sits on the `OverviewTab` Identity section, via `card.death.pastLine`.
  - All three render through the shared `PastLineText`.
- **Placeholders:** `{place.founded_ago}`, `{place.founder}`, `{ruin.empire}` and `{ruin.fall}` resolve through `proseEnrichment`, fed from the scene place's `getPlacePast`. `{ruin.fall}` strips rather than falls back, and the `{?has_ruin_fall}` / `{?no_ruin_fall}` conditionals guard a sentence built on it.
- **Tooltips:** five entries under `ui.*`. The plan named `location.*` and `agent.*`, but those prefixes resolve world-model kinds and live nodes, so a static concept filed there would need a world-model node.
- **Debug:** `await window.__DEBUG.getWorldPast({ fogged: true })` returns the player's view with `known` flags, plus the chapter as flat text.
- The interface row `world-past-reaches-the-chronicle` is no longer PARTIAL. Its reader half is registered.

## Evidence

On seed 42 medium (`?seeded`), the chronicle shows three empires, the Last Siege, 13 settling rows, two wars and zero wonders seen. One fallen commander is unnamed ("One of the commanders of the march of Shadow-Kept Shade fell in it."), because their resting hex is unseen. The other is named, because theirs is seen. The small world's burned town reads "This town burned four decades ago, when the march of Shadow-Kept Shade fought the hold of Witness Skyfield." Screenshots are in `Docs/evidence/thr-1656/`.
