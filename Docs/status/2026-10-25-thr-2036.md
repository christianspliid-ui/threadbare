# THR-2036 — the restless road ends in its story

**Shipped 2026-10-25.** On seed 42 (medium, the `restlessRoad.heavy.test.ts` "every nearby story told out" fixture) The First took the road to loc_97 for `encounter.anomaly.sap_of_ages`, walked 12 hexes, arrived "goal kept" at t31, and the story never started.

**Cause (traced).** The goal was on her board at loc_97 with the 2.5× arrival commitment, and still lost the live board. Which entry beat it depended on test order (module state shifts the forecast window): alone, an undertaking (`cell.create.pilgrim_way`, 0.29) beat the goal (0.106), because the story sits "above" her window (forecast 0.93, fit 0.1); after the first test, `rest_and_recover` five hops away (fatigue desire ×6.7, 1.44) beat the goal (1.06). Either way she left.

**Fix.** The road was the decision: it chose that story only after the window judged it drawable. A restless-road journey now carries `movementState.restlessRoad` (kept through movement, dropped by any re-route, cleared on arrival). On the first decision after arrival the board ranks that story's entry first whatever its score (`RESTLESS_ROAD_ARRIVAL_PRECEDENCE`, `src/data/movement-content.ts`; `false` reverts to the ordinary commitment). Scores are unchanged, so the trace still shows what the board thought; the entry carries `restlessRoadGoal: true` in `decision_board_comparison`. Only restless-road arrivals are affected; every other journey keeps `ARRIVAL_GOAL_COMMITMENT_MULTIPLIER`.

**Evidence.** Heavy test asserts she reaches the named place and starts the road's story within the run: 2/2 in file order and 1/1 alone. New `decisionBoard.test.ts` case: the road goal wins against an entry at 3× its committed score. `npm test` alone 25400/25400. `npm run gate`: typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, cli-smoke PASS; its parallel `npm test` hit the two known load timeouts (`wanderingHealerAftermathProgression`, `mutation-observability`, already in the impediment log), both pass alone (14/14).
