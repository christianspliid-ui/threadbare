## The half-and-half ratio broke — and that is the useful finding

`acquire` came in **8 full mad-libs to 5 wedged-token**, against `build` 7/9, `trade` 7/8, `assist` 6/9 and `lead` 5/9. It is the first family to land on the *mad-lib* side of half, and by the widest margin any family has shown.

Batch 5 called the ratio predictive; batch 6 called the variance real; this run puts the spread on **both** sides of half. So the honest statement for whoever scopes `explore` (30) is: the ratio estimates a slice well enough to choose between two families, and not well enough to replace reading a sample. The eight full mad-libs here cost roughly what their line count implies, which is exactly what the wedged-heavy families did *not*.

## Referent binding is now grep-able (impediment #563, occurrence 2)

Batch 6 discovered that `{their}`/`{they}` are **actor-bound** — `enrichProse` substitutes from the actor's pronouns with no notion of which noun the token modifies — and could only report it. Three more turned up here, all pre-existing:

- `spell_bargain` — *"The mage names `{their}` price"* → renders the **actor's** pronoun for the mage's price
- `forbidden_tome` — *"slip past `{their}` protections"* → the house's wards, rendered as the actor's
- `war_trophy` — *"{actor} must outmaneuver `{them}`"* → the rival, rendered as the actor

**What this batch adds is the tell:** a pronoun token whose *nearest preceding noun is not the actor*. In practice that means any `{their}`/`{them}`/`{they}` inside a clause whose subject is a scene NPC. That is readable in source, so the class no longer needs the render pass to *find* candidates — the render pass is still what confirms them, because the rendered line is grammatical either way and only the referent is wrong.

`the_rich_vein` also carried the `{they}`-plus-bare-verb agreement defect batches 3–6 each met (*"{they} carry a {adj} injury for their boldness"*), with a literal `their` in the same clause. Dropped the pronoun subject, per the now-settled rule that `{s}` cannot reach it.

## Vagueness 11 → 8, and the 8 are deliberate

Zero **evasive**, zero **nominalised**, zero **second-person** — the classes enforced at zero in every field class are clean.

The three `way` hits all sat in **outcome** prose, where the natural-indefinite set *is* enforced at zero, and all three rephrased at no cost (*"on the way in"* → *"on the descent"*; *"the way a hand finds"* → *"as a hand finds"*; *"remember it the way"* → *"remember it as"*).

The remaining 8 are all the comparative conjunction **`rather than`**, left exactly as batches 4–6 ruled: intensifiers are warn-level by construction (THR-899), count into no failure, and rewriting a correct comparative to dodge a warn-level substring is the damage that rule exists to prevent.
