# 2026-10-16 — THR-1900: map camera and controls

**Ticket:** [THR-1900](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1900). Slice "Camera and controls" of the [THR-1878 plan](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1878-map-lenses-and-chrome.md) (D6–D9). UI pillar, with a small Engine read on the view seam and Content words.

The map now has controls, keys and a sensible opening view.

- **Control cluster** (bottom-right of the map canvas, 16 px in, in `MapCornerChrome` so the Stage can hide it and the minimap with one condition): Zoom in (+) · Zoom out (−) · Centre on my First (carved `show-on-map`) · Show known land (carved `show-known-land`). Buttons are 40 px at 1080p and 45 px at 1440p. Each has an aria-label and a `ui.map.*` tooltip. At a limit a button is disabled with its reason in the tooltip ("Closest view", "Widest view", "No one to centre on yet"), and a press shakes.
- **Keys** come from one list, `MAP_COMMANDS`: PageUp / PageDown zoom, arrows pan a third of the canvas, Home centres on the First, F shows known land. They are inert in text fields, in composite widgets (tabs, lists, menus) and under anything that covers the map (drawer, Stage, ceremony, dialog, menu). Tooltips and in-map panels do not mute them. `TOP_BAR_KEYS` moved to `src/data/top-bar-keys.ts`. `mapKeys.coverage.test.ts` fails on any collision with the top bar or lens keys.
- **One centring zoom.** The handle gains `focusOn(hex)` at `FOCUS_ZOOM` 10, plus `zoomBy`, `showKnownLand`, `panBy` and a camera subscription. The eye icons, the seat button, the Stage's "Show on map", the active-locations bar and debug `gotoAgent` call `focusOn`. `RETINUE_EYE_ZOOM_SCALE` (k 20) is gone.
- **Opening view.** The run opens centred on the First at the known-land fit, clamped to k 6–10. It falls back to the seat, then the known land, then (fog off) the whole grid. ZOOM-05's k-15 close-up is retired. The labels' zoom state is now seeded from the transform actually applied, so the first frame draws the right tier. Measured: k 6.74 at 1920×1080 and k 9.13 at 2560×1440, both `centredOn: 'first'`.
- **Traces and debug.** `ui_map_camera_jump` is emitted on every programmatic jump. `getMapChrome()` gains `controls` and `opening`. Interface contract `explored-hexes-frame-opening-view` was added. The unrendered `AvatarHUD`, its test and its IA-manifest row were deleted. The UI-debt ratchet went down 4.
- **Key choice (veto invited).** PageUp/PageDown and the arrows replace THR-1831's `+`/`−`/WASD, because THR-1828 keeps `+`/`-` for speed and `W` for widen.
