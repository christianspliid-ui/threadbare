# THR-2020 — A liege pays for its vassal's raise

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** Deferred from THR-1974 (kill criterion "companies stay armyless"). Sworn free companies fielded an army on only 12–33% of their sworn days. A company that lost its host never raised again, because the wage step pays only armies that already exist.

**What changed.**
- **Engine.** The crown phase gets a new step, `fundRaises`, after the wages. A sworn vassal that keeps a standing army (`keepsStandingArmy`), is not a realm, fields none, and is refused a raise by its purse alone (`armyRefusal` = `purse`) gets an army raised by its liege. Like a self-raise, the liege must hold the raise plus a first day above its `LIEGE_WAGE_RESERVE_DAYS` reserve, and is charged `ARMY_CREATION_GOLD_COST`. The new army is stamped `liegePaidTick` before it aims, so its first objective is picked for the liege (`spawnArmy` opt `liegePaid`). The vassal gets at most one such raise per `LIEGE_RAISE_MIN_GAP_DAYS` (5), recorded in `sworn_to.liegeRaisedTick`. Switch: `LIEGE_RAISE_ENABLED`. Trace: `oath_raise_funded`.
- **Realm vassals are excluded.** With realms included, seeds 42 and 99 broke kill criterion 3: on some days more than a quarter of realm-vassal armies fought for their liege. A realm vassal raises from its own land.
- **Measure.** `measure:crown-wages -- --debug-companies` now prints each company's refusal gate per day.

**Why seed 7 is below half.** On 51 of its 53 armyless sworn days, the gate is `no_commander`: the company has no member left with the Iron tier to lead a host, and no coin fixes that. Filed as [THR-2027](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2027).

**Known gap, filed.** Seed 42 shows 10 ticks of "march on the paymaster" (0 with the raise off). The cause is one company army. It came into its liege's pay (THR-1974's wage step) while still holding an objective it had picked under its own banner, and it marched on until the daily re-aim. It never attacked, because siege and battle start read the commander. A take-up re-aim was tried and dropped: it did not clear the case, and it skipped the siege and agenda guards. Filed as [THR-2026](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2026).
