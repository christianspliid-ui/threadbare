# THR-2005 — a notable agenda that fails late now stays on the Notables list after it fails

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** Failed compositions were garbage-collected `COMPOSITION_FAILED_RETENTION_TICKS` (20) after their *earliest* activation — after they began, not after they failed. A notable agenda (or rival scheme) that ran longer than 20 ticks and then failed was pruned on the tick it failed, so the Notables panel never showed its "Abandoned designs on …" row.

**Change.** `ActiveComposition` gains an additive `failedAtTick?: number` (`src/types/gameState.ts`). Both failure write sites stamp it: notable-agenda counter-play (`src/engine/notableAgendas.ts`) and rival-scheme counter-play (`src/engine/orchestrator.ts`). The GC in `src/engine/phaseComposition.ts` anchors failed retention on `failedAtTick`, falling back to the old earliest-activation anchor for records written before this change. Completed compositions are unchanged.

**Evidence.** New `phaseComposition.test.ts` cases: a composition that fails 30 ticks after it began is still present 10 ticks later, and is dropped once retention has passed since it failed. `notableAgendas.test.ts` and `rivalSchemes.test.ts` assert the failure tick is recorded. `npm run gate` code track (test, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, 30-tick CLI smoke) all PASS. UI and Content: N/A — `NotablesPanel` already renders failed rows.
