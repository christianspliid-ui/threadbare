# THR-1379 — grievance, grudge and heat are seated in the glossary

**2026-09-02 · docs-only.** THR-1298 shipped the reactive loop across seven slices and closed the same morning. The three words that loop is *made of* — grievance, grudge, heat — appeared in the rulebook, in constants, in prose tables and on the mortal sheet, but not in the glossary that arbitrates terminology when sources disagree. This seats them.

## Where they landed, and why not Graph

All three go in the **Agents** shard, not Graph. The proposal left the choice open ("Agents, probably also Graph for the edge semantics"), and the shard already carries the precedent: `mentors (edge)` is an Agents entry, while Graph holds the architecture-level terms (`Node`, `Edge`, `EdgeType`, the position model). `hostile_to` is a relationship between agents, not a fact about how the graph is built — it belongs with the agents it relates. Splitting `grudge`'s definition across two shards would have put the reconciliation in one file and the mechanism in another.

## The judgement call, resolved rather than forked

The proposal flagged one thing for the executor to settle: *"grudge" already appears in canon prose for band contests ("There is blood between them and …") — reconcile the two senses.*

They are not two senses. Verified in the tree:

- Band opposition writes `hostile_to` with `cause: 'group_engagement'` (`bandOpposition.ts:424,447`).
- A cooled grievance writes the **same** edge with `cause: 'grievance_cooled'` (`grievanceLifecycle.ts:190,221`).
- Both go through the **same single writer**, `writeGrudge` in `grudgeEdge.ts` — which exists precisely because the grievance lane refused to add a second writer with its own spelling.
- The mortal sheet renders both through one clause map: `GRUDGE_CAUSE_CLAUSES` keys `group_engagement` and `grievance_cooled` alongside `mentorship_break` and `excommunicated`.

So the entry says one edge with a provenance field, and records that the band sense and the personal sense are the same mechanism read at two actor scales — bands write grudges between groups, grievances between individuals, and the edge does not care which.

## Two anchors did not survive checking

Every code anchor was grepped against the tree rather than inferred from the plan doc. Two failed and were corrected before commit:

- **`openGrievance` does not exist.** The mint-side entry points are `GrievanceSeed` / `resolveGrievanceDisposition` / `findActiveGrievanceEdge`.
- **The Heat anchors were vague.** Now `decayGrievance` and `grievanceHeat01` — the decay pass and the board-weight reader — instead of a prose gesture at the lifecycle file.

`GRUDGE_PROVENANCE` was kept: it is module-private in `undertakingMotive.ts` rather than exported, which is not a reason to omit it — it is the symbol that makes provenance a closed set, and the entry explains why (a motive gate that fails open on a misspelling is worse than one that fails closed on an unknown enum).
