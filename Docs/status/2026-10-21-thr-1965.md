**Shipped 2026-10-21.** Three surfaces now give their reasons. The Notables list says why it shrinks. The Doom clock says why The First's road sits under it. The doom-echo complication says what slows the doom.

**What changed.**

The lead is condensed to "A slow five-stage clock to the world's unmaking." and "Open it to see what it is building toward." is dropped (the hover already appends the next-stage hint). The result is 197 characters.
* **Doom-echo prose.** The four `complication.worsening_convergence.doom_echo` lines now name the stake and the mandates that slow it. Effects and ids are unchanged.
