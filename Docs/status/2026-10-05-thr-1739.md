# 2026-10-05 — THR-1739: encounter summaries read as player prose

**Ticket:** [THR-1739](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1739) · **Project:** Encounter Experience · **Plan:** the ticket body (self-scoped; no plan doc) · **Pillars:** Content (61 `description` rewrites); Engine (a `check:encounter` register gate, pinned); UI N/A (the Codex, story beat, actions block and veil fallback read the same field, so the census covers them; THR-688 rule C, no browser evidence owed).

## Why

Christian, on The Granary Riot (2026-10-05): the summary *"looks more like a prompt than an actual prose text."* THR-1728 stopped the veil showing `description`. But the field still reaches players in the Codex entry, the story-beat modal and the actions block. The factory's package format and prompts called it "designer voice for the Codex", so drafts filled it with a design spec: step counts, bands, "the mortal", payoffs, sequels.

## What shipped

- **61 descriptions rewritten** as one or two narrator-mode sentences of situation and stake. Census at pickup: 57 tripped a marker. Four more read as spec and tripped none:
  - the slice's missed-branch sequel;
  - the apotheosis capstone;
  - two shell proofs.
  - Scope: every template defined under `src/data/encounters/` plus every `encounter.*` template (the three shell proofs live in `effect-shell-proof-templates.ts`).
  - The 41 matching package JSONs were edited in the same pass, so package and module agree. `encounterPackage.test.ts`'s bell-tower round-trip still deep-equals.
- **Gate:** `src/data/content-eval/descriptionVoice.ts` has 16 pinned markers: step count, band + job/test, test for a band, "the mortal", "the agent", plants / earns / wins the trust, sequel, self-named "encounter", missed branch, `Reach:`, by query, `#tag`, `$binding`, and a Reach/persona in parentheses. `check:encounter` reports a hit in its register block, so `--all` and `--package` both fail designer voice.
- **Factory docs define the field as player prose:**
  - format card § Description, with a wrong/right pair;
  - the draft, implementation and systems prompts;
  - the nudge-authoring spec's register table.

## Judgement calls (veto welcome)

- **Scope stays on encounters.** Divine, hex and NPC verbs say "the mortal" or "the agent" because those words name the target of an intervention. Those descriptions are tooltips, not scene prose. The gate runs only inside `check:encounter`.
- **"earns" and "plants" are banned outright** in encounter descriptions, as the ticket asked. A narrator rarely needs either word in a two-sentence summary.
- **The mortal stays unnamed** ("someone", "a stranger", "the traveler") rather than `{actor}`. The Codex prints `description` without a mortal to fill a token.
