# THR-1478 — one header block above the prose; the difficulty and the forecast become marks

**Shipped 2026-09-12.** Pillars: UI (Engine N/A, Content N/A).

## What the director found

Attended chat, 2026-09-12, first sitting on the [THR-1220](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1220)
slice, on The Swindled Family's scene screen. Two screenshots, four asks:

> we have redundancy in the interface. please merge these two into one. i think the right placing is above the prose.
>
> remove the word forecast. make the forecast score "uncertain" into an icon like a dice … lets stay away from numbers.
>
> remove the objective (hear them out). it is noise.
> find a way to iconify the difficulty (fair) without a text. use our color coding and an icon if possible.
>
> Image 2; the balance;: rethink how this is shown or if it needs to be shown.

The redundancy was structural, not cosmetic: `ContextStrip` (above the prose) and
`nudge-test-panel` (below it) each drew **the agent's portrait and the step's reach**,
on opposite sides of the fiction, from two different components.

## The merge, and why it needed the hand lifted

The marks now render **inside** `ContextStrip`, which is the block that already owns the
portrait, the name and the location — so the stage contributes marks into the veil's row
rather than owning a header of its own. `ContextStrip` takes two optional slots
(`inlineMarks`, `belowBlock`); the aftermath and the legacy choice path pass neither and
draw exactly what they drew before.

**The non-obvious part is the state.** The merged header shows the *live* forecast, which
moves as cards toggle — and the cards render below the prose, inside `NudgePhaseShell`.
Two subtrees reading one selection means one state, so `useNudgeHand` is now owned by
`EncounterVeil` and passed down; the shell takes an optional `hand` prop and calls the hook
with `undefined` when a host supplies one (a conditional *argument*, not a conditional
call, onto the hook's documented fail-soft path). Had the header kept its own hand, the die
above the prose and the cards below it would have disagreed silently — which is why the
test for it computes the expected tier from `forecastWithNudges` and asserts the rendered
die matches, rather than merely asserting a die is present.

The reading itself lives in one new component, `NudgeStageHeader`, in two exported pieces
(`NudgeReadingMarks`, `NudgeBalance`). `NudgePhaseShell` still draws both on the standalone
path — the meeting beats mount it with no context strip above them — so there is **one
implementation of the reading and two placements of it**, the same shape THR-972 established
with `renderMotiveIntro`.

## Two new drawn icons, and what each channel carries

**`ForecastDie`** — five tiers, five die faces, one pip through five. The pip count *is* the
rung on the `doomed … fated` ladder, not a quantity, which is what keeps it inside Law 13:
it is the same ordinal word vocabulary drawn as a shape. Colour is the existing
`FORECAST_TIER_COLORS` ladder. **The separate `OddsPips` row under the old word is gone** —
two magnitude readings of one number sitting beside each other is precisely the redundancy
the ticket was filed about. The cards keep their pips, where fine resolution is what a player
actually compares.

**`DifficultyScales`** — the `⚖` character became a drawn balance whose **beam tilts per
band** (`gentle -12°`, `fair 0°`, `steep 11°`, `severe 21°`). This is the part that was not
optional: with the word gone, colour would have been the band's *only* at-a-glance channel,
and Law 11 puts shape first. A glyph cannot tilt, and a rotated `⚖` reads as a rendering
fault — hence an SVG. The **frame survives the word**: *"the difficulty cant stand alone"*
was a director ruling about the reading, not about the word, and a lone mark floating in a
row of chips is what it was against.

Both icons are colour-agnostic (`currentColor`) so the caller applies the ladder and the icons
stay pure shapes that take theme colours — the icon set must not import from a component
module, and `FORECAST_TIER_COLORS` lives in `shared/CardFace`.

**I looked at them before shipping.** Rendering the generators into a page and screenshotting
it caught a real defect an assertion never would have: the pans were rotating *with* the beam,
so the icon read as scales falling apart rather than as a balance weighing. Pans now
counter-rotate by `-tilt` about their own attachment point and hang plumb. A second pass
widened the pans and scaled the stroke with the size (clamped 1.1–2.2), because at the 14px
legend size the pans had dissolved into hooks.

## The Balance — disposition

**Dropped as a labelled per-sentence concept**, per the ticket's recommendation (process.md
rule 4). Every factor line already states its reading in its own colour — green for, red
against, unmarked context — so `ui.nudge_factors` was a rulebook entry attached to content
that did not need one, firing on every line. The colour vocabulary it taught moved into the
stage's **first-contact legend** (Law 12), where a vocabulary belongs, dismissable and
remembered across sessions (Law 51, `threadbare.ui.nudgeReadingLegendSeen`). **The registry
entry was not deleted** — it is that legend item's tooltip, so the one tooltip registry keeps
its concept (Law 17).

The legend draws the *same* marks the row does, at legend scale. A key whose symbols differ
from the surface's is worse than no key.

## Law 15, stated rather than hidden

Law 15 says pips "annotate words, never replace them", and on this surface the die **replaces**
the tier word. The binding law here is Law 11, not Law 15 — a die face is not a pip row, and
Law 11's requirement for a glyph carrying meaning alone is that it state its reading in words
one hover away, which the die does (`aria-label`, `title`, `ui.nudge_forecast`, and the legend).
Recording it because the director's ask and a standing law point in different directions on
this one surface, and that should be visible rather than quietly resolved.

### The browser caught a defect the tests could not

Stacked under the die, `was Uncertain` is **wider than the die it belongs to**, so the first
nudge grew the header row's height and lifted the die out of alignment with the name and the
location: the block visibly jumped on the very click that was supposed to draw the eye to the
die. It is now inline beside the die, and the fix is measured, not asserted — die top and
block height are **identical before and after** the nudge (153px / 142px). No render test
would have seen this; jsdom has no layout.

The re-verification also re-ran impediment #1027's stale-transform trap, and my first probe
fell into the half of it the impediment warns about: I matched on a **comment** marker, which
vite strips, and read `false` on a tree that was in fact fresh. Re-probed on code symbols
(`gap: 7`, one surviving `flexDirection: 'column'`) and it came back clean.

**Two anti-vacuity guards fired during authoring and changed the fixture**, which is the
part worth keeping:

1. The "objective is gone" arm passed against a fixture that authored **no** `purposeLine` —
   it was asserting the absence of nothing. The fixture now authors one.
2. The live-forecast arm's guard rejected a capability-less actor: measured base tier → tier
   with all five cards played was `{}` doomed → doomed and `heart: 6` doomed → doomed (the
   capability sigmoid still near its floor). `heart: 12` gives perilous → favorable, so the
   hand has somewhere to move the die to. Without the guard the die could have been wired to
   a stale second hand and the arm would still have gone green.

A third fixture correction: `buildNudgePhaseModel` reads essence from `gameState.essencePool`,
not from the veil's `essence` prop — with no pool, four of five cards were withheld and the
fifth came back `essence_unavailable`, so nothing would toggle.

The merge claim is asserted as **composition**, not presence: containment (the marks are
inside the one header block, so they cannot be a second strip that merely sits above the
prose) and document order (`compareDocumentPosition`). A surface drawing the marks in a
second block below the prose would satisfy "the die is on screen".

`encounterVeilLaws.test.ts`'s four file-list sweeps (Laws 30, 27, 41, 45) now name
`NudgeStageHeader` alongside the veil and the shell. The ceremonial tokens and the factor
polarity colours moved into it; a guard whose file list stops where the code went is a guard
with a hole in exactly the wrong place.

## One finding for the director, not fixed here

The two factor lines on this exact screen read **`threads shifting`** (a bare fragment) and
**`Vara is oracle in eye.`** (broken English — the capability tier is slotted into an
`is {tier} in {reach}` template with no article). Both come from the factor-line *producer*
and render byte-identically before and after this change, so they are pre-existing, not a
regression. They are also **more visible now**: the merge moved the factor lines above the
prose, so the first sentence a player reads on the scene screen is one of these. Filed as
[THR-1494](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1494) rather than fixed inline — it is a
content/engine producer defect, a different pillar from this ticket's.
