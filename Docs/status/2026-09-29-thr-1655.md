# 2026-09-29 — THR-1655: the player sees who matters here

**Ticket:** [THR-1655](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1655) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1630-notables-and-ties.md` § UI pillar, § Content pillar items 3–4 · **Pillars:** Engine (pure selector, card field, tooltip arm); Content (bond words, four tooltips); UI (sheet, settlement page, Notables panel).

## What was wrong

S1 (THR-1630) seeded kin, friends and rivals, and S2 (THR-1654) seeded a notable in every settlement with a holding, an old quarrel, a secret or favour and local agendas. The player saw none of it by name. The sheet listed a bond as a name and a trust word, and `topBonds` dropped the basis. No settlement page named its notable. The Notables panel mixed rulers' agendas with local ones.

## What shipped

- **The sheet.** `BondsTab` Relationships puts the bond word before the name as a chip: kin, friend, rival and the rest, from `BOND_BASIS_WORDS` through the alias table (`bondBasisWord`). Each chip carries an `agent.bond.*` tooltip. The name is now a link. `card.topBonds` carries `targetId` and `basis`. The `known` gate is unchanged. A basis with no word renders no chip and warns once.
- **The settlement page.** `settlementNotable.getSettlementNotable(graph, locationId, { familiarityMap })` finds the living, resident seeded notable. It returns clauses read live from the edges `seedNotables` wrote: `holds` (`owns`), `at_odds_with` (`hostile_to`, cause `old_quarrel`), `knows_secret_of` (unrevealed) and `is_owed_by` (live `owes_favor`). A clause whose edge is gone, or whose other end is dead, is dropped. The secret shows only once the player knows the notable at `known`+ (`secretWithheld` reports it). `NotableLine` renders a **notable** chip (`agent.notable`), the name and one sentence, with every noun a link. It heads Inhabitants in both layouts. **Every seeded settlement has Places, so its page uses the Places layout, where the flat Inhabitants list never renders.** The line sits above the Places list there. Rendering it only in the flat layout would have shipped an invisible surface.
- **The Notables panel.** Two groups, **Rulers** and **Local**, each with a count, split by the engine's `agendaFlags.local`. The panel now scrolls inside its dropdown (max 70vh): a busy world's 18 rows ran off the viewport. Each row's name and target are on their own truncating lines: the `ListRow` primitives are inline spans, so side by side they ran together and under the family label. Both defects predate this ticket; they were fixed because this ticket owns the surface now.
- **Tooltips:** `agent.bond.kin`, `agent.bond.friendship`, `agent.bond.rivalry` and `agent.notable` in `src/data/agent-concept-tooltips.ts`. `resolveTooltip`'s `agent.*` arm answers them before the live-agent lookup, so a context-free `<Tooltip id>` resolves.
- **Debug:** `await window.__DEBUG.getSettlementNotable(locationId, { unfogged? })`.
- **Interface map:** `seeded-notable-reaches-the-settlement-page` added, **LIVE**.
