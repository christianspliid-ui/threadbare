# THR-1409 — one owner per worldgen constant, and a tuning panel that tells the truth

**Shipped 2026-09-04.** Engine + UI. [THR-1409](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1409/three-worldgen-constants-are-declared-twice-with-different-values-the).

## What was wrong

Three terrain constants were declared in two modules with **different values in each**, and the two copies had disjoint consumers — which is why the divergence never surfaced as a conflict:

| constant | `terrainPipeline/types.ts` | `worldGenData.ts` | in force |
| -- | -- | -- | -- |
| `RIVER_MIN_LENGTH` | 5 | 4 | **4** |
| `LAKE_SIZE_MAX` | 6 | 5 | **5** |
| `GREAT_LAKE_SIZE_MAX` | 15 | 12 | **12** |

The generators (`riverGeneration.ts`, `lakeGeneration.ts`) read `worldGenData.ts`. The CMS tuning panel rendered `terrainPipeline/types.ts`. So the surface a designer opens to change a number displayed values no world had ever been generated with, and edits to it landed on a copy nothing reads — an NFP #1 (Tunability) failure of the sharpest kind: the tuning surface silently absorbed the change.

The sweep the ticket required turned up a **fourth** duplicate the finding had not named: `TEMP_ALTITUDE_PENALTY`, declared in both `terrainPipeline/types.ts` and `worldgen/constants.ts`. Same value (0.3) in both, so the row was accidentally truthful — but it was still credited to a module the climate pass does not import.

## What changed

- `terrainPipeline/types.ts` no longer declares any of the four. Each now has exactly one declaration, in the module its consumers import, with a comment at the old site naming the owner and why.
- The CMS panel's four rows re-point at those owners — `worldGenData.ts` for the river/lake limits, `worldgen/constants.ts` for the altitude penalty — with their `sourceFile` and `usedBy` attributions corrected to match.
- **Values preserved, not adopted.** The live 4 / 5 / 12 survive; the divergent 5 / 6 / 15 are gone. Which value wins was a game-feel call the ticket had already decided, and this change makes no tuning decision.

## The pin

Two guards, because the failure had two independent halves — and each was falsified in both directions before shipping, not merely observed green.

`src/engine/__tests__/worldgenConstantOwnership.test.ts` intersects the **runtime namespace keys** of the three worldgen constant modules and fails on any name declared by more than one. Runtime keys rather than parsed source, so it compares exactly the declarations a consumer can import a value from. Falsified by re-declaring `RIVER_MIN_LENGTH` in `terrainPipeline/types.ts`: the guard named the collision and its two modules.

`src/components/CMS/__tests__/configManagerWorldgenConstants.test.tsx` renders the real `ConfigManager` and asserts each row's painted value is the live constant — plus an absence arm for the four numbers the panel used to show. Falsified by re-pointing the `RIVER_MIN_LENGTH` row at a literal 5: both the presence and absence arms fired, reproducing the reported defect verbatim (*expected 5 to be 4*).

Neither guard subsumes the other: the first would miss a panel re-pointed at a fourth module, the second would miss a fresh duplicate nothing renders yet.

## Worldgen output is unchanged

Measured, not asserted. A terrain/river/lake readout on **four** seed × map-size combinations, captured on `origin/main` (`7fd33ed7`) and on the fix branch, is byte-identical in every one:

| seed / map | tiles | river hexes | lake hexes |
| -- | -- | -- | -- |
| 42 / medium | 768 | 28 | 0 |
| 42 / large | 1728 | 10 | 0 |
| 99 / medium | 768 | 31 | 7 |
| 99 / large | 1728 | 54 | 14 |

Seed 99 carries the lakes, which is why it is in the set — seed 42 generates none at either size, so a comparison on 42 alone would not have exercised `LAKE_SIZE_MAX` at all.

## What this did not fix

`src/engine/terrainPipeline/` contains only `types.ts` and `__tests__/`. **There is no pipeline** — no passes, no orchestrator, no caller — and after this change its remaining 22 constants are still rendered by the CMS panel and read by no generator. Those 22 mostly have no live counterpart to re-point at: the terrainPipeline design models continents and fault lines, and the live noise-field pipeline in `worldgen/` models neither. Deciding what that panel group should show is a design call rather than a de-duplication, so it is filed as [THR-1418](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1418/terrainpipeline-has-no-pipeline-22-more-cms-tuning-rows-render-a) rather than silently widened into this ticket.

The duplicated *type* names `RiverPath` and `WorldGenData` stay. They are structurally different interfaces in modules with disjoint consumers, not copies of one another, and they carry no value that can drift numerically — the reason is recorded in the guard.
