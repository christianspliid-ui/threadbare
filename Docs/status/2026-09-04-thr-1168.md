# THR-1168 — the encounter tension reveal is retired; the director said no audio

**2026-09-04 · UI · Deferral · Project: Encounter Experience**

## What this closes

THR-1168 carried a two-member wire-or-retire call, filed by THR-1167 at closeout for the "built ahead
of its wiring" half of its fork. **Member 2 (the registration cue) shipped retired on 2026-08-18** on
mechanical grounds. **Member 1 (the Moment 1 tension reveal) was the open half**, and it was never an
executor's call to make: whether committing a hand of nudge cards should carry ~1.6 seconds of held
breath before the outcome lands is a feel question with no right outcome to test against.

It went to the director in game terms and came back in three words — *"thr 1168. no audio please."*
`human gate satisfied via chat review 2026-09-04`. This run is the deletion that verdict authorises.

## What was deleted

The module had had **no live consumer at all**. THR-1049 removed the D1/D2 encounter screen; THR-1167
removed `ThreadOverlay`, whose only import of the hook had been type-only — an importer count reports
that identically to a real edge, which is why it survived one sweep. What was left was `useThreadReveal`
as the sole caller of all three cue functions, reachable from its own tests alone.

| Deleted | Was |
|---|---|
| `src/audio/encounterSoundDesign.ts` | `beginTensionReveal` / `playResolveNote` / `endTensionReveal`, the pure helpers and the test seams |
| `src/hooks/useThreadReveal.ts` | the six-phase beat clock (commit 60 + inhale 380 + draw 520 + taut 420 + resolve 240 ≈ 1.62s) |
| `src/audio/__tests__/encounterSoundDesign.test.ts` | 11 synthesis + fail-soft cases |
| `src/audio/__tests__/encounterSoundDesignWiring.test.tsx` | Moment 1's 6 wiring cases |
| every `ENCOUNTER_*` constant in `audioConstants.ts` | levels, timings, filter corners, cello root, envelope, nine-reach semitone map |

A retired contract's tests go with it — green tests on a dead contract are the pathology the interface
map exists to kill, and after this deletion nothing remained for them to assert.

## Where the design went, rather than being destroyed

The ticket's own standing constraint was that deleting these would destroy authored spec recoverable
only from git history (NFP #6). That is answered by keeping the design as **spec rather than code**:

- **Canonical UI spec §3.3** (`Docs/plans/2026-05-04-encounter-ui-canonical.md`) keeps its full
  three-cue table — every timing, every level, the three spec-named intervals — and its retired-surface
  note now records that the implementation is gone and this table is the record. The note previously
  read as *built but unrouted*, which stopped being true today.
- **`audioConstants.ts`** carries a consolidated retirement note in the convention Member 2 established:
  what the numbers were, why the ruling went the way it did, and that revival needs the ruling revisited
  first. Both members are now described in one place instead of two half-notes.
- **`Docs/plans/wiring-checklist.md`**'s `encounterSoundDesign` row is rewritten to RETIRED IN FULL. It
  had promised Moment 1 as live wiring — a checklist that describes a deleted module as a seam is worse
  than one with no row at all.
