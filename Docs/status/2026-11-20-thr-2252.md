# THR-2252 — The player can read the race

**Shipped 2026-11-20.** Ticket 2 of 4 of `Docs/plans/2026-11-19-thr-2246-the-player-can-read-the-race.md` (Content and UI pillars). It puts on screen the reading that THR-2251 added to the engine.

- **Rail.** The folded Mandate row trades its trend word for the race word while the race is shown: "Goal · Lands first" / "Goal · Doom lands first" / "Goal · Close finish" (`stalled` reads "Doom lands first"). The word carries the new `ui.race` hover. While hidden it keeps "Goal · {trend}". At 1920×1080 "Goal · Doom lands first" does not truncate (scrollWidth 140 = clientWidth 140).
- **Mandate block.** The forecast sentence takes the flavour line's slot. It uses the same size, is not italic, and carries the `ui.race` hover, so the block's height is unchanged.
- **Mandate dialog.** Order: forecast, then the lever line, then the flavour line. A chapter lever links the mortal (`EntityLink`, Law 21) and puts the sphere hover on the sphere word. Stay offers "Stay it" through a new `onStay` (DialogHost: close, then open the god's page, the Doom dialog's handler). A held Stay shows "You are holding it back." with no button.
- **Doom dialog.** The forecast and the chapter lever sit above THR-2240's Stay answer. The rate-blind "Remaining" row is dropped while the race is shown (PC-6).
- **Warm recap.** A new `race` line kind: "While you were away, how the age ends came into view." (first sight) or "… shifted." followed by the forecast. It is kept after `doom` and shown last.
- **View model.** `selectRaceView` / `selectRaceLever` (godSheet), `MandateRowView.race`, `GodRailRow.summaryTooltipId`, the god-rail stamp keyed on the shown race state. New `src/components/Game/RaceForecast.tsx`.
- **Evidence.** Bash-vite on :5263 at 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`. `getRace()` read `hidden` at load. After `__DEBUG.tick(300)` it read shown = computed = `doom_first`, 15 samples, lever chapter/mind/The First, and the rail read "Goal · Doom lands first". With `forceRace`, the four states each showed their sentence on the block and in the dialog. The lever showed in `doom_first`, `close` and `stalled`, and was absent in `mandate_first`.
