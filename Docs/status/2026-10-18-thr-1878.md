# THR-1878 — Lenses I: the lens system, the Threads lens and the Dominion pressure line

**Shipped 2026-10-18.** Slice Lenses I of the map-lenses plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1878-map-lenses-and-chrome.md` § D1–D5, D11). The map has two lenses, one at a time, none by default: Dominion (key 1, as built) and Threads (key 2). A new hex lens is one `MAP_LENSES` row, one builder and its `MAP_MARKERS` rows; it writes no layer code (Law 58).

- **One list drives everything.** `MAP_LENSES` rows carry a `layer` family; `LENS_SELECTORS` (`views/lenses/lensView.ts`) is total over `MapLensId`; HexMapV2's `LENS_LAYER_FACTORIES` is keyed on the family. GameView and GameMap no longer branch on the literal `'dominion'`. Lens state (`activeLens`, `lensSuppressed`) lives in `GameScreenState`.
- **Threads.** `threadsLens.ts` lights each mortal threaded at court the_first / retinue / watched (dormant stays dark), arrows a moving mortal to its destination's place-tier hex, and names them in hover lines ("Maren Ashfold, your First, is here, on her way to Hesk Crossing.") built as ref segments. Nothing lands on unexplored land (`views/knowledge.ts`). The agent layer draws each lit mortal one step up (×1.25) with a name tag.
- **The generic layer.** `HexLensLayer` dims every land hex not lit at Dominion's Foreign alpha (by import) and draws marks through `LENS_MARK_RENDERERS`; an unknown kind draws a fallback badge and warns once. No lens object is pickable, so the hex click is unchanged (D11).
- **The bar** is rebuilt on `Tabs kind="toggle" allowNone` (which gained optional `tooltipId` / `testId`); the legend is a glass `Panel` whose mark row derives from `MAP_MARKERS`. Its limits are in rem so the longest legend fits at 1440p (Law 57).
- **Dominion pressure clause.** `dominionHexLineVM` gains an optional `pressure` line from the lending place's sphere scores ("Pressed by a power of Entropy; your Life holds here.").
- **Debug:** `setMapLens` / `getMapLens` / `setLensSuppressed`; trace `ui_map_lens_changed`; selector `lens.hex`.

Evidence: `?view=game&seeded&size=medium` on a worktree Vite, Playwright at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440 (the WebGL dim painted): Threads lit 1 hex with one medallion, `layersActive` shows exactly one `lens:*` at each step of dominion → threads → off, suppression hides the layer and keeps the button pressed, hover line "Kael Thornweaver, your First, is here.", console 0 errors. Not built here (later slices): Trouble and Trade (THR-1898), lens zoom-matrix rows, routing the hover names (the hover tier is pointer-events: none), the name tags joining label collision.
