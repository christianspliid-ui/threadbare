### 2026-10-03 — THR-1707: developer text no longer reaches players in the Codex or a mortal's panel

**The bug.** Cold playtest round 2: the Codex showed "Engine bridge: creates a `narrowed` `knows_clue_of` edge…" under an Effect heading with a WIRED · ENGINE badge, and a mortal's panel read "Encounter Pool / Decision: Queue Movement / Heading: Temple / Idle reason". Testers called it "computer settings, not story". The Effect block is the THR-610 catalog audit, for whoever builds the game. The planner rows were the decision fields THR-1008 had left ungated.

**The fix.** `CodexDetailPanel` shows the Effect block and its badge only in a dev build or under the designer view. A player keeps the entry's Mechanics summary. `EncounterDecisionPanel` in `ThreadDetailView` now reads, in normal play, as one sentence under "On their mind": "Heading for the ruins", "Resting after what they have just done", "Working toward a larger plan". The Decision, Heading and Idle reason rows moved behind the designer view with the funnel and scores. "What they are weighing" and its foreshadowing prose are unchanged.

**Evidence.** `codexTechnicalEffectGate.test.tsx` renders every Codex entry with an effect line under a stubbed production build. None shows the block, the badge, "Engine bridge" or "wired". The designer view and a dev build both bring the block back. `ThreadDetailView.test.tsx` checks the sentence is there and the planner rows are gone in normal play, and the reverse under the designer view. Browser-verify substitution: jsdom-render. This was an unattended run with no dev server it could start, and the Done-when is about which text is present or absent.
