**The aftermath asked the player to choose, offered one option, and never said what the option meant.** Christian's review of `encounter.slice.unsafe_bridge` caught three defects in one paragraph; the third was content and stays with THR-973. **On the one-option prompt I did not do what the ticket suggested, and the reason is the interesting half.** The ticket proposed folding an `effects: []` reaction into the footer as a bare acknowledgment. So a lone reaction is *not* an inert control, and Law 25 was never about the button. It was about the **prompt**, which posed a decision that did not exist.
