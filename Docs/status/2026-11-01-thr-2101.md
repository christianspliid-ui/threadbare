# THR-2101 — Art-led pass, slice 5: the dialogs

**Shipped 2026-11-01.** UI Visual Overhaul — Design System v1. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-11-01-art-led-dialogs.md`.

**Faces (S5.1).** Every dialog about a mortal shows that mortal's face through the one knowledge-gated path (`mortalFace`). The Chapter Ledger's rows carry a chip face beside the mortal's name (`ledgerRowFace`, resolved for the shown page only), and a chapter opens on a 3:4 face at `--dialog-subject-portrait-w` (7rem at 1920, 9rem at 2560/3440; mirrored in `layoutContract.ts`). A mortal who has died keeps the designed tile under the archived name. The receipt puts the target's medallion (mortal, place or faction) before the sentence that names them; the choice shows the chooser; a beat about a mortal shows their face in the hero medallion. The court's seats, picker and The First draw through the same path (`courtFaces`); the raw `<img>` and gradient fallback are gone. All descriptors are built in `DialogHost` (`engine/views/dialogDressing.ts`); no dialog reads the graph.

**Heading shapes (S5.2).** `Modal.Header` takes an optional `icon`. `DIALOG_HEADING_ICONS` (`view-content.ts`): Ledger `chapter-ledger`, receipt `receipt`, choice `dilemma`, settings `settings`, court holdings `place` / `relic`. The court, The First, Apex, Inner, Outer and the beat eyebrow have no fitting idea (`DIALOG_NO_IDEA_HEADINGS`, for THR-2104). The warm-start wait draws the current season's shape; Settings' ⏱ / 📌 are now `waiting` / `held`.

**The receipt's changes (S5.3).** A change with a category draws `ICON_FOR_CONSEQUENCE`; a change naming a relic shows its painting as a 40px tile (`receiptChangeArt` → `getAttachmentArtUrl`).

**The court in sentences (S5.4).** "Position / Minimum tier", "Cost", "Insufficient … Required / Available" and "Structure Bonus:" are sentences (`COURT_WORDS`, `ui-content.ts`) with the essence shape and its tooltip. A title's bonuses and weaknesses are word chips (`TITLE_BONUS_WORDS`, `scry-content.ts`), never counts or numerals. The two "◇ Coming Soon" rows are quiet slots.

**The beat's fallback (S5.5).** A granted card with no template no longer prints its raw id; the row is dropped and the warning stays (no catalog entry means no card name to print).

**Debug.** `openHomedSurface` now opens `chapter-ledger`, `divine-court`, `choice-set` (a fixture choice on The First) and `ascendant-beat` (`forceOffering` + enter), each returning `dressing = { face, headingIcons, quietSlots }`.

**Not live-verified:** the receipt, world message and warm start keep hint-only levers (each needs a real act, a doom stage or a reload); the receipt is covered by the jsdom test. An offering beat names no mortal, so it reports `face: 'none'` by design.

**Review gate (parked after round 2).** Fixed after round 1: the beat face now reads the introduction beat's bound id (GameView narrows `beatSubjectRef` to factions); title chip tooltips no longer promise effects the engine never applies (that gap is THR-2121). Fixed after round 2, awaiting the next review cycle: the seat sentence no longer doubles "The"; the beat lever checks `forceOffering`'s result and anchors on the beat modal. Still open: no production beat binds a mortal (introduction beats bind factions and cultures), so the beat's face never draws and the plan's "beat reports a face" Done-when cannot be met as written.
