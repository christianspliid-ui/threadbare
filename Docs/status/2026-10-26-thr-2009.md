# THR-2009 — The Return's Reach gates see a Reach: Transcendence opens, Sacrifice keeps its Shadow ban

**Shipped 2026-10-26.** Engine only (`src/engine/returnEngine.ts`, `src/engine/meetingEncounter.ts`, `src/types/meetingEncounter.ts`). Content N/A (Transcendence prose already exists); UI N/A (the gates are not shown; the Return vignette renders whichever outcome wins).

**Why.** `passesFoundingGate` read `meetingRecord.intentPrimaryReach`, a field no record builder wrote. In live play the intent reach was always `undefined`, so Transcendence (Star/Veil/Eye only) was unreachable in every run and Sacrifice never applied its Shadow ban.

**What changed** (design-lane decision 2026-10-25: intent = who the mortal is, never the mark):
- `MeetingChoiceRecord` gains optional `primaryReach` / `secondaryReach`, written by both record builders from the chosen candidate. `secondaryReach` is there for THR-2034.
- `intentReachOf(record)` resolves the reach: the record field, else the spark vision's `requiredPrimaryReach` (saved worlds bonded earlier), else none. With none, an exclusion does not match and a required-Reach gate fails (unchanged fail-soft behaviour).
- The `return_resolution` trace carries `intentReach` and `intentReachSource` (`record` / `vision` / `none`).
- Fixtures moved off `intentPrimaryReach` (0 hits left in `src/`). Pins: a Star-family First passes Transcendence; a Shadow-family assassin (mark Iron) fails Sacrifice; a Gold powerbroker (mark Shadow) passes Sacrifice; a legacy record resolves through the vision.
- The THR-1644 golden bond snapshot gains the two new record fields (additive; nothing else moved).
