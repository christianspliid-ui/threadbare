# THR-2044 — A loan plants its own collection

**Shipped 2026-10-28.** M3: Dynamic Economy. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-28-thr-2044-loan-plants-its-collection.md`.

**What changed.** Every band of The Loan now plants a Debt Collection seed on its lender, ripening 24 ticks later (half the 48-tick `agreement.debt.minor` term). The lender comes back for what they are owed while the debt still stands. The ordinary counting-house draw of Debt Collection is unchanged. Two constants in `mortal-means-constants.ts` (`LOAN_COLLECTION_SEED_ENABLED`, `LOAN_COLLECTION_SEED_DELAY_TICKS`), one row in `theLoanBand()`, no engine change. `npm run census:debts` now also prints the seeds' fate.

**Evidence** (`npm run census:debts`, medium, 600 ticks):

| seed | Debt Collection resolved (main → now) | no_debt_owed (main → now) | seeds planted / fired / withered |
|---|---|---|---|
| 42 | 12 → 42 | 100 % → **36 %** | 28 / 27 / 1 |
| 99 | 13 → 40 | 92 % → **48 %** | 21 / 21 / 0 |

Both seeds meet the Done-when (≤ 50 %, resolution count not halved). Kill criteria not triggered: seed 99 is under 50 % at delay 24, and 1 of 49 seeds withered.
