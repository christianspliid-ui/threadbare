# 2026-09-22 — THR-1448: a held town is a faction position — holding opens the Realm's encounters and steers the holder's work

**Ticket:** [THR-1448](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1448) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** Engine, Content, UI. **Plan:** [`Docs/plans/2026-09-21-thr-1448-held-town-faction-position.md`](../plans/2026-09-21-thr-1448-held-town-faction-position.md). The second clause of Christian's THR-1287 ruling (2026-09-10): *"a commitment and probably also a faction position"*.

## What shipped

**The standing is a reading.** `src/engine/holdStanding.ts` computes, on demand from the active stances and the political map, what a mortal's hold means to a faction: the town, the Realm whose **ground** it sits on (`hexRealmId`, never `getLocationHolder` — a claimable town has no faction edge by rule), the Realm's own holdings, and the grip. No field on the stance, no property on the `controls` edge, no new node or edge type. A hold in unclaimed wilds is just a hold.

**One write, one site.** `reconcileHoldStandings` runs in `2a.55` after the neglect loop, keyed on a session ledger (`runtime.holdStandings`): each active stance on a Realm's ground opens its standing **once** — `joinFaction` mints the `member_of` edge, the reputation is seeded at `HOLD_STANDING_REPUTATION_SEED` (0.2, inside *subject*), `rank` is never written, `position_opened` is traced with the Realm and whether the membership was minted, and the chronicle says *keeps Ashford for the Realm of the Vael*. A stance that is gone traces `position_closed`; **the membership survives** and fades by the court's own decay. The claim arm was deliberately not made a second write site: neither `executeStrategicAction` nor the review lever has the runtime, and the one-tick lag against a ten-tick grace is invisible where two drifting writers would not be.

**Two doors for the keeper's content.** `UnifiedActionTemplate.requiresHold: { ofRealm: true }` is read in the filter beside `requiredReputationWith` with the same fail-open convention, and offered by a new supply arm in `generateFactionQuestCandidates` **past the rank's `encounterAccess` allowlist** — so a keeper faded to *stranger* still receives the town's business while the court's general rows stop. The `realm-content.ts` docblock that said `encounterAccess` was *read by nobody* was false (the supply path reads it on the live decision path) and is corrected; impediment row logged.

**The board leans.** `computeTemperamentWeight` gains a fifth input on the `grievanceHeat01` pattern: `HELD_TOWN_AFFINITY_WEIGHT` (0.3) × `1` on a Location the actor holds, `HELD_REALM_AFFINITY_SHARE` (0.5) on the Realm's other holdings, `0` elsewhere — keyed on the candidate's object, so it discriminates by construction. `boardTop[].heldTownAffinity` carries the input for the census.

**Content.** Two town-keeper encounters through the factory line — *The Keeper's Petition* (`heart`, `condition` + `omen`) and *The Crown's Reckoning* (`iron`, `membership` → `standing` by the one recorded swap, + `omen`) — tagged `#crown_errand` + `#town_keeper` (seated), `requiresHold`, class-scoped meta rows at `minRank: 'stranger'`, spending `$realm`. `check:encounter` green on both.

**UI.** The mortal sheet's Faction strand gains the hold line beneath the rank — *keeps Ashford for the Realm of the Vael · grip firm* — town and Realm as doors, the grip a word (`HOLD_GRIP_WORDS`), a wilds hold with no Realm, and the strand rendering for a keeper with no faction card. `__DEBUG.getHoldStanding`, CLI `hold`, the strategic debug tab's control rows name the Realm.

## Measured

The generated-world test (heavy lane, seed 42, small): a real mortal claims a real town on a Realm's ground → `position_opened` names that Realm, membership minted at the seed and derived to *subject*, the chronicle line lands; the keeper is supplied both town-keeper rows and all three court rows, a stranger in the world neither; with the keeper's reputation dropped below *subject* the court rows stop and the keeper rows continue; on the keeper's own standing the board scores the held town's `use` cell above the same cell elsewhere by exactly the weight; collapsing the stance traces `position_closed`, the membership stays. Holds are organically rare (the THR-1439 census: `claim × Location` started 8 · 0 on seeds 42 · 99), so the generated-world test is the acceptance, as the plan says.
