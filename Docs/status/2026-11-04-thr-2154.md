# THR-2154 — The goal says what fills it

**Shipped 2026-11-04.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience. Decided by the design lane on THR-2138 (item 1). No plan doc; the ticket was self-scoped. No rule change: the Mandate's measure is still the one THR-2081 set.

1. **View model (`src/engine/views/subjects/godSheet.ts`).** `MandateRowView` gains `boundCount` and `fillLine`. `flavorLine` is now `def.description`, the hunger's own words. Before, it was the current stage's description ("Establish the opening momentum…"). The stage descriptions still show in the Mandate detail's stage list. New `countBoundMortals` returns `godsPeopleIds` minus the avatar(s): the First plus every threaded mortal. It is fail-soft and returns 0.
2. **Content (`src/data/ascendant-bar-content.ts`).** `MANDATE_FILL_LINE` has three templates (none / one / many) and `mandateFillLine()` fills them. The count is spelled out ("three"), not a numeral. `MANDATE_RAISED_BY_LINE` is the detail's new line.
3. **UI.** `MandateBlock` renders the fill line under the hunger line at `--text-xs`, the rail's body size. `MandateDetail` "Raised by your own hand" → "Raised by your hand and the deeds of the mortals bound to you".
4. **The god page.** Its Mandate highlight reads the same `flavorLine`, so it now shows the hunger too. `artLedDressing.thr2098.test.ts` is updated for this.
5. **Review-gate fixes.** `countBoundMortals` counts only living actor nodes. A `thread` edge can target a relic (Beat 2), and a dead mortal keeps its thread edge (markMortalDead `retain`, Aspect echo). Both were confirmed by the gate's verifier and are covered by tests.
6. **Test.** `mandateFillLine.thr2154.test.ts` covers the hunger line, the fill line for n = 0, 1 and 3 (the avatar is not counted), fail-soft, and a hunger with one sphere or none.
