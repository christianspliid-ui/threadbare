### 2026-10-05 — THR-1737: a mortal setting out for a meeting prices its work at the longest roll

**What was wrong.** A mortal whose promised meeting is close (the `departing` regime) should only start local work it can finish before setting out. The filter priced that work at `totalTickCost`, the sum of each step's `duration.min`. On seed 42, a departing mortal with 9 ticks left took `encounter.town.cathedral_loan`: two 1–2-tick steps, priced 2. It ran 4 ticks and the promise went `lost`. THR-1688's new master encounters exposed this, and the THR-1669 test arms had to set the mortal's chore down again (`TODO(THR-1737)`).

**Engine.** The departing drop now prices the encounter's own ticks at its longest roll. A new `totalTickCostMax` on the cache entry (`computeTotalTickCostMaxUnified`: each step's `duration.max`, a branch at its longest arm) is set on every entry builder: location cache, social offers, and faction/guild quests. The review gate caught the faction builder missing on round 1. `departingWorkTicks` in `appointments.ts` reads it, never prices below the minimum, and falls back to `totalTickCost` if the field is missing. Kill switch: `APPOINTMENT_DEPARTING_PRICES_LONGEST_ROLL`. `leaning`, `waiting` and the scorer keep `totalTickCost`. **Content / UI:** N/A. The player sees the result as meetings kept rather than broken.
