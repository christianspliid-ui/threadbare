# 2026-10-10 — THR-1797: a ruler's card speaks plainly

**Ticket:** [THR-1797](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1797) · **Found by:** the THR-1780 browser pass. Scorvin's card read `TRUE_BELIEVER` under the name and listed each friendship chip twice.

- **Engine: every subtitle key is worded.** `subtitleForNode` (`detailPageGenerator.ts`, now exported) gives a narrative archetype its authored name (`getArchetype(id).name`, "True Believer"). Any other key (role, category, factionType, locationSubtype/terrain, eventType, groupKind) is humanised and lower-cased, and empty or non-string values fall back to the kind's default word. The card header renders upper-case, so the player now sees "TRAGIC HERO" instead of `TRAGIC_HERO`.
- **Engine: one thread chip per (person, kind).** `threadsBetweenThemResolver` dedupes on (other id, basis), because a bond is two edges, A→B and B→A. The cap `THREADS_BETWEEN_THEM_MAX_CHIPS` (6) now counts distinct chips rather than raw edges, so duplicates no longer crowd out real ties.
- **Proof.** `detailPageWording.test.ts` covers 17 cases: each page kind's worded key and fallback with a no-underscore predicate, plus A→B and B→A of one kind producing one chip while a second kind keeps its own. The event-page snapshot moved from `encounter_outcome` to `encounter outcome`. Browser: `preview_start` was refused, so the check ran on the PR's Vercel preview through Claude-in-Chrome. Skaskenisor's Tier-2 card (opened from a Chronicle founder link) reads "TRAGIC HERO" with one "Threads between them" chip, no `_` in the dialog, 0 duplicate chips and no console errors. Screenshot: `Docs/evidence/thr-1797/thr-1797-actor-card-tragic-hero.jpg`. Two limits: `__DEBUG` is tree-shaken from the production build, so the state assertion was a DOM read; and Chrome's window capped the viewport at 1763×1172 rather than 1920×1080. UI Laws: 1 and 13/14 (no internal key on the surface) pass; 17, 21 (the card opens from its link with breadcrumbs and back), 33 (the card fits the viewport with nothing below the fold), 36 (one chip per concept) and 37 hold.
