# 2026-10-09 — THR-1755 S3: The First carries the god's mark

**Ticket:** [THR-1755](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1755) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-06-thr-1644-threading-ceremony.md` (slice S3, decision D5)

The First now carries one visible blessing from the god in their own reach, worth a companion's skill there. Later threads carry none.

- **Engine.** `src/engine/firstMark.ts` (new). `grantFirstMark` fills the rite writer's step-5 hook in `applyThreadingRite`. It fires when the thread's court position is `the_first`. The reach is the meeting's spark reach (`markReach`, resolved from the spark vision), else `spark.reach`, else the mortal's `primaryReach`, else their highest reach. A First never carries two marks. An unknown reach skips the mark, and the skip is recorded as `markSkipped` on `rite.applied`. The dev-seeded First (`?seeded`) takes Heartfire the same way, so the seeded sheet matches the player's path.
- **Content.** The eight `GOD_GIVEN_TRAITS` keep their ids and gain `markLine` and `reachWork`, written against the canon reach themes. `seedFirstMarkTraits` seeds them at world init as `destiny` traits tagged `god_mark`, each with `domainContributions: { [reach]: FIRST_MARK_REACH_CONTRIBUTION }` (2). They are minted lazily at the grant if missing.
- **UI.** The sheet's Identity section shows a gold mark chip and line: "Heartfire — The god's mark. People hold to them without being asked." The chip's tooltip says what the mark does, and the Reach word opens the registry's Reach tooltip. The mark is left out of the generic trait chips so it never shows twice.
- **Constants:** `FIRST_MARK_REACH_CONTRIBUTION` 2, `FIRST_MARK_IMPORTANCE` 0.8, `FIRST_MARK_ENABLED` true (`src/data/threading-rite-constants.ts`).
- **Executor calls:**
  - The plan put seeding in `gameInit.ts`. The seed now sits beside `seedEncounterTraitDefinitions` in `worldSeed.ts`, the world-init trait-seeding home.
  - The mark size is the one constant rather than a per-entry `contribution` field. The ticket scope names the constant.
  - The marks are `destiny` traits, not the plan's `bestowed`: every `bestowed` trait node is a Power the reward pool deals out as loot, and the sheet lists it as a Bestowed Power. The review gate caught this. A copy of a mark under another id never counts as held.
  - The tooltip reads "You marked them when you bound them as your First" rather than "Your First carries your mark", because a First cleared by Return keeps the trait.
- **Evidence:** `firstMark.thr1755.test.ts` (16 tests, including "a seeded world offers no mark as a Power candidate"). `computeRawScore` rises by exactly the constant in the mark's reach and by 0 elsewhere. The card-route First carries its primary reach's mark and the second thread carries none. The meeting First carries exactly the spark reach's mark on seeds 42 / 99 / 2.
