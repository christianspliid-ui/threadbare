# THR-745 — 57 capability artifacts stop lying about what they do

**Shipped 2026-08-18.** THR-718 built the whole `stat_contribution` mechanism — the effect
primitive, `collectStatContributions`, the `computeRawScore` hook, the `StepDots` /
`DomainCard` dots, the `ITEM_STAT_BAND_*` ceilings and a LIVE interface contract — and
proved it on **nine** items. Everything else in the reward catalogs kept shipping with the
same design intent and none of the mechanism: a Blackiron Blade shaped its bearer's combat
*rolls* and left their Iron *capability tier* exactly where it found it. This pass migrates
**57** of them.

## What moved

| Catalog | Subcategories migrated | Entries |
|---|---|---|
| `reward-attachment-catalog.ts` | arms, vestments, tomes_scrolls, tools_instruments, relics_talismans | 51 |
| `anomaly-reward-catalog.ts` | the durable signature artifacts | 6 |

Each entry gained one `stat_contribution` effect as the final element of its `effects[]`,
a `// THR-745:` comment carrying that item's magnitude justification, and a
`mechanicalSummary` rewritten to the THR-718 convention — the leading roll term becomes
`+0.12 Iron roll · Iron capability +0.8 / Heart −0.4 while borne, …`, so the player-facing
string names the tier channel alongside the roll channel instead of quietly omitting it.

## Calibration was read off the exemplars, not invented

The nine THR-718 items are the scale, and every value here is placed against them: Iron
Blade `0.3` at T1, Ashenmane's Fang `0.6` at T2, Whispering Eye `0.7 / −0.3` at T3,
Worldforge Anvil `1.5 / 1.0` legendary. That yields **primary reach ≈ 0.2–0.3 at T1,
0.4–0.55 at T2, 0.7–0.8 at T3, 1.2–1.3 at T4**, with the secondary reach at roughly half
the primary and penalties mirroring the item's own authored cost. The ticket's own rule —
*items already carrying strong roll modifiers take the low end* — did real work rather than
sitting in the body:

- **The Quiet Blade** (T4) sits at `iron: 1.2`, not at the `2.0` ceiling its tier would
  allow, because the entry carries a standing note that its passive roll total already
  overruns `EFFECT_PER_ITEM_CAP`. A third channel at full strength on top of an
  acknowledged overrun is how an item becomes the only item.
- **Chronicle of the Falling** sits at `eye: 0.45`, the floor of its band, because its
  near-miss shaper is unusually wide (margin 5).
- **Lens-Sighted Arbalest** sits at the same floor for the opposite reason — its shaper is
  narrow (margin 2), so the item earns its lift through the lens, not the rescue.
- **Codex of Unmaking** carries the steepest penalty in the pass (`heart: −0.6`) because
  the item *already* gates Heart actions outright; the tier cost states in capability what
  the gate states in permission.

Penalties are not new taxes — they mirror what each item already says about itself. The
Rusted Mace's authored Iron/Heart tradeoff, the Assessor's Scales' explicit "not a fighting
weapon" disclaimer, the Weeping Icon's mercy drift: each reappears at tier scale.

## The scope seam, stated rather than fudged

The ticket warned that 116+ per-item justifications exceed one pass, and the promotion
comment was blunter: *a partial pass with real justifications is a better outcome than a
complete one with invented ones.* The cut is **not** "however many fit before time ran
out" — it falls on a real seam in the file.

`REWARD_POSSESSIONS` has two halves. The designed tiered sections (Arms T1–T4, Vestments,
Tomes, Tools, Relics, …) are authored content. Everything from
`// ─── Effect Primitive Exercisers` onward exists to demonstrate exactly **one** effect
primitive with nothing else attached — Ember Edge is `conditional` alone, Moonstone Pendant
is `cooldown` alone, Veteran's Shield is `stacking` alone. Adding a second primitive to a
single-primitive proof weakens what it proves, so that block was held whole.

That rationale is honest for the pure demos and **not** honest for the named T3/T4
artifacts sitting in the same block — `Null Circlet`, `Fatesight Lens`, `The Anvilbone` and
three others read as genuine content that happens to live on the wrong side of a comment.
Rather than quietly absorb them or quietly drop them, that per-entry call is **THR-1169**,
carrying this ticket's predicate verbatim and naming the six by hand.

Three entries inside the migrated sections were excluded individually, on the predicate
rather than on time: `letters_of_introduction` (pure `content_grant`, nothing borne),
`hearthglass_ward` (one-shot `prevent_loss`, shatters), and `duelists_luck_token` (pure
`test_shaper` — luck, not competence, and the nearest call of the three). All three are
named in THR-1169 for confirm-or-reverse. `provisions`, `mounts_beasts` and `intelligence`
are excluded by the predicate itself as consumables, mounts and one-shot dossiers.

## The guard was falsified, not just observed passing

`item-stat-bands.contract.test.ts` was green before this change and green after, which on
its own proves nothing about whether it can see the new entries — a ceiling test passes
happily against a catalog it never reaches. Pushing Hollowfang to `iron: 2.9` fails it by
name (`reward_arms_hollowfang: iron=2.9 (> 2)`); reverting returns it to green. The
double-dip half is satisfied by construction: no migrated entry carries a legacy
`domainContributions` bag.
