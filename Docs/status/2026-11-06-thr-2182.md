# THR-2182 — Rank words show their ladder

**Shipped 2026-11-06.** Warm playtest · round 3. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-11-06-thr-2182-rank-words-show-their-ladder.md`.

1. **A reach tile says where its word sits.** `personRows.ts` `domains` fills a new optional `CardTile.subline` from the tier the card already carries: "Middle of the ladder · next Fearsome", or "Top of the ladder" at tier 4. The place words are `RANK_PLACE_WORDS` in `data/person-sheet-words.ts`. The pure helper `rankPlaceLine(domain, tier)` lives in `data/domain-words.ts` and returns `undefined` for any tier that is not an integer 0–4.
2. **The rank word hovers its ladder.** `CardTile.wordHover` wraps the first line's text in a `Tooltip`: "Iron, low to high: Meek, Trained, Formidable, Fearsome, Legendary." (`rankLadderHover`, built from `DOMAIN_WORD_SCALES`). The ▲ gain mark keeps its own hover. The title gets the existing `reach.{domain}` hover through `CardTile.titleTooltipId`.
3. **The row card count says "cards".** The full count chip gains an optional `noun` drawn after the number (`aria-hidden`; the label already speaks it). `RowCardCount` passes `rowCardsNoun(n)` from `INSPECTOR_WORDS.rowCardsNoun`. Other count chips are unchanged.
4. **The faction top word is "Foremost".** `FACTION_REACH_WORDS` / `FACTION_REACH_THRESHOLDS` in `data/subject-sheet-words.ts`. "Formidable" now names only the middle of a mortal's Iron ladder.
5. **Styleguide (Law 29):** a count chip with a noun, and a reach tile with a subline and a word hover.
