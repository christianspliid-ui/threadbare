# 2026-10-11 — THR-1580 (slice 9): the god's reach tiers read the reach share

**Ticket:** [THR-1580](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1580) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-24-thr-1575-forecast-window.md` § Systems design 4 · multi-PR deferral, ticket stays open.

- **The readers (one module, three sites).** `phaseAscendantProgression` — the god's tier per permanent reach, whose upward crossings enqueue Deepening beats; `accruePlayerReachPractice` — the practice damping `max(1 − cap × 0.7, 0.1)`; `getAscendantProgress` — the readout the Reaches bar, the Codex and `__DEBUG.getAscendantProgression()` show. All three read the pre-refit curve (midpoint 10, k 0.4).
- **Measured** (`npm run measure:ascendant-progression-scales`; the god's raw is its `reachPractice` alone, raw 0 at bond on seeds 42 · 99 · 7). Actions to tiers 2…10, primary reach, difficulty 0.5: pre-refit 12 · 18 · 22 · 25 · 29 · 33 · 38 · 45 · 58; reach share 11 · 22 · 34 · 47 · 62 · 78 · 96 · 117 · 142; dice 7 · 35 · 56 · 74 · 92 · 113 · 138 · 174 · 237. The live run on this tree reproduces the share row on all three seeds.
- **Verdict: reach share**, constants unchanged. A tier is a standing level, like the mentorship tiers (slice 7). The pre-refit curve put five Deepening beats in 15 actions and called a raw-15.5 god tier 10, a journeyman by mortal standards; the share spaces the beats evenly and puts tier 10 where a raw-40 mortal master stands. Rejected the dice: one beat at action 7, then a 28-action silence, and a 237-action arc.
- **Cost, stated:** the full Deepening arc lengthens from 58 to 142 in-domain actions at difficulty 0.5. `PLAYER_PRACTICE_PER_ACTION` (0.4) is the lever. Side effect: the share damps practice more gently at low raw, so after 58 actions a god holds raw ≈ 19 rather than ≈ 15.5 and a primary cast reads ≈ 0.72 rather than ≈ 0.65 on the dice (≈ 0.91 at tier 10). The tier word for a fresh god is tier 1 on both scales, so the opening Reaches bar is unchanged.
- **Remaining THR-1580 families:** capabilityGrowth, domainCapability profile (`computeFullProfile`), encounterAwareness (idle-rate check mandatory; mutex with a THR-1757 cap change).
