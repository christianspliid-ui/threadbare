# THR-1041 — the cast and the stakes reach the player, and a resolved step says how it ended

**Shipped 2026-08-08.** Project: Encounter Experience. From the THR-1039 composition audit.

## What was wrong

`buildUnifiedEncounterStageModel` has built three things nobody read.

`model.cast` (`:222-248`) resolves a scene's bound actors from the encounter's support bundle — who the spawner reused, who it had to conjure, what each one is there to be. No component consumed it, so a cast member reached the player **only as a name inside prose**: no face, no tooltip, nowhere to go. That is Law 1's definition of a concept that is not really present, on every encounter with a bound cast.

`model.falloutPreview` (`:578-607`) reads the current step's own success and failure metadata and states what the step puts on the record. Also unread — so the veil asked the player to choose without ever saying what was at stake.

And the step ladder asked a coarser question than the archive did. `chapterArchive.ts` resolved a resolved step's afterimage **by outcome band**; the stage model resolved only success-versus-failure. An author who wrote a bespoke `critical_failure` line had it stored in the chapter ledger and discarded at Scene So Far — the one surface whose whole job is narrating what has happened so far.

## What shipped

**UI.** A cast strip and an at-stake row in `EncounterVeil.tsx`, between the prose and the choices, in the order the scene needs them: who is here, what it costs, what you do. Cast chips carry `EntityVisual` art through the viewer's knowledge gate, a `Tooltip` explaining the role and whether this person predates the scene, and a kind-routed link to the agent surface — live only where a bound node exists, plain styled text otherwise (Law 21, fail-open).

**Engine.** `afterimageForOutcome` moved to `types/unifiedAction` and is now shared by the ledger and the stage model, so the two cannot drift apart again. A new `engine/supportRoleWords.ts` bands `supportRole` authoring keys into English (`mct_ledger_clerk` → "Ledger Clerk"): mounting the cast strip made that field player-facing for the first time, and Law 14 admits no raw internal keys. It derives rather than tabulating, because authors add roles faster than anyone maintains a 54-entry table — which is exactly how a vocabulary decays back into printing keys.

**Testing.** Band coverage per outcome, each asserting the bespoke line *and* that it is not the coarse one — the pre-change code returned the coarse line for every band, so an assertion on the bespoke text alone is what falsifies it. Cast-model binding, reuse provenance, and the unbound-spec arm. A corpus sweep over every `supportRole` authored anywhere in `src/data`, enumerated rather than sampled, so a role added next week is covered the day it is written.

**Falsified before trusted:** with the two source files stashed, 12 of the new assertions fail; the two that still pass are the empty-state arms, which is correct — nothing rendered before and nothing renders for empty data now.

## What this did not fix, and why that is the honest answer

The prototype tree (`EncounterScreen`, `CastRail`, `CastTile`, `EffectRegistration`) stays styleguide-only. Its patterns were reused — the section label, the role line, the presence dimming — but its components could not be: they are built on `CastTileData`, whose fields have **no producer** in the live model, so adopting them would have meant inventing data at the surface. Filed as THR-1049 with that finding recorded, rather than left as an undecided third tree.

Two pre-existing Law violations were found on the veil **as composed** and filed as THR-1048: the legacy choice card prints `+15% success` (Law 13 — no percentages, anywhere, player-facing) and heads each card with its raw `interventionType` enum (Law 14). Neither is introduced here; both sit on the legacy choice path that the nudge-stage rebuild has not reached.

Also worth recording for whoever picks up the fallout work: several encounters route through `buildSimpleEncounterStageModel`, not the unified adapter, and that adapter emits an empty cast and fallout by construction. The at-stake row is therefore live only on unified-adapter templates today — verified on `crafting.quest.flawed_steel`, absent on `encounter.merchants_gambit`.
