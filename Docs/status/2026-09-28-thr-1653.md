# 2026-09-28 — THR-1653: graduation into the deciding tier goes through the attention budget

**Ticket:** [THR-1653](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1653) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1630-notables-and-ties.md` § S3 · **Pillars:** Engine (the budget path); Content: N/A (no prose or templates); UI: N/A (the ledger is read by `getSpotlightLedger` and the CLI `spotlight` command, no screen changes).

## What was wrong

`phaseNpcGraduation` promoted a notable into the spotlight with no budget check. An ambition's pull (THR-1348) goes through a swap-or-overflow budget, but graduation was a second door that did not. Slice 2 of notables and ties is about to seed one notable per settlement, which fills the notable tier from tick 0. Without this slice, every seeded notable would be a latent decider.

## What shipped

- **`admitToSpotlight(graph, actorId, tick, via, options)`** in `spotlightPull.ts`, extracted from `pullHolderIntoSpotlight`. It runs the same three steps: swap with the best demotion candidate, else admit as overflow while `countOverflowPulls < overflowAllowance`, else refuse with `budget`. It keeps THR-1348's protections exactly. `pullHolderIntoSpotlight` now calls it after its own strategic-template and pulled-once checks, so the budget has one path and two callers.
- **`phaseNpcGraduation`** admits through it behind `NOTABLE_GRADUATION_BUDGETED` (default `true`). A refused graduate stays notable and is re-tested next tick. A mortal stepped back by this tick's admissions is not re-tested in the same tick, which would otherwise let the snapshot swap them straight back.
- **The ledger names the door.** Pulled entries carry `reason: 'ambition' | 'graduation'`, and refused entries carry `via`. An admitted graduate's earlier refusal is cleared, so `refused` lists only mortals who are still left out. The `spotlight_pull` trace carries `via: 'graduation'` on graduation rows, and the CLI `spotlight` command prints it.

## Measured (the S3 gate and the kill criteria)

- **Graduation does not fire today.** In 200 unattended ticks on seeds 42 and 99 (medium), notable → spotlight graduation fired 0 times, with the flag on and with it off. The two arms produce identical decider curves, identical ms/tick, and identical attended firing rows. The plan's "spotlight grew 19 → 47" did not come from this arm. Counted as living deciders, the world goes 20 → 19 and 23 → 20. The slice is protection for slice 2, not a change to today's worlds.
- **Invariant:** deciders at t200 ≤ t0 + overflow allowance + threaded + pulled holds (19 ≤ 24, 20 ≤ 28).
- **Kill criteria not triggered.** `readers/reach.ts 42,99 200` shows 127 of 514 drawable templates fired (floor 121). `readers/attended.ts 42,99 150` shows The First's longest gap between encounters at 25 · 21 ticks (ceiling 30). The flag ships `true`.
