### 2026-10-03 — THR-1572: every tradition teaches its own spells

**The world writes its own magic.** Until now, all 109 seeded casters on seed 42 held the same spell (Height Anchor), because no caster carries a sphere at worldgen and everyone fell through to the fallback cantrip. Now every caster belongs to a **tradition**, chosen by their role and their faction's lean. Each tradition in use gets a six-spell library, generated at worldgen from authored cores and dressed in the tradition's words. Casters are seeded from their tradition's lowest tier.
- **Engine:** `src/engine/spellGenerator/` contains `casterTradition`, `generateSpell`, `validateGeneratedSpell`, `describeSpell`, `spellLibrary`, `notice` and `readBack`.
  - A generated spell's template lives on its definition node, and the nine former `getSpellTemplate` sites read it through `resolveSpellTemplate`.
  - `create × Power` learns from the caster's own library first.
  - A transgression spell places one `forbidden_contact` hidden mark per caster per spell (notice).
- **Content:**
  - 24 cores survived re-measurement against the two vocabularies. Fourteen of the prototype's 37 cores were dropped as stateful, outside the carried set, or modifier-only, and one teleport core was added.
  - Each core carries two flavour lines.
  - The tables hold 34 tradition rows, role themes, price leans (holy and heal never transgress; curse and death are never free), the Foundation-sphere shelves (tier 3–4 only) and the cast vocabulary.
- **UI:** the sheet's spell row says *Taught by*, *What it does*, *What it costs* and *What goes wrong* in words.
- **Debug:** `getSpellLibraries` and `previewGeneratedSpell` in the debug bridge; `generate spells` and `spells` in the CLI.

**Two engine findings along the way.**
- **`self_remove` on a shared spell would have deleted the spell for every bearer.** The payload removes the attachment node and every edge into it. It is now refused in carried spells.
- **NPC ids come from a process-wide counter.** A draw keyed on the actor id gave one seed two different spell worlds. Draws now key on the caster's name (impediment #1129).
