# 2026-09-24 — THR-1564: the war reaches the chronicle whether or not the debug panel is open

**Ticket:** [THR-1564](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1564) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-24-thr-1564-war-news-from-state.md` · **Pillars:** Engine (the reporter, seven call sites, a retired phase) · Content (one line per war event, in the game's register, no numbers) · UI N/A (the lines reach the existing Chronicle panel; `Browser-verify exempt: no src/components change; the lines reach the existing Chronicle panel, proved headlessly`).

## What was wrong

`phaseArmyNotifications` built the war lines by reading the trace buffer. Tracing is off unless the debug panel is open, so in normal play it wrote nothing: the player was never told an army was raised, a battle fought or a town taken. Every CLI run always traces, which is why nobody noticed. Even with tracing on, the army-raised line was dead by phase order, the lines about strangers sat below every player surface (0.2, and 0.7 for a town changing hands, against a chronicle threshold of 0.8), a loser's visibility was judged after the aftermath had removed it, and the messages were debug summaries with momentum numbers in them.

## What shipped

- **`reportWar(state, news)`** in `src/engine/armyNotifications.ts`. Each war writer calls it where the event happens: `spawnArmy`, `disbandArmy` (new `reason`; the aftermath's `'battle'` does not report), the attrition threshold crossing, `createBattleNode`, `createSiegeNode`, `resolveBattle` and the conquest. It judges visibility at the site, builds one line, and pushes one `TickEvent` into `state.tickEvents` with a deterministic id and `refs`. `phaseNarrative` promotes it the same tick.
- **`resolveBattle` captures before the aftermath** (the sides, the place, the town's holder, the visibility) and reports after it. A siege that took its town is told once, by the territory line.
- **The loudness table.** Beginnings reach the chronicle only when threaded. Every battle's and siege's ending reaches it (`WAR_NEWS_STRANGERS_ENDINGS_IN_CHRONICLE`, the lever). A town changing hands reaches it flat (`REALM_TERRITORY_EVENT_SIGNIFICANCE` 0.7 → 0.85). An army fraying is recorded, never shown.
- **The lines.** Plain sentences with no numbers, e.g. *The Shattered Sanctum held against sovereignty of Open Earth.* A siege whose besiegers broke apart first reads *The siege of X is lifted.*, never "a nameless host". `battleOutcomeSentence` is the one builder for a battle's ending, for THR-1528 to reuse.
- **Retired:** phase 2.358 and its orchestrator call. Every trace at every war site stays as it was.
- **Debug:** `window.__DEBUG.getWarNews()`; a new `war.reported` trace per call.

## Evidence

- `warNews.test.ts` (21 tests, tracing disabled, real writers): each kind in the same tick at its significance; a threaded mortal's losing army whose commander dies in the aftermath still reports threaded; a siege that takes its town writes only the territory line; no digit in any line; tracing on and off write identical lines.
- Headless, seed 42 medium, 150 ticks, **tracing off**: 80 war lines (9 raised, 13 sieges laid, 39 fraying, 8 broken apart, 11 endings). The 11 endings reached the chronicle, and there were 0 digits. The same run with tracing on is identical line for line. Before this change the tracing-off run wrote 0. No town changed hands in that window.
