# THR-2062 — Attention has a price

**Shipped 2026-10-28.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-27-thr-2062-attention-has-a-price.md`. Attention is now what it costs to look in on a mortal who lives on without you. The Attention gauge on the god rail is back, but only once the court holds a mortal on *Lives on*.

**What changed.**
- **E1:** a pause-mode thread (*Asks you*, The First by default) raises no chapter Thread Tug. `phaseAttention` §§ 4a/4b trace the skip as `curator_decision` `skipped` / `pause_mode_thread`.
- **E2/E3:** a livelihood tug fires once per crossing. `ResourceInstance.livelihoodPulledTier` records it and clears when the good returns to adequate. The tug carries `kind: 'livelihood'` plus its good and tier.
- **E4:** `attendThreadTug` (`attentionPool.ts`) is the single writer of a spend. It traces `attention_pool` `attend_tug`. The badge hook no longer writes the pool, and `grep "properties.attentionPool =" src/components` is empty.
- **E5:** `attentionCanBeSpent` drives `GodRailAttention.spendable`.
- **C1/C2:** caption and tooltip copy are new. The caption is shortened to 15 words to stay under the caption word cap: *"Your free notice. Looking in on a mortal who lives on spends it; it refills."* The badge says *"hard · look in, leaves you Strained"* / *"you cannot spare the attention"* / *"their home is short of grain"*, with no numerals.
- **U1/U2:** `ATTENTION_GAUGE_SHOWN = true`. The gauge renders behind `spendable`, and its reveal row has no `newLabel`. The tooltip is in words only.
- New constant `LIVELIHOOD_TUG_ATTEND_COST = 0`.
- The rulebook §2 *Scan* clause is now [IMPL]. Two interface contracts were added.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 42 | 0 chapter · 6 (full) | 90 · 4 | 25 % | 5 |

Before (plan census, `main` @ b6b9533a): First alone, 37 · 18 · 15 tugs; 3 retinue, below half on 99 · 87 · 88 % of ticks. All kill criteria hold: no pause-mode tug, no livelihood repeat inside a crossing, the First-alone pool never leaves full, and 3-retinue worlds sit between too loose and too tight. `ATTENTION_BASE_REGEN` is unchanged.

**Browser evidence** (Playwright DOM, 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, worktree Vite on port 5461):
- (a) With The First on *Asks you*, the gauge is absent, and there are 0 tugs after `tick(24)`.
- (b) After the Threads-row switch moves to *Lives on*, `getAttentionCadence().attentionMode === 'auto_resolve'` and the gauge is present.
- (c) A pull came 14 ticks later: *"The thread pulls, moderate — look in"*. Answering it marked the tug attended and dropped the fill from 100 % to 91.7 % (pool 6 → 5.5 at The First's ×0.5). One pull does not leave Focused, so the state word stays.
- There were 0 console errors.

**Review gate:** two rounds. Both findings were fixed in `resourceStockTiers.ts`. The once-per-crossing marker is now stamped only when a tug is actually raised, so a famine that began before anyone was bonded still reaches the first bonded mortal at home. A glut that follows a famine while the famine's tug lingers is also still told. Each has a regression test.
