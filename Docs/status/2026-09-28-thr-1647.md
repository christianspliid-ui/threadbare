# 2026-09-28 — THR-1647: the opening gifts wait for the player

**Ticket:** [THR-1647](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1647) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1605-the-opening.md` § S4 · **Pillars:** Engine (the spine gate, the player-act counter); Content: N/A (the gifts' copy and ceremonial sphere-tinted cards are unchanged); UI: one line in the avatar-move handler so a move command counts as an act. No surface changes.

## What was wrong

Round-1 testers got 5–7 one-button gift popups before they acted once. Spine beats fired on `minTurn` 0/2/4/6/8. A tick is one real second, and resolving a beat resumes time, so the next beat was already due.

## What shipped

- **The gate.** `phaseAscendantBeatDirector` offers spine gifts 1–4 (`the_seat`, `thing_left_behind`, `the_first_word`, `a_path_opens`) only when all four hold: (a) the gift's own `minTurn` has passed; (b) The First is bonded; (c) `BEAT_MIN_GAP` (4) ticks have run since the last gift resolved; (d) the player has acted `SPINE_PLAYER_ACTS_BETWEEN_GIFTS` (1) times since then, **or** `SPINE_IDLE_FALLBACK_TICKS` (36) ticks have run. Beat 0 ("Reach Down") and the pool, deepening and milestone beats are unchanged.
- **Player acts.** New optional `GameState.playerActCount`, bumped by `recordPlayerAct` (`src/engine/playerActs.ts`) in three places: `commitPlayerCast`, the avatar move command (only when a path is found), and a player Follow (sources `arc_panel` / `encounter_ui`). Init seeding and the debug/URL follow levers do not count. The plan also named "Observe", but no distinct player Observe command exists; `observe_agent` is a card, so it already counts as a cast.
- **Stamped at resolve.** `resolvePendingBeat` stamps `ascendantBeats.lastSpineResolvedTick` and `playerActCountAtLastSpine` when a spine beat resolves, so acts taken before a gift resolved do not pay for the next one. For a save without the stamp, the director falls back to the newest spine record in `history`.
- **Inspectability.** `spineGateBlockedBy(state)` is the gate as a pure read. `getOpeningState()` gains `playerActCount`, `nextSpineBeat` and `spineGateBlockedBy` (`min_turn` | `first_not_bonded` | `min_gap` | `awaiting_player_act` | `null`). A held gift traces `beat.spine_deferred` once per (beat, reason). The interface row `player-acts-pace-spine-gifts` is 🔵 UNVERIFIED-OK.

## Verified

- `spineGiftPacing.test.ts` (9 tests): with the First bonded and no act, gift 1 arrives at exactly tick 36; after one real `commitPlayerCast`, it arrives at the next gap (tick 4); the gate reads `min_turn` → `min_gap` → `awaiting_player_act`, then clears on an act or the fallback; acts before a resolve do not count; gifts 1–4 wait for the bond; the deferral trace is deduped; old saves still get gifts through the fallback; which follow sources count.
- `avatarMoveCountsAsPlayerAct.test.ts` (jsdom, 3 tests): a move with a path bumps the count; a click with no path does not.
- Headless CLI, seed 42 medium: The First is bonded and Beat 0 is resolved by `eval` at tick 10. At tick 20, `the_seat` is held with `awaiting_player_act`. One act later, it is offered at tick 21.
