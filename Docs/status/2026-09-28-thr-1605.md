# 2026-09-28 — THR-1605: the meeting comes to the player

**Ticket:** [THR-1605](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1605) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1605-the-opening.md` § S1 · **Pillars:** Engine (`pickMeetingLocation`, `isFirstBonded`); UI (the GameView auto-trigger); Content: N/A (the meeting's prose is deliberately untouched — moving the meeting to a real settlement is what keeps its 107 settlement lines true).

## What was wrong

On the real first-run path (StartPage → Remembrance → GameView) the avatar starts at the Sacred Grove, a `shrine`. The Meet-The-First beat auto-triggered only when the avatar stood in a settlement, and nothing told the player to walk there. 0 of 3 round-1 cold testers met a mortal.

## What shipped

- **`pickMeetingLocation(graph, ascendantId)`** in `meetingEncounter.ts` picks the settlement nearest the avatar: outer-tier Locations only (never a Place), settled subtypes only, hex distance from the avatar's hex resolved upward through `located_at`. A settlement with a current culture within `MEETING_CULTURED_PREFERENCE_RADIUS` (6) beats a nearer uncultured one. Ties break on node id, with no PRNG. It returns `null` when no settlement exists, and the caller then uses the avatar's own location.
- **`isFirstBonded(graph, ascendantId)`** is now the single read of "has the player met their First?". `isMeetTheFirstAvailable` uses it, and S2/S4/S5 will too.
- **The trigger** in `GameView.tsx` no longer reads where the avatar stands. It fires once, as soon as spine Beat 0 ("Reach Down") is in the beat history, when the meeting is available and an ascendant identity is present. The identity check closes the gap where an identity-less start spent the one-shot flag on a flow that never mounts. The `tick >= 2` wait is gone.
- **Inspectability:** a `meeting.location_picked` trace (distance, cultured, fallback). `GameState.meetingLocationId` is an optional field. `window.__DEBUG.getOpeningState()` returns `firstBonded`, `openingBeatResolved`, `meetingAutoTriggered` and `meetingLocationId`.
- **Interface map:** new row `meeting-bond-writes-the-first` (🔵 UNVERIFIED-OK).
- The manual "Meet The First" card and `?firstunmet` still work. CLAUDE.md's `?firstunmet` row now describes the new trigger.

## Verified

- On the real first-run path at 1920×1080 in Playwright, with a fresh world, the remembrance flow, Ascend, one tick, then **Reach Down**: `MeetTheFirstFlow` opened immediately with no avatar movement. `getOpeningState()` read `meetingLocationId: "loc_7"`, which `inspectLocation` names as The Bone Coast, a `city`. After the bond it read `firstBonded: true`, and Aldric appeared in the Threads panel.
- 10 unit tests in `meetingLocationPick.test.ts` cover: nearest wins; shrine, ruin and Place excluded; the cultured preference inside and outside the radius; the id tie-break; the Place-to-parent hex resolution; the `null` fallback; the map-centre fallback; and `isFirstBonded`.
