# 2026-09-10 — THR-1318: the lens overlay engine is retired, and the corpus is why

**Issue:** [THR-1318](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1318/lens-overlay-prose-engine-is-authored-tested-and-has-no-caller) · **Project:** Content Architecture · **Pillars:** Engine · Content

## The charter, and which arm the measurement picked

THR-1213 slice 2 filed this as a deliberate fork rather than a bug: `src/engine/ascendantLens.ts`
exported `resolveLensOverlay`, `shouldFireMortalEcho` and `composeLensedProse` — a complete,
unit-tested prose engine with authored content on the other side and **zero production callers**.
Activate it and the player reads something new at the bonding beat; retire it and a promise with no
mechanism leaves the tree. Both arms were legitimate; the ticket's standing default was retire, on
sunset-by-default.

The ordering gate was THR-1213's content pass, because `shouldFireMortalEcho` reads
`emotionalRegister`, non-empty on only 10 of 167 dilemmas at filing time. That gate is discharged —
THR-1213 is Done and the pass landed. **Measured at pickup, `emotionalRegister` is now 167/167.**

So the half the ticket worried about is healthy. The half nobody had measured is not:

| Corpus fact | Measured 2026-09-10 |
|---|---|
| Dilemmas carrying a populated `lensOverlays` | **10 of 167** (157 shipped `lensOverlays: []`) |
| Distinct Hungers those overlays cover | **1 of 12** — every overlay is `hungerId: 'gather'` |
| Overlays per populated dilemma | 1 |
| `echoThreshold` values | `2`, on all ten |

That inverts the ticket's framing. The question was never *how much of the god's voice belongs on
top of the mortal's scene* — for eleven of twelve gods **no voice was ever written**. Activation
would have handed `gather` gods an occasional extra paragraph and everyone else nothing, on 6% of
scenes: an inconsistency a player reads as a bug, not as authorship.

The echo could not fire either. `buildLensFromIdentity` narrows `identity.mortalTags` to the closed
`ResonanceTag` vocabulary, and the shipped dev identity yields exactly one tag — overlap of at most
1 against a threshold of 2. The fallback that would have widened it (`hunger.dilemmaResonanceTags.slice(0, 3)`)
only fires when `driveTags` is *empty*, which one tag is not. Structurally unreachable, not merely rare.

**This is a measurement verdict, not the creative fork the ticket reserved for Christian.** The
orchestrator's promotion comment set that boundary explicitly: take retire as the standing answer and
the ticket is an executor call end to end; escalate only *if the measured corpus makes activation look
like the better game*. It makes it look worse. No chat gate opened.

## The decisive fact: the Hunger already reaches the meeting

Retiring this costs the player nothing, because the channel that carries the Hunger into the bonding
beat is **selection**, not overlay prose. The live `hunger-resonance-weighs-the-meeting-deal`
interface contract (THR-1213) scores `emotionalRegister ∩ hunger.dilemmaResonanceTags` to decide
*which formative tests your First is put through*, with blocking coverage of ≥6 dilemmas for all
twelve Hungers (measured: gather 59 … sever 8). Its surface test already asserts that two identities
differing only in `hungerId` put different authored prose in the DOM.

The overlay engine was a **second, redundant channel with 1-of-12 coverage** layered on top of a
working one — which is what made this a retirement rather than a loss.

## What was deleted

- `src/engine/ascendantLens.ts` — the three functions and the now-unused `LensOverlay` import.
  `buildStubAscendantLens` and `buildLensFromIdentity` **stay**: both have live production callers
  (`MeetingEncounterModal`, `MeetTheFirstFlow`) and both are named in the live contract row.
- `src/types/meetingEncounter.ts` — the `LensOverlay` interface and `EnrichedDilemmaTemplate.lensOverlays`.
- `src/data/meeting-dilemma-library.ts` — all 167 field occurrences (157 empty + 10 populated blocks,
  237 lines). Verified structurally: `artTags:` 167 → 167 and `emotionalRegister:` 167 → 167 across
  the edit, so the strip removed the field and nothing adjacent.
- `src/engine/__tests__/ascendantLens.test.ts` — **deleted whole**, per the interface-map rule. All
  174 lines tested only the three retired functions; a green test on a dead contract is the pathology
  that rule exists to kill. The survivors keep their coverage elsewhere
  (`hungerShapesTheDeal.test.tsx`, `hungerResonanceGate.test.ts`).

The module header is rewritten rather than trimmed: it had claimed *"Every scene the player reads
passes through this"*, which was the write-without-consumer stating itself as fact.

**No interface-map row retires.** The grep-checked rows naming this module
(`hunger-resonance-weighs-the-meeting-deal`) cover `buildLensFromIdentity` / `scoreDilemmaResonance` /
`selectDilemmasScored` — all survivors. The overlay engine was never written down as a contract,
which is itself why it went dormant unaudited for months.
