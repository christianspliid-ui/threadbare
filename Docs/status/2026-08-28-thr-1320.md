# A road that stands long enough to be taken (THR-1320)

**The counter-play that had never once landed.** THR-1308 registered `trade_route` as an undertaking kind on the tier's own terms — until a kind can be undone, it is not a kind — and shipped `strategic_blockade_route` as its undoing. The verb was authored, gated, unit-proven and genuinely *offered*: 9 blockade undertakings on seed 42, 16 on seed 99, over 150 ticks. It had suspended nothing, ever.

The defect was not where the shape suggests. `createTradeRoute` is the sole minter of `trades_with` and mints at `volume: 1`; `TRADE_ROUTE_DECAY_RATE` is also 1; `isRouteStale` fires at `lastTraded + TRADE_ROUTE_FRESHNESS_WINDOW` (5). Nothing in the strategic path ever refreshes `lastTraded` — the only refreshers are the divine trade verbs in `tradeRouteOps` / `graphOpExecutor`. So a route founded by a **six-tick undertaking** went stale at +6, lost its only point of volume on that tick and was removed. A season of work that deleted itself before the next season. `blockadeRoute` then resolved the endpoint's edges, found none standing, and returned `no_route` every single time.

**What makes it worth recording is that nothing looked wrong.** Candidates generated, undertakings completed, census liveness 94.6% / 97.1% — every board-level instrument read healthy while the counter-play did nothing. It was found on an organic 150-tick run at another ticket's closeout, and the unit suite structurally could not have seen it: those tests assert the blockade works *given* a route, which it always did.

## The fix

A route carrying an `establishedBy` stamp was made by someone — it is a *work*, in the holdings sense — and now stands out a **founder's warranty** before the freshness rule applies to it at all: `TRADE_ROUTE_FOUNDING_GRACE_WINDOW`, 36 ticks. A route with no founder gets no grace, so weather still decays on inactivity alone, which is the scope line the ticket drew and the reason the pre-existing decay tests are untouched by the rule.

Three sizing decisions, each load-bearing:

- **36, not a rounder-sounding number.** It has to outlast the counter-play's whole detect-and-complete cycle or the kind is still unreachable: one `UNDERTAKING_CHECKPOINT_INTERVAL_TICKS` (6) for a warlord to notice and select the candidate, plus `strategic_blockade_route`'s `projectDuration` (4). 36 is three days (`TICKS_PER_DAY` 12) — about three of those cycles, leaving room for the second or third warlord to be the one who comes.
- **A window, not an exemption.** Making a held route immortal was the larger behaviour change: routes would accumulate for a whole run and inflate prosperity across the map, and dissolving an abandoned route would move onto paths that do not all exist yet. The window keeps the decay economy intact and dying on the same rule as before. It only stops the world deleting a season of work before anything can react to it.
- **Option 1 was available and was not taken.** Minting at a higher `volume` is the cheapest fix, and it lands in `strategicGraphOps.ts` — a file open in THR-1309's PR #1690. The coordination block named that mutex as conditional on the option chosen; options 2 and 3 are disjoint, verified against `gh pr view 1690 --json files` rather than assumed, so the reversal is recorded rather than argued.

**A latent bug surfaced on the way.** `readTradeRouteProps` read `raw.established`; every writer in the tree stamps `establishedTick`. So the field resolved to its `0` fail-soft default on *every route that has ever existed*. It was load-bearing in one place — `totalTicksActive` on the dissolved trace, which therefore reported the absolute tick as a lifetime — and it would have silently defeated a grace window keyed on founding tick. Now reads the spelling writers use, with `established` kept as a legacy fallback.

## Evidence

The gate is the organic arc, not a unit test — that is how the defect survived to be found at closeout in the first place. Seed 42, 150 ticks, medium map:

**The Thornhaven–Hawkgate Road** was founded, minted its identity node, was christened, and was **blockaded by `ind_7` at tick 96**. Hawkgate's prosperity fell **35.35 → 6.73**, then recovered to **29.58** by tick 130 as `routeEvents` lifted the horizon — the counter suspending rather than deleting, exactly as the kind was designed. The chronicle resolved *"A Road Falls Silent."* The T2 vertical slice is whole for the first time: a route founded, owned, and taken back.

Golden comparison, both seeds, same instrument on both arms (the baseline arm sets the constant to 0, which reproduces pre-fix behaviour exactly — proven by 5 of the new tests failing there and all 14 pre-existing ones passing):

| | seed 42 before | seed 42 after | seed 99 before | seed 99 after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Routes standing at t150 | 0 | **2** | 0 | 0 |
| Identity nodes blockaded | 0 | **1** | 0 | 0 |
| Total prosperity | 1485.97 | 1465.79 | 2127.825 | 2127.825 |
| Tick events | 110 | 92 | 135 | 135 |

**Seed 99 is byte-identical on every metric** — it mints zero routes even at baseline, so the grace window is unreachable there and the change is provably inert. That is the ideal golden result for a seed whose code path never fires; it is also a *second, separate* defect, filed rather than fixed here.

**Every seed-42 diff, explained.** Routes 0→2 and blockades 0→1 are the intent. The rest is divergence cascade and should be read as such: once the first route survives, PRNG stream consumption changes and the world's trajectory forks — agents 539→536, locations 912→920, events 110→92. The −1.36% total-prosperity move is *not* the Done-when's evidence and should not be read as one; the endpoint-level measurement above is, and it moves in the right direction and the right magnitude.

`npm run census:undertakings`: liveness **95.0%** (seed 42) and **98.7%** (seed 99), both far above the ≥50% the ticket asks for. The census *verdict* is FAIL on one gate — `undertaking share in [0.1, 0.35]` reads 9.7% on seed 99 — and that is **pre-existing, not from this change**: the two arms produce byte-identical seed-99 output, verdict line included.

## Deferred, not swept in

Seed 99 mints **zero** trade routes across 150 ticks, at baseline and after. Founding starves upstream of anything decay does, so the same kind stays unreachable on that seed for an unrelated reason. Filed as its own ticket rather than chased here, on the THR-1310 precedent this ticket was itself filed under.
