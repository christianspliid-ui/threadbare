### 2026-10-03 — THR-1700: a blessing you already hold is no longer offered again

**Hearthfire Blessing locks on a tavern the god already blesses.** Before this, the card was still castable there. Re-casting it charged the full establishment price, played the success narration, and established nothing, because THR-662's spawn guard refuses the duplicate without telling anyone. The card now reads **Already held** on that tavern. It is castable again once the held blessing lapses, on any other tavern, and when someone else holds it.

- `getTargetActionSlots` takes `heldControlEffects` + `controlOwnerId`. Gate 10, the held lock, runs before the essence and range locks: when the verb is held, that is the true reason.
- One predicate now serves both sides: `findHeldNonStackingEffect` (`controlEffectSpawn.ts`). The spawn guard and the hand read it, so they cannot drift apart.
- Both hand surfaces pass it: `useTargetActions` and `useAgentInteraction`, with `gameState.controlEffects` in their memo deps.

**Decision recorded: stacking verbs stay castable.** Consecrate and Sanctify are left open on a target already held. Their per-tick thread auras add up, and THR-662 left them out of `NON_STACKING_CONTROL_TEMPLATE_IDS` on purpose. The lock reads that set, so making a verb non-stacking is one entry in it.

**Evidence.** jsdom render through the real hook, the real template, and the real `CardFace`: held → "Already held" on the card; none held or lapsed → castable, with no lock text.
