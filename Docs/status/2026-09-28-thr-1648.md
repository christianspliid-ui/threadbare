# 2026-09-28 — THR-1648: a quiet first screen

**Ticket:** [THR-1648](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1648) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1605-the-opening.md` § S5 · **Pillars:** UI (top bar, ascendant bar, ledger launcher); Engine: N/A (reads the existing `isFirstBonded`); Content: N/A (the doom wake line is THR-1646's, already a toast).

## What was wrong

Round-1 cold testers met rival war toasts, a climbing doom bar, rotating omens and a Chapter Ledger badge in the hundreds inside their first minute, before they had met a single mortal.

## What changed

- `src/components/Game/GameView/firstScreenReveal.ts` — `selectFirstScreenReveal` (pure) and `useFirstScreenReveal` (latched: a surface, once shown, stays).
- Until The First is bonded, `GameViewTopBar` hides the doom bar, the rivals button, the notables button and the omen indicator, and `AscendantBar` hides the Mandate section (Law 53).
- At the bond, doom, mandate and notables appear together; the THR-1646 wake line arrives as a toast on the wake tick. Rivals appear with the first rival action (an intervention or a launched scheme). Omens appear with the first omen, as before.
- `?seeded` pre-bonds The First, so every dev URL shows the full HUD unchanged.
- The Chapter Ledger launcher badge counts chapters that appeared since the ledger was last opened (UI-local, resets on reload), not the running total.
- After the ledger closes, focus goes to the game surface instead of back to the launcher, so the next Enter no longer reopens it.

## Evidence

Playwright on the real first-run path (StartPage → RemembranceFlow → GameView) at 1920×1080: before the bond the top bar shows only time, world-soul and attention; after the bond it shows Doom and Notables, and the Mandate section is back on the ascendant bar. Rivals appeared about 60 ticks later with the first rival action. The ledger badge read 1, then 0 after opening. Focus returned to `BODY`, and Enter did not reopen the ledger.
