### 2026-10-03 — THR-1709: the Divine Court fits the screen, sits on top, and stops the clock

**The bug.** Cold playtest round 2: the court screen opened with its top cut off under the header and no reachable close button, the top bar and the Active Locations strip painted over it, and the world kept ticking behind it. The overlay centred content taller than 1080 px inside a scrolling `fixed inset-0` layer, so the header and ✕ were pushed off-screen. It mounted inside an `AnimateMount` whose animated wrapper is a stacking context, so its z-50 was trapped below the z-30 top bar and z-20 Active Locations. And it was not in the interrupt registry, so the auto-pause never saw it.

**The fix.** `ScryOverlay` now portals itself to `<body>`, so no ancestor can trap it. The outer layer never scrolls. The panel is centred with `margin:auto` and capped at `SCRY_PANEL_MAX_HEIGHT` (75vh, the Modal primitive's value). The header and ✕ sit outside the scroll area, and only the court body scrolls. GameView mounts it with a plain `scryVisible &&` (no `AnimateMount`), and the overlay carries its own `anim-fade-enter`. The court is registered in `interruptRegistry.ts` as `ScryOverlay` (`courtOpen`), so it pauses the clock and holds the popup channel like the Chapter Ledger. The debug list's separate `ScryOverlay` push was removed: the registry now reports it. Clicking a courtier closes the court before it opens the action drawer, so the drawer can't open hidden behind the body-level overlay. Escape stays two-stage: the first press closes the picker, the second closes the court.

**Evidence.** `npm run gate`: test, typecheck, build and encounter all PASS. New tests: the court stops the world and holds a queued popup (`interruptRegistry.test.ts`); the overlay is a direct child of `<body>`, the outer layer is `overflow-hidden`, the body scrolls, and the ✕ is outside the scrolling body (`ScryOverlay.test.tsx`). Browser at 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, court opened from Investiture while the clock ran: `getInterruptState()` went from `{open:[], running:true}` to `{open:["ScryOverlay"], running:false, wasRunningBeforeInterrupt:true}`. The ✕ is at y=172. `elementFromPoint` at the top-bar and Active-Locations positions returns court elements. The court body holds 1246 px of content in a 669 px box and scrolls. Closing resumes the clock. Clicking The First closes the court and opens the ActionDrawer. No console errors.
