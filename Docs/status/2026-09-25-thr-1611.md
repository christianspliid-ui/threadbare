## What was wrong

Canon had delegated wayfinder decisions to "the session that works them" since 2026-09-11 (process.md rule 4), and the ways-of-working mandate has always put authoring downstream of agreement. But no routine lane was allowed to be that session: the `wayfinder` skill still said *HITL means Christian, live, in chat*, orchestrator T1.5 surfaced every grilling/prototype ticket to him as an ask, and orchestrator T2 staged plan docs "for an attended Opus session" on a Sonnet premise measured dead on 2026-09-21. On 2026-09-25 that left ten open decisions on the living-world map (THR-1589) and five plan-doc tickets (THR-1570, THR-1571, THR-1572, THR-1274, THR-1586) waiting for Christian to open a chat.

## What changed

- **New lane `tb-design-lane`** (`14 2,8,14,20 * * *`) and skill `.claude/skills/design-lane/SKILL.md`. One unit per run: decide an unreserved map ticket by delegation (decision record + outputs on the ticket), close a cleared map with its carve-up, or author a plan doc through `design-session` with every gate. Prompt mirrored at `Docs/ops/scheduled-task-prompts/tb-design-lane.md`.
- **Christian keeps** charting, tickets under a map's new `## Reserved for Christian` section, forks with no agreed outcome, and "not fun" calls. Charting sessions now ask "any of these yours alone?" before leaving.
- **Veto, not ask.** The lane's report carries `## Decided for you`; `keep-work-flowing-cc` folds those lines (≤24 h) into a briefing section that never enters the ask list or rings Discord. No plan doc starts on a lane decision younger than 24 h. The lane applies vetoes from the briefing's *From Christian* before any new work.
- **Reconciled:** `wayfinder` (HITL rule, map template, lanes table), `orchestrator` + `tb-orchestrator` prompt (T1.5 surfaces only reserved tickets; T2 stages for the lane; the split re-justified without the Sonnet premise), `design-session` (unattended note), process.md rule 4 (fifth case), ways-of-working mandate, scheduled-task registry.
