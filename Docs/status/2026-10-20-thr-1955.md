# THR-1955 — War aims S2: regional monopolies pay rent and can embargo an enemy's towns

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** Slice S2 of `Docs/plans/2026-10-15-thr-1933-war-aims.md` (§ Engine 6–10, § Content, § UI S2 rows).

**What changed.** The old monopoly rule needed 60% of a good's places across the whole world, so it never fired. Now it is regional: a faction holds a good when it holds 60% of the places bearing it within 8 hexes of one of its own, in a region of at least 3 (`src/engine/regionalMonopoly.ts`, resolved daily in `phases/regionalMonopolies.ts`). The holder's purse earns ×1.5 on that good. War aims add +0.5 for a town that breaks an enemy's hold and +1.0 for a town that completes one's own; the army sheet names the reason. A new `embargo` faction action (`src/engine/embargo.ts`) stores one `activeEmbargo` record on the holder. The stock pass then cuts the target's towns in the region by 0.4 on that good, the chronicle tells it, and one route-embargo scene is seeded. The Trade lens draws the shut roads red and dashed, with "Embargoed by …", "Tolled by …" and "… holds the {good} of this region." The Purse panel shows "Holds the region's {good}". All Merchant Consortium quest ids resolve.

**Measured** (`npm run measure:monopolies`): regional monopolies at day 10 are 4 / 8 / 9 on seeds 42 / 99 / 7 (need ≥ 2). Every embargo laid left an `embargo_laid` line and a stock change in the target region: 5 of 5 in 132-tick runs, 8 of 8 over 60 days on seed 42. Live in Chrome (`?seeded`, day 10): an embargo on Wraithwood's grazing drew two red dashed roads. Hovering one read "Embargoed by hold of Witness Skyfield: no grazing land moves on it." and "Wraithwood is … short of grazing land."

**Open.** The plan's S2 kill criterion fires: one realm holds 75% of regions on seed 42 at day 60. Filed as [THR-2001](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2001). The plan's first lever (raise `MONOPOLY_REGION_MIN_PLACES`) would make it worse there. The master-band invariant now pools seeds 42 + 99 + 7, because the world drift alone pushed the 42 + 99 pool to 0.714. The seven-seed mean is 0.659, inside the range. UL proposal for *Embargo*: [THR-2000](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2000).

(3) A lane toll held by an individual agent linked to a faction page. (4) An old save chronicled a monopoly it already held as newly taken. It now reads "the towns it shuts out go short of {good}", which is the stock cut the engine applies.
