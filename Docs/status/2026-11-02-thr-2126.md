# THR-2126 — Ledger rail: a long Chronicle Now tab no longer squeezes Threads

**Shipped 2026-11-02.** UI Visual Overhaul — Design System v1. Bug filed from THR-2103's browser verification; no plan doc.

**Cause.** `LedgerRail.tsx` sized the Chronicle section `flex: 1 1 auto`, so its flex basis was its content height. With "Before you woke" open on the Now tab that content runs to ~25,000 px, and the shrink took Threads (`0 1 auto`, `minHeight: 0`) down to ~12 px.

**Fix.** The Chronicle grows from a zero basis (`flex: 1 1 0`) above its 12 rem floor, so it fills only the free height and its entries scroll inside the panel (Law 57). Threads keeps its content height and scrolls only once the Chronicle is at its floor, which is what `LEDGER_CHRONICLE_MIN_REM` always documented. The sizing lives in an exported `ledgerSectionStyle()` with a unit test.

**Evidence (1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, 30 ticks, Now tab, Before you woke open).** Threads region 236 px tall (one `thread-entry` is 115 px; was 12 px). Chronicle entries scroll inside: 457 px window over 25,385 px. Page height 1080 = viewport. Screenshot: `Docs/evidence/thr-2126/ledger-now-tab-1920.png`.
