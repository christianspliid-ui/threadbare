# THR-2051 — An oath from tribute

**Shipped 2026-10-27.** M3: Dynamic Economy. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-26-thr-2051-oath-from-tribute.md`.

**What changed.** *The Tribute Exchange* now swears what its last step promises. Its critical success, success and success-at-a-cost bands fire the crown's existing `faction_broker_fealty` effect with a new optional `liegeFrom: 'scene_town'`. The actor's own house (their top swearable non-realm faction) swears a `persuaded` oath to the crown the hall's town answers to. That crown is the realm that holds the town, or that realm's liege, or the realm the town's temple order, company or guild holder is sworn to (`townLiegeOf` in `crown/brokeredOath.ts`). Failure pays the tribute and swears nothing. The Means rows are unchanged. Every crown rule still applies through `swearBrokeredOath`. A refusal writes nothing and traces `no_realm`, `no_vassal`, `may_not_swear` or `cooldown`. Both `faction_broker_fealty` traces now carry `liegeFrom`. *Faction unification* and *the Warlord's Tribute* keep the default `broker_realm`.

**Evidence.** `crownTributeOath.test.ts` (10 tests) covers four cases. A temple-order-held town swears the guild to the order's realm, and the sheet reads *"won by Wren Callow's word."* A realm-held town swears to that realm, or to its liege one layer up. An unheld town or one held by an unsworn company gives `no_realm`, and a realm-only actor gives `no_vassal`. Without `liegeFrom`, THR-1944 is unchanged. The content test checks that only the three winning bands carry the oath and that the Means rows are untouched. `crownBrokeredOath.test.ts` 19/19 passes unchanged. Census (scratch, medium, 360 ticks): on seed 42 there were 14 tribute attempts (no_vassal 7, no_realm 5, may_not_swear 2) and 16 oaths in the world. On seed 99 there were 5 attempts (no_realm 2, no_vassal 2, may_not_swear 1) and 18 oaths. No tribute oath was sworn on either seed, so the kill criterion (more than a third of oaths) is not tripped. That matches the plan's census: the actor's house is the scarce side. No UI code changed. The browser screenshot is replaced by these refusal traces, which the plan accepts as evidence.
