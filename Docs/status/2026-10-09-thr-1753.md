# 2026-10-09 — THR-1753: elder magic is found, and finding it opens its cards

**Ticket:** [THR-1753](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1753) · **Follows:** THR-1749 (Buy your spheres)

Once gods buy their spheres at Remembrance, nobody holds chaos, order, light or darkness, and the six card types those spheres sign (gambit, stumble, favor, whisper, veil, undertow) had no way into a hand. Canon says Foundation magic is found in ruins (rulebook §5). Finding it now opens those cards.

- **The rule (Option 1 of three).** A Foundation sphere is *found* once the god has earned `FOUNDATION_DISCOVERY_ESSENCE_THRESHOLD` (1) lifetime essence in it (`discoveredFoundationSpheres`, `src/engine/nudgeCardRepertoire.ts`). A found sphere opens its signed cards at the secondary tier (discounted). Identity still outranks it: a god who holds the sphere keeps it at full strength. Its attunement members then deepen it at 20 and 60 as before.
- **Why this route.** Every elder grant already lands on `essenceEarnedBySphere` through the phase-merge seam, so there is no new state. The rule is pure over run state, so a saved run replays it. A milestone per sphere (option 2) would mint six ids for one rule. Gating at the attunement marks (option 3) would make one ruin find worth nothing, because a find pays 1.25 per sphere.
- **Provenance.** A found card's repertoire source is `discovery`. When dealt, it reads "From your repertoire — Darkness found in the ruins." The dealer counts it as earned (provenance bonus).
- **One list.** `elderEssenceReward.ts` now spreads over the shared `FOUNDATION_SPHERE_NAMES` instead of a private copy. New contract: `elder-essence-finds-foundation-cards`.

**Evidence.** `foundationDiscovery-seededRun.test.ts` (heavy, seed 42 small) checks four cases. An ordinary run finds nothing. One elder hidden-site reveal opens all six types. A non-elder reveal opens none. The smallest ruin award opens only its own sphere. Unit tests in `nudgeCardRepertoire.test.ts` cover the access rule and the Insurance⁺ edge, and `dealtCardProvenance.test.tsx` renders the dealt face.
