# 2026-10-09 — THR-1753: elder magic is found, and finding it opens its cards

**Ticket:** [THR-1753](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1753) · **Follows:** THR-1749 (Buy your spheres)

Once gods buy their spheres at Remembrance, nobody holds chaos, order, light or darkness, and the six card types those spheres sign (gambit, stumble, favor, whisper, veil, undertow) had no way into a hand. Canon says Foundation magic is found in ruins (rulebook §5). Finding it now opens those cards.

- **The rule (Option 1 of three).** An elder grant that pays at least `FOUNDATION_DISCOVERY_ESSENCE_THRESHOLD` (1) into a Foundation sphere *finds* it. `recordFoundationFinds` (`src/engine/elderEssenceReward.ts`) writes `GameState.foundationSpheresFound` at the two sites that apply an elder award, the hidden-site reveal and the ruin transformation, and emits `ruins.foundation_sphere_found`. A found sphere opens its signed cards at the secondary tier (discounted). Identity still outranks it: a god who holds the sphere keeps it at full strength. Its attunement members then deepen it at 20 and 60 as before.
- **Why this route, and why not the ledger.** The first cut read finds off `essenceEarnedBySphere`. The review gate showed that income pays every sphere a 4% floor (`distributeBySpherePoints`), so every god would have "found" all four spheres within a few dozen ticks without touching a ruin. The seeded test now pins that negative: a 60-tick run earns Foundation essence and finds nothing. A milestone per sphere (option 2) would mint six ids for one rule. Gating at the attunement marks (option 3) would make one ruin find worth nothing, because a find pays 1.25 per sphere.
- **Provenance.** A found card's repertoire source is `discovery`. When dealt, it reads "From your repertoire — Darkness found in the ruins." The dealer counts it as earned (provenance bonus).
- **One list.** `elderEssenceReward.ts` now spreads over the shared `FOUNDATION_SPHERE_NAMES` instead of a private copy. New contract: `elder-essence-finds-foundation-cards`, a state field written at the two grant sites and read by the dealer, the nudge phase and the debug readout.

**Evidence.** `foundationDiscovery-seededRun.test.ts` (heavy, seed 42 small) checks four cases. Transforming a real ruin of the seeded map finds all four spheres and opens all six types, with four traces. A catastrophic ruin opens only its own sphere, and a Creation-sphere ruin opens none. An elder hidden-site reveal finds all four spheres, and a plain one finds none. An ordinary income run finds nothing. Unit tests in `nudgeCardRepertoire.test.ts` cover the access rule and the Insurance⁺ edge, and `dealtCardProvenance.test.tsx` renders the dealt face.
