## What shipped

- **`monsterLairBranch`** (new, `src/engine/monsters/monsterFelling.ts`), the first shipped entry in `FIGHT_END_BRANCHES`. For a fight whose opponent `isMonster`:
  - `overcome` → `markMortalDead(cause 'fight', retain, slainBy fighter)`. Only on `died`, and only while the lair is an active `lair` whose `namedEliteId` still names this monster: at **major** `clearLair` credited to the victor's first `member_of`, then `touchStructure`; at **legendary** `clearingProgress += 0.5 × 14` (7), `namedEliteId` cleared, the monster faction left standing.
  - `warded` / `echo` / `not_a_mortal` → nothing to the lair, so two `overcome` dispatches credit it once.
  - `driven_off` → `clearingProgress += 1`.
  - Every write lands as `fightState.lairOutcome` and a `monster.felled` / `monster.driven_off` trace (registered in the trace trio).
- Constants `MONSTER_FELLED_CLEARING_PRESSURE` (0.5) and `MONSTER_DRIVEN_OFF_CLEARING_PROGRESS` (1) in `src/data/monster-families.ts`. `lairClearing.ts` exports its credit rule as `clearingCreditFactionOf`.
- `DEFAULT_FIGHT_END_BRANCHES` + `resetFightEndBranches()`: tests now restore the shipped branches instead of emptying the list.
- The fight calibration harness raises its fixture opponent between fights; a felled beast otherwise ended every later fight `opponent_gone` (99.8% `broke_off`).
- Interface map row `fight-fells-monster-clears-lair`; wiki page `armies-battles-reference`; rulebook §7 **Monsters** flips felling to IMPL.
