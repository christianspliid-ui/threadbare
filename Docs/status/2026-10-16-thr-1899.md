# 2026-10-16 — THR-1899: one steady marker size per zoom band; place names step aside and weigh by importance

**Ticket:** [THR-1899](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1899), slice "Map markers and labels" of [THR-1878](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1878) (plan § D5 plain rows, § D14; THR-1831 § 4 and § 6, THR-1833 § 5). Engine: one additive field on the `map.agents` view row (`isThreaded`). Content: the `MAP_MARKERS` list. UI: agent sprites, place and realm labels, label collision.

- **`MAP_MARKERS` (`src/data/map-markers.ts`).** The one list of marker kinds the map draws on the land. Plain rows: `agent`, `threadedAgent`, `avatar`, `army`, `bandDrop`, `battle`, `siege`. Each row has a lens (null = plain), a draw kind with per-band overrides, screen px per zoom band (or `'mesh'`), icon concept, ink, legend entries and whether place names step aside for it. Named test `mapMarkers.coverage.test.ts`. Battle and siege carry their legend words; they join the Trouble legend when Lenses II adds that lens.
- **Screen-fixed markers.** A sprite's world scale is `sizePx / k`, so each marker keeps one size inside a band: a threaded mortal is a 12 px gold pip at full-world, a 32/40 px medallion at continental/regional and a 64 px portrait close in; the avatar keeps its 82 px close-up; a plain mortal is a 10/14 px faction dot, then a 48 px portrait. The world-space growth to `SPRITE_SCALE_REFERENCE_ZOOM` and the dead retinue-dot branch are gone. Click targets follow the drawn size.
- **Names step aside.** Threaded medallions and the avatar (every band) and close-up portraits are label obstacles (`overlay/markerObstacles.ts`). A place name that hits one moves 14 px down to its free slot or hides; the marker never moves. Measured: one pass with 400 labels and 40 obstacles at 2560×1440 has a median of 0.41 ms in Node; the browser's slowest pass was 0.3 ms. Resizing 300 agent sprites per zoom event has a median of 0.024 ms.
- **Label type.** Place names are in Alegreya Sans, light on a dark halo (`paint-order: stroke`). Sizes step by band (16/14/13/– at k 5–10, 19/16/14/12 at 10–15, 22/19/16/14 close in). Weights are capital 800, city 700, town 500, small 400; 800 falls back to 700 until THR-1879 ships the cut. Realm names are title case at 0.02em, Cinzel 600 from 22 px, Alegreya Sans 700 below.
- **Debug:** `__DEBUG.getMapChrome()` now also reports `k`, `tier`, `band`, `markers` (`byKind`, `visibleByKind`, `sizePx`, `drawnPx`) and `labels` (last and max pass ms, obstacles, moved, hidden).
