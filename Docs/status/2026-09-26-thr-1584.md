# 2026-09-26 — THR-1584: Mastery is permanent, and graduating a mentorship is worth something

**Ticket:** [THR-1584](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1584) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** the ticket body (the agent's call under the 2026-09-24 forecast-window ruling; Christian may veto) · **Pillars:** Engine + Content. UI is N/A: nothing on screen changed, and the sheet already reads the dice curve (THR-1583).

## What shipped

1. **Mastery decay is retired by a switch.** `MASTERY_DECAY_ENABLED = false` (`src/data/mastery-trait-content.ts`). While it is off, the seven reach-mastery definitions carry no `decayPeriod`, and `processTraitDecay` reads that as "never decays". The decay had never fired anyway: `grantMasteryTrait` writes `appliedAt`, not `lastReinforcedTick`, so the elapsed time was `NaN`. It was retired on purpose rather than fixed, because fixing it would make mortals lose skill over time, which the ruling "failure never costs skill" rules out. The two economic masteries (Trade Baron, Smuggler) are status marks re-judged every tick, so they keep their own decay.
2. **Each mastery level is now worth `MASTERY_RAW_PER_LEVEL` = 5 raw** (Anointed: 6, keeping its old 1.2× premium). It was 0.10.

## Evidence

- **Before/after for a graduate:** the apprentice starts at raw 30 in heart. `resolveMentorship` graduates it at level 2, which adds +10 raw (40). On the dice curve its heart capability goes from **0.50 to 0.69**. The old +0.2 raw moved it by under half a percentage point. Pinned in `masteryPermanence.thr1584.test.ts`, which drives the real mentorship path.
- **Permanence:** a graduate's mastery level is unchanged after 200 ticks of `processTraitDecay`. It stays unchanged even when the grant carries a `lastReinforcedTick`, which guards against a later timestamp "fix".
- **Gates:** `npm test` 1350 files / 21818 passed. `npm run test:heavy` 36 files / 220 passed. Ratchet OK at 2818, unchanged. `npx vite build` OK. 30-tick CLI smoke on seed 42 medium: 487 agents at tick 30.
- **Headless:** no seed-99 world produced a graduate by tick 178 (the twilight phase stops the CLI run there). Graduates are rare, 0–2 per 300 ticks by the ticket's own measurement, so the delta comes from the test rather than a live world.
