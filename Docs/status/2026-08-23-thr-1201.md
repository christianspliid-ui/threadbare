# THR-1201 — the turning-point slot takes the noun, and the failure class is a different one

**Shipped 2026-08-23.** The second consumer of the same defect THR-1200 fixed one table over, and the fourth defect found on the backstory stratum in one day (after THR-1187's pole inversion and THR-1199's `{fear}` phrase). THR-1200's description explicitly forbade repointing this one along the way — *"must not repoint"* the other `VALUE_LABELS` consumers — so it got its own render review rather than riding along. That instruction earned its keep: the review found a failure class THR-1200's table does not have.

## What was wrong

`turningPointResolver` substituted `VALUE_LABELS` — the **adjective** register — into `TURNING_POINT_PROSE`'s `{value}`, where every slot follows a verb, a preposition or a possessive. All 9 pairs shipped lines like:

```
Something shifted in Mira when they chose Merciful in a situation where the other option was genuinely available.
Mira's relationship with Merciful was shaped by a circumstance in which the cost of it was visible.
The moment Mira settled into Ascetic as an operating principle came through a specific event.
What settled Ascetic for Mira was an inventory taken at a bad time.
```

## The measurement, and the thing it found that the ticket did not

The ticket predicted this was plausibly a one-line repoint, conditional on a render review agreeing — and asked for the same sweep THR-1200 ran. Sweeping all **55 bodies × 2 poles = 110 renders** through the substituted noun:

- Every `{value}` slot is a noun slot. No adjective slots, no exceptions — so the repoint fixes all of them and breaks none.
- **No body in this table puts a determiner before the placeholder at all.** THR-1200's article-agreement probe — its distinctive guard, which caught a real `"a Ambitious"` there — would be *vacuous* here. Copying that guard across unexamined would have added a green test that inspects nothing.
- What this table has instead: **two bodies OPEN a sentence with `{value}`.** Harmless while the register was the capitalized adjective; a lowercase noun in a capital position the moment it is not:

```
...leaving someone behind. loyalty is the record of what they answered.
...The decision took a moment. candour has held since.
```

So the repoint alone would have fixed 53 bodies and broken 2. Both read `"Their {value}"` now — one word, preserving the sentence, and the possessive makes the noun slot explicit to the next author.

## What shipped

- **Engine** — `turningPointResolver` reads `VALUE_NOUNS`. One line, as predicted, plus the comment naming why.
- **Content** — the two sentence-initial bodies (`loyalty_ambition`, `revelation_discretion`); the `TURNING_POINT_PROSE` header now documents the noun contract and the do-not-open-a-sentence rule; `strand-content.ts`'s `VALUE_NOUNS` doc comment, which still described this defect as open, records what the review found.
- **Tests** — a `composed sentence (THR-1201)` block mirroring THR-1200's: a coverage guard, per-key noun-register assertions that **recover the substituted token** by matching each body's own literal text around the placeholder, a **sentence-initial probe** in place of the inapplicable article probe, a probe-liveness canary, and 18 golden renders.

## Falsified in two arms

Reverting is the only evidence a negative assertion is live, and THR-1199 measured that regex shapes alone missed 16 of 18 keys.

- **Arm A — resolver reverted to `VALUE_LABELS`, bodies left fixed:** 36 tests red, exactly 18 keys × 2 layers (register assertion + golden render). Both layers catch it independently.
- **Arm B — the two bodies reverted, resolver left correct:** exactly 4 tests red — the two affected keys × two poles — and the **other 14 keys stayed green**, so the probe discriminates rather than blanket-failing.

Arm B is the one that matters: the golden renders sample body `[0]` of each key, and the two defective bodies are `[4]` and `[5]`. The golden layer cannot see this class, and the probe cannot see the register. Each catches what the other misses, which is why both are here.
