# THR-1986 — Words that wait for their moment

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** Slice S2 of the "words teach themselves" plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-18-thr-1980-words-teach-themselves.md`, E4–E5, U6–U7). Round-4 cold testers listed Attention, Dominion, Threads, Elder powers, "Asks you", "Stop interruptions", "Let it stand" and the Chronicle's "shadow tier 6" / "+0.02Q" as never understood.

**First-use captions.** A new shared primitive, `FirstUseCaption` + `FirstUseCaptionZone`, shows one muted line under a panel until the player hovers its tooltip, presses inside it, or presses "got it". At most two show at once; the rest wait in table order (`FIRST_USE_CAPTIONS_MAX_VISIBLE`). Dismissal is remembered per caption (`threadbare.ui.firstUseCaption.<id>`, Law 51). The twelve captions live in `src/data/first-use-captions.ts`: essence, threads, "Asks you" and "Stop interruptions" (both name the mortal, never a guessed pronoun), the Chapter Ledger, Attention, Dominion, Doom, Reaches, World-Soul, Omen and Elder powers. Doom and Omen sit in the top bar, which has no room for a line and clips what overflows it, so their caption floats below the item through a portal. `Tooltip` triggers now carry `data-tooltip-trigger`, so a zone can tell a tooltip hover from a pass over the panel.

**Panels arrive later.** Attention now arrives with The First's first chapter, the first moment that draws on it. Dominion is a new reveal row on the *A path opens* gift: the toast reads "New beside the map: your Reaches, Dominion, the World-Soul and Omens." Until then, no inspector page carries the Dominion row. The Elder powers header stays off the rail until the first elder charge or the first hover of a card that draws an elder sphere, then stays (latched, `threadbare.ui.elderPowersSeen`).

**The Chronicle speaks in words, about people you follow.** `TickEvent` gains `playerProse` and `chronicleGate: 'threaded_only'`. Both tier-up sites write `"Vara is now subtle in Shadow and is called Shadow Master."` (`CHRONICLE_TIER_UP_LINE`, reach word from `getCapabilityWord`, article-aware). `phaseNarrative` admits a gated event only for a mortal the god follows (a thread, or the god's own avatar by `avatar_of`), and traces each skip as `chronicle.intake.skipped`. Both rarity-graduation blocks ask the same gate before their direct Chronicle push. Resolved-action events carry a `playerProse` without the `Q` suffix, and the Chronicle card no longer prints `quintessenceDelta`. `event.message` is byte-identical. `CHRONICLE_THREADED_GROWTH_ONLY = false` restores the old intake; a test pins that.

**"Let it stand" → "Go on".**

**Executor decisions.** The plan's Dominion caption was 16 words, over the 15-word rule, so it now reads "…Your own ground costs less." No `DominionBox` component exists on main; the "sheet box" is the inspector's Dominion section (`pageResolvers.ts`), and that is what the reveal gates. The card chip has no live surface today (only `AscendantBeatModal` previews render `ActionCard`, and their slots carry no Dominion band), so no card gate was added; the review gate confirmed it. "Show every panel" also brings back the inspector's Dominion row. "Asks you" carries its caption on The First's row only.

**Measured in the browser** (bash-vite :5319, Playwright, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`): `getFirstUseCaptions()` listed `essence` and `threads` visible with four queued. Hovering an essence row's tooltip dismissed `essence` and stored it, and after a reload `essence` was still dismissed. Dismissing two more brought up Attention (in the rail) and Doom (floating over the map edge, the topmost element at its point) at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440. The page did not scroll at either size, and the console showed 0 errors.
