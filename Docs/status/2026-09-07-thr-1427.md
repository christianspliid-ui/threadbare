# THR-1427 — the undertaking grid's third view: subsystem × verb

**2026-09-07 · Engine · Thematic Pressure & Living World**

## What shipped

`npm run generate-undertaking-grid` now emits a third section, **Subsystems × verbs**, into both
`Docs/canon/undertaking-grid.generated.md` and `public/undertaking-grid-reference.html`. It is the
reader THR-1407's join was built for: that ticket made `WORLD_OBJECT_KINDS[].owningSystem` resolve
to a registry subsystem for all 34 kinds and pinned it, but nothing consumed the join, so the
question it existed to answer — *which of the world's systems does a mortal's own work actually
reach, and what happens to what it leaves behind* — still had no surface.

Each of the 27 `SUBSYSTEM_NAMES` members gets exactly one row: the kinds it owns, the strongest
cell status per verb, a mechanical status, and who reads what its live cells leave.

**The verdict, on `origin/main` at `6260806b` + this change:**

| Status | Count | Subsystems |
|---|---|---|
| LIVE-TOUCHED | 9 | World Generation, Terrain & Places · Mortal Economy & Prosperity · War, Armies & Battles · Factions & Succession · Companies & Group Travel · Attachments, Items & Possessions · Reputation & Influence · Secrets & Favors · Effects & Conditions |
| OPEN-ONLY | 1 | Agent Lifecycle |
| UNTOUCHED | 17 | the remainder |

**Agent Lifecycle being the sole OPEN-ONLY row is the view working.** Mortal is sovereign — every
verb on it is `no` — except `destroy × Mortal`, the premeditated killing Christian decided yes on
in THR-1397 and which nothing has built. One subsystem in the whole world sits reachable-but-unbuilt,
and it is the one holding the heaviest fork on the board. No count could have said that.

## The status is mechanical; the reader column is curated

Status derives from cells the generator already computes, joined by `owningSystem`: LIVE-TOUCHED if
any live cell rides an op on a kind the subsystem owns, OPEN-ONLY if only `wanted`/`later`/`open`
cells reach it, UNTOUCHED if none does. Nothing is authored, so nothing can be argued into place.

The reader column is the one curated half, transcribed from
`Docs/audits/2026-09-03-undertaking-systems-coverage-research.md` § 1 into a new `SUBSYSTEM_READERS`
export beside the existing dispositions. **Two deliberate exclusions kept plausible-sounding data
out.** The audit lists three sites no subsystem claims — `mentorshipOutcomes.ts`, `binding/binder.ts`,
`binding/remoteAnchor.ts` — and they are omitted rather than attributed by guess; under-claiming a
reader is a smaller lie than inventing one. And the audit's own § 3 split of the untouched list into
*by design* and *gap* is **absent by design**: that verdict is THR-1401's question for Christian, and
a generator that renders it would be laundering a research opinion into canon.

## The guard is falsified, not asserted

The ticket named the trap exactly — a third section that renders empty and exits zero passes every
gate while proving nothing. The new totality check is the same three-way contract the cell
dispositions already carry, and all three arms were falsified by breaking them one at a time and
watching the generator exit 1 by name:

- **missing** — deleted the `Effects & Conditions` reader entry → `LIVE-TOUCHED but has no SUBSYSTEM_READERS entry`
- **unknown name** — renamed a reader to `Conditions & Effects` → `reader 'Conditions & Effects' is not a registry subsystem`
- **stale** — added readers for `Culture`, which no live cell reaches → `readers for a subsystem no live cell reaches — stale, remove it`

`check-generated-freshness.ts:148-149` already runs this generator, so a non-zero exit is enforced by
the required CI check — the guard is live, not aspirational.

The pin itself is in `src/data/__tests__/worldObjects.test.ts`, beside THR-1407's join pins, and
asserts **names, never a count** (THR-688 rule A): every `SUBSYSTEM_NAMES` member has exactly one
row, no row names something the registry lacks, every row carries a status, and every LIVE-TOUCHED
row fills its reader column. **Red baseline proven, not assumed** — both pins fail by assertion on
`origin/main`'s generated markdown (`has no "## Subsystems × verbs" section`), not by compile error,
which is why the pin reads the artifact rather than importing the generator: `generate-undertaking-grid.ts`
calls `main()` at module scope and importing it from a test would run it.

## Two small things the rendering forced

**Subsystem names contain commas** — `War, Armies & Battles`, `Attention, Chronicle & Narrative` — so
the reader column joins on ` · `. A comma-joined list of five reading subsystems is unparseable by
eye, and the World Generation row has nine.

**`renderHtml` already had a local `rows`** (the kind table's HTML string). The new parameter is
`subsystemRows` there and `rows` in `renderMarkdown`; esbuild caught the collision immediately.

## Caveat 1 honoured

`scripts/subsystems-registry.ts` is **read, never edited**. `interface-contracts.ts:2792` validates
every contract's producer/consumer against `SUBSYSTEM_NAMES`, and `drift-scan/interface-coverage.ts`
plus `lint-plan-doc.ts` read `SUBSYSTEMS` too; a rename there sweeps three further consumers. The
import is safe by the registry's own header — pure data, no side effects, deliberately not an entry
point, which is exactly why it was extracted in THR-717.
