## What was decided

The six `family` tags `#military`, `#supply`, `#community`, `#stewardship`, `#contraband` and
`#blackmail_evidence` were seated for four army reward recipes and worn by no catalog entry.
[THR-1496](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1496) repointed those recipes at live
content, which left the six **orphaned** — asked for by nothing, worn by nothing — and the
sunset rule's deletion predicate held. The decision (recorded on the ticket by the
daily-backlog-grooming lane, veto invited via the briefing and not taken) was **sunset**, not
author bearers: authoring army-logistics attachments is new content work and belongs in its own
ticket if the army fictions are ever revisited; re-seating a tag then costs one row.
