## What shipped

A step carrying the new `ActionStep.fightRole` (`'nerve' | 'clash'`) is a **fight step**. It still resolves through `resolveStepCore`; one pure function, `resolveFightStepInputs` (`src/engine/fights/fightStepInputs.ts`), derives the four inputs that differ:

- **Difficulty** from the opponent's card (`readOpponentCard`, `src/engine/fights/opponentCard.ts`): Dread for nerve, Might for clash, via `FIGHT_RATING_DIFFICULTY`, plus the opponent's own modifiers for that reach. The card is read from `monsterState` (plan doc 3 mints it), derived from a mortal's raw Iron score, or defaulted (fair, fair, clock 3).
- **Reach**: authored, then the card's override, then the fighter's own `encounter_reach_override`, before capability is read.
- **Scale**: always `FIGHT_STEP_SCALE` (`regional`), at all three reads: the core's scale, the trace, and the critical-failure severity.
- **Modifiers**: the fighter's standing modifiers as the named term `standing`, read in a combat context (`computeResolutionModifiers` gained an optional `encounterType`), so an `in_combat` item works on a clash the card moved to Eye.

After the roll, the resolved reach and difficulty ride `resolutionStats` to every post-roll reader: growth, tier promotion, consumable charges, the step-prose record, telemetry and the event node's `reachTested`. Band opposition skips fight steps. Fight steps also skip the company group step (the named fighter always rolls). One `fight.step` trace per fight step. Six UL terms are seated: Fight, Opponent Card, Dread, Might, Nerve Step, Clash Step.

**Known gap, owned by FB2 (THR-1538):** an unbound `opponentRef`, a self-target or a deceased opponent rolls against the default card for now, with `opponentStatus` on the trace. FB2 routes those through the no-roll end.
