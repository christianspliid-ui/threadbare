# THR-1067 — 18 templates name the result, and a dilution pin is inverted rather than deleted

**Shipped 2026-08-12.** Every template in the re-derived predicate now reads
`vaguenessByClass.outcome === 0`. The corpus sweep at close returns the predicate
**empty**, and THR-929's adjacent class stays at **0** — no regression into the
defect this ticket was split away from.

## The predicate has two conjuncts, and applying one of them widens it 8×

The ticket's membership predicate is a conjunction: a template must report a
**`vagueness` failure**, *and* carry non-zero `vaguenessByClass.outcome`, *and*
have at least one outcome hit in `EVASIVE_VAGUENESS_TERMS`. The first sweep here
applied only the second and third clauses and returned **145** — a population
eight times the real one, and one that would have sent an executor rewriting
prose that no gate objects to.

```
outcome-class vagueness non-zero (any density): 286
vagueness-FAILING (or pinned) with outcome hits: 18   <- the ticket's population
  ... >=1 evasive outcome hit (THR-1067)        : 18
  ... indefinite-only (THR-929's class)         :  0
```

The re-derivation matched the filed list exactly — the same 18 ids, no drift, and
none of the three ids the THR-1083 comment warned might have drifted in
(`npc_befriend`, `npc_curse`, `encounter.shell_proof.reckless_wager`) is in it.
That is worth stating plainly because the ticket, correctly, told its executor not
to trust the count: this time the count survived, and only a sweep could establish
that.

## The three density-invisible templates

The comment left by THR-1083 was load-bearing. Widening `collectClassedTemplateProse`
to walk `aftermathConfig` brought ~59k words into every density denominator, which
pushed three templates under the 2.0/100w threshold with their **absolute** hit
counts unmoved. A naive re-derivation returns 15 and silently drops them.

Working population was therefore **15 density-visible + 3 pinned = 18**, and all
18 were cleared.

## Rewrites — name the result, do not dodge the term

`something` is the canonical target: *"it cost them something"* has a sentence's
shape and no result in it. Every rewrite names what actually happened rather than
finding a synonym the detector does not list.

- `encounter.decipher_ancient_inscriptions` — *"reveals something about the
  civilization… something that rewrites history"* → *"names the people who raised
  this place, and the year they walked away from it — a century before any record
  says they existed."*
- `cg.quest.escort_prisoner` — *"the prisoner knows something {name} does not. The
  tension of it fills the walk"* → *"the prisoner keeps glancing at side streets
  {name} has not thought about. That look fills the walk."* The failure afterimage
  now plants the ambush it precedes.
- `encounter.tend_the_dead` — *"The dead require something {they} cannot give"* →
  *"The dead want a name, a kin, a {adj} truth — and {they} carry none of the
  three."*
- `cg.social.citizen_petition` — *"left with something. What it was matters less
  than that they had something to carry away"* → *"left with an answer. What it was
  matters less than that they had one to carry away."*

Scene prose was left alone throughout, as the ticket required — `encounter.investigate_anomaly`
still opens *"Something is {adj} wrong here"*, which is correct scene writing and
outside the outcome scope.

## Two densities moved the wrong way and were chased down

Removing words shrinks the denominator, so an outcome rewrite can raise a density
it never touched. `divine.self.reveal` went **7.37 → 7.53** on abstraction when
*"the moment of revelation collapses"* became *"the revelation collapses"* — the
banned term left, the nominalisation stayed, the line got shorter. Rewritten again
to *"the true face begins to show, then folds back behind the veil"*, which drops
the nominalisation too: **7.37 → 6.19**. Still failing abstraction, still
pre-existing, still out of scope (THR-1092's fork), but no longer worse for having
been touched.

`encounter.pilgrimage_trial` retains a pre-existing `not-X-but-Y x2` failure, and
`npc_request_shelter` / `npc_bless` retain their second-person failures — the
ticket explicitly scoped those out ("clear the vagueness, leave the rest").

Two failures were cleared as side effects: `encounter.decipher_ancient_inscriptions`
abstraction 5.03 → 3.35 and `cg.quest.escort_prisoner` 4.52 → 3.96, both now
passing.

## The dilution pin was inverted, not deleted

`DILUTED_BUT_STILL_DEFECTIVE` in `aftermath-prose-coverage.test.ts` is a *defect*
assertion — it passes while the defect exists and fails when someone fixes the
prose. Its docstring says the right move on a fix is to delete the entry, "the
list may only shrink". All three were fixed, so following that literally deletes
the block.

**That would restore the exact blind spot the block was written to cover.** These
three sit at 1.6–1.9/100w against a 2.0 threshold, so their outcome prose is
invisible to the density gate in both directions: re-introducing `something` into
any of their afterimages trips nothing. The polarity flipped; the coverage should
not. The block now asserts `outcome === 0` with a failure message naming why no
other gate will catch a regression there.
