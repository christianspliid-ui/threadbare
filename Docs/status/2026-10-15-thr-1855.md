# 2026-10-15 — THR-1855: a gift says what it gave

**Ticket:** [THR-1855](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1855). Warm playtest round 2: 3/3 testers received a gift and could not say what it was. Engine: the signature preview and the received-cards event. Content: the rung words name the card, 87 market effect lines. UI: the gift modal shows the resolved card.

- **The modal shows the card.** The secondary-signature gift (rung 2's first key) resolves its card per god, so its beat has no static `grantsActionIds` and the modal showed no card. `previewReachSignatureGrant(state, beatId)` (ascendantBeat.ts) resolves the same card the resolve path will grant; GameView passes it as `signatureGrantId`, and the modal adds it to the reveal row. Selection beats are unchanged — the signature is not one of the choices.
- **The words name it.** The three rung-opening lines carry `{card}` ("…and it gives you one freely: The Opened Way."). The rung chronicle line fills it too. Names are the card face's own (`playerFacingTemplateName`: spell name when there is one), so the prose matches the card shown. `RUNG_GIFT_UNNAMED_CARD` is the fail-soft word.
- **Receive says what arrived.** `resolvePendingBeat` raises a chronicle-tier toast event for every newly unlocked card: "A new card is yours: {cards}." (`GIFT_CARDS_RECEIVED_LINE`, receipt-content.ts). A gift whose card was already held raises nothing.
- **Market cards each have their own line.** 87 of the 143 god cards fell to the composed line, so Amplify the Flow and Ward Against the Deep read the same sentence. Each now has an authored line in `ACTION_EFFECTS_PROSE`; land cards whose only effect is a sway or corruption change say exactly that. The guard test now covers `godCards()` and fails if two market cards share a line.
- **Debug.** `__DEBUG.fireBeat('beat.rung.2.signature.secondary')` now force-offers rung gifts.
- **Evidence.** `Docs/audits/2026-10-15-thr-1855/` — the modal naming The Opened Way and the toast after Receive, both at 1920×1080.
