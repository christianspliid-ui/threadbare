# THR-2170 — Four warm-round-3 faults fixed; the god-rail hand split to THR-2177

**Shipped 2026-11-05.** Warm playtest · round 3. The ticket listed five small faults. This ships four of them. The fifth turned out to have a different cause than the ticket named, and it is now its own ticket.

1. **One mortal no longer fills two cast slots ("Cast: Shiaru Shiaru").** `BindingRequest` gains `excludeNodeIds`. `enumerateCandidates` (`engine/binding/binder.ts`) leaves those ids off the board, so they are excluded, not just scored down. Both binders fill it in:
   - The encounter support bundle (`engine/encounterSupportBundle.ts`) passes every actor already bound in the bundle, on both the legacy matcher and the scored-binder route.
   - The undertaking bind pass (`binding/undertakingBindPass.ts`) passes the nodes live-bound to the project's *other* cast keys. The slot's own holder stays a candidate, so carry-forward continuity is unchanged.

   Tests: two same-role slots bind two people, and a second slot never re-binds the only scholar present. Both bundle tests fail without the fix.
2. **Personal names have a length cap and a repeat check.** `NAME_MAX_LENGTH = 11` and `NAME_MAX_REPEATS = 2` (`engine/culturePhonetics.ts`). `hasRepeatsMoreThan` rejects a name in which any syllable chunk (onset + nucleus, via the new `syllableChunks`) or any letter pair occurs three times. "Oetgotkoetgoeyt" fails both checks. The reject trace gains the reasons `length` and `repeats`. A new sweep test generates 10 000 seeded names across 4 foundations × every sphere: none is over the cap, none repeats a syllable three times, and fewer than 5 % of draws fall back to the curated pool.
3. **The fealty loyalty word no longer flips daily.** The crown's daily pass writes `loyaltyTrend` to the `sworn_to` edge. It is an exponential moving average (`LOYALTY_TREND_EMA_ALPHA = 0.25`) computed in the new `engine/crown/loyaltyTrend.ts`. `trendClause` reads it through `LOYALTY_TREND_DEAD_BAND = 0.003`:
   - a liege's means swinging between rich and destitute (±0.01 a day) reads "holds steady" throughout;
   - a steady strained fall reads "falling" from its fourth day;
   - a lost town reads "falling" the same day.

   Oaths written before this change fall back to that day's sum.
4. **The out-of-range card names the avatar and the reach.** "Too far from here." becomes "More than three hexes from your avatar." It is built by `outOfRangeReason` from the slot's new `rangeLimitHexes`, which `targetActions` sets to `DEFAULT_LOCAL_RANGE`. Range is measured from the avatar's hex, so the reason says "avatar", not "seat". With no limit on the slot, the fallback reads "Too far from your avatar."
5. **God-rail hand → [THR-2177](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2177).** The 30vh cap the ticket named applies only to the folded rows, not to the hand, which is the rail's `fill` section. Measured at 1920×1080 on `?warm=150` with every row closed: the pinned Essence block is 456 px (eight 50 px pool rows), so a five-card hand already groups and its card region gets 21 px. The fix is a design pass on Essence density.

Also: `accountRecord.test.ts` now reads its `account.world_shaped` trace before it builds a second world. One world's generation already filled most of the 2000-entry trace ring: 1924 traces before this change, 2053 after the new name rejects.
