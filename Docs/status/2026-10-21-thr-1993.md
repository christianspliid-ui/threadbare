# THR-1993 — Ordinary scenes stop recasting The First

**Shipped 2026-10-21.** The follow-up to THR-1984. That build gave The First her trade and gated the five job-cast chapters. Ordinary scenes still handed her a trade she never had: a healer was given iron stock as a smith, called "master builder" by a town that never knew her, or put on trial for her name as a star-reader.

**What changed.** The census (`Docs/audits/2026-10-20-thr-1983-ordinary-scene-trade-census.md`) is built as written.
- **Six gates.** `protagonistRoles` is added to `bf.quest.craft_commission`, `encounter.trial_of_flame`, `encounter.healers_oath`, `encounter.rally_faithful`, `encounter.town.masons_commission` and `encounter.town.comet_disputation`.
- **21 phrase rewrites.** They go into the Fellowship, the Company, `encounter-content.ts` and 14 factory encounters. Three of them use `{?has_trade}` / `{trade}`: `bf.join`, `mc.join` and `master_craftsman_challenge`.
- **Pinned test.** `src/data/__tests__/ordinary-trade-census.test.ts`.

**Deviation.** The `encounter-content.ts` raw-entry converter is a field allowlist, so `protagonistRoles` authored there would have been dropped. The converter now passes it through. This is additive, and no other entry declares it.

**Evidence.** `npm run gate` PASS 6/6 (code track): test, typecheck ratchet, build, check:encounter, god-cards, ui-debt. The 30-tick CLI smoke (seed 42, medium) reaches tick 30 with 552 agents. The census test runs 31/31. It also checks the dev First's trade (`DEV_FIRST_TRADE` = priest): the role-fit stage rejects `masons_commission` for her with an `encounter.filter.role_fit` trace and keeps `rally_faithful`.
