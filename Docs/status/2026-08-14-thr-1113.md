### THR-1113 — the Codex stops naming its own enums (2026-08-14)

The Law 13/14 residue THR-1103 scoped out: raw `agreementType`, raw effect-type enums, raw resource categories, raw sidebar labels, and three magnitude rows still quoting numbers. All closed. **Item 8 split to [THR-1114](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1114)** — correcting two authored `sphereAffinity` values is a cosmology decision, not a display one.

**Three of the ticket's premises were wrong, and the probe is why.** The build was enumerated before a line was written, rather than the data or the ticket text:

- **`reachBonus` has no population at all** — absent from all 119 possessions, so the row the ticket filed item 6 against never renders. It is fixed anyway (one call; otherwise whoever authors the first one ships `+2 Iron`), but pinned by a **direct unit test**, because a catalog sweep over zero rows passes while asserting nothing and reads as coverage.
- **`Domain Effects` rendered blank on twelve of thirteen rows.** `{}` is truthy, so the guard `...(p.domainContributions ? [row] : [])` emitted a label with nothing after it. That is a worse defect than the numeral the ticket was filed about and it named neither. Swept catalog-wide now, since the guard shape repeats across every mapper.
- **The sidebar leaked nine ids, not the four named** — and not the same four. `agreement` resolves fine; `intelligence`, `talisman` and `charm` do not, and were invisible because they only exist in the *built* rail, not in the data.

**The Law 13 call, made and recorded in the code.** THR-1103 kept a numeral for essence, but only on a written, ratified reason — a price has to be countable. Nothing comparable exists for a domain contribution or a reach bonus: not a price, never paid from a counter, no sibling surface quoting it as a number. So the default applies and they band. Not with `GROWTH_MAGNITUDE_BANDS` though — those band a *delta* and read as adverbs of motion (`grew steadily`), which is the wrong tense for a standing property of a condition the player may never have carried. Adjectives of size instead, anchored on `capabilityGrowth.ts`'s own full-weight `1.0`, and sign kept as a word: `a slight edge in Gold, a faint edge in Heart`. The two authored corpus values land on **adjacent rungs** deliberately — a ladder that cannot separate its own corpus is pinning nothing.

**`Duration` got a real unit rather than a band, and that is the Law 13 amendment applied rather than ignored.** `48 ticks` → `four days` via the game's own `TICKS_PER_DAY`, count spelled out so no numeral escapes. Christian's verdict on `grew steadily` was *"how can a player use that word to gage anything"*; `four days` is not an adverb, it is a quantity in a unit the player already reasons in. Banding it to `brief`/`lasting` would have thrown that away.

**The `?? id` tail is retired** for `resolveDisplay`, so an unlisted subcategory takes the plain-English fallback and warns once instead of painting raw — self-healing, and it says so.
