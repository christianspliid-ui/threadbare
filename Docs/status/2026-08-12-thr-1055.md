# THR-1055 — the ten Holy Order quests can now end badly, or well, or expensively

**Shipped 2026-08-12.** [THR-1055](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1055/the-ten-re-keyed-hod-templates-author-1-2-outcome-bands-under-the)

[THR-1054](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1054/ten-hod-templates-key-aftermathconfigvariants-by-step-index-band-so) made ten `hod.*` templates' authored endings reachable again — and that is what made a pre-existing gap visible: they authored **one or two** outcome bands against the Composition Contract floor of three, and **none authored an extreme**. Before the re-keying the gate reported `authors 0 outcome band(s)` for all ten, so a shortfall was indistinguishable from having no aftermath at all.

**17 new bands** bring all ten to the floor with at least one extreme each — `success_at_cost` on `temple_vigil`, `deliver_judgment` and `inquisition`; `critical_success` on `escort_pilgrims` and `lead_crusade`; `critical_failure` on `purify_shrine`, `slay_abomination`, `cleanse_corruption`, `holy_war` and `divine_trial` — with the missing plain success/failure band filled alongside wherever one was absent.

The extremes are the substance. They are the endings a playthrough rolls least, which is exactly why they go unwritten unless a contract asks: THR-1054's own verification landed `temple_vigil` on `success_at_cost` and correctly rendered the un-banded base ending, because nobody had written that band. They were authored to **cost**, not to be bigger — `temple_vigil`'s keeps the reliquary and opens the forearm, and the log records the vigil and not the arm; `inquisition`'s breaks the cult and takes two clean names with it; `holy_war`'s `critical_failure` is not the existing retreat-in-good-order louder but the host ceasing to exist in an afternoon. Seven plant a seed or a hidden mark, so a tail leaves the world changed rather than the paragraph.

**The bespoke-vs-inherited reaction split rests on a property, not a budget.** `applyAftermathOutcomeBand` layers field by field, so a band that omits `reactions` keeps the variant's. The ten extremes author their own because the tail changes what the god can usefully *do* — the divine trial's refusal offers standing with the one the Dawn turned away, which the base pair ("bear witness" / "pass by") cannot express. The seven plain fill bands inherit, because restating a correct pair is duplication that drifts.

## Two scope calls

**`concepts` on all 25 new changes; the 13 pre-existing ones untouched.** [THR-1053](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1053/the-composition-contract-requires-concepts-on-every-aftermath-change) is the unsettled question of whether that rule survives at all — the contract requires `concepts` on every change, and `EncounterAftermathChange.concepts`' own doc comment says the opposite for exactly this case. Declaring them on new work satisfies either settlement (they are fail-open decoration) without pre-empting a ~191-template retrofit ruling. Every `[aftermath]` finding still open on these ten is therefore a pre-existing change, and none is a new one.

**`polarity` uses the real union** — `gain` / `loss` / `mixed` — not the surrounding file's `positive` / `negative`, which is red-baseline rather than convention.
