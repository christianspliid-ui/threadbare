# THR-1170 — The opening beat stops dealing one plate 424 times

**Shipped 2026-08-19.** Batch 5 of the encounter image library: three situational nudge generics, and the extension to the corpus sweep that stops this class recurring on a surface the template sweep cannot see.

## The defect, confirmed before it was fixed

`src/data/meeting-dilemma-library.ts` authored **424** nudge `imageTag`s across exactly three values — `crowd` ×198, `mercy` ×120, `blade` ×106 — and none of the three was an `ENCOUNTER_IMAGE_LIBRARY` row. `BondBeat` and `FormativeTestBeat` consume `NudgePhaseShell` whole, and that shell resolves with `{tag, kind: 'nudge', sphere}` and no `concepts`, so the tag-query rung cannot fire: sphere alone scores 4 against an `IMAGE_MATCH_MIN_SCORE` of 10. Every card fell through exact-tag → tag-query → the `nudge` category generic, `generic.blessing`.

So every nudge card in every hand of the Meet-The-First flow showed the same plate — wisps settling over a plain clay bowl — on the first encounter a new player ever sees. Fail-soft by design, which is exactly why it never surfaced as a bug.

## Which fix, and why

The ticket filed two candidates and called the choice a design call: **(1)** repoint the three tags at existing rows, **(2)** generate three plates and open a new `ENCOUNTER_IMAGE_PLAN` batch. Fix (2) was taken, which is what the ticket itself recommended — and the asymmetry the filing comment flagged is the reason. Under fix (1), `mercy` would repoint to `generic.blessing`, the plate it already falls back to, so **120 of the 424 references would gain nothing a player can see**; the cheapest fix leaves a third of the corpus exactly as broken as it was, while turning the sweep green over it.

**A third option was measured and rejected, and the measurement is the interesting part.** Every nudge carries its own `sphere`, and all 16 shipped `generic.*` nudge plates are sphere-keyed — so retagging each nudge from the existing vocabulary looked like it would satisfy the Done-when with real per-card variety and *no* new art. It does not: **128 of the 424 nudges carry no `sphere` at all**, so a sphere-keyed retag cannot cover 30% of the corpus, and for the rest the sphere→concept mapping is a guess (does a worsening wound draw `life`'s `vigor` or `entropy`'s `decay`?). Recorded because "reuse what exists" is the right instinct and it fails here for a countable reason, not a taste one.

The authored vocabulary was kept rather than replaced. `crowd`/`mercy`/`blade` reads as a deliberate trio that was never generated, and the sampled card names bear that out across all three — `crowd` spans *"The council sits late"*, *"The books are open"*, *"Who else is listening"*; `mercy` spans *"The cup within reach"*, *"The breathing changes"*, *"Who eats if the wheat fails"*; `blade` spans *"The anvil itself"*, *"The tools still in {agent.name}'s hands"*, *"What can be checked"*. Those are three coherent concepts, so three plates were made for them.

Ids ship **namespaced** — `generic.crowd` / `generic.mercy` / `generic.blade` — because the library's own type doc states ids are namespaced `generic.*` / `scene.*` / `fate.*` / `portrait.*`, and a bare id would have been the one row family breaking it. Retagging the corpus to match is three string edits over 424 references.

## What the three rows deliberately do not carry

`NUDGE_CONCEPT_ART`'s sixteen name *kinds of divine help* — what the god does. These three name *the situation the nudge reaches into* — what the card is about. That difference is why they carry **no `sphere` and no baked magic**, against the pattern of every sibling row.

`crowd` alone is authored across time, mind, spirit, matter, order, life and light; add the 128 sphere-less nudges and there is no sphere any of the three could honestly claim. Colouring a plate with one sphere's thread would assert an intervention the step may not have made — which is precisely the reasoning the library already records for why `nudge`'s category generic is `generic.blessing` rather than `generic.strength`. Their light is ordinary flame instead, which no card's sphere can contradict.

## The sweep was extended, not duplicated

Per the Done-when. THR-1052 added *authored imageTags resolve against the manifest*, and it walks `UNIFIED_ACTION_TEMPLATES` only — `meeting-dilemma-library.ts` is not in that collection, which is the whole reason an identical defect sat undetected on the opening beat while the template corpus was clean. Writing a second guard would have left the same seam one file over, so the existing assertion now walks `[...authored, ...meetingAuthored]`.

**Both polarities are pinned, with literals.** Every assertion in that sweep is satisfied by an empty population, so without a non-vacuity check the whole thing proves nothing: `meetingAuthored.length` is pinned to **424** and its distinct tag count to **3**. Literals rather than a re-derived constant — keying the expectation off the same expression under test would make it a tautology.

**Falsified before trusted.** Unregistering `SITUATIONAL_NUDGE_ART` from the library spread — reproducing the pre-fix state exactly — turns the sweep red with all 424 offenders listed by name (`AX-IRON-01 / ax-iron-01-speech -> generic.crowd`, …). The manifest-integrity tests stay green through that revert, which is the point: they guard the library's honesty about itself and are structurally blind to whether authored content names a row that exists.

## Generation notes

Three plates at `/concept-art/nudge/<concept>.jpg`, 1376×768, JPEG q92 progressive, 184–257 KB — in line with the batch-2 siblings. Prompts carried the four guards the earlier batches paid for: text *carriers* enumerated rather than a bare "no text"; forbidden colours named rather than only the wanted hex; and absences asserted positively rather than added to a negative list.

The last of those did the real work on `crowd`, which is the only one of the three that needs people in frame and therefore the only one that could reproduce batch 2's `generic.vigor` failure. Rather than lengthening the "no faces" list, the subject was changed structurally so a face is not on offer: a ring of **hooded figures seen from behind**, backs and covered heads only. That is the same motivated-occluder fix the portrait archetypes landed on, and it held first try.

**Contact-sheet reviewed before registration**, per the batch procedure — all three read at full size for baked text, stray figures, palette drift and invented incident. `check:image-library` cannot catch any of those: it proves paths exist, and a wrong picture at the right path passes it. Library goes 125 → **128 rows**, plan still 0 slots.
