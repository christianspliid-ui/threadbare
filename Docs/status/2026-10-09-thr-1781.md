# 2026-10-09 — THR-1781: God's Will steers change what The First does

**Ticket:** [THR-1781](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1781) · **Found by:** warm playtest round 1, 3/3 testers (the skimmer's quit point)

Paying essence in the God's Will premonition now changes The First's next choice, and the player hears how it went.

- **Whispers are read.** A whisper wrote a `whisper_*` divine influence that nothing read. `applyWhisperPull` (`src/engine/premonitionSteer.ts`) now multiplies matching candidates by `1 + WHISPER_PULL_SCALE × strength`. A fresh whisper gives ×1.6, and the pull is gone after `WHISPER_INFLUENCE_DURATION`. The pull scales `finalScore` and rides on the candidate as `whisperPull`, which the live board multiplies in. The board never reads `finalScore`, so that is the channel that reaches the decision. Each category matches its own encounters: reach, sphere, gather-strength (low threat) and gather-courage (high threat). The top list is re-cut, so a whispered encounter just outside the five can reach the board.
- **Compulsions are held.** The popup shows ten ticks after the board it was built from, so The First is usually mid-chapter when the player pays. The old override fired only within three ticks of the click, and a busy mortal skips the decision path. The steer is now held until the next full decision, or `COMPULSION_HOLD_MAX_TICKS` (72). The paid entry is flagged `compelledTarget`, so the story breath and the 40-slot cap keep it (cap Phase 1e-bis). On the first measured run the cap had cut the target. If the top five dropped it, it is pulled up from the ranked list, and it overrides an undertaking or idle verdict. Otherwise it lapses as `expired` or `unavailable`.
- **Whispers are held too.** A whisper's pull used to run from the click, so a mortal busy for more than 25 ticks never felt it. A board-pulling whisper is now flagged `awaitingFirstRead`. The next full decision re-stamps its clock (`anchorHeldWhispers`), and the decay phase keeps it until then. If the wait passes `WHISPER_HOLD_MAX_TICKS` (72), the whisper lapses with a toast ("Your whisper fades: …") and a `WhisperOutcomeTrace`. While held, the influence chip shows it at full strength with the hold time left. This was the review gate's open finding; it was fixed in the 2026-10-09 unstick run.
- **Feedback.** Paying shows a toast that names the steer. Each compulsion ending emits a `divine_premonition` toast event ("Your will holds: …" / "Your will fades: …") and a typed `CompulsionOutcomeTrace`. `divine_premonition` now routes to the *divine* notification category.

**Evidence.** On a generated world (seed 42), a compulsion paid mid-chapter is held past the old window and taken at the next decision (`premonitionSteer.integration.test.ts`). In the browser at 1920×1080, a Gold whisper put whisperPull ×1.596 on the board's top three entries, all of them Gold encounters.
