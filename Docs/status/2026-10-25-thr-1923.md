# THR-1923 — the settlement economy: towns grow from surplus, build to their land, seats make cities, and leftovers start trade routes

**Shipped 2026-10-25.** Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1923-settlement-economy.md`. Christian's four directions (2026-10-14): goods flow à la Civ, not along roads; sinks, not stockpiles; a diverse map; only a faction seat makes a city.

**Engine.**
- New daily phase `settlement_economy` (`src/engine/phases/settlementEconomy.ts`, pre-economy, before `resource_stock_tiers`). Helpers in `src/engine/settlementEconomy/`: `land`, `growth`, `building`, `seat`, `surplusRoutes`, `tier`, `shocks`, `measures`, `describe`. Every number in `src/data/settlement-economy-constants.ts`; tables `terrain-yields.ts` (every terrain), `settlement-buildings.ts` (19 buildings), `special-goods.ts`, `faction-building-lean.ts` (reads the purse's natural-places table).
- Each settlement works its hexes, grows from food surplus, raises buildings (Places with `isBuilding`) into tier slots, sends a share of surplus to its holder's seat, and sells what is left on a surplus route (`trades_with`, `establishedBy: 'surplus'`). It writes `population`, the meters, `foodBalance`, `buildBalance`, `resourceBalance` and `targetTier`.
- A 100-day muted settling run at the tail of `seedWorld` sets day-0 sizes.
- Readers switched: `phaseSettlementPromotion` follows `targetTier` (a capital can now drop), the stock-tier phase sets food staples from `foodBalance`, the unrest target's contentment is the economy's happiness term (THR-1934's planned switch), and fourteen new location traits. The four economic god verbs append a land shock that fades over a season.

**Content.** Fourteen location-trait definitions with step terms, pool rows and prose; six chronicle triggers; tooltips for every building, the size word and the land line; five new Place types with category art.

**UI.** The place page gains a Size row ("A town, growing.") and an economy Livelihood line (fed or short, where the surplus goes, what comes in, a blight or siege while it lasts); building Places read "built by the town" or why they idle; the cast receipt gains a land line; `__DEBUG.getSettlementEconomy` / `getSettlementDiversity`; a Settlements table in the Economy debug tab.

**Measured.** Phase alone, 150 days after the settling run: all four diversity measures PASS on seeds 42 and 99 (seed 42: size CV 0.51 → 0.52, 81% of same-tier pairs distinct, 23 trait sets, top trait Market Town 47%). Day-0 tier within one step of the genome: 36 of 38 (seed 42), 50 of 54 (seed 99). Phase 1.6–1.9 ms a day on medium; settling run about 170 ms. Tuning: `MARKET_TOWN_ENTER` 3 → 4 (Market Town read 53% on seed 42 at 3).

**Deferred.** THR-2032 (check the WebGL map icon redraws on a runtime tier change — not visible to Playwright), THR-2033 (a rising sacks the costliest building).
