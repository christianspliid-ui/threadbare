# THR-2257 — A lost chapter never raises reputation

**Shipped 2026-11-20.** Cold playtest round 8 (3/3 testers): "Seren lost Decipher Old Markings" sat over "Seren's reputation rose a little", against the "At Stake" line that promised a fall on failure.

- **Cause.** Two-step templates author about +0.02 on a step's success and −0.01 on its failure, and each step's delta lands as the step resolves. "Pass step 1, fail step 2" netted +0.01.
- **Rule (engine, no template edits).** When the chapter ends lost (`failure`, `critical_failure`, `contested_lost` — the outcomes the ending sentence calls "lost"), the earlier steps' reputation gains are forfeited and the ending keeps only its losses; the settled net is never above zero. `settleLostEncounterReputation` / `isLostActionOutcome` in `src/engine/reputationAftermathFold.ts`, applied at the THR-1789 fold site in `unifiedActionResolution.ts`. The correction is applied to `reputationScore` so the sheet agrees with the row (Law 56), and traced as `reputation_lost_encounter_settled`; the step-level movement stays in the trail.
- **Boundary.** A chapter "won, at a price" (`success_at_cost`) is a win and keeps what its steps earned.
- **Out of scope.** Faction standing (`processFactionEncounterReputation`) is a separate row and is unchanged.
