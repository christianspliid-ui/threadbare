**2026-09-04 — THR-1410 (UI, Bug): the encounter veil's commit button comes out from under the footer.**

The authored-choice veil could not be committed at 1920×1080. The footer strip (`◆ N essence · Look away`) is `position: absolute; bottom: 0; z-index: 20` floating **over** the content column, not sitting in flow beside it, so the column has to reserve the strip's height at its own bottom or whatever scrolls to the end lands underneath it. The column reserved `8vh` — 86.4px at the viewport contract — against a strip measuring 94px. On the nudge shell that 7.6px shortfall showed as an 8px overlap that `elementFromPoint` still resolved past; on `crafting.quest.flawed_steel`, one `ChoiceBlock` taller, it put `stage-commit` under the strip entirely and every click landed on the footer.

**The fix is the pairing, not the number.** Two named constants now sit beside the veil's other layout floors: `VEIL_FOOTER_HEIGHT_PX = 94` and `VEIL_FOOTER_CLEARANCE_PX = 24`. The footer pins the first as a `minHeight` (with `box-sizing: border-box`, so at the contract viewport it is exactly the height it already was and a floor everywhere else), and the column reserves their sum as a **px** `paddingBottom`. The unit change is the substance of the repair: a `vh` bottom padding tracks the *viewport*, and what the column has to clear is the *strip*, so the two were free to drift and did. Deriving the reservation from the strip's own geometry means retuning the footer is one number in one place (NFP #1) instead of a hunt through two inline style blocks that have no reason to know about each other.

Both commit controls are covered by the one edit — `stage-commit` (authored choices) and `nudge-commit` (`NudgePhaseShell`) render as the last block inside the same scroll column, so the reservation sits above whichever one mounts.

**Three tests, on the production veil rather than a fixture,** with `data-testid`s added to the column and the footer so they can be reached at all. The load-bearing one reads `paddingBottom` off the rendered column and `minHeight` off the rendered footer and asserts the first is at least the second — **both sides read from the DOM, neither from the imported constant**, so a constant edited on one side only still fails. A second pins the commit *inside* that column, because a reservation only protects what it contains and the suite would otherwise keep passing if the control ever moved out. A third asserts the reservation is not a `vh` and is non-zero: the column sets `padding` shorthand and `paddingBottom` longhand in one style object, and getting their order backwards silently restores the bug.

Falsified before being trusted — restoring the pre-fix `padding: '6vh 5vw 8vh 3vw'` and dropping the longhand turns 2 of the 3 red (`expected '8vh' not to match /vh$/`); the containment test correctly stays green, since it guards a different property.
