# THR-1992 — Four things the retired sheets showed are back on the Inspector

**Shipped 2026-10-21.** When the Inspector replaced the old sheets (THR-1911/1912), four displays had no row on any Inspector page, though the engine still wrote their data. The player had not seen them since S3.

**Fix.**
- **The Blood line** — the person page's Bonds tab has a `blood` row: "There is blood between them and {name} — {cause}." The name links. It reads `card.grudges`.
- **The pilgrim way** — the place page has `pilgrim_way` ("Pilgrims come here — a way of {congregation}.") and the faction page has `pilgrim_ways` ("Pilgrim ways to {towns}."). Each name links. They read the `sacred_route` edges through `selectPilgrimWays`.
- **The lair's monster card** — the lair's place page has `lair_monster`: a card with the monster's name (a link), its Dread/Might sentence, square clock pips with the clock word, and "slain by {name}" when the killer is known. In the land page's Lair row, the monster now sits under its lair, with the clock word as its fact. Both read `buildLairMonsterCardModel`. `CardTile` gains the optional `clock` and `byLine` fields, and `Section` draws them.
- **The fate word** — an Act-row card cast on this subject shows the word for how its last cast landed (Law 37). It comes from the latest `playerActionReceipts` entry whose template and target match, shown in `ActOnCardVM.fate`.

Parity: `sheetParity*.test.ts` gives each of the four a home row. The four interface contracts list the new readers, and the `THR-1912 … (THR-1992)` placeholders are gone.

**Evidence.** `restoredRows.render.test.tsx` (12 jsdom tests) checks that each display shows its words and is absent when there is no data, with no digits. Live check: worktree Vite at 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, `openRef('location','loc_4','sheet')`. Wraithwood's page reads "Pilgrims come here — a way of The Witness Skyfield Congregation of the Spheres.", and the console shows no errors (`Docs/evidence/thr-1992/place-pilgrim-way-1920.png`).
