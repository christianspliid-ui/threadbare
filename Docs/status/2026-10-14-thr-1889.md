# 2026-10-14 — THR-1889: fair luck — tier-first loot, a hidden Legendary deadline, a marble bag

**Ticket:** [THR-1889](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1889). Christian asked in chat, after three candlesan videos on loot tables and controlled randomness: *"fix and add the relevant game design principles to our relevant game design laws."* Engine + Content (re-tuned curves); UI N/A by Game Design Law 7 (the protections are invisible).

- **What the player gets.** A reward's rarity is rolled first, at the odds written down. How many items exist at each rarity no longer changes how often that rarity comes up, and a scene with nothing at the rolled rarity hands out the next one down. The player's own mortals (threaded by the player's god) roll their d100 from a marble bag: every tenth of the range once per ten rolls, in a secret order. The odds they are shown are exactly what they get, and long losing streaks cannot build. Each of those mortals also has a hidden deadline, between its 30th and 50th prize, by which its next Legendary will have arrived. Rivals' mortals and the background world keep plain dice.
- **How.** `drawTierFirst` in `rewardPool.ts` is now the pick inside `drawSeededReward` and the legacy orchestrator route. The tier curves are re-tuned to the rates the old flat draw actually paid in live play (fitted on 5,356 draws, seeds 42/99/7; reproduced within 0.3 points per outcome), which keeps today's feel and makes it stable. Before, a success's Legendary was set at 5% and paid about 1.5%. `luck.ts` (new) holds the bag and the deadline; their memory is three counts on the mortal (`properties.luck`). The three roll sites (encounter steps, a threaded fight opponent, undertaking checkpoints) pass `luckRoll` into `resolveStepCore`, which still spends its first rng draw so the shared stream is unchanged for everyone. `seedRarityTier` scales by the distribution's own total, so a short distribution no longer hands its missing weight to Legendary. New traces: `luck.bag_roll`, `luck.legendary_cycle`.
- **Laws.** New book [`Docs/game-design/laws.md`](../game-design/laws.md), § I Randomness and luck (Laws 1–8), the sibling of the UI Laws. Design governance names it in its checklist; the rulebook gains two `[IMPL]` paragraphs.
- **Deferred.** Category shares inside a tier ([THR-1890](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1890)).
- **Evidence.** See the PR body for the gate verdict block and the before/after measurements.
