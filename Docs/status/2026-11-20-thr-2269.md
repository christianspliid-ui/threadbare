# THR-2269 — The Doom dialog fits at its last stage

**Shipped 2026-11-20.** At Doom stage 5 the dialog's body held 1484px of content in a 745px box (UI Law 33). It now fits at 1920×1080 with the stage's cards, the race, the Stay line and Where it feeds all present. Built from the design lane's decision on the ticket; all in `src/components/Game/DoomClockDetail.tsx`.

1. **One copy of each card.** A passed or current stage shows its cards from `state.resolvedEvents`, once. The authored `stage.events` title list is gone (the same card, dealt 1:1 on stage entry).
2. **Recent consequences is gone.** It repeated up to four of the same cards.
3. **A card reads as its title and what it did.** The `· 1.23x` severity is dropped (Law 13); the second line is `effectSummary`, or the effect type when there is none.
4. **Passed stages fold.** A passed stage with cards is a disclosure row (the Rival/Chronicle chevron, as a ghost `Button`); open state survives closing and reopening the dialog within the session (`sessionStorage`, `threadbare.doom.openStages`). The current stage is always open; future stages stay name-only.
5. **Stage, not chapter.** "Current stage", and the footer "Stages 1–4 tighten the omen. Stage 5 is the climax, where the world-scale fallout lands."
6. **The fallback was needed.** With items 1–5 alone, the live stage-5 dialog still ran 9px over (scrollHeight 792 vs 783). So at stage 5 Where it feeds shows its newest two rows (`DOOM_DIALOG_CLIMAX_FEED_ROWS` = 2, `src/data/game-config.ts`); earlier stages keep all three.

**Evidence.** Unit: `DoomClockDetailOneCopy.thr2269.test.tsx` (each title once, no Recent consequences, no `x` severity, "Current stage", fold open/close, survives reopen), `DoomClockDetailFeedList.thr2256.test.tsx` (three rows at stage 4, two at stage 5). Live (bash-vite + private headless Chromium, 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium&warm=600`, ticked to stage 5): scrollHeight 742 ≤ clientHeight 742, 2 cards, race, Stay, 2 feed rows, 0 console errors — `Docs/evidence/thr-2269/doom-stage5-fits-1920.png`.

Out of scope: the left column's raw numbers ("Doom debt +1.0", "Speed modifier ×0.90"), filed as THR-2270.
