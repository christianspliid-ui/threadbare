# 2026-10-10 — THR-1804: five small faults from cold playtest round 3

**Ticket:** [THR-1804](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1804) · **Found by:** cold playtest round 3 (veteran, skimmer and story personas).

- **1. Setup cards take no clicks while invisible.** `DriveBeat`, `OriginBeat` and `TransformationBeat` set `pointerEvents: 'none'` on their card row until it fades in (the `RevealBeat` pattern), and the click handlers also ignore a hidden row, so keyboard Enter on a still-hidden card does nothing either. Browser: right after a drive pick, the hunger row mounted at `pointer-events: none`; `elementFromPoint` at a card's centre hit nothing and no court step opened. Once visible it was `auto` and hittable.
- **2. No map debug data on the player surface.** `HexDetailView` shows grid coordinates, elevation/temperature/moisture and sphere resonance only under the designer view (`window.__DEBUG.setNudgeDesignerView(true)`). The same applies to `HexTooltip`'s "(col, row)" line and raw terrain key. That tooltip line was the "Wilderness (18, 11)" leak the skimmer reported. The cast bar's "Casting on Hex (14, 11)" now names the place: `buildHexTargetContext` takes the graph, and the new `hexPlaceName` returns the settlement on the hex, or else the terrain ("the deep forest").
- **3. The bonding hand fits the viewport.** `FormativeTestBeat` drops its `overflow-y-auto` / `78vh` column. `ComicPanel` gains a `topSpacer` prop (the beat passes `FORMATIVE_TOP_SPACER` = 3vh instead of 30%). The new `fitToViewport.ts` zooms the column to fit the panel, never below `FORMATIVE_FIT_MIN_SCALE` (0.7). Browser, 1920×1080 on `?view=game&firstunmet&size=medium`: before the fix, a 7-card hand put its second row at y=1034 and below. After it, zoom is 0.911, the panel reads `scrollHeight 1048 <= clientHeight 1048`, all 7 cards are inside the viewport, and the commit's bottom is at 1040.
- **4. A chapter does not come back minutes after it ends.** `filterByCooldown` takes a `repeatFloorTicks` floor. For the bonded First (a `thread` edge at `courtPosition: 'the_first'`) it is `FIRST_CHAPTER_REPEAT_FLOOR_TICKS` = 60 ticks (five days), which lifts the completed and failed cooldowns that small-pool scaling cut to 2–3 ticks. It is fail-soft: if the floor would leave no candidate, the scaled cooldowns apply. Under the floor, successful chapters are also read from `chapterArchive`. The review gate caught this: the First's chapters run as unified actions, which are pruned 20 ticks after they resolve, so without the archive read a success came back at about tick 20.
- **5. Narration says what it is.** `ProseTtsButton` idles on a speaker glyph (lucide `Volume2`) instead of ▷. While the voice model downloads, it shows visible text: "Fetching the narrator's voice… 40%". `useEncounterNarration` now exposes `loadProgress`. The `TypedDetailPages` snapshots were updated to the new loading markup.
- **Proof.** New tests: `oneClickChoice.test.tsx` (4 hidden-row cases), `HexDetailView.test.tsx`, `HexTooltip.coords.test.tsx`, `targetContextBuilders.test.ts` (hex naming), `firstChapterRepeatFloor.test.ts`, `fitToViewport.test.ts`, `ProseTtsButton.test.tsx`. `npm run gate`: test, typecheck, build, encounter, cli-smoke and test-heavy all PASS. `preview_start` was refused unattended, so the browser run used a Bash Vite server on port 5297 with Playwright at 1920×1080. The only console error was the pre-existing local TTS health probe (`:3001`). Screenshots are in `Docs/evidence/thr-1804/`. UI Laws: 1 (cards keep their art), 13/14 (no raw keys or coordinates), 17, 21, 33 (the hand fits with no scroll) and 37 hold on the changed surfaces.
