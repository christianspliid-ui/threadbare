## What shipped

- **`src/engine/kpi/engagementKpi.ts`.** When a mortal commits to a unified encounter, `phaseAgentDecision` stamps its capability on the template's primary reach, the difficulty the steps demand after the scale offset (`demandedDifficultyOf`), the planner's `completionProb` and whether a compulsion picked it. The stamp goes into `SimulationRuntime.engagementLedger`, keyed by `actionId`, so `UnifiedAction` gains no field. The orchestrator's newly-resolved transition folds the outcome into lifetime per-band totals and a bounded log (`ENGAGEMENT_LOG_MAX`). Decisions that reach the board and decisions that take the idle path are counted.
- **KPIs** in `computeGameplayKpiReport().engagement`: per-band success and mean attempted difficulty, in-window share, idle rate, `retry_after_failure_rate`, `max_failure_streak` p95, `attempted_difficulty_trend`. `npm run gameplay-report` prints them. The three threshold rows are **advisory** until THR-1582 makes them bind.
- **Constants:** the plan's `KPI_*`, `PROFICIENCY_BAND_EDGES`, `RETRY_WINDOW_TICKS` and `ENGAGEMENT_LOG_MAX` in `kpiConstants.ts`, plus `ENGAGE_WINDOW_LOW`/`HIGH` (0.50/0.65) in `agent-behavior-constants.ts`.
- **`npm run measure:roll-spread`** (`scripts/measure-roll-spread.ts`) prints the P histogram, the floor-pinned share, success by raw band and by authored difficulty, and content coverage per band.
- **`resolution.input` gains `reach`**, so the measurement reads raw score directly instead of inverting the sigmoid.
- **`measure-nudge-headroom.ts` fixed.** `runTick(state, runtime)` passed the runtime as the interventions list and dropped the result, so every tick after tick 0 crashed.
- **Heavy test** `engagementWindow.invariant.test.ts`: a wiring test (stamped > unknown on a real world) plus the level-success invariant, `it.skip` with `TODO(THR-1582)`.
- **Authoring paragraph** in `Docs/canon/encounters.md` and the systemic wiring guide. Interface-map row `resolved-actions-feed-band-kpi`.

## A defect caught on the way

The first wiring keyed stamps on `action.id`, a field `UnifiedAction` does not have (it is `actionId`). Every stamp collided on `undefined`, and half the resolutions fell to band `unknown`. The heavy wiring test caught it before any baseline was recorded.
