## Why no master content

The expert floor has been met since THR-1680, and experts still attempt novice work. A board probe (a spy on `runFilterPipeline` plus `runtime.eligibilityFunnel`, seeds 42/99 × 120 ticks) found the cause. **The candidate cap cuts the expert everyday encounters on ~99% of the decisions that could see them.** Expert content standing at an *expert* mortal's own location reaches scoring 2.3% / 0.0% of the time; novice content at the same mortals reaches it 43%. Eight master encounters would sit behind the same cut.
