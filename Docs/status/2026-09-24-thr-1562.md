## What was wrong

Content authors wrote every capability requirement on a 0–1 scale, but the engine read them against four different numbers:
- **Ambition floors, milestones and abandonment** compared the raw store (10–40+).
  - Every mortal passed every floor.
  - Milestones were met on the first check.
  - Abandonment for lack of reach never fired.
- **Ambition choice** scored raw × weight, so the reach term was 100% of the winning score.
- **Spell `minReach`, the `reach_drain` check and `reach_above:`** read `properties.domainCapability` (singular), which nothing writes.
  - No shipped spell could pass its prerequisites.
  - The one item trickle never paid.
- **Guild joins** compared the dice curve against requirements authored raw (15–25), so no guild join with requirements was ever offered.
- **Strategic floors and the premonition reach bias** compared the raw store, so they always passed or never entered their window.

## What shipped

- **`computeReachShare`** (`domainCapability.ts`) = effective raw ÷ `REACH_SHARE_FULL_RAW` (40), capped at 1. `computeReachShares` returns all eight reaches.
- **The read sites that moved onto it:**
  - the ambition snapshot, plus worldgen's and The First's initial assignment (both built their own snapshot);
  - `agent_reach_above/below`, through a `ConditionContext.reachShare` reader. A caller without it gets a base-only share that fails closed;
  - spell `minReach` and the `reach_drain` check;
  - `reach_above:`;
  - guild joins (new `meetsJoinPrerequisites`);
  - strategic `checkReachFloors`;
  - the premonition reach bias.
- **Content:**
  - the 25 `agent_reach_above` milestone thresholds × 1.25, capped at 1;
  - the five guild `joinPrerequisites` ÷ 40, so the old raw requirement is kept exactly.
- **Debug:** `__DEBUG.getReachShares(agent)`. CLI `agent <name>` prints base → share.
- **Census:** `npm run census:reach-gates` (`scripts/reach-gate-census.ts`). It is self-contained, so the same file runs on an older tree for a before arm.
- **Tests:**
  - `reachShare.test.ts` (22): the function, each site, a corpus test (every authored threshold kind is 0 < t ≤ 1) and the abandonment idiom;
  - `graphConditions.test.ts`: rewritten on shares, including a fail with the reader supplied and an un-migrated fixture that fails closed;
  - 16 fixture files converted to raw (× 40), and the singular property dropped from three.
- **Docs:**
  - the rulebook requirement sentence (§2, §7 casting, §10.6);
  - UL *Domain Capability* and *Prerequisite* (delegated seating);
  - interface-map row `capability-thresholds-read-the-reach-share` (LIVE);
  - wiki pages `agents-reference`, `encounters-manual-reference` and `factions-cultures-reference`;
  - the wiring checklist and the systemic wiring guide.
