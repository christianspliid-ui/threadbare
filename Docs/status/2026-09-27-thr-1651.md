# 2026-09-27 — THR-1651: Oneiric Sending and Divine Compulsion change what the mortal chooses

**Ticket:** [THR-1651](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1651) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1606-what-your-hand-did.md` § B2 · **Pillars:** Engine (drift resolution in the `apply_influence` executor, traces), Content (template payloads, six receipt lines, effect prose); UI: N/A (B1 mounts the sheet chips).

## What was wrong

All three round-1 cold testers: *"I couldn't name one consequence of anything I'd done."* `divine.dream` and `divine.persuade` wrote an `apply_influence` entry with no `valueDrifts`, so `buildValueOverlay`, the agent re-score and the motive receipt's divine term all computed nothing.

## What shipped

- **`valueDriftRule`** on `InfluencePayload` (`src/types/graphOp.ts`). No new effect type. The executor resolves it at apply time through `resolveCastValueDrift` (`src/engine/castInfluenceDrift.ts`): the caster's primary reach picks the pair (`REACH_VALUE_PAIR`), and the rule writes one signed `valueDrifts` entry. The rule itself is not stored.
  - **Oneiric Sending:** `own_lean`, `DREAM_VALUE_DRIFT` 0.08. It deepens the lean the target already has. A target at exactly 0 gets **no entry** and an `influence.no_lean` trace, so nothing shows that is not there (Law 56).
  - **Divine Compulsion:** `first_pole`, `COMPULSION_VALUE_DRIFT` 0.15, toward `ARCHETYPE_NAMES[pair].positive`. **The pole table exists**, so the plan's own-lean fallback was not needed.
  - A caster with no reach affinities writes nothing and traces `reason: 'no_reach'` (fail-soft).
- **Live reader verified before wiring:** `valueDrifts` → `buildValueOverlay` → `agentSelection` and `encounterScoring.resolveProfile` (incl. the THR-641 motive-receipt divine term).
- **Six receipt lines** in `receipt-content.ts` (two verbs × clean / at cost / found nothing). `castInfluenceReceiptLine` re-resolves the same rule, so the `{pole}` word is always the pole written, and a landed line needs the entry to actually be on the target.
- Traces `influence.applied` / `influence.no_lean` registered. `__DEBUG.getActiveInfluences(agent)`. CLI: `cast <template> <agent>`, and `agent <name>` now prints a *Divine influences* block.
- Interface row `cast-influence-shifts-target-values`. Rulebook § 4 flipped from DESIGN to IMPL for B2. Wiring-guide Capability 29.

## Evidence

- `castInfluenceDrift.test.ts` has 15 tests over the real template ops and the real executor: the lean deepens either way, zero lean gives no entry and the trace, compulsion drifts toward the named pole whatever the lean, the pair follows the primary reach, `no_reach` is fail-soft, and the receipt lines (landed, found nothing, failed, never-written).
- Headless CLI, seed 42 medium: `cast divine.dream Dara` → `tick 5` → `agent Dara` shows `dream (mind) — preservation_transformation -0.08 × strength 0.27, 16 ticks left`.
- Control-vs-cast harness (same seed, cast at tick 3, 5 ticks): Renn's re-score at t5 differs on 5 candidates for the dream (axiological +0.15 on `tribute_exchange`, `pickpocket`, `vault_heist`, `steal_secrets`) and on 4 for the compulsion. Receipt toasts: *"Renn dreams of who they already are, and wakes leaning harder toward preservation."* **Kill criterion not triggered.**
- Observation, not a defect of this slice: the two casts push the same pole but move those candidates' axiological score in opposite directions (Δ ±0.15 as a step). The motivation scorer treats a lean near its extreme non-monotonically. Worth a look if B1's chips ever read the score delta rather than the drift.
