## Decisions made in-lane (veto welcome)

- **`intrinsicTier: 'shaping'`, not `background`.** Background templates are capped at 0.45 a step (`NUDGE_OFF_REACH_MAX_DIFFICULTY`), so no background template can be expert. The cap exists so that an off-reach mortal is never floored with a hand that cannot move the word. The forecast window is now the audience selection the cap asks for: only a mortal of about 0.75 on the reach commits to a 0.62 step. `shaping` is the tier the checklist defers to. For the First nothing changes, because background already promotes to shaping. Retinue sees these as shaping moments. THR-1679…THR-1681 inherit this.
- **Skipped reaches: iron and eye.** Iron had the only expert encounter. The other seven tied at 0, so the tie broke on the band below, where eye has the most journeyman content (10).
- **Parallel drafting**, as in THR-1677, with the brief's variance table as the guard. No two hands share a card-type composition.
