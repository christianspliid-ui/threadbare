# THR-2234 — the First's Apotheosis follows the Ordeal

**Shipped 2026-11-15.** Encounter Experience. Decided by the design lane on THR-2232.

- **The rule.** For the First, the Apotheosis now comes once, after the Ordeal vision is answered at tier 4, and before the Return. The Return waits while it is due, seeded or being played. At Doom 0.95 the Return fires anyway (`FIRST_RETURN_APOTHEOSIS_WAIT_DOOM_CAP`). The premonition fires when the Crisis vision is answered. A First below tier 4 at the Ordeal gets no Apotheosis. Three kinds of First never hold the Return: a dead one, a missing one, and one that is no longer an individual. A First moved out of the seat mid-journey (made dormant, for example) falls back to the ordinary rule. Other threaded mortals keep the 60-tick hold and the 90-tick re-offer.
