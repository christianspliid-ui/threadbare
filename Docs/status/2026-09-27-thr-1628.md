## What was wrong

After the dice re-fit (THR-1581) the duel calibration row was reported, not gated. In a duel each duellist's raw clash score sets both their own dice and, through `deriveMightWord`, the card the other side faces. Re-stamping raw to keep one side's odds flipped the other side's card, so both sides could not keep `main`'s odds. The row drifted to `stronger_by_clock` ≈ 61–64% against 49%.

## What shipped

- **A harness-only card pin.** `src/engine/fights/calibrationCardPins.ts`: `withCalibrationCardPins(pins, fn)` pins named opponents' **derived** cards at given words for the synchronous duration of `fn`, and `applyCalibrationCardPin` applies them inside `resolveFightStepInputs`. Outside a pinned run it returns the card unchanged. `readOpponentCard`, the production read shared by the UI, the lair and the debug bridge, is untouched. A guard test fails if anything outside `src/testing/` sets a pin.
- **The duel fixture splits card from dice.** The card raw stays 30 / 15, so the cards read *severe/steep* and *fair/gentle* as on `main`. Each side's clash raw is solved with the fight fixture's `oddsPreservingCapability`. `main`'s clash capability is the card raw on the pre-refit curve (1.000 strong, 0.881 weak), which gives dice capability 0.550 strong and 0.515 weak. Nerve keeps its THR-1581 solve.
- **Re-gated.** `duelCalibration.test.ts` gates the four named classes at ±8 again and asserts both sides' clash odds equal `main`'s. `calibrate:duels` exits 1 on a miss again.

## Result

`npm run calibrate:duels`, 400 duels, seed 1264: stronger by clock 50.5 (49), weaker by clock 3.5 (4), struck down 25.3 (23), broke off 13.3 (13), routed 7.5 (11, diagnostic), yielded 0. PASS.
