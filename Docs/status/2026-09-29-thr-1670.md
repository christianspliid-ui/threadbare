# 2026-09-29 — THR-1670: a caster casts in the scene (power runtime S2)

**Ticket:** [THR-1670](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1670) · **Project:** Powers & Item Generation · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-29-thr-1571-power-runtime.md` § S2.1 · **Pillars:** Engine (the decision, the odds line, the band outcome); Content (the shipped `castProse` lines now render, chip copy in `spell-cast-screen-content.ts`); UI (the odds line with a Power-card link, the cast line after the afterimage and in the chapter ledger, one chip per cast write, `?spell=`).

## What a player sees now

A mortal who wields a deliberate spell reaches for it when a step turns against them. The spell must fit the step: an encounter spell on a step of its Reach, a fight spell on a clash. The trigger is odds before any god card below the mortal's own threshold: 45% for a steady mortal, 55% for a brave one, 35% for a cautious one.

That threshold sits below the 50–65% window mortals choose to engage at, so casts are rare by design. They come on a hard step, not on an ordinary day.

The step shows "Vara is casting *Pact of the Hollow Crown*." on its odds, with the spell's tier of help as pips. The name opens the spell's Power card. The step's own roll decides the spell: the god's cards bent that roll, so they bent the spell. The cast line (landed or fizzled) and any backlash line follow the step's afterimage, in the veil and in the chapter ledger. Each thing the cast changed is a chip in the aftermath, such as a strain or a price condition.

## Decisions taken in the build

- **One pure decision, read twice.** `decideStepCast` is pure: it reads no card and writes nothing. The forecast calls it through `stepCastFor`. The roll calls it too, then writes the record to `UnifiedAction.stepCasts[i]`. The plan allowed the forecast to write first; this build doesn't, because a UI render must not mutate engine state. A paused veil holds the world still, so both reads see the same inputs.
- **The spell rides the standing term** (`kind: 'spell'`), on the ordinary roll and inside the fighter's standing on a clash. There is one channel, so the forecast and the roll cannot diverge.
- **A price condition is a write.** Hollow Crown's `condition_inflict` price wrote a real `has_trait` edge that S1's `writes` never listed. `resolveCast` now reads it back off the graph (`fromPrice`), and the node is named in sheet words ("Paranoia Whispers", not `paranoia_whispers`).
- **A spell link opens its Power card.** The veil's `attachment` route resolves only artifacts, so a spell node rendered "Unknown". A spell definition node now opens its `power_template` content card.

## Evidence

- `stepCast.thr1670.test.ts`, 15 tests:
  - forecast and roll carry the same line, and a written record wins for both;
  - brave and cautious mortals at the same odds decide differently;
  - a declined cast adds no line;
  - a success band lands the cast, a failure fizzles it, the price is paid either way;
  - one chip per write;
  - Soulfire fits a clash, never the nerve step.
- Full `npm test`: 1394 files, 22,359 tests passing.
- Browser (Playwright, 1920×1080): `?view=game&seeded&size=medium&testavatar&spell=hollow_crown&spawn=encounter.master_craftsman_challenge&outcome=success`. Step 2 (Gold, difficulty 0.50) decides to cast at pre-card 0.35 and shows the odds line. The link opens the Power card. The step lands, the afterimage cast line renders, and the Paranoia Whispers chip shows.
- The fight link (`…&spell=soulfire&spawn=fight.lair.confront&outcome=failure`) recognises the Soulfire clash. The balanced avatar declines there, because its pre-card odds are 0.57 ("odds good"). That is the rule working, and the unit test pins the fight path.
- The ticket's Gold link, `the_garrisons_price`, declines for the balanced avatar (pre-card 0.475). So the review link uses a harder Gold step.
- **Census** (`scripts/census-step-casts.ts`, medium map). The natural world has no deliberate wielders: seeded knowing deals every caster the fate-woven fallback. In the arm where every caster also wields Hollow Crown and Soulfire:
  - seed 42, 150 ticks: 23 eligible steps, 0 casts. No step reached the odds check: 19 were one sealed caster carrying the Hush Stone, 4 had no target.
  - seed 42, 300 ticks: 35 eligible steps, 1 cast (fizzled). Declines: 14 sealed, 13 no target, 6 cooldown, 1 odds good.
  - seed 99, 300 ticks: 15 eligible steps, 0 casts (13 odds good).

  Neither kill criterion fires in earnest: casts are rare and not zero. The sample stays thin until the generator ([THR-1572](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1572)) puts deliberate spells in hands.
- Tick cost (`measure:tick-cost --map medium --ticks 100`, steady state): 146 ms/tick on the branch against 147 on `origin/main`.

## Not done

- A landed Hollow Crown's own effects (an aura and a conditional) are modifier-only and write nothing. Its only in-scene effect is the odds line. `resolveCastTarget` also ignores the ally/enemy filter: on the review link, Hollow Crown was aimed at The First. Both are in [THR-1683](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1683).
- Contested (paired) steps do not cast. Only the uncontested step path decides.

**Also fixed on the way:** `vite.config.ts`'s worktree watch-ignore was unanchored. It ignored the whole of any dev server started inside a worktree, which was the root cause of impediments #1098, #1100, #1102 and #1104. It is now anchored to the config's own directory, and HMR works in worktrees.
