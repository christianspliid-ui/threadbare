# 2026-10-09 — THR-1749: buy your spheres

**Ticket:** [THR-1749](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1749) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-06-thr-1749-buy-your-spheres.md`

A god's sphere identity is now a set of points it buys at Remembrance, not the pair its hunger hands over.

- **The buy.** A new `spheres` sub-step sits between court and the sphere reveal. It shows four opposed-pair tracks (Force ↔ Mind, Matter ↔ Time, Energy ↔ Spirit, Life ↔ Entropy), five measures to pour, at most three in one sphere. Levels and what is left read in words ("a current of Life", "Left to pour: some"). The step opens on the hunger's 3 + 2 preset, so one Continue keeps today's god. An overspending position is dimmed and names its reason, and Continue is disabled with its reason until everything is poured. Kill-switch: `REMEMBRANCE_SPHERE_BUY_ENABLED`.
- **The vector.** `spherePoints` lives on the ascendant node and the identity. `createAscendant` validates it and derives `sphereAlignment` as the two largest buys (ties go in canonical order), so the ~95 readers of primary / secondary read the bought pair unchanged. A missing or invalid vector falls back to the preset and emits `sphere_points.fallback`.
- **Income.** Both private `distributeByAlignment` copies were replaced, in one commit, by the shared `distributeBySpherePoints` (`src/engine/spherePoints.ts`). Every sphere keeps a 4% floor (`UNBOUGHT_SPHERE_INCOME_SHARE`) and the rest follows the points. A preset lands 0.352 / 0.248 / 0.04, against 0.35 / 0.25 / 0.04 before. Upkeep is still charged from the primary sphere, which is now the largest buy.
- **Canon.** Haunt is now spirit / entropy, Illuminate mind / energy, Reshape force / matter. Foundation spheres are not chosen at chargen (rulebook §5), and an opposed pair cannot be held. A catalog test keeps every hunger's preset legal. The Foundation-signed cards that lose their identity route are tracked in [THR-1753](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1753).
- **Reveal.** "Life and Force pour through you. Matter and Spirit stir beneath." Display words are used, never raw keys.
- **Debug:** `await window.__DEBUG.getAscendantSpheres()` → `{ spherePoints, derived, stored, source }`.

Evidence: a real Remembrance run poured 2/1/1/1 and got `source: 'bought'` with derived life / force. `?seeded` and `?firstunmet` both boot Mind 3 / Spirit 2. Screenshot: `Docs/evidence/thr-1749/spheres-step-1920.png`.
