# 2026-10-15 — THR-1877 (S1): one home list, one foreground, the rest held and named

**Ticket:** [THR-1877](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1877), slice S1 of the encounter Stage plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1877-encounter-stage.md` § D1, D2). Engine: the home list and the hold rule (pure, render-time, no tick change). Content: the held lines and holder words on the list. UI: the three hosts render by the list.

- **One list (Law 58).** `src/data/homed-surfaces.ts` holds `HOMED_SURFACES`, the 16 surfaces that stop the world or come forward on their own: kind, surface, home (Stage / dialog / ceremony), stops time, waits its turn, arrival, palette, clock-holder words and held line. `INTERRUPT_SURFACES`, `INTERRUPT_HOLDER_NAMES` and `DIALOG_CONTEXT_SURFACES` are now derived from it (same ids, words and rows). `HOMED_SURFACE_OPEN_WHEN` gives each kind its open predicate, and a kind without one is a type error. The cycle harvest joins as a non-stopping ceremony.
- **One foreground.** `resolveInterrupts` keeps THR-1853's arrival holds and adds D2: ceremonies and Stage moments share one slot. The one on screen keeps it, and a free slot goes by list order (ceremonies first). Dialogs render over it, the divine receipt waits for the foreground, and the popup waits for everything. Everything else that wants the screen is `held`, named, with what it waits behind. New resolution fields: `rendering`, `foreground`, `stage`, `held`, `wished`. `open` and `anyOpen` keep their meaning, and a held stopping surface keeps the clock stopped.
- **Reconciled with THR-1853.** An arrival on screen still steps back for an encounter, a gift or the ledger that opens over it (THR-1853's tested rule), instead of keeping the slot as D2 R2 would have it. Every case in `interruptRegistry.test.ts` passes unchanged.
- **Hosts by list.** `StageHost`, `DialogHost` and `CeremonyHost` each render through one exhaustive switch over their home's kinds, and only kinds in `rendering`. DialogHost keeps each surface at its old mount position, so equal-z stacking is unchanged. `HomedSurfaceFallback` covers a kind with no case. Its Continue sets the kind down (`skippedKinds`) until its flag clears, so the clock is never held forever.
- **Receipt toast guard (D7).** While a moment holds the foreground, a receipt toast shakes, shows "Opens when this chapter closes." and opens nothing.
- **Inspectable.** The new `ui_stage` trace category emits `held` and `fallback`, once per transition. `__DEBUG.getInterruptState()` gains `rendering`, `foreground`, `stage`, `held` and `homedSurfaces`.
- **Evidence:** at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440, with an encounter open, `forcePremonition('@hero')` leaves only the encounter on screen and `held` names `premonition` behind `encounter`. After the encounter is set down and one tick passes, the premonition opens. Screenshots are in `Docs/audits/2026-10-15-thr-1877/`.
- **Not in this slice:** `collectHeldMoments()` and the Needs-you list (THR-1875's top-bar slice adopts `held`), the Stage frame, map dim and camera (S2, THR-1892), and `CEREMONY_SURFACES` (derives from `CEREMONY_HOMED_KINDS` when THR-1884 lands).
