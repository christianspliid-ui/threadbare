# THR-1968 — Every economic card says what it changed

**Shipped 2026-10-21.** God's stake in the economy, slice S2 (plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-17-thr-1935-the-gods-stake-in-the-economy.md` § Engine 6, § Content S2).

- **The cast snapshot reads the economy.** For a town (or the town a sublocation belongs to) it reads the prosperity word, the unrest chip, each staple's stock tier and the trade roads touching it. For a mortal it reads Means. `castEconomyReceipt.ts` turns each real move into one `economy` row on the receipt.
- **A crossed band opens the dialog.** Bless the Harvest on a struggling town that it lifts says "Millbrook is no longer struggling: it is modest again." in the receipt dialog. A move inside a band rides the toast: Open the Markets on a calm town says "Its people's anger cools." The prosperity word is the same one the Fortunes row prints.
- **One `economy` kind.** Mortal wealth's kind gains `crossed` and `economyQuantity`. A mortal's row keeps `meansChangeSentence`'s words. Rows come from `economy-receipt-content.ts` (one entry per quantity × direction × crossed), and a completeness test covers the table.
- **Guide the Caravan** now uses the Gold Reach on its card, the Reach its step rolls.
- **Not in this slice:** the settlement economy's land line (THR-1923, not built). When it lands, its rows go above these.
