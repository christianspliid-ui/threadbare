### 2026-10-03 — THR-1686: the lead survey and the kept visit

**The clue climb now gets past its middle rungs.** Mortals heard rumours of ruins and then almost never went to look: on main, seeds 42 · 99 · 4 · 8 surveyed a ruin 1 · 9 · 3 · 10 times in 300 ticks. The board was scoring the survey as a dice challenge, and a survey has no dice. When a mortal did arrange a visit and stood waiting at the ruin, nothing held it there, so the board, or its company, walked it away.

- **Engine.** Three parts, each behind its own kill switch:
  1. **The lead survey skips the window.** On the decision board, an instant candidate carrying `leadPull` takes advance probability 1 and fit 1, and its zone reads `certain` (`CLUE_LEAD_SURVEY_SKIPS_WINDOW`). Every other instant cell still faces the window.
  2. **A waiting mortal stays.** In the `waiting` regime, a candidate off the mortal's own hex is dropped when the trip there and back would outlast the due tick. The trip is priced by `waitingTripOverruns`: the encounter's own ticks plus `APPOINTMENT_HEX_TICKS_PER_HEX` a hex each way (`APPOINTMENT_WAITING_HOLD_ENABLED`). Company travel asks the same of each member through `holdsWaitingMemberAtPlace`, called from `groups/groupMovement.ts`; so do idle drift and the forced-travel fallback (found by the review gate).
  3. **`leaning`'s discount reaches the board** as `ScoredCandidate.appointmentDiscount` (`APPOINTMENT_DISCOUNT_ON_BOARD`). Before this it scaled only `finalScore`, which the board never reads.
- **Executor findings, under part 2's agreed outcome** ("a waiting mortal stays"). The plan's hold alone still lost visits, by two routes it had not named:
  - **Company travel.** On seed 99, a company walked a waiting surveyor one hex off a wonder, and the visit was lost three ticks later.
  - **Underpriced trips.** The shared `overruns` test prices a hex at one tick. It read a one-hex encounter that ran seven ticks as returnable, and on seed 2 that cost the visit.

  Both are now covered. `departing` keeps its own test unchanged.
- **Content / UI.** N/A, as planned. The zone and the discount are fields on `decision_board_comparison`; the player meets the result as the visit encounter and the sheet's lead line.
