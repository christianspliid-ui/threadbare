### 2026-10-03 — THR-1686: the lead survey and the kept visit

**The clue climb now gets past its middle rungs.** Mortals heard rumours of ruins and then almost never went to look: on main, seeds 42 · 99 · 4 · 8 surveyed a ruin 1 · 9 · 3 · 10 times in 300 ticks. The board was scoring the survey as a dice challenge, and a survey has no dice. When a mortal did arrange a visit and stood waiting at the ruin, nothing held it there, so the board, its company, or an idle drift walked it away.

- **Engine.** Three parts, each behind its own kill switch:
  1. **The lead survey skips the window.** On the decision board, an instant candidate carrying `leadPull` takes advance probability 1 and fit 1, and its zone reads `certain` (`CLUE_LEAD_SURVEY_SKIPS_WINDOW`). Every other instant cell still faces the window.
  2. **A waiting mortal stays** (`APPOINTMENT_WAITING_HOLD_ENABLED`).
     - **Which candidates drop.** In the `waiting` regime, a candidate off the mortal's own hex is dropped when the trip there and back would outlast the due tick. The trip is priced by `waitingTripOverruns`: the encounter's own ticks, plus `APPOINTMENT_HEX_TICKS_PER_HEX` a hex each way.
     - **The board input.** The board's input list is re-cut from the full ranking, so a local encounter ranked sixth still reaches it.
     - **Other movers.** Company travel, idle drift and forced travel all ask `holdsWaitingMemberAtPlace`. Every hold is traced on `appointment_regime` with `heldFrom` / `heldBy`.
  3. **`leaning`'s discount reaches the board** as `ScoredCandidate.appointmentDiscount` (`APPOINTMENT_DISCOUNT_ON_BOARD`). Before this it scaled only `finalScore`, which the board never reads.
- **Executor findings beyond the plan, all under part 2's agreed outcome** ("a waiting mortal stays"):
  - **Census.** Company travel walked a waiting surveyor off a wonder (seed 99). And the shared one-tick-a-hex `overruns` test read a one-hex, seven-tick encounter as returnable (seed 2).
  - **Review gate.** Idle drift and forced travel were unguarded. The board's top-five cut hid local work. The holds left no trace.

  `leaning` and `departing` keep their own `overruns` and the scorer's top five. Widening their board was measured to cost kept visits and add misses, because their test prices a hex at one tick.
- **Content / UI.** N/A, as planned. The zone, the discount and the holds are trace fields; the player meets the result as the visit encounter and the sheet's lead line.
