# THR-1984 — The First keeps her trade

**Shipped 2026-10-21.** Cold-playtest round 4: 2 of 3 testers said the game forgot who their First was. The meeting showed a healer, saved none of it, and five regional chapters recast her as an archivist, a prophet, a magistrate, a mason-lord or a merchant-prince.

**What changed.** Each of the 24 meeting vignettes carries an authored trade (`role` + the word the player reads). The bond writes `npcRole` and `tradeWord` onto The First before her Means is born (`src/engine/firstTrade.ts`). The dev seed writes priest / Shrine-keeper. Role affinity, Means, caster tradition and the binder census now see her role. The five job-cast chapters gain `protagonistRoles`, and a new fail-open filter stage, `protagonist_role_fit` (3c), keeps them for mortals whose trade fits. The person page's *Who they are* panel gains a Trade row (tooltip `ui.trade`). The header, the land index and Connections read the trade word first. `{trade}` / `{?has_trade}` exist for future prose. Traces `first.trade.written` and `encounter.filter.role_fit`; `__DEBUG.getFirstTrade()`.

**Deviations from the plan.** The Inspector had replaced `OverviewTab`, `LocationView` and `HexChronicle` by the time this was built. The trade row went into `personRows.ts` (the plan's own grey-zone rule), and the role readers that remain (`subjectPage` header, `landIndex`, `connections`) switched to `displayTradeLabel`. `SoulCard` has no importer. The `ui.trade` tooltip names no mortal, because `PanelRow` tooltips take no `{name}`.

**Evidence.** `npm run gate` PASS 7/7 (code track, engine): 25 191 tests, typecheck ratchet, build, CLI smoke. On `?view=game&seeded&size=medium` at 1920×1080: `getFirstTrade()` → `{ role: 'priest', word: 'Shrine-keeper' }`, the sheet shows Archetype / Trade / Calling, and the console has 0 errors. UL-proposal: THR-2007.
