## What shipped

- **`FightState`** (`src/types/fight.ts`, `src/engine/fights/fightState.ts`) on `UnifiedAction.fightState`. It is created at the block's first fight step and holds the opponent, the clock, the exchanges, forks and the result.
- **The clock** (`src/engine/fights/fightClock.ts`). `advanceFightClock` moves the opponent's clock by `FIGHT_CLOCK_BY_BAND`. A monster's clock is persistent in `monsterState` and applies lazy recovery (`FIGHT_CLOCK_RECOVERY_TICKS`) before its own delta. A mortal's clock lasts one fight and crosses between fights only through the `FIGHT_CLOCK_MAILBOX_PROP` mailbox. Every write traces `fight.clock` with `filledByThisWrite`.
- **Early end and the result.** A full clock falls to the next landing blow, never on its own. `advanceStep` ends a decided fight early, and the action resolves with `FIGHT_RESULT_ACTION_OUTCOME[result]`. The action-trigger ladder reads the result.
- **The no-roll route.** `no_opponent`, `opponent_gone` and `separated` each end `broke_off` with no roll, through `StepResolutionResult.fightEnd`, the loop pass-through and `executeStepResult`'s no-roll branch. This closes FB1's known gap.
- **The result memory** sits at `fightResultIndex(steps)`, so no step owns it. `fight:<result>` aftermath variants resolve off it.
- **`onFightEnded`** (`src/engine/fights/fightOutcome.ts`) is an empty dispatcher that runs exactly once per fight and merges branch patches onto `fightState`. Each fight traces one `fight.end`.
- **The `'fight'` death cause.** The sheet word is *slain*, and `killerIsKnown` treats a fight death as seen.
- Docs: rulebook §7, the systemic wiring guide, the wiring checklist, the UL (Fight Clock, Fight Result and others), two interface-map rows (`fight-writes-opponent-clock`, `fight-result-keys-aftermath-variants`) and three wiki pages.
