# 2026-09-26 — THR-1621: Hex lore says "not fully explored" once, not three times

**Ticket:** [THR-1621](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1621) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** the ticket body (a deferral from THR-1602, cold playtest round 1) · **Pillars:** UI + Content. Engine is N/A: the ruin descriptors are already authored data; only how the chronicle phrases them changed.

## What shipped

The Ruins section of the hex chronicle used to run each of a region's first three ruin descriptors through one fixed sentence, so a hex showed three bullets that differed by one noun ("The white stone walls have not been fully explored…", "The geometric foundations have not…"). The builder now lives in `src/components/Game/chronicle/explorationHooks.ts` (`buildExplorationHooks`, `MAX_EXPLORATION_DESCRIPTORS = 3`) and returns at most one line per hex that names the descriptors together: *"The observatory domes, astral charts etched in stone and collapsed orreries have not been fully explored. What remains within may reward — or punish — the curious."* Blank and duplicate descriptors are dropped.

`explorationHooks.test.ts` covers the empty, single, several and duplicate cases, and checks every authored historical culture for at most one line.
