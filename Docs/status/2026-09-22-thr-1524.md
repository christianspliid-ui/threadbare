## What the row was saying

THR-1518 measured the reachability row **UNREACHED** on every seed: the Crossroads bargain fired once in 1000 ticks on the live board and the mortal refused. The primitive was proven live by the harness and unreached by the world — the THR-1497 shape — so the two THR-1479 contracts stayed 🔴 LEAKED-with-ticket pointing here. No batch had landed one, so this is route 2.

## What was wrong — two content defects on the parent, neither in the engine

**Setting supply.** `settings: ['wayside']` expands to `camp | oasis | wilderness`. On seed 42 / medium that is 8 of 974 locations, and a per-tick probe (sampled every 10 ticks over 200) put **5.5% of mortal-ticks** at a wayside place against urban 50%, rural 37%, ruin 2.3%. None of the four wayside-only slice encounters (Bridge, Pass, Caravan, Crossroads) fired in 200 ticks; the only slice firings were the urban swindler sequel. A crossroads at dusk is a road thing, not a camp thing: the bargain now registers at **rural + ruin + wayside** with one opening per class (`'{name} comes to the crossroads outside {location} at dusk, where the lane meets the road.'` for rural, `'… below {location} at dusk, where the old road still runs.'` for ruin). Only the parent needed the wider envelope — the kept sequel is a literal id, which `evaluateEncounterSeeds` does not subtype-gate at spawn, and the missed sequel already registers at every class.

**The pole trap.** `motivations: ['tradition_novelty']` named the fork's own axis, per the spec's step 6. But `computeDesireScore` sums the **signed** profile value (its docstring says "absolute values so both poles contribute"; the code does not), so the board drew Archivists — the positive pole, who *refuse* — and floored Heretics — the negative pole, the arm that plants — at `MINIMUM_DESIRE`. 34 of 65 profiled mortals on seed 42 lean novelty, and none of them ever met him; the one who did was an Archivist. Selection moved to the scene's own Eye axis (`revelation_discretion` — a Seeker is drawn to take the measure of a man who knows too much); the fork, its `decidedBy`, and both leaning cards stay on `tradition_novelty`, so the bargain still turns on the mortal's values and the god's lean.

## Findings filed, not fixed here

- **The signed desire score vs. the spec's step 6.** Every spec-authored fork that plants on its negative pole is starved by construction. Whether `computeDesireScore` should read the absolute value (its docstring's claim) or step 6 should stop naming the fork axis as `motivations` is a design decision with corpus-wide blast — filed as a design ticket, not decided here.
- **Sequel templates are board-reachable.** The Full Moon Reckoning (`#crossroads_debt`, every class) fired once on seed 42 and twice on seed 99 *from the board*, to a mortal who had broken no promise; the swindler sequel fires ~15 times per 200 ticks with its parent never firing. No seed-only flag exists on a template. Filed as a Deferral.
- The Bridge, Pass and Caravan share the wayside starvation; out of scope here, named in the Deferral.
