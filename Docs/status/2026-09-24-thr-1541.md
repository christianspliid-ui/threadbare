## What shipped

- **`src/engine/fights/fightEvents.ts`** (new). It has one raise per moment of a fight, each a thin `raiseEffectEvent` call with the other side as `counterpartId` and `encounterType: 'combat'`:
  - `combat_started` on both sides at the handler's first run, before that step's outcome;
  - the fighter's `encounter_outcome { reach, success, combat: true }` on every fight step, on the resolved reach;
  - on a landing clash, `attacked` on the opponent (and on the fighter too on near miss or at cost), then `damaged { amount }`;
  - `opponent_overcome` (stack `on_kill`) on a win;
  - `combat_ended` on both sides when the fight ends.
- **The handler** (`landFightBand` in `fightState.ts`) raises them at step (2) of the clock-full check: after the band's landing write, before the mailbox drain. So a reactive's clock write counts as this step's blow. The rolled route raises `combat_ended` after `finalizeFightEnd`. The no-roll route's inline raise now goes through the same helper, and still only mid-fight.
- **`EffectEvent`** gains an optional `combat` field on `encounter_outcome`, which `getStackTriggers` counts as a combat reach, and two new variants: `attacked` (reactive `attacked`) and `opponent_overcome` (stack `on_kill`). `EffectEventSite` gains `fight_start`, `fight_step`, `fight_clash` and `fight_overcome`.
- **`raiseEffectEvent`** now hands `counterpartId` to every reactive execution as `ExecutionContext.targetId` (THR-1530 §4a) on every raise site. When a site passes `encounterType`, the reactive also gets a predicate context carrying it.
- New constant `FIGHT_TRADED_BLOW_BANDS` (near miss, at cost).
- Docs: rulebook §7 (a new `[IMPL]` rule), the systemic wiring guide (a fight-events table), and the interface map (row `fight-raises-effect-events`).
