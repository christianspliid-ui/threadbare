# THR-1480 — the retrofit corpus drains, and the clamp follows it

**Shipped 2026-09-12.** [THR-1472](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1472) shipped the rule, the gate and the vertical-slice migration, and deliberately left the gate on the `[warn]` channel with **57 findings standing across the retrofit corpus** — gating then would have turned a green corpus red for work ticketed here. This is that work. All 57 are re-authored, and the gate is now an error.

## What changed

**Content — 57 chip nouns, 63 authored sites.** Six change ids recur across bands, which is worth recording: a first pass that rewrote one occurrence per id left six warns standing on their *second* band and read as a near-complete drain. The families, each decided once and applied everywhere:

**Five concepts written, not re-phrased.** The remaining chips named states the game had no player-facing word for, which is the deeper defect the rule exists to surface — so the fix is the word (the THR-1472 / THR-1171 route): `ui.thread` (the title concept itself, three shrine chips were tagging it and anchoring the mortal at one end of it), `ui.quintessence`, `ui.ambition`, `ui.compulsion`, `ui.goods`.

**Engine — the promotion.** `chipStateNounWordingViolations` moves out of `check-encounter.ts`'s warn array and into `checkCompositionContract` as an `aftermath` violation, beside the two Law 56 clauses an author already fixes in one sitting. It is reported in one channel, not two.

## The prior-ticket collision, and why it resolves this way

Ten test failures were not breakage but **tests pinning the anchoring the new rule forbids** — and two of them were deliberate work by [THR-1317](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1317), which had anchored the five knowledge chips and the ambition chip at `$actor` so their click would land somewhere. That looks like a reversal until the comment THR-1317 left is read: *"no tooltip concept names an ambition."* The carrier anchor was never the intent — it was the only shape that rendered, because `fromConceptRef` needs `entityId` **and** `visualKind`, and no concept existed to point at instead. `ui.ambition` now exists, so this ships the option THR-1317 would have taken. The knowledge family is the same story and THR-1472's own slice migration had already answered it with `ui.knowledge`.

**The trade is recorded rather than buried:** those chips lose a click-through to the actor's sheet and gain a hover naming the concept. That is the ruling's deliberate shape — one concept on the tag, the particulars one hover away — and every repointed test carries the reasoning rather than a bare new value, per the no-green-tests-on-a-dead-contract rule.

`the-garrisons-price`'s fix #2 kept its *substance*: its guard was "do not claim trust moved when the failure write carries no `trustDelta`", and the `not.toBe('trust with the quartermaster')` assertion is still there. `reputation` is broader than `regard`, so it cannot over-claim either.
