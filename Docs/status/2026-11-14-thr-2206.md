# THR-2206 — a greyed card says which pool is short and how fast it refills

**Shipped 2026-11-14.** Dominion — Player Power Progression. Plan doc: `Docs/plans/2026-11-13-thr-2201-make-the-purse-bite.md` (slice B, § UI pillar). Complaint class PC-1. Laws 13, 17, 21, 37, 43.

- **The greyed reason names the pool.** A card dimmed `essence_unavailable` on an identity run now reads "Not enough Mind — refills slowly" (a signed card: its own sphere) or "Not enough in your spheres — Mind refills steadily" (a sphere-less card: the primary, which pays first). The refill word is the essence bar's own `ESSENCE_REFILL_WORDS` row word in lower case, read from `readEssenceRefill`. Strings live in `NUDGE_ESSENCE_SHORT_REASONS` (`nudge-stage-content.ts`).
- **Fail-soft forms.** No refill band (the ledger read failed) → "Not enough Mind" / "Not enough in your spheres — Mind pays first". Executor decision: the plan quotes no bare sphere-less form, and a bare "Not enough in your spheres" would name no pool, breaking the Done-when's reason == `shortSphere` pin. A legacy run with no identity keeps "Not enough essence" and reports no `shortSphere`.
- **Live dimming agrees.** Every card with a payer carries `essenceShortReason`, so when `useNudgeHand`'s live remainder greys a card it shows the same words and sets `shortSphere` to its paying pool.
- **Price hover lines.** `CostPips` takes a `tooltipNote` appended to the `ui.card.cost` registry text; `CardFace` passes the model's `costNote`. A sphere-less card adds "Paid from your own spheres, Mind first."; a home-discounted card adds "One less: Mind is yours."
- **Debug bridge.** `getEncounterNudges().dimmed[]` gains `shortSphere`, through the shared `nudgeShortSphere` helper the stage uses.
- **Tests.** `greyedCardReason.thr2206.test.tsx` (6): the exact strings and bare forms; the hover lines; `nudgeShortSphere`; reason names the `shortSphere` pool on the engine model and on the rendered face; the hook's live dimming; the legacy arm.

**Evidence:** `npm run gate` PASS (test 141 s, typecheck 70 s, build 26 s, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, cli-smoke). Browser (bash-vite on :5317, Playwright, 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, `spawnNudgeExemplar('@hero')`): hovering *Steady Breath*'s price reads "…Your pools are listed under Essence on your bar. Paid from your own spheres, Mind first." `getEncounterNudges('@hero','ua_1')` returned `dimmed: []`, so no card was greyed live. Every pool covered its cards (scale 1, pools full) and the bridge has no lever to drain a pool. The greyed reason == `shortSphere` pin is therefore jsdom-render evidence on the real `buildNudgePhaseModel` → `NudgePhaseShell` path, not a live browser assertion. The live assertion becomes reachable once THR-2216 tunes the purse.

**UI Laws on the changed surface:** 1 (the sphere word on the cost row keeps its tooltip; unchanged), 13/14 (words only, no new numeral), 17 (the short pool is named before the commit; the hover text extends the one registry entry), 21 (one reason per card; the stage and the bridge share `nudgeShortSphere`), 33 (no layout change; the reason line already existed), 37 (the discount explains itself), 43 (refill words reused, no new vocabulary).
