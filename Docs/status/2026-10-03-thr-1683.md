### 2026-10-03 — THR-1683: a landed spell holds, and it is never aimed at a friend

**The cast channel.** A spell's lasting effects (an aura, a conditional, a duration) went through `executeEffect`, which traced them and wrote nothing. So a landed Pact of the Hollow Crown changed one step's odds and nothing else, and Veilwalk's shadow bonus never existed anywhere. Now the landed branch of `resolveCast` routes those effects to `castChannel.ts`:
- They ride one shared condition per spell, `trait.condition.cast.<spellId>`.
- The caster bears it through the one condition writer, for the spell's `duration` ticks or `CAST_CHANNEL_DEFAULT_TICKS_BY_TIER` (4 / 6 / 8 / 12).
- The existing readers do the rest. An enemy within a hex of a Hollow Crown caster reads −0.08 Gold until `decayConditions` ends the bearing, then 0.
- Stateful families (`stacking`, `decay`, charges) cannot share one definition. They are skipped and named in the trace.
- The chip reads "Pact of the Hollow Crown holds around Ilse for a while."

**The target filter.** `resolveCastTarget` applies `targeting.filter` on the named target and on the fallback. `isAlly` moved to `src/engine/allegiance.ts` so `create × Condition` and the cast read one allegiance. An `enemy` spell takes the stranger beside the caster, not the bonded ally. With only an ally beside, it refuses `no_target` and the step cast declines.

**Mutex.** The THR-1572 mutex was reversed: that ticket is a review-gate park (In Dev, no assignee, PR #2178 unarmed and DIRTY). Whichever PR lands second rebases.

**Not met: the census.** A 150-tick seed-42 medium run resolves zero casts of any kind, on main too. Primed with Hollow Crown on all 104 casters, it made 68 step-cast decisions, and all were declined (51 `no_fitting_spell`, 15 `sealed`). No in-play cast has exercised the channel yet. That is the casting supply, not this change (impediment #1132).
