# THR-1213 slice 2 — the resonance weight fires

*2026-08-28 · Wave-1 design B, hunger vocabulary unification · Content Architecture*

## What shipped

Slice 1 made one hunger vocabulary. Slice 2 makes it **do something**: the Hunger
the player chose in remembrance now weighs which formative tests the meeting
deals to their First.

Before this, three things had to be true at once for that to be false, and all
three were:

1. **The reader was unwired.** `selectDilemmasV2` and `scoreDilemmaResonance`
   lived in `src/engine/dilemmaSelection.ts` and were imported by nothing but
   their own tests. Both live meeting paths called the older `selectDilemmas`,
   which took no lens and scored nothing.
2. **The lens was a stub, and the stub was dead.** `GameView` built an
   `AscendantLens` from *archetype* spheres — not the player's chosen Hunger —
   into a memo nothing consumed.
3. **The vocabularies were disjoint.** The scored field, `hungerResonance`, held
   bare hunger ids; it was compared against theme tags. Zero matches across all
   167 shipped dilemmas, for every god, always (THR-1158).

Each of those alone would have made the weight inert. Having all three is why no
test ever went red.

## The three moves

**The reader moved into the live picker.** `selectDilemmas` gains an optional
`lens`, and its two uniform picks become resonance-weighted ones through a single
`pickFromPool`. Scoring reads `emotionalRegister ∩ hunger.dilemmaResonanceTags` —
one tag space compared with itself — plus `driveResonance ∩ lens.driveTags`. The
anti-resonance valve carries over from V2, so a god is not always mirrored back
at themselves. `dilemmaSelection.ts` is deleted with its duplicate weights and
with the fixtures asserting the dead vocabulary, per the interface-map rule that
retiring a contract deletes the tests asserting its dead side.

**The lens comes from the identity.** `buildLensFromIdentity` resolves the hunger
through `toHungerId` against the merged catalog and takes `driveTags` from
`identity.mortalTags` — *narrowed*, not cast, because that list is genuinely
mixed: a remembrance fragment contributes `knowledge` and `devotion` alongside
`scholar`, `rural` and `recent`, which are not themes at all. `MeetTheFirstFlow`
passes it; the identity-less quick-start path builds the sphere stub at the one
call site that needs it; `GameView`'s dead memo is gone.

**The knob names got fixed.** `HUNGER_RESONANCE_WEIGHT` existed three times in
the tree and the only live one priced *vignette candidates*, not dilemmas — so
tuning "the hunger resonance weight" could silently mean either system. It is now
`VIGNETTE_HUNGER_RESONANCE_WEIGHT`, and the dilemma weights have one home.

## The draw-count call, which the gate turns on

The handoff flagged this as the grey zone to settle first, and it decides whether
the no-op gate means anything. `pickFromPool` runs on every pick **whether or not
a lens is present** and consumes the same draws either way — one anti-resonance
roll, then one jitter draw per candidate. A lens changes only the score term.

With no lens every base score is 0, so jitter alone orders the pool and the pick
is uniform over it: the same distribution as the `floor(rng() * length)` it
replaced, though not the same element. That is the cost, and it is the right one
to pay. Had the draw count been lens-conditional, *every* hunger's deal would
differ from the no-lens deal by stream offset alone — the gate's central
assertion would pass unconditionally and certify a dead weight, reproducing the
exact failure it exists to catch. The gate therefore asserts **both** that at
least one hunger differs *and* that fewer than all twelve do.

## What the corpus actually supports

Measured, not estimated: 10 of 167 dilemmas carry a non-empty
`emotionalRegister`, and those ten were authored around one hunger. Coverage is
`gather`=10, `preserve`=4, `reclaim`=1, `bind`=1, and zero for the other eight.
So exactly one hunger — `gather` — deals differently from baseline today, which
is both the proof the channel is live and the measure of how much content work
remains.

The gate ships **advisory** on coverage for that reason: it reports the full
per-hunger distribution and asserts only that the channel is not dead, because
"every hunger is served" is what the content pass buys and asserting it early
would just mean a disabled test. The `HUNGER_RESONANCE_MIN_COVERAGE = 6` floor is
in the file, unused, waiting for the pass to flip it blocking.
