## What changed

The rotating free-slot fill is on (`CAP_FILL_ROTATE = true`), with a new first pass: up to `CAP_FILL_LOCAL_SLOTS` (30) free slots go to distinct templates on the mortal's own hex before the rotation takes the rest. The interface row `shortlist-reaches-every-template` is 🟢 LIVE.

## Why the local pass

After THR-1639 landed, rotation alone still cut total firings 1,552 → 676. The journey defect was not the cause. Rotation spent the free slots on other hexes, so a mortal standing in a town stopped seeing what it could start there. On seed 42, `start_local` decisions fell 549 → 225 while journeys stayed flat. Hex distance 0 is what `start_local` needs, so the fill now offers that first.

It misses the fourth, cap-first-gate ≤ 10, with 16 · 15 against a baseline of 75 · 63. I took that trade deliberately. The gate exists to get written encounters to fire, and 30 fires the most distinct templates of any arm. The First's longest gap between encounters is 25 · 30 (ceiling 30).
