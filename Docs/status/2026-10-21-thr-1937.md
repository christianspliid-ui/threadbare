# THR-1937 — Undertakings in the economy S1: a founded town has a ruler and a founder, a founded lane is tolled, a founded guild has a calling

**Shipped 2026-10-21.** Slice S1 of `Docs/plans/2026-10-17-thr-1937-undertakings-in-the-economy.md` (§ Engine 1–3, § Content S1 lines, § UI S1 rows).

**What changed.** A town a mortal founds now goes up on free ground (`findUnclaimedSite`, moved to `src/engine/foundingSite.ts`), never on the hex of the town the work started from; with none free nearby nothing is founded and the moment card says so. The town gets a ruler: the founder's town-holding faction where they rank highest (a realm wins a tie, then the lower id), else the founder alone. The ruler gets a `controls` edge the purse taxes through. The founder keeps a freehold through `grantHolding`. A founded lane is tolled by its founder (`controlledBy`), and the economic chronicle writes "A new trade route opens between …" for it. A founded guild takes a reach profile from its founder's two leading Reaches and a calling (`FOUNDED_GUILD_TYPE_BY_REACH`), so the purse pays it guild work. The place page has a "Founded by {founder}." row (linked, tooltip `ui.place.founded`); `__DEBUG.getFoundingRecord` / `getLaneHolders` and an Economy-tab held-lanes table expose it. Master switch `UNDERTAKING_ECONOMY_ENABLED`.

**Measured** (`npm run measure:undertaking-economy`, 600 ticks): founded towns 5 / 4 / 8 on seeds 42 / 99 / 7; every faction-ruled one (5 / 2 / 7) is in its ruler's `taxHolderOf` — the kill criterion does not fire. New shared settlement hexes: 0 on all three. Founded lanes tolled by founder: 0 / 1 / 4. Rollback: with the switch off, seed 42 at t300 matches `origin/main` in every `controls`, `owns` and `trades_with` edge (144 edges, identical hash). Live (`?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, Playwright 1920×1080): Whamu founded a hamlet at t122, ruled by a realm on a rank tie; its page reads "Founded by Whamu." and the name opens Whamu's page.
