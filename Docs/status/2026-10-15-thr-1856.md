# 2026-10-15 — THR-1856: Escape on an aftermath acknowledges it; the exit stays on screen

**Ticket:** [THR-1856](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1856). Found in warm playtest round 2. Pillars: UI (the veil), Engine N/A (no engine path changed; the notification resolve is a pure runtime helper), Content N/A.

- **Escape no longer bounces.** On an aftermath, Escape and the context strip's leave control now call the acknowledge path, not the disregard path. `handleEncounterDisregard` also routes an aftermath through the new `resolveNotificationsOnVeilLeave`, so no host can miss the rule. Before, Escape resolved only the record on screen. A sibling step notification then reopened the same ending behind a black fade (the THR-1778 bounce through a second door). Test: leaving an aftermath resolves exactly what "Return to the world" resolves, and the auto-open scan finds nothing to reopen.
- **The exit and the reaction choice are pinned.** They share one block that sticks to the bottom of the scroll column (`aftermath-pinned-exit`, the THR-1732 hand-bar pattern). The column's 10vh top and bottom inset became 4vh top. At 1920×1080 with the consequence legend open, "Return to the world" sits at y 991–1031 and takes the click.
- **A wheel anywhere on the veil scrolls it** (`forwardVeilWheel`). Before, a wheel over the art or the void beside the 52–65% column did nothing. This holds on the aftermath and the step view.
- **Split out:** the six-card hand. Under Law 33's four-per-row rule, two rows of 368 px cards cannot fit beside the step's chrome at 1080. A compact card face is a design call, so it is now [THR-1942](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1942).
