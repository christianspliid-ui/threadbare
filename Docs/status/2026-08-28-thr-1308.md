# T2 undertaking tier — works that are places, and the verbs that take them back (THR-1308)

**Shipped 2026-08-28.** Plan doc: `Docs/plans/2026-08-27-thr-1297-action-library-works-holdings-naming.md` §5/§6 and "The three vertical slices" #2.

## What landed

Tier 2 of the undertaking kind registry, on the same terms tier 1 shipped: **a kind arrives with the verb that undoes it, or it does not arrive.** Two rows, `trade_route` and `place_location`, and the one engine op the tier could not exist without.

**`create_location` — the op the tier needed.** Every T1 kind's object was an edge (`knows_secret_of`), a possession (masterwork, treasure map) or an actor-side record (intelligence, network), and none of those needs a node standing on the map. `createSublocation` mints a room *inside* somewhere that already exists — `parentLocationId` is required, and that field is the sublocation discriminator. Nothing minted the *somewhere*. The new op does, place-tier by construction: `hexCol`/`hexRow`, no `parentLocationId`, which is exactly what `isPlaceTierLocation` tests, so a founded settlement is visible to `getPlaceTierLocations` sweeps and absent from `getSublocationNodes` ones without either learning a new shape.

**Six new verbs, across three packs.** The builder founds and grows a settlement (`strategic_found_settlement`, `strategic_grow_settlement`); the merchant opens and widens a road (`strategic_establish_trade_route` gained its missing seams, plus a new `strategic_extend_route`); the warlord takes both back (`strategic_blockade_route`, `strategic_raze_settlement`). Both destroys are **cross-family and motive-gated** — the D column is what the *world* can do to take a work back, and a self-spend is a use, not a counter.

**The route gained an identity.** `trades_with` stays the economic authority every existing consumer reads; an edge simply has nowhere to carry a name, an owner or a blockade state, so the kind's object is a `locationSubtype: 'trade_route'` node minted alongside it. The blockade suspends rather than deletes — it writes `threatened`, the property `phaseProsperity` already skips a route on, so the owner feels it as their towns going hungry, and `routeEvents` lifts it after its horizon. A blockade that never lifted would be deletion wearing a counter's name.

## What the organic run found that the unit suite could not

Three things, and all three are the reason the Done-when asked for a 150-tick CLI arc rather than a green suite.

**1. A mid-list insertion silently killed a shipped verb.** `generateStrategicCandidates` walks an ambition's `templateIds` **in authoring order** and breaks at `STRATEGIC_MAX_CANDIDATES_PER_AMBITION` (5). Inserting `strategic_extend_route` after its sibling pushed `strategic_found_guild_chapter` from position 5 to 6 — out of reach — and took six assertions across two files with it. The fix is not a fixture edit: the templates are **appended**, so no existing verb moves. Worth stating plainly because the failure looked like brittle tests and was not; the tests were right and the insertion was the defect.

**2. The route names had a stutter.** `The The Shattered Sanctum–Greycity Road` — settlement names already carry their own article. Caught by reading the actual names out of a real world, which is the only place it was visible.

**3. The blockade has never once landed, and every dashboard says it is healthy.** This is the finding worth carrying forward. `createTradeRoute` mints at `volume: 1`; `phaseTradeRouteDecay` removes a route six ticks later unless something refreshes `lastTraded`, and nothing in the strategic path does. So a road founded by a six-tick undertaking deletes itself before the next season. Measured at 150 ticks: **0 surviving routes at ticks 40/60/80/150 on either seed**, and **0 routes ever carrying `blockadedBy`**.

Be precise about where that bites, because the obvious reading is wrong: the blockade verb is *not* starved at generation — it is offered and pursued **9 times on seed 42 and 16 on seed 99**. It is the mutation at completion that no-ops, returning `no_route` every time. Candidates generated, undertakings completed, census liveness 94.5% / 98.8% — every board-level instrument reads healthy while the counter-play does nothing. Filed as **THR-1320** rather than fixed inline, for the reason THR-1310 was filed separately: route durability is a shared economy with five consumers and needs its own golden comparison, not a line in a kind-authoring slice.

## Evidence

All six T2 verbs fire organically. Seed 42 / 150 ticks / medium, from `state.strategicState.history`: found_settlement 15, grow_settlement 15, raze_settlement 6, blockade_route 9, establish_trade_route 5, extend_route 2 — plus 5 route identity nodes and 9 founded settlements standing. Seed 99 reaches four of the six (16 found_settlement, 26 grow_settlement, 8 raze, 16 blockade); the two merchant route verbs do not complete there inside 150 ticks, which is pre-existing and not new to this tier.

Census liveness held at **94.5%** (seed 42) and **98.8%** (seed 99) against the ≥ 50% floor, against a 93.8% / 98.6% baseline measured before the change. Seed 99's decision-mix gate remains a **pre-existing** FAIL and moved in the right direction — undertaking share **7.2% → 10.0%** against a `[0.10, 0.35]` band it now sits on the boundary of.

## Still absent, deliberately

`sublocation` is the third T2 kind and is **not** registered: its six build templates ship, its raze does not. Same rule as ever — until a kind can be undone, it is not a kind. T3 (`warband`, `faction`) lands in THR-1309.
