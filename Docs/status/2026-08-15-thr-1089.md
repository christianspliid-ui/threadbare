# THR-1089 — the prune sweep deleted eleven modules and spared four, on re-verification rather than on the filed evidence

**Shipped 2026-08-15.** Seven prune tickets were consolidated into one sweep (Christian's direction, 2026-08-11 chat review: one batched sweep instead of seven queue slots). Their evidence was gathered 2026-07/08, and the sweep's own membership predicate required re-verifying every candidate against the **current** import graph before deleting anything — "the sweep's evidence is a candidate, not a verdict."

That re-verification is the whole story of this run. Of the candidates, **four dropped out**, and one of them had gained a real production caller in the interval.

## Deleted (11 units, 20 files, −2085 lines)

**Five engine modules** (THR-950), each with zero importers and unreachable from every entry point: `artSelection.ts`, `color.ts`, `culturalInsight.ts`, `ghostDots.ts`, `hexTooltipProse.ts`. `color.ts` carried a **second** test file the ticket did not name (`color-fallback.test.ts`); a prune that deleted only the named one would have left a test importing a deleted module.

**Three tail modules** (THR-952): `compositionResolver.ts` (with `compositionTypes.ts`, imported only by it), `hexGrouping.ts`, `types/climate.ts`.

**Two dead exports**: `checkMarkReveals` (THR-847) — six unit tests pinning a query nothing called, next to the near-identical `evaluateMarkReveals` that is live — and `summarizeEncounterPoolDominance` with its `EncounterPoolDominanceSummary` type (THR-1012). THR-1012 framed the latter as a design call between prune and wire; consolidating it into a *prune* sweep is the direction, so it was pruned and the `TODO(THR-1012)` in `ThreadsPanel.tsx` replaced with what actually happened.

**`RetinuePanel`** (THR-1022) and its 20-case test, plus the unused `GameView.tsx` import, two stale `StepDots.tsx` doc comments, and the two `ia-manifest.ts` `reader` rows. The manifest rows were the part that mattered beyond dead code: the IA manifest is a commitment document browsable at `?view=cms#ia-surfaces`, and it asserted that two GameState fields were read by a surface that does not exist. `ThreadsPanel` does **not** read `unifiedActions`, so the rows had no successor to repoint at and the whole `game.retinue-panel` surface was removed rather than reassigned.

## Spared, with the caller that saved each

- **`services/narration/encounterNarration.ts` gained a production importer.** `ProseTtsButton.tsx:18` imports from it. The 2026-08-02 evidence recorded 0 non-test refs and was correct then; this is exactly the case the predicate's re-verification clause exists to catch.
- **`src/composition-dsl/` is not a closed island.** THR-952 recorded it as one and instructed deciding the directory "as a whole unit, not member by member". As a whole unit it is **live**: `schema.ts` is imported by `phaseComposition.ts`, `notableAgendas.ts`, `rival.ts`, `types/gameState.ts` and `CompositionView.tsx`. A `mutationGate` ↔ `findCard` ↔ `validator` sub-island does have no outside importers, but deleting members piecemeal is what the ticket explicitly forbade.
- **The SceneStatePanel cluster is unwired, not retired** — THR-951's Done-when #3, its expected-and-valid outcome. `Docs/plans/2026-05-05-encounter-constants-tuning-playtest.md:125` already records that the C4 surface "shipped but has zero importers and is not mounted", and points at **THR-964** for the retire-or-wire decision. THR-964 sits at `Idea`, undecided: it owns the `pendingChoiceCommits` pipeline that has no producer, whose `DETECTION_THRESHOLD_*` and `CHOICE_DRIFT_MAGNITUDE_*` constants are precisely what `DetectionThread` and `DriftIndicator` render. Deleting the cluster here would have pre-empted a design call another ticket owns.
- **The `AgentDetailPanel` cluster is larger than THR-952 assessed.** Its candidate was `AttachmentRow.tsx`, gated on resolving its only importer first. `AgentDetailPanel` is indeed unrendered — `scripts/interface-contracts.ts:30` already calls it a "documented-dead component" — but it exports the `ActivitySummary` type consumed by `src/engine/activitySummary.ts`, and `getActivitySummary` itself has **zero non-test callers**. So the real unit is `AgentDetailPanel` (1186 lines) + `AttachmentRow` + `activitySummary.ts` + two test files, and removing it means editing live engine code to relocate a type. Predicate rule 3 — "if removal would change any live behavior, stop and note it" — so it is noted, not done.
