# THR-2159 — The mortal decides the reputation encounters, half one

**Shipped 2026-11-04.** Encounter Experience, slice 3 of 5 from the THR-2135 decision. The pattern is the THR-866 Apotheosis conversion (`Docs/plans/2026-08-11-thr-866-apotheosis-nudge-conversion.md`); no new design.

Each template used to key its endings to the ids of the cards the god clicked on step 0, so the click picked the ending ("Accept the tribute." / "Demand their oath instead."). Each now forks through `decidedBy`. The mortal decides; the god argues with a composed hand.

1. **Fork.** Pole mode: `decidedBy: { axis }`; branch variants, `aftermathConfig.variants` and `stakes.arms` re-keyed to `positive` / `negative`; every fallback (and base stakes) was already the positive pole. Route mode (first content use of THR-898): `decidedBy: { routes }`, one distinct reach per route; route keys are the old variant keys, so aftermath and stakes keep theirs. `branchOnStep` stays 0 (THR-979).
2. **Hand on step 0.** Two specials plus `deal: { count: 3 }` from the Repertoire. Pole mode: one special per pole. Route mode: the Tribute's specials name the accept and oath routes (withdraw is argued by its axis and the mortal's Stone capability); the Consultation's argue `revelation_discretion` + (toward the plain reading, away from refusal) and name the withholding route. Step 0 also gains a `purposeLine` and the three missing afterimages.
3. **Step 1 prose re-voiced** so the mortal acts ("The agent let the demand show…", "The agent held back from the text…"), not the god. Step-1 arms carry no hand: they had no cards before, and THR-2140's "the branch step offers no move" test on the Merchant's Favor stays true.
4. **`authoredChoices` removed** from all seven. `REPUTATION_GATED_README.md` gains Rule 3a (the mortal decides; pole vs route; the per-template table) and drops the "choice card intent text" home for `{title}`.
5. **Tests.** New `mortal-decides-reputation-half-one.test.ts` pins the Done-when over the shipped catalog: no `authoredChoices`, the fork shape (axis + pole keys, or route keys with distinct reaches and every special arguing a declared course), a non-empty step-0 hand, the reachability and producibility invariants, `checkNudgeHand` / `checkComposedHand` clean, and the stakes arms. The authoredChoices population floors in `unifiedTemplateAftermathReachability.test.ts` and `resolveInterveneChoice.test.ts` carry the same relaxation as THR-2158's PR (byte-identical hunks).
