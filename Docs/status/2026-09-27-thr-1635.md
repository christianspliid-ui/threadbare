# 2026-09-27 — THR-1635: an encounter opening states one fact about the town's people or the place's power

**Ticket:** [THR-1635](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1635) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1635-culture-sphere-openings.md` (slice 1 of 2; slice 2 is THR-1638) · **Pillars:** Engine, Content, UI (no new component; a debug read and a Fragments-tab row).

## What was wrong

Every world generates cultures and place sphere affinities, but no encounter prose read either. An opening in a town of open witnesses read the same as one in a town of sworn silence.

## What shipped

- **The carrier.** `compileOpeningColoration` puts a reserved `{frag:place_fact}` token at the end of step 0's situation paragraph, after the `{frag:opening}` envelope paragraph. It runs at the catalog assembly points: `ENCOUNTER_TEMPLATES`, the pools behind `getAnyEncounterById` (compiled once through a WeakMap memo), `LOCATION_BRANCHING_ENCOUNTER_TEMPLATES`, and the encounter-shaped members of `UNIFIED_ACTION_TEMPLATES` (`isEncounterShapedTemplate`). At render, `enrichProse` resolves the slot with `resolveOpeningColoration`: the town culture's custom first (foundation × reach), else a sphere fact when the dominant sphere holds at least 55% of the place's score, else nothing. With no line, the paragraph is byte-identical to before.
- **Coloration axes.** `foundation` and `sphere` join the fragment module as coloration axes. They never create a surface or touch `computeSurfaceKey`, and a template authoring one is reported.
- **Keyed on the town.** `src/engine/openingColoration.ts` reads the Location tier's current `belongs_to` culture (a Place walks up to its town) and the place's own sphere first, then the town's. It never keys on the actor's culture.
- **The stamp.** `stampCultureCustomVariants` runs once in `worldSeed.ts`. It is an ordinal among same-foundation cultures and consumes no rng. `readCultureCustomVariant` derives the same number for older saves.
- **Content.** `src/data/culture-sphere-lines.ts`: 36 culture customs (4 foundations × iron/stone/eye × 3) and 24 sphere facts (life/matter/darkness/order × iron/stone/eye × 2). They are ported from the prototype, with the missing variants written and the sphere-jargon phrasings rewritten. The four Foundation spheres joined `SPHERE_VOCABULARY`, which now covers all twelve.
- **Doctrine.** `NUDGE_WORD_BUDGETS.colorationLine` (28) and `colorationLineProblems` check budget, tokens, one sentence and sphere jargon. The opening checker ignores the token.
- **Debug.** `__DEBUG.getOpeningColoration(locationId?, reach?)` and `getColorationCensus()`; the `opening_coloration_bound` trace; a reserved-slot row on the Fragments tab.
- **The chapter archive threads the template's fragments and reach.** It re-renders step prose "as the player read it" but passed no template id, so every `{frag:*}` slot stripped there, including the opening envelope.

## Result

- Corpus guard: 798 entries (528 ids) each carry exactly one token. Exempt: 20 slice entries and 1 branch-first template.
- Leak guard, seeds 42 and 99: all 528 templates render with no raw token. 248 carry a culture line at a culture town and 248 a sphere line at a strong-sphere place.
- Census on seed 42: 41 culture towns and 35 strong-sphere places (re-measure: 40 / 35). Three cultures, three distinct stamps.
- Browser: *Master the Local Craft* at Great Silverhold (Darkness) reads "… The Oerkoyrir pass a craft by initiation; the last step is taught only to those the circle has tested, and Vara has not been tested."

## Lane decisions (veto invited)

- "Encounter-shaped" registry members are those the ascendant cannot cast and that are not `action.*` / `npc_*` verbs.
- The pools are compiled at `getAnyEncounterById`, their one shared read point, not in eight modules.
