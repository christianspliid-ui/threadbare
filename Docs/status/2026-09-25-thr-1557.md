## What shipped

- **The victor decides over a beaten loser**, whichever side lost (`decideBeatenDuellist`, `src/engine/fights/fightEnding.ts`). Beaten = clock filled or struck down. The victor's live `mercy_ruthlessness` (`FIGHT_MERCY_AXIS`) through `decideBranchPole`; the god's hand counts only when the victor is the action's actor (`FightEndContext.handNudges`, passed from the rolled route). Mercy → **spared** (Scarred + `blood_drawn`, victor drifts toward mercy). Ruthlessness → D1's guards (The First, the avatar), then one kill draw at `FIGHT_DUEL_KILL_CHANCE_RUTHLESS` (0.25) → **slain** through `markMortalDead`, else **mauled**. `killStruckDownFighter` takes a `loserId`.
- **A yielded or fled duellist is never finished.** Yielded → humiliated at their own home + drift toward prudence; routed → drift toward prudence; a double yield humiliates nobody.
- **`fightState.opponentEnding`** is filled on every duel: the opponent's face, or `overcome_mortal` when they won (standing toward the actor's faction ?? home — D2's `victorStanding` reused on a yield, never written twice — and drift toward courage). The loser's record carries `mercy` (`FightMercyRecord`).
- **One `fight.ending` trace carries the fork:** `victorPole`, `victorProfileLean`, `victorCardLean`, `mercyDecidedBy`, `opponentFace`, `opponentLoss`, `bothStruckDown`, `opponentKillRoll`, `opponentGuard`, `opponentOutcomeNodeId`.
- **The chronicle tells the loser's story:** a duel the actor won emits one `fight_ended` event with the opponent's face (id suffix `_opponent`); a double knockout emits both.
- `getFightState(...).opponentEnding` on the debug bridge. NPC-mode fights are unchanged (no `opponentEnding`, no mortal kill).
