# 2026-09-25 — THR-1613: six seed families that always withered now resolve

**Ticket:** [THR-1613](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1613) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** Content = the fix · Engine N/A · UI N/A.

## What was wrong

Fourteen shipped templates planted follow-up seeds under six `encounterFamily` prefixes: `stone.legacy`, `star.referred_pilgrim`, `veil.quiet_devotion`, `eye.kept_record`, `stone.accused_vengeance` and `investigation`. None of the six had an alias row, and no template id started with any of them. Every such seed withered, wherever the target stood. Measured by THR-1597 (seeds 42 · 99, medium, 200 ticks): 19 · 41 of the 24 · 46 withered seeds came from these families. `mason_lord_wall` and `page_beneath_saint` are among the most-fired templates, so the loss repeated every run.

## What changed

The 21 authored seed sites now carry a `query` in place of the dead prefix. Each query names the errand family whose story fits the seed's label:

| Old family | Now resolves to | Why |
| -- | -- | -- |
| `stone.legacy` (wall legend, a contested deed) | `#fellowship_errand` | Builders' work: foundations, walls, surveys |
| `star.referred_pilgrim` ("…seeks the god") | `#temple_errand` | Observance and the spheres' asking |
| `veil.quiet_devotion` (faith by its fruit) | `#temple_errand` | The same |
| `eye.kept_record` (the record becomes conscience) | `#covenant_errand` | Lorekeepers: what is written down, and keeping it |
| `stone.accused_vengeance` (the accused find allies) | any of `#court_errand` / `#thieves_errand` | Deniable work for someone with a grudge |
| `investigation`, anomaly sites | `#circle_errand` (vault, glowcap, dreams) · `#thieves_errand` (drowned hoard) · `#fellowship_errand` (star-metal crater) | Who would come asking about each find |
| `investigation`, army sites | `#company_errand` (army raised, mutiny) · `#watch_errand` (refugees turned away) | Who reacts to each event |

The dead-family ratchet in `encounterSeedLiveness.test.ts` drops from 168 to 118. A new named block there checks two things: no planter names a retired family again, and every repointed seed resolves to at least one drawable template that a single mortal can perform.

## Measured after (THR-1597 reach reader, seeds 42,99 × 200, medium)

Withered seeds fell from **24 · 46** to **8 · 15**. Every one left is `subtype_mismatch`: the target was standing somewhere the family's members do not accept. No seed withers for "no query, zero members" any more.

## Noticed, not fixed

Most of the remaining withers are errand families judged in a **hamlet**, the most-occupied Location subtype. Hamlet mortals account for about 1,500–2,500 of ~5,000–6,400 occupancy samples. `#temple_errand` and `#covenant_errand` accept `village`, but the map has no subtype of that name. The hamlet is the village, so those members never admit a hamlet mortal. The other 33 dead families (118 sites) remain on the ratchet. Their planters rarely fire, so none showed up in the ledger run.
