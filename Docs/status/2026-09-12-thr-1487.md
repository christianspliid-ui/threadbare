# THR-1487 — the content query, and the gate that was checking one recipe in 482

*Slice 3 of THR-1481 (content model). 2026-09-12.*

Content can now ask for content **by kind and tags** instead of naming an id, and exactly
one resolver answers. `ContentQuery` (`src/types/contentQuery.ts`) + `resolveContentQuery`
(`src/engine/contentQuery.ts`) are the shape and the rule; the reward pool is the first
consumer and the condition pool the second, both moved onto it under shared-path tests
that pin their candidate sets byte-identical against a frozen copy of the pre-change
predicate.

**The finding.** Widening `validateRewardDrawPools` → `validateContentQueries` to cover the
step route (`ActionStepOutcomeMetadata.rewardPool`) alongside the `reward_draw` effect
turned up the measurement that matters: the corpus holds **1 recipe on the effect route
and 481 on the step route**. The gate that has guarded reward liveness since THR-1146 was
checking one recipe in 482 and reporting the corpus clean. Sweeping both surfaced **16
step-route recipes that promise a prize and draw nothing** — nine wanting tags no entry
carries, four naming a `categoryWeights` key that is not an `AttachmentCategory` at all.
They are grandfathered in `CONTENT_QUERY_RETROFIT_PENDING` behind a ratchet that fails in
both directions, and repaired by [THR-1496](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1496).

**What an author gets.** `{ kind: 'item_template', tags: ['#weapon', '#entropy'] }`, with
`classes` to narrow within a kind, `anyTags`, a tier window, and `exclude`.
`window.__DEBUG.queryContent(query)` and CLI `query <json>` answer "would this match
anything?" against a live world in one line; `content.query_resolved` /
`content.query_empty` say what happened at runtime. Wiring guide **Capability 29** and
`Docs/canon/content-objects.md` § The content query carry the rules.

**One structural decision worth knowing.** The resolver is split in two: `contentQuery.ts`
holds the matching rule and the graph carve and imports only the registry;
`contentCatalogView.ts` holds the reach into the catalogs. That is not tidiness — reaching
a catalog means importing all 32 of them, and `src/data/undertaking-objects.ts` calls the
resolver from inside that import graph, so a single module closed an initialisation cycle
that reddened 31 unrelated test files (impediment #1030).
