# THR-1213 — the Hunger you chose now steers every god's meeting, not one god's

**Shipped 2026-08-28.** Closes the hunger-vocabulary unification (plan doc:
`Docs/plans/2026-08-27-hunger-vocabulary-unification.md`). Slice 1 merged the two
catalogs, slice 2 wired the weight into the live picker, slice 3 landed the gate
advisory; **this slice is the content pass that makes the gate mean something**,
and with it the coverage assertion goes blocking.

## What changed

**The 157 unlabelled dilemmas learned what they are about.** Every one of the
167 shipped meeting dilemmas now carries an `emotionalRegister` (3 tags) and a
`driveResonance` (2 tags), drawn from the closed `ResonanceTag` union and
authored from each scene's own text — labels, never rewrites. Before this pass
10 dilemmas carried a register and all ten had been written around a single
hunger; `gather` was the only god whose choice at remembrance changed what the
world asked of their First.

Coverage now, measured rather than asserted:

```
gather=59  witness=58  reclaim=26  reshape=40  preserve=65  kindle=20
sever=8    bind=26     wander=32   consume=18  haunt=37     illuminate=50
```

**The gate is blocking.** `hungerResonanceGate.test.ts` no longer prints an
advisory warning and asserts a floor of one served hunger; it now requires every
hunger to resonate with at least `HUNGER_RESONANCE_MIN_COVERAGE` (6) dilemmas,
and fails naming the starved ones with the full distribution attached.

## Two things worth stating plainly

**`sever` is thin at 8, and that is the honest number.** Freedom, rebellion,
independence, chains, escape and solitude are a narrow band in a corpus of
scenes set inside settlements a mortal is bound to. Its eight are the scenes
that genuinely turn on getting out. Spraying `freedom` across the rest to
flatter the distribution would have restored this ticket's own defect one layer
up — a number that looks alive because the labels were written to make it look
alive — so the floor stays at 6 and the real minimum is recorded instead.

**A guard's stated reason was falsified by the change it gates, while the guard
stayed green.** The gate's second assertion requires that *fewer than all twelve*
hungers deal differently from the no-lens baseline, and its comment explained
why in terms of the old corpus: a hunger that resonates with nothing must land
on exactly the baseline. After this pass no hunger resonates with nothing — yet
the assertion still passes, because 4 of 12 differ at seed 42 and the other
eight hold the baseline on coverage they *have*, their tags simply not
reordering the top of any pool that seed draws from. Same guard, same failure
caught, different mechanism underneath. Nothing went red, and nothing would
have: the comment would simply have gone on explaining a premise that had
stopped being true. It is corrected in the file, and the pattern is logged
(#890), because a green test with a false rationale is how the dead channel this
whole ticket repairs survived for the life of the feature.

## Verified

Falsified in both directions rather than merely run. Drifting one hunger's
`dilemmaResonanceTags` out of the dilemma vocabulary turns the gate red naming
that hunger (`haunt=0`); reverting the library to its pre-pass state turns it
red at 11/12 below the floor, reproducing the pre-pass distribution exactly
(`gather=10, preserve=4, reclaim=1, bind=1`). So the content is what flipped the
gate, not a loosened threshold.

The interface-map contract row `hunger-resonance-weighs-the-meeting-deal` and
its served wiki spotlight both carried the pre-pass numbers as evidence; both
are updated here, since a contract row citing falsified measurements is the
drift that map exists to catch.

## Left open

`sever` at 8 is a content-supply observation, not a defect — the next batch of
meeting dilemmas is the place to widen it, and the floor at 6 leaves room to
notice if it narrows. THR-1318 (overlay-engine activation) remains ordered after
this pass, as its checkpoint recorded: `shouldFireMortalEcho` reads the same
field this pass changed, so tuning its threshold earlier would have been tuning
against a number that has now moved.
