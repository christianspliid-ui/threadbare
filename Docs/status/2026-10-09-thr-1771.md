# 2026-10-09 — THR-1771: the cadence pool's identity tilt reads the right scale

**Ticket:** [THR-1771](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1771) · **Source:** `Docs/audits/2026-10-06-thr-1769-god-card-grant-inventory-research.md` § 3

The fix covers three faults in the god's cadence-pool draw. All three came from the THR-1769 research on the Dominion map.

- **Identity bias.** `computeIdentityBias` now divides the raw `domainAffinities` value (2–5) by `BEAT_REACH_AFFINITY_FULL_SCALE` (4, the hunger catalog's top reach) and clamps it to [0..1]. A raw-4 reach draws at exactly `BEAT_REACH_BIAS_CEILING` (×3). The code used to read the raw 4 as 1, which gave ×9. With the sphere bonus, the showcase god's `the_unveiled_eye` weight drops from 54 to 18. Its share of the first draw falls from 40% to under 20%.
- **Signature beat retirement.** `hasUnacquiredReachSignature` now considers ranks 0 and 1 only. Only those two signatures are ever granted, so a three-reach god's beat now retires once both are held.
- **Delivery share.** `DELIVERY_BEAT_WEIGHT` is now derived from a named target, `DELIVERY_FIRST_DRAW_SHARE` (0.2, today's measured share), and from the count of deliverable encounters. Before, it was a hand-set 0.1 under a comment that said "~23 beats, ~10%"; the real numbers were 84 beats and about 20%. Adding encounters no longer changes how often visions arrive.

**Monte Carlo** (in-repo test, audit § 3 recipe, 300 runs per god in draw units): mean first arrival of the non-identity investment beats is Shepherd 13.09 draws and showcase 13.51 draws (+3%, bound 20%).

Tests: `ascendantBeatIdentity.test.ts` (raw-4 = ceiling, raw-5 clamps, unveiled-eye share < 20%, delivery share = named constant at any count, Monte Carlo), `ascendantBeat.test.ts` (three-reach retirement). Wiki: `run-lifecycle-reference.html`.
