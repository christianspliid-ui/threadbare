# 2026-09-26 — THR-1619: A new trade route never ends where it starts

**Ticket:** [THR-1619](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1619) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** none (self-scoped bug, found by THR-1595) · **Pillars:** Engine. Content N/A (no authored data changes). UI N/A (no surface changes).

## What shipped

1. **The route far end excludes the actor's own settlement.** The `location_subtype` target rule sorts by proximity, so the Location the actor stood in (distance 0) was the first far end offered. Both route writers then found no near end distinct from it: the `create × route` cell failed `no_endpoints` at completion, and the legacy `create_trade_route` arm wrote nothing. `findValidTargets` now drops three ids before the cap, for route-creating templates only: where the actor stands, that place's parent Location, and the durable Location. New exported helpers `createsRoute(template)` and `actorOwnSettlementIds(...)` live in `src/engine/strategicActionCandidates.ts`. Other `location_subtype` templates (building a place in your own town) are unchanged.
2. **`census:cells` reports verb failures.** `strategic_world_change` traces with `succeeded: false` are now counted as `verbFailures` (`<verb>.<kind>:<error>`), with samples giving the actor's current and durable Location. Before this, the history ledger recorded these completions as `completed`, so a cell that failed every time looked healthy.

**Mutex note:** the fix stays in `src/engine/strategicActionCandidates.ts`. `src/data/undertaking-objects.ts` is untouched, so the conditional mutex with THR-1615/THR-1617 does not apply.

## Evidence

- Cause confirmed in the field on the old code: `census:cells` seed 42, 300 ticks, `create.route:no_endpoints` ×1 at t198. `agent_garrison_culture_0` targeted `loc_4`, and `loc_4` was both its current and its durable Location.
- After the fix: `census:cells` seeds 42/99 at 150 ticks, zero `create.route` verb failures. At 300 ticks, zero `no_endpoints`. One `route_already_exists` remains on seed 42, which is a separate refusal.
- Unit tests (`strategicActionCandidates.test.ts`): legacy and cell arms, with the actor standing in the settlement or in a place inside it. The own settlement is never offered and the far town is. A control test shows `cell.create.place` still targets the home town. All four route tests fail with the exclusion line removed.
- `strategicRemoteAnchorGate.test.ts`: three refusal assertions are now scoped to the guild template. They had checked that *no* template targeted the far town, which held only because the route template spent its single slot on the merchant's own capital.
