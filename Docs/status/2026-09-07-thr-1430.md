# THR-1430 — the dormant kinds II: rings and the plot

**2026-09-07 · Engine + Content + UI · project Thematic Pressure & Living World**

Second half of the *dormant kinds* band on the wayfinder map [THR-1396](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1396), after [THR-1429](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1429). Four cells decided in THR-1397, one dormant kind woken, one new way for a mortal to leave the story, and the death funnel two maps were waiting for.

## The one death funnel

`markMortalDead` in `agentLifecycle.ts`. Three places killed mortals and they did not agree: the lifecycle asked the `death_prevented` ward and the Aspect echo and then **removed** the node; `bandOpposition` and `aspects` **retained** it and asked nothing. Whether a ward saved you depended on *how* you were being killed.

The funnel carries ward → echo → write, with `mode: 'retain' | 'remove'`. All three writers repointed, **behaviour preserved exactly**: the lifecycle keeps `remove`, the other two keep `retain`. Whether *every* death should retain is deliberately not settled here — it moves every node-absence reader (`bindingRegistry`, `isAgentGone`, the spatial sweeps) at once, and is the map's next question for the Agent Lifecycle seam. The Physical Conflict fight framework ([THR-1258](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1258)) is the next caller; the JSDoc says so.

A retained body is what let the rest ship — the chronicle can name the dead, the grievance lane can avenge them, and the succession phase can see the seat is empty.

## The network kind

`GroupKind` had `'network'` and nothing minted one: `createGroup` stamped `'company'` unconditionally. Two halves were needed and they are separate on purpose:

- the group phase enumerates `GROUP_PHASE_KINDS` (`company`, `network`) for upkeep, cohesion and dissolution;
- only the **movement** sub-step is gated on `GROUP_KINDS_THAT_TRAVEL` (`company`).

Widening the enumeration without gating movement makes rings travel; gating movement without widening the enumeration leaves them invisible to upkeep and dissolution. Each half alone is a defect wearing the other's clothes, so the tests assert them separately. `getAllGroups` still defaults to companies alone, so its ~40 callers are unchanged.

## The plot

`destroy × Mortal`, which needed a new object type: `mortal` was a `WorldObjectKindId` but not an `UndertakingObjectTypeId`. It is **self-owned** — no edge says who holds a person, and the motive gate asks about the owner, so without that the gate read every mortal as unowned and refused every plot by construction.

Licensed only by `grudge` or `faction_war` (`PLOT_MOTIVES`) — opportunism does not get to kill. Staged over three checkpoints. A followed target gets a `peril` moment (`interrupt`) after the positioning and the strike defers `PLOT_PERIL_GRACE_TICKS`; the card never names the plotter, and the god answers with levers that already exist.

`action.shadow.assassinate` is retired — its only effect was a node deletion the chronicle cannot remember. `action.gold.commission-assassination` keeps its place and stops deleting, through a new `mark_mortal_dead` graph op.

## Four things the plan got wrong, corrected against the code

1. **`groupCohesion` has no proximity term to switch on.** Cohesion is event-driven and already position-agnostic. Nothing to do.
2. **`isAgentGone` already reads `deceased`** — which answers the plan's grey-zone question (yes, a dead bound cast member is gone).
3. **The recruit filter read the wrong axis.** "Leans Shadow" is the *Reach*, not the darkness *Sphere* — orthogonal axes. Measured on seed 42: the Sphere reading found zero candidates among eleven co-located mortals.
4. **And it should not have been a gate at all.** An ordinary NPC carries no capability traits, so `computeCapability` returns ~0.02 for them and a hard threshold left a settlement full of people supplying no recruits — this plan's own kill criterion, reached before shipping. Affinity now *sorts* (faction-mates, then Shadow-leaners, then whoever is standing there), as `raiseWarband` does.

Two more found by the tests: reinforcing a ring re-added its own members (`isGrouped` resolves through `getGroupOf`, which is company-scoped, so ring members read as ungrouped and the duplicate edge id took the reinforcement down); and the catalog guard the plan asked for — a sweep over `remove_node` on `$target` — also catches `action.iron.conquer` and `action.stone.demolish`, which delete a *place* and are right to. The effect layer does not type its target, so that sweep cannot be written; it was narrowed to the two cards that ever deleted a mortal, with the real rule enforced where the type is known.

## The seat

`phaseFactionSuccession` / `factionNetwork` carried no `deceased` read. `getAnointedLeaderId`'s docblock had always *promised* a dead leader was ignored, and the promise held only because death removed the node. Both seams now read it — the anointed edge and the score fallback — because guarding one alone would have re-derived the corpse as leader by standing.
