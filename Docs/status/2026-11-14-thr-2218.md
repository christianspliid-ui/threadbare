# THR-2218 — A won chapter credits the cards

**Shipped 2026-11-14.** Thematic Pressure & Living World (warm playtest round 4, 3 of 3 testers in Flawed Steel).

- **The tie goes to the hand.** `decidingStepIndex` (`src/engine/aftermathWords.ts`): on an ending that was won, a band tie between two steps goes to the step the god's hand was on before it goes to the later step. A lost ending keeps the later-step tie-break. Flawed Steel with cards on step 1 and both steps going well now reads *"Kael won Flawed Steel. The work on Iron went well, and your cards held it."*
- **A won ending never says "your cards were on another step".** When an untouched step decided a won chapter and another step had the cards, the line names the deciding step plainly, then credits the touched one in its own sentence: *"… The work on Gold went well. The work on Iron had your cards behind it."* (`CAUSE_GOD_ON_STEP`). A lost chapter decided by an untouched step still reads *"your cards were on another step, not this one"* — that case is true.
- **The hand says which step it plays into.** The veil's hand bar shows *"These cards go to step 1 of 2."* above Play (`nudgeHandStepLine`, the tracker's own words; silent on one-step chapters and when a host passes no step count).
