# 2026-09-26 — THR-1566: a commander killed in battle now dies, not vanishes

**Ticket:** [THR-1566](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1566) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** Engine = the fix · Content = one sheet word (*fell in battle*) · UI N/A (the existing death line on the mortal card reads it).

## What was wrong

When a battle's aftermath rolled `killed` for the losing commander, `applyAftermath` ran `graph.removeNode(commanderId)`. That skipped the death funnel. No body was left, no one grieved, no culprit was recorded, and the commander's bonds, memberships and every `pursues` naming them disappeared without a trace.

## What changed

- The kill goes through `markMortalDead` with `cause: 'battle'` and `mode: 'retain'`. The funnel's ward and Aspect echo apply. If a ward holds, the commander counts as *retreated*.
- `slainBy` is set to the victor army's commander, when that army has a living one.
- When there is a culprit, a `named_death` outcome node (`evt_und_battle_<battleId>_<tick>`, a new non-undertaking source kind `battle`) is written. THR-1536's routing then carries the grief to the dead commander's warmest living bonds.
- A siege won by the town itself has no commander on the winning side. The commander is still retained, but no culprit is named and no one grieves. That is THR-1536's rule for a harm with no culprit.
- `MortalDeathCause` gains `'battle'`. The sheet word is *fell in battle*. `killerIsKnown` treats a battle death as witnessed.
- The `mortal-dies-through-one-funnel` interface contract lists `battleAftermath.ts` as a write site.

## Evidence

- `battleAftermath.test.ts`: 5 new tests. They cover a retained-deceased commander with cause and killer, grief reaching a bond, no culprit when the winning side has no commander, and captured and retreated commanders left unchanged. The fate seed is searched for, not hard-coded.
- `warNews.test.ts` drives the real `resolveBattle` and used to detect a commander's death by the node being gone. It now reads the `deceased` mark, so it requires the retained shape.
- The full suite, the heavy lane, the typecheck ratchet (unchanged at 2818), the vite build and a 30-tick smoke are all green.
- CLI, 200 ticks on seed 42 medium: **0 commanders killed** (2 captured). Seeds 99 and 7 also had 0, and so did seed 42 run to 400 ticks. A kill needs a total rout plus a 30% roll, so it is rare. The real-path proof is the `warNews` arm.
