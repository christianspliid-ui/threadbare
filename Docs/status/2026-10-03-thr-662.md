### 2026-10-03 — THR-662: the last two no-op divine verbs now do something

**Sanctify and Hearthfire Blessing are real.** They were the last two god-verbs in the action catalog that took essence and only narrated. The catalog now has no `effectSource: "none"` entries.
- **Sanctify** (`sub.sanctify`) is a Place-scale consecrate: a sustained hold on a shrine, temple, ruin or cave. Each tick it costs `SANCTIFY_PERTICK` spirit and advances every thread standing there by `SANCTIFY_DEVOTION_PER_TICK` (half a temple's consecration).
- **Hearthfire Blessing** (`sub.sanctify_tavern`) is a sustained hold on a tavern. Each tick it costs `HEARTH_BLESSING_PERTICK` life, and every mortal deciding there gets `HEARTH_BLESSING_SOCIAL_BOOST` on the tavern's social multiplier (×1.3 → ×1.6).
- The promises nothing read ("repels hostile forces", "drawing agents from neighbouring hexes") are gone from both descriptions.

**Root-cause fix along the way.** `spawnControlEffect` now accepts a Place target: when the node has no hex coords it anchors on the parent Location's hex and keeps `targetNodeId` on the Place. Seeded worldgen Places happen to carry their own coords, so this guards Places minted without them (and the legacy `sublocation` node type).

**Evidence (headless, seed 42 medium, 30 ticks, two arms).** Sanctified beacon-shrine: the aura fired 30/30 ticks and touched a threaded pilgrim each tick. Blessed tavern: 6/6 tavern social traces carry `hearthBlessingApplied`, multiplier 1.6. The unblessed arm: 0/6, multiplier 1.3.

**Not done.** No UI change was needed: the Sources section and the sustained-status prose already handle both verbs.
