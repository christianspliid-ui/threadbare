# THR-1046 — The Package View, one page per encounter's whole content package

**Shipped 2026-08-09.** Encounter Factory implementation item 4 (plan:
`Docs/plans/2026-08-08-encounter-factory-workflow.md`, approved with rulings
2026-08-08). Christian's requirement, verbatim: *"a way of easily being able to
see the entire encounter content package."*

`?view=cms#encounter-packages?template=<id>` renders every composed block of one
encounter, **resolved**: steps with prose and the full afterimage ladder side by
side, each hand drawn by the encounter stage's own card row, cast bindings with
roles and delivery, reward recipes and persistent consequences, every aftermath
variant × outcome band, seeds with their target encounters as live links, every
image tag with the art it actually hits, and THR-1045's per-block composition
verdict inline. `?batch=<ids>` renders up to six side by side — ruling 1's review
surface, whose job is making variance visible.

## Why resolved, not raw

A designer reading template source already has the file. What the file cannot
tell them is what the *engine* will make of it: `imageTag: 'generic.focus'` is
either a manifest hit, a scored fallback, or a category generic, and only the
resolver knows which. So every block reports its **resolution source** rather
than its authored value alone — which is the class of gap (declared, not
resolving) the composition audit found and could not show.

The other half is bands. A playthrough rolls one; the endings that most need
review are the rare ones, so they are the ones that go unwritten. The package
shows the whole ladder at once, with **unauthored bands drawn as rows** rather
than omitted — a shorter list would hide exactly the gap the page exists to find.
That is why the band row count is band-driven, and why a test asserts removing
content never shortens it.

## What it found on first load

The surface earned its keep before it shipped. On `encounter.slice.unsafe_bridge`
— the flagship nudge-era encounter — two blocks fail: five aftermath changes
declare no `concepts` (THR-1053's rule), and three card `imageTag`s name no
library row (THR-1052's finding, now visible per-card rather than in a sweep). In
the six-package batch, `encounter.slice.bargain_at_crossroads` shows a step whose
hand is **0 cards**, and reach spread, band coverage and seed presence differ
column to column at a glance.

One genuine defect was found and filed rather than absorbed: **THR-1054** — ten
`hod.*` templates key `aftermathConfig.variants` by *step index → band* instead of
`choiceId → AftermathVariant`, so their authored endings resolve to nobody. It
surfaced because the model is built for **every live template in a test**, not
only for a fixture: `checkCompositionContract` threw `variant.changes is not
iterable` on `hod.quest.temple_vigil`, against its own documented "never throws"
contract. Two unguarded spreads in that module are now guarded, so the gate and
this surface report violations instead of dying; the content defect is THR-1054's.

## Decisions worth knowing

- **The verdict is delegated, never re-derived.** `checkCompositionContract`
  (THR-1045) owns what composition-complete means; this page calls it and groups
  its violations by block. A second predicate over the same corpus is precisely
  the drift `check:predicate-copies` exists to prevent. The one list that *is*
  local (`PERSISTENT_KINDS_SHOWN`) decides a caption, not a verdict, and says so.
- **The card row is reused, not re-rendered.** `NudgeCard` is exported from
  `NudgePhaseShell` and `cardModelFor` from `buildNudgePhaseModel` (as
  `buildNudgeCardModel`). A second card implementation here would let the
  designer surface and the player stage disagree about the very thing the surface
  exists to review (Law 27). Cards render with `interactive: false` — a display of
  authored content, not a control that cannot fire (Law 25).
- **Authored prose is shown un-enriched, with its slots chipped.** A resolved
  `{they}` would hide whether the author wrote a token at all. But a raw
  `{frag:opening}` on screen reads as the Law 43 defect rather than the authored
  value it is, so tokens render as hoverable slot chips: visibly a slot,
  impossible to mistake for prose.
- **CMS hash routing had to be built, and is the prerequisite rather than a
  nicety.** The registry has documented an entry id as "used as URL hash
  fragment" since it was written, and CLAUDE.md has advertised
  `?view=cms#ia-surfaces` for months — while **nothing read the hash**. Every
  such link landed on the empty CMS and the visitor picked the entry by hand.
  `useCmsHashRoute` closes that for every CMS entry, and is what gives this page
  one shareable URL per template. Writes `replaceState` rather than pushing, so
  browsing nine packages does not bury the back button.
- **`AFTERMATH_BANDS` is `UnifiedActionOutcome`, deliberately not `StepOutcome`.**
  The two overlap on six names and differ on two: `near_miss` is a step outcome
  and not an outcome band, `contested_won`/`contested_lost` the reverse. Typing
  the list as the wrong union type-checks and then draws rows nobody can author —
  the trap `AftermathOutcomeOverride`'s own doc comment warns about, and the
  typecheck ratchet caught it here.
- **Batch columns are summaries, not six full packages.** The comparison a
  reviewer makes across six is structural — steps, reaches, hand sizes, band
  coverage, systems, tone — and six full packages in one viewport would be six
  unreadable columns. The column header opens the full package for the one that
  looks wrong.

## Scope notes

- The picker covers the **whole** registry (682 templates), not only the
  nudge-era 15: the plan's Done-when asks for one retrofitted *and one legacy*
  encounter, and the contrast between a composed package and a thin one is the
  point. Rows sort hand-bearing first.
- Packages are built **on demand**, not eagerly, because building all 682 would
  resolve every image tag in the corpus on page load for the one package the
  visitor came to read.
- No engine change, and no engine smoke: the diff touches `src/components/**`
  plus one authoring-time module (`src/data/content-eval/compositionContract.ts`),
  no tick-loop, orchestrator, phase or agent-decision file.
