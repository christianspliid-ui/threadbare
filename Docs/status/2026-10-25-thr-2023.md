# THR-2023 — debts in the money encounters

**Shipped 2026-10-25.** A debt can now end before its term. Five money encounters now write the debt their prose already describes.

**Engine.** A new aftermath effect, `debt_settle` (`src/engine/debtSettle.ts`, `settleDebt`). It removes the oldest live debt owed to a creditor, with ties broken by edge id. On `paid` the debtor pays `DEBT_REPAY_MEANS`. On `written_off` no coin moves. Owed nothing, it no-ops with a `debt_settle_noop` trace and the band's other rows still apply. It ends debts only, never favours. `creditorAgentId` is a sentinel field. New traces: `agreement_settled` and `debt_settle_noop`. Kill switch: `ENCOUNTER_DEBT_SETTLE_ENABLED`. One fix found on the way: `bond_change` no longer writes sentiment onto an agreement edge. The Loan's reciprocal resentment would otherwise have landed on the debt itself.

**Content.** Debt Collection now settles the collector's own oldest debt: paid on the good bands, written off on the bad ones. Its failure prose and stakes now say the debtor hid the coin, not that they left town. The Loan casts a must-persist `borrower` who owes the acting lender. The principal moves from lender to borrower, failure adds resentment, and the stakes name the coin. The debt arbitration's failed third leaves the master's note on both paths, and its critical failure leaves none. The toll-gate writ's success at a cost writes the toll-book debt. The cathedral loan's five bands move the chapter's coin between the dean and the factor.

**UI.** On the sheet, a debt reads *Owes {name}* / *Is owed by {name}*, with a "what a debt is" tooltip on the tile. The ascendant bar's vows row uses the same words. Four new chips appear on the branching scenes, each backed by a write in its own band.

**Census (600 ticks, medium).** Seed 42: The Loan resolved 21 times (19 on main) and made 21 debts. Debt Collection settled 10 times, all `no_debt_owed`. Seed 99: The Loan resolved 21 (21 on main) and made 21 debts. Debt Collection settled 18 times: 16 `no_debt_owed`, 2 written off. Across both seeds, 93% of settles found no debt, which is over the plan's 90% line. Per the kill criterion, that is filed as Deferral [THR-2044](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2044) and the draw is not tuned here. The Loan did not lose resolutions, so the second criterion does not fire.

**Not measured.** The cathedral grey zone (whether a spawned priest dean belongs to a temple faction with a purse) was not measured. The coin still rides the dean's Means as the plan decided.
