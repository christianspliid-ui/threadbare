## What was wrong

THR-1348's pull swapped out only spotlight mortals with **no** strategic ambition. Every standard ambition template is strategic, so every worldgen protagonist was protected for life, and the swap pool was a handful of seeded mortals. After the first overflow pulls, every newborn's want was refused `budget`, and each refusal was counted twice (once per want).

## What shipped

- **A second candidate class** in `src/engine/spotlightPull.ts` (`rankedDemotionCandidates`). It is offered only after every ambition-less candidate. It covers a builder (pulled or not) who meets all of these:
  - not travelling;
  - not followed and not threaded;
  - has gone `SPOTLIGHT_UNWATCHED_BUILDER_TICKS` (72) with no witnessed scene and no undertaking progress. The count runs from the latest of their witness, their own pull, their projects' `lastProgressTick`, and tick 0.
- **Ordering:** the longest-unwatched builder goes first.
- **Cap:** at most one unwatched builder steps back per 12-tick window (`spotlightUnwatchedDemotedTick`).
- **The lever:** `SPOTLIGHT_UNWATCHED_BUILDERS_ENABLED` switches the new class on and off.
- **Two corrections, in every mode:**
  - one pull per holder per batch (`spentSpotlightPull`), in births and in the mint/re-evaluation pass;
  - the retained dead hold no overflow slot, do not count toward the allowance, and are never candidates.
- **Wiring:** the four in-play call sites pass `followedAgentIds` and `projects`. If either option is omitted, the new class fails closed.
- **Reporting:**
  - the trace gains `demotedReason` / `demotedUnwatchedTicks`;
  - the ledger gains `unwatchedDemotions`;
  - CLI `spotlight` and `census:undertakings` report both. The census also reports refusals per mortal and the kill-criterion share.
