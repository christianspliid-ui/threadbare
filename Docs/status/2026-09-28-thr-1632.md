# 2026-09-28 — THR-1632: faith and politics as world settings (S1, engine)

**Ticket:** [THR-1632](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1632) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-28-thr-1632-faith-and-politics-settings.md` (S1) · **Pillars:** Engine (settings block, congregations, holy-place floor, fringe culture, guild labels, pilgrim routes); Content (congregation name, sphere line, fringe label constants); UI: S2, [THR-1659](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1659).

## What was wrong

Every world started with one world-wide Temple, holy places sitting off every culture's ground (none at all on seed 42), half the mortals without a culture, and 34–41 town guilds that nothing could tell from a lair faction. None of it was tunable.

## What shipped

- **One settings block.** `src/data/world-scenario.ts` holds `WorldScenario`, `DEFAULT_WORLD_SCENARIO`, `WORLD_SCENARIO_TODAY` and `resolveWorldScenario` (clamps, warns once). `initializeGameState` takes it as a trailing optional argument, feeds the wilderness counts to the province pass, stores it on `GameState.worldScenario`. **The all-"today" block reproduces `origin/main`'s t0 graph byte for byte** (sorted node+edge hash, medium seeds 42 and 99).
- **A Temple congregation per living culture**, seated at its capital, halls only on its heartland (0 off-heartland halls on both seeds), named *The {culture} Congregation of the Spheres*, carrying `veneratedSphere` and a `belongs_to` edge. Dispositions now fan out to and from every instance.
- **Holy-place floor** of 2 per heartland, on its own PRNG stream, never off the culture's ground. Where a small heartland is fully blocked by settlement spacing the trace records `shortBy` (seed 42 culture_2 got 1 of 2; seed 99 culture_1 got 0 of 2).
- **Fringe culture**: a settlement within 4 hexes of a heartland takes the nearest culture as a current, half-strength link, never promoted or Realm-held. **Lowered from the plan's 8 per its kill criterion:** 8 hexes cost +17% steady-state tick time on seed 99; 4 hexes +10.3% (paired same-session runs: 180.6 vs 164.0, 179.9 vs 163.0 ms/tick). With the fringe off it's still ~+10%, so the rest is congregations and holy places. Seed 42 swings ±20% run to run on unchanged code (125 / 126 / 152 ms) and shows no reliable difference.
- **Town guilds** labelled `factionType`/`factionClass: 'guild'` (34/34 and 41/41).
- **Pilgrim routes restored**: one `sacred_route` per congregation to its seat; all 3 capitals pool `encounter.pilgrimage_trial` at t0 on both seeds (0 before). Taken in a 200-tick run: 1× on each seed, not yet at a capital.
- `worldgen_scenario_applied` trace, `window.__DEBUG.getWorldScenario()`, census reader `readers/faith.ts`.

## Latent defects the new worlds exposed (fixed here)

`scripts/realm-census.ts` counted fringe links as domain ground; `owes_favor` schema refused the appointment planter's place creditor; `groupCommand` wrote `member_of` without `rank`. Four world-shape-pinned tests were recalibrated to assert their property rather than the old world's shape (coloration census re-pin, grievance heir, found-core reachability, instant-terminal growth). Impediment #1099.
