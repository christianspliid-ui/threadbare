# 2026-09-27 — THR-1652: thread upkeep is charged, so the income readout is true

**Ticket:** [THR-1652](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1652) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Found by:** THR-1645 · **Pillars:** Engine (new tick phase, trace); Content: N/A (costs already exist as `TIER_MAINTENANCE`); UI: N/A (the income readout already showed the net figure, and it is now correct).

## What was wrong

`processInfluenceMaintenance` had no production caller. The income readout (`computeEssenceIncome`) subtracted each thread's upkeep from the primary sphere, but the tick loop only ever added gross income. So the readout promised a lower net than the pool actually received. Because nothing advanced `ticksAtCurrentTier` either, threads could only climb tiers through the co-located aura.

## Decision

**Option (a): wire it.** This matches the design intent the wiki already stated: *a large, deep court quietly taxes the very Sphere you rely on most*. The ticket marked the choice agent-decidable, with a veto invited.

## What shipped

- **`phaseInfluenceMaintenance`** (`src/engine/phaseInfluenceMaintenance.ts`) is a new inline phase, `influence_maintenance`, placed immediately after `essence` and before `control_effects`.
  - It charges `GameState.essencePool` through `processInfluenceMaintenance`.
  - It is its own phase rather than a call inside `phaseEssence`. `applyEssenceEarned` banks each phase's *net* movement as attunement, so upkeep inside `phaseEssence` would have eaten income before it was counted.
- **Trace:** one aggregate `influence_maintenance` trace, emitted only on ticks where some thread lapses or is restored.
- **Fail-soft:** a missing ascendant, alignment, pool or thread is a no-op.

## Balance (seed 42, medium, two tier-2 threads added via CLI `eval`, primary = chaos)

| | chaos per tick (ticks 1–10) | chaos at tick 100 | thread `ticksAtCurrentTier` at tick 100 |
|---|---|---|---|
| before (`origin/main` 3d83e1fc) | +0.42 | 55 (capped) | 0 (never moves) |
| after | −1.58 (= 0.42 − 2 × 1.0) | 0.67 | 59, `maintenanceCurrent: false` once dry |

What this means for a default seeded run: The First is tier 1, and its 0.5/tick upkeep roughly cancels base primary income (~0.385 without a seat, ~0.735 with one). At 30 paid ticks it promotes to tier 2 (1.0/tick), and from then on the primary pool drains unless the court earns more. A thread the pool cannot cover stalls rather than rising.
