## What a calling is

*Trader. Reaver. Mender.* The word the world uses for what a mortal does — derived, never stored as a stat — from three inputs: the **leading reach pair** (their two highest domain capabilities, the primary counting double), the **active ambition** (its category, plus the undertaking kinds its own strategic templates build — the volatile input, weighted heaviest because deeds move it, review M1), and the **personality lean** (the sign of the value pair a title is cut for). A deterministic argmax over `CALLING_ROWS`, ties broken by table order, no PRNG.

The table is seeded from the ten retired family names and grown past them: twenty-three titles, every family seed with at least one leaned sibling — Merchant → *Trader* / *Smuggler* / *Magnate*, Builder → *Mason* / *Founder*, Scholar → *Seeker* / *Archivist*, Zealot → *Zealot* / *Prophet*, Court → *Spider* / *Chancellor*, Underworld → *Knife* / *Fence*, Warlord → *Reaver* / *Captain* / *Warden*, Caretaker → *Mender* / *Steward*, Artist → *Crafter* / *Maker*, Wanderer → *Wayfarer* / *Pathfinder* — and one no family seeded, *Avenger*, for the vendeance-driven. A row that matches neither the mortal's reaches nor their ambition scores zero; the personality half-credit is a tie-breaker between fits, never a reason to name someone.

## Hysteresis — the grill-me risk row

Wrong values flicker or fossilise. Recompute is **event-driven** at three sites — ambition assignment / completion / abandonment (`ambitionTick.ts`), undertaking completion (`strategicActionLifecycle.ts`), reach tier promotion (`orchestrator.ts`) — never per tick. A challenger replaces the incumbent only when both hold: the incumbent has been held `CALLING_MIN_HOLD_TICKS` (36, three days), and the challenger beats the incumbent's *re-scored* score by `CALLING_SCORE_MARGIN` (0.15). The incumbent is re-scored against today's inputs, so a name the deeds have left behind loses its lead honestly rather than defending a stale score. A change on a spotlight mortal emits `calling_changed` at `CALLING_CHANGE_SIGNIFICANCE` (0.85) — a chronicle moment, per the ruling — and every derivation traces `calling_change` with both scores.

Every mortal is named at world init (`cause: 'initial'`, no event), so no living agent ever renders the fallback; a newborn is named by their first ambition.

## The one non-additive move

The four family render sites — `ThreadsPanel` rows, `AgentInfoCard`, `ThreadDetailView`, `DebugTabContent` — swapped in one edit through `AgentStrategicSummary.calling`, which `getAgentStrategicSummary` fills from `getCallingPresentation(node, behaviorFamily)`: the stored calling when one exists, else the persisted family's seed title (`BEHAVIOR_FAMILY_TO_CALLING`, total over the enum — a test pins it), else the fallback. Old history renders through the legacy map. The `BehaviorFamily` enum and its template fields are untouched; their mechanical retirement is docs 4/6 conversion ground. The `OverviewTab` identity block gains *Called Trader* under the archetype epithet.

## The telemetry gate

`npm run telemetry:calling` — seeds 42 and 99 — is the narratable-band instrument (review M1): no spotlight mortal changes calling more than three times per hundred ticks, and at least one change somewhere in a population with completed undertakings. Outside the band the plan's kill criterion applies (`CALLING_CHANGE_SIGNIFICANCE = 0`, static title, recorded here).

**Measured, 150 ticks per seed.** Seed 42: fourteen spotlight, seventy-two named, two changes (both on an ambition change), at most two-thirds of a change per hundred ticks on any one mortal, sixteen titles in use. Seed 99: fourteen spotlight, fifty-six named, five changes (four on an ambition change, one on a finished work), at most one and a third per hundred ticks, sixteen titles. **Inside the band on both seeds** — no flicker, no fossil — so the chronicle-moment claim ships as designed and the kill criterion is not applied. The plan asked for 300 ticks; the script defaults to 150 because that is the run — a medium world leaves `playing` around tick 170, and a 300-tick request measured over twenty minutes of wall-clock against under a minute, with nothing the gate reads in the difference. `--ticks 300` remains available to anyone who wants to insist.

## What is deliberately not done

Calling changes do not yet appear on the arc strip (`getAgentArc`) — they need a persisted record rather than a trace, and slice 6 decides whether the history ledger or a node-side list carries it. `wantedKinds` reads the ambition's *template* kinds; ambitions themselves do not yet name the kinds they want built (docs 4/6 ground).
