# THR-1509 — the `?outcome=` verdict judges the path on screen, and every path authors the losses it can reach

**Date:** 2026-09-17 · **Pillars:** Engine · Content · UI-N/A (no component; the lever's surface is the console line and `window.__DEBUG.getOutcomePinVerdict()`)
**Issue:** [THR-1509](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1509/authored-band-coverage-is-measured-per-template-but-reached-per)
**Plan doc:** none — the ticket description is the spec.

Pinning `?outcome=critical_failure` on `encounter.slice.swindler_found` and letting the fork take the law arm printed:

```
[?outcome] Showing the authored "critical_failure" ending. Bands authored on this encounter: critical_success, failure, success_at_cost, critical_failure.
```

while the ending on screen was *"The town's justice has the man, the paper, and a list of everyone else he sold it to."* The diagnostic was not silent; it reported the opposite of the surface.

## What was wrong, in one sentence

`authoredOutcomeBands()` unioned the `byOutcome` keys across every variant plus the fallback, but `resolveAftermathVariant` layers a band from **one** variant — the arm the choice picked — so a band authored on the alley arm counted as "authored" for a player standing on the law arm.

## The decision the ticket asked for

The ticket offered three rules. **Option 3 (make the verdict variant-aware) was mandatory regardless** and is done. For the authoring half, the choice was between "a variant owes what its steps can reach" (rule 1) and "the union becomes a contract" (rule 2). Neither survives contact with the slice as written:

- Rule 2 demands dead content: every arm authoring every band the template authors anywhere, including winning bands whose base ending already reads as a win.
- Rule 1 taken literally is rule 2 in disguise, because every rolled step can reach `critical_success`, `success_at_cost` and `critical_failure`.

**The rule adopted, and stated where the next author meets it (`vertical-slice.ts` header rule 3, the wiring guide § Capability 18, `Docs/canon/encounters.md`):** every path a player can land on owes the **losing** bands it can reach — `critical_failure` always, because `advanceStep` ends the action there on a critical step whatever the `failBehavior`; `failure` when any step on that path is `fail_action`. Winning bands are optional per path. The reason is register, not completeness: every base ending in the slice is written as a win, so on a loss it lies; on a win it does not.

**Which paths.** Every `variants` key, plus `fallback` only on a choice-less config. A fork's `fallback` is unreachable in play — `applyAgentDecidedBranches` decides the arm before `advanceStep` lands the deciding step's outcome, so even a step-0 critical failure resolves onto a decided arm — and gating it would demand content nobody can see.

Measured before the fix, per path, only one template fell short of that rule: `swindler_found` (law arm missing `critical_failure`, alley arm missing `failure`). Both are authored now, each with its own reactions on purpose, because each base reaction pays a repute the band's event did not earn (the law arm's gain for using the town's law when the law arrived to nothing; the alley arm's ill-repute for a knife in the story when no fight happened). The two arms still author *different winning* bands, and the config now says why: the law path's win belongs to the town, the alley path's win costs the body.

## Engine

- `recordOutcomePinVerdict(template, outcome, choiceHistory)` takes the resolved action's choice history — the same value aftermath assembly hands `resolveAftermathVariant` — and judges against `authoredOutcomeBandsOnPath`, which recovers the base variant through `resolveAftermathVariant` itself (passed no outcome, it returns the chosen variant untouched). One lookup, not two: a second implementation of "which arm did the choice pick" is the shape that drifts.
- The verdict grew `variantKey` (the arm, or `'fallback'`), `templateBands` (the old union), and `authoredBands` now means *on this path*. The `unauthored_band` message names the arm and what exists elsewhere on the encounter — the sharp case reads *"the "positive" path it resolved on authors no band for it … elsewhere on this encounter: success_at_cost, critical_failure"*.
- `authoredOutcomeBands` (the union) survives for the corpus gates that ask "anywhere"; `authoredOutcomeBandsOnVariant` and `reachableLosingBandsOnPath` are the gate-side twins, exported so the slice gate and the console line share one predicate — the same reason THR-1468 shared the union function.
- Fail-soft: a config whose variant lookup throws (a recorded choice but no `variants` map — the corpus shape THR-1030's own test found) is judged as the fallback path rather than throwing from a read-only diagnostic.

## Falsified before trusted

- The two-variant disjoint fixture pinned on the arm that lacks the band asserts `templateBands` **does** contain it (so a regression to the union is caught by the status assertion, not by a fixture that happened not to author it) and then asserts `unauthored_band`. The control arm, same pin, other choice, asserts `band_rendered`.
- The slice gate's falsification arm removes `failure` from the swindler's alley arm only: the union still says `failure` is authored — that is the laundering — and the per-path gate reports `encounter.slice.swindler_found::negative owes failure`.
- The corpus test pins both losing bands on both swindler arms against the real template and asserts `band_rendered` four times.
