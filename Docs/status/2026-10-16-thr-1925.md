# 2026-10-16 — THR-1925: one death no longer keeps a town in unrest for good

**Ticket:** [THR-1925](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1925). No plan doc (the ticket carries the fix). Engine pillar; Content and UI N/A (no new words or surfaces; the hunger-and-unrest build THR-1934 shows the input).

Death-site haunting used to add the doom identity's `deathSiteUnrestBonus` to unrest every tick at any place with `deathCount > 0`, and `deathCount` never falls. Against a decay of 1 a tick, Reckoning (+3) and Breach (+2) pinned the town at 99 for good after one death.

- **Engine.** Haunting is now a target, not an add. Once a day (`DEATH_SITE_HAUNTING_CADENCE_TICKS` 12) a place is lifted toward `bonus × DEATH_SITE_UNREST_TARGET_SCALE` (4), capped at `DEATH_SITE_UNREST_TARGET_MAX` (12). The target fades to 0 over `DEATH_SITE_HAUNTING_WINDOW_TICKS` (120, ten days) after the last death. It never lowers unrest that other causes raised. `markMortalDead` now stamps `lastDeathTick` and counts the death on the outer-tier Location (a death in a tavern counts for its town, the tier the fight and battle stamps already use); the last death is the latest of that stamp and the fight and battle stamps. A place whose dead predate the stamp is not haunted. The current target is written as `deathSiteUnrestTarget`, which THR-1934's S1 reads as its `deathSite` input. Constants: `src/data/death-site-unrest-constants.ts`; pure read: `src/engine/deathSiteHaunting.ts`. The spirit-pressure half of step 9.5 is unchanged.

**Measured (seed 7 medium, 600 ticks, headless, Reckoning):** main had 397 settlement-ticks at 70+ unrest, all from haunting alone (Thornhaven pinned at 99). This branch has 0; the highest haunting target is 12 and no settlement reaches 70 (five towns carry deaths once tavern deaths count for their town). Unit tests: `src/engine/__tests__/deathSiteHaunting.test.ts` (a place with `deathCount` 5 under Reckoning stays at or below 12 and decays to 0 after the window).
