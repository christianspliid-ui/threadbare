# THR-1096 — Companion attachments

**Shipped 2026-08-14.** A companion is a named person who walks with a mortal and makes them
better at something — a card at the mortal's side, never a simulated agent. The reference is
Eldritch Horror's Ally assets, and the four properties that made them worth copying all survive:
they are individuals who *join you*, their bonuses are small but always on, they come in two tiers
(generic professions and named story characters), and they are a currency of loss.

## What landed

**Engine.** New `companion` NodeType and `accompanies` EdgeType, both purely additive on unions with
125 and 278 importers. `src/engine/companions.ts` owns the lifecycle — `mintCompanion`,
`removeCompanion`, `getCompanions`, `expireCompanions` — and with it three invariants: one bearer per
companion (a second edge is refused at mint), no silent departures (every removal emits a trace with
a named reason and returns the authored departure line), and names drawn from the world's own
generator threaded with the caller's PRNG, so the same seed meets the same person.

The capability walk was the load-bearing integration: `accompanies` joins `possesses`/`bonded_to` at
**both** sites in `domainCapability.ts` — the raw score and `getTopContributors` — so a companion's
bonus reaches the roll through the identical path a sword's does, and earns a factor line under
their own name rather than an anonymous delta. Companions ride the existing condition-expiry beat
rather than adding a phase; a permanent companion carries no counter and costs nothing per tick.

**The mercenary migration is behaviour-*correcting*, and that is the point.**
`action.gold.hire-mercenaries` had been minting an `add_node` of type `attachment` — a node type
outside `NodeType` entirely — carrying `ironCapability: 30` that **no reader ever consumed**. The
mercenaries it hired granted nothing, for the life of the template. It now mints the
`companion.sellsword-band` template through a new `grant_companion` GraphOp, and the Iron bonus
starts existing. Proven in `companionsIntegration.test.ts` against a real generated world.

**Content.** `src/data/companion-templates.ts` — 8 profession templates spanning road, settlement,
wilds and court, plus one unique (`Aunel of the Nine Charts`) as the reference implementation of the
once-per-world tier. Each carries a join/depart sentence pair written to the cause→change rule with
a `{name}` slot substituted at mint. A library test asserts every contribution against
`COMPANION_CONTRIBUTION_RANGE` (raw 1–3) — the guardrail that keeps a companion a nudge on the roll
rather than a decision, deliberately below the agent-scale band.

**UI.** A Companions row on the agent detail surface, leading the attachments tab because people
lead. Initials tile via `EntityVisual` (the resolver gained a `companion` kind, and it joins
`PERSON_KINDS` — a companion is a person before they are a bonus), name, profession, the *good for*
line, signed reach deltas, and a duration readout for contracted companions only. **Deliberately
ungated**: unlike the attachment fields beside it, companions do not sit behind intimate-knowledge —
a mortal's companions belong to the bearer, and the bearer's god sees the people around their own
threads.

**The word is settled and the code obeys it.** Player surfaces say *Companions*, never *retinue*,
which names the divine court and nothing else (THR-1099 arbitration). That is asserted by a test,
not just a convention. The same rule decided the veil chip's click behaviour: a companion is a
person but not an agent node and not a thread, so routing one through the agent drawer or the
`ThreadCategory` stub-modal path would open the wrong sheet — the tile renders and does not
navigate. Non-interactive beats wrong.

## Verification

1009 test files / 16,139 tests green, including 34 new unit tests, 5 integration tests against the
real `initializeGameState` → `runTick` pipeline, and 10 render assertions on the real
`AttachmentsTab`. Typecheck ratchet unchanged. 30-tick CLI smoke clean (383 agents, 45 events).

A gate earned its keep: `trace-vocabulary.test.ts` caught both new trace categories missing from
`TRACE_CATEGORIES`, which would have made them faithfully emitted and invisible in the inspector.

**The pixel capture is owed, not claimed.** `preview_start` is refused in unattended runs
(impediment #574/#546), so the contractual 1920×1080 screenshot has no reachable route from this
lane. Substituted with jsdom render assertions per THR-754 and filed for an attended session —
this change *adds* a rendered section, so unlike a pure attribute change it does not clear
impediment #546's own scope test for honest substitution.

## What this does not do

No shipped encounter grants a companion yet — THR-1097's content sweep is the consumer. The
`lured_away` departure reason exists and is unauthored by design: it arrives with the content that
writes it. Companion art is one `portrait` field away when a template ships one; every companion
today renders the designed initials tile.
