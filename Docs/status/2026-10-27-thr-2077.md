# THR-2077 — A sealed mill lease gives the town's mill to a guild

**Shipped 2026-10-27.** M3: Dynamic Economy. *The Lease Sealed* won the abbey's mill "for the fellowship", but the fellowship was not a node, so nobody held the mill afterwards and no purse earned from it. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-27-thr-2077-mill-lease-guild.md`.

**What changed.** A new aftermath effect, `guild_hold_place`, gives the town building to the town's own guild. `pickTownGuild` (`src/engine/factionPurse/townGuild.ts`) picks the first of artisans, builders, merchants, traders, miners and bankers with a purse faction present in the town, then the one with the most members standing there, then the lowest id. It never picks a realm. The guild gets a `controls` edge with `role: 'guild_holding'`. An unheld mill is taken up. With none standing, one is raised as a town building if a slot is free. A mill another faction holds stays theirs. No guild present, or no free slot, writes nothing. The purse pays a held mill `HELD_BUILDING_DUES.mill` (0.2 a day at the prosperity pivot, zero in a ruin), keyed on the Place type. The Purse row reads "the mill at Ashford". `markMortalBuilding` now drops the economy's `builtBy` stamp, so a Place that `raiseBuilding` minted reads as adopted and survives the building's loss, as its doc already said.

**30-day run (seeds 42 / 99 / 7, medium, 360 ticks).** 8 / 9 / 7 sealed-lease resolutions. `encounter.place.guild_held` pooled: 2 raised, 3 already theirs, 9 no room, 2 no fellowship. The kill line is no fellowship above 60%; this run is at 12.5%. A sealing in a town razed to ruins traced `aftermath_target_invalid` (`not_a_settlement`). No town had two mills. Realm border hexes were identical with and without the guild edges.

**Evidence.** `guildHoldPlace.test.ts`: 18 tests covering every branch, the picker's ordering, the dues (stamped and unstamped, ruin), the phrase and the content tables. `npm run gate` verdict block is in the PR body.

**Observed, not acted on.** `no_room` is the most common result (9 of 16). Most towns that seal a lease already fill their building slots. The plan sets no kill criterion on it.
