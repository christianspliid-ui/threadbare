# THR-1203 — POLE_MANIFEST pins every body, and says where it still cannot see

**Shipped 2026-08-23.** Closes the coverage bound THR-1202 measured on `FEAR_PROSE`'s only aboutness check. Test-only change; no prose body moved.

## The bound being closed

`POLE_MANIFEST` is the one genuine *aboutness* pin on `FEAR_PROSE` — every other check on that table reads grammar, register, placeholder presence or density, none of which can see which pole a body belongs to. It held one distinctive fragment per key, asserted the fragment sits on its own key, and asserted it does *not* sit on the sibling pole. That catches a pole swap, which is the THR-1187 defect it was built for.

Each key holds six bodies. It pinned one. THR-1202 measured the other five in two arms: an off-axis body in an **unpinned** slot left the suite 272/272 green, while the same body in the **pinned** slot went red on exactly that key. THR-1202's own defect sat in bodies 1–4 and was caught; in bodies 2–3 it would not have been.

## What shipped

The manifest is now one entry per body — 18 keys × 6 bodies = **108 fragments**, each read off the body it pins and assigned against the pole authority (`FEAR_DESCRIPTIONS` in `strand-content.ts`: each pole fears the undoing of what that pole is committed to). The ticket's option 1, and the reading is the product — a generated fragment set would launder unaudited content as audited, which the ticket explicitly forbade.

Three assertions replace the old two:

- **Per-body coverage** — every key carries exactly as many entries as it has bodies, so a seventh body cannot be added without someone reading it.
- **Bijection** — each fragment matches exactly one body, and a key's six fragments match six *different* bodies. Without this, six fragments could all be read off the same body and the manifest would report per-body coverage it does not have. Asserted as a bijection rather than an index match so it survives reordering.
- **Presence and sibling-absence**, unchanged in kind, now over all 108.

## The honest part: `witness`

The reading found that not every body *can* witness a pole swap. Eleven of the 108 are pole-agnostic — they read equally well under either noun, so no fragment drawn from them discriminates. The two trailing bodies of each `honesty_cunning` key are the known case (authored pole-agnostic, and the reason they survived THR-1187 in place); the read found nine more.

Every entry therefore carries a `witness` field, on the line, because a partial manifest that does not say so reads as full coverage — the exact trap the ticket was written to avoid:

- `'pole'` — the fragment names something only this pole's fear could produce. A red is a real aboutness failure. **97 bodies.**
- `'edit'` — the body is pole-agnostic. The entry still pins the text against a silent rewrite and the sibling-absence check still passes non-vacuously, but a *green* says nothing about which pole the body belongs on. **11 bodies.**

The count of 11 is itself pinned by a test whose failure message lists the offenders, so the hole can only grow by a deliberate edit, never by a new pole-agnostic body slipping in unremarked. The message says in terms not to flip a witness to `'pole'` to make it pass.

## Falsified

THR-1202's Arm A rerun against the widened manifest: body 2 of `preservation_transformation_positive` — a previously **unpinned** slot — swapped back to its pre-fix control/authority text. Under the old manifest this was 272/272 green. Now it reddens two tests naming exactly that key and that fragment:

```
preservation_transformation_positive: fragment "every year of care spends some of what is left" matches 0 bodies
preservation_transformation_positive should contain its reviewed fragment "every year of care spends some of what is left"
```

Reverted; 80/80 green. The bound is closed for all five formerly-invisible slots by construction — each now carries its own assertion.

## Found by the reading

`mercy_ruthlessness_negative` bodies 1 and 3 carry the right fear for their pole (*becoming heartless*) but name the agent's disposition **"the patience"** in the same sentence that renders `{value}` = "ruthlessness" — *"the ruthlessness they display … the moment when the patience finally exhausts itself"*. On-axis but noun-mismatched, so neither THR-1187 nor THR-1202 would have caught it, and the widened manifest does not catch it either: a fragment witnesses which pole a body sits on, not whether the body's own nouns agree with what `{value}` resolves to. Filed as **THR-1204** with its coordination block, not folded in — this ticket widens the manifest rather than rewriting bodies.
