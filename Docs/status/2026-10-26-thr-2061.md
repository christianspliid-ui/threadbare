# THR-2061 — Readouts say what they show

**Shipped 2026-10-26.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · Cold playtest round 5; slice S2 of `Docs/plans/2026-10-26-thr-2058-one-thing-at-a-time.md`.

**What changed.** The essence arrow now reads the refill band (`essenceDirection`: drains → ↓, any other band below the cap → ↑, full → —), so it agrees with the row's word. The hover's trend word reads the same direction. The two-window movement history now only supplies the hover's "fed by / drawn by". The ▼ spend mark reads `playerSpendSince` over the row's movement record, so it lights only on `spend_nudge` / `spend_cast` growth. Its timer lives in a ref, so a refill render can no longer cancel it and leave the ▼ stuck. The card legend is built per hand (`nudgeGlyphLegendForHand`): only the hand's own pip shapes, weakest first, with the weakest tier's hollow shape attached (`●○`), labelled "odds: more filled is stronger". A hand with no odds shows price and setback only. Each aftermath legend word carries a meaning (`CONSEQUENCE_LEGEND_MEANINGS`: "SCAR · what it cost"…), and the size line reads lower case: `▲ slight · ▲▲ clear · ▲▲▲ great`. Needs-you `count` is Waiting + Held (`NEEDS_YOU_COUNT_INCLUDES_NEWS = false`), and a new `total` keeps the pill visible. With only News the pill shows "Needs you" with no number (`Chip` gains `showWhenZero`). `getEssenceRefill()` adds a per-sphere `direction`.

**Review gate fixes (round 1, two confirmed).** (1) The essence phase clamps a pool to its cap, and upkeep then takes its share in the same tick, so a full primary pool sits at cap − upkeep between ticks. `essenceDirection` now reads full at `cap − drawnPerTick` (the ledger's upkeep plus sustained-control costs, a new field on `EssenceRefillSphere`). (2) `PLAYER_SPEND_CAUSES` also covers `premonition` (the phase never moves the pool, so every premonition movement is a paid whisper or compulsion) and a new `spend_other` cause, which Read the Threads and the Scry court title now file under instead of `other`.

**Executor decisions.** The category word keeps its chip-tag form (`SCAR`) so the legend and the tag read the same; the meaning follows it in lower case. A window roll counts the new window's whole spend, plus the growth of the closed window when it was kept as `previous`.

**Evidence.** `npm run gate` PASS (test 25,553, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, cli-smoke). New `src/components/Game/__tests__/readoutsSayWhatTheyShow.thr2061.test.tsx` covers the direction per band and at the cap, spend reads per cause and across a roll, the per-hand legend, the meanings, and the news-only pill. `readableSpend.test.tsx` adds "a refill render after a spend does not cancel the flash timer" (fake timers) and "no flash for a sustained drain". Live at 1920×1080 (`?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, 40 ticks): every row showed ↑ and `getEssenceRefill().spheres[*].direction` was `rising` for all twelve spheres, with nothing off-screen.
