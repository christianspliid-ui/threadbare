# THR-2259 — Popups wait for the player's moment

**Shipped 2026-11-20.** Cold playtest round 8 (2/3 testers): "Take the Seat" opened over a chapter's answer and hid its Aftermath, and an armed Piercing Gaze card was replaced by a vision before it could be cast.

- **(a) The answering card holds the gift.** The live answering card (`encounter-answering`) stops no time, so it was missing from the registry's `open` list. On Play the list emptied, which read as a resume boundary, and the Seat entered over the answer. `giftHoldingInterrupts` (`src/components/Game/giftDelivery.ts`) now counts the answering card while it renders, so the step from the chapter's veil to the answering card is no boundary; the gift enters only when the chapter's Aftermath closes.
- **(b) An armed card holds arrivals.** The armed-card flag (`cardArmedHold`, until now a clock hold only) now feeds the arrival hold (`InterruptSnapshot.cardArmed` → hold reason `card_armed`), the world-opened chapter scan (`worldOpenedEncounterHeldBy`) and the gift hold (`GiftDeliveryInput.cardArmed`). A vision, premonition, story beat, moment, popup, world-opened chapter or gift waits until the card is cast or set down. An arrival already on screen keeps its slot; Beat 0 stays exempt.
- **Evidence.** Unit: `src/components/Game/__tests__/arrivalHoldsThr2259.test.ts` (15 tests) replays chapter → answering → Aftermath → closed through the real registry. Live (bash-vite, 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium&forceencounters`): with Agent Thread armed and 60 ticks run, `getInterruptState()` held `JourneyVignetteModal` and the Star Pilgrim chapter by `card_armed`, and `getGiftDelivery()` held the Seat by `card_armed`; after `disarmCard()` the chapter opened and the vision waited behind it. The live answering-card case was not staged (it needs a committed step on a running clock); the unit replay covers it.
