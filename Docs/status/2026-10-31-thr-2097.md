# THR-2097 — the art-led person page (slice 1)

**Shipped 2026-10-31.** UI Visual Overhaul — Design System v1. Christian approved the character-page mock in chat and asked for the same treatment on every UI layer. This slice builds the shared pieces and dresses the person page with them (plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-31-art-led-ui-pass.md` § Slice 1).

**What changed.** At wide and Full, a person page now leads with a 3:4 portrait (`--inspector-portrait-w`: 15rem at 1920, 18rem at 2560 and 3440). The name sits under the portrait. The First wears a "Your First" badge with the god's face on the portrait's bottom edge. Where-line place chips carry their place icon. Every tab carries a carved icon, and so does every concept heading. Overview draws the page kind's own icon, and Prowess gets a provisional `prowess` idea (`sword-brandish`; THR-2104 holds the final pick). "What they carry" sits in the aside under the Act row as a two-column gallery of 16:9 relic paintings that open the relic. The Overview opens on the bond strip (the god's face, the bond mark, the person's face), drawn only while a thread is held. "What they want most" is the page's one gold highlight: the ambition's icon, its name as the headline, and its flavour line under it. Who they are reads as one sentence, each fact a linked or tooltipped term. Dominion is one line. Nature is chips, with the first reading "{word} above all". Core and Personality are two-pole bars that keep the leaning word and print no number. An empty Act row is a quiet dashed line. New primitives: `BondStrip`, `QuietSlot`, the identity chip's `icon` slot. Each has a styleguide entry and component-selection and primitives rows.

Executor decisions: the trade clause reads "who works as a {trade}", because no verb phrase exists for a trade word. The highlight's sub-line is the ambition's flavour line, so the name is not said twice. Chips with no sphere now draw a neutral edge, not the force red. Rows with no fitting icon draw none: `fate`, `story_so_far`, `revealed_notices`, `under_your_hand`, `intention`, `current_work`, `allegiance`, `company`, `reputation`, `words`, `origin`, the Prowess and Bonds rows, `knows`, `inactive` and `arc_so_far`. They are listed for slice 2 (THR-2098).

**Evidence.** `artLedDressing.thr2097.test.ts` has 9 tests: the who-they-are composer, the bond strip for a threaded person and none for an unthreaded one, the Your First VM, where icons and the registry icons. `npm run gate` passes test, typecheck, build, ui-debt and the CLI smoke. Browser (bash-vite on :5363 + Playwright, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, Kael opened wide): `getInspectorState().dressing` is `{ asideSections: ['slots'], bondStrip: true, firstBadge: true, galleryTiles: 4, iconFallbacks: [] }` at both 1920×1080 and 2560×1440. The body has no digits, the page does not scroll, the name and Act row sit above the fold, and the console shows 0 errors.
