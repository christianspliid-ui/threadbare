# THR-998 — the focused card stops claiming odds it cannot deliver

**Shipped 2026-08-12.** Project: Action System & Unlocks. Pillars: Engine + UI (Content N/A — no template re-pricing).

## What was wrong

`riskHintLine(focusedSlot.maxStepDifficulty)` bucketed the template's **authored** step
difficulty into *steady / uncertain / perilous*. That is not the number the roll subtracts.
`applyScaleDifficultyAdjust` caps difficulty from above so the per-scale probability floor
holds — `capability − MIN_PROBABILITY_BY_SCALE[scale]` — and for a fresh god that cap is
**0** at `personal` and `local`, which is **85% of the actor-target slot list**. So two cards
priced 0.20 and 0.50 read *"A steady working."* and *"A perilous working."* while resolving
to the identical probability. The line was true about the template and false about the
player's odds — the exact failure a risk hint exists to prevent.

## The fix — direction 2, and why not direction 1

Christian delegated the direction pick to the executor (chat, 2026-08-12; canon
`Docs/canon/process.md` § User review interface, rule 4). The sacred outcome he set: *the
focused card never claims odds that are not real.*

**Direction 1 (bucket the same three words on the resolved probability) was implemented far
enough to measure, then rejected.** A fresh god reads P = 0.70 at `personal` and 0.65 at
`local`, so 85% of the slot list collapses onto a single word. Truthful, but degenerate —
and one word swallowing the card face is the very failure the risk line was introduced to
avoid (`playerCastBalance.test.ts` bounds any one word at 60%).

**Shipped direction 2.** The card's line is now a function of `effectiveCastDifficulty` —
the difficulty that survives the scale offset and the floor and actually reaches
`P = capability + sphereFactor − difficulty + mods`:

| effective difficulty | card face |
|---|---|
| authored 0 (guaranteed casting) | no line — unchanged, certainty on the soul-verbs is a design statement |
| 0, but the template was priced | the **scale** line — *"A working the size of one place."* |
| > 0 | the risk word, now truthful |

**Two casts with equal odds have equal effective difficulty and therefore read the same
line — by construction, not by measurement.** That is the whole point: the old shape could
only be kept honest by re-measuring the corpus; this one cannot go wrong.

It is also self-maintaining. The cap is `capability − floor`, so as `reachPractice` (THR-613)
walks a god's capability across a run, more of the authored price survives, more cards
differentiate, and the harder words become reachable — the card starts reporting risk exactly
when risk becomes real. No threshold re-tune and **no template re-pricing** (that would burn
authored intent for no mechanical gain — the THR-736 anti-pattern).

Direction 3 (lowering `MIN_PROBABILITY_BY_SCALE`) stays ruled out: those floors govern mortal
resolution too, so it is not a player-cast-local change.

## What changed

- **New** `src/engine/playerCastReadout.ts` — `effectiveCastDifficulty()` (runs the resolver's
  own `applyScaleDifficultyAdjust`, so card and roll cannot drift) and `castCapabilityByReach()`
  (mirrors the resolver's player branch, reading the graph so a deepened god is not understated).
- `src/data/player-cast-constants.ts` — `SCALE_HINT_LINES` + `castHintLine()`; `riskHintLine`
  now documented as taking the effective difficulty.
- `src/engine/targetActions.ts` — slots carry `effectiveStepDifficulty` and `scale`; the
  hardest-step helper now also reports the reach it is rolled in (capability is per-reach).
- `src/engine/wheel.ts`, both drawer hooks, `src/components/Game/ActionDrawer.tsx` — wiring.
- `RISK_HINT_THRESHOLDS` and every template price are **unchanged**.

## A note on the pinned "difficulty is inert" assertion

`playerCastBalance.test.ts` said that test "must go red when THR-998 lands". It stays **green**,
deliberately — that expectation assumed direction 3. The engine property is real and unchanged;
what THR-998 removed is the *card* reporting it to the player as a per-template risk. Both the
file header and the inline comment are corrected to say so rather than left to rot.
