# THR-1407 — the world-object catalogue and the systems inventory now name the same subsystems

**2026-09-07 · Engine · Thematic Pressure & Living World**

`src/data/world-objects.ts` gives every kind of thing in the world an `owningSystem`, and `scripts/subsystems-registry.ts` is the authority on what subsystems are called. Nothing joined them, so they drifted: of 34 kind rows, **32 named a subsystem that does not exist** — 16 of those by capitalisation alone, `Set.has` being case-sensitive. The pin is the deliverable; the string edits are its consequence.

## What shipped

- **Two contract tests** in `src/data/__tests__/worldObjects.test.ts`, both asserting **names, never counts** (THR-688 rule A). Forward: every `owningSystem` is a member of `SUBSYSTEM_NAMES`. Reverse: every writer's module domain is claimed by some row's `domains`.
- **32 `owningSystem` values corrected** — 16 recases, 11 renames (`Cosmology` → `Spheres & Quintessence`, `Trade routes` → `Mortal Economy & Prosperity`, `Agents & decision` → `Agent Lifecycle`, `Cultures` → `Culture`, `Reputation & standing` → `Reputation & Influence`), and 5 rows onto a new subsystem. `'Traits & attachments'` split per kind, which it always was: `condition` → Effects & Conditions, `trait` → Personality & Emergent Traits.
- **One new registry row**, `World Generation, Terrain & Places`, for the five rows (`area`, `hex`, `location`, `place`, `sublocation_node`) whose home did not exist. Badges 🟢 ACTIVE.
- **Domain claims added to eight existing rows** so the reverse pin closes: 18 unclaimed writer domains → 3.
- **`companion` dropped as an alias** of Companies & Group Travel. `companions.ts` mints the Companion kind under Attachments — its module header is explicit that a companion is *not* an actor — so the alias pointed designers at the wrong subsystem.

## The measurement that changed an answer

The spec proposed `activityKeywords: ['terrain', 'worldgen', 'genome']` for the new row. **All three are absent from the activity vocabulary** — measured against the standard 120-tick seed-42 medium run, the same run the badge is computed against. The subsystem does most of its work in `initializeGameState`, not in a tick phase, so none of its obvious nouns ever appears as a trace category or event type. Shipping the guess would have badged a demonstrably live subsystem **DORMANT** — the false-DORMANT error `subsystems-registry.ts:136-140` already records once, and the dangerous direction, because it hides live substrate from designers who then green-field a duplicate. `hex`, `settlement` and `location` are present; those ship.

The same measurement caught `phaseMatch`. Bare `settlement` claimed *Settlement Prosperity*, contradicting the row's own note in the same commit — what a settlement produces is Mortal Economy's. Narrowed to `settlement tier`, which claims promotion/demotion and genome reassessment and leaves prosperity alone.

## Where the reverse pin diverged from its spec, and why it ships differently

The spec named **five unhomed op modules**. That count came from clustering *all* of `src/engine/`, where 130 of 179 domains are unclaimed — a gap far too large for this ticket and not what the predicate asks. Scoped to the writers the kind rows actually name, the real set is **18 domains**, and two of the spec's five (`traits.ts`, `domainCapability.ts`) are not writers of any kind at all.

Fifteen have an unambiguous home and are claimed. Three do not, and are listed in a new exported `CROSS_CUTTING_DOMAINS` rather than forced onto a row: `domainOf()` buckets by the leading token of a basename, so `phaseFactionActions.ts` and `phaseDoom.ts` are both `phase`, `gameInit.ts` boots every subsystem at once, and `unifiedActionResolution.ts` is the pipeline every verb runs through. Claiming one of those for a single subsystem would attribute all its siblings to that subsystem in the generated inventory — a misattribution worse than the gap it closes. Listing them explicitly keeps the pin failing on a genuinely unhomed writer, which is the guard's whole point.

## The two naming decisions, recorded

- **`'Ascendant & divine economy'` (×2) → `Ascendant Beats & Progression`.** The field's own JSDoc says `owningSystem` names who owns the kind's *writers*, and `gameInit` mints the ascendant. The `god` row has **no writers anywhere** — verified, no `actorType: 'god'` or `'place_spirit'` assignment exists in `src/` — so the writer criterion is vacuous there and it stays with its shape-sibling. The spec's counter-evidence resolves the same way: `divine_receipt` already resolving to `Essence & Divine Economy` verbatim shows the mint/spend split is deliberate, and these two are the mint side.
- **Hex folds into the new worldgen row** rather than taking its own. The default, and nothing argued against it: both would badge identically off the same keywords, so a second row would buy a longer table and no information.

The reverse half cannot be expressed against `origin/main` at all — the exports it imports do not exist there — so its red is the measured 18-unclaimed figure above, which the same `domainOf` produces. Both green after: 32 → 0 and 18 → 3.

No browser evidence owed: engine/content, accepted via CLI and headless sweeps (THR-688 rule C).
