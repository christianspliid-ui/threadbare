# 2026-08-29 — THR-1349: the variety term landed; the cutover did not, and the reason is not variety

**Current focus:** the unified decision board's §4 cutover. THR-1349 was filed as the
blocker in front of the flip, with a stated root cause: board score carries no variety
term, so a live board concentrates on the highest-EVT templates and
`strategic_establish_trade_route` stops happening.

The variety term was owed regardless and is now shipped. **It does not restore trade
routes**, and the flip therefore still does not land. This document records what was
measured, because the ticket's remaining Done-when items are unreachable as written.

## What shipped

**1. The variety term the plan promised and never delivered.** Plan §4 asserts the legacy
scorer's live components "survive as candidate-generation features feeding EVT inputs, not
as a parallel currency". For `varietyPenalty` that is false: it lands in
`ScoredStrategicCandidate.finalScore`, which the board never reads — the board recomputes
EVT from the candidate's own inputs. So at cutover the variety mechanism was not relocated,
it was dropped.

`computeBoardVarietyMultiplier` consumes the *existing* per-candidate `varietyPenalty`
(board duplicates + the actor's recent starts) as a proportional discount:

```
boardScore = EVT × desire × temperament × varietyMultiplier
varietyMultiplier = 1 − BOARD_VARIETY_PENALTY_WEIGHT × clamp01(varietyPenalty)
```

**It enters multiplicatively, and that is not a style choice.** `varietyPenalty` is a
`[0, 1]` quantity; board score is EVT-scaled with a median strategic entry of `1.9e-3`.
Subtracting `0.18 × penalty` would not discount a repeat, it would annihilate every
undertaking on the board — the same units error `BOARD_SCORE_FLOOR` shipped and this file
has now documented twice.

**2. A composition gate on `scripts/undertaking-census.ts`.** The three §4 criteria read the
share *between* families and are all satisfiable by an undertaking family that has collapsed
onto one template. The census now reports starts, distinct templates started, the top
templates by start count, and `trades_with` edges — and gates on
`CENSUS_DISTINCT_TEMPLATE_FLOOR`.

`strategic_action_started` gained a `templateId` field so the gate reads a field rather than
parsing it back out of `candidateId` (`strat_${templateId}_${targetId}_${tick}`, where every
template id itself contains underscores).

## Why the flip still does not land

The ticket's fourth Done-when is "`trades_with` edges written over 150 ticks on seed 42 is
non-zero under a live board". Measured, medium, 150 ticks:

| arm | seed 42 distinct / routes | seed 99 distinct / routes |
| -- | -- | -- |
| `'shadow'` (shipped) | 56 / **2** | 45 / **0** |
| `'live'`, no variety term | 40 / 0 | 34 / 0 |
| `'live'`, **with** the variety term | 41 / **0** | 33 / 0 |

The remedy the ticket prescribes is implemented and the number it was prescribed to move
did not move. Two measurements say why.

**The route is not starved of candidacy, and it is not out-scored on EVT.** Instrumented on
seed 42 under a live board, `strategic_establish_trade_route` reaches the strategic scorer's
top-3 on **62 boards** and is refused **0** times. Its EVT is `3.9e-2` against a winning
encounter's `8.5e-2` — the same order. Its `payoffValue` is an authored `1.2` over a
`projectDuration` of 6, giving `checkpointsRemaining = 1`, the minimum: on EVT alone it is
one of the strongest undertakings in the corpus.

**What kills it is the desire multiplier: `0.0112` against the winner's `2.7750`.** `0.0112`
is `MINIMUM_DESIRE` raised through `PERSONALITY_SCORE_EXPONENT` — the floor. The route sits
at the desire floor for the actor proposing it, and six of the twelve most-frequent
undertaking templates sit at that same floor value. Contest B never read axiological desire
at all, which is why the route survived under `'shadow'` and dies under the board.

So the mechanism is not "the winners repeat". It is **the board introduced axiological
desire into undertaking selection, and this template's authored `motivations`
(`asceticism_extravagance`, `loyalty_ambition`, `tradition_novelty`) mismatch the profile of
the only actor who proposes it.** A discount of at most 18% on the leaders cannot close a
gap of 30–250× in desire, and the measurement confirms it does not.

## Why `trades_with` is reported and not gated

Gating it would red the census on the **currently shipped** configuration: seed 99 writes
zero trade routes in `'shadow'` today. THR-1329, shipped earlier the same day, already
established why and it is not the board — seed 99's three `ambition_dominate_trade` holders
are two `notable` and one `ambient`, and `phaseAgentDecision` runs the decision loop over
spotlight-tier individuals only, so all 300 holder-ticks emitted no strategic board at all.
Its 12-seed sweep found **exactly one route-capable seed (42)**.

Seed 42's two routes come from a single spotlight actor (`ind_0`). "Non-zero trade routes"
is therefore an n=1 observation on the one outlier seed the route economy has ever been
validated on — one seed's luck, not a property the healthy configuration holds. A gate must
not be built on it.

`CENSUS_DISTINCT_TEMPLATE_FLOOR` is set to `30` and its docblock states plainly that **it
does not by itself catch this failure**: a live board still starts 33–41 distinct templates
while writing zero routes. Setting it high enough to separate the arms would put it at
~41–44, one point under seed 99's healthy 45, where it would be measuring *throughput* (the
live arm starts 228–538 undertakings against shadow's 673–953) rather than variety. A gate
that fails for a reason other than the one it names is the vacuity this file has shipped
twice already, so it is deliberately not that.

## What remains, and the seam

`UNIFIED_DECISION_BOARD_MODE` stays `'shadow'`; contest B, `STRATEGIC_ENCOUNTER_SCORE_BRIDGE`
and the strategic clamp are untouched — they go in one commit with the flip or none, since
under `'shadow'` contest B is the only thing that selects undertakings at all.

The remaining question is not an implementation detail, it is a design fork with its own
balance envelope, and it is a different fork from the one THR-1349 names:

> Should an undertaking whose authored `motivations` mismatch its proposer's axiological
> profile be effectively unselectable — the desire floor is a *multiplier*, so a floored
> template needs ~90× the EVT of a matched sibling to win — or should the board floor
> undertaking desire the way the encounter path floors its own?

Both answers are defensible and they produce different worlds, so the choice belongs in a
plan doc rather than in a constant nudged by an executor. Whichever way it goes, the
acceptance signal for the flip cannot be `trades_with > 0` on one seed.
