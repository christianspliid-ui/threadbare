# 2026-09-26 — THR-1585: a notable's agenda reads as theirs in the Chronicle

**Ticket:** [THR-1585](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1585) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** none (self-scoped bug) · **Pillars:** Engine (the phase title is set where the agenda is built). UI: the Chronicle card is unchanged and renders the new title; evidence is a jsdom render (`Browser-verify substitution: jsdom render of ChronicleEntryCard fed by buildNotableAgenda → phaseComposition — text-only headline, unattended run`). Content N/A (no prose changed).

## What was wrong

A notable's agenda phase reached the Chronicle as `NOTABLE-AGENDA-NPC_86-CAMPAIGN-T120 — BANNERS-CALLED`. The agenda's phases had no title, so the runner fell back to ids. THR-1602 had already cut that down to the humanised phase id ("Banners called"), but the headline still did not say whose agenda it was.

## What shipped

- `buildNotableAgenda` gives every phase a `title` through the new `notableAgendaPhaseTitle`: `"Harn Vosk's campaign: banners called"`. This matches how rival scheme phases are titled.
- `ChronicleAgendaHeadline.test.tsx` builds each of the five agenda families through `buildNotableAgenda` and then the composition runner, and renders the resulting entry in `ChronicleEntryCard`. It asserts that the header names the notable and the agenda, and that no `notable-agenda-` or `npc_` text appears. Negative control: with the title line removed, all 5 cases fail.

Compositions saved before this change have no titles and still show the THR-1602 fallback.
