# 2026-09-23 — THR-1525: a scene draws both poles of the value it is about

**Ticket:** [THR-1525](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1525) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** Engine, Content (UI N/A: no player-facing reader of the desire score). **Plan:** [`Docs/plans/2026-09-23-desire-score-both-poles.md`](../plans/2026-09-23-desire-score-both-poles.md). Director ruling 2026-09-23: option 1, the engine reading.

## What shipped

**`computeDesireScore` does what its docstring always said.** An unpinned motivation reads the mortal's **conviction**, `|v|`, so a scene about tradition draws the Heretic as surely as the Archivist. The reading is the tunable `DESIRE_SCORE_POLE_MODE` (`'absolute'`, CMS-registered). `'signed'` restores the pre-THR-1525 virtue-only draw exactly and is the one-constant rollback. An optional **pole pin**, `motivationPoles` on `UnifiedActionTemplate` / `StrategicActionTemplate` and copied onto the encounter cache entry, narrows one axis to one pole (signed in that direction). The pins pass through scoring steps 7 and 7a and through `computeBoardDesireMultiplier`, so undertakings read the same function. The THR-1349 empty-set neutral branch is untouched. Its docstring now says the floor catches **indifference** and **pinned opposition**, not the opposite pole. The candidate trace gains an optional `pinnedAxes` count.

**Authoring.** Spec step 6 says `motivations` names what a scene is *about* and draws both poles. It adds that pinning a fork's own axis draws one arm's mortals. `check:encounter` warns on exactly that, via `motivationPoleChecks.ts`, which is falsified by fixtures in both directions and is silent on the corpus. UL **Motivations** (Encounters shard), rulebook §7, both wiki pages, and wiring guide Capability 31 § 4 plus the template-field table are all updated. The Crossroads keeps its Eye-axis selection, and only its comment rationale changed.

## Finding — the plan's pick-rate criterion cannot see the defect

The plan's pass bar was a magnitude-matched, per-capita pick-rate ratio in [0.8, 1.25], expected "far below 0.8" under `'signed'`. Measured, it lands in the **same spread in both modes**: 0.71 / 1.09 / 1.06 / 0.71 signed against 0.74 / 1.01 / 0.89 / 0.55 absolute. So it does not discriminate. Every autonomous mortal picks something every tick, and the desire multiplier does not decide *which* template wins, because capability, travel and value-per-tick dominate the ranking. Flaw-leaners under `'signed'` kept picking scenes about their values at close to the same rate while 58–82% of those picks sat at the desire floor. Diagnosis per the plan's kill criteria: the miss is not a caller dropping pins or a cache entry missing the field. The picked-candidate desire shows the new reading live on every pick. The ratio stays in the census output as a reported line, and the header records why.

**Design observation, not in this ticket's scope:** that same fact means personality steers *which scene a mortal takes* much less than THR-531's "strong & legible" framing implies. A mortal floored on a scene's values still takes it at nearly the rate of one who is drawn to it. This is worth a tuning look (`PERSONALITY_SELECTION_WEIGHT` / exponent against the value-per-tick spread) if the world's scene mix still reads personality-blind in play.

**Not measured:** undertaking-start pole balance. `strategic_action_started.actorId` did not resolve to a profiled actor node in the census on either seed, so that line reports all starts unresolved. The mirrored test covers the undertaking path in the function itself.

## Blocked before merge — the flip halves undertaking throughput (run 1, 2026-09-23)

`npm run census:undertakings` (seeds 42/99, 150 ticks, the board's standing acceptance instrument) **PASSes under `'signed'` and FAILs under `'absolute'`**:

| | undertaking share | starts / mortal / 100 ticks (floor 4) | variety sample (150) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| signed, seed 42 / 99 | 28.9% / 29.5% | 5.6 / 5.2 | filled (24 / 30 distinct) | **PASS** |
| absolute, seed 42 / 99 | 13.6% / 15.9% | **2.3 / 2.8** | **never filled** (73 / 105 starts) | **FAIL** |

`census:desire-poles` agrees: undertaking starts over 200 ticks, medium, fall from 252 / 235 to 119 / 92. `undertakingCapabilityGrowth.live.test.ts` (heavy) goes red for the same reason: 8 growth-paying completions against a floor of 10.

**Mechanism.** Under the signed sum a flaw-leaner's *encounter* desire sat at the floor (0.011), while an undertaking kept its ambition-centrality boost above the floor. So the decision board handed flaw-leaners to undertakings. About half the undertaking share the census floors were calibrated against was produced by the defect. Under the absolute reading flaw-leaners rank the two families exactly as virtue-leaners always have.

**Why not ship it anyway.** The plan's kill criterion is *"do not ship a flip that empties the board"*. The share envelope still passes, but the throughput and variety floors fail, and THR-1523's Done-when requires this census to stay PASS. **Why not retune here.** Restoring undertaking throughput means re-weighting the board for *every* mortal. The plan explicitly scoped that out, and payoff scaling is ruled out (THR-1292 carve-up).

**Fixed this run, independent of the block:** `appointment-generatedWorld.test.ts` assumed only its planted mortal would trace an appointment regime. Under the flip the live world plants its own (1 organic appointment on seed 42), so the assertion is now scoped to the planted mortal and the organic ones are reported.
