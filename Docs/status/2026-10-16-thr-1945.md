# 2026-10-16 — THR-1945: noun subjects keep their verb's s for a they/them actor

**Ticket:** [THR-1945](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1945). Content pillar (prose fix plus a content lint); Engine and UI N/A.

`{s}` agrees with the scene's actor, so a line whose subject was a noun ("The captain nod{s} at {name}") read "The captain nod" when the actor used they/them. All such sites were in two encounter files: 33 verbs in `builders-fellowship-encounter-content.ts` and 17 in `merchant-consortium-encounter-content.ts` now carry a plain `s` / `es`. This includes the second verb of a compound sentence ("reviews it and approves") and "it goes".

- **Lint.** `templateSeams.thr1857.test.ts` now fails on `the|a|an <word> <verb>{s|es}` anywhere in `src/data`. It also checks that the regex catches both shapes and leaves `{They} nod{s}` alone.
- **Evidence.** `npm run gate`: test, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, CLI smoke and test-heavy all pass. No surface change, so no browser evidence is owed.
