# 2026-10-16 — THR-1945: noun subjects keep their verb's s, and `{es}` stops leaking raw

**Ticket:** [THR-1945](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1945). Content pillar (prose fixes and content lints), plus one Engine fix the review gate found; UI N/A.

`{s}` agrees with the scene's actor, so a line whose subject was a noun ("The captain nod{s} at {name}") read "The captain nod" when the actor used they/them. About 80 such verbs, nearly all in the Builders' Fellowship and Merchant Consortium encounter files, now carry a plain `s` / `es`. They include subjects with no determiner ("Every calculation checks out"), possessives ("The master's workshop runs"), `{cast:factor}` and `{ally:strongest}` subjects, and "it goes".

- **Engine (review round 1).** `enrichProse` never resolved `{es}`, so "{name} watch{es}" printed the raw token on the encounter path for every actor. `enrichProse` and the rite-prose fill now resolve `{es}` like `{s}`. `bindNameSubjectVerbs` binds `{name} <verb>{es}` to "es".
- **Content (review round 2).** Three `{es}` on stems that do not take "es" (`check{es}`, `decid{es}`, `carry{es}`) are rewritten.
- **Lints** in `templateSeams.thr1857.test.ts`. One fails on `the|a|an <noun> <verb>{s|es}` (possessive forms included) and on a `{cast:x}` / `{ally:x}` subject followed by `{s}`. The other fails on `{es}` after any stem other than s, x, z, ch, sh or o.
- **Deferred:** a name subject's second verb ("Kael holds the gate and process{es}") still takes pronoun agreement. Filed as [THR-1958](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1958).
- **Evidence.** `npm run gate`: test, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, CLI smoke and test-heavy all pass. No surface layout changed, so no browser evidence is owed.
