# 2026-10-18 — THR-1876 S1: the Inspector's page-layout table, its pages and the Inspector lab

**Ticket:** [THR-1876](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1876). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1876-the-inspector.md` (slice S1, § D6, § D7, § D12, § D16). Engine: the table, subject view models, Connections, *In this land*, the land prose move. Content: the land, god, record and content rows; the Inspector's words (`src/data/view-content.ts`). UI: `InspectorPage` at three sizes and `?view=inspector-lab`. S1 touches no `GameView.tsx`: nothing in play opens the Inspector until S3 ([THR-1911](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1911)).

- **One page-layout table.** `DETAIL_PAGE_REGISTRY` is `Record<PageLayoutKind, …>` over the six graph kinds plus `content`, `land`, `god` and `record`. Each row may say `visibleAt` (hover · docked · wide · full) and `tab`. `composeSections` is the one row loop: `generateDetailPage`, `generateContentPage` and the new `generateLandPage` / `generateGodPage` / `generateRecordPage` all use it. It copies a row's width and tab onto its section and never drops one by width. `sectionVisibleAt` is the one filter. Two shapes are new: `links` (identity chip + one fact) and `cards`. A `panel` row may now carry `ref`, `tooltipId` and `meter`, and `prose` splits paragraphs on a blank line.
- **Subject pages.** `selectSubjectPage` (registered as `subjectPage`) dispatches every `WorldRefKind`, content refs and graph event nodes to a page with a header, an Act row (`getTargetActionSlots`, the drawer's exact parameters), a body from the table and Connections. A gone subject gets the missing page ("Unknown"). The god's own page is the ascendant's agent ref. `CONNECTION_RECIPES` is per-class data over real edge types. *In this land* reads `hexContents`, which now also holds armies, artifacts, lairs, ruins (including discovered sites once `hexRevelation` reveals them, the first production read), halls, resources and covenants.
- **Moved, output unchanged.** HexChronicle's The Land / The Soul / The Ruins prose now lives in `views/subjects/landProse.ts`. A golden test against a verbatim copy of the memos pins three generated hexes paragraph for paragraph. The god rail's selectors moved to `views/subjects/godSheet.ts` (old path re-exports), `explorationHooks` to the engine, the danger ladder and the god's nature words to data files. One land naming rule ("The plateau at Hawk-crest", else the Area, else "Open tundra") also replaces the Covenants row's "(col, row)" fallback.
- **S1 fixes.** The Faction allegiances chip shows the reputation rank word, not `member_of.rank`. "N turns ago" and "at tick N" go through `elapsedLabel`. Relation bases are worded. An encounter-step event is named by its template, not "(step N)".
- **The lab.** `?view=inspector-lab` builds the seeded world (dev identity, seed 42, medium, the First, the test package, 30 ticks) and draws 14 rows at docked / wide / Full: one per page kind, two chain rows and the stress case. A 1920×1080 / 2560×1440 toggle switches the root size and the frame tokens. `__DEBUG.getInspectorLab()` reports each cell's shown sections, folded groups, what the size hides, sideways overflow and raw-id/numeral leaks. The style guide keeps one sample of every shape and links to the lab.
- **Review gate.** Two rounds, six confirmed findings, all fixed. Story rows never rendered: events now travel as record refs that open their event. Companions read `accompanies` the wrong way. Friends and foes missed one-way rivalries. The First check used `'first'` where the edge says `'the_first'`. Record pages had no Connections.
- **Evidence.** `npm run gate`: test, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, cli-smoke and test-heavy pass (5 detail-page snapshots updated for the intended word and attribute changes). Browser (bash-vite :5247, Playwright) at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440: 42 cells, `overflowX` false everywhere, no leaks, no blank cell, console 0 errors (warnings are worldgen's pre-existing GraphSchema notices). At 1440 the root is 18 px and docked is 717 px. The stress cell folds Who to six rows with "Show all 30" docked and shows every row wide and Full; its two wide-only sections are listed under "More when widened". Laws 1, 13/14, 17, 21, 33, 57 and 58 hold in the lab as composed; Law 37 is N/A (no encounter).
