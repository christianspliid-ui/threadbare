# 2026-10-06 — THR-1740: the forecast window re-plan — sure things stop paying twice

**Ticket:** [THR-1740](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1740) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-05-thr-1740-forecast-window-replan.md` · **Pillars:** Engine; Content N/A (master-content rung routed to THR-1742); UI N/A (headless gauge).

## Why

Mortals are meant to take on work they would win about half the time. Only ~45% of free choices did. THR-1689 showed it was choice, not availability: unthreaded mortals won near-certain branching quests at full strength (the quest too-easy exemption), and value per tick counted the odds a second time on top of expected utility.

## What shipped

- **Decision 1:** `BRANCHING_QUEST_WINDOW_EXEMPT_SCOPE = 'threaded'` — a quest keeps the too-easy exemption only for a mortal threaded to the god; reachability (outgrowth exemption, cap reserve, curator lift) unchanged. The `exempt` trace stamp uses the same predicate.
- **Decision 3:** above `ENGAGE_VALUE_ODDS_PIVOT` (0.575) a free choice's value per tick is scaled by pivot / F (`ENGAGE_VALUE_ODDS_NEUTRAL`); a threaded mortal's quests are left out. `ScoredCandidate` gains `oddsNeutralScale`, `questKeepsShipped`, `engagementWindowLow/High`.
- **Decision 2:** the commit stamp records the window the scorer used; `ownWindowShare` is judged against `KPI_IN_WINDOW_MIN` 0.50 (was 0.60 against the static window). The static share is still reported.
- Invariant file: expert and master level rows, *experts > journeymen*, and *most free choices are in-window* (own window) re-armed; *masters > experts* re-pointed to THR-1742.
