# THR-2103 — Art-led pass, slice 7: the lists and notices

**Shipped 2026-11-02.** UI Visual Overhaul — Design System v1. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-11-01-thr-2103-art-led-lists.md`.

**One helper for row pictures (S7.1).** New `src/engine/views/rowVisual.ts`: `rowVisual(ctx, ref)` maps a ref kind to its picture (person, companion, place, place within, faction, army, relic or gift; every other kind returns null and the row keeps its kind icon). People pass the god's knowledge of them, so a stranger draws the silhouette tier (Law 8). A place draws its landscape when the tiles are given. `agentRowVisuals`, `toastRowVisuals` and `hoverRowVisual` cover the digest, the toasts and the hover card. The hosts resolve; the components never read the graph. The harvest's `echoVisual` now reads actors and places through `rowVisual` (one path, Law 3).

**The rows (S7.2).** Chronicle entries lead with their first linked name's picture (the seeded world's 167 entries declare no linked names yet, so none draws one and none counts as bare). The Ledger tab's rows lead with the mortal's face. Notables rows lead with the notable's face. Threads replaces the initials medallion (`ThreadPortrait`, deleted) with the shared chip on every row; hex and source rows draw as before. Read the Threads leads each named mortal's line with their face. A toast leads with the picture of what its click opens and keeps its trailing kind icon. Find's world rows show their picture instead of the kind icon, with the kind word kept as the accessible name; card and Codex rows keep their icon. The hover card shows its subject beside the name. Past omens carry their mark. Non-person tiles take `--radius-control`.

**The headings (S7.3).** `LIST_HEADING_ICONS` in `view-content.ts`, with the ten no-idea headings in `LIST_NO_IDEA_HEADINGS` (for THR-2104). `SectionHeading` gains `icon`; `ListHeadingIcon` draws a styled label's shape. Threads' text chevron became the carved `disclosure` icon. That icon points up at rest, so the plan's "as the Years toggle" turn (`rotate(90deg)` open) pointed it right; Threads and the Years toggle now use the god rail's turn (90° folded, 180° open). `HandSection` keeps the old turn: THR-2127.

**Decision 8.** `DoomClockDetail` reads `DOOM_ARCHETYPE_GLYPHS`; its second table is gone.

**Debug.** `data-row-subject` / `data-row-art` / `data-heading-icon`; `__DEBUG.getListDressing()` and `__DEBUG.pushToastFixture()`.

**Browser evidence (Playwright, seeded medium, 30 ticks).** Chronicle, Notables (10 rows), Threads, Find ("a": 17 subject rows), the toast fixture on The First (`art`) and a hover card on The Bone Coast (`art`) all report `subjectRowsBare: 0`. Heading shapes: `chronicle`, `notables`, `threads`, `person`, and Find's `place`, `person`, `artifact`, `cards`, `codex`. Console: 0 errors.
