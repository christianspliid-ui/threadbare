# THR-2008 — a faction pays for a member's founding they cannot afford (the founding patron)

**Shipped 2026-10-24, behind the founding-costs switch (still off).** Decided on THR-2006 (spec `Docs/audits/2026-10-21-thr-2006-founding-patron.md` § 4). Once `MEANS_CELL_COST_ENABLED` is on, a mortal who cannot pay a costed cell (found a town, place, lane or guild) can still take it when their highest-ranked purse faction's treasury covers the cost. That faction, the patron, pays the whole cost and the founder pays nothing. While the switch is off, nothing changes in play.

**Engine.** New `src/engine/foundingPatron.ts`: `findFoundingPatron` (rank via `getDerivedMembershipRank`, tie by id, skips treasuries below the cost and dissolved factions), `isFoundingUnaffordable` (the board's test, used in `decisionBoard.ts`), `chargeFoundingPatron` (through `applyTreasuryDelta`). `chargeFoundingCost` (`strategicActionLifecycle.ts`) charges the patron when the founder cannot pay, traces `economy.founding.patron`, and charges nobody if no patron exists by then. Switch `FOUNDING_PATRON_ENABLED` (true) and `FOUNDING_GATE_SEEDS` (42, 7, 99) in `mortal-means-constants.ts`.

**The switch gate is two predicates.** `npm run measure:means` now says founding costs MAY switch on only when comfort pace holds **and** the founding-starvation floor holds on every gate seed with the patron on. It reads the verdict `npm run measure:founding-starvation` writes, and only a 600-tick medium-map verdict built from code unchanged since counts. The `TRADE_RUN_PAY_MAX` kill-line lever is retired; the next lever is the town row of `MEANS_CELL_COST`.

The floor now passes on all three seeds. The audit measured seed 42 failing (1 against 2.5) on an older main. The switch stays off because comfort pace still fails (514 ticks against 120).

**Shipped in two PRs.** PR #2550 carried the patron, the board and charge, the patron arm and the two-predicate gate. The follow-up added review-gate cycle 2 on the measurement tooling: each cached row is stamped with the commit its bundles were built from, and `measure:means` accepts a verdict only when nothing in src/ or scripts/ changed since. It also corrected the cost switch's comment, which still said seed 42 fails the floor.

**Measurement tooling.** `measure:founding-starvation` gained the patron arm. One arm on one seed takes about 10 minutes at 600 ticks, so each arm × seed result is cached under `.cache/founding-starvation/` and stamped with its bundle hash. `--arms`, `--seeds`, `--no-build` (for parallel commands) and `--report-only` assemble the verdict across commands.
