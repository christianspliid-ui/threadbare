# 2026-09-28 — THR-1607: a readable spend

**Ticket:** [THR-1607](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1607) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1606-what-your-hand-did.md` § B4 · **Pillars:** UI (essence block, card face, reach rows, identity strip, mandate/doom labels); Content (tooltip registry entries, fold and concept labels); Engine: N/A (no engine write changes; the pool was always deducted, the bar never showed it).

## What was wrong

All three round-1 cold testers failed to say what their essence was. The essence bar filled against `/10` while pools start at fifty, so every bar sat full and no spend ever moved one. Rows also re-sorted by level, so a spend reordered the list. There were no numbers. "Doomed" on a cast card read as a threat, although a player cast never fails outright (the THR-728 floor). "Owing" and "Sealed" read as states, not rungs, and "ABSOLUTE" read as a boast.

## What shipped

- **Essence rows** (`EssenceBlock`, `selectEssenceRows`, new `essenceDisplay.ts`):
  - Each bar fills against `ESSENCE_BAR_CEILING` (bound to `INITIAL_ESSENCE_PER_SPHERE`).
  - Each row prints its whole-number balance (Law 13's pool-balance exception).
  - The order is fixed: the god's spheres first, then canonical order.
  - The four Foundation spheres fold under *Elder powers*, closed by default unless the identity holds one.
  - Every balance carries `ui.essence.row`.
  - After a spend of at least one measure, the row shows the Law 15 delta cluster for `ESSENCE_SPEND_FLASH_MS`.
  - Hovering or focusing a live card lights the row it will draw from, and opens the fold if it has to.
- **Card face:**
  - The price carries `ui.card.cost`.
  - The cast forecast word now routes to `ui.forecast.cast.<tier>`, which says *how cleanly* a cast lands, never *whether*.
  - Law 10 check: the Star reach icon is an SVG heraldry charge, not `✦`, so the cost pip keeps its glyph.
- **Reach rows** read "Eye — Blinkered". The reach carries `reach.*` and the word carries a generated `ui.reach_tier.<reach>.<word>` tooltip that names the rung and the next word. The raw `title` is retired (Law 17).
- **Identity strip** names *Quintessence* beside its word (`ui.ascendant_quintessence`). The ladder line ("whole and present") carries the band's `quintessence.<band>` entry.
- **Registry entries** for Counter-Omens and Doom Debt (on the doom clock, mandate tracker and mandate sheet), Investiture and Covenants.
- **Interface map:** a new `essence-spend-moves-the-bar` contract, with a spotlight on the Systems & Interfaces wiki page.

## Evidence

Evidence is in `Docs/evidence/thr-1607/`. On `?view=game&seeded&size=medium` at 1920×1080, a Dream cast on Kael took Mind from 38 to 37 and its fill from 76% to 74%. The row order was unchanged and the spend cue fired ("Mind fell a little"). Tooltips were confirmed live on the reach tier word, Quintessence and the essence balance, with no console errors. The card cost and cast-forecast tooltips were verified in jsdom, because the seeded drawer holds no cast cards at the opening.
