# THR-2211 — Stop the world only for real decisions

**Shipped 2026-11-14.** Encounter Experience. Plan doc: `Docs/plans/2026-11-13-thr-2203-stop-only-for-real-decisions.md` (slice A; slice B is THR-2212).

- **An ending the god had no part in waits on its badge.** The aftermath poster (`phaseEncounterVisibility`) marks an ending with `godTouchedEncounter` false as `badgeOnly: 'untouched_ending'`, stamped with the chapter's wait word. `shouldAutoOpenEncounterNotification` declines it. The thread-row badge reads "theirs alone · open to read", "played out without your hand · open to read", or, at near-miss or worse, "went badly without your hand · open to read" in the band's accent. An ending the god was present for still stops: a committed card, or an empty committed hand (`godPresentForEncounter`). An empty commit writes no `choiceHistory` entry (THR-1123), so the step advance now keeps it as `UnifiedAction.emptyHandCommitted`; on the final step it is still `activeNudges: []`. The review gate caught this in round 1: the first cut read only `godTouchedEncounter`, which an empty hand never sets. Round 2 found two more problems, both fixed:
  - An untouched ending that offers an aftermath reaction still stops. For the god's own mortals only the Aftermath stage applies the pick, because the autonomous pass skips them.
  - A fused step the player opened and then set down was not held. Its deadline made the set-down skip the hold. The set-down now disarms the fuse (`disarmFatedFuse`: no deadline and no `fatedFuse`) and writes the hold, so the beat waits on a "waiting for you" badge like any pause beat.
- **A Fated step with only dealt cards plays out within the day.** On a pause thread, a step with a deal, no authored nudges and a base forecast of `fated` is posted with `fatedFuse` and `autoResolveTick = tick + FATED_STEP_FUSE_TICKS` (8). Grey zone 1 resolved: the existing deadline path covers it. The post-time hold and the auto-open scan both skip a non-null deadline, and `expireOverdueEncounterNotifications` retires the badge at the deadline. No new resolver. The badge reads "Fated — plays out soon unless you step in", with a tooltip that explains Fated and states no probability. Opening the badge shows the normal Stage.
- **One forecast call (Law 18).** `baseStepForecast.ts` holds the base forecast lifted out of `buildNudgePhaseModel` (`computeBaseStepForecast`, plus `baseStepForecastTier` for the poster). A unit test pins Stage tier = poster tier on three steps.
- **Ending lines.** `aloneLineFor` adds "{name} played this chapter out without your hand." for an untouched "Waits for you" chapter, and the failed-fuse line for a fused step that landed below success.
- **Rule texts** amended: rulebook §3 and the quick reference, game Law 27, UI Law 39, the Vision core-loop sentence.
- **Trace** `encounter.stop_skipped` (`fated_fuse` / `untouched_ending`). The debug bridge's `inspectEncounterPipeline` and the Journey debug panel show `fatedFuse` / `badgeOnly`.
