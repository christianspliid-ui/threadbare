# THR-1496 — sixteen recipes promised a prize and drew nothing

**Date:** 2026-09-13 · **Pillars:** Content · Engine · UI-N/A
**Issue:** [THR-1496](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1496/sixteen-step-route-reward-recipes-promise-a-prize-and-draw-nothing-the)

## What was wrong

[THR-1487](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1487) widened `validateContentQueries` from the `reward_draw` aftermath effect to **both** routes a `RewardPoolRecipe` can sit on. The old gate walked 1 recipe in 482 and called the corpus clean; widened, it surfaced **16 rows (13 distinct sites)** where the prose says the player is paid and the player is not — the [THR-844](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-844) rot class. They were held in `CONTENT_QUERY_RETROFIT_PENDING` so the gate could be fatal for everything else.

## The two shapes, and what each turned out to be

**Shape A — a filter no entry can satisfy (9 sites).** Not one needed new content authored. Every case was a filter that had drifted off the vocabulary its own neighbours use:

| Site | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `encounter.anomaly.moons_tears` | `#pearl #spirit` | `#pearl` | `#spirit` is a **sphere** tag and **no anomaly entry carries a sphere tag at all**. Its own signature trio wears `#pearl #anomaly`. Exactly mirrors `gleaming_vein`'s `['#gem']`. |
| `encounter.anomaly.dreaming_light` | `#fungus #mind` | `#fungus #vision` | Same slip. The spore trio literally wears `#fungus #vision #anomaly`; mirrors `singing_dark`'s `['#crystal','#arcane']`. |
| `tg.quest.warehouse_raid` | `#shadow #contraband` | `#shadow` | All seven siblings in the file use a single live tag (`['#gold']` / `['#shadow']`). These two alone added a never-authored second. |
| `tg.quest.blackmail_ledger` | `#shadow #blackmail_evidence` | `#shadow` | Same. |
| `army.threshold.supply_crisis` | `#military #supply` | `#gold` | Step reach is `gold`; the scene negotiates a resupply convoy with a town factor. |
| `army.supply.forage` | `#military #supply` | `#heart` | Step reach is `heart`; the scene sets terms that leave a country still willing to sell next season. Condition is the heaviest weight (0.5) and `#heart` is what populates it. |

**Shape B — a `categoryWeights` key that is not a category.** The ratchet named 4 sites. A corpus sweep found **9**, across two spellings: `mastery` (not a category at all) and `tomes_scrolls` (a possession *sub*category). The other five were invisible to the empty-pool gate **because their remaining categories resolved** — the pool is non-empty, so nothing complains, while the recipe pays out less than it declares.

Weights are applied per entry (`categoryWeight * tierWeight`), not normalised across declared keys, so an invalid key contributes **zero entries and zero dilution**. Deleting it is therefore provably behaviour-preserving; `tomes_scrolls → possession` is the one that changes anything, and it changes it to the item the prose describes: `enc.letters_of_introduction` now draws `reward_tomes_scrolls_letters_of_introduction`.

**`tsc` had all nine the whole time** — nine `TS2353`s sitting inside the ~2870-error red baseline ([THR-489](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-489)). A signal that large is not a signal. So the class is now asserted where it fails alone and by name, in `contentQueryGate.test.ts`, with a falsification arm and an anti-vacuity arm.

## The ratchet is gone

`CONTENT_QUERY_RETROFIT_PENDING` reached zero, so — as its own header promised — the file, the `grandfathered` arm of `RewardDrawPoolReport`, and the two ratchet tests are deleted. The gate is simply fatal. The two *falsification* tests stay: they prove the gate is fatal, which is a different claim from the ratchet's bookkeeping.

## A premise this change falsified, and what was done about it

Repointing four army filters left six `family` tags — `#military`, `#supply`, `#community`, `#stewardship`, `#contraband`, `#blackmail_evidence` — with **no bearer and now no reader either**. Two surfaces asserted the old premise in prose ("a `tagFilters` query site asks for it and no entry wears it") and would have gone on asserting it, telling the weekly retro to author a bearer when the sunset rule's actual verdict is *delete*.

So the tag-catalog generator now **computes** the verdict instead of asserting it, splitting the DEAD table three ways: *derived* (never deletable), *asked-for* (a hole in the corpus), *orphaned* (deletable). The instrument is a text scan of `src/data`, which is sound **only** because it is asked solely of zero-bearer tags — a tag with no bearer appears in no catalog entry, so any occurrence left is a query site. `__tests__` is excluded, or the allowlist that tracks a tag's deadness would keep it permanently "asked for".

Deleting vocabulary is a design-session call, not an executor's, so the six stay seated and the decision is [THR-1501](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1501).
