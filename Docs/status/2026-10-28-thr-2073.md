# THR-2073 — The god's steering verbs reach the mortal's pick

**Shipped 2026-10-28.** M3: Dynamic Economy. The scorer adds eight pulls after its multipliers — Mark the Ground (`attractionBonus`), Draw Together (`convergenceBonus`), Whisper Intuition (`hunchBonus`), relocation intent, appointment pull, broken drift, ruins and anomaly — into `finalScore` only. The live board re-ranks the top five on its own product and never read them, so a god verb could put a scene on a mortal's shortlist but never make the mortal prefer it. No plan doc; the ticket's Done-when predicates were the spec.

**What changed.** The eight terms join THR-2071's context pull: `boardContextSum` adds them × `BOARD_LATE_PULL_WEIGHT` (1; 0 is the kill switch), so the same clamp [0.5, 2] and per-board geometric-mean normalization hold. `ScoredCandidate` gains `anomalyBonus`, `relocationBonus` and `brokenDriftBonus` (optional). `BoardContextTerm` gains the eight names, which ride `contextTerms` on the board trace at weight 0 too. The `decisionBoard.ts` classification comment now lists them as Board terms with the reason; `appointmentDiscount` is noted as a different channel (it discounts an overrunning scene; the pull leans toward the promised place). New `npm run census:board-late-pulls`.

**Census** (`scripts/board-late-pulls-census.ts`): seeds 42/99/7 × 240 ticks, medium, pooled. Every 10 ticks it casts each verb on 4 rotating mortals, writing what the verb's handler writes (Draw Together skips its threaded-only filter, and a whisper target with no thread edge gets a census-only one, because a headless world threads almost no one). Tallied on targeted mortals' boards where the term varies across encounters: how often the winner holds the board's top value of it, against the share of encounters that do.

| Verb | Weight 0 (before) | Weight 1 (shipped) | Board share (on / off) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mark the Ground | 43.3% (312 boards) | **61.5%** (247) | 59.0% / 54.0% |
| Draw Together | 47.3% (545) | **56.1%** (506) | 55.5% / 49.9% |
| Whisper Intuition | 29.2% (48) | **49.0%** (49) | 37.3% / 40.0% |
| Undertaking share of non-idle | 7.6% | 7.2% | (−0.4 pt, inside the 1-pt line) |

Before the change every verb's winner held the pull *less* often than its board share — the board was blind to it. After, each beats its share. Whisper's sample is small (≈49 boards pooled); the direction is consistent with the other two.

**Not measured:** relocation, appointment, broken drift (gate off by default), ruins and anomaly still do not fire on an uncast headless world; they reach the board by the same sum and are pinned by unit tests, not a census.

**Evidence.** `decisionBoardLatePulls.test.ts`, 11 tests (each term named and summed, weight scaling of late terms only, a marked hex winning in the scorer's proportion, the tie at weight 0). `npm run gate` verdict in the PR body.
