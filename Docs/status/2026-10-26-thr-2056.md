# THR-2056 — The map no longer drifts to the right edge after a chapter

**Shipped 2026-10-26.** Cold playtest round 5, PC-4 (the game acts for the player without saying so). All three testers saw the map slide to the right edge chapter after chapter.

**What changed.**

- **Close re-centres a stranded place.** The Stage eases the lit place into its window at the map's right edge. On close, the camera used to move it only `STAGE_CLOSE_EDGE_MARGIN_PX` (48 px) inside the canvas, so each chapter that opened by itself left the map further right. Now `recentreOnClose` (`stageCamera.ts`) eases a place outside the visible map's middle third (`STAGE_CLOSE_CENTRE_BAND_FRACTION`, 1/3) to the visible map's centre, at the kept zoom. A place already inside the middle third stays, so the camera moves only when it must. The visible map is the canvas after the rails settle, so a docked Inspector is already outside it. The camera still never goes back to its pre-Stage view (THR-1829 § 5).
- **Debug read.** `StageScreenState.closeCamera` records the last close (`hex`, `recentred`). `__DEBUG.getStage().afterClose` reports where that place sits now, the canvas rect, and `inMiddleThird`.
- Retired: `nudgeInside` and `STAGE_CLOSE_EDGE_MARGIN_PX`. `Docs/design-system/motion.md` § Stage close is updated.

**Evidence.** `npm run gate`: PASS (test, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt). New `useStageCamera.test.tsx` (the close effect targets the canvas centre; a middle-third place does not move), plus 5 `recentreOnClose` tests including three closes in a row. Browser (bash-vite :5297, Playwright, 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`): an encounter staged hex 16,12 at x=1780 in the window. After close, `getStage().afterClose` = `{ recentred: true, litHexScreen: { x: 950, y: 564 }, canvasRect: 320–1580 × 48–1080, inMiddleThird: true }`, the exact canvas centre (the old nudge would have left it at x=1532).
