# THR-1966 — A building a mortal raises is the town's own next building, and the builder holds it

**Shipped 2026-10-25.** Undertakings in the economy, slice S2 (plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-17-thr-1937-undertakings-in-the-economy.md` § Engine 4, § Done when S2).

- **The town's next building.** The settlement economy's day now leaves its chooser's pick on the settlement (`economyNextBuilding`) while a slot is free. `create × place` raises that pick, not a workshop by default. It picks at the start (`plannedBuildingId`, so the activity line says "sets about raising a storehouse in Ashford"). At completion it re-checks the pick: still not standing and a slot free, else the town's current pick, else nothing, with the no-room line. The building goes up at once, with no build meter.
- **The builder holds it.** The builder gets a freehold through `grantHolding`. The Inspector's Connections tag reads "built by Kael Thornweaver" where a town-raised building reads "built by the town". If the building is lost to idling, it stays as the builder's Place.
- **Full towns are not targets.** The cell's eligibility refuses a settlement with no pick (`no_building_slot`).
- **Pack Place types** map onto the building table (`LEGACY_PLACE_TYPE_TO_BUILDING`): granary and warehouse → storehouse, guild chapter → counting house, garrison → walls. Court, estate and research circle stay non-economic.
- **Fail-soft:** a settlement the economy has not run on yet (no `economyLastDay`) raises as before.

**Diversity (kill criterion).** A new heavy arm runs 150 economy days with mortals raising the pick at a deliberately heavy rate (a one-in-ten chance per settlement-day). All four measures pass on both seeds. Seed 42: 25 raised, CV 0.54 → 0.55, distinct 82%. Seed 99: 29 raised, CV 0.64 → 0.66, distinct 80%.
