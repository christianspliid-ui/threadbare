## F5 was the worst, and the measurement changed the claim

The always-load card told **every session** that the game's opening encounter still ran the rejected pre-nudge model — untrue since 2026-08-10. The replacement is a measurement, not a flip:

- Batch A is **64 templates** — 40 `axiological` (slot 1, 5 per reach × 8) plus 24 `reach_specific` (slot 2, 3 per reach × 8) — and **both halves carry a `test`**.
- Slot 1's predicate is `targetValuePair === REACH_VALUE_PAIR[primaryReach]`. Only Batch A's axiological templates can satisfy it: the five `AX-META-*` sit on `META_VALUE_PAIR` (`courage_prudence`), which is no reach's pair. So **every run's first formative test is a converted one**, and the whole-meeting legacy fallback (`convertedTests.length === 0`) survives as fail-soft rather than as a path players travel.
- The honest residue is narrower and is now stated as such: of the library's **167 templates, 64 carry a `test`**. A meeting draws two or three dilemmas, so a later slot can still land on one of the 103 that do not.

The ticket asked which denominator was real — 63 or 45. Neither: the discriminator is the `test` field, not the `nudges:` blocks a first count reaches for, and the old text's *"one template converted as the worked reference"* was wrong by 63.
