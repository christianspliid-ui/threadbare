# THR-2255 — The Doom feeds at a place

**Shipped 2026-11-20.** Slice 1 (Engine + Content) of plan `Docs/plans/2026-11-19-thr-2247-dooms-last-stage-is-a-stand.md`, Decisions 1–3 and 5. Slice 2 (the Doom panel, THR-2256) carries every surface.

- **The record.** A failure's `doom_micro_tick` (`complicationEffects.ts`) resolves its top-level place (`placeOf`). On ground the god tends (`buildTendingIndex` / `isTended`) it pushes `magnitude × (1 − DOOM_FEED_ABSORB_SHARE)` (nothing at 1.0), traces `doom.feed_absorbed`, and the complication shows its new `heldProse` line. On untended ground it pushes as before, writes `GameState.doomFeedings[place]` (`engine/doomFeedings.ts`, pruned to `DOOM_REBUKE_WINDOW_DAYS`) and traces `doom.fed`; with Rebuke held the fed line adds "Rebuke it at {place} and the doom finds nothing there." (`engine/doomFeedProse.ts`, picked by tick, no rng draw).
- **Rebuke.** `rebukablePlaceIds` adds untended doom-fed places; `rebukeAnswerFor` returns `{kind:'rival'}` (wins ties) or `{kind:'doom'}`. `rebukeTargetFor` stays rival-only so the THR-2209 behaviour and tests are byte-identical. A doom landing tends through `rebukedPlaces` (plus `DOOM_REBUKE_TENDS_RING`, 0), sets `heldUntil`, raises no temper, traces `rival.rebuked` with `targetKind`. The first-rebuke gift now keys on `rivalRebukablePlaceIds` — its line is about a rival's hand.
- **The alert.** `phaseDoom` → `doomFeedAlert`: from stage `DOOM_FEED_ALERT_FROM_STAGE` (5), at most once per `DOOM_FEED_ALERT_EVERY_DAYS`, while Rebuke is held and affordable at a doom-fed place, one non-pausing `doom_feeding` alert (doom category, opens the place, Chronicle-exempt). The stage-5 notice gains "From here a failure can feed the doom. Ground you tend starves it."
- **Review fixes.** Round 1: the alert opened nothing (`deriveNavigationTarget` read location refs only for two types); `doom_feeding` now routes to its place. Round 2: `transitionToNewCycle` carried `doomFeedings` / `doomFeedAlertTick` into the next age (its tick restarts at 0, so old ticks would hold places and silence the alert); both now reset. Rebuke's card text named only a rival, but the card is now offered where the Doom fed; it now reads "…or the doom where it fed… A rival will remember." Noticed, not changed: `rebukedPlaces` (THR-2209) has the same cross-age carry.
- **The census.** `scripts/agency-census.ts` answer arm Rebukes each doom-fed place once per feeding; in the last stage it answers the alert's place instead (answering every feeding at once leaves the alert nothing to name). Seeds 42 / 7 / 99, `--arm answer --warm 300 --ticks 950`:

  - predicate NOT MET on all three: last-stage share 0.14 / 0.05 / 0.15 (min 0.5); alert days 1 / 0 / 0.

Rebuke casts were refused 3809 / 5833 / 5418 times — Stay's upkeep and the hands keep the primary empty, so Rebuke was rarely affordable and the alert (gated on affordability) almost never fired.
