# THR-2255 — The Doom feeds at a place

**Shipped 2026-11-20.** Slice 1 of 2 of plan `Docs/plans/2026-11-19-thr-2247-dooms-last-stage-is-a-stand.md` (Engine + Content; the UI is slice 2, THR-2256).

- **The record.** A complication's `doom_micro_tick` now resolves the top-level place it happened at and writes it to `GameState.doomFeedings` (`src/engine/doomFeedings.ts`). On ground the god tends (claim, seat, a doom-Rebuked place) the push is absorbed by `DOOM_FEED_ABSORB_SHARE` (1.0) and traces `doom.feed_absorbed`; elsewhere it traces `doom.fed`.
- **The lines.** Each doom complication carries a `heldProse` line for tended ground. On untended ground, while the god holds Rebuke, the fed line adds "Rebuke it at {place} and the doom finds nothing there." (`doomFeedProse.ts`, `doom-feed-content.ts`). The line the player saw is the one the Stage and the chapter archive store (review round 1 fix: they used to keep the raw template line).
- **Rebuke answers it.** `rivalRebuke.ts` targets the union of rival-pressed and doom-fed places (a rival wins ties). A Rebuke on a doom-fed place tends it for `DOOM_REBUKE_WINDOW_DAYS` (3), raises no rival temper, and reads its own clean / crooked result line.
- **The alert.** From Doom stage `DOOM_FEED_ALERT_FROM_STAGE` (5), `phaseDoom` raises a non-pausing daily `doom_feeding` alert ("The doom fed at {place}. Rebuke it there."), only while Rebuke is held and affordable at that place.
