# THR-875 — Meeting Batch A: the iron and gold reaches convert

**Shipped 2026-08-09.** Nine dilemma templates gained a `test` field, taking the
converted population from 1 to 10 and completing the **iron** and **gold**
slot-1 sets. A player whose primary reach is either of those now draws a
formative test from slot 1 on every meeting, rather than a legacy choice scene.

## What converted

`AX-IRON-02` … `AX-IRON-05` and `AX-GOLD-01` … `AX-GOLD-05`, authored against
`AX-IRON-01` as the worked reference and against the contract enforced by
`src/components/MeetTheFirst/__tests__/meetingNudgeStage.test.ts`. Every one
carries a 7-card hand: two free sphere-less commons leaning opposite poles,
five sphered cards across `mind`/`life`/`spirit`/`time`/`matter`, one
`floor_at_cost` rider, and all five `bandProse` slots. Difficulties are spread
0.35–0.6 rather than parked on the 0.5 default, so the reaches do not all
forecast identically.

Three setups carried a childhood or youth framing that the present-tense
re-situation rule does not allow, and were re-cut to the adult reading:
`AX-IRON-02` ("the youngest face in the room"), `AX-GOLD-04` ("asking a youth
for bread"). No scenario in this batch had to be killed — all nine re-situated
honestly.

## The pole convention, and where it inverts

Pole `a` is the first-named pole of the `ValuePair`, matching the sign
convention in the authored `axiologicalShifts`. Two tests invert against the
naive reading and say so in a comment, because an author skimming the pair name
would get them backwards:

- `AX-IRON-05` — the merciful pole is the **refusal** to hold the blade. The
  condemned man dies either way, so pole `a` buys {agent.name}'s hands, not his
  life.
- `AX-GOLD-04` — neither pole refuses the meal. The axis is what is taken in
  return, which is the only honest reading of the two authored choices.

## Slot 2 is blocked, and this is the finding

The ticket's Batch A is 40 slot-1 templates plus 24 slot-2 (`reach_specific`,
3 per reach, clearing `MEETING_POOL_MIN_CONVERTED`). **Slot 2 cannot be
converted as specified.** `DilemmaTemplate.targetValuePair` is optional, and all
40 `reach_specific` templates leave it undefined — they key on `targetReach`
instead, which is what `selectDilemmas` pools them by. But the contract test
asserts `test.valuePair === template.targetValuePair`, and `FormativeTest.valuePair`
is a required `ValuePair`. A converted `RC-*` template would therefore have to
satisfy `valuePair === undefined`, which the type forbids.

That is a content decision, not a typo: it asks which value pair each reach
trial is *about*. `REACH_VALUE_PAIR[targetReach]` is the obvious candidate and
is wrong at least sometimes — `RC-IRON-02` is blade-versus-shield, decisive
ending against enduring protection, which is not `mercy_ruthlessness`. Filed as
THR-1062 rather than guessed at.

## Evidence

Contract test green over all 10 converted templates (up from 1 — the suite runs

suite, typecheck ratchet, and build green; both tree-diffing freshness gates run
last. No engine or UI file touched — `FormativeTest` renders through the
existing `FormativeTestBeat`, which is why the ticket marks both pillars N/A.
