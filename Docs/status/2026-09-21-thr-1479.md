# 2026-09-21 — THR-1479: the appointment primitive — a mortal keeps, or misses, a meeting at a place by a time

**Ticket:** [THR-1479](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1479) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** Engine, Content, UI. **Plan:** [`Docs/plans/2026-09-21-thr-1479-appointment-primitive.md`](../plans/2026-09-21-thr-1479-appointment-primitive.md). Slice 1 of three; slices 2 (harness, THR-1518) and 3 (grid, THR-1519) are children.

## What was wrong

A Bargain at the Crossroads asked the mortal to *collect it here at the next full moon*, and the engine had no way to make that true: `encounter_seed` carried a tick and a cast and no place, so the sequel fired wherever the mortal stood. THR-1476 made the prose honest by removal (the stranger finds them) and wrote prose rule 7b. Christian's direction: make it true by construction, and wire the new tool into every place an authoring agent looks, or it ships dead.

## What shipped

**Engine.** An `encounter_seed` may carry an `appointment` block — place (`$here` / `$cast:<key>` / literal), window, counterparty, and a `missed` branch (gated literal or query). **The seed is the appointment's one record**; the planter (`encounterAftermath.ts`) binds the sentinels, refuses a fourth (`APPOINTMENT_MAX_PER_MORTAL`), and writes the promise as an `owes_favor` edge carrying `properties.appointment` — the Agreement kind's `favor` class, no new type. New `src/engine/appointments.ts`: slack (`due − now − travel`, priced by `findShortestPath`), the leave margin (a formula over `courage_prudence` and `loyalty_ambition` that can go negative — a Renegade chooses to miss), the five regimes, the pull, and the favour/Event helpers. `phaseAgentDecision.ts` resolves one context per holder per tick: leaning discounts candidates that would outlast the slack (`APPOINTMENT_OVERRUN_DISCOUNT`) and adds `computeAppointmentPull` on the relocation channel inside `scoreAndSelect`; departing drops them, filters undertakings longer than the slack, and queues a journey through the ordinary `initMovementState` writer when `scoreMovementCandidate(APPOINTMENT_JOURNEY_PULL, travel)` beats the best remaining encounter — before the board, which is untouched. `evaluateEncounterSeeds` judges kept (on the hex, in the window → fires at the place with `resolutionLocationId`, favour redeemed, `appointment_kept` Event), missed (window closed → seed rewritten into its missed branch, favour `broken`, `appointment_missed` Event with reason), wait, and place-lost (kept branch fires placeless, favour released). The favour expiry sweep skips appointment favours. Twelve named constants in `movement-content.ts`; four traces registered at all four sites; `EVENT_TYPES` gains two kinds; `check:encounter` fails an appointment with no missed branch (`appointment_missing_branch`).

**Content.** The Crossroads accept path plants the appointment (`$here`, `$cast:stranger`, missed = `#crossroads_debt`) and stops granting `promise_given` — one claim on the sheet. The opening says *collect it here at the next full moon* again; The Full Moon Collection returns to the crossroads under the moon and may say *They kept the night they promised*, because now they did. **The Stranger Finds Them** (`encounter.slice.full_moon_reckoning`, tagged `#crossroads_debt`, all eight setting classes so the missed branch finds it wherever the mortal stands) is the corpus's first `appointment_missed` user: one heart test, the broken promise as the BOND chip, a reputation tally and `shaken`. Rule 7b's exception seated in the authoring spec, the SKILL's REVISE trigger and the systems auditor (Live primitives + Aftermath Supportability). UL **Appointment**; rulebook + quick-reference sentences; canon paragraph; four wiki pages; wiring checklist; two interface contracts registered LEAKED-with-ticket (THR-1518) — they flip green on a census hit, never on a gate that reads the code.

**UI.** The PATH chip's appointment sentence (*A meeting at The Wayside Crossroads, in two weeks.*, the place resolved through the same anchor the effect binder uses, clickable to the place); the thread row's clock line (`data-testid="thread-appointment-line"`, regime on hover); the sheet's Bonds row (*They owe The Stranger a meeting at the Crossroads.* / *— broken*); the seeds debug tab's appt row; `__DEBUG.getAppointments`; CLI `appointments`.
