# 2026-09-24 — THR-1526: seed-only sequels never reach the board

**Ticket:** [THR-1526](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1526) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-24-thr-1526-seed-only-encounters.md` · **Pillars:** Engine and Content. UI N/A (no component change; `Browser-verify exempt: no src/components change`, per the Done-when).

## What was wrong

A sequel template is ordinary content, so the encounter cache registered it wherever its envelope matched, and the decision board offered it like any other scene. THR-1524's census caught it on seed 42: the Full Moon Reckoning fired once and the Swindler Found 16 times from the board, while their parents fired 0 times. Fired that way, the Reckoning tells a mortal who never gave their word that they broke it.

## What shipped

- **`UnifiedActionTemplate.drawable?: boolean`** (absent = drawable). `false` marks a seed-only sequel.
  - `isDrawable` (`encounterCache.ts`) skips it at all four appends of `buildEntriesForLocationAndSublocations`.
  - `isDeliverableBranchingEncounter` refuses it as a divine-vision beat.
  - The deprecated array-scored path carries the same one-line gate.
  - Seed resolution never reads the flag.
- **Content:**
  - The four sequels are flagged: the Full Moon Collection, the Full Moon Reckoning, the Swindler Found and the Grateful Kin. They keep their catalog membership and their envelopes.
  - The Swindled Family widened from wayside to `['wayside', 'rural']`, with the plan's authored rural opening.
  - Every `encounter.*` seed target on the board declares `drawable` explicitly: the Crossroads, the Family, the Table That Holds, One Body Short and Rest and Reflect.
  - The legacy `encounter-content.ts` converter now passes the field through, since it is an allowlist.
- **Gates:**
  - `src/engine/seedOnlySequels.ts` walks every place the corpus names an encounter: template seeds, appointment branches, undertaking-cell appointments and strategic catalysts.
  - Five fatal corpus tests in `encounterSeedLiveness.test.ts`: appointment branch targets are non-drawable; the foreign-query pin; no flagged template on any draw path; every flagged template has a planter; the four pins.
  - Two `check:encounter` warnings: a flagged template that nothing plants, and an undeclared `encounter.*` seed target.
- **Evidence tool:** `npm run census:firings` attributes every firing to a seed or to the board, and names the route.

## Measured

`npm run census:firings` (seeds 42 and 99, medium, 200 ticks):
- The four sequels had **0 board firings** on both seeds.
- The Swindler Found fired once per seed, **seeded** by the Family.
- The Family fired **2 and 7** times. It fired 0 before the widening.

## Executor decisions

- **The support bundle.** The Family's widened envelope resolved its setting-keyed default cast to *rural*, so wayside meetings lost their wayside keeper. The Family now composes both classes' defaults, which is the Crossroads precedent. They are bind-only, so they add no one to the world. The plan assumed no cast change was needed.
- **Undertaking-cell appointments are outside the "every appointment branch is non-drawable" gate.** They name generic faction-quest families (`#thieves_errand`, `#court_errand`), whose members stand alone and must stay drawable. The foreign-query pin keeps sequels out of those families.
