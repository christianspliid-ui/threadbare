# THR-1928 — The Crossroads on screen (slice B)

**Shipped 2026-11-07.** Dominion — Player Power Progression. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1926-the-crossroads.md` (§ UI pillar, § Done when → Slice B). Engine and content were THR-1926.

1. **The person page reads the next roads.** New Inspector row `crossroads` (D7.1 Person, Overview, after `intention`; `src/engine/views/subjects/crossroadsRow.ts`): "Next, Kael weighs", the set / leaning / torn line, and up to three roads, each a tile with its kind word, its one line (foreshadowing for a chance, the work's own words for a work) and its place or target as a link. Gated as the lean is — Crossroads on, a thread, a readable mind, a reading — or nothing renders.
2. **Lean, arm-then-fire.** "Lean toward" on every road and "Lean away" on the first. A press arms that tile: the forecast word, the price as cost pips and the confirm sentence before paying, then "Lean" / "Not now"; an unpayable price dims "Lean" with its reason; Escape disarms. The forecast and price come from the functions `placeCrossroadsLean` uses. Held: the held line, the leaned road marked, one control — "Lift your hand" (no refund, a toast).
3. **The strip is a row-shape option, not a new component tree.** `CardsSection.act` / `CardTile.offers` / `marked` (additive), drawn by the new shared primitive `CardActStrip` inside `Section`'s cards renderer; handlers arrive through `CardActContext`, which `InspectorHost` provides from `useCrossroadsActs` (the `handleCompulsionChoice` pattern: place once on the live state, spend in the updater via `withEssenceSpend(…, 'crossroads')`). Styleguide entry, barrel export and both design-system rows land with it (Law 29).
4. **The torn badge.** `crossroadsBadgeModel.ts` + `CrossroadsBadge` on the thread row, from the same snapshot and `settledness`; clears while a lean is held. Not an interrupt; a click opens the person at Overview scrolled to the row.
5. **God's Will is off.** `COMPULSION_PREMONITION_ENABLED = false` (Christian's ruling, chat 2026-10-14). A God's Will already paid in a save still resolves; the flag is the veto path. The legacy "What they are weighing" list was already gone with THR-1912.
6. **Kill criterion census** (600 ticks × seeds 42, 7; 318 decisions): on decided leans `likely` is followed 64% and `not_enough` 25%; torn on 36% of decisions — passes. About a third of `likely` leans lapse because the road leaves the board first; filed as THR-2188.
