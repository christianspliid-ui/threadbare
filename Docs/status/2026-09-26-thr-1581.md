## What shipped

1. **Skill now moves the odds.** The capability curve the dice read is re-fitted (midpoint 30, k 0.08). The roll is `0.40 + 1.25 × (capability − difficulty) + modifiers`, so an even match reads *uncertain* at every level. The scale floors are retired, and so is the difficulty cap that encoded them. Readers that do not feed a d100 stay on `computeCapabilityPreRefit` until [THR-1580](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1580) re-fits each one.
2. **Mortals choose challenges they can win about half the time.** `computeEngagementFit` scales every free-choice candidate on the encounter line and the undertaking line alike. Its too-easy side replaces the outgrowth filter.
3. **A failed challenge is not retried straight away, for real this time.** The failed-template cooldown read resolved actions only, but those are pruned after 20 ticks, so every failure cooldown was silently cut to 20. On seed 99, 19 of 121 failures were retried at 21–24 ticks. `filterByCooldown` now also reads the chapter archive, which outlives the prune, and retries fell to 0. The fix was measured at the plan's starting multiplier (3), so no calibration was needed.
4. **Calibration fixtures keep their odds, not their numbers** (second amendment, decision 3).
   - **Fight:** the bold guard is re-stamped so each step rolls `main`'s odds on the new dice (clash 0.579, nerve 0.492). The re-stamp exposed a harness bug. The reset between fights never stripped the experience traits a fight grows, so the guard drifted from its stamp across 400 fights (on `main` too, hidden by the saturated curve). Result: the row passes (won 3.0, broke off 76.5, routed 5.8, struck down 14.8), and mean segments and harm now match THR-1531. A capability-1.0 master is reported as a diagnostic: won 18.5, struck down 2.5.
   - **Duel:** reported, not gated, until [THR-1628](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1628). There is no harness-only seam to pin the derived card that one side's raw score sets for the other. The cards and nerve odds are preserved. `stronger_by_clock` 61.3% against 49%.
5. **About 70 old-formula test pins re-pinned.** Where a test pinned a premise the ruling retired, it now pins the new property instead. Examples: player-cast "difficulty is inert at local", the THR-998 equal-odds arms, the THR-821 floored subset. The golden fixture's `roll` column is unchanged on all 32 rows.
