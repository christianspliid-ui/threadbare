# 2026-10-13 — THR-1750: Dominion on the map and on every sheet

**Ticket:** [THR-1750](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1750). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-12-thr-1750-dominion-on-screen.md`. Engine, Content and UI. This is lens one of THR-1823.

- **The lens.** `src/engine/dominionProjection.ts` gives each land hex the band of the nearest top-level place within two hexes. Past that radius the terrain read decides, and water has no band. Ties go by `DOMINION_HEX_TIE_PRIORITY`, then by place id. The runtime owns the cache (`ensureDominionProjection`): the assignment is rebuilt on a structure bump, and the bands when the lens opens, when a ledger band moved, or every 6 ticks.
  - HexMapV2 builds `DominionLensLayer` on toggle and disposes it on toggle-off, so it is never hidden by alpha. The layer has three parts: a near-black dim (Touched 0.15, Foreign 0.45, Hostile 0.60 with a faint hatch), an additive Sovereign glow in the god's sphere colour, and a gold frontier that is thicker against Hostile.
  - The key `1` and `MapLensSwitch` come from the new `MAP_LENSES` registry. The switch sits top-left of the map, because the Active Locations bar holds bottom-left. The legend shows three lines and five band words. The hex tooltip adds "Held ground, by Hesk Crossing" while the lens is on.
  - The band-drop badge (a broken gold ring, 36 ticks) reads the ledger, because traces are off in play.
- **The sheets.** `dominionSheetVM` feeds one `DominionBox`:
  - the place page, after Livelihood, with the tend line (seat / Claim / a threaded mortal / an anointed faction, or "Left untended, it will slip back.");
  - the faction page and the artifact page (an unborne artifact is Foreign);
  - a row on the mortal sheet after *Attuned to*, which reads her own scores.
- **The price.** `WheelSlot.dominionParCost` carries the Foreign price beside the band price.
  - The card shows "Held · cheaper here" with faint struck pips, or "Hostile · dearer here" with red pips. Foreign ground and free cards show no chip.
  - The cast bar adds the band clause.
  - `DOMINION_PRICES_LIVE` is now **true**, and the rulebook's cost and odds clause is now tagged IMPL.
- **Debug.** `setDominionLens`, `getDominionLens`, `getDominionHex`; `snapshotScene().layersActive` lists `dominion_lens`; a lens line in the Sphere State tab's Dominion row.
- **Evidence.** Bash-vite on :5217 with Playwright at 1920×1080 (no scroll):
  - Key `1` adds `dominion_lens` and no other layer.
  - 32 of 32 Touched hexes agree with `getDominion` of the place that lent the band.
  - The place box on Hawk Pinnacle reads Touched, and so does `getDominion('loc_40')`.
  - The card chip and cast bar are covered by unit and jsdom tests (preview cost = charged cost on Sovereign and Hostile; no chip on Foreign or a free card). No debug lever opens a place's card drawer.
