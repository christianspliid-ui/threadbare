**The register, calibrated against the exemplar and not re-derived.** Plain, concrete, one idea per sentence, dry over ornate —
`Docs/canon/prose.md`'s baseline. The three failure modes the mad-lib shape produced are each answered directly:

* *Labels instead of change.* `"{Their} honor is {adj} restored, and the court {verb}s in {adj} approval"` became prose that shows
  a behavioural shift — the sanctuary's success is now *"By evening a side hall has washing strung across it, which is how
  {actor} knows it worked."*
* *Failure as negation.* Every rewritten `onFailure` is story-generative rather than a mirrored loss. The failed unification does
  not "scatter the factions" — it leaves *"a record of who refused what, and it will be read back for years."*
* *Adjectives carrying emotional payload.* No rewritten field asks a substituted word to do the work of a sentence.

**Two verifications worth naming.** A vagueness-lexicon sweep over the added lines caught one hit — *"rock that gives up nothing
quickly"* — fixed to a concrete measure (*"yields about a hand's breadth a day"*), so the batch lands at **zero** detector hits.
And the residual token grep against the 7 templates returns only the `// THR-1101:` and `// THR-1036:` comments that *document*
the shape, never prose.

guard against a vacuous empty-population pass, not a requirement that tokens survive. At 146 remaining it is still satisfied with
wide margin, so **no deliberate lowering was owed this batch**. The ticket's instruction to lower or retire it deliberately falls
to whichever later batch crosses 50; recording the current figure here so that batch inherits the number rather than re-deriving it.
