# 2026-10-11 — THR-1818: the map tooltip names terrain in player words and gets out of the way on click

**Ticket:** [THR-1818](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1818) · HexMap UI bug, no plan doc.

- **Names.** `HexMapV2` passed the engine key (`plateau`, `temperate_forest`) as the tooltip's display name and never passed `terrainKey`, so the tooltip's water labels never fired. `terrainDisplayName` (`HexMapV2/palette/terrainPalette.ts`) is now the one display-name map. It carries the water overrides (`TERRAIN_DISPLAY_OVERRIDES`, moved out of `HexTooltip`) and Title Cases everything else. The tooltip and the hex detail panel both read through it. A side effect: the detail panel now says "Ocean" / "Shallow Waters" for water subtypes, matching the tooltip.
- **River.** The 🌊 emoji became the word "River" (`HEX_TOOLTIP_RIVER_LABEL`), the same word the detail panel's Feature row uses. No icon vocabulary owns map features. `hasRiver` is now actually passed, and before this the emoji never rendered.
- **Click.** The new `useHexTooltipDelay` hook owns the hover delay. `suppressTooltip()` runs on every map click: it hides the tooltip, cancels a pending one, and keeps it hidden until the pointer reaches a different hex or leaves the map.
- **Evidence.** The unit test covers every `TerrainType`, palette and override key, plus the hook's timing cases. Browser evidence at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440: hovering shows "Plateau", and the tooltip is absent 2 s after a click.
