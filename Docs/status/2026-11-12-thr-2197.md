# THR-2197 — popups wait for the player's hand to go quiet

**Shipped 2026-11-12.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience, cold playtest round 7 (2/3 testers). No plan doc; scope from the ticket.

1. **More kinds wait.** The threading rite, a ruin's emergence dilemma and the divine receipt now carry `arrival: true` in `HOMED_SURFACES`, so they wait for a quiet moment, for the player's own surfaces to close and for every other interrupt (THR-1853's rule). The time stays held while they wait.
2. **Named exemptions.** `ARRIVAL_EXEMPT_REASONS` lists every kind that is not an arrival, with its reason. The Seat and gifts (`ascendant-beat`) already wait through the gift-delivery quiet hold. Choice sets open from the player's own confirm. Meet-The-First follows straight off the player's own resolve of the opening beat. A test fails if a new kind has neither the flag nor a listed reason.
3. **Dialog arrivals render.** Before, the registry had no path for a dialog-home arrival, so the receipt could not be flagged. It now waits for quiet and then keeps its place. A receipt the player clicks open from its toast mounts at once (`playerOpenedKinds`).
4. **The quiet window counts more input.** `installQuietWindowStamp` (`inputQuietWindow.ts`) stamps `wheel` and `touchmove` on every firing, and `pointermove` at most once per 250 ms, as well as clicks and keys. `scroll` does not stamp, because panels scroll themselves. The same stamp feeds the arrival hold, the world-opened chapter hold and the gift-delivery hold.

5. **Review-gate fixes (round 1).** A rite already on screen keeps its ceremony slot when the world opens a chapter. As an arrival it would otherwise have stepped back and lost the tests already played. The receipt keeps its turn ahead of arrivals queued after it, as before this change.

**Evidence:** `npm run gate` PASS (test, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, cli-smoke). New test `thr2197ArrivalHold.test.ts` (10 cases). Browser check at 1920×1080 (bash-vite + Playwright, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`): a wheel on the rail, then a dilemma staged with `__DEBUG.openHomedSurface('emergence-dilemma')`. While wheels continued for 1.6 s, `getOpenModals()` was `[]` and `heldArrivals` was `EmergenceDilemmaModal ← recent_input`. About 2.5 s after the last wheel, the dilemma opened. Console errors: 0.
