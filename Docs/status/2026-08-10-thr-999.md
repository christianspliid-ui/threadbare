# THR-999 — four company verbs were castable and uncatalogued; the sweep found two more prefixes and a whole missing tab

**Shipped 2026-08-10.** Filed during THR-769 (company card art) by someone looking for a Codex surface to check the new art against, and finding the cards were not in the Codex to show it. The art had landed fine.

## The gap, and why it recurs

`getAllCodexEntries()` assembles action entries by filtering `UNIFIED_ACTION_TEMPLATES` against a **hand-maintained list of id prefixes** — `divine.` / `invest.`, `action.`, `hex.`, `loc.`, `sub.`, `artifact.`, plus ten hand-listed thread/observation ids. A prefix with no arm falls through silently. The template still resolves, the drawer can still hold it, the card art still renders — and the Codex, the player's browsable catalog of what a god can do, never mentions it.

That is a defect class, not an incident, which is why the ticket asked for a sweep rather than four names. The sweep is what the run turned on.

## What the sweep found

Taking the universe to be every template a player can cast — `actorAffinities` including `'ascendant'`, **142 templates across 10 prefixes** — three things were invisible, in ascending order of how long they had been:

* **`company.*` (4)** — `bless`, `draw_together`, `reunite`, `sunder`. The ticket's subject. Shipped uncatalogued under THR-74 / THR-732.
* **`thread.*` (2)** — `thread.dormant`, `thread.reactivate`. The same defect, undetected and unticketed, sitting next to the `bind_thread_*` verbs that *were* catalogued.
* **The entire `resources` category (18)** — one layer further on, and the one the invariant test caught rather than the prefix scan. `mapResourceClass` has produced entries with `category: 'resources'` since the P3 economy work (`92383535`), but `catDefs` never gained a matching row, and `Codex.tsx` renders tabs strictly from `getCodexCategories()`. Eighteen entries were being built on every call and were unreachable in the UI — catalogued into a category that had no tab.

The third one matters most for what this ticket is about: the prefix list was not the only hand-maintained join. Producing an entry and declaring a category are two separate edits, and *either* one missing makes content invisible in a way nothing was checking.

## Where the two new prefixes went

The catalog is organised by **what a verb acts upon** — hex, place, artifact, thread. So:

* **`company.*` gets its own category, "Company Actions"** (⁂, asterism — three marks travelling together). A company is its own target class. Folding these into Divine Actions would blur a category that otherwise reads as *what you do to a person*, and these four act on a band of travelling mortals. Sub-tabs fall out of the shared mapper as Heart 3 / Shadow 1.
* **`thread.*` joins "Thread & Insight"** (10 → 12). Dormant and Reactivate manage an existing thread's court position; they belong with the binding verbs, not in a category of their own.

## The invariant, and the predicate it does not fork

The Done-when asked for a test that pins the rule rather than the four ids. `src/components/Codex/__tests__/codexCatalogCoverage.test.ts` asserts that **every player-castable template reaches the catalog**, with the failure grouped by prefix so the diagnostic names the missing arm rather than listing its members.

The universe predicate is **imported, never re-derived**: `isPlayerReachableTemplate` was already the audited definition of "player-castable" inside the THR-659 orphan inspector, where its docstring cites the drawer source. It is now exported and shared. A second hand-copied definition of the same idea is precisely the drift this test exists to catch — writing one here would have reproduced the ticket's own defect in the test meant to prevent it.

A third assertion covers the `resources` class of failure: every catalogued entry must route to a **declared** category. That is the one that found the missing tab; the prefix sweep could not have, because resource entries do not come from `UNIFIED_ACTION_TEMPLATES` at all.

**Falsified before being trusted.** Reverting both new arms fails it with the diagnostic it was written to produce:

```
Player-castable templates absent from the Codex. Add a bucketing arm in
getAllCodexEntries() (and a CodexCategory if it needs a new home):
company. (4): company.bless, company.draw_together, company.reunite, company.sunder
thread. (2): thread.dormant, thread.reactivate
```

Both guard-the-guard floors are asserted (castable universe > 100, catalog > 200), because every assertion here is vacuously true against an empty population and a silently-empty import would sail through all of them.

## Evidence

Live at `?view=codex`, 1920×1080, `innerWidth`/`innerHeight` measured in the same pass as the capture and `scrollWidth <= innerWidth` (viewport contract holds):

```
★Divine Actions 28   ⬡Hex Actions 49   ⌂Place Actions 23   ✦Artifact Actions 5
⁂Company Actions 4   ⁘Thread & Insight 12   ⚔Mortal Actions 67
◆Possessions 119   ✕Conditions 52   ☍Agreements 6   ⚖Resources 18
```

`Company Actions` and `Resources` are new tabs; `Thread & Insight` moved 10 → 12. All four company thumbnails load (`naturalWidth: 1376`) against the canonical `ACTION_ART` paths. Console: 0 errors, 0 warnings.

`window.__DEBUG.listActions()` on the seeded game route returns all six as live registry members — `company.bless`, `company.draw_together`, `company.reunite`, `company.sunder`, `thread.dormant`, `thread.reactivate` — out of 572. The gap was never in the registry; it was only ever the catalog. (The same call on `?view=codex` returns `[]`, because `listActions` is registered by `GameView` via `_registerActionBridge` and that route mounts no world — an unregistered bridge, not a missing template.)

**UI-Laws judgment** (Laws 1, 13, 14, 17, 21, 37 on the surface as composed): Law 1 holds — the cards carry art, click through to a detail panel, and render rarity as the word `STORIED`. Law 13 holds — no raw magnitudes; the sidebar integers are catalog counts, as on every existing tab. Law 21 holds. Law 37 is N/A (not an encounter flow). **Law 14 is violated, pre-existing:** the shared mappers render `template.crudType` raw in every card subtitle (`Heart · update`) and label a detail row `CRUD`. It reaches all 181 action entries through mappers this change did not touch, so it is filed as **THR-1076** with the law number rather than absorbed — fixing it is a display-vocabulary decision across the whole catalog, not a company-verbs change.
