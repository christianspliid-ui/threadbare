- **The reader.** `computeBondModifier` (`socialEncounterGeneration`) adds `STRANGER_CURIOSITY_BONUS` (0.15) to the stranger penalty when the agent's Eye is above `STRANGER_CURIOSITY_THRESHOLD` (0.3). Protagonists keep their curiosity; a stray Eye trait on an ordinary townsperson no longer reads as perceptive. The dice would have stripped curiosity from ~40% of protagonists, which is a tuning call, not a scale fix. `STRANGER_CURIOSITY_THRESHOLD` remains the lever.
