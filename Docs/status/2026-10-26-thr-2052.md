# THR-2052 — A First's chapter never plays out unseen, and the next step opens on the last one's answer

**Shipped 2026-10-26.** Cold playtest round 5, Urgent, 3 of 3 testers. On a running clock a committed step closed the veil and resolved out of sight; the player met step 2 with no word of step 1 short of "re-read the finished step". And a First's step whose veil never opened, or was replaced before it was set down, played out alone ("faced it alone").

**Engine.** A pause-tier unified step is now held for the player from the tick its beat is posted (`holdStepsForPostedBeats` in `src/engine/playerStepHold.ts`), called by the orchestrator after both encounter-visibility passes (2a.6 and the 2b.2 pause pass). Before, the THR-1730 hold was written only on minimise. The release paths are unchanged: commit, authored choice and dismiss in GameView; a step change, a thread that is no longer pause, or a resolved action in Phase 1. Kill switch `PLAYER_HOLD_ON_POST`.

**UI.** The next step's veil opens on the answer to the last one: `Step 1 held. You: Cut The Thread.` above its afterimage (`lastStepAnswer` in `src/data/nudge-stage-content.ts`, rendered as `veil-last-step-answer` in `EncounterVeil.tsx`). The answering card stays off on a running clock: it would add a click and change the pause behaviour.

**Not in scope.** Putting the auto_resolve countdown on the badge (fix direction item 3) is not in the Done-when.

**Evidence.** On the dev build at `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, 1920×1080, clock set to run at 2×: The Swollen Ford spawned on Kael Thornweaver. Committing "Stay silent" closed the veil, and step 2 opened by itself 1.2 s later, starting with "Step 1 held. They found the line: stake to stake…". `__DEBUG.getPlayerHolds()` showed step 1 held with `live: true`. After Escape, `__DEBUG.tick(20)` left the hold live at `heldTicks: 21` on stepIndex 1. `sinceTick: 1` is the posting tick, not the Escape, so the hold came from the post. Console: only the known TTS-server `ERR_CONNECTION_REFUSED`.
