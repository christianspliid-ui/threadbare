# 2026-10-15 — THR-1902: realm borders in their holder's ink; uncharted land as parchment

**Ticket:** [THR-1902](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1902), slice "Border ink and uncharted parchment" of [THR-1878](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1878) (plan § D12; look from THR-1831 § 5). Engine: one read-only selector on the THR-1822 seam. Content: N/A (no words change). UI: the border, the seat dots, the capital rings and the fog overlay.

- **Why the player saw black where the map was built to draw parchment.** The fog overlay was mounted, on top, and its texture loaded. Its custom shader wrote the sampled texel straight out. The renderer outputs sRGB and the texture is decoded to linear on sampling, so without the output encode (`colorspace_fragment`) the dark-umber parchment (mean ≈ #352920) landed as its linear value (≈ #0a0705): the `#0a0a0c` THR-1831 measured. The shader now encodes, samples the paper in world space (one sheet with mirrored repeats, not a copy per hex), and fades to the scene background over 1.5 hexes where the grid ends, so off-grid stays dark (Law 32).
- **`fogUnexploredFill` palette token.** Golden Hour: the parchment texture with its old `#3d3025` fallback tint. Dark Parchment sets its own: the same paper cooled by a slate tint, `#2a2d36` when it cannot load. `__DEBUG.getMapChrome().fog` reports `parchment` or `tint`, whether the texture loaded, and how many hexes it covers.
- **Borders in the holder's ink.** New selector `map.realmInk` (`src/engine/views/map/realmInk.ts`) gives each Realm its faction's heraldic index — the same index its armies carry (the faction-index map is now shared). `scene/borderInk.ts` mixes the holder's colour 40 % toward the shadow of the terrain on its side, at 0.45 opacity and 1.0 wide, one draw call with vertex colours. Where two Realms meet each draws its own half, so a frontier is two inks side by side. Seat dots and capital rings take the same ink.
- **Red and gold stay out.** Heraldic red → forest green, orange → indigo, and magenta → plum: the resolved-ink test found magenta drifting inside 30° of the doom red once mixed toward warm terrain. The test checks every heraldic colour against every terrain colour of both palettes and names any pair that reads as red or gold.
- **Evidence:** `Docs/audits/2026-10-15-thr-1902/` (1920×1080 full-world parchment and borders, regional borders, 2560×1440 parchment), console 0 errors.
