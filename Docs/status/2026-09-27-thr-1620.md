# 2026-09-27 — THR-1620: named heroes start with a working faction standing

**Ticket:** [THR-1620](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1620) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Found by:** THR-1594 research (map THR-1589) · **Pillars:** Engine (the worldSeed membership writer). Content N/A (no quest or catalog entry changed). UI N/A (engine-only evidence per THR-688 rule C).

## What was wrong

`worldSeed.ts` gave ~70% of spotlight protagonists a starting Realm membership (`edge_member_<id>`) written as `{ role, rank: 0.3, joinedTick }` — no `reputation`, no `factionDefId`. Every standing reader keys on those two fields, so `generateFactionQuestCandidates` skipped the membership and `meetsFactionRankRequirement` read it as closed. The deciders were the only members in the world with no working standing; seeded townsfolk had one.

## What shipped

- The membership now stamps the target Realm's `factionDefId`, `reputation: SEEDED_PROTAGONIST_MEMBERSHIP_REPUTATION` (0.2, pinned to `HOLD_STANDING_REPUTATION_SEED` — a *subject* on the realm ladder, above the empty-access *stranger* rung) and `lastFactionActivityTick: 0`.
- `rank` is no longer a hand-written 0.3: it is the tier the seeded reputation lands in on the Realm's ladder, the value `getDerivedMembershipRank` derives.
- No new rng draw — same seed, same world.
- The `getDerivedMembershipRank` docblock no longer lists worldSeed among the definition-less mints.
- Out of scope, left on THR-1594: whether the membership should target the protagonist's *home* Realm rather than a random one.

## Result

Seeds 42 · 99, medium, t0: every protagonist membership carries both fields, and the court offers at least one protagonist a quest at t0. At t30 (CLI): 9 · 14 memberships, 0 missing a field. Regression test on a generated world: `worldSeed.protagonistMembership.test.ts`.
