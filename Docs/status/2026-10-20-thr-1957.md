# THR-1957 — hunger and unrest S2: the town turns

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** Unrest's top two bands now act. **On the edge** is a new place condition (enter 70, release 55, so a town that shows it also shows Lawless). Once a day a held town on the edge weighs every faction holding a settlement within 8 hexes on four things: protection (the crown's offer term over this one town), standing (−1 for a siege by that lord in the last 30 days, else +1 for a hall or a resident member), fed (the share of that lord's towns not hungry) and solvent (not Destitute). If the best lord beats its holder by the margin, the town turns. `controls` moves (`via: 'defection'`, `takenFrom`), the old holder's grudge drops past the enemy line, and it takes up a revenge ambition naming the lord and the town. War aims now aims a named revenge at that lord. At unrest 90 a held town **rises**: the holder's resident members split off through the shared splinter writes (new `resident_of` selector), or the highest-ranked resident founds a one-town free town. The new faction takes the town and is `hostile_to` its old holder both ways. A 20-day cooldown and a relief push follow every turn.

On screen: the On the edge chip, the Fortunes row ("Ferris is struggling, and it is on the edge of turning from House Ashfold with hunger.", with the holder as a link), four chronicle lines (on the edge, defects, rises, empties), an event popup only when a threaded mortal lives there or the god holds a source there, and the Trouble lens **Hunger** mark plus On the edge hover words. `__DEBUG.getTownView` shows each lord's view parts.

Seeds 42 and 7 were run at the plan's margin of 0.25, seed 99 at 0.4. At 0.25 one seed-99 town turned 3 times in 100 days, so the plan's kill rule raised `DEFECTION_MARGIN` to 0.4. Every turn moved `controls` and left a chronicle line for a full-detail town. 0 towns at 70+ were driven by grief alone. 0 leave-rule violations.
