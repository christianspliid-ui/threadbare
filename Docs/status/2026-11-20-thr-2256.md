# THR-2256 — The Doom panel shows where it feeds

**Shipped 2026-11-20.** Slice 2 of 2 of plan `Docs/plans/2026-11-19-thr-2247-dooms-last-stage-is-a-stand.md` (UI; slice 1 was THR-2255).

- **The view model.** `src/engine/views/doomFeedings.ts` (pure) reads `GameState.doomFeedings` once: `selectDoomFeedings` (rows newest first, at most `DOOM_FEED_LIST_MAX`), `doomFeedHoverLine` and `doomFedLead`. Each row is "fed here once" / "fed here twice" (two feedings; added beyond the plan table so the row never reads a false "once") / "fed here often" (`DOOM_FEED_OFTEN_COUNT`) / "held" (tended now) / "no longer held" (the hold ended inside the window).
- **Doom panel.** A **Where it feeds** section under the Stay line. Each place is a link that closes the panel and opens the place page. A Rebuke button shows only while the god holds Rebuke and a Rebuke there answers the Doom (UI Law 25). When the Doom has fed nowhere lately it reads "The doom has not fed anywhere lately."
- **Doom bar hover.** One added line: "It feeds on failures at {place} and two other places."
- **Place page.** A new `doom_fed` lead row (order 8.5, beside the rival-pressed lead): headline "The doom fed here.", then "It fed when {name} failed." (the name links, Law 21; a highlight headline is a plain string) with "Rebuke it here, or tend it." (Rebuke held), or "Ground you tend here starves it." (not held). A rival's landing on the same place keeps the lead, as it keeps Rebuke's target.
- **Alert.** The last-stage `doom_feeding` alert now opens its place (`deriveNavigationTarget`).
- **Debug.** `await window.__DEBUG.getDoomFeedings()` returns the view, the hover line and the raw record.
- **Not done here:** the Rebuke card face's doom variant (`DOOM_REBUKE_CARD_FACE`). The Act row's card VM carries no face text, so the line has no surface yet. Filed as THR-2268 (Deferral).
- **Viewport (UI Law 33), filed as THR-2269.** At Doom stage 5 the dialog scrolls at 1920×1080: content 1484px against a 745px body. Without this section it is still 1318px. Resolved events are shown twice, in their stage and again in Recent consequences. So the Done-when line "nothing scrolls with three rows" cannot hold until THR-2269 removes that duplication.
