# THR-1473 — the chip sentence gets a budget, and the slice gets cut to it

**Shipped 2026-09-12.** Director feedback, attended sitting on the THR-1220 slice:
*"for both scars the flavour text is way too long. I suggest we keep it to max 15
words, and a test that it does not communicate any of the same info as the
aftermath."*

## What was wrong

The chip sentence — `causeClause` + `detail`, drawn beside the `CATEGORY · NOUN`
tag — was **the one player-facing prose field with no row in `NUDGE_WORD_BUDGETS`**.
The opening, factor lines, band prose, effect lines and card names were all priced;
this one was not, so no check ever reported it and the corpus grew to paragraph
length. Measured across the vertical slice at filing: 56 categorised chips, median
**31 words**, 47 over 15.

Length was only half of it. Nothing compared a chip's words with the band
`overview` it renders *under*, and spec rule 1 ("cause → change, in one sentence")
actively invited re-telling the cause the overview had just told. The scar the
director was reading spent 31 words saying, again, what the paragraph above it had
already said about the fire and the kindling.

## What shipped

**Doctrine.** `nudge-authoring-spec.md` § *Word budgets* gains a chip row — both
fields summed, because the surface draws them as one sentence and pricing them
apart would admit 15 + 15 as a lawful 30-word chip. Consequence Draw gains rule
**1b**: the change *is* the sentence, the cause earns one short clause and only when
the reader could not otherwise tell which beat produced the state, and no fact the
overview carries may appear twice. `encounter-pipeline` SKILL.md carries it as
REVISE trigger 33.

**Gate.** `NUDGE_WORD_BUDGETS.chipSentence = 15` and
`CHIP_OVERVIEW_OVERLAP_RUN_WORDS = 4`, read by `chipSentenceProblems` in
`doctrineV2Checks.ts` and reported through the existing `check:encounter` warn
channel. The overlap arm reports any four-word run a chip shares with its band's
overview — a **lower bound on retelling, never a proof of its absence**, which is
why it warns rather than gates and why THR-1474's whole-page read is still owed.

The check reuses `compositionContract`'s `aftermathFaces` walk (now exported, and
now carrying each face's resolved `overview`) rather than re-deriving the
variant × band resolution: a second walk over the same config is a second answer
waiting to drift from the first.

**Content.** All 53 over-budget chips in the vertical slice re-authored, plus 7 that
were inside the budget and still repeating a clause of their overview. The slice is
clean on both arms. The retrofit corpus still reports 103 over-budget and 22
overlapping chips — warn-level by the clamp-follows-the-corpus rule, promoted to
error when that corpus is drained under its own ticket.

## One thing worth carrying forward

The first re-authoring pass landed **15 chips on exactly 16 words**, every one of
them 15 words of prose plus the em-dash the surface draws between the fields. The
counter was measuring the joined string, so a budget of 15 was silently a budget of
14. `chipSentenceWordCount` now counts per field and sums, and a test pins it.
A word budget that counts its own separator is a rule nobody agreed to.
