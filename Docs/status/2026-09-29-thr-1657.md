# 2026-09-29 — THR-1657: the past feeds ambitions

**Ticket:** [THR-1657](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1657) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-28-thr-1631-world-with-a-past.md` § S3 · **Pillars:** Engine (the mint); Content N/A (ambition templates and grievance prose already exist); UI N/A (past drives surface on the existing sheet's intent line, "… after the death of *name*" / "Because of the tale of *name*, who found *wonder*").

## What was wrong

S1 wrote a past onto the graph at worldgen and S2 showed it to the player, but nobody alive cared about it. The fallen commanders had no kin and the wonders' finders had no heirs to their legends. At t0 the past gave no one a reason to act.

## What shipped

- **`src/engine/worldPastAmbitions.ts` — `mintPastAmbitions`.** It runs at the tail of `seedWorldPast`, injected by `gameInit` through `SeedWorldPastOptions.mintAmbitions`. It lives in its own module because `worldPast.ts` is read low in the import graph (words, enrichment, the debug bridge). Importing the ambition, grievance and faction chain there closed a cycle that failed 56 test files with TDZ errors.
  - **Revenge.** For each war in living memory, the heir is the losing Realm's member protagonist with the most standing (reputation, then id) and a free slot. The heir becomes **kin** of the fallen commander (`relates_to` basis `kin`, `origin: 'worldgen'`, both directions). The commander's death then opens as a grievance through `resolveGrievanceDisposition`, whose succession passes a dead victim's drive to their strongest bond, which is the same route a run-time death takes. The culprit is the winning Realm's current leader (`getFactionLeaderId`), and the harm is a named death (1.0).
  - **The wonder.** For each wonder with a finder, the nearest protagonist within 12 hexes who has a free slot gets `ambition_chase_the_wonder`, with `targetNodeId` set to the wonder.
  - Holders are living deciders only, with `skipSpotlightPull: true`, and each holder gets one past ambition. Both caps are 2. The mint is drawless and deterministic. Every source that mints nothing is named with its reason in `world_past_seeded.ambitions` (`no_member_protagonist`, `no_free_slot`, `none_in_range`, `no_leader`, …).
- **Constants:** `revengeAmbitionsMax`, `wonderAmbitionsMax`, `wonderPullMaxHexes`, `revengeHarmMagnitude` and `revengeChainDepth` are in the `WorldPastConstants` bag.
- **Interface row** `world-past-mints-ambitions` is registered (LIVE).
- **Census reader** `readers/past.ts` now reports the minted and skipped ambitions and the t0 decider headcount.
