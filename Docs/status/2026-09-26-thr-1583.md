# 2026-09-26 — THR-1583: The sheet and the skill line say the same word for a mortal's reach

**Ticket:** [THR-1583](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1583) (forecast window S5; also closes the parent THR-1575) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-24-thr-1575-forecast-window.md` § UI pillar, § Slices S5 · **Pillars:** Engine + UI. Content N/A: the word scales are unchanged.

## What shipped

1. **One capability-to-word read.** `getCapabilityTier` / `getCapabilityWord` (`src/data/domain-words.ts`) map a `computeCapability` value (0–1, the dice curve with traits and items) to the reach tier through `CAPABILITY_TO_DOMAIN_SCALE`. The encounter skill line (`deriveSkillLine`) now calls this shared helper instead of its private copy.
2. **The sheet reads the dice curve.** `AgentDetail.reachCapabilities` holds all eight reaches from `computeCapability`, fail-soft per reach. The info card's `domains` (Prowess tab, thread detail, info card) take their word and tier from it. Before, the raw seeded `domainCapabilities` (10–40+) were clamped by a 0–10 function, so every mortal showed the top word in every reach.
3. **The other player-facing reach words follow.** The agent tooltip (`tooltipResolver`) and the encounter cast reference (`narrativeLinker`) read the same helper. FactionSheet shows a number, not a word, so it is out of scope.

## Evidence

- `reachWordAgreement.test.ts` builds a generated small world on seed 42. For every mortal and every reach, the sheet word matches the skill line and the tier matches `getCapabilityTier`. A spotlight protagonist shows ≥ 3 distinct words, and fewer than 10% of mortals read the top word everywhere. Against the pre-fix `agentDetail.ts`, the agreement and clamp arms both fail.
- Prowess tab at 1920×1080: `Docs/evidence/thr-1583/prowess-tab-1920x1080.png`. Kael Thornweaver reads Formidable / Bartering / Unseen / Attuned / Revered / Perceptive / Handy / Destined, and the pips vary by tier.
