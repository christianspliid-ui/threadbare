### 2026-10-05 — THR-1716: the world arrives with its first beat already open

**What was wrong.** Beat 0 ("Reach Down") is due on turn 0, but beats are only offered inside a tick, and the world arrives paused. So the first story beat waited behind a Play button nothing pointed at. Round-2 testers lost about 8 clicks before it started. The remembrance also took two clicks per picture (zoom, then confirm).

**Engine.** `offerArrivalSpineBeat` (`ascendantBeat.ts`) offers the opening beat at tick 0, reusing `forceOfferBeatById`. It runs only when the cursor is 0, nothing is pending and The First is unbonded. It draws no PRNG and traces `beat.arrival_offer`. It is called from `useSimulation`'s initial state, never from `initializeGameState`, so the CLI and fixtures are unchanged (the CLI smoke and `test:heavy` pass). Pre-bonded dev routes (`?seeded`) are a no-op.

**UI.** The arrival beat enters itself, with no offer banner. The bond's *"Let them walk."* starts time the first time the clock runs (`runIfHeld` on `useInterruptAutoPause`, through `releaseClockOnBond`). After that, resume-to-prior is unchanged. Until the clock first runs, the Play control pulses (a static ring under reduced motion) and the status line reads *"Time is still. Press Play or Space to let the world move."* (`FIRST_RUN_PROMPT_DELAY_MS` 1500). All four remembrance picture screens now choose in one click. Stirring and drive hold with "Choose again" and Escape. Origin and transformation keep their Continue, and "Choose again" stays available until Continue. Arrow keys move a focus ring along the row. The four timings are named constants. `getOpeningState()` gains `arrivalBeatOffered` and `clockEverRan`.

**Content.** `ui.sim_first_run` tooltip, the prompt caption, "Choose again".

**Seen in the browser** (worktree Vite, 1920×1080). On `?view=game&firstunmet`, the "Reach Down" modal is open with no tick and no click (`arrivalBeatOffered: true`). The meeting played to the bond, and "Let them walk." left the clock running (`running: true`, `clockEverRan: true`). On `?view=game`, the prompt shows after Reach Down, and Space clears it. On `?seeded` nothing changed. From New World, one click shows the chosen picture with "Choose again".
