# THR-1843 — Echoes stand in the new world

**Shipped 2026-10-25.** Slice 2 of [The world remembers](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1813). Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1813-the-world-remembers.md` (E7, Decided 1–3 and 5–7).

**What changed.**
- **Engine.** `seedEchoes` places each live map echo of the account at world creation, in Decided 5's order (divine first, then strength × significance), at most `MAX_SEEDED_ECHOES` (6). It runs after the seed snapshot, so neglect fades a kin monument's lift, and before the faction aggregates. A throw starts the world with no echoes.
  - **Monument** — a named `ruins` sublocation on the place that best matches the echo's spheres among those reading Foreign for the new god (not the start, the seat, a lair or a nest). Its host is lifted until the new god reads Held (still warm) or Touched (fading) when kin, Hostile when opposed; unrelated gods get a named landmark only.
  - **Legacy** — the wealthiest ambient resident of the best-matching settlement, promoted to notable, stamped `echoLineageOf`, lifted in the echo's spheres, with one kin tie.
  - **Relic** — an unborne artifact named after the old one, at the best-matching ruin or wild place; it reads Foreign until someone takes it up.
- **UI.** *Before you woke* opens with *Earlier worlds*: "The Long Barrow still stands near Silver Patch." · "The line of Maren Ashfold lives on in Shuuwhu." · "The Hollow Bell lies somewhere near Iron Glade, unclaimed." Every name links. The heir's sheet reads "Of the line of Maren Ashfold".
- **Debug.** `await window.__DEBUG.getSeededEchoes()`.

**Decisions made in the build.**
- **Whole points, not half points.** Sphere scores are integers (neglect walks them down a point at a time), so the plan's 0.5 step is a *mean* per sphere: each step deals one point round-robin to the least-lifted echo sphere.
- **No monument or relic in a monster's home** (`ECHO_EXCLUDED_SUBTYPES`: lair, nest). The first browser pass put both in a lair, which is not the plan's "ordinary ground".
- **The 300-tick neglect test runs in the heavy lane** (`seedEchoes.neglect.test.ts`); the placement tests stay on the PR gate.

**Measured** (seeds 42 / 99 / 7, medium): kin god → still-warm host Held, fading host Touched; opposed god → Hostile; after 300 untended ticks the kin host falls at least one band and the opposed one still reads Hostile. Tilt census: `Docs/audits/2026-10-25-thr-1843-echo-tilt-census.md` — +1 Touched-or-better place, all of it the monument.

**Found and filed.** The account's tilt changes the cosmology profile but no place's sphere scores: [The world tilt never reaches a place](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2028).
