### 2026-10-04 — THR-1725: no stock "thread" filler on any encounter surface

**The defect.** The Unsafe Bridge showed the factor line "The threads are shifting." (Christian: "makes no sense"). It was the encounter contract's stock forecast factor, minted for every beat with nothing authored by `buildLiteEncounterContract`, by the adapter's fallback contract, and by 23 per-encounter copies of the builder under `src/data/encounters/*`. THR-1724 already hid it on the stage; this removes it at source so no surface (the hand-filter forecast, the contract metadata) carries it.

**The change.**
- `DEFAULT_FORECAST_FACTORS` is `[]` (builder + 23 copies); the adapter's two fallback beats carry `[]`. The contract schema accepts zero factor lines and `EncounterForecastFactors` is `readonly string[]` (the 0–3 bound stays in the schema).
- Sibling defaults, audited. None of them is rendered by a designed surface — production reads only the contract's `forecast_factors`, `ascendant_hand_filter`, `place` and `items_relevant`; the contract→template direction runs only in the worked-example round trip. They did ride in the serialized contract, which leaks into the illustration's alt text (filed as THR-1733). Verdict for each: **drop and replace with the template's own authored text.**
  - `DEFAULT_AGENT_REACTION` ('the moment shifts') → `choice.intent`, else `choice.label`; the fallback choice uses the step's `successAfterimage`, else `narrativeTemplates.success`.
  - `DEFAULT_FALL_FORWARD` ('the threads tighten') → `choice.likelyBurden` / the step's `failureAfterimage`, else `narrativeTemplates.failure`.
  - `DEFAULT_INITIATION_PROSE` / `DEFAULT_SUCCESS_PROSE` / `DEFAULT_FAILURE_PROSE` → removed. `getNarrativeText` already read the template's narrative templates first; its last resort is now `template.description`, else `template.name`. The name is used only because the schema needs a non-empty string.

**Evidence.** `src/data/__tests__/thr1725-no-thread-filler.test.ts` sweeps every shipped template (UNIFIED_ACTION_TEMPLATES ∪ ENCOUNTER_TEMPLATES ∪ the `src/data/encounters/*` exports) through the same contract read the stage and hand filter use. The output was `[THR-1725 sweep] templates=744 contracts=733 strings=74353 hits=0`. The test also checks the player-shown forecast factors, and that a beat with nothing authored carries no factor line. Eleven templates yield no contract: they are non-encounter actions whose reach the schema rejects, and the surfaces already swallow that case. `npm run gate` is green.
