# THR-2090 — Spirit, life and entropy get a cheap place card, and doom's tide stops erasing a life god (slice 2)

**Shipped 2026-10-31.** Thematic Pressure & Living World. Plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-31-thr-2090-spirit-life-entropy-place-cards.md`.

**Three tier-1 market town cards at 2 essence** (settlement / hamlet / town / city / capital):

- **Tend the Sick** (`loc.tend_sick`, *The Fever Breaks*, life): `populationHealth +LOC_TEND_SICK_HEALTH_DELTA` (10).
- **Let the Damp In** (`loc.let_damp_in`, *Cough in the Walls*, entropy): `populationHealth −LOC_LET_DAMP_IN_HEALTH_DELTA` (8). Sicken the Wells stays the fast, loud tier-2 form.
- **Keep the Vigil** (`loc.keep_vigil`, *Lamps for the Dead*, spirit): `unrest −LOC_KEEP_VIGIL_UNREST_DELTA` (4).

**Stoke the Forges** drops from 3 to 2 essence. `npm run check:god-card-progression` now reports no `hole: no cheap <sphere> place card` for any sphere.

**Doom's tide is no longer Mandate opposition (Decision 1).** `phaseDoom` builds its per-stage entropy push's `sourceId` from `DOOM_STAGE_PUSH_SOURCE_PREFIX` (`doom-tier-`). `isMandateOpposition` (`mandateLedger.ts`) leaves that push out of the erosion debit's numerator, behind `MANDATE_DOOM_STAGE_PUSH_EXEMPT` (`false` restores the THR-2081 ledger). The push still lands and darkens the world. Doom's archetype stage cards and rivals still take back what the god built.

**Executor decisions.**
- The plan's spell name *The Cough in the Walls* broke the four-word rule; it shipped as *Cough in the Walls*.
- Keep the Vigil carries `answers_prayer`: the THR-1935 completeness test requires every unrest-calming town card to answer a prayer or name a reason, and the exemption list is empty.
- **Review gate, round 1 (high):** nothing seeds `populationHealth`, and `applyNodeChanges` counted an absent value as 0, while every reader assumes 80. So Tend the Sick wrote 10 on a fresh town (making it sick), and Let the Damp In wrote −8. `graphOpExecutor.ts` now starts a relative change from `RELATIVE_CHANGE_ABSENT_DEFAULTS` (populationHealth → `LOC_HEALTH_DEFAULT_BASELINE`). The fix also covers the same latent fault in Bless the Harvest, Blight and Sicken the Wells. A test pins the absent-property shape.
