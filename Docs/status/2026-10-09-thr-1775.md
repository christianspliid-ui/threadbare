# 2026-10-09 — THR-1775: a god casts better in its own reaches again

**Ticket:** [THR-1775](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1775) · **Source:** `Docs/audits/2026-10-06-thr-1772-starting-god-stats-and-cross-run-persistence-research.md` § 1

The THR-1581 dice re-fit moved the capability sigmoid from midpoint 10, k 0.4 to midpoint 30, k 0.08. That left a fresh god's player cast in the curve's flat low tail: capability 0.128 off-domain, 0.137 secondary, 0.152 primary. Its own reaches cast barely better than any other.

The re-fit is a linear rescale of the raw axis (`y = 5x − 20`), so `ASCENDANT_CAST_BASE_RAW` goes from 6 to 10 and `ASCENDANT_CAST_AFFINITY_WEIGHT` from 0.5 to 2.5. That restores the THR-766 capabilities exactly: 0.168 / 0.231 / 0.354.

At-cost stays dominant, about one primary cast in three now lands clean, surges stay rare, and casts never fail. A primary reach lands clean about three times as often as an off-domain one, where before it was 1.4 times.

- **Pins:** `playerCastBalance.test.ts` pins 10 / 2.5 and the three capabilities, and requires primary ≥ 2× off-domain on both capability and clean share. The at-cost ceiling goes back to THR-766's 0.75 and the clean floor rises to 0.20, so the next drift into the tail fails a test.
- **Golden fixture:** `stepResolutionGolden.test.ts` re-pins its three priced `player/*` rows. Capability goes 0.147 → 0.310 and the regional row's P 0.05 → 0.163. Every `roll` and every band is unchanged.
- **Card words:** the forecast-tier truthfulness and spread checks on the live slot list now run at a real fresh god's capabilities (0.354 / 0.231). They used to run at a hard-coded 0.55 / 0.40, which matches no fresh god on any curve. Both pass.
- **Known consequence (THR-1580's job):** `reachPractice`, about 0.4 raw per action, now walks a raw axis five times wider, so the Deepening arc moves the dice about five times more slowly than THR-613 tuned it to.
