# One card, one router — slice 1 (THR-1490)

**2026-09-12 · shipped** — `Docs/plans/2026-09-12-thr-1482-one-card-one-router.md`, slice 1 of 3.

Anything the game names now opens the same way. One router dispatches on `WorldRefKind`;
one registry says what each kind opens; a kind with no row is a build failure.

## What changed

**The registry.** `src/data/surface-registry.ts` maps every `WorldRefKind` to the card it
opens and the sheet it reaches, as a `Record` that is total *by type*. Three rows withhold
a sheet and each quotes the ruling that withheld it. Every row has a card: there is no
longer such a thing as a kind you cannot look at.

**The router.** `useRefRouter` is the only thing in the tree permitted to push onto the
detail stack. The three routers that opened things before — `useNotificationNavigation`
(8 arms), the thread panel's `ThreadCategory` switch (5), `EncounterVeil.openEntity` (7) —
are adapters over it now, each with a coverage test falsified by removing one mapping.
Nothing lost an arm; the veil gained every kind it had never been able to name.

**The mount (THR-966, open since 2026-08-02, resolved as *mount*).** The detail-page
cluster — built in THR-301 Phase E with a generator, resolvers, fallback prose,
breadcrumbs and snapshot tests, and imported by nothing but the style guide ever since —
is live in `GameView`. `ProseTtsButton` rides the card body, which was THR-966's own
Done-when.

**New surfaces.** A `group` page kind, because an Army is neither a person nor a place nor
a thing nor a happening — it is a body of people with a commander and a stance, and asking
the actor resolvers for its portrait and disposition would have produced a card about a
column of soldiers' feelings. A `HoverCard` at Law 20's Tier 1½: the glance between a
tooltip and a card, after a dwell, never stacked and never interactive.

## Three decisions the ticket left open, and how they went

1. **Scope item 1's `via` list did not cover the rows its own pin would hit** — the
   orchestrator measured this before pickup: seven `worldRef: null` rows were absent from
   the list and one row in the list did not need the column. Resolved by making the pin
   *reachability* rather than *column presence*: every kind resolves through `worldRef`,
   through a `via` chain that must terminate and must not cycle, or through `contentKind`.
   Three rows reach nothing and say so in a new `noSurface` field — Sphere and Reach are
   axes rather than objects (both rows said so already), and the cosmology node is never
   minted. The test holds an **allowlist of exactly those three ids**, so a fourth is a
   test edit someone has to argue for rather than a sentence someone can write.

2. **`EntityLink`'s new prop is `entityRef`, not `ref`.** The plan and the ticket both
   write `ref`; that name is React's. React 19 hands an unclaimed `ref` to a function
   component as an ordinary prop, so it would have *worked* — which is the problem: the
   collision is silent rather than loud, it costs the component the ability to ever take a
   DOM ref, and every reader has to stop and decide which `ref` is meant. The agreed shape
   (one prop, a `WorldRef`, routing by kind) is unchanged.

3. **The traces are `ui_ref_opened` / `ui_ref_unroutable`, not `ui.ref_opened`.** The
   union they join is ~200 members of unbroken snake_case, and `TRACE_CATEGORIES` seeds
   the DebugPanel's filter list off it. Two dotted members would be the only dotted
   members in the inspector and in every grep over it.

## What the gates caught

Four existing guards went red on this change, and each was right to:

- The **anchor catalog** refused to publish `artifact`, `attachment` and `army` as
  "absent with a reason" once their arms shipped — a curated absence outliving the absence
  it describes is the exact drift that generator exists to prevent.
- The **orphaned-payload ratchet** refused two trace interfaces declared but not joined to
  `TraceEntry`, which sent them into the union properly and removed the `as unknown as`
  casts the cast ratchet then also refused.
- `worldRef.test.ts` asserted the old contract as if it were a ruling. It was not:
  `ArtifactSheet`, `AttachmentDetailView` and `ArmySheet` were all mounted, and the only
  thing missing was a `NavigationTarget` arm to name them by.
- The **jsdom render evidence** caught a defect in this change before it shipped: the
  narration button rendered on no *actor* card, the most common card in the game, because
  an actor page leads with a `portrait` section whose paragraph lives in `bodyProse` and
  the first filter took `kind === 'prose'` only. Now swept across all six card kinds.
