# 2026-10-11 — THR-1580 (slice 2): the reputation walk weighs Shadow and Heart by how much each agent holds

**Ticket:** [THR-1580](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1580) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-24-thr-1575-forecast-window.md` § Systems design 4 · multi-PR deferral, ticket stays open.

- **The readers.** `perceiveReputation` (`src/engine/reputationWalk.ts`) judges a stranger through mutual acquaintances. Each intermediary on the best path adds `shadow × 0.15` distortion, and the source damps it by `1 − heart × 0.3`. Both are magnitudes of how much Shadow or Heart an agent holds, not d100 chances. Both read the pre-refit curve (midpoint 10, k 0.4), which saturates at raw ~20.
- **Who the walk reads.** Only agents with a `relates_to` edge take part (56 · 69 · 68 per world). About a fifth of them sit past raw 20 in Shadow or Heart (17.9–23.5%, seeds 42 · 99 · 7), and the pre-refit curve read every one of them as ~1.0.
- **Measured** (`npm run measure:reputation-walk-scales`, every ordered pair with no direct edge but an indirect path, 281 · 705 · 528 pairs, medium, tick 0). The figures are the size of the move away from the capability-free walk. Mean |shift|: pre-refit 0.143 · 0.159 · 0.146, reach share 0.095 · 0.132 · 0.102, dice 0.065 · 0.101 · 0.071. Pairs whose ally/neutral/enemy reading at ±0.3 changes: pre-refit 12.1 · 22.3 · 9.1%, reach share 4.3 · 14.2 · 2.1%, dice 2.5 · 5.5 · 1.5%.
- **Change.** Both readers use `computeReachShare`, through an optional `readCapability` parameter that only the measurement script sets. Constants are unchanged. The old magnitude came from the curve saturating, not from tuning, so `SHADOW_DISTORTION_FACTOR` stays the lever. Two `TODO(THR-1580)` sites are gone. `reputationWalk.ts` is now a read site of `capability-thresholds-read-the-reach-share`. Two new tests check that raw 20 and raw 40 Shadow distort by half and by full measure, and that raw 20 Heart resists by half.
