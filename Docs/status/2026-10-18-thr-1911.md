# THR-1911 — the Inspector in the game

**Shipped 2026-10-18.** Slice S3 of the Inspector plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1876-the-inspector.md`). Every link, card and map click now opens one panel beside the map. `InspectorHost` sits between the map and the Ledger rail, and the rail folds to its strip while it is open (`inspectorOpen`). It reads a new `inspector` slice of `GameScreenState`: history, size and pin, with pure transitions in `inspector/inspectorState.ts`.

- **Sizes.** Docked and wide sit beside the map. Full covers the map region and holds the clock as "the full view". A pause-tier interrupt steps Full down to docked. `W` cycles the size; Esc steps one size down through the escape stack.
- **Moving.** Back and forward, Alt+← and Alt+→, a trail, the pin, and the eye (`focusOn`).
- **Router.** `useRefRouter` gains `openInspector`. A `card` open lands at the registry's new `opensAt` (factions and past events open wide). A world `sheet` open lands wide; a live encounter still asks the Stage first.
- **Map.** Open land opens the land page. Agents and armies open themselves. Place labels are clickable: map places carry their node id now.
- **The Act row casts in two beats** (Law 48) through one path, `useCastOnTarget` with `TARGET_CONTEXT_BUILDERS`. The sublocation builder sets `subtype: 'sublocation'` and keeps its category for the subtype gate, so Place and artifact cards are castable. Meet The First joins a place's Act row.
- **Retired from the screen.** Both `ActionDrawer` mounts and the detail column. Callers of the stub sheets, the god's sheet, the attachment sheet, the profile, and the `location` and `hex-zoom` views keep their calls and reach the Inspector. The S3 copy rows changed, and `InspectorFull` joined `GIFT_HOLD_SURFACES`.

Browser evidence (bash-vite, Playwright; 1920×1080 and 2560×1440):
- `openInInspector('faction', …)` reports size `wide`.
- The Bone Coast → Temple Quarter → Remy → Congregation chain walks in four clicks, and Back returns step by step.
- Full reads "held by the full view · stays paused".
- The page never scrolls.
