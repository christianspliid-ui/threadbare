# THR-2047 — The Toll Dispute fires: its trigger reads the shared enemy rule

**Shipped 2026-10-26.** Engine only (`src/engine/phases/routeEvents.ts`). No content or UI change.

**Why.** A lane plants a toll-dispute seed when the factions controlling its two ends have a quarrel. The test read only a `hostile_to` edge between them, and generated factions never carry one: their quarrels live on `relates_to` (`isRival`, `basis: 'war'`, sour `sentiment`). The lane-event census found 0 toll seeds and 0 resolved disputes on seeds 42 / 99 / 7. THR-1922 found the same gap for armies and wrote `isEnemyFaction` (`src/engine/warAims/enemy.ts`) to close it.

**What changed.** The toll trigger now asks `isEnemyFaction` of the two endpoint controllers: `hostile_to` either way, rivalry, declared war, a monster faction, or sentiment below the rivalry line unless allied. Precedence (ambush > embargo > toll), chance (`ROUTE_TOLL_CHANCE` 0.25) and the per-scan cap are unchanged.

**Measured** (lane-event census from `proto/thr-2046-lane-census`, medium, 360 ticks, one seed per process):

| Seed | Toll seeds before → after | Toll disputes resolved before → after | Ambush seeds after |
|---|---|---|---|
| 42 | 0 → 19 | 0 → 19 | 8 |
| 99 | 0 → 22 | 0 → 22 | 16 |
| 7 | 0 → 8 | 0 → 8 | 18 |

Two to five lanes per world have enemy controllers from about day 10 on (topology probe). The toll is now about as frequent as the ambush. Its outcome rows (a toll for the town's faction, a thinner road) come with THR-2046.
