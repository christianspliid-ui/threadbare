# THR-1392 slice 2 — cells on the board, and prose that knows what it is about

**2026-09-03 · engine + content.** A verb × object cell is now a thing the board can offer and the sheet can describe, behind the same flag slice 1 landed. `src/data/undertaking-cells.ts` synthesises one `StrategicActionTemplate` per (verb variant, object type) the registry declares a semantic for — twenty-odd cells, none authored per cell: the object target rule under the variant's ownership rule, the motive gate and harm class on the two counter-play variants only (`undo`, `control:seize`), difficulty / payoff / length from the verb tables, two value pairs per verb for the desire signal, and the verb line-sets for prose. `control` is two cells, claim and seize, because they differ in exactly what a template carries; both dispatch the `control` verb and the resolver re-reads ownership at completion, so the two agree by construction. `control × settlement`'s claim cell is the sustained `claim_control` mode; `use × mark` is instant; the rest are checkpointed projects.

**The walk.** `generateStrategicCandidates` reads an ambition's work ids through one helper, `profileWorkIds(profile, model)`: on `templates` it is the profile's `templateIds`, on `cells` the profile's new `cells` list *ahead of* them (cells lead so the per-ambition cap cannot starve them — the THR-1388 ordering lesson). Twelve profiled ambitions carry a `cells` list chosen by what each is about. A cell with no object of its type in the world is refused as `no_object_in_range:<type>` and traced `undertaking_cell_unreachable` **once per world per cell** (`no_object_exists` or `no_owned_object`, the two worlds a designer wants told apart); the candidate carries the object's tier read at proposal, and the board and the checkpoints scale a cell's payoff and difficulty on that tier instead of the template's T2 default. A `control` cell never offers what the actor already holds. The three other readers of `templateIds` (kind reachability, the calling, the reverse lookup) go through the same helper.

**The words.** `resolveUndertakingProse(line, ctx)` in `src/engine/undertakingProse.ts` is the one place the four slots are filled — `{object}` from the object's own name (a mark reads *the hold on Old Maerin*), `{owner}` from its holder, `{actor}`, `{place}` resolved to the settlement a room sits in — and it returns the concepts it named so a surface can chip them (Law 1). A slot the world cannot fill takes a neutral word and an unknown token is stripped; a raw `{token}` never reaches a screen (Law 43). `src/data/undertaking-verb-prose.ts` carries four activity lines, four completion lines and one GM narration per variant in the encounter register. Lines are chosen deterministically per project. The sheet's activity line and the completion moment's message are composed this way for a cell; templates render as before. `STRATEGIC_PROSE_TOKENS`, empty since the contract was written, now names the tokens, so the contract's `tokens` block checks them. A cell with no kind row christens from the family its object type names (`WORK_KIND_FAMILY` gained the seven types) and takes a per-type noun (`OBJECT_TYPE_NOUNS`) when the created node has no more specific one.

**Nothing player-visible changes.** `UNDERTAKING_MODEL` stays on `templates`; the CLI smoke at tick 30 matches the pre-change run. The CLI's `undertakings` readout shows `verb × object` and the object's name for a cell project.

## Deliberately still undeclared

The same list slice 1 recorded: `improve` × attachment / room / route / faction / mark; `use` × everything but mark; `control:seize` × company and × faction; `found` × faction and × mark; `survey` × everything (THR-1393). Each is a consequence someone decides once; none is faked. A cell override (`CELL_OVERRIDE_MAX_PER_CELL`) is the slice-3/4 surface for the shipped prose worth keeping.
