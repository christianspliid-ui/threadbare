# THR-1934 S1 — unrest is pressure, and a hungry town says so

**Shipped 2026-10-17.** Unrest no longer decays by a flat 1 a tick. Once a day every settlement's target is summed from what it lives through — hunger (10 on the first hungry day, +3 a day, cap 55), want (below prosperity 35), a siege (30, lingering 5 days), sickness (10 below health 40) and THR-1925's death-site haunting, less contentment (−10 above prosperity 60) — and every tick unrest drifts toward it by at most 4 a day. Pushes (Incite Unrest, doom cards, complications) ride on top and fade. A new **Restless** trait (enter 30, release 15) makes a town lose a person every 3 days to the nearest fed, calm town within 6 hexes, and its most recent arrival leans that way (taking up *Flee the Ravaged Land* when hunger drives the town). **Lawless** halves a town's tax and, the day a hungry town turns, seeds a granary riot. The scarcity arc's unrest jumps retire (its unrest phase now seeds the granary riot). The place page gains a **Fortunes** row ("The Bone Coast is struggling, and its streets have turned lawless with hunger."); Threads now speaks `getProsperityTier`'s five words ("Stable" is gone). `UNREST_PRESSURE_ENABLED = false` restores the old model byte-for-byte.

Measured (`npm run measure:unrest`, 600 ticks, medium): towns ever Restless 8 / 9 / 4 on seeds 42 / 99 / 7; Lawless peak 14% / 9.6% / 3.6% of settlements; 0 towns at 50+ held by the dead alone; 0 leave-rule violations; 0 arc jumps. Rollback: flag off matches main in every settlement's unrest at tick 120 and 300 (seeds 42, 99). Population moving waits on the settlement economy (THR-1923); On the edge and Rising are S2 (THR-1957).
