# 2026-09-22 — THR-1348: attention follows ambition — a strategic ambition pulls its holder into the spotlight

**Ticket:** [THR-1348](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1348) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** Engine, Content (one ambition template repaired, two mint sites stamped, one chronicle line, rulebook + UL + canon), UI N/A (no component edit — the tier split already surfaces a pulled mortal; evidence by CLI census and a `hexMapAgentVisibility` assertion).

## What was wrong

Only spotlight mortals run the decision loop, so an ambition held at notable or ambient
tier was *silenced*: never carried to the board, never refused, never traced.
`npm run census:reachability` measured the whole `merchant-expansion` family unreachable on
eleven seeds in twelve — the route economy had been validated on the one seed where the
trade ambition happened to land on a spotlight mortal. `ambition_forge_legend` had zero
holders of any tier on every seed for a different reason: its `requiredTraits:
['master_smith']` gate named a trait only a tier-4 cursed artifact grants, which
`seedPossessions` never deals, so eligibility rejected everyone before scoring ran.

Christian's ruling (attended chat, 2026-09-10): reading 1 in its *attention follows ambition*
form. The aperture is the intended attention budget and is not widened; the holder moves.

## What shipped

**The pull** ([`src/engine/spotlightPull.ts`](../../src/engine/spotlightPull.ts), new).
`pullHolderIntoSpotlight` runs inside `assignAmbitionToActor` after the `pursues` edge: a
strategic-profiled ambition assigned below the spotlight promotes the holder through
`hydrateToTier` and, to hold the deciding population flat, demotes the least-recently-witnessed
spotlight mortal with no strategic ambition to notable (`demoteToTier`, new in
`npcGraduation.ts`, writes the tier and strips nothing). Never demoted: the avatar, a
followed or retinue mortal (incoming `thread` edge not `dormant`), a mortal commanding an
army (`commanded_by`) or holding a seat (`leads`, or `member_of` with `role: 'commander'` —
the seeded captains and commanders carry no ambition of their own and were the swap's first
pick on every seed; on the small map their stepping back halved the growth-paying
completions, 19 → 9, which the heavy `undertakingCapabilityGrowth.live` arm caught), a
mortal mid-encounter or mid-undertaking, one already pulled. The
order is a total order — witness tick, then `importance`, then id — never graph order.
`lastWitnessedTick` is a real timestamp now, stamped at encounter resolution on the actor, an
actor target and the bound cast. A mortal is pulled once (`spotlightPulledTick`); with nobody
to swap the pull runs net-additive up to an allowance scaled to the attention budget
(`SPOTLIGHT_AMBITION_PULL_OVERFLOW_SHARE = 0.1` of the deciding population, capped by
`SPOTLIGHT_AMBITION_PULL_MAX = 2` — one on a small world, two on a medium one), then is
refused `budget`. Five constants, one aggregate `spotlight_pull` trace per tick, the ledger
on the node, `window.__DEBUG.getSpotlightLedger()`, CLI `spotlight`.

**Why the overflow is a share and not a flat two:** the heavy `undertakingCapabilityGrowth.live`
arm (small map, seed 42, 150 ticks) reds at a flat two — the two extra deciders crowd the
checkpointed work and the growth-paying completions halve (19 → 10, floor > 10); at one they
hold (19), and on medium the census passes at two.

**Every writer routes through the one funnel.** The plan named `ambitionTick`'s two inline
`pursues` writers; the tree had five more (`worldSeed`, `gameInit`, `agentLifecycle`
births, plus the binder and the card, which already routed). All now pass
`assignAmbitionToActor` — the binder alone with `skipSpotlightPull`, because its contract
pins a support mint as an ambient face and the same person whatever tick the queue drains,
and a pull would break both — which resolves templates across all three pools and takes
`extraProperties` so the mint lane's provenance lands in the inline key order —
[`ambitionAssignment-routing.test.ts`](../../src/engine/__tests__/ambitionAssignment-routing.test.ts)
pins the edge and node bags byte-identical by `JSON.stringify`. The births routing is what
makes the pull reach anything at 40 ticks: the mint and re-evaluation lanes first fire at the
lcm of their cadences, tick 75.

**`forge_legend` repaired**: the gate is a boost (`master_smith` in `boostingTraits`), the dead
`rare_ore_secured` milestone reads `agent_reach_above stone 0.3`, the abandonment trigger is
the reach-floor mirror `agent_reach_below iron 0.3`. The `trait_grant` contract evidence and
`grantedTraitConsumers.test.ts` are repointed to the boosting side; the trait-ref ratchet
drops its one `noResourceModel` entry. Mercenary commanders stamped `'spotlight'`, dev
counterparties `'ambient'`; the `?? 'spotlight'` default is not flipped.

**The census** measures all three ambition pools, excludes the avatar, counts families on
their own denominator, and names every pull, displacement and refusal per seed;
`census:undertakings` reports pulls / swaps / refusals beside `meanAutonomousMortals`.

## Proof

`census:reachability -- --seeds 42,99,7` (40 ticks, medium): `merchant-expansion` reachable on
**2 of 3** seeds (baseline 1 of 3) — seed 99 reaches it through `born_lc_10 ←
ambition_dominate_trade`, pulled at tick 3. Pulls 2 / 2 / 2 (net-additive, the allowance);
refusals 6 / 20 / 24, all `budget`. `forge_legend` holders on 2 of 3 seeds (baseline 0 of 3),
silenced — see the deferral.

`census:undertakings` (150 ticks): seed 42 187 starts, 6.0 per mortal per 100 ticks (baseline
5.7); seed 99 194 starts, 5.2 (baseline 4.1) — **verdict PASS on both seeds**. Baseline and
the pull-off control arm both FAIL seed 99's variety gate (140 / 149 starts, the sample never
fills). `measure:tick-cost` medium steady: seed 42 79 → 85 ms, seed 99 111 → 122 ms, under the
+25 % criterion. Heavy lane green, including the small-map rider arm at 19 growth-paying
completions.

**A measurement lesson worth the line:** the first landing arm handed the births stream to
the pull and read seed 42's undertaking share at 37.7 % — a false regression over the 35 %
ceiling, because every draw after a pull had moved. The pull now always draws from a stream
derived from `(seed, tick, actorId)`, so a world with the pull differs from one without it only
where a pull ran, and the before/after census attributes to the pull rather than to a shifted
stream.

**Deferred:** [THR-1523](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1523) — every base ambition
template is strategic, so the swap pool is the handful of ambition-less seeded mortals and
every newborn's strategic want after the first few is refused `budget` (~220 per 150 ticks
per seed). The refusals are the plan's own fail-soft, reported honestly; whether that is the
intended shape is a design fork with four readings, filed to `Todo`.
