# 2026-09-26 — THR-1567: the wayside-only slice scenes reach the board

**Ticket:** [THR-1567](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1567) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** none (follows the THR-1524 / THR-1526 widening pattern) · **Pillars:** Content. Engine N/A (no engine change). UI N/A (`Browser-verify exempt: content only`, per the Done-when).

## What was wrong

Three vertical-slice encounters registered only at `wayside` (camp · oasis · wilderness). That is 8 of 974 places on a seed-42 medium world, and all three fired zero or near-zero times in 200 ticks.

## What shipped

- **The Unsafe Bridge** now registers at wayside + rural. New rural opening: *"{name} reaches the toll bridge on the lane out of {location}."* Its cast composes both classes' default casts, plus the materialized toll keeper.
- **Riders Behind the Caravan** now registers at wayside + rural. New rural opening: *"{name} falls in with a caravan as it passes through {location}."* This line is what makes the traveler "last to join" in the spine. Its cast is composed the same way as the Bridge's.
- **Snow on the Pass stays wayside-only on purpose.** The template records why. A setting class is all-or-nothing, and no class is honest about high ground. Widening it honestly would need a terrain axis on the registration path, which is the same finding as the Bridge's river (THR-1153).
- **Test:** the `vertical-slice.test.ts` THR-1567 case pins all three decisions and checks that the default casts are composed.
- **Evidence tool:** `census:firings` now watches the three templates.
