# THR-1297 slice 1 — `groupKind` and the `member_of` wrapper sweep

*2026-08-27 · [THR-1297](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1297) · plan doc 2/6 of Proactive Agent Actions*

**Plan doc:** [`Docs/plans/2026-08-27-thr-1297-action-library-works-holdings-naming.md`](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/Docs/plans/2026-08-27-thr-1297-action-library-works-holdings-naming.md)

## What shipped

The first of six sequential slices. This one is entirely substrate: it makes the group
family tell you what it *is* instead of leaving every reader to guess from which property
bag happens to be present.

Four things reach the graph as `actor` + `actorType: 'group'` — a **company**, an **army**,
a **battle** coordinator, and (landing with the undertaking kinds) a **network**. Until now
they were told apart by presence: `groupType` meant company, `armyState` meant army. That
worked exactly as long as companies were the only non-faction `member_of` target — which is
the premise `graphQueries.isFactionMembershipEdge` rests on, and which the network kind
makes false. A network takes `member_of` contact edges the way a company takes members, so
without a discriminator every raw faction read would report an agent's contact web as their
faction.

- **`src/engine/groupShape.ts`** (new) — one home for the rule. `groupKind` is read first;
  the old presence tests survive as a back-compat fallback so saved worlds and existing
  fixtures keep resolving.
- **`groupKind` stamped at all five mint sites** — `groupFormation` (company),
  `armySpawning` ×2 (army, warhost), `battleResolution` (battle), the debug warhost spawn.
- **The sweep** — 48 agent-sourced `member_of` reads migrated onto
  `getFactionMembershipEdges`, taking the routed total to 62 of 69 raw call sites. The 7
  left raw are deliberate and now say so in place: army-sourced reads (an army really *is*
  `member_of` its faction), the three group-scoped resolvers whose whole job is finding the
  company, and `reputation.ts`'s target-addressed a→b finder, whose membership leg must
  keep resolving a mortal's standing with their own company.
- **One live defect fixed in passing** — `strategicActionCandidates.ts`'s `faction` target
  rule returned a *company* as a faction target, so any faction-targeted undertaking could
  be aimed at a band of three wanderers.
- **Schema prose** — `commanded_by` amended to name all three commanded entities and to
  state that co-location is *not* implied; `member_of` now says its target may be a
  group-family node and points at the shape module.

## The gate

The plan's kill criterion for this slice: *"golden faction-membership comparison on seeds
42+99 gates the slice (any changed read = stop)."*

`src/engine/__tests__/factionMembershipGolden.test.ts` is that gate, built as a
**differential** rather than a captured literal — it re-derives the pre-THR-1297 rule inline
on every run and asserts the wrapper agrees agent-by-agent, so it cannot degrade into the
refactor agreeing with itself.

Worth recording because it nearly shipped vacuous: the first version compared the two rules
over the bare seeded worlds, and a seed-42/99 world **at tick 0 contains no companies at
all** (`createGroup` runs from a tick phase, not worldgen). Falsification proved it — with
`isGroupMembershipTarget` stubbed to `return false`, the per-seed differentials stayed
**green**, and only the separately-constructed pin went red. The fix was to construct a real
company into each seed world before comparing. Second falsification: 5 of 11 red, both seed
differentials included.
