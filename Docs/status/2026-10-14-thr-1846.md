# 2026-10-14 — THR-1846: battles and sieges reach the map, and a siege draws as a siege

**Ticket:** [THR-1846](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1846). Found by THR-1824, re-measured by the THR-1830 lens decision. UI only (Engine N/A: the engine already writes `battleType`; Content N/A: no words change).

- **What the player sees.** A town under siege now shows the orange siege ring; a field battle shows the crossed swords. Before this change neither drew in the game at all.
- **Root cause, wider than filed.** Battle and siege actor nodes have no `hexCol`/`hexRow`. The engine places them with a `located_at` edge to the hex (`battleResolution.ts`, `siegeResolution.ts`). GameView's adapter read `node.properties.hexCol`, so its `battles` list was always empty. It also built the old `BattleRenderData` shape, which has no `isSiege`, and passed a `sieges` prop that HexMapV2 does not have.
- **How.** `battleIndicatorForNode` was factored out of the tested `buildBattleIndicatorData` (`BattleIndicatorLayer.ts`). GameView calls it in its single actor pass, so every entry carries `isSiege` and resolves its hex through `located_at`. The dead `sieges` prop, the `siegeRenderData` memo and two orphaned modules (`SiegeIndicatorMesh.ts`, `BattleIndicatorMesh.ts`, no callers) are removed. `snapshotScene()` already counts `battlesVisible` and `siegesVisible` separately from the `battles` prop.
- **Live updates (review-gate round 1).** HexMapV2 built the battle layer only in its scene-init effect, whose dependencies do not include `battles`. A siege that started mid-run would therefore still not draw. The layer now keeps one group and refills it in an incremental effect keyed on `battles` (`rebuildBattleIndicatorLayer`). A signature skips identical lists, since the list is recomputed every tick. `snapshotScene()` counts the sprites actually drawn (`countRenderedBattleIndicators`), not the prop.
- **Evidence.** `npm run gate` and `--final` verdicts are in the PR body. Tests: 4 new adapter tests in `armyVisibility.test.ts` and 5 new layer tests in `BattleIndicatorLayer.test.ts`. Browser run (bash-vite :5347 + Playwright, 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, fog off):
  - At tick 0, `snapshotScene()` read 0 battles and 0 sieges.
  - Over ten steps of `__DEBUG.tick(10)`, `siegesVisible` followed `getBattles()` exactly (3, 2, 2, 2, 2, 1, 2 …) as sieges started and ended.
  - The orange ring sits on The Bone Coast and The Forge of Sorrow (`Docs/audits/2026-10-14-thr-1846/`).
  - Console showed 0 errors. The page does not scroll.
- **Follow-up filed.** [THR-1847](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1847): hovering a besieged hex says nothing about the siege (Laws 1 / 17). The gap existed before this change, but was hidden while nothing drew.
