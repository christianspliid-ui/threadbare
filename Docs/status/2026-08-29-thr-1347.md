### THR-1347 — The settlement genome's NPC roster reaches a world for the first time

`runSettlementGenome` builds an NPC roster across four of its five passes, slices it to a
tier-scaled `NPC_BUDGET`, and `materializeGenome` stores it on the location. Nothing read
it. `INFRASTRUCTURE_NPCS`, every sphere and reach menu's `npcRoles`, every culture
baseline's `npcRoles` and every archetype's `capstoneNpcs` were authored content that
could not become a person, because no consumer existed on either side of THR-1344's
ordering fix.

**Reconcile arm, not wire or retire.** The population path stays single: `npcSeeding` is
still the only module that mints an NPC, and a new `seedGenomeNpcsAtSettlements` seeds
only the roles a settlement's own genome authored that its generic per-tier roster
missed. Both producers now go through one `mintNpc` helper — two call sites hand-copying
node shape is how two producers drift into minting subtly different actors.

**Where it runs is the load-bearing decision.** `seedNpcsAtLocations` fires ~300 lines
upstream of the genome's second worldgen pass, so the roster stored at that point is
missing its `culture` and `reach` contributions — 286 of 497 authored slots on seed 42,
the majority. Consuming the field there would have discharged the ticket's letter and
left most of the content it names exactly as unreachable. So the top-up runs at the tail,
after the second pass, on its own PRNG stream; `assignFactionsToExistingNpcs` moved two
statements later so genome NPCs are routed to factions on the same merit-scored path as
roster NPCs instead of being born faction-less.

Measured on generated worlds. Seed 42 / medium: 351 → 459 NPCs, genome roles with no
matching NPC at their settlement 232 → 124. Seed 99: 459 → 603, unmet 302 → 158. All
five passes win slots; `culture` and `reach` reach a world for the first time. Zero
duplicated roles at any settlement, zero cap violations, zero NPCs holding a role their
settlement did not author.

The test asserts on **actor nodes in a generated world**, never on `GenomeResult.npcs` —
that field is exactly the thing that reads as live while being inert, and the existing
hand-built materialize fixtures stayed green through the whole life of the dead read.
Its load-bearing claim is deliberately not "genome-stamped NPCs exist" (that would only
prove the stamp is written) but "roles the tier roster is structurally incapable of
producing are standing in the world": **0 before, 87 after on seed 42; 0 before, 121
after on seed 99.** Falsified by zeroing the cap — 7 of 11 assertions go red.

**One latent defect surfaced and fixed.** The larger population made
`tickEventIdUniqueness` red: `ua_ua_band_counter_ua_34_step1` minted on ticks 40 and 41.
Not a new bug — THR-817 derived the band-counter id from the action it answers and rested
uniqueness on three facts that all hold *within* one tick, but a counter is re-synthesized
on every tick its initiator is still completing, each resolving step 1 under the same id.
The tick is now part of the derivation, which is the id telling the truth rather than a
uniqueness patch; determinism is untouched.

Also removed: `GenomeResult.npcs[].preferredSublocation`, an optional field with no writer
and no reader — the same dead-field shape in miniature.

**Not covered, and worth knowing:** the settlement genome has neither a design-wiki page
claiming its sources nor a subsystem row in the interface-contract registry, so neither
the wiki-freshness gate nor the contract ratchet could ever have seen this leak. Logged
as an impediment rather than filed, per the process-work throttle.
