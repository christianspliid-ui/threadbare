## What shipped

- **`src/engine/fights/fightForks.ts`** (new). `runFightForks` takes a clash's forks after the clock-full check and the step's costs, in this order: temper, then concession. No fork runs once a result is set.
  - **Concession.** After a wounding clash (`FIGHT_CONCESSION_BANDS`) that is not the last, the fighter decides through `decideBranchPole` on `courage_prudence`: their live profile lean plus the dealt hand's lean, with the step rng drawn only inside the neutral band. The negative pole sets `yielded`.
  - **Temper.** It fires once, when the clock first reaches `ceil(clockSize × FIGHT_TEMPER_CLOCK_FRACTION)`, or at the first clash for a clock that starts there (traced `temperAtStart`). Skittish sets `driven_off`. Berserk sets `berserk`. Bargainer puts terms to the fighter on `mercy_ruthlessness`: mercy sets `bargained`, ruthlessness refuses. Stubborn does nothing. `monsterState.temperShown` is set only on an opponent carrying `monsterState`.
  - **`resolveQuarterOffer`**, `fight_offer_quarter`'s side rule. A behind fighter's concession runs now, and lapses on the last clash. Otherwise the opponent's temper shows now. FB7 wires the complication to it.
- **Forks are never choice memories.** They are recorded in `fightState.forks` and traced as `fight.fork`, which is registered in the THR-928 trio.
- **`applyFightStepResult`** now sets `broke_off` on an undecided last step *after* the forks, so a flight or a bargain on the last clash still decides the ending. The call site passes the step rng and the dealt step's cards.
- Docs: rulebook §7 (forks and temper moved to `[IMPL]`), the systemic wiring guide, and the wiring checklist.
