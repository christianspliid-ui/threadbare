# 2026-10-08 — THR-1768: sphere scores land where Dominion reads them

**Ticket:** [THR-1768](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1768) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-06-thr-1768-sphere-score-seeding.md`

Every node now carries a sphere bag: the god, every mortal with a culture, every culture, every place on both tiers. Every faction carries a derived aggregate. The Dominion formula ticket (THR-1760) has data to read.

- **The god** seeds 2 / 1 from its `sphereAlignment` (D6). It had no bag before, because it was created after the seeding loop and its alignment strings were filtered out as non-numeric.
- **Mortals** seed 2 / 1 from their strongest culture's venerated spheres (D1); cultures seed themselves. Before this, 509 of 509 were zero.
- **Late mints** (lairs, elder ruins, `loc.start`, births, waypoints, mint-queue NPCs) are caught by `backfillSphereAffinity` (D2). It runs once at world init, at the head of the sphere-pressure phase, and once more at the tail of `runTick`. It seeds only missing, `null` or malformed bags.
- **Lairs and ruins** gain `LOCATION_TYPE_BONUS` in their declared sphere (D4).
- **Factions** get `sphereAggregate`, the rounded mean of their individual members (D3). Battle aftermath reads own + aggregate, and a faction with no sphere now presses nothing instead of `chaos`.
- **Erosion** removes whole points only (D5).
- **Lair reinfestation** now measures the sphere a cleared lair *accrued since its birth seed* (`lairSteepedInvestment`: `triangleTotal(score) + progress − triangleTotal(seed)`) against `triangleTotal(LAIR_REINFESTATION_SPHERE_THRESHOLD)`. D4 gives a lair terrain + 2 at birth (usually 4–5), so the old raw-score gate (calibrated on lairs born at 0) would have brought every cleared lair back. The calibrated asymmetry is kept: a den cleared while still minor stays cleared.
- **Re-pinned:** the THR-1635 coloration census, strong-sphere Locations 35 → 59 (seed 42) and 24 → 70 (seed 99). Lairs and ruins now carry bags with their declared sphere. The culture counts, the leak guard (573 lines per seed) and the no-unauthored-cell sweep are unchanged.
- **Traces:** `sphere_pressure` traces are emitted at last, carrying every writer's `sources` / `sourceIds`. A new `sphere_seeded` category records seeds.
- **Debug:** `window.__DEBUG.getSphereSeedCensus()`.
