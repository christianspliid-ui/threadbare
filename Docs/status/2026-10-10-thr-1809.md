# 2026-10-10 — THR-1809: a ready opening gift waits for a quiet moment

**Ticket:** [THR-1809](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1809) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-10-thr-1805-gift-waits-its-turn.md` · **Found by:** cold playtest round 3 (all three testers; THR-1805).

- **The fault.** A cast is a player act, so it makes the next opening gift due one tick later. The gift then opened its modal on the next render, on top of whatever the player had just clicked: Observe, Cast, the Chapter Ledger or a profile.
- **The rule.** The new pure `src/components/Game/giftDelivery.ts` provides `resolveGiftDelivery`. It holds gifts 1–4 while a player-opened surface (`GIFT_HOLD_SURFACES`, 17 ids) or another interrupt is open, while a journey vignette is pending or beats are suppressed, and for `GIFT_QUIET_AFTER_INPUT_MS` (2000) after the last pointer or key input. Beat 0 is exempt (`GIFT_HOLD_EXEMPT_BEAT_IDS`). Readiness, spacing and order are unchanged; the engine is untouched.
- **GameView.** A window listener (capture phase, passive) stamps `lastInputAtRef`. The old auto-enter effect is replaced by one that calls the resolver, with one recheck timer for a recent-input hold. `openPlayerSurfaces()` now feeds both the hold and `getDebugOpenModals`, which gains `Codex`. The ActionDrawer surface uses the drawer's render guard (`wheelSlots`): the browser run found the flag true with no drawer on screen, which would have kept a gift waiting behind nothing.
- **The pill.** While a gift is held, `AscendantBeatOfferBanner` reads "✦ A GIFT WAITS — <gift eyebrow> · Open ▸" with a tooltip, at `GIFT_PILL_Z` (62, new row in `layout-zones.md`) so it shows above the surface holding the gift. Clicking it opens the gift. The pill rises only while the player's own surfaces or recent input hold the gift. Under an interrupt or a vignette it stays at 40, below the backdrop, so it cannot stack the gift over them (review-gate finding, round 1). Pool beats keep their pill and its band (40). Copy: `GIFT_WAITS_COPY` in `ui-content.ts`.
- **Debug.** `window.__DEBUG.getGiftDelivery()` → `{ pendingBeatId, entered, heldBy, heldBySurfaces, msSinceLastInput }`. A dev-only `console.debug('[gift-delivery] held', …)` line fires once per hold change.
- **Proof.** Tests: `giftDelivery.test.ts` (30) and `giftWaitsPill.test.tsx` (3). `npm run gate`: test, typecheck, build and encounter all PASS. Browser run at 1920×1080 on `?view=game&seeded&size=medium` with a worktree Vite on :5287. Beat 0 entered at once over a profile. `the_seat` was held behind ActionDrawer + AgentProfileModal and entered about 2 s after close. `thing_left_behind` was held behind the ActionDrawer alone (`recent_input` at 1.9 s, entered by 4.2 s). `the_first_word` was held behind the ChapterLedger and `a_path_opens` behind the Codex, and each entered about 2 s after close. Screenshots are in `Docs/evidence/thr-1809/`. One console error predates this change: a duplicate React key `beat_gift_beat.spine.the_seat_0`, built by the engine's `ascendantBeat.ts:1178` (THR-1606) and seen only on the debug path (a gift resolved at tick 0). UI Laws 1, 13/14, 17, 21, 33, 37, 39, 40, 47 and 49 hold on the changed surfaces.
