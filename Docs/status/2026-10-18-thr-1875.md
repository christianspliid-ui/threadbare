# THR-1875 — the top bar and Needs you (frame slice 3)

**Shipped 2026-10-18.** The last slice of the screen frame (plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1875-screen-frame.md` § F3–F5). The top bar is one row of `--topbar-height` (48 / 56 px) drawn from `TOP_BAR_ITEMS` (`src/data/frame-registry.ts`): time (play, the speed stepper, date over the status line), **Needs you**, the opening direction line, then Doom, Omen, Rivals, Notables, Read the Threads and Settings — every control a carved icon and its word. When the bar is short of room it applies `TOP_BAR_YIELD_ORDER` (centre slot → time status → doom stage name → omen name → direction short form), moving text into tooltips; no control hides or loses its word. `NOT_YET_LISTED` is gone: every opening-reveal surface is claimed by a frame list.

**Needs you** (`src/engine/views/needsYou.ts`, `frame/NeedsYou.tsx`): a gold pill shown only while something waits without stopping time — the beat offer (the floating offer pill retired; its THR-1809 raise moved to the pill), unacknowledged toast-tier receipts, the avatar arrival's **named pause** (the 5 s toast retired; the status line reads "paused · …"), moments held behind the one on screen, and the alert channel's news. `N` opens it; it grows to 60vh and only then folds into "and N more" rows (13 at 1080p / 16 at 1440p with three groups); a same-kind burst in one tick is one row "· and N more like it"; opening never clears the count; rows are read-only while an interrupt holds the clock.

Engine: the alert router now appends every alert (the same-icon same-tick overwrite was a silent drop), lapses by age (`NEEDS_YOU_NEWS_KEEP_DAYS` × `TICKS_PER_DAY`) and counts trims at `ALERT_SAFETY_CAP` (`alertsTrimmedAtCap`); `ALERT_MAX_VISIBLE` and `AlertBar` are deleted; the mandate milestone fallback no longer prints a raw stage id; the severe-complication alert drops its "⊘" glyph; the interrupt registry moved to `src/engine/views/` (old path re-exports).

Evidence: bash-vite + Playwright at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440 with every item revealed (`__DEBUG.setFrameFixture`) — `getFrameState().topBar` rows 1, overflow 0, height 48 / 56, nothing yielded; map share 0.627 / 0.653. Not exercised in the browser: the opening direction line together with every item (the seeded world is past its opening) — `topBarFit.test.tsx` drives that yield path with stubbed widths.
