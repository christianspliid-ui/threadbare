# 2026-09-25 — THR-1614: encounters with other mortals can finally be chosen

**Ticket:** [THR-1614](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1614) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** Engine = the fix · Content N/A (existing templates become reachable) · UI N/A.

## What was wrong

`generateSocialCandidates` builds each decider's encounters with the mortals it can see — social, social-scene, tavern, secret-discovery and faction-social templates. `phaseAgentDecision` appends them after the shared location cache, and the cap stage fills its 40 slots from the head of a list thousands of entries long. THR-814 closed that cut for faction quests with a `personallyOffered` reserve; the social generator never set the flag. The THR-1590 census measured 0 of ~1,000 offered social entries surviving the cap on seeds 42 and 99, and 0 social-path firings in 150 ticks.

## What changed

- Social entries carry `socialOffer: true` and claim one of `SOCIAL_OFFER_CAP_RESERVE = 6` slots in a new Phase 1d of `capWithDiversity`, after the diversity, branching and faction reserves.
- **Sibling reserve, not a share of THR-814's:** social entries sit ahead of faction quests in the tail and run ~16 per decider, so one pooled reserve would starve guild work. A test pins both reserves holding at once.
- The first pass takes distinct templates, so one crowd cannot fill every slot with the same scene.

## Evidence

Headless census (`Docs/audits/2026-09-25-living-world-data/readers/attended.ts`, seeds 42 + 99, medium, 150 ticks): social-path firings **0 → 45** attended, **0 → 44** unattended. Board firings roughly unchanged (800 → 811, 835 → 777). THR-814's faction-reserve tests still pass.

## Remaining

Scoring concentrates the newly reachable pool on `social.forge_alliance` (~34 of 45). Tavern, scene and secret templates reach scoring but have not yet won a draw in this sample — a scoring-variety question, not a reachability one.
