### 2026-10-05 — THR-1689: why mortals choose outside the forecast window — choice, not availability

**What was asked.** Only 45% of mortals' free choices sit in the 50–65% forecast window, and fixing the shortlist (THR-1687) did not raise it. The ticket asked which out-of-window choices mortals make, and whether an in-window option was on offer when they did.

**Answer.** It is choice. Of 1,025 out-of-window free choices on seeds 42 · 99 · 7 (120 ticks, the `gameplay-report` world), an in-window encounter was genuinely missing in only 97 (9.5%). The rest split into four groups that don't overlap:
- **31% branching quests at near-certain odds.** Quests are exempt from the window's too-easy discount, so they win on value. That covers 42% of masters' and 26% of experts' free choices.
- **22% inside the mortal's own shifted window.** The KPI judges the fixed window.
- **40% just past an edge.** The in-window option lost by a median 2.5×, on value per tick and desire.
- **7% deep outside.**

Surveys and harvests never enter the share, because the ledger stamps encounters only. No compulsions occur in these runs. Experts' in-window work succeeds at or below its forecast; their high overall success comes from the near-certain quests.

**Probe (never shipped).** With the quest exemption off, the in-window share goes 0.461 · 0.433 · 0.462 → 0.524 · 0.499 · 0.497, expert attempted difficulty rises 0.20–0.23 → 0.28–0.30, master success comes toward level, and idle stays at 0. That is still below `KPI_IN_WINDOW_MIN` (0.60, not the 0.50 the ticket quoted).
