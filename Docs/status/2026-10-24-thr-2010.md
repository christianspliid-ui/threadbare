# THR-2010 — Reach relabels from the mislabel audit

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** This is the first build from the THR-1940 audit (`Docs/audits/2026-10-21-thr-1940-reach-mislabel-worklist.md` § 3 Relabel / Step relabel, § 5). Thirty encounters change their Reach, six move only the steps the audit names, and the three proof templates join the Eight (Stone / Star / Iron). The wager's `fortune` sphere becomes `chaos`.

**What changed.**
- **Catalogue.** Thirteen content files: the legacy `encounter-content.ts`, the faction catalogues (Adventurers' Guild, Holy Order, Merchant Consortium, Temple of Spheres, tavern, governance) and the factory files (`vertical-slice.ts`, `hunt-trail-cold.ts`, `the-executioners-commission.ts`, `effect-shell-proof-templates.ts`). The legacy aliases `'spirit'` and `'dominance'` on `rally_faithful` and `council_mediation` are gone. When a legacy template's new primary Reach equalled its old secondary, the two were swapped.
- **Reward tags.** The garrison gossip reward tag moves from `#heart` to `#eye`.
- **Re-stamp.** `encounter.sharpen_blades` was stamped on Iron. On Stone it re-draws possession + condition (the same place → condition swap). Possession is now wired by a `rewardPool` on the whetstone step.
- **Pin.** `src/data/__tests__/reach-mislabel-relabels.test.ts` pins the 41 ids and checks that no encounter template declares a Reach outside the Eight.

**Deviation.** The Easter reckoning pair (`encounter.town.easter_reckoning`, `town.easter_reckoning_heard`) stays Star. On the everyday settlement board the reckoning is one of three master-band Star templates. Moving it to Eye left Star at 2, under the master floor of 3, which the ticket's own Done-when forbids. Follow-up: [THR-2014](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2014) (the re-stamp was already tried there and passes `check:encounter`). The blessing row reads "walk stays star", but the walk already rolled Veil, so it was left as it was. All three blessing steps now roll Veil.

**Evidence.** `measure:roll-spread --coverage-only` before → after on the everyday board: gold 20→18 novice, heart 18→20, star 7→6 novice and 3→4 journeyman, stone 7→8 novice and 4→3 journeyman (at its floor). No Reach is under any floor. `npm run gate` code track: test, typecheck (ratchet 2691, down 2), build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt and the 30-tick CLI smoke all PASS.
