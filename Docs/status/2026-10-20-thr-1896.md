# THR-1896 — News that leaves Needs you lives on in the Chronicle

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** The frame promised that a News row which lapses out of Needs you "lives on in the Chronicle". It did not: the Chronicle intake (`phaseNarrative`) only takes events at significance 0.8 and above, and it runs before the `post-narrative` slot. So a mandate stage or victory (raised post-narrative) never reached the Chronicle, and neither did a faction promotion (0.7), an abandoned ambition (0.5) or a town's prayer (0.4).

**Fix.** A new end-of-tick pass, `phaseAlertChronicle` (`src/engine/alertChronicle.ts`), runs at the end of the tick, after the resolved-action block that raises the live faction promotion alerts. Every event on the tick with `notification.channel: 'alert'` gets one Witness-voice Chronicle line in its own words (`playerProse ?? message`), unless the Chronicle already holds its id, `ALERT_CHRONICLE_POLICY` exempts its type, or the threading gate suppresses it (a stranger's alert never reached News, so the Chronicle does not tell it either). One `chronicle.intake.alert` trace per tick lists what was written, exempted and suppressed.

**Classification.** All eight alert event types are chronicled; none is exempt. Each of the 13 alert sites (one is a conditional channel, the mandate checkpoint) in `src/engine` carries an `// alert-chronicle: <type>` marker, and `alertChronicle.test.ts` scans the source and fails on an unmarked site, an unclassified type, or a dead policy row.

**UI copy.** The News group's tooltip now ends "Older news is in the Chronicle." (`NEEDS_YOU_NEWS_TOOLTIP`, `ui.needs_you_news`).
