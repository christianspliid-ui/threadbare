**The wiki freshness gate now covers two subsystems it was silently blind to — and the third "gap" turned out to be a trap the ticket would have walked into.** THR-1061 came from the weekly hygiene coverage sweep, which compares recently-changed `src/` files against every page's `sources` glob and reports what matches nothing. It named three clusters. On inspection they split three different ways, and only reading the pages could tell them apart. **Two were genuine, and the pages prove it in their own prose.** `armies-battles-reference` documents army supply in detail — the four supply tiers, `SUPPLY_ATTRITION_PENALTY`, `ARMY_SUPPLY_MAX_HOPS = 4`, the THR-626 bounded BFS over road and `trades_with` conduits, the relief-line rule that lets a besieged city hold out twice as long — while its `sources` listed movement, attrition, spawning, battle, siege and lair and **not one of the three supply files**. `factions-cultures-reference` cites `src/engine/groups/bandSpawner.ts` **by path in its own Designer Notes** and carries 21 mentions of bands, while `src/engine/groups/*` matched no page anywhere. Both are now covered. **The third was the trap.** Item 1 asked to extend `world-map-reference` with `src/components/HexMap/*` and enumerated eleven files — `HexMap.tsx`, `HexTile.tsx`, `AgentDots.tsx`, `MovementTrails.tsx` among them. That directory does not exist; CLAUDE.md's own *Rejected Approaches* list records those exact filenames as the V1 SVG hex map **deleted in Phase 8**. The glob would have validated, matched zero files, and closed the reported gap while changing nothing — a no-op indistinguishable from a fix at every gate the repo has. Correcting the path to `HexMapV2` would have been *worse*: that directory holds 94 files and the page contains **zero** occurrences of render, mesh, shader, canvas, zoom, sprite, palette, Three, WebGL or HexMapV2, because it is a Manual page whose own template forbids code identifiers in its player-facing half. Arming a blocking CI gate to fail on every palette tweak against a page about terrain and fog would have trained the gate to be waived — the exact failure the freshness contract exists to prevent. **So the renderer is recorded as deliberately out of scope, with the reasoning, at the section the sweep's own escape-net bullet points to** (`Docs/design-reference-wiki.md` § *Uncovered ≠ a gap*) — because a sweep that re-derives its candidates weekly will re-file this ticket forever unless the repo says why the last attempt declined. The encounter-stage adapters are recorded the same way, on their own contracts: `buildAftermathConsequences.ts` calls itself *"a re-presentation, never a second vocabulary"* and `buildNudgePhaseModel.ts` *"calls only functions resolution itself calls"* — they cannot change documented behavior unless an already-covered engine source changes, so covering them would double-fire the gate on one change. **Where a cluster genuinely had no home, the answer was a `backlog` entry rather than a glob bolted onto the nearest page**: the travelling-company lifecycle (formation, cohesion, movement, parting, reunion, seeking, fray, dissolution) is documented nowhere, and backlog entries accept `sources` but are never read by `check-wiki-freshness`, so `companies-reference` records the intent without arming a gate against a page that does not exist yet. The two group files that *are* documented went to the pages that actually describe them — `groups/band*.ts` to factions-cultures, `groups/groupEligibility.ts` to encounters-manual, which cites it by path for THR-74.
