# 2026-10-06 — THR-1713: what you spend and what you risk now reads on hover

**Ticket:** [THR-1713](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1713) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-05-thr-1713-readable-on-hover.md` · **Pillars:**
- **Engine:** a per-sphere, per-cause record of why essence moved (`src/engine/essenceMovement.ts`).
- **Content:** 34 registry entries (odds tiers, 21 card keywords, per-tier forecast words, helps/hinders, Reaches), cause phrases, trend words.
- **UI:** essence row, nudge-card marks, cast forecast word, forecast pill, factor kind tags, Reaches on bar and sheet, quintessence on the strip and sheet.

## Why

Round 2 of the cold playtest: 0 of 3 testers could say what an essence bar is for or what "doomed" means. They hovered "sphere bars, Reaches, stars, dice" and got nothing. THR-1607's copy existed but sat on the wrong elements: the essence tooltip on the 13px numeral only, card-mark hovers switched off on the dilemma's cards, the trend arrow a hard-coded placeholder.

## What shipped

- **Essence rows explain themselves.** The whole row is one hover: the sphere's role, a trend word, and what fed or drew it lately, in words — *"Ebbing. Fed by the cosmos's flow. Drawn by your threads' upkeep."* The arrow reads the same record, so it is real now.
- **The record behind it.** `GameState.essenceMovement`: two rolling windows per sphere, filed by cause at both phase funnels (`runInlinePhase`, `runRegisteredPhases`) and at every out-of-tick spend (hand, authored choice, cast, boost, peek, scry, thread reading). One `essence_movement_roll` trace per window close; `__DEBUG.getEssenceMovement()`.
- **Nudge-card marks hover.** Cost, odds pips, sphere and keyword chip each carry a registry tooltip; the raw `title` on the pips is gone. The ★ testers asked about now says it is the *Fated odds* pip, not a price.
- **Forecast words explain themselves.** The dilemma pill hovers its own tier ("As things stand, this attempt will almost surely fail…") and changes as the hand moves it. Cast cards print "Favorable", not `favorable`.
- **Factor lines carry a word.** *helps* / *hinders* before each coloured line (Law 31); only the tag hovers, saying it is about the attempt, not about the player.
- **Reaches** hover on the sheet's tier word and on both headings, saying Spheres are a separate axis.
- **Quintessence** leaves the identity strip while at its top word (≥ 0.9); the sheet now shows it always.

## Executor decisions (flagged for veto)

- **Six more spend sites than the plan's three** were tagged (boost, peek, meeting spend, scry, thread reading, intervention cast). A missed one shows as an unexplained drop.
- **A `ruins` cause was added.** On a 150-tick seed-42 run the only unmapped mover was `delve_emergence` — 100% of that sphere's inflow, past the plan's one-third bar.
- **A negative `income`** is the essence phase clamping a pool back to its cap, so it reads "the limit of what you can hold", not an ebb.
- **The sheet gains a quintessence line.** The plan assumed it already had one; it did not, and hiding it from the strip would have broken Law 13 parity.
- **Done-when 7's premise did not hold on `?seeded`:** the seeded god starts at *Rooted*, not Absolute, and climbs to *Radiant* by tick 150 — so it moves, and the strip correctly keeps showing it. The rule (hide at ≥ 0.9) is implemented and unit-pinned.

## Evidence

Playwright 1920×1080 on this branch's dev server: after `__DEBUG.tick(150)`, Mind's record read upkeep −95 against income +57.75 and the row read Ebbing with that hover; every other held sphere read Rising. Nudge-card geometry measured identical to the deployed main build (210×322.25). 19 render tests + 15 engine tests.
