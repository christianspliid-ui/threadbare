# THR-2209 — Rebuke: a god card that answers a rival on the place it just pressed

**Shipped 2026-11-14.** Thematic Pressure & Living World. Plan doc: `Docs/plans/2026-11-13-thr-2202-give-both-clocks-a-verb.md` (slice 3). This closes the three-slice programme (THR-2207 Doom, THR-2208 Mandate, THR-2209 rivals).

- **Rebuke** (`loc.rebuke_rival`, "The Turned Hand"). A tier-1 generic milestone gift priced in the primary pool (`RIVAL_REBUKE_COST` 3, difficulty 0.35). It is offered only on a place where a rival's probe landed **unabsorbed** within `RIVAL_REBUKE_WINDOW_DAYS` (3). `getTargetActionSlots` gains a fail-closed `rebukeTargets` set, which `actOnSlots` fills from `rebukablePlaceIds(state)`.
- **A landed cast** (`resolveRebuke`, `src/engine/rivalRebuke.ts`, hooked beside Shift Dominion in `phaseUnifiedActionProgress`):
  - It writes `GameState.rebukedPlaces[place] = tick + 4 days`. `buildTendingIndex` reads this as a fifth `TendedBy`, `rebuke`, through `rebukeTendingOf(state)` at every reader. So the next rival landing there is absorbed, neglect pauses, and the place page reads "Held against rivals by your rebuke."
  - A clean landing pushes `RIVAL_REBUKE_PUSH` 4 of the god's primary at the place (`causedBy` the god, so the Mandate credits it). A crooked landing (`success_at_cost`) pushes nothing.
  - Any landing raises the rival's temper by 0.08 with the cause `rebuked`. The panel then reads "You rebuked it at {place}."
  - The cast receipt names the rival and the place. The trace is `rival.rebuked`.
  - Lapsed entries are pruned on the next write.
- **`raiseRivalHostility` moved** from the orchestrator to `rivalRebuke.ts`, so the cast and the rival phase share one temper rule. A temper the god's act (a counter, or now a rebuke) put above the scheme ceiling keeps that act as its cause, as before.
- **The toast now tells the truth.** `RivalState.lastPressed` records `absorbed` when tending held, so an absorbed landing is not rebukable and does not trigger the gift. `rivalLandingLine(…, holdsRebuke)` picks the line:
  - god holds Rebuke: "…Rebuke it there, or tend the place, and its next blow is turned."
  - god does not hold it: "…A place you tend turns its next blow."
  - The old "Tend it and the next blow breaks" promised a break that never happened.
- **The gift.** `beat.milestone.the_first_rebuke_due` fires once some rival's `lastPressed` is unabsorbed with the clock awake. It is keyed on state, like the first-stage beat.
