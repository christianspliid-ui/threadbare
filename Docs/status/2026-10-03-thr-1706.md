### 2026-10-03 — THR-1706: essence tells one story

**The bug.** Cold playtest round 2: all three testers listed essence under "never understood". They saw three numbers for one resource. "Essence · 12" in the ascendant bar was the number of sphere rows. The "600 essence" in a dilemma was all twelve pools added together, which matched no bar. Meet The First showed 598 while the player picked cards, then committed without charging, so the number went back to 600. A sphere-less card (a Gold path) was billed to the god's primary sphere, and nothing on the card said so.

**The fix.**
- The Essence section has no count.
- Each nudge card has a `payingSphere`. Sphere-gated cards pay from their own sphere; sphere-less cards pay from the phase's `budgetSphere`, the god's primary. The cost row names it in words. The commit path bills `phase.budgetSphere`, so the name on the card and the pool charged are the same by construction. Authored choices on the encounter veil name their sphere too, and are now priced against that one pool instead of the total.
- The hand's line counts down the paying sphere's own pool: "39 Mind essence left", matching the Mind bar. With no sphere identity it falls back to the old pooled total.
- Meet The First charges. `FormativeTestBeat` and `BondBeat` call `onSpendEssence` with `meetingSpendRequests(...)` before resolving. `GameView` spends through `spendNudgeEssence` (primary first, then spill) and emits a new `meeting.essence_spent` trace.

**Evidence.** `essenceOneStory.test.tsx` (8 tests) and `meetingEssenceSpend.test.tsx` (5 tests, with a falsification run: removing the charge fails the pool test). Browser check with Bash-started worktree Vite on `?view=game&firstunmet&size=medium` at 1920×1080: the budget read "39 Mind essence left" with the Mind bar at 39; selecting a 2-essence card moved it to 37; committing took the Mind bar from 39 to 37.
