# THR-1433 — one rule for reading a mortal's mind

**2026-09-07 · Engine + UI (the sheet, the receipt) · project Thematic Pressure & Living World**

The god read what a mortal wants only by watching them. Nothing a *mortal* learned — a secret dug up by a spider, what a network's people were near — reached the god's reading of anyone, so running a ring or holding a mark changed nothing the player could see on a sheet. One predicate now answers every surface.

## The rule

`canReadIntention(state, god, mortal)` in `src/engine/intentionReading.ts` opens through any of three doors, plainest first:

1. **familiarity** — watched to `INTENTION_KNOWLEDGE_TIER` (was `AMBITION_PRIMARY_KNOWLEDGE`; kept at THR-721's `recognised` — the ticket's `known` was the pre-THR-721 hardcode, and the ruled value wins);
2. **a mark** — a mortal the god follows holds an unrevealed `knows_secret_of` on them;
3. **a network** — a ring whose leader the god follows has a living member within `NETWORK_READ_REACH_HEXES`, which *is* `RING_REACH_HEXES` (a ring that can mark a man can tell its patron what he is about).

**A secret** (`SECRET_CELL_IDS`, initially the plot) closes the first door: knowing a man well does not tell you he is planning a murder; his enemy's spy does. `through` and `via` come back so the surface can say how (Law 17), never a numeral or a tier word.

## Where it is applied

- **The sheet.** The hook that builds the card stamps the live answer (`card.intentionRead`) and the intention line (`card.intention`: *Set on Seek Revenge — Raise a warhost*); the Overview tab renders the line under the calling with the door in the tooltip, and nothing at all when the door is closed (Law 25). The Journey and Chronicle ambition gates call `readIntentionFromCard` instead of their own tier compare — the one compare outside the predicate lives in that helper, for cards built without a stamp.
- **The receipt.** The thread-card foreshadowing tooltip (`getEncounterForeshadowingById`, which holds the state) takes the receipt-driven path — the mortal's real motive — only through an open door, else the composed guess; `compositionKeys` carries `read:<door>`. The ranked-pool panel path holds no state and reads as open, unchanged.
- **Two debug levers** so the proof is constructible: `__DEBUG.spawnMark(holder, subject)` (the observe cells' own `mintLeverageMark`) and `__DEBUG.openAgentSheet(ref)` (the info card's own route), plus `__DEBUG.canReadIntention(ref)`.
- **The map.** `god-reads-mortal-intention` is 🟢 LIVE; the plot's grid note says it is a secret.

## Proof

- Unit: each door falsified at its owning layer with its absence arm (below the tier; unfollowed or revealed mark; muted default-follow; member one hex past the reach; unfollowed leader; disbanded ring; the plot at `transparent`, open through a mark); the rendered tab through each door and closed.
- Browser (pane, emulated 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`): Ashara (`ind_3`), a `stranger`, read `readable: false`; after `spawnMark('ind_dev_the_first','ind_3')` the predicate read `through: 'mark', viaName: 'Kael Thornweaver'` and her sheet showed **Set on Become the Greatest Healer** with the tooltip *"Kael Thornweaver, whom you follow, holds a secret of Ashara's — and what a follower knows, you know."* Before the mark the line was absent from the DOM. Console clean.

## Left honest

- The ranked-pool foreshadowing panel (`ThreadDetailView`) still composes from the receipt without asking the rule — it holds no game state. Its agents are threaded, so the familiarity door is open in practice; a stateful pass would close it properly.
- The thread-card foreshadowing cache keys on (agent, encounter); a door that opens mid-life of a cached entry shows on the next cache clear, not the next frame.
