# THR-1130 — retrofit batch 3: the hand the brief called impossible

**2026-09-11 · Content · `tb-opus-pickup`**

Batch 3 of the Encounter Factory pilot volume. One encounter —
`encounter.shrine_offering` — off the `RETROFIT_PENDING` ratchet and onto the full
Composition Contract. With it, **the fifteen are closed** except for the two the
`concepts` ruling still holds.

## The headline: no swap was spent

The [batch-3 brief](../plans/encounters/2026-09-10-retrofit-batch-3-brief.md) drafted
on 2026-09-10 recorded this template's drawn hand — `thread` (weight 8 in star) and
`place` (weight 4) — as **unwirable 2-of-2 against a swap budget of 1**, and parked the
batch on one question: *do we wait for THR-1446?* The recommendation was yes.

THR-1446 shipped on 2026-09-10 at 09:52Z, and it took the widest of its options rather
than the cheapest: it added `$ascendant` and `$here` to `SCENE_SENTINEL_FIELDS`. So both
halves of the brief's blocker are gone, and the hand is authored **at full weight with
the swap budget untouched** — the outcome the 13:32Z orchestrator comment predicted when
it observed that both branches of the parked question now converge on *run batch 3*.

That matters beyond this template. Batch 2 spent **both** of its swaps on exactly this
pair (`sharpen_blades` place→condition, `ward_the_camp` thread→condition), and that
overspend is what filed THR-1446. Batch 3 is the first batch to wire the pair as drawn,
which is the evidence that the valve was the problem and not the hand.

- **`thread`** — `thread_strengthen` / `thread_weaken` between `$ascendant` and `$actor`.
  This is the encounter's actual subject: a mortal gives to a god and waits to hear back,
  so the line between them is what the outcome should move. It is a **mutation, not a
  creation** — an unthreaded mortal's offering strengthens nothing and traces
  `edge_missing`, which is the honest reading of a stranger leaving a comb on some stones,
  not a defect.
- **`place`** — `apply_condition` on `$here`, landing a new location condition on the
  stones. First authored use of `$here` in shipped content.

## What the encounter owed, and what it now carries

Measured before (`check:encounter`): four failing blocks — `setting`, `cast`, `aftermath`,
`systems` — and four warnings. After: **clean, zero warnings, four systems** against a
quota of three.

- **Setting envelope** — `sacred` + `ruin` + `wayside`, one opening each. The old
  `locationTypes: ['shrine', 'temple', 'ruins']` maps to exactly `sacred` + `ruin`;
  `wayside` is the deliberate widening the brief invited, because the rite needs stones
  and a hollow, not a roof. Not `urban`: a city shrine has a keeper and an offering box,
  which is a different scene about a different kind of giving.
- **Cast** — `the_one_before` / *The One Who Came Before*, whose gift is still in the
  hollow. Declared rather than inherited because a three-class envelope inherits no family
  default (THR-1044), and chosen because it is the one cast honest in all three classes: a
  temple has a keeper and a ruin does not, but *somebody gave here last* is true of a
  forecourt, a fallen roof and a roadside cairn alike. It is also what turns "leave an item
  on a rock" into a scene with a second person's judgement in it.
- **Aftermath** — four `byOutcome` bands against ruling 7's floor of three, on the axis the
  fiction already had: did anything answer, and what did the giving cost. Every chip is
  backed by a write in its own band's reaction (Law 56).
- **Systems** — `cast`, `rewards`, `seeds`, `conditions`. The `encounter_seed` on
  `critical_success` is load-bearing rather than decoration: `thread_*` effects contribute
  **no** system connection, so without the seed the quota lands at two.

## A new location condition, and why it had to be new

`trait.condition.location.tended_shrine` — *A Tended Shrine*. The `place` family had no
honest kind to land on: `festival` and `under_watch` describe other things entirely, and
`standing_welcome` is retired to read-tolerance with zero writers (THR-1206). The noun is
the *stones*, not the building, which is what keeps it class-honest across the whole
envelope.

## The finding, filed rather than worked around silently

**`$here` binds effect fields but is not a chip anchor.** `classifyAnchorDeclaration`
resolves five forms — `$actor`, `$target`, `$artifact`, `$cast:<key>`, `$faction:<defId>`
— and refuses everything else. So the effect lands the condition *on the shrine* and the
chip reporting it cannot point at the shrine: `$target` is the actor on a self-targeted
encounter, which is the very case `$here` was added for, and a literal location id is
minted per world and correctly refused.

The BOON chip therefore anchors the **attachment template** and names *what was granted*
rather than *where it landed* — gate-legal, routable, and not what the sentence is about.
Filed as THR-1462 with its coordination block rather than left as a comment.

## Calibration decided, not escalated

The three `Let …` card names — the last of the cohort batch 2 ruled on — are renamed
**id and name together** so the two cannot drift: `read_the_grain`, `trust_the_hand`,
`wait_for_dark`. Same verdict batch 2 reached: `Let` can open an imperative, so it is not
a lexicon gap, but the doctrine's bar is an *instruction* and "Let the dark come" is a
permission. Nothing outside the brief referenced the old ids.

The one intensifier warning was a lexicon false positive — *"left it there **rather** than
kneel again"* is a comparative, not an intensifier — so the line was reworded (`sooner
than`) rather than the detector widened or the warning suppressed.

**The brief's own scope call held up.** It predicted the prose was in better shape than
the batch-2 precedent implied — already narrator-mode third person, zero register
violations — so this was a density pass and three card names, not a rewrite. It was.

## A population pin moved on purpose

`conditionProxyEvents.test.ts` pins the condition catalog at an exact count so a catalog
that stops exporting fails loudly instead of shrinking the sweep to nothing. The new
condition took it 60 → 61. Bumped deliberately, with the reason written at the assertion —
this guard is *designed* to require that, and absorbing the change silently would be the
defect it exists to prevent.
