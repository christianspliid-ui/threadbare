# 2026-10-12 — THR-1814: the map's words explain themselves

**Ticket:** [THR-1814](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1814) · plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-11-thr-1807-terms-explain-themselves.md`, half A (UI items 1–5 and 11). Cold playtest round 3, PC-1.

- **Top bar under a veil.** The right group is no longer `inert`. It stays dimmed and `aria-disabled`, and a capture-phase guard swallows presses on every control in the group, because each panel would open beneath the veil. Hover and focus stay live: Doom and Omen explain themselves and add "Opens when this chapter closes." (`TOPBAR_LOCKED_SUFFIX`).
- **Registry.** World-Soul, Omen and Attention now read from the registry (`ui.world_soul`, `ui.omen`, `ui.attention`). Omen and Attention keep their live labels as overrides. `ui.doom_bar` is rewritten to "A slow clock toward the world's unmaking … cannot end the world early." Its label stays "Doom Clock", because other entries link to it inline and the top bar overrides it anyway. New ids: `ui.signatures`, `ui.hooks`, `ui.clock_held`, `ui.step_test`.
- **Reach tiers read as ranks.** A five-dot rung marker sits beside the tier word on the bar and the sheet (`ReachRungMarker`, the same `StepDots` magnitude row a mortal's `ReachStanding` uses). It sits inside the word's existing tooltip, and the step count comes from the register.
- **Empty sections hide.** Signatures, Covenants and Hooks render only when they hold something. Their placeholders are gone. Signatures and Hooks carry tooltips.
- **The clock names its holder.** Every interrupt now names what holds the clock: "held by the Chapter Ledger · runs on when it closes" / "held by The Healer's Oath · stays paused". The names come from `INTERRUPT_HOLDER_NAMES`, keyed by registry surface id, and a unit test covers every surface. `window.__DEBUG.getClockHold()` returns `{ held, holder, runsOnAfter }`.
- **Step chip.** The encounter's skill chip carries `ui.step_test` ("…It is not a change: her skill only moves in the aftermath.").
- **Review gate (2 rounds, both fixed).** (1) React routes portal events through their owner, so the group's guard also swallowed the backdrop close of a panel the group had opened before the veil (OmenDetail, the Notables modal). It now swallows only presses whose DOM target is inside the group. (2) The `ui.clock_held` hover said time "runs again" even on the stays-paused branch. That branch now carries `CLOCK_HELD_STAYS_PAUSED_DESC`.
- **Evidence.** 23 jsdom tests in `termsExplainThemselvesThr1814.test.tsx`. Bash-vite and Playwright at 1920×1080 on `?view=game&seeded&size=medium&forceencounters` with *The Healer's Oath* veil open: the Doom tooltip renders above the veil (z 70) with the locked line; `getClockHold()` returned `{ held: true, holder: "The Healer's Oath", runsOnAfter: false }`; a dispatched Doom click opened no dialog; three Reach rows showed 1/5 rung markers.
