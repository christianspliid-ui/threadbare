# THR-1901 — The minimap

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** The map's bottom-right corner now holds the whole world in small, above the control cluster (plan `2026-10-14-thr-1878-map-lenses-and-chrome.md` § D10).

**What the player sees.** A 240×180 glass panel. Known land is drawn in a flat tone per terrain family (water, lowland, forest, wetland, hills, mountain, arid, frozen). Uncharted land is drawn in the map's own uncharted tone, the First is a gold pip, and the current view is an outlined rectangle. Click the minimap to look there (eased). Drag the rectangle to pan. It shows no lens. It is open by default when the map canvas is at least 1400 px wide, so it is open at 2560×1440 and folded at 1920×1080. The cluster's fifth button ("Show the minimap" / "Hide the minimap", key V) toggles it, and the choice is kept in `localStorage` (`tb.map.minimapOpen`).

**How.** `map.minimap` (`src/engine/views/map/minimap.ts`) builds the cells from the fog `visibilityMap` with the camera's known-land rule. `MINIMAP_TERRAIN_FAMILY` is a `Record<TerrainType, …>` (Law 58 row in `extending.md`). Each family's tone is the active palette's colour for its representative terrain, through `resolveTerrainColorHex`, which is now shared with `getHexColor`. The camera gained a `viewport` readout and `lookAt` (trace reason `minimap`). Evidence: `getMapChrome().minimap` → `{ open, source, viewRect }`.

**Decisions (veto invited):** the fold button is always present, so the player can fold the minimap at 1440p too. V is its key.
