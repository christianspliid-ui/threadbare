# 2026-08-25 — THR-1247: Dealt hands — the Repertoire fills the encounter hand

An encounter can now author only its **specials** and declare a fill: `ActionStep.deal?: { count, tags?, exclude? }`, filled by a pure zero-PRNG dealer from the god's own Repertoire. Two new library tables carry what a member was missing — `PLAY_PROFILES` (cost, odds, rider, alternate costs, grants, context tags) and `BAND_FRAGMENTS` (per-member band prose, authored once per member instead of once per card per encounter). `mintDealtNudge` turns a member into an **ordinary `StepNudge`**, so partition, pricing, commit, dispatch, riders, forecast and echo harvest are all unchanged and unaware.

**The load-bearing find was where composition has to happen.** The plan put it in the stage adapter; resolution does not receive the hand the player saw — it re-derives its step from the template, and every mechanics reader (`collectNudgeModifiers`, `selectActiveRider`, `dispatchNudgeCommitments`, `collectNudgeBandProse`) then skips a committed id it cannot find. Adapter-only composition would have shipped a card that renders, prices and charges and then contributes no delta, no rider, no cost channel, no grant and no band prose — and passed every liveness gate on the way out. `composeDealtStepFromState` therefore runs at both resolution sites too, which is sound exactly because dealing is deterministic. Four test arms assert each of those mechanics on the real functions, with a falsification arm proving the uncomposed step sums to zero.

Two smaller judgment calls, both recorded in code: the dealer lives in `src/engine/`, which is barred from importing the authoring guardrails, so the hand bounds are **mirrored** into `nudge-constants.ts` and pinned equal by test rather than imported across the boundary; and the provenance line names a sphere, so it ships as **declared parts** (`conceptLabel` + `conceptTooltipId`) rather than a sentence — UI Law 2 forbids the surface hunting for concepts inside English.

Ships with two reference profiles (one universal core, one sphere signature) proving both access paths; the corpus, the spec's § *dealt hand*, and the first composed encounter are THR-1248. No shipped template declares `deal`, so the corpus is byte-identical. PR #1633.
