# THR-2033 — A town that rises sacks its costliest building

**Shipped 2026-11-19.** M3: Dynamic Economy. Plan doc: `Docs/plans/2026-10-16-thr-1934-hunger-and-unrest.md` § Engine 10 (the sack).

- **The change.** `src/engine/unrestPressure/rising.ts`: once a held town rises (split or free-town founding), its costliest standing building — highest `cost` in `src/data/settlement-buildings.ts`, ties by building-table order (the ticket's rule; the plan doc said "ties by id") — is lost through the settlement economy's `loseBuilding`. A Place the economy raised is removed; an adopted one stays and stops being a building. `costliestBuilding()` is exported. A rising with no one to lead it sacks nothing; a town with no buildings rises as before (`sackedBuildingId: null`). A loss that throws leaves the building standing (fail-soft).
- **Trace and chronicle.** `RisingResult` gains `sackedBuildingName`; the `unrest.rising` trace carries it (new optional field on `UnrestRisingTrace`) and its summary ends "; its town walls (walls) is sacked". The "A Town Rises" chronicle line gets one sack sentence appended from `TOWN_RISES_SACK_LINES` (`src/data/unrest-chronicle-content.ts`, seeded pick).
- **Burned or plundered.** `loseBuilding` removes only a Place the town raised; an adopted or mortal-raised Place stays standing and stops being a building. The chronicle tells the two apart: the town's own building gets a burned line (`TOWN_RISES_SACK_LINES`), a standing Place gets a plundered one (`TOWN_RISES_PLUNDER_LINES`), and the trace carries `sackedPlaceRemoved`. The review gate caught this in round 1.
- **Tests.** `src/engine/__tests__/unrestPressureS2.test.ts` gains five cases: two buildings → the costlier is lost and named on trace and chronicle; a cost tie goes to table order; an adopted Place is plundered, not burned; no buildings → `sackedBuildingId: null`; no one to rise → nothing sacked.
