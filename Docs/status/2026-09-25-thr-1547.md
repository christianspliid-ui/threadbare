## Finding: why natural play never confronts

Lair nodes carry no `adjacent` or `road` edges (seed 42: 14 live lairs, 0 with adjacency), so pathfinding never ends a journey at a lair. Mortals do stand on lair nodes, but only as road waypoints: `movementExecution` binds each road hex to a location on that hex, which is sometimes a lair. Examples: seed 42 lair_2/lair_11 hosted 8 distinct travellers heading for `loc_38` / `loc_51`, and seed 99 hosted 2. Confronting those waypoints would make the trigger hex-granular in effect, which is the plan's M4 kill criterion. So the trigger stays on final arrival. The plan already schedules a furniture re-measure at plan doc 6's closeout (the hunts). How mortals reach lairs is the question that re-measure owns.
