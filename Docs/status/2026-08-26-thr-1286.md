# THR-1286 — Control claim→decay→re-claim churn

**Shipped 2026-08-26.** `strategic_control` ate ~40% of all spotlight decisions and produced almost
nothing: on seed 42 at 150 ticks, 811 of 2053 decisions were control claims, yielding **17**
successful claims.

Three defects compounded, each hiding the others. `claim_control` was the one strategic verb that
never wrote history, so the recent-duplicate variety guard was structurally blind to it and the same
target could be re-proposed every tick. Collapsed stances were never retired — and a dead record is
not inert: `computeControlPressure` read its frozen `neglectTicks` and returned the *maximum*
pressure, so a dead stance outranked live work. And the stale `controls` edge made every re-claim
fail `already_controls`: the decision was spent, nothing changed, the identical candidate returned.

The fix declines a claim it can prove will do nothing, and retires what collapse leaves behind.
`STRATEGIC_CONTROL_RECLAIM_COOLDOWN_TICKS = 30` bars re-claiming a target the actor let collapse —
sized against the claim-to-collapse arc so losing a hold costs as long as holding it earned, and
read out of history, so it must stay under `STRATEGIC_HISTORY_WINDOW_TICKS`. Collapse retires the
record, writes that entry, and calls the new `releaseControl`, which matches **only** edges stamped
`controlType: 'strategic'` — worldgen's carry `influence` and are 144 of the 161 in a seed-42 medium
world. A target the actor still actively holds is no longer proposed at all.

| | seed 42 | seed 99 |
|---|---|---|
| `strategic_control` before | 811 / 2053 (39.5%) | 915 / 2363 (38.7%) |
| `strategic_control` after | 222 / 1958 (11.3%) | 103 / 1801 (5.7%) |
| dead records / orphan edges after | 0 / 0 | 0 / 0 |

Freed budget went to real work: `strategic_project` 188 → 441 on seed 42. Two ticket premises
corrected: the "161–178 dead edges" were overwhelmingly worldgen's (the real cost was the ~794
wasted decisions), and tick wall-clock rose 13% rather than staying flat, because agents now do more
real work rather than more scanning.

**Left standing, filed as [THR-1287](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1287):** nothing resets
`neglectTicks`, so control upkeep is structurally impossible and every stance collapses on schedule
regardless of its holder. Declining the no-op claim stops the waste without deciding what upkeep
should mean.
