# THR-2236 — the Mandate says what moves it

**Shipped 2026-11-15.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience. No plan doc (the ticket body is the plan); builds on THR-2208 (plan `Docs/plans/2026-11-13-thr-2202-give-both-clocks-a-verb.md`, slice 1) and does not change its credit rule. Answers THR-2199.

- **The fill line names the act.** `MANDATE_FILL_LINE` (`ascendant-bar-content.ts`) now says "Rises when you spend {spheres} essence on places." / "… on {mortal}'s chapters, or on places." / "… on the chapters of the {count} mortals bound to you, or on places." The two sphere words join with "or". `selectMandateRow` passes the one bound mortal's name through the new `boundMortalNames` (`godSheet.ts`; `countBoundMortals` now counts that list). A nameless mortal reads "on the chapters of a mortal bound to you" (`oneUnnamed`, the deed line's fallback without a possessive). `MANDATE_RAISED_BY_LINE` reads "Raised when you spend this essence on a bound mortal's chapter, or on a place."
- **"Rising" lasts three days.** `MANDATE_TREND_WINDOW_TICKS` 12 → 36. The `ui.mandate_tracker` tooltip says "how it moved in the last three days. Any card you play for a mortal bound to you, paid in its spheres, counts." Executor decision: the ticket's text pushed the tooltip to 230 characters, over the 200-character registry cap, so the opening clause was shortened to "Races the {{ui.doom_bar}}; a kept checkpoint blunts its next blow." The ticket's new sentences are verbatim.
- **Aftermath row.** It shows. It reads "BOON · MANDATE — Kael Thornweaver's deed raised your Mandate in Mind." The chip renderer heads it with its state noun (Mandate), not the change's `title` "Your Mandate rose", the same as every other consequence chip. Nothing was wrong with the row, so nothing changed.
