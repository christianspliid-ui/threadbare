# THR-1467 — one reach moved twice, and the ending said it twice

The aftermath of Snow on the Pass drew two `BOON · STONE` rows that the pre-flight sweep found **byte-identical in the DOM** — same testid, same tag, same noun, same `▲▲`. The ticket named the fork correctly and left it open: *a double render is a UI bug; a double grant is an engine bug the UI is reporting faithfully.* Establishing which was the first job.

## It is a double grant, and the grant is correct

`SLICE_SNOW_ON_THE_PASS.steps` is `[PASS_CLIMB_STEP, PASS_NIGHT_STEP]` and **both declare `reach: 'stone'`**. The growth producer in `unifiedActionResolution.ts` mints one change per step, keyed `${actionId}:step:${n}:growth:${domain}`, and `appendAftermathChanges` accumulates across steps. So two changes, both Stone, both real.

Measured on the shipped template before anything was written, via the CLI (`spawn encounter @hero encounter.slice.snow_on_the_pass` → `tick 12`):

```
'ua_1:step:0:growth:stone | Stone | band=3',
'ua_1:step:1:growth:stone | Stone | band=3'
```

Two entries, identical on every field the chip draws. **Neither the engine nor the UI is wrong about a fact here** — the climb grew Stone, the night grew Stone. What is wrong is that the *ending* is a per-step log where the player reads it as a summary: there is nothing to tell the two rows apart, because there is nothing distinguishing to tell. One reach moved, twice.

## So the ending states the reach's net movement

The fold lands at the producer, not the chip surface. A repeat growth in the same reach absorbs the earlier change and re-derives its sentence from the **summed** amount; distinct reaches never fold, because a Stone step and an Eye step are two different things that grew and already read as two chips.

Two details are load-bearing:

- **Re-band the total, never the bands.** `magnitudeBandIndex` is not additive — `▲▲` plus `▲▲` is not `▲▲▲▲`. That needed the raw amount to survive banding, so `EncounterAftermathMagnitude` gains an optional `raw`, documented as designer data that is never rendered. The words-never-numerals gate (THR-1004) still reads `detail`, which is built from the band's *word*.
- **A tier crossing on either step keeps the folded change a `beat`.** Mastery turning over is the story, and it must not be swallowed by a later incidental step in the same reach.

Folding here rather than at the adapter keeps one reading of the encounter: the receipt and the trace consumers read the same list the player does, and the surface stays a renderer.

After, on the same command and the same seed:

```
'ua_1:growth:stone | Stone | band=3 raw=0.296852413256634'
```

The id is step-free once folded — pinning it to whichever step happened to land last would be the same half-truth the two chips told.

## The second finding was not a defect

The sweep also reported that the boon's noun is *"underlined but inert"* — the SCAR nouns are `button`s that open their condition, the BOON nouns are `span`s. That reading stops one tier short.

`NarrativeSegments` draws three tiers and the underline is earned by **`explains` alone** (THR-1172): a segment whose tooltip the registry can answer is underlined, focusable, and wrapped in a `Tooltip`. `reach.stone` resolves — it is the same registry id `AscendantSheet`, `StrandView` and `DomainCard` already hover. So `STONE` is the hover tier and the hover tier is live. It is a `span` rather than a `button` because **there is no Reach page to open**, which is Law 21 satisfied by *not* promising a click, not violated. The two tiers are also not visually identical as reported: a link takes `linkColor` (gold), the hover tier takes `plainColor`.

Reported as a technical verdict rather than changed. Making the noun clickable would have invented a destination; dropping the underline would have hidden a working explanation.

## Verification

`preview_start` is refused in unattended runs, so the sanctioned substitution applies — **`Browser-verify substitution: jsdom-render — unattended run, no startable dev server`** (`Docs/canon/verification-gates.md` § Browser-verify). `snowOnThePassBoonChips.test.tsx` renders the real components: the chip census through the real `buildAftermathConsequences` (one row folded, the two-row pre-fix shape asserted so the census reads as a *change*, two reaches still two rows), and the noun tier through the real `NarrativeSegments` in jsdom — underlined ⇒ answers ⇒ `tabindex=0`, and the falsification arm proving a noun that answers nothing carries no underline.

`growthChangeFold.test.ts` proves the fold on a real resolution rather than a fixture, with a one-step control arm run through the same path so "summed, not replaced" is measured against what the growth curve actually pays. Its premise — that both shipped steps are Stone — is asserted, not assumed. **Red baseline confirmed**: disabling the supersede reddens exactly the one-row assertion, `expected length 1 but got 2`.

Suite green (1257 files, 20334 tests), ratchet **2872 unchanged and baseline NOT refreshed**, build green, 30-tick CLI smoke tick 30 / 486 agents / 49 unified actions.

**UI-Laws judgment.** Law 56 (chips are state-backed) is what the fold restores — the chip now answers for the reach's net movement, which is the quantity the sheet will show, where two rows answered for a per-step record the player has no access to. Law 21 (a control that looks live must be live) holds on the noun as composed: link tier clicks, hover tier explains and is keyboard-reachable, and neither promises what it cannot do. Law 1, 13/14 (no raw keys, no naked numerals) unaffected — `raw` is designer data behind the band word and reaches no surface. Law 17 (hover tier) is the rule the second finding was measured against and found compliant.

## Where the fold deliberately stops

Scoped to `growth`, because that is the only producer with a **demonstrated** duplicate — a measured pair of identical rows on a shipped template, reproduced above on the live sim.

`reputation` is the same structural shape (up to three changes per step, three flavours) and could in principle collide the same way across steps. It is left alone on purpose: no such pair has been observed, its three flavours already carry distinguishing clauses (*"as the checkpoint's judgement landed"*), and the process throttle is explicit that hardening against a failure that has not happened is not qualifying work. If a duplicate reputation row ever surfaces, the fold has a shape to copy and `magnitude.raw` is already there to carry it — but it is not filed as a ticket on a hypothesis.
