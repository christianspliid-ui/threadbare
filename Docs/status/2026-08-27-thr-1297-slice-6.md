# THR-1297 slice 6 — the census, and what it says the cutover still needs

**Shipped 2026-08-27.** Sixth and closing slice on plan doc 2/6 of Proactive Agent Actions
([plan doc](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/Docs/plans/2026-08-27-thr-1297-action-library-works-holdings-naming.md)).
Slices 1–5 landed the group discriminator, the kind registry and its motive gate, the `owns`
edge, the work namer, and tier 1 whole. This one measures what they did, files the work they
exposed, and closes the issue.

Its deliverable is mostly a **verdict**, so the diff is small on purpose: one instrument, one
defect the instrument found, seven tickets, and two evidence hand-offs.

## The instrument, made re-runnable

`npm run census:undertakings` (`scripts/undertaking-census.ts`) runs both acceptance
measurements the plan's Done-when asks for — checkpoint liveness and the decision mix against
the cutover envelope — over seeds 42 and 99 at 150 ticks, and prints a per-gate PASS/FAIL.

It is committed rather than run as a scratch one-off for one reason: **THR-1301 has to re-run
it to decide the cutover**, and a gate nobody can re-run is a claim, not a gate. It reads the
same `BOARD_UNDERTAKING_SHARE_RANGE` / `BOARD_ENCOUNTER_SHARE_FLOOR` / `BOARD_IDLE_SHARE_CEILING`
constants the gate is defined by, so the two cannot drift apart.

Two design notes that are load-bearing rather than cosmetic:

- **The wanderer pack is reported apart from the six established packs.** Slice 5 shipped the
  chart verbs `requiresLocation: false` on measured evidence, and that flag is exactly what a
  rolled-share number responds to — pooling the new pack in would let the remedy inflate the
  score for the corpus it was *not* applied to. That is the mixed-population failure
  `decisionBoardLiveness.test.ts` already records one floor down, and it would have been easy
  to repeat here without noticing.
- **An empty population fails rather than passes.** A census over zero checkpoints reporting
  "≥50% rolled" is the vacuous-probe shape; absence of board verdicts is likewise a FAIL on the
  idle ceiling, not a pass.

## The cutover envelope still fails, and the failure is informative

| | seed 42 | seed 99 | criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **before** (THR-1292 slice 6, `motivations` unauthored) | 11.9% ✅ | **4.1%** ❌ | `[0.10, 0.35]` |
| **after** (this census) | **12.7%** ✅ | **8.3%** ❌ | `[0.10, 0.35]` |
| encounter share | 69.2% ✅ | 79.9% ✅ | ≥ 0.15 |
| idle share | 18.1% ✅ | 11.8% ✅ | ≤ 0.40 |

Seed 99's undertaking share **doubled** and is still 1.7 points under the floor, so the flip
does not land and `UNIFIED_DECISION_BOARD_MODE` stays `'shadow'`.

**The number is a receipt for THR-1292 slice 6's diagnosis.** That slice ran a k-sweep scaling
strategic scores by a constant and projected seed 99 reaching 8.3% at **k = 5**. Authoring
`motivations` reached the same 8.3% at **k = 1** — no payoff inflation at all. The missing term
really was personality signal rather than payoff scale, exactly as that slice argued against
its own predecessor's read.

Which is also why the remaining 1.7 points must not be closed with `UNDERTAKING_PAYOFF_SCALE`:
the plan's kill criterion forbids it, the curve saturates (twelvefold moved seed 42 only 3.6
points), and a constant documented as a verb-impact→payoff-units bridge cannot honestly absorb
that correction. The residual is routed to THR-1302, whose `getAmbitionBoostForEntry` term is
now the **sole** remaining pinned input in that product rather than one of two.

## The defect the census found

The census bucketed 9 seed-99 checkpoints as `unrecorded` — neither rolled nor deferred. They
were the terminal `actor_lost` case, which passed `deferred: undefined` and no band, so the
row carried neither field and its summary rendered the literal string **`undefined → undefined`**.
The one checkpoint outcome the readout could not name.

`ended?: 'actor_lost'` is now a field of its own on the trace, **deliberately not a fourth
`deferred` member**: a deferral is retried on the next interval and this is terminal, so one
field for both would make them indistinguishable in exactly the readout that exists to tell
outcomes apart. Guard falsified — red with the fix reverted, green with it. The `unrecorded`
bucket stays in the census as a live bucket rather than being retired, because it is what
surfaced this, and a census that silently folds unnamed outcomes into a named reason reports a
tidier number and hides the next one.

Every other census figure is byte-identical before and after, so it is a labelling repair with
no behavioural reach.

## Seven tickets filed, each with its coordination block

Two tiers, one blocker, three recon findings, one UL proposal — all in `Todo` with the
`Deferral` label, so `tb-orchestrator` T1 owns promotion rather than the queue absorbing seven
items at once:

- **THR-1308** / **THR-1309** — the T2 (route + blockade + `create_location`) and T3 (warband +
  `create_group`, absorbing THR-1295) tiers. Both `blockedBy` THR-1310.
- **THR-1310** — the proximity blocker. `findValidTargets` scans all 868 locations and slices
  eight with no proximity filter; T1 could ship around it with `requiresLocation: false`, and
  T2 cannot, because founding a settlement at a site sampled from all 868 is worse than
  charting one.
- **THR-1311** — the settlement genome's faction reach contribution reads `member_of` *incoming
  on a location*, which the schema forbids (`targetNodeType: 'actor'`). Measured: **0 such edges
  across 730 locations**. Not live corruption — a dead term, with a comment asserting behaviour
  the graph shape cannot express.
- **THR-1312** — **17 of 17** lairs are named `Lair 0`, `Lair 1`, …. Newly urgent rather than
  merely untidy: slice 5 just pointed a whole behaviour family at the wilderness, so a surface
  that was rarely visited is now a primary destination, and a chart verb that christens *The
  Saltway Ring* then points the player at *Lair 7*.
- **THR-1313** — `worldSeed.ts`'s prefix ternary has byte-identical branches. Filed as a
  question, not a cleanup: collapsing it silently ratifies current behaviour, which is the open
  question.
- **THR-1314** — the UL proposal for *work / holding / kind row / christening / failure-name
  register*, carrying one real collision: **`holding` is already in the UL** as a group-cohesion
  prose state (`bound · holding · frayed · breaking`), quoted in the rulebook. Both senses are
  player-facing, so it goes to Christian as a naming call rather than being settled by
  whichever landed second.
