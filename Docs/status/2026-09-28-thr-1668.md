# 2026-09-28 — THR-1668: a mortal who walks to an encounter starts it on arrival

**Ticket:** [THR-1668](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1668) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1633-written-encounters-land.md` § S2, § S4b (deferral from THR-1641) · **Pillars:** Engine (the arrival commitment moves onto the unified board); Content: N/A (no template or prose change); UI: N/A (no surface change — headless evidence per THR-688 rule C).

## What was wrong

Four in five encounter journeys ended with the mortal doing something else. On `main`, 10 of 51 arrivals on seed 42 (and 15 of 70 on seed 99) started the encounter the mortal walked to within two ticks.

The ticket suspected the recency penalty stamped at departure. That penalty is real, but it was not the cause. THR-1639's arrival commitment multiplied the goal's `finalScore`, and since THR-1349 the live decision is made by the unified board, which ranks encounters on `valuePerTick × desire × forecastFit` and never reads `finalScore`. The commitment moved only the legacy pick, which the board then overwrote. The board also sees only the scorer's top five, so a goal ranked lower could not win at all. Measured with the new probe: the goal was always still on the list at arrival ("kept"), and it was selected only when the board happened to agree.

## What shipped

- `scoreUnifiedBoard` takes an optional `arrivalGoal` (template + destination) and multiplies that encounter's entry by `ARRIVAL_GOAL_COMMITMENT_MULTIPLIER`. The instance at the destination gets it if one is there; otherwise every instance does. The entry carries `arrivalCommitment` on the `decision_board_comparison` trace.
- `phaseAgentDecision` passes the goal to the board and appends it to `topCandidates` when it ranks below the top five.
- `ARRIVAL_GOAL_COMMITMENT_MULTIPLIER` 1.5 → **2.5**, swept at the board: 1.5 → 50%, 2 → 62%, 2.5 → 79%, 3 → 78%. It is still one decision, then consumed; a board that has genuinely moved (e.g. the forecast now sits far outside the mortal's window) still wins.
- New probe `readers/journeys.ts`: arrival → start share, plus the board's verdict on the goal and each journey's fate. Evidence: `Docs/audits/2026-09-25-living-world-data/output/journeys-2026-09-28-thr1668.json`.

**`ANOMALY_SITE_MAX_HEX_DISTANCE` stays at 1 — agent call on the gate, open to veto.** The Done-when asked for the bound raised to 2 with ≥ 3 anomaly templates and firings at or above `main`. With the fix, bound 2 fires 7 · 6 anomaly templates but 1,713 · 2,015 firings (−11% · −6%). That cost is no longer wasted arrivals, which now land. It comes from more mortals making two-hex trips. Bound 1 with the fix already clears the ≥ 3 bar with firings level with `main` over the four seeds. That was the outcome the bound-2 raise was meant to buy.
