# THR-1974 — Hiring a free company: a liege pays, feeds and commands the soldiers it pays

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** Built from `Docs/plans/2026-10-19-thr-1974-hiring-a-free-company.md` (decision THR-1939). A hire is the crown's bought oath; no new edge, no contract system. Four general rules, all behind `CROWN_WAGES_ENABLED`:

- **Price.** `oathValueOf` (`crown/eligibility.ts`) = max(income, the upkeep a faction fields, or, for a standing-army faction fielding nothing, the upkeep of what `wouldRaiseSize` says it would raise). `affordableGift` and `giftCost` read it; pride still reads income. `spawnArmy` now uses the same `wouldRaiseSize`.
- **Wages.** A new step in the `crown` phase, between loyalty and the tax: the liege pays each vassal army the vassal's purse left unpaid, whole armies in id order, from its purse above `LIEGE_WAGE_RESERVE_DAYS` (2) days of its own upkeep. Stamps `paidThroughTick` and `liegePaidTick`; writes `armiesPaidByLiege` / `wagesFromLiege` / `wagesToVassals` beside the purse verdict. Trace `oath_wages`; chronicle `wages_taken_up` / `wages_stopped` (one per pair per 5 days, one per liege per pay day). The liege's Means counts yesterday's wages.
- **Command.** `commanderFactionOf` (`warAims/enemy.ts`): the paying liege while the stamp is fresh (12 ticks), read from the live `sworn_to` edge. Used by the raise objective, re-aim and pick, defence, siege start and battle start; `commandBarsAttack` keeps the own faction's halls and towns barred.
- **Supply.** `supplyFactionsOf` adds the liege's towns; `isSupplyHost` / `resolveSupplyLine` take a list.

**UI.** Army sheet: "Holding where it stands for hold of Witness Skyfield, which pays its soldiers: no enemy within reach." (liege routes, tooltip `ui.army.paidBy`). Fealty: "Its liege pays its soldiers." (partial variant). Liege Purse: "It pays the soldiers of The Scarlet Company." The vassal's own Purse no longer says its soldiers slip away when its liege paid them. Debug: Oaths table wage columns, Armies tab "Fights for" / "Fed by", `__DEBUG.getArmyPaymaster`, lever `__DEBUG.setFactionWealth`.

**Measured** (`npm run measure:crown-wages`, seeds 42/99/7, 1080 ticks, medium): no zero-price company day; 0 reserve breaches; kill 1 never fires; 0 marches on a paymaster; kill 3 max 0%. Companies field armies on 12% (seed 42) and 33% (seed 7) of sworn days, none sworn on 99 — under half, so [THR-2020](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2020) (a liege pays for its vassal's raise) is filed. Reach: Iron Wolves on seed 42 reached 40 days (main today: 37; the plan's 45 was an older main), seed 99 identical to main. Rollback: with the switch off, a 300-tick seed-42 run is hash-identical to main in faction wealth, army disbands and objective picks.
