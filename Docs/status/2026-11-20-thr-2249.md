# THR-2249 — A place you tend fills the Mandate by what it cost

**Shipped 2026-11-20.** Ticket 2 of 3 of plan `Docs/plans/2026-11-19-thr-2245-a-well-played-age-can-be-won.md` (Decisions 3 and 4).

- **The credit.** `mandateCastCredit` (`src/engine/mandateLedger.ts`, pure): `essencePaid × MANDATE_CAST_POINTS_PER_ESSENCE × MANDATE_DEED_BAND_FACTOR[band]` on the cast's sphere when it is a Mandate sphere, keyed to the place (so rival wear can take it back). `MandateCredit.source` gains `'cast'`.
- **The hook.** `mandateCastForPlace` (`src/engine/unifiedActionResolution.ts`) runs beside `mandateDeedForChapter` on the resolving step (rolled route and the no-roll end): only the ascendant's own `source === 'player'` cast on a location of either tier credits. It rides the deed pipe (`mandateDeedCredits` → `pendingMandateCredits` → `phaseMandate`), adds one aftermath row ("Your hand on {place} raised your Mandate in {Sphere}.", fallback "a place you hold") and the existing `mandate.deed_credit` trace with `source: 'cast'`. The pressure level-up credit is unchanged.
- **The fit.** `MANDATE_CAST_POINTS_PER_ESSENCE = 1.0` held on the first try. `npm run census:agency -- --all --arms none,greedy,full --ticks 900` (2026-11-20, 30 min): `win predicate: hands-off finishes 0/3 (max 0) ✓ · full finishes before Doom 0.8 3/3 (min 2) ✓ · chapters-only finishes before Doom 0.8 0/3 (max 0) ✓ · chapters-only ≥0.1 above hands-off 3/3 (min 2) ✓ · purse refused 675/832 place casts (0.81, min 0.25) ✓ → HOLDS`. The full player finished at t616 (Doom 0.74), t494 (0.59) and t604 (0.76) on seeds 42 / 99 / 7; hands-off and chapters-only both ended on Doom.
- **Review fix.** The cold reviewer found that a place card with no sphere of its own (Claim Dominion, Shift Dominion) pays from the god's primary yet earned nothing. `preparePlayerCast` now stamps the charged sphere on the action (`UnifiedAction.castSphere`) and the credit reads it, falling back to `sphereAffinity`. The census reading stands: its place cards all carry their own sphere, so the charged sphere equals it.
- **Noticed.** Seed 99's full player wins at Doom 0.59, earlier than the plan projected (it assumed the `success_at_cost` floor). The predicate does not test "too easy"; the next warm playtest is where that reads.
- **Not done here.** A cast credit does not join the Mandate block's "last deeds" list: that list renders a mortal's line, and a place line there is a UI change. The aftermath row tells the player.
