# THR-2069 — Salt is a good

**Shipped 2026-10-27.** M3: Dynamic Economy. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-26-thr-2069-salt.md`. The game talked about salt everywhere (two smuggling scenes, the war-aims and purse examples, ~155 prose lines) and had none.

**What changed.** `salt` is the ninth common good: strategic, matter/order, value 1.0, scarcity sensitivity 0.6, renewable at 0.2. It is seeded on steppe 40–70, plateau 45–75, desert 40–75, rocky desert 30–55, sand dunes 25–50 and coast 15–35 (no mountains). It has land prose and Livelihood tier lines. The Salt Train at the Ford now leaves salt in the hamlet on every success band of both variants (a big gain on a critical, a small one otherwise); failure bands bring none. Smuggle Goods is unchanged, because its salt leaves the town. The Salt Monopoly scene is unchanged. Salt's scarcity and glut chronicle lines are its own (`RESOURCE_OWN_CHRONICLE_TEMPLATES`), because the strategic pool speaks of builders waiting.

**Review-gate fix.** The review found that a small salt delivery into a busy town with no salt read as "short of salt" (5 salt against the town's demand), so a win showed a shortage. `shiftStock` (`src/engine/economyShift.ts`) now lands a good a town lacks at no less than the quantity that reads adequate at that town's demand. A quiet town still gets exactly the delivered amount.

**Seeded tests moved.** `resourceSeeding.test.ts` "empty resources for desert terrain" now uses tundra (still empty), and a new case pins desert to salt only. No other seeded figure moved.

**Evidence.** `npm run gate` PASS (test, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, cli-smoke). New `saltGood.test.ts` (ford bands, no Smuggle Goods row, smuggled salt +5 into a saltless town counts in the bearing index). 30-day headless census (`tick 720`, medium): salt places 21 / 14 / 15 and roads carrying salt 14 of 60 / 19 of 67 / 17 of 61 on seeds 42 / 99 / 7; salt leads 12 / 18 / 12 manifests, under the half-of-roads kill line. 1920×1080 Playwright screenshot of The Forge of Sorrow (plateau city, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium&nofog`), whose place page reads *"Salt is cheap in this place, and the curing sheds are never empty."*

**Note.** On a settlement the Livelihood row is written by the settlement economy (food and wares), so salt's tier lines show on non-settlement places; on towns salt is named in the place's land prose.
