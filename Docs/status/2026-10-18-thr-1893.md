# THR-1893 — Screen frame slice 1: the Ledger rail

**Shipped 2026-10-18.** Slice 1 of the screen-frame plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1875-screen-frame.md` § F1 slice 1, § F7, § F8). The right rail is now drawn from lists: Threads, then the Chronicle with the World-Soul line and the tabs Now · Years · Ledger. It folds to a 56 px strip. World Pulse is gone.

- **One list per kind (Law 58).** `src/data/frame-registry.ts` holds `LEDGER_RAIL_SECTIONS`, `LEDGER_TABS` and `LEDGER_LINES`. Their bodies are `Record`-keyed maps (`LEDGER_SECTION_COMPONENTS`, `LEDGER_TAB_PANELS`). `frameRegistry.test.ts` checks that every opening-reveal surface is claimed exactly once. A `NOT_YET_LISTED` set names the surfaces that the god rail (THR-1894) and the top bar (THR-1875) will list. `L` is declared once (`LEDGER_RAIL_HOTKEY`) and reaches `TOP_BAR_KEYS` through `FRAME_HOTKEYS`.
- **The view model.** `views/ledgerRail.ts` (selector `ledgerRail`) carries the World-Soul line (its banding moved here from `WorldSoulIndicator`), the Now window (`CHRONICLE_NOW_SEASONS` = 4), the year groups, the Ledger tab's rows and the three strip counts. The mood line now changes once a season; it used to flicker every tick.
- **One chapter predicate.** `engine/threadedChapters.ts` builds the ledger's rows once. The reader's default view, the Ledger tab and the unread count all read `selectThreadedChapters` / `countThreadedChapters`. Pressing a tab row opens the reader at that chapter (`initialRecordKey`). The tab marks its new rows before it records them as seen (Law 40).
- **Grow before scroll (Law 57).** The Chronicle's 320 px cap is gone; the Chronicle takes the rail's free height.
- **The fold.** The rail folds when the player folds it (Fold control or `L`, remembered) or when the Inspector is open. The Inspector input (`GameScreenState.inspectorOpen`) stays false until THR-1876 writes it. With the Inspector open, pressing a strip item opens the flyout (an `Overlay` in the `hud` band), and Esc closes it.
- **Moved or retired.** The speed stepper moved to the top bar's Time slot (`ui.sim_speed` now reads "Speed"). The World-Soul left the top bar. Deleted: `WorldPulse.tsx`, `worldPulseCount.ts`, `WorldSoulIndicator.tsx`, the four `ui.world_pulse_*` ids, the `world_pulse` reveal row and the Chapter Ledger launcher. The thread pip now reads "A chapter waits".
- **Debug:** `getFrameState()` (the `ledgerRail` part and the measured `mapShare`), `setFrameInspectorOpen(open)`, `getView('ledgerRail')`.

Evidence: a worktree Vite build with Playwright on `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440. The rail is 340 / 420 px open and 56 px folded at both sizes. `L` folds the rail (`foldedBy: 'player'`), and the fold survives a reload. With the Inspector forced open, `foldedBy` is `'inspector'`; pressing the Chronicle strip item opens the flyout with one escape layer, and Esc closes it to none. Console: 0 errors.

**Open:** with both rails open at 1920×1080, the measured `mapShare` is 0.597, below the 0.60 floor. The rails are at their token widths (the token share is 0.627). The shortfall is the top bar, which measures 97 px instead of 48 because the Doom item stacks three rows. Slice 3 (THR-1875) rebuilds the bar as one row, which clears the floor. For the same reason, the folded share at 1920×1080 is 0.732, below the plan's 0.76 (it would be 0.768 with a 48 px bar). At 2560×1440 it is 0.628 open and 0.760 folded.
