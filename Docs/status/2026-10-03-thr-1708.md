### 2026-10-03 — THR-1708: player text no longer says "The The", names the wrong card, or credits a nudge nobody played

**The bug.** Cold playtest round 2 quoted three wrong lines. "The The Builders Fellowship intelligence is thorough": the complication template supplies "The" and the faction's own name already starts with it. "The Living Balm completed Observe": the tester had cast Piercing Gaze, the name on the card and in the Codex. And "Your nudge left … behind in the world" closed every encounter, including the chapters The First resolved alone while the player was in menus.

**The fix.** A new `substituteFactionName` (`src/engine/factionNameSubstitution.ts`) handles `{faction}` with the article in mind. When the template writes "The/the {faction}" and the name already has an article, the name's article is dropped. A bare `{faction}` keeps the name as written. A lowercase fallback that opens a sentence gets a capital. Both `{faction}` resolvers use it: complications and `enrichProse`. Resolved-action lines and the aftermath overview now call `playerFacingTemplateName` (`spellName ?? name`). The overview says "Your nudge left…" only when `godTouchedEncounter` is true: a nudge hand was committed on some step, essence was spent on a recorded choice, or a hand is live. Otherwise it reads "<mortal>'s choices left…".

**Evidence.** `thr1708PlayerText.test.ts` covers all three. Every complication line with `{faction}` is substituted with "The Builders Fellowship" and with the "the faction" fallback, and no output has a double article. CLI sweep, seed 42, medium map, 120 ticks, over events, chronicle, chapter archive and live actions (3.6 MB of text, 530 archived chapters): 0 × "The The", 0 × "completed Observe", 0 × "Your nudge left", and 971 × "'s choices left" for the encounters mortals resolved alone. One existing test asserted the old wording ("completed … Dream"). It now expects the spell name, "Oneiric Sending".
