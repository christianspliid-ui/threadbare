# 2026-10-10 — THR-1801: a chapter's second step opens with an empty hand

**Ticket:** [THR-1801](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1801) · **Found by:** cold playtest round 3, skimmer persona: "my cards were silently re-used and charged for step 2."

- **Engine — the step advance spends the hand.** `advanceStep` (`unifiedActionLifecycle.ts`) now drops `activeNudges` when it moves to the next step. Dealt card ids repeat across steps (`dealt.<memberId>`), so step 1's hand used to match step 2's cards. Step 2 then resolved with cards nobody paid for, and the stage restored them as `committedIds`. The final-step and fight-result returns keep the hand, because the aftermath dispatch reads it. The per-step record stays in `choiceHistory` (THR-1123).
- **UI — the hand restarts per step.** `useNudgeHand` keys its selection on `handStepKey` (`actionId:stepIndex`) and resets during render when the key changes. The veil stays mounted across a step change, so step 1's picks used to survive by themselves and were charged again at the step-2 commit.
- **Proof.** `freshStepHand.thr1801.test.tsx` drives a two-step chapter through the real builder, `advanceStep` and the hook, with a same-step negative control. Browser check at 1920×1080 on *Decipher Old Markings*: step 1 commits Whisper + Press The Odds (38 → 34 Mind), step 2 opens with no card pressed and "34 Mind essence left", and `getEncounterNudges` reports `activeNudges: []` and `committedCost: 0`.
