# THR-1101 batch 10 — the hire family stops reading as mad-libs

**Date:** 2026-08-14 · **Issue:** [THR-1101](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1101) · **Lane:** `tb-opus-pickup`

## What shipped

The **`hire` family is drained**. All 9 token-carrying `encounterType: 'hire'` templates are authored out of the `{adj}`/`{verb}`/`{noun}`/`{action}` shape — 74 token lines across 77 prose fields:

`recruit_militia` · `sway_mercenary` · `court_noble` · `bind_spirit` · `rally_faithful` · `hire_guide` · `debt_collection` · `caravan_guard` · `guild_initiation_trial`

Register: **a hire buys a promise, and the promise is only priced when keeping it costs the hired party something.** Every template in the family runs the same three-beat arithmetic at a different scale — find the party, agree the terms, then discover what the terms are worth on the day they bite. That single premise carried a militia of farmers, a mercenary band, a noble's retainer, a bound spirit, a congregation, a trail guide, a debtor, a caravan escort and a guild trial without the voice fragmenting. Calibrated against `encounter.trial_by_combat`, the THR-1036 exemplar.

Predicate re-derived at pickup: **39** of 168 templates, matching batch 9's hand-off figure exactly, family breakdown line-for-line (`explore` 30/194, `hire` 9/74). Eight consecutive runs of a stable scan. Post-batch: **30**, one family.

## The ratio

`hire` came in **6 full mad-libs to 3 wedged-token** — identical to `steal`, against `acquire` 8/5, `create` 6/7, `build` 7/9, `trade` 7/8, `assist` 6/9, `lead` 5/9. Eight families in, the spread still runs 5/9 to 6/3 with no trend, and batch 7's conclusion stands unchanged: the ratio picks between two candidate families and does not substitute for reading a sample.

The split was legible in the source here: the six mad-libs are the contiguous "HIRE (6 templates, all new)" block, authored as scaffolding in one sitting; the three wedged-token ones were written later, individually, as real prose with tokens pushed into working sentences.

## The finding — the render pass caught six defects, and the instrument itself was wrong

Batch 9 established (impediment #567) that the token scan is a *scoping* instrument, not a defect detector, and recommended rendering the whole family rather than the diff. This batch did that and found six defects, **two of them pre-existing on lines carrying no token**:

- `guild_initiation_trial.prove_worth` held `{they} stand for`, rendering **"she stand for"** — untokened, so invisible to the campaign's own scan.
- The same template's `criticalSuccessAfterimage` carried three vagueness hits (`something`, `way`, `things`) in an authored line no batch had touched.
- `{es}` **is not a token.** This batch wrote `finish{es}` reaching for verb agreement; only `{s}` exists, so the line shipped a literal `{es}` into the rendered output. Caught by the unresolved-token check, not by review.
- Three `the balance` / `nothing` hits of this batch's own, all rephrased at no cost.

**The instrument lesson is the durable one.** The first version of the bare-verb check used a hand-written allowlist of common verbs. It passed clean — and then a read of the rendered output showed *"she begin"* and *"she have"*, both outside the list. An allowlist encodes the bugs you already thought of; replacing it with a generic rule (a `she`/`he` subject followed by a word that is neither auxiliary nor `-s`-carrying) immediately found both. A probe that reports zero is only as trustworthy as its ability to report non-zero.

## The corpus sweep — a class the campaign cannot fix, quantified

Widening that generic check from the family to the corpus flagged **52 lines of 1,394**, of which **21 are genuine** (the other 31 are correct past-tense verbs and adverbs — *"he demanded"*, *"she privately"* — which a naive guard would "fix" into errors). Filed as [THR-1107](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1107) with the full list, sequenced behind this campaign's final batch.

The one that matters: **`trial_by_combat` renders "she stop"**. It is the THR-1036 worked exemplar every batch of this campaign calibrates its register against, and it has been broken for he/she agents the entire time. Nothing could have caught it — no token, green tests, and the source line reads correctly.

Deliberately **not** folded into this batch: the 21 lines sit across ~16 templates in 8 families, and a mechanical grammar sweep inside a diff reviewed as authored prose serves neither.
