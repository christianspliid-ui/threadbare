# 2026-09-29 — THR-1571: the power runtime S1 — spells that work

**Ticket:** [THR-1571](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1571) · **Project:** Powers & Item Generation · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-29-thr-1571-power-runtime.md` § S1 · **Pillars:** Engine (the one cast resolver, the preconditions, seeded knowing, per-bearer state for carried spells); Content (agency / arena / castReach / castProse on the five spells, two fate-woven ports, eight Strained conditions, UL **Strained** seated — THR-1673); UI: N/A for S1 (engine + data; the sheet already lists spells and conditions — the visible cast is S2, [THR-1670](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1670)).

## What was wrong

No mortal in the world held a spell (the only writer of `knows_spell` needs a caster in the deciding tier), and a spell that was cast charged its price and applied nothing: `use × Power` read the outcome and dropped `appliedEffects` and `backlashEffect`, behind a coin keyed on the caster id's *length*. The `reach_drain` price wrote a property nothing reads; backlash could only fire on a fixed-coin failure; one caster's cooldown was everyone's; `dispel` deleted the shared condition node it matched (curing every bearer) and deleted possessions outright; `teleport` / `forced_move` executed nothing; the spell-weaver mastery arm of `isCaster` could never match its hyphenated id.

## What changed

- **`resolveCast`** (`src/engine/spellCasting.ts`) is the only path to a cast. The band that already landed decides it (`CAST_LANDED_BANDS`); effects go through `executeEffect` → `applyExecutionResult`; backlash is read against the band by the price layer (`BACKLASH_ELIGIBLE_BANDS_BY_TRIGGER`) on its own seeded stream; the soul price is queued as `spell_price`; `'spell_cast'` is raised on the caster's carried triggers; `spell.cast_resolved` / `spell.backlash` trace it. `use × Power` passes `ctx.outcome` and refuses a fate-woven spell (`not_castable`).
- **Strain** lands `condition.strained.<reach>` through `applyConditionToActor`; the dead `domainCapability` write is gone. **Cooldowns** live in `GameState.castCooldowns`, keyed `caster::spell`.
- **`dispel`** lifts one bearing and silences an item for `DISPEL_ITEM_SUPPRESS_TICKS` (new `suppressRequests` channel) — never `remove_node`. **`teleport` / `forced_move`** land on a place-tier Location (`castRelocation.ts`), traced `effect.teleported`.
- **Carried spells**: a fate-woven spell's node carries its `passiveEffects`; stateless primitives only. Found while building it and fixed: `applySuppressions` and the effect walker keyed state by *node* id, so a seal on one bearer would have silenced a carried spell world-wide (and a trigger's default 6-tick cooldown would have run for all bearers at once). A spell node's state is now keyed by the bearing edge (`attachmentStateKey`), the agreements' precedent.
- **Seeded knowing** (`seedSpellKnowing`, worldgen tail): every caster starts knowing and wielding one spell, sorted picks, no draws. `isCaster` moved to `engine/casterIdentity.ts` with `'spell-weaver'` added.
- Two fate-woven ports: **The Wayfinding** and **Height Anchor**.

## Not done here (deliberately)

- The modifier families (`duration`, `aura`, `conditional`, `stacking`, `decay`) have no per-cast channel, so a *cast* spell's modifier effects (Hollow Crown's aura and conditional, Veilwalk's shadow bonus) still apply nothing — noted on S2 ([THR-1670](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1670)), where casting in scenes makes it matter.
- `transfer`, `compel`; spells as divine gifts and found tomes ([THR-1672](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1672)); the reserve upkeep switch (not needed at this cost).
