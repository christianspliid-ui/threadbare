# THR-1918 — Realms keep their members

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** Realms and guilds lost most of their members over a run because `excommunicate` needed no reason.

**Cause.** `executeExcommunicate` (`src/engine/phaseFactionActions.ts`) scored every member and always struck the top one, whatever its standing. The action costs nothing and needs only two members, so for a poor faction it was nearly always eligible, and the weighted pick chose it each time its 20-tick cooldown ran out. The struck members had a mean standing of 0.28–0.41, so these were ordinary members, not weak or disloyal ones.

**Fix.** A member is struck only with a cause: it also belongs to a rival faction, or its standing is at or below `FACTION_EXCOMMUNICATE_MAX_REPUTATION` (0.1, `src/data/faction-constants.ts`). A new prerequisite, `has_excommunication_cause`, takes the action out of the pick when no member is at cause, so a faction does not lose its action slot on an empty purge.

**Measured** with `npm run measure:realm-membership` (600 ticks, medium), realm members t0 → t600:

| Seed | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 42 | 31/21/15 → 21/13/1 | 31/21/15 → 24/16/9 |
| 99 | 44/27/42 → 30/13/32 | 44/27/42 → 36/21/30 |
| 7 | 30/63/25 → 10/51/14 | 30/63/25 → 23/51/22 |

Every realm keeps at least 60% of its members. Excommunications across all factions dropped from 205–248 per run to 61–87, and the struck members' mean standing is now 0.01–0.15.

**Side effect on a heavy test.** The fix re-rolls every world, and seed 99's master success rose to 0.724, just above the per-seed ceiling of 0.70 in `engagementWindow.invariant.test.ts`. Seed 42's fell from 0.696 to 0.662. Across seven seeds the mean moved from 0.620 to 0.647. The master row now pools seeds 42 and 99 (0.695), as THR-1572 already did for the journeyman and expert rows.
