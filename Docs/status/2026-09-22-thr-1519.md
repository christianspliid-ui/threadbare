# 2026-09-22 — THR-1519: appointment primitive slice 3 — a work whose payoff is a meeting, and the kept-appointment milestone

**Ticket:** [THR-1519](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1519) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Pillars:** Engine, Content (UI N/A — the seed, the chip, the sheet row and the debug readout are slice 1's and read the same record). **Plan:** [`Docs/plans/2026-09-21-thr-1479-appointment-primitive.md`](../plans/2026-09-21-thr-1479-appointment-primitive.md) § Done when › *Slice 3 — the grid*.

## What shipped

**A work can arrange a meeting.** `StrategicActionTemplate` gains an optional `appointmentPayoff` — `{ meeting: ContentQuery, seedLabel, missed: { query, seedLabel, delayTicks? }, delayTicks?, windowTicks? }`. On completion, `maybePlantAppointmentPayoff` (all three completion arms of `strategicActionLifecycle.ts`) plants an appointment seed on the actor: the place is where the work stands, resolved exactly as the catalyst anchor is; the counterparty is the site when the site is a mortal; the due tick is completion + `UNDERTAKING_APPOINTMENT_DELAY_TICKS` (18). No chance roll — a promise is the work's product, not its wake — so the rng stream of every earlier run is untouched. The first carrier is `create × Agreement` (`UNDERTAKING_CELL_APPOINTMENTS`, the `UNDERTAKING_CELL_CATALYSTS` pattern, overridable per package): the digger arranges a meeting with the one the secret was dug up about, where that mortal stands. Kept branch `#thieves_errand` (the one leverage family with a `hamlet`-accepting member — the kept arm is judged *at* the place), missed branch `#court_errand`.

**One planter, not two.** Slice 1's inline plant in `encounterAftermath.ts` is factored into `plantAppointmentPromise` (`appointments.ts`): live-target and hex checks, the `over_max` refusal, the `owes_favor` promise edge, and the `appointment_planted` trace, which now carries `source: 'encounter' | 'undertaking'`. The aftermath binds `$here` / `$cast:*` and hands resolved ids across; the lifecycle resolves the site and does the same. The 14 slice-1 aftermath tests and the 28 module tests are unchanged and green. The seed's query resolves at a new `ContentQuerySite`, `undertaking_appointment`, keyed on `UNDERTAKING_APPOINTMENT_REACTION_ID`.

**The gate.** `check:undertaking`'s `catalysts` block fails a payoff whose meeting or missed query is empty (a promise that cannot be kept *or* missed is the cutscene-with-a-walk the ruling forbids), and the meeting is recorded in the write set so a cell whose only product is a meeting is not read as vacuous. `undertakingCellAppointments.test.ts` asserts family coverage per settlement tier rather than assuming it.

**The milestone.** `GraphCondition` gains `{ type: 'agent_kept_appointment', minCount }` — the count of `appointment_kept` Event nodes the mortal `participated_in`; missed ones never count; fails soft to `false` on a missing agent. `ambition_uncover_secrets` lists it as a fourth milestone, `secrets_kept_word`, `completion: 2 of 4` — a kept word is a fourth road to the same end, not a harder end. `LIVE_CELL_NOTES` row updated; the undertaking grid regenerated.

**Declined, per the plan:** `use × Agreement:appointment` — keeping an appointment is a journey the decision phase makes, not a work at a site with checkpoints.

## A slice-1 gap the payoff exposed, fixed here

**Keeping is presence.** The first live probe (seed 42, medium, 150 ticks) showed three `create × Agreement` completions, three promise edges written — and three still on the sheet unbroken at tick 150 with zero `appointment_kept` Events, though the diggers had stood at the place on the due tick. The kept arm handed the seed to the resolution ladder with `resolutionLocationId = place`; the place was a *ruins* Location (the Forge of Sorrow), `#thieves_errand` is gated to settlements, the query withered, and the withered path consumed the seed without the kept bookkeeping — so a kept word left a dangling promise and an uncounted milestone. `evaluateEncounterSeeds` now records a kept appointment from both exits through one `recordKeptAppointment` writer (fired *and* withered — the trace's `resolvedTemplateId` reads `withered:<query>` on the second). Same probe after: three `appointment_kept` Events (t120, t144, t144), two mortals with a kept word, no dangling promise. Family coverage at non-settlement sites is a content observation for the retro (no shipped family has a location-ungated member that reads as a meeting); the primitive is correct without it.
