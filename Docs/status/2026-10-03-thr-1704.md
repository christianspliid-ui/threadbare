### 2026-10-03 — THR-1704: the Threads panel shows The First the moment the bond lands

**The bug.** Cold playtest round 2: a tester bonded their First, came back to the map, and Threads said "No Threads". `createAgentFromMeeting` writes The First and its `the_first` thread into the live graph in place, and nothing touched the runtime. The Threads and retinue memos key on graph identity plus `worldVersion`, so they kept the empty pre-bond answer. The clock comes back paused, so no tick refreshed them either.

**The fix.** GameView now bonds through `bondFirstFromMeeting` (`src/components/Game/meetingBond.ts`). It calls `createAgentFromMeeting` and then `touchStructure(runtime)`. A new node and new edges are a structural change, so the encounter and foreshadowing caches learn the new mortal too.

**Evidence.** A hook test drives `useAgentInteraction` across the bond with no tick in between. The First is listed in `threadedNodes` and the retinue. A falsification arm shows that the bare `createAgentFromMeeting` call leaves the panel empty. Browser check on `?view=game&firstunmet&size=medium`: after the bond, the clock is still paused, `getOpeningState().firstBonded` is true, and Threads lists Solenne.
