# THR-2196 — The First's first chapter tells one story

**Shipped 2026-11-12.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · Cold playtest round 7. No plan doc; scope from the ticket.

1. **A lost join test is not a joining.** Nine guild join chapters had one ending, the welcome, so a failed test read "Seren is a Fellow" and paid a tally. `bf.join`, `ac.join`, `mct.join`, `rb.join`, `tg.join`, `uk.join`, `ag.join`, `lk.join` and `mc.join` now carry an `aftermathConfig.fallback.byOutcome` band on `failure`, `critical_failure` and `contested_lost` (the `cg.join` pattern): one "sent back" reaction, no effects, no chip. Success endings are unchanged. `ts.join` (a lost reading still joins, with one sphere resisted) and `hod.join` (the god chooses whether to bless the oath) keep their endings by design.
2. **bf.join waits on the god's hand.** Its single step declares `deal: { count: DEAL_DEFAULT_COUNT, tags: ['craft'] }`, the THR-2179 pure fill, so `stepOffersPlayerMove` is true and the chapter no longer reads "This chapter was Seren's alone".
3. **The meeting tells the trade the bond writes.** `DilemmaTemplate.castsTrade` (optional) names the trades a dilemma's backstory casts the mortal in. `selectDilemmasScored` takes an optional `tradeRole` and deals such a dilemma only to a candidate holding one of those roles (`dilemmaFitsTrade`, kill switch `MEETING_DILEMMA_TRADE_GATE`). Tagged: `AX-HEART-01` (smith's apprentice), `AX-GOLD-03` (merchant's apprentice), `RC-SHADOW-02` (scribe's apprentice), `DS-ENERGY-03` (works the forge). MeetTheFirstFlow passes the candidate's vignette trade; the threading rite passes the bound mortal's `npcRole`. No PRNG change: the gate is a pure eligibility filter.

**Evidence:** `npm run gate` PASS on the closing head; `src/engine/__tests__/firstFirstChapter.thr2196.test.ts` (17 tests) resolves every patched join on each losing outcome, asserts bf.join's step offers a move, and asserts a Shrine-keeper candidate is never dealt `AX-HEART-01` over 200 seeds while the ungated control still deals it. Browser-verify exempt: the one UI-pillar line passes an argument to the dilemma deal; what changes is which dilemma is dealt, asserted in the unit test.
