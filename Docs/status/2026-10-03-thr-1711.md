### 2026-10-03 — THR-1711: six small interface faults from cold playtest round 2

**The bugs.** Each was hit by at least one round-2 tester. (1) Escape, pressed to close the cards, also threw away The First's dream: the premonition passed its decline handler to `Modal` as `onClose`, so Escape and a backdrop click declined it for good. (2) Clicking a card's name opened the codex instead of selecting the card, because the whole title was the codex link with `stopPropagation`; and re-clicking a selected card unselected it, the opposite of the setup screens' "click again to choose". (3) "Attention" was styled like the Doom and Omen tab labels, which open a detail, so testers clicked it and nothing happened. (4) Map labels flickered into piles: overlap removal ran once per 60 ms and the frames between rendered every label, and the collision boxes used 20/12 px estimates under labels that render at 16/13/11 px, hung below their icon. (5) Toasts were `fixed` to the viewport's bottom-right and covered the Chronicle. (6) A pause pressed inside a popup was undone on close: Space and the play button flipped `running`, the interrupt hold forced it back to false, and closing restored the state saved when the popup opened.

**The fix.** (1) `PremonitionModal` ignores Escape and backdrop clicks; only its decline button declines. (2) `CardFace` renders the name as plain card text and puts the codex link on a small ⓘ mark beside it; `ActionDrawer` keeps a re-clicked card armed (Escape still disarms). (3) `AttentionPoolIndicator` is a `role="meter"` with a help cursor and the shared Tooltip ("Nothing to click: it is a gauge") in place of a native `title`. (4) `LocationLabelOverlay` re-applies the last collision verdict between recomputes (`applyCachedVisibility`), and location labels pass their real font size and top anchor to `estimateBBox`. (5) `ToastStack` is `absolute` inside the map area, at the top: the browser pass showed a bottom-anchored toast sitting on the action drawer's Cast button. (6) `useInterruptAutoPause` gains `toggleIfHeld` / `pauseIfHeld`; while an interrupt holds the clock, the hotkey, the play button and the avatar-arrival pause change the state the clock returns to instead of `running`. The time control shows that saved state while a popup is open, with a "held · runs on after" / "held · stays paused" status line, so the button shows what a press will do (the review gate caught that it showed Play while a press paused).

**Evidence.** `npm run gate`: typecheck, build and encounter PASS. The test lane's only failures were 8 NudgeCard snapshots, which changed by the new wrapper span around the card name; they were updated after checking the diff. New or extended tests for each item: `PremonitionModal.test.tsx` (Escape, backdrop, decline button), `ActionCard.test.tsx` + `ActionDrawer.test.tsx` (name selects, ⓘ opens codex, re-click keeps armed), `AttentionPoolIndicator.test.tsx` (meter, no native title, hover explains it), `labelCollision.test.ts` (cached verdict, real-size and top-anchored boxes), `ToastStackAnchor.test.tsx`, `useInterruptAutoPause.test.ts` (pause inside a popup survives close, with a falsification arm).
