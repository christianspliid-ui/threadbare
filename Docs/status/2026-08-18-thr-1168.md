# THR-1168 — the registration cue's key space was retired out from under it

**2026-08-18 · execution session (`tb-opus-pickup`) · [THR-1168](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1168/two-authored-encounter-audio-moments-have-no-live-caller-wire-or)**

THR-1167 kept two encounter audio moments rather than deleting them, and filed this
ticket to force a wire-or-retire call on each. One is answered and shipped; the
other is Christian's and is parked with the question put to him.

## Member 2 — the registration cue: **retired**

The ticket named the consequence-chip block in `EncounterVeil.tsx` as the producer,
on the stated ground that `ENCOUNTER_REGISTRATION_SEMITONES` "is already keyed by
exactly that vocabulary." **It is not, and the two vocabularies share no keys at all.**

- The semitone map is keyed by the **engine effect** vocabulary of canonical UI spec
  §4.1 — `intelligence`, `condition_attachment`, `reputation_tally`,
  `reputation_score`, `encounter_seed`, `hidden_mark`, `recent_event`,
  `spawn_artifact`, `faction`, `archetype_drift_register` (10 keys).
- The chip's rendered `kind` — what `data-consequence-kind` carries on the named
  surface — is `EncounterStageConsequenceKind`: `prize | standing | toll | wound |
  seed | mark` (6 keys). THR-1082 introduced these *deliberately to replace* the
  mechanical effect buckets: "The six display kinds were mechanical buckets; these
  four are what the change" reads as.
- Intersection: **zero**. Even at the pre-classification wire level
  (`EncounterAftermathChangeKind` in `unifiedAction.ts`: `growth | trait | item |
  reputation | faction_reputation | reputation_tally | shell_state | future_hook`)
  the overlap is a single key, `reputation_tally` — 1 of 8.

So wiring it as instructed would have sent **every** chip through
`?? ENCOUNTER_RESOLVE_SEMITONES_FALLBACK` and played one pitch forever, with the
authored ten-entry design sitting behind a live-looking call proving nothing. That
is the vacuous-wiring shape, and it would have passed a "the cue fires" test.

The alternative producer — sonifying at the engine effect layer, where the
vocabulary actually lives — puts Web Audio inside the deterministic tick pipeline
and fires during headless CLI runs and background/shaping resolutions, which are
exactly the resolutions the player is not attending. Not a real option either.

Retired: `playRegistrationCue`, `registrationCueFrequency`, the first-registration
latch (`hasRegistrationCueFired` / `resetRegistrationCueLatch`) and the four
`ENCOUNTER_REGISTRATION_*` constants, with their tests, behind retirement notes in
both files following the convention already in `encounter-experience-constants.ts`.
This is a **technical** verdict, not a feel one: the module's key space no longer
reaches a player surface, so there is nothing for a consumer to consume.

## Member 1 — the tension reveal: **parked, question put to Christian**

Not the same case, and the mechanical half comes out the other way. `playResolveNote`
is keyed by **reach**, which is live current vocabulary the veil holds at commit —
so unlike Member 2 there is no disqualifier. What the investigation does settle is
that `useThreadReveal` is the wrong *vehicle*: it is a six-phase visual state machine
built to sequence `ThreadOverlay`'s SVG, and that overlay is gone. Wiring it would
mount five timers and re-render the veil six times to drive nothing visible; the
audio would attach directly at the commit and resolve beats instead.

That leaves exactly the question the ticket reserved: **should committing a nudge
hand be followed by ~1.6s of held breath before the outcome lands?** Pure pacing,
no agreed outcome to test against — Christian's fork, and left untouched rather than
half-dismantled while it is open.
