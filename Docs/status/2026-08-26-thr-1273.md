### THR-1273 — `check:encounter` now walks branch arms (2026-08-26)

**The defect.** Every gate in the encounter stack filtered `ActionStepBranch` out of
`template.steps` first. A branch node carries no prose, hand, reach or difficulty of its
own — those live on its arms — so a forked encounter had its plain half checked and green
reported over the rest. Eight blocks were blind: step quality, hand rules, `imageTag`
resolution, the systems quota, the register detectors, the enrichment dry-run, forecast
arithmetic and grant liveness. Measured on the factory's first `personality_fork`, three
editorial defects lived in the unchecked half and would have shipped green; the blindness
also forced `membership_change` into an optional reaction, because the quota could not see
an arm's `successMetadata`.

**The fix.** One canonical descent — `runnableStepSites` in `src/types/unifiedAction.ts`,
the only module all four consumers already import. It yields each arm with its position and
key, so a violation reads `step 1 variant 'negative'` instead of a filtered index naming no
line in the authored file. `plainSteps` is **deleted, not narrowed**: its premise is true of
the *node* and false of the *encounter*, and a helper whose name invites that substitution
is a trap to re-enter. Step count moved to positions — a branch is one position because the
player runs one arm, where `plainSteps` counted it as zero.

**Coverage.** +113 previously-ungated steps across 39 forked templates. Corpus verdict
unchanged (205 checked / 20 clean / 0 failing / 185 on ratchet): the sweep exposed exactly
one real defect in clean content — `swindled_family`'s `positive` arm carried a 5-word
`purposeLine` against a 4-word budget — fixed here, not ratcheted.

**Two tests were working around this gate** and are now repointed (impediment #800).
`encounterImageResolver.test.ts` had already *measured* the blindness under THR-1052 — 42
dead imageTags, the gate reported 41 — and swept around it. `standing-the-line.test.ts`
built synthetic templates substituting each pole's arms as plain steps. Both went green and
left the gate as blind as they found it, which is why the defect was re-found from scratch
and re-paid in full. A test comment explaining what a gate *cannot* do is a bug report
someone filed as a comment.

**Evidence.** 18181/18181 tests · ratchet 3009 unchanged · build clean · 30-tick smoke at
384 agents. Non-vacuous by controlled arm: reverting the descent reddens 7 of the 8 new
contract tests, the 1 that passes being the deliberate control.
