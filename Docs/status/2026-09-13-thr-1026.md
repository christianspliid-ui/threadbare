# THR-1026 — the notice board says which silence it is

**Shipped 2026-09-13.** Deferral from THR-818, Encounter Experience.

THR-818 gave all twelve factions' halls a Guild Postings board. The engine kept
posting to one: `phaseRuinQuestHooks` stamps a ruin only when an *adventurers'
guild* hall sits within `GUILD_QUEST_RADIUS`. So 23 of 26 hall settlements on a
seeded medium world rendered a board that read *"The notice board is quiet. No
contracts have been posted since the guild's last sweep"* — promising a sweep
that, at a merchant consortium or a civic guard, is never coming.

The design question the deferral held open was ruled on 2026-09-11: **one
faction.** An expedition into a ruin is what an adventurers' guild exists to
commission, and the three quest templates are that guild's by voice and id;
widening the posting phase without re-authoring them would put the wrong guild's
words on the board. This pass implements the "answer is one" branch.

## What changed

- **The literal became a named constant.** `RUIN_QUEST_POSTING_FACTION_DEF_ID`
  in `src/engine/ruins/constants.ts` carries the ruling in its doc comment, so
  the *why* sits on the thing itself rather than in a comment beside one of its
  two former copies.
- **One predicate, two callers.** `locationPostsRuinQuestHooks(graph, locationId)`
  is exported from `questHooks.ts`. `findGuildLocations` uses it (behaviour
  unchanged), and so does the panel — so the board's claim about itself is
  answered by the phase that fills it, not by a second copy of the literal.
- **The empty state says which silence it is.** At a posting hall, the old
  temporary copy stands. At any other faction's hall it now reads *"This board
  posts no ruin contracts — delves are The Adventurers Guild's trade"*, with the
  guild carrying its `faction.*` tooltip and a keyboard stop. The name is read
  from `FACTION_DEFINITIONS`, not typed into the copy.
- **Both hall predicates keep their comments**, each naming why they differ.
  Who has a board is wide (THR-818); who posts to it is one (THR-1026). The
  comments exist to stop a later sweep "aligning" them.

## What the sweep found instead

The Done-when asked for a CLI run showing stamped ruins near adventurers' halls.
It showed **zero stamped ruins anywhere** — which made that check vacuous and
turned up a larger finding, now filed as THR-1506.

On seed 42 / medium at tick 175: 103 ruins, **34 of them within radius of a
posting hall**, but only **1 with any clue evidence at all** and none meeting
both conditions. Proximity is not the binding constraint; the clue pipeline is.
The phase itself is healthy — seeding one `knows_clue_of` edge at magnitude 1.0
onto a ruin in radius makes it stamp `ag.elite.lost_city` at the next 30-tick
boundary, while an identically-seeded ruin near only non-adventuring halls
correctly does not. That controlled pair is the non-vacuous proof of the
predicate this ticket was about.

The honest consequence: today the board is empty at all 26 halls, not just the
23. This change makes 23 of them honest about it; THR-1506 is what makes the
other three true.
