# THR-1929 — A templateId sequel waits for its place

**Shipped 2026-10-26.** Built from the design lane's decision and build spec (2026-10-25e).

**What changed.** A ripe encounter seed that names one template (`templateId`) used to spawn wherever the mortal stood, skipping the template's `locationSubtypes`. Now, in `evaluateEncounterSeeds`, right after the templateId resolve and before the busy check:

- `templateAcceptsWhereTheyStand(graph, agentId, template)` (exported from `encounterSeeding.ts`, free of seed/query types for THR-2039) passes when the template is ungated, or its gate holds the Location-tier subtype or the mortal's Place `sublocationTypeId`. So a tavern rematch plays in a tavern Place, a town scene anywhere in town. A missing or dangling location is false.
- On a fail inside `SEQUEL_PLACE_WAIT_TICKS` (48, `movement-content.ts`) the seed stays queued and traces `encounter_seed_waiting` once (`placeWaitTraced`).
- After the window it withers with the existing withered line and `discardReason: 'place_never_reached'`. It never falls through to query or family.
- Exempt: a kept appointment (its arm judged the place), and — a choice made in the build — a meeting whose place the world lost, which the appointment arm deliberately fires placeless (THR-1479). That exemption rides the seed (`firesPlaceless`), so a busy mortal's requeued seed keeps it; review round 1 caught the per-tick local that lost it. `encounterSeeding-appointment.test.ts` pins both. The apotheosis capstone is planted `firesPlaceless` too (`aspects.ts`): its template promises its envelope binds prose, never whether it fires; review round 2 caught that it would have withered at a fort.

**Evidence.** `npm run gate`: PASS (test, typecheck, build, encounter, cli-smoke). New `encounterSeeding-placeWait.test.ts` (8 tests). Two fixtures (`tableThatHolds-seedChain`, `encounterAftermath-actor-sentinel`) had the hero standing nowhere; they now place the hero in an accepted town. Re-census (medium, 300 ticks, seeds 42/99/7): gated templateId sequels fired 55 / 90 / 64, against the baseline 187 ok + 77 misplaced; waits 6 / 46 / 9, withered 2 / 33 / 6. One per seed reads "misplaced" in the census only because it reads location at end of tick: each passed the gate at spawn (no waiting trace) and moved in the same tick. `mct.quest.negotiate_contract` still fires on all three seeds (8 / 10 / 9), so no chain went dark.

**Engine-planted seeds that now wait or wither** (re-census, three seeds): `encounter.town.granary_riot` ×3, `encounter_route_embargo` ×5, `encounter_toll_dispute` ×4, `encounter_route_ambush` ×1 withered with `place_never_reached`, all on seed 99. They fall under the decision as written (every templateId seed).
