# THR-2081 — Only the god's own hand fills the Mandate

**Shipped 2026-10-28.** Thematic Pressure & Living World. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-28-thr-2081-god-caused-mandate.md`.

**What changed.** The Mandate no longer compares the world's whole total of the god's two spheres against a baseline. Every sphere-pressure push now names who caused it (`causedBy`). `phaseSpherePressure` credits the god's magnitude share of each level-up in the Mandate's two spheres, weighted as the world total weighs the entity (place 1.0, mortal 0.2). A push counts as the god's when its source is god-only (claim, seat, carry, attunement, prayer, unbidden aid), when the god caused it, or when it is the scene of a mortal the god threads. Rival and doom erosion takes back at most what the god holds on that entity (`src/engine/mandateLedger.ts`). `phaseMandate` folds the credits into `mandateState.godLedger`. The targets are points of the god's own making, **87 / 23**, written at the settle, with the relative target and checkpoint deltas rescaled to them. Kill switch: `MANDATE_GOD_LEDGER_ENABLED`. The generated Mandate words drop "global strength" and every percentage. The Mandate detail's track line reads "Raised by your own hand" (or, for a sphere no god card carries, "… rises only slowly, through your bond with the land; none of your cards carries it yet.").

**Refit (Done-when 2).** At the plan's 80 / 20 the all-in god finished at doom 0.42 (t378), earlier than the 0.5–0.75 window: magnitude-weighted shares credit a little more than the proto's count-based ones. 87 / 23 puts completion near doom 0.6.

**Hunger sweep (report-only).** Only Witness completes. Every other Hunger's all-in god reaches under half its first sphere by doom 0.75 (life 0.2, time 7.0, matter 15.3, entropy 24.2, force 32–36, spirit 36.2; the energy Hungers 1–2 until THR-2086). Mind has a 1-essence sublocation card; every other sphere's cheapest place card costs 4. Per the kill criterion this is a card gap, not a constant, so it is filed as [THR-2089](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2089). A real player also earns from threads, sources and claims, so the sweep is a lower bound.

**Review gate.** Round 1 found two defects, both fixed. (1) A live scene of a threaded mortal resolves as `divine_action`, not `encounter`, so it never counted. Now both count. The unattended seeded world still holds 0.2 / 2.0. (2) The counter-omen margin stayed a fixed 0.02 while the targets were rescaled, so it scaled with world size. It now keeps its share of the target. Round 2 raised one more finding, refuted as plan-specified. The god's share is read from the pushes on the tick a level completes, so progress built on earlier ticks goes to whoever tips the level. That is a known limit of the per-tick rule. The 87 / 23 fit ran on this exact rule.
