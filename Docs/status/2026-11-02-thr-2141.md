# THR-2141 — the live map holds still under the player's hand

**Shipped 2026-11-02.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience. Found by cold playtest round 6. Two of three testers saw a target slide away while they aimed. One missed three tab clicks on a running clock.

1. **An armed card holds the clock.** Testers blamed the camera, but map follow mode never switches on (`setFollowAgent` has no caller). What moved was time. `clockHoldOpen` (`useInterruptAutoPause.ts`) now counts an armed card as a hold, alongside a registry interrupt and the Inspector's Full view. Cast, Esc or putting the card down releases it, and the clock returns to its prior state (Stellaris rule). The TIME readout names the holder: "held by your armed card" (`INSPECTOR_WORDS.armedHolder`, Law 52). `__DEBUG.getInterruptState().cardArmed` reports it.
2. **The right rail's Chronicle tab row stays still.** Threads now takes a fixed share of the rail (`LEDGER_THREADS_GROW`, zero basis), so its per-tick rows scroll inside it instead of pushing the Chronicle down. The World-Soul line always takes its three-line clamp height (`WORLD_SOUL_LINES`). Years holds its place before it opens: a `reserved` tab in `Tabs` is unseen, unannounced and skipped by the arrow keys. Tab counts sit in a fixed slot (`TAB_COUNT_SLOT_REM`), so the first unread does not widen Ledger.
3. **Dead follow mode is marked DORMANT** where it is defined (`HexMapV2.setFollowAgent`) and where it would run (`useAgentAnimations`), so the next reader does not chase it. It was not deleted: its camera-gate prop chain runs back to `GameView`.

**Tests.** `armedCardHoldsClock.test.ts` covers arm → held → disarm → runs; a paused world stays paused; a cast that opens a scene keeps the hold. `Tabs.test.tsx` covers the reserved tab and the count slot. `ledgerSectionStyle.test.ts` pins Threads' fixed share.

**Browser evidence.** `?view=game&seeded&size=medium` at 1920×1080 (`innerWidth`/`innerHeight` read back as 1920×1080). Over 173 ticks stepped with `__DEBUG.tick(1)`, the Chronicle tab row's `getBoundingClientRect().top` was 770.6 on every one of the 32 ticks the rail was on screen; on the rest, encounters held the Stage and the rail hides. Ledger's left edge stayed at 1737. Live arming: with the clock running, `armCard('whisper_insight')` gave `running:false, cardArmed:true` and `getClockHold()` gave holder "your armed card", runs on after. It stayed held 1.2 s later; `disarmCard()` gave `running:true`. Screenshot: `Docs/evidence/thr-2141/thr-2141-armed-hold-1920.jpg`. Console: only the unrelated `localhost:3001/api/tts/health` refusal. UI Laws 1/17 (holder words in `view-content.ts`), 13/14 (no numerals added), 21 (no new surface), 33 (`scrollHeight` 1080, nothing below the fold), 37 (rail sections scroll inside their share), 52 (the hold names its holder).
