# 2026-10-11 — THR-1810: encounter prose no longer leaks a raw `{are}`

**Ticket:** [THR-1810](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1810) · Bug, no plan doc (fix option (b) from the description).

- **Cause.** `{is}` / `{are}` were resolved only by `ambitionProse` (`{is}`) and the rite prose's own filler (`{are}`). The encounter enricher `enrichProse` never replaced them, so `borderland.feral_dogs`' failure line and `encounter.anomaly.dreaming_light`'s spore line would reach the Chapter Ledger as "{they} {are} bitten and walking".
- **Change.** `enrichProse` (`src/engine/proseEnrichment.ts`) resolves both spellings as one copula, derived from the pronoun set's `s` suffix: "is" for he/she, "are" for they. No caller's pronoun shape changes. Content is untouched.
- **Lock.** `thr1810-encounter-copula-token.test.ts` renders both lines in each pronoun arm, asserts no `{` remains, and sweeps every encounter, borderland and anomaly template: no actor pronoun or verb-agreement token (`{is}`, `{are}`, `{does}`, `{es}`, `{has}`, `{were}` and similar) may survive `enrichProse`.
