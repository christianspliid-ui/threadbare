# THR-2096 — "Faction Bounty" now names its faction

**Shipped 2026-10-31.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience. Christian asked of the Faction Bounty aftermath: "What faction are we talking about here?" The eight generic `fa.*` set-pieces bound no faction, titled themselves with the system word, and said "the faction" about 38 times.

**Fix.** Engine: a template opts in with `bindsFaction`. `'posting'` binds the actor's own faction when it keeps a hall or holding at the location, else the faction present there. `'rival'` binds the actor's faction and a declared rival (`relates_to` `isRival`). `factionEncounterBinding.ts` resolves it as a pure function of the graph. Both draw paths call the same resolver as a gate, so with no faction to bind the template is not offered. The prose context reads the same resolver, so the veil names the faction that let the encounter be offered. `{faction}` now resolves for any membership, not only senior ones (new `factionName` field; `factionRank` and `{?has_faction}` unchanged). `{rival_faction}` is new. Content: each template has a token-free `name` ("A Posted Bounty") and a `nameTemplate` ("The {faction}'s Bounty"). Every "the faction" in step prose, afterimages and openings is now a token. Stakes and outcome lines stay token-free, because two ledger rows read them unenriched. UI: no component changes. The veil header, the clock's "held by", the aftermath overview ("Vara won The Builders Fellowship's Bounty.") and the chapter record read the enriched title. A title whose token did not bind (a `?spawn=` or seed staging past the gate) falls back to the plain name.

Found on the way: five conditionals authored in these templates (`has_faction_membership`, `loyalty_high/low`, `iron/shadow/other_dominant`) were never resolved, so their raw markers reached the veil. They now resolve. Also, `fa.*` actions are not archived as chapters (`isEncounterAction` excludes them), so they have no chronicle row to rename. Unchanged here.

**Evidence.** `src/engine/__tests__/factionEncounterBinding.test.ts`, 9 tests. The resolver covers own-faction-present, present-only, the rival pair and no faction. A location with no faction offers no `fa.*` template. `{faction}` resolves for a rank-0 member. Titles fall back when nothing binds. A seeded-world sweep builds every `fa.*` stage model, live and resolved, and finds no `{` and no bare "faction" in the title or prose. Browser, bash-vite on :5317 + Playwright at 1920×1080: the `?spawn=fa.bounty_hunt&outcome=success` veil, after moving Vara into The Merchant Consortium Hall (Frost Clearing), reads "THE BUILDERS FELLOWSHIP'S BOUNTY" in the header and the aftermath. `__DEBUG.getClockHold().holder` is "The Builders Fellowship's Bounty". The overview opens "Vara won The Builders Fellowship's Bounty." The only console errors are the local TTS health probe.
