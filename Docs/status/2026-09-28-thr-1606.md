# 2026-09-28 — THR-1606: after a cast the player sees who it touched and what changed

**Ticket:** [THR-1606](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1606) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1606-what-your-hand-did.md` § B1 · **Pillars:** Engine (target snapshot and diff, digest on the target, gift placement events, traces), Content (chip nouns, hover sentence, change lines, story lines, gift lines), UI (the chip block mounted on the thread detail and the profile sheet, the toast link).

## What was wrong

All three round-1 cold testers: *"I couldn't name one consequence of anything I'd done."* B2 (THR-1651) gave the two casts a real effect. What the player could see of it was still broken:

- The resolver's aftermath diff only read the actor, meaning the god. A cast's writes on its target were never recorded, so the receipt was actor-centric.
- Player casts were never digested at all, so the mortal's Story So Far never heard of them.
- The one renderer of a mortal's active effects lived in `AgentInfoCard`, which is mounted nowhere live.
- Take the Seat and Leave Your Mark changed the graph with no event.

## What shipped

- **Target capture** (`src/engine/castTargetChanges.ts`). `executeStepResult` snapshots a player cast's target before and after its ops. `snapshotTargetChanges` records each real write as a change carrying the new optional `EncounterAftermathChange.subjectId`: a new influence (with its chip noun) and a trait placed or lifted. A cast that wrote nothing produces nothing (Law 56).
- **The receipt names the target.** It leads with the first target change when the overview never names them, stays on the toast tier (a target-side change no longer promotes the receipt to its modal), and declares the target in `refs`, so the toast opens the mortal (Law 1). Trace `receipt.target_changes`.
- **The target's story remembers.** A cast with a target change files a notable digest entry under the target carrying `castLine`. `composeThreadStory` tells it verbatim, and one cast beat is enough to tell a story.
- **Chips on the mortal.** `ActiveEffectChips` is the one renderer, shared with `AgentInfoCard`. It is mounted under *Under your hand* on the thread detail and the profile sheet. Chips read *Dreaming* / *Compelled*, and the hover reads *"Your hand is on them: revelation weighs more in their choices, fading in two days."*
- **Spine gifts announce themselves.** `seedBeatGraph` returns a `placement`, and `resolvePendingBeat` emits a chronicle-tier toast linked to the place or bearer (*"A seat is raised for you in Sacred Grove."*). It also touches the world version and traces `beat.gift_placed`.
- `__DEBUG.getLastCastConsequence()`. Interface rows: `cast-target-changes-reach-receipt`, `player-cast-lands-in-target-story`, `active-influences-render-on-sheet`. Wiring-guide Capability 31. Four wiki pages.

## Notes for the next slice

- `AgentInfoCard` is still imported but unmounted in `GameView.tsx`. Its chip block now delegates to `ActiveEffectChips`, and the rest of the component is dead as far as live surfaces go. It was not deleted here, per the plan.
- `__DEBUG.tick(n)` routes only the last tick's events to the toast router, so a receipt that lands mid-batch never toasts. That is a property of the lever; step with `tick(1)` to see a toast.
