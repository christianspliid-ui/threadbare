# THR-1912 — Inspector S4: the retired detail surfaces are gone

**Shipped 2026-10-18.** Slice S4 of the Inspector plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1876-the-inspector.md` § D13, § D14). Since S3 every link, card, map click and notification opens the Inspector; this slice deletes what that made unreachable — the character sheet and its tabs, the place, faction, army, artifact, god and attachment sheets, the detail column (`ThreadDetailView`, `HexDetailView`), the hex-zoom and location views (`HexBreadcrumb`, `HexSidebar`, `HexChronicle`, `LocationView`, `useHexZoomData`), the `DetailModal` stack (`DetailModal`, `DetailModalStackContext`, `DetailBreadcrumb`, `useDetailModal`, `DETAIL_*_W/_H`), the `ActionDrawer`, `LiveLocationBar`, the unmounted `HexZoomView` / `HexFlavorPanel` / `HexPoiPanel` and both `AgentInfoCard`s, plus four sub-components only they used. 88 files with their tests, about 30,000 lines.

`useRefRouter` now requires `openInspector` and has no stack; `viewLevel` is only `'world'`; the gift-hold surface list drops the seven retired ids; `ROUTED_SHEETS` names `InspectorHost` / `InspectorPage`; `ia-manifest.ts` has one `game.inspector` family; `layout-zones.md`, `component-selection.md`, `primitives.md`, `ui-patterns.md` and `public/interface-reference.html` describe the Inspector. Copy: "sheet" → "page" in the moment, fight and item lines. UI-debt baseline lowered (uppercase −48, display-face-small −50, raw-button −43, radius −91, …).

Follow-ups: [THR-1990](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1990) (the avatar's "— your mortal shape" header, missing from the parity inventory) [THR-1991](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1991) (24 modules the deletion left with no importer), and [THR-1992](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1992) (four displays the sheets drew that no Inspector row reads — the Blood line, the pilgrim way, the lair monster card, a cast card's fate word; found by the review gate).
