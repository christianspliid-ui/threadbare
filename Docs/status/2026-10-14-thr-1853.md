# 2026-10-14 — THR-1853: popups wait their turn, so the player's click lands where they aimed

**Ticket:** [THR-1853](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1853). Warm playtest round 2, 3/3 testers: clicks on Rivals, the Chapter Ledger and Doom opened a different queued popup instead, a premonition and a journey moment drew on top of each other, and a gift entered from the pill opened over a chapter step. The veteran never managed to open the Chapter Ledger (a soft would-quit). This recurs THR-1805 for gifts; premonitions and journey moments were never in that fix's scope.

- **One rule for every world-pushed interrupt (`interruptRegistry.ts`).** Registry entries now carry `arrival: true` when the world opens them on its own: the premonition, the journey moment, the story beat, the moment card and the popup channel. An arrival mounts only when (a) nothing else is on screen, (b) the player has none of their own surfaces open, and (c) the last click or key press is over `INTERRUPT_QUIET_AFTER_INPUT_MS` (2500 ms) old. Only one arrival mounts per pass, in priority order, so two never co-render. Once on screen, an arrival keeps its slot through clicks and holds. It yields only to a blocking surface that opened since (an encounter, the meeting, a gift), and it returns once that surface closes. A held arrival still holds the clock, and the TIME readout names it. It is already due, and a premonition expires 3 ticks after it becomes showable, so a running clock could drop it unseen (review-gate finding). `__DEBUG.getInterruptState().heldArrivals` names each waiting arrival and what holds it.
- **Rivals and Notables report their open state.** `RivalsButton` / `NotablesButton` take `onOpenChange`, threaded through `GameViewTopBar`. `openPlayerSurfaces` lists `RivalsPanel` / `NotablesPanel`, and both are in `GIFT_HOLD_SURFACES`. Both dropdowns now hold arrivals and a waiting gift, as Doom and the sheets already did.
- **A held spine gift waits for its pill or a resume boundary (`giftDelivery.ts`).** The 2 s quiet window was not enough: testers click every 7–10 s, so the gift entered between clicks and took the next one. A spine gift (beats 1–4) now opens on its own only at the moment the interrupt in front of it closes (`GIFT_RESUME_BOUNDARY_WINDOW_MS`). The click that closed that interrupt is spent, so it does not hold the gift. Off a boundary the gift is held with the new reason `awaiting_boundary` and waits behind the "A gift waits" pill. Beat 0 is unchanged.
- **The pill can no longer enter a gift beside another interrupt**, and it sits below the top bar: `PILL_TOP` reads the bar's rendered height (`--topbar-live-height`, 97px at 1920×1080). It used to sit at `var(--space-4)`, on the DOOM control, so a click on Doom entered the gift.
- **Tests.** `interruptRegistry.test.ts` gains 9 cases (THR-1853 describe). A premonition, a journey moment and a gift pending while the Ledger, Rivals, Notables or Doom is open do not mount until it closes, and then only one arrival mounts. Other cases cover the quiet window, the keep-slot latch, an encounter taking the screen from a showing journey moment, and the moment card and popup holding. `giftDelivery.test.ts` covers the boundary rule.
- **Browser evidence** (Vite on :5287, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, 1920×1080, clock running): Rivals → `getOpenModals()` = `RivalsPanel`, running true; Doom → `DoomClockDetail`; Chapter Ledger → `ChapterLedger`, clock held by "the Chapter Ledger". All three opened on the first click, with 0 console errors. A journey moment arrived only after 6.7 s without input. Screenshots are in `Docs/audits/2026-10-14-thr-1853/`.
- **Seen, not changed.** A world-opened encounter can still start behind an open Doom panel. It renders under the modal (veil z 50, modal z 60), so the player keeps the panel and finds the encounter when it closes.
