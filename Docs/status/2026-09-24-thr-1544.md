# 2026-09-24 — THR-1544: every lair's beast gets a fighting card (Monsters M1)

**Ticket:** [THR-1544](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1544) · **Project:** Physical Conflict · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-23-monsters-as-opponents.md` (slice M1) · **Pillars:** Engine (mint, hardening, `isMonster` exclusions) · Content (eight families, four temper traits, `#temper` seated) · UI N/A (plan doc 4 owns the monster's surface; `Browser-verify exempt: engine + content only`).

## What was wrong

Every major lair already minted a named elite, but nothing read it. It had no card for the fight block to price against, so `readOpponentCard` fell back to deriving it like a mortal. It also leaked into systems meant for mortals: it could be the target of a plot and was eligible for NPC graduation.

## What shipped

- **The card at mint.** `createNamedElite` now calls `mintMonsterCard` (`src/engine/monsters/monsterCard.ts`), which writes `monsterState`: the family picked by the lair's sphere from `MONSTER_FAMILIES` (`src/data/monster-families.ts`, one per creation sphere; a foundation sphere falls back to `beast`), Dread, Might, the nerve and clash reaches, clock 4 and `temperShown: false`. It adds a `has_trait` edge to the family's `trait.temper.*`. Traced `monster.minted`.
- **Legendary hardening.** The major → legendary tier calls `hardenMonsterCard`: clock 5 and Dread one word up, capped at severe. The wounds (`clockFilled`) are kept. Traced `monster.hardened`.
- **Temper is a Trait.** Four definitions (`src/data/temper-trait-content.ts`, class `temper`, tag `#temper`, no capability): stubborn, berserk, skittish, bargains. They are seeded at world init and registered under the Trait content kind, the hover index and `TRAIT_SUBCATEGORIES`. `#temper` is seated on `family`.
- **`isMonster`** (`src/engine/monsters/isMonster.ts`) is the one definition. The plot (`isPlottableMortal`), NPC graduation, lair clearing's challenger scan and social visibility all exclude through it.
- **Debug:** `window.__DEBUG.listMonsters()` (pinned `ListedMonster` shape, plus `cardMissing`) and CLI `monsters`.
- **Docs:** UL *Temper* seated by delegation 2026-09-24, and UL *Trait Category* corrected to twelve classes. Rulebook §7 has a new "Monsters" paragraph, with a matching line on the quick reference. The `#temper` seat is recorded in the content-objects canon. The interface contract `lair-escalation-mints-monster-card` is LIVE. Six wiki pages and the wiring checklist are updated.
