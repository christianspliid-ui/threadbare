### 2026-10-04 — THR-1658: the old blood can want the old land

**What shipped.** *Raise the Old Banner* (`ambition_raise_the_old_banner`), the descendant's drive, sibling to the exile's *Reclaim the Homeland* (unchanged). A deciding mortal descended from a dead empire may take it up when a slot frees at re-evaluation. They finish it by standing on an elder ruin of that empire (`agent_at_ancestral_ruin`) and by taking ground on its old land since the drive began (`agent_took_ancestral_ground`, window-bound). There is no culprit and no heat, so the rulebook gains § 10.7 *An old fall is not a wrong*. The drive's edge carries `mintedByLabel`, so the sheet's intent line reads *"Because of the old blood of …"*.

**How it is gated.** A new template field `requiresDescent` is checked by `passesEligibility` against the snapshot's `descentCultureIds` (filled in `buildAmbitionAgentSnapshot`). It fails closed, and worldgen, birth and binder snapshots carry no descent, so nobody starts with the drive. The template sits at the tail of the pool, so no other template's draw moves. The refill offers it only to existing deciders (plan D4), so it can never spotlight-pull anyone. "The old land" is one predicate, `historicalCultureOfRegion` in the new `src/engine/descent.ts`, and the worldgen writer (`worldPast.ts` S1e) now imports it. The lift is behaviour-neutral: the t0 digests are identical.
