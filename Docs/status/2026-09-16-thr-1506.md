# THR-1506 — ruin quest hooks fire in a normal run

*Deferral from [THR-1026](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1026), whose Done-when
sweep found zero stamped ruins anywhere and so could not test the faction predicate it
was there to test. Product defect: 26 settlements rendered an empty Guild Postings board
for an entire run.*

## The diagnosis

The ticket asked which of three constants was wrong — spawn rate, decay TTL, or the
quest threshold. Measured on seed 42 / medium to the twilight stop at tick 175, the
answer was "the feed does not exist", with the other two compounding it:

- **No organic producer.** The ruins design reserved four clue-bearing encounters
  (`ruins.glossed_tome`, `ruins.drunk_cartographer`, …) and the `CLUE_SPAWN_*_BASE`
  constants to drive them. The encounters were never authored and the constants had
  **no reader anywhere in `src/`**. The only live producers were seven reputation/delve
  encounters' `spawn_clue` effects: one edge in 175 ticks.
- **The one clue landed at random.** `$nearest_ruin` resolved through `findAnyRuinId`
  to a uniformly random ruin anywhere on the map — which is how the run's single clue
  fell outside every posting hall's radius.
- **The unit could never post a minor ruin.** A clue's `magnitude` was the *ruin's*
  magnitude, summed against `CLUE_QUEST_THRESHOLD` (0.5): a minor ruin's single lead
  (≤ 0.33) could never clear it, so `ag.quest.ruin_delve` — the entry-tier contract —
  was unreachable by construction. Meanwhile the strategic `spawnClue` op already wrote
  a *lead strength* into the same field: two writers, one unit, two meanings.
- **TTL 20 against a 30-tick sweep.** `clue_decay` runs before `ruin_quest_hooks` in
  the same tick, so a third of vague clues expired unseen.

## What shipped

Engine, with content-facing constants; no UI (the panel already renders a stamped ruin,
proven under THR-1026). Everything tunable lives in `src/engine/ruins/constants.ts`.

- **`phaseClueRumors`** (`src/engine/ruins/clueRumors.ts`, phase `clue_rumors`, after
  `secrets_favors`, before `clue_decay`). Every `CLUE_RUMOR_INTERVAL_TICKS` (10), each
  settlement owning a tavern/inn, library/archive, or spy net rolls its kind's
  `CLUE_SPAWN_*_BASE` × `CLUE_RUMOR_SWEEP_SCALE` (0.1) for a `vague` clue about its
  nearest ruin within `CLUE_RUMOR_RUIN_RADIUS` (6) hexes, handed to one of its own
  people by Narrative Gravity. Capped at `CLUE_RUMOR_MAX_PER_SWEEP` (3). Keyed on the
  settlement, not presence at the place: at ticks 40 and 100 nobody stood at a tavern,
  inn, or library. One aggregate `ruins.clue_rumor_sweep` trace per sweep.
- **`$nearest_ruin` means nearest.** `findNearestRuinId` resolves the actor's hex; any
  ruin only when the actor is unplaced.
- **Lead strength by precision.** `CLUE_LEAD_STRENGTH_{VAGUE,NARROWED,LOCATED}` =
  0.5 / 0.75 / 1.0, written by `produceClueConsequence`. One rumour posts a contract;
  the delve itself still needs a `located` clue, so observe → clue → delve is a climb.
- **`CLUE_MAX_AGE_TICKS_VAGUE`** 20 → 30, one posting interval.

## Measured after (medium map, tick ~175, no hand-seeded clues)

| seed | clues over run | stamped ruins | templates posted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 42 | 17 (13 tavern, 3 spy, 1 library) | 2 | `ruin_delve`, `deep_expedition` |
| 7 | 30 | 4 | `deep_expedition` ×3, `ruin_delve` |
| 99 | 34 | 5 | `ruin_delve` ×3, `deep_expedition` ×2 |

Before: 1 clue, 0 stamps, on seed 42. The membership predicate — at least one ruin
stamped on a seeded medium world by tick ~200 without hand-seeding — holds on all three.

## What is deliberately not here

The four designed clue-bearing encounters are still unauthored. The sweep is the
systemic floor those encounters would sit on top of, not a substitute for them — an
authored `spawn_clue` now adds to a live economy instead of being its only input.
