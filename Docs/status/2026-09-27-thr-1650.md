# 2026-09-27 — THR-1650: "A Vision — Witness" plays its scene

**Ticket:** [THR-1650](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1650) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1606-what-your-hand-did.md` § B3 · **Pillars:** Engine (playback, offer filter, aftermath retirement), UI (Witness handler, modal copy); Content: N/A, the branching templates play unchanged.

## What was wrong

A delivery beat ("A Vision — …") wraps a hand-authored branching encounter. Pressing Witness opened nothing. Worse, resolving the beat ran every fallback reaction of that mortal encounter **against the god**, which is the THR-1526 untrue-scene class. The modal showed placeholder copy because `beatProseOverride` looked up the prefixed beat id.

## What shipped

- **`prepareDeliveryEncounter`** (`src/engine/deliveryBeatAdapter.ts`) is the non-debug sibling of `prepareDebugEncounterSpawn`. It mints the `UnifiedAction`, the clearance gates and the notification for The First, anchored where The First stands. It writes no thread edge and stamps no test avatar.
- **`bindDeliverySubject`** decides whether a vision can fall on The First. The First must be bonded, must stand somewhere, and must stand at a kind of place the encounter is set in (`locationSubtypes`; a template with none is place-agnostic). *Executor decision:* binding is structural only. It does not re-apply the mortal-pathing prerequisites (reputation, marks, court), because THR-506 built visions to sidestep exactly those.
- **Director offer filter** (`ascendantBeat.ts`): a vision that cannot bind is withheld and never shown. Each draw that withholds any emits one aggregate `beat.delivery_skipped` trace (`filteredCount`, `reason`).
- **Retired:** `resolvePendingBeat` skips `runBeatTemplateAftermath` for `kind === 'delivery'`. No existing test asserted that path.
- **UI** (`GameView.tsx`): the modal's resolve goes through `handleWitnessBeat`, which plays the vision (`beat.delivery_played` / `beat.delivery_skipped`), opens the veil and then records the beat. `dismissBeats` / `suppressBeats` still call `handleResolveBeat`, so they never open a veil. `beatProseOverride` resolves through `sourceTemplateIdOf`.
- Orphaned `TODO(THR-514)` removed. Interface row `delivery-beat-plays-its-encounter` added as 🟢 LIVE.

## Evidence

- `deliveryBeatPlayback.test.ts` has 12 tests. A spy on `applyEncounterAftermathReaction` is never called for a delivery beat, while the pool-beat control still runs. The suite also pins the four bind refusals and the minted action's actor and target, and checks that with no First the Director never draws a delivery beat and traces the withholding.
- Browser (Playwright, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, 1920×1080): Kael Thornweaver was moved to a spawned hamlet and `fireBeat('beat.delivery.encounter.slice.the_table_that_holds')` was called. The modal showed "The Table That Holds" and its real teaser. Witness then opened `EncounterVeil` on Kael Thornweaver, with the trace `beat.delivery_played … opened on ind_dev_the_first`.
