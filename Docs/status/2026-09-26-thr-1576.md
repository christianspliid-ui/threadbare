# 2026-09-26 — THR-1576: Who notices whom now depends on how sharp-eyed they are

**Ticket:** [THR-1576](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1576) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** the ticket body (deferral of THR-1562, reach on one scale) · **Pillars:** Engine only. Content and UI are N/A: no authored content reads these two formulas, and nothing on screen shows the chance.

## What shipped

1. **Colocation detection reads the reach share.** `detectionChance` (`src/engine/phaseColocationDetection.ts`) reads the observer's Eye and the target's Shadow through `computeReachShare` (0–1). Before, it multiplied the raw stored capability (≈ 10–40) by weights written for 0–1, so about half of all capable pairs sat at the floor (0.01) or the ceiling (0.95).
2. **The weights are re-tuned:** `EYE_PERCEPTION_WEIGHT` 0.15 → 0.8, `SHADOW_STEALTH_WEIGHT` 0.15 → 0.4 (`src/data/colocation-content.ts`). Eye outweighs Shadow on purpose. With equal weights the two cancel to the base chance and the world's detection rate halves.
3. **Strategic role fit reads the reach share.** `computeRoleFit` (`src/engine/strategicActionCandidates.ts`) is now the weighted mean of the actor's shares over the template's `reachProfile`. It used to saturate at 1 for every capable mortal. It stays dormant while `UNDERTAKING_MODEL = 'cells'`.
4. **New census:** `npm run census:colocation` computes both formulas on the same live pairs, tick by tick.
