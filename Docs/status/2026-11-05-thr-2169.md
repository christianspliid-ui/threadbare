**Needs you counts only what a press can open, and action toasts expire on their own.** Warm playtest round 3 saw "Needs you 1" all session with nothing to open, and "compelled toward The Gallows Pardon" left on screen long after its chapter ended. (1) The pill's number now counts only rows that open something (`needsYouRowCounts`: `opens !== 'none'`). A moment held behind the one on screen, a pause with no place and a gift blocked by another moment (new `NeedsYouBeatOffer.blocked`, set from `giftOtherInterrupts`) stay in the list with "after this" but add nothing to the number. When only such rows are listed, the pill reads "Waiting" with no number. (2) Action toasts (steer results, nudge rejections, chapter answers) moved into `useActionToasts`. It prunes expired toasts every `ACTION_TOAST_PRUNE_MS` (500 ms) while the clock runs, the same rule notification toasts follow. Before, they were pruned only when the next one was pushed. Dismissing a toast now clears it from both channels.
