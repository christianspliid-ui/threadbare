### 2026-10-05 — THR-1738: the encounter test panel no longer shows raw `{cast:…}` tokens

**What was wrong.** A step can carry a line forward from the step before it (`carryoverFactorLines`), such as *"{cast:drover} knows they found the carrion."* in The Wolf-Winter Watch. The test panel showed that line with the token still in it. The engine passes carryover text through unchanged, by design. The stage adapter then enriched every authored, trait and fight line, but not this one.

**UI.** `buildNudgePhaseModel.ts` runs the carryover line through the same `enrich()` as the step's authored factor lines. The other derived lines (skill, standing, terrain, spell) are built from resolved names and stay untouched. A new test in `nudgeStage.test.ts` pins a carryover line carrying `{actor}` and `{cast:drover}` resolving to *"Old Brannoc knows Sera Vance found the carrion."*, and it fails on the old adapter. **Engine / Content:** N/A. The four shipped lines (`flood-dyke-mending.ts` ×2, `wolf-winter-watch.ts` ×2) now render as written.

**Seen in the browser** (worktree Vite, 1920×1080, `?spawn=encounter.town.wolf_winter_watch&outcome=success_at_cost`). Step 2 of 3 shows *"Col Brannock knows they found the carrion."* and the page holds no `{cast:` or `{actor}` token.
