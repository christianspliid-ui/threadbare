# THR-2195 — the bond scene opens at its first line and fits one screen

**Shipped 2026-11-12.** Onboarding & First-Run Experience, cold playtest round 7 (3/3 testers). No plan doc; scope from the ticket.

1. **Cause.** `BondBeat`'s test stage centred an over-tall column with `justify-content: center` inside an `overflow-y-auto` box. Half the overflow landed above the top edge, where scrollTop was already 0, so the bond scene opened mid-paragraph and the wheel could not reach its setup.
2. **Safe centring.** All three bond stages (test, reveal, naming) now use the top-aligned `BOND_STAGE_CLASS` and centre their column with `margin-block: auto`. A short column stays centred, and a tall one starts at the top.
3. **Fit.** The test column now uses `useFitToParentHeight`, the same fit `FormativeTestBeat` uses (THR-1804). Fitting alone stopped at the 0.7 floor with two cards still below the fold. Two layout changes make the scene fit: the column's content box is exactly one hand row wide (`BOND_HAND_ROW_WIDTH_PX`), so 8 cards take two rows instead of three, and the portrait sits beside the god voice and setup instead of above them.

**Evidence:** `npm run gate` PASS (test, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt). New test `bondStageSafeCenterThr2195.test.tsx` (4 cases). Browser, headless Chrome reaching the bond step through `?view=game&firstunmet&size=medium`: at 1920×1080 the fit scale is 0.834, scrollHeight = clientHeight = 1080, the setup's first line is at y=112, and "Stay silent, let fate answer" is at y=991–1034, with no "more cards below" line. At 2560×1440 the fit scale is 1, scrollHeight = clientHeight = 1440, and the commit is at y=1298. Console errors: 0. Screenshots: `Docs/evidence/thr-2195/bond-test-1920.png`, `bond-test-2560.png`.
