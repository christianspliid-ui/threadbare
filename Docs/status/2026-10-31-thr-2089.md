# THR-2089 — Force, matter and time get a cheap place card (slice 1)

**Shipped 2026-10-31.** Thematic Pressure & Living World. Under the god-caused Mandate only the god's own cards raise a sphere, and force, matter and time had no card a god could cast on a town (Fortify had no legal target on the seeded world; matter and time carried no tier-1 card at all).

**Three tier-1 market cards on existing ops, 2 essence each, targeting settlements** (plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-30-thr-2089-cheap-place-cards.md`):

- **Steel the Walls** (`loc.steel_walls`, *Iron in the Mortar*, force): `fortify_location`, the same op as Fortify. Fortify is the strongholds card at 4; this is the towns card at 2.
- **Lay the Footings** (`loc.lay_footings`, *The Cornerstone Set*, matter): `buildProgress +LOC_LAY_FOOTINGS_BUILD_DELTA` (5).
- **Keep the Stores** (`loc.keep_stores`, *Grain That Never Spoils*, time): `foodStore +LOC_KEEP_STORES_FOOD_DELTA` (12), clamped by the economy's store cap.

`loc.fortify.targetSubtypes` gains `fort` and `tower`. `godCardProgressionCheck` warns `hole: no cheap <sphere> place card` for each Hunger sphere with no tier-1 market place card at ≤ `CHEAP_PLACE_CARD_MAX_COST` (2). `scripts/mandate-census.ts` now casts only on legal targets (`targetSubtypes`) and stops casting on a met half.

**Executor decisions.** The plan's spell name *Grain That Does Not Spoil* broke the four-word spell-name rule (`unified-action-templates.test.ts`); shipped as *Grain That Never Spoils*. The review gate found that Steel the Walls did nothing on a city or capital (both start at `FORTIFICATION_GRAND`, the fortify cap), so it targets settlement / hamlet / town only; a test pins that every listed subtype starts below the cap. The census above was measured before that change. The new check also warns for **energy**: Stoke the Forges (THR-2086) costs 3, over the cheap line. The plan expected only spirit, life and entropy; energy is noted on THR-2090.
