# THR-1970 — War aims: a richer town can now outbid a nearer poor one

**Shipped 2026-10-24.** Deferred from war aims S1 (THR-1933). Its predicate "of contested picks, at least half choose a town other than the nearest" measured 1 of 6.

**What the sweep found.** `WAR_AIM_HALF_VALUE_HEXES` at 6, 8, 10 and 12 on seeds 42/99/7/13/2026 (600 ticks, medium, S2 monopoly bonuses live) left the raw share at 2–6 of 27. A `--verbose` candidate dump showed why: in most contested picks the nearest enemy town is also the richest (often the army stands on it, 0 hexes), and no distance discount should overturn that pick. The raw share was measuring map layout, not whether value can beat distance.

**What changed.**
- **Engine.** `war.aim.picked` carries `richerThanNearest`: some in-reach town is worth more than the nearest before the distance discount (value × (1 + bonuses), × the revenge focus where it applies — the review gate caught the focus missing). `WAR_AIM_HALF_VALUE_HEXES` 6 → 10.
- **Measure.** `measure:war-aims` prints "value wins" (of the picks with `richerThanNearest`, the share not choosing the nearest), a totals line, and `--verbose` (each contested pick's top candidates).
- **Docs.** The war-aims plan doc's predicate is recalibrated to the value-wins share (raw share still printed) and its constant row; the interface contract and the armies wiki page name the new constant.

**Evidence (value wins / picks with a richer option).**
- Half-value 6: 2/10 · 8: 3/9 · 10: **6/9** · 12: 6/9. 10 is the smallest that clears half.
- At 10: non-enemy picks 0, non-enemy sieges 0, own-hall sieges 0, silent holds 0, ambition mismatch 0 on all five seeds; defence fired on seeds 99 and 7; re-aim pass median 3–8 ms.
- `npm run gate`: test, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt and CLI smoke all PASS.

**Veto invited.** The predicate recalibration is an agent call on the measure, made because the raw share cannot reach half without making distance meaningless. Reverting is one constant and one plan-doc line.
