# THR-1897 — The Stage, slice 4: the last four moments play on the Stage

**Shipped 2026-10-20.** Slice S4, the last slice of the encounter Stage plan (`Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1877-encounter-stage.md` § D4, D5, D6, D9, D10). Journey moments, undertaking moments, premonitions and the ruin emergence dilemma no longer open as centred dialogs. Each plays on the Stage beside the god rail, over the dimmed map with its place lit in the window. Their words are unchanged. No Stage-home surface renders through `Modal` any more.

- **Journey moment** and **undertaking moment**: compact layout, lit at the mortal's place, with the Stage's context line ("Kael Thornweaver at Sacred Grove") under the title.
- **Premonition**: full layout. The mortal's portrait and name sit on the left. The header, the dream, the options and the dismiss sit on the right, on the premonition's own ground. The place tag reads "{place} · where {mortal} is". Hovering or focusing a compulsion option shows a dashed ring in the dream's tone on that option's place, lifts the dim there (an option in fog stays a ring only), and the tag reads "{place} · where the dream pulls". The camera frames the mortal's place and every option's place when they all fit at zoom 5 or closer; otherwise it frames the mortal's place alone. Escape still does not decline (THR-1711).
- **Emergence dilemma**: lit at the ruin (else its mortal), `elder` palette, compact because a ruin has no art of its own.

Engine: `stageView` takes a premonition's option Locations (`optionLocationIds`) and returns each option's hex, its place name and whether it is in fog. The emergence dilemma lights its ruin before its mortal. `momentCardModel.ts` moved into `src/engine/views/stage/`, with a re-export at its old path (THR-1822 D16 step 5). UI: `StageFocusLayer` builds the option rings and per-option plates, and exposes `setHoveredOption`. `HexMapV2` exposes `setStageOptionHover`. `GameScreenState.stage.hoveredOption` carries the hover to `useStageCamera`. `chooseStageFrame` decides what the camera frames. With no frame provider (a lab or a test), the Stage shows at once. `__DEBUG.openHomedSurface` gains the journey-moment and emergence-dilemma levers and routes the premonition (a whisper about The First) and the undertaking moment (`forceMoments`). `MeetingEncounterModal` (610 lines, never mounted) is deleted, and the UI-debt baseline is lowered with it. Content: the player manual's journey-vignette row is removed and its cross-link names journey moments on the Stage. The IA manifest's journey row mounts on `stage`.

**Measured in the browser** (bash-vite, Playwright, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`): at 1920×1080 the compact panel is 860 × 984 at (344, 72) with a 692 px window, and the full panel is 1272 × 984 with a 280 px window. At 2560×1440 the compact panel is 967.5 × 1336 with a 1144.5 px window, and the full panel is 1592 × 1336 with a 520 px window. In each, the lit hex is in the window and `scrollingBelowCap` is empty. Evidence: `Docs/evidence/thr-1897/`. The compulsion premonition's ring was not captured live, because `forcePremonition('@hero','compulsion')` did not emit within 50 ticks on this seed. The undertaking moment was not captured live either, because no checkpoint fired. Their behaviour is pinned by `StageFocusLayer.test.ts`, `stageView.test.ts`, `stageCamera.test.ts` and `stageMomentsThr1897.test.tsx`.
