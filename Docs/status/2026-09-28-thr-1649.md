# 2026-09-28 — THR-1649: the world reads bigger (avatar sight 2, camera zooms out to the whole map)

**Ticket:** [THR-1649](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1649) · **Project:** Onboarding & First-Run Experience · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1605-the-opening.md` slice S7 · **Pillars:** UI (camera), Engine (one sight constant); Content: N/A (no prose or templates).

## What was wrong

In cold-playtest round 1, testers saw about 7–10 hexes, scrolling would not zoom out, and the world read as "a tiny or broken world". There were two causes:

- `AVATAR_SIGHT_RANGE = 0`: the avatar saw only its own hex.
- `CAMERA_CONSTANTS.MIN_ZOOM: 5`, commented "capped so fog edge is never visible". It clamped the fit-to-grid opening zoom upward, and the wheel handler stopped at that clamp.

## What shipped

- **`AVATAR_SIGHT_RANGE` 0 → 2** (`src/types/visibility.ts`). The god's own shape sees further than a mortal (`AGENT_SIGHT_RANGE` stays 1). `FOG_CONSTANTS.DEFAULT_SIGHT_RANGE` now derives from it, so the two cannot drift.
- **`MIN_ZOOM` → `MIN_ZOOM_FLOOR: 2`** plus `ABSOLUTE_MIN_ZOOM: 0.5` (`D3ZoomCamera.ts`).
  - New pure helpers: `computeFitZoom` (the zoom at which the whole grid fits the canvas) and `computeMinZoom`.
  - The zoom-out limit is the floor, dropped to the fit zoom when a map is too big to show whole at 2 (large and epic maps). So every map size can scroll out to its full outline.
  - The opening fit zoom is no longer clamped up.
- **Kill criterion not triggered.** At 1920×1080 on medium, full zoom-out is k = 2 on a 1200×986 canvas (fit zoom 2.02). The whole map outline shows, unexplored ground renders as clean fog with no seams or z-fighting, and the avatar's revealed patch sits at the centre. The full change shipped, not the fallback.

## Noticed, not changed

At full zoom-out, the political border's outer segment along the map's bottom edge draws half outside the fogged hexes, so a thin red line shows over the fog there. It was already possible before this change by panning to the edge; it is just easier to see now. It is cosmetic, and I left it out of scope.
