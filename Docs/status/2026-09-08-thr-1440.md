# THR-1440 — the capability rider is paid, and it costs something to earn

**2026-09-08 · Engine · project `Thematic Pressure & Living World` · parent map [THR-1396](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1396)**

Christian's standing rider on the undertaking grid ([THR-1397](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1397), 2026-09-03) — *"a completed undertaking grows capability in the Reach it leaned on"* — was recorded on the map and paid nowhere: nothing in the undertaking lifecycle wrote `domainCapabilities`. It is paid now, by one writer, and the interesting part of the ticket is the half that is **not** paid.

## What shipped

`src/engine/undertakingCapabilityGrowth.ts` is the single writer. On a completed undertaking it grows the actor's `domainCapabilities[reach]` by `UNDERTAKING_COMPLETION_CAPABILITY_GROWTH[tier]` — **0.5 / 1 / 2 on the raw 0–100 scale**, deliberately slow because the capability sigmoid is flat above ~20 — clamped at `CAPABILITY_MAX`. The Reach is the highest-weighted entry in the template's `reachProfile` (a cell's is `CELL_REACH_BY_VERB[verb]`, a legacy template's is its own), tie-broken by `REACH_DOMAINS` order so the answer never depends on which key was written first. The growth rides the history entry (`capabilityGrowth: { reach, delta }`) and the `strategic_project_progress` completion trace that already fires — no new trace category, for the ring-buffer reason the christening note records. It is paid **before** the calling recompute at the same terminal, so a Reach that crosses a tier renames the mortal's calling on the same tick, which is the point of the rider.

`CAPABILITY_MAX = 100` is not a new number: it is the ceiling `generateDomainCapabilities` in `worldSeed.ts` has always clamped to, now named once and imported by both writers rather than living as two literals that could drift.

## The deviation: an instant cell pays nothing

The ticket binds the rider to *every* cell. It is paid at the **project** terminal only, and that narrowing was forced by measurement rather than chosen.

An instant cell — `use` and `observe`, both `UNDERTAKING_VERB_DURATION` 0 at every tier — has no checkpoints and therefore cannot fail. The rider's own price clause ("only a completed outcome grows; a failed or abandoned work grows nothing") has nothing to bite on there, and paying it makes a no-risk capability farm. That is not a theoretical objection: with the instant path paying, the existing starvation contract (`threaded-agent-balance.contract.test.ts`) went red, and the reason was not a stale budget. The contract strands a hero with all eight Reaches zeroed on a barren hex and expects it to idle into forced travel. With the rider on the instant path that hero **never idles at all, even at 60 ticks** (`idleReasons {}`, Eye 0 → 3): `observe × area` always targets the actor's own hex, so it is always available, and the first free watch lifts Eye off zero, which widens awareness, which supplies the next watch. The idle → forced-travel safety net stopped being reachable.

Extending the test's tick budget would have papered over a disabled safety net. Restricting the rider to work that was actually risked restores the contract with its original budget untouched, and states the rule the rider was already implying: growth is the reward for finishing something that could have gone wrong.

## Measured, with a control arm

Small world, 150 ticks, one seed per process (impediment #994):

| | seed 42 | seed 99 |
|---|---|---|
| completions | 66 | 61 |
| …of which checkpointed work paying the rider | **22** | **28** |
| non-completed terminals paying the rider | **0** | **0** |
| carriers whose Reach rose | 12 | 10 |
| …on their *leading* Reach | 7 | 4 |
| tier crossings (rider on) | 31 | 41 |
| tier crossings (rider disabled — control arm) | 29 | 41 |

**The rider's own contribution to tier crossings is +2 and 0.** The run's crossings are overwhelmingly other `computeRawScore` contributors — traits gained in play — and recording the gross count as the rider's work would have been a false claim. This is the conservative constants behaving as specified, not a defect; `UNDERTAKING_COMPLETION_CAPABILITY_GROWTH` is the named lever if crossings are wanted sooner.

The control arm also earns its place as a falsification rather than a decoration: with the writer disabled, **zero** carriers rose on either seed, which is the assertion that nothing else writes `domainCapabilities` during a run.
