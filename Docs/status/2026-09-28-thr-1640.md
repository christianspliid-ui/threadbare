# 2026-09-28 — THR-1640: spotlight mortals join guilds

**Ticket:** [THR-1640](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1640) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-27-thr-1633-written-encounters-land.md` § S3 · **Pillars:** Engine (candidates, scoring, the join hook, excommunication); Content: N/A (no templates or prose change); UI: N/A (read through `window.__DEBUG.getGuildJoinFunnel()` and the `guild_join_resolved` trace, no screen changes).

## What was wrong

The mortals the player watches almost never belonged to a guild. At t200 on seeds 42 and 99 they held memberships only in the Mercenary Company, and 83 · 84 guild templates had no deciding member to offer them to. Four breaks, found by tracing rather than guessed:

1. **Only the first hall counted.** `generateFactionLifecycleCandidates` read `sublocations[0]`.
2. **A join was scored as worthless** (reward 0.05), so it rarely won a board.
3. **A chosen join never became a membership.** `processFactionOutcome` was only called from the `encounterProgress` loop, which no production path fills. On `origin/main`, seed 99 resolved 7 joins by t200, 3 of them in a success band, and wrote 0 memberships.
4. **The newest member was always the next one struck off.** A guild's `excommunicate` action picks the lowest-reputation member, and a fresh join starts lowest. Once break 3 was fixed, both of seed 99's first joiners were excommunicated 20–24 ticks after joining.

## What shipped

- **Every hall offers.** One join (or promotion) per guild with a hall at the Location, each keyed to its own hall, behind `FACTION_LIFECYCLE_ALL_HALLS`.
- **Guild fit rides the join's reward.** `FACTION_JOIN_FIT_BONUS` (1.0) × the mortal's fit, which is the mean `computeReachShare` over the guild's two highest `reachWeights`. It is added to the reward rather than to the additive bonuses, because the live decision board ranks encounters by `valuePerTick × desire × forecastFit` and never reads the additive terms. The first attempt added it as a bonus: joins reached more boards but won no more of them.
- **The live join hook.** `processResolvedFactionLifecycleAction` (`factionOutcome.ts`) runs at the orchestrator's newly-resolved transition and routes a join or promotion that landed in `FACTION_LIFECYCLE_SUCCESS_BANDS` through the existing `processFactionOutcome`. Each resolved join emits one `guild_join_resolved` trace naming its band and, if no edge was written, the reason.
- **A novice grace on excommunication.** A member who joined in play (`joinedTick > 0`) cannot be excommunicated for `FACTION_EXCOMMUNICATE_NOVICE_GRACE_TICKS` (100). Worldgen members are unaffected.
- **`window.__DEBUG.getGuildJoinFunnel()`** reports reached → chosen → resolved by band → joined, plus exact spotlight guild coverage against `GUILD_SPOTLIGHT_COVERAGE_MIN` (5).
- Contract `spotlight-mortal-joins-guild` is registered as LIVE.

- **Calibration.** At bonus 0.6, seed 99 covers 4 guilds, so the gate fails. At 1.2, seed 42's joins win 41% of boards, so the ceiling fails. 1.0 is the only value tried that passes both, and seed 99 sits exactly on the coverage floor. The kill criterion did not fire.
- **Drawable firings:** 161 → 159, within noise (floor 121).
- **Reader fixes.** `guild-join.ts` keyed unified actions on `id`, which they do not have (the key is `actionId`). It therefore counted only the first action it ever saw, which is where THR-1633's "0 fired" came from. Its per-sample probe also calls `runFilterPipeline`, which writes engine state. The probed world is not the unprobed one: seed 99 had 2 joins unprobed against 10 probed. Gate numbers now come from `PROBE=0`.
