# 2026-10-05 — THR-1741: one ending per band in Tend to Wounds and Old Blood

**Ticket:** [THR-1741](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1741) · **Project:** Encounter Experience · **Pillars:** Content only (prose in two templates). Engine N/A: no logic changes. UI N/A: no surface changes, and the Done-when owes no browser evidence (THR-688 rule C).

## Why

While authoring stakes for every encounter (THR-1728), two templates turned out to tell different endings on different surfaces. A player could read one ending on screen and another in the Chapter Ledger.

## What shipped

- **Tend to Wounds** (`encounter.tend_to_wounds`, `src/data/encounter-content.ts`). The final step's `criticalFailureAfterimage` said the arm set crooked. It now tells the death: *"They set the arm while he went grey. The wound that killed him was under his belt."* That matches the step-1 critical failure, the aftermath `critical_failure` overview (*"The one who was carried in does not get carried out."*) and stakes `lostBadly` (*"lost him to the wound under his belt"*). The `dull_the_pain` nudge's `critical_failure` line told the same crooked-arm ending, so it was rewritten too: *"…and he did not come back from it."* The crooked arm stays the plain `failure` ending: step `onFailure`, aftermath `failure` and stakes `lost` (*"bound the arm as it lay, out of true for life"*).
- **Old Blood** (`fight.duel.grudge`, `src/data/encounters/fight-duel-grudge.ts`). `narrativeTemplates.success` and `failure` were swapped. They now read from the actor's side, like `RESULT_LINES` and the stakes. Success: *"{name} walked away from the fight the winner, and {target} did not."* (stakes won: *"beat an old enemy in front of the square"*, result `overcome`). Failure: *"{name} walked away from the fight beaten, and {target} saw it done."* (stakes lost: *"was beaten by an old enemy in the square"*).
