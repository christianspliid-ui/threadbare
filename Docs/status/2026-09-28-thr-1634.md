# 2026-09-28 — THR-1634: finish the encounters the player meets, slice 1

**Ticket:** [THR-1634](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1634) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-28-thr-1634-finish-the-encounters-the-player-meets.md` · **Pillars:** Content (afterimages, deals, band endings); Engine (a converter passthrough and a completion ratchet, no tick-loop change); UI: N/A (existing stage and chapter archive).

## What was wrong

Success at a cost is 21.6% of attended resolutions, but the templates that fire most had no at-cost line. `afterimageForOutcome` fell back to the plain success line, so a cost read as a clean win. Critical results read as ordinary ones for the same reason. Only 7.2% of firings landed on a template with a hand. Also, a `deal` authored on an `encounter-content.ts` entry never reached the shipped template: the converter builds steps field by field and did not list `deal` (the THR-838 trap).

## What shipped

- **E1:** `deal` passes through `toUnifiedTemplate`. Entries that declare none convert as before.
- **Content, the ten most-fired templates:** every step now has an at-cost line, a critical-success line and a critical-failure line. Each at-cost line names the cost. The nine `encounter.*` entries declare a `deal` fill on every step (`DEAL_DEFAULT_COUNT`, two context tags each).
- **`crafting.quest.flawed_steel`:** the step-0 ladder, at-cost lines on the three arms, and `failure` / `critical_failure` endings on all four aftermath paths. The endings reuse only chips each path already had, and drop the ones a loss makes untrue. Its two steps keep their authored choices instead of a hand. A declared `deal` would make the stage build the hand in place of the fork's choice screen.
- **E3:** `FIRED_TEMPLATE_COMPLETION` (`src/data/content-eval/firedTemplateCompletion.ts`) is a growing list. `firedTemplateCompletion.test.ts` asserts the ladder, a verb on every step, and the per-path losing bands on the shipped templates.
- **Contract** `raw-entry-deal-reaches-template` is registered and LIVE.

## Measured

- Coverage on the same 1,542 attended firings: at-cost prose 8.0% → 31.5%; full ladder 7.2% → 30.7%; a hand 7.2% → 28.5%.
- Dealing changes no draw. `readers/reach.ts 42,99 200` gave byte-identical output with and without the deals. `readers/attended.ts 42,99 150` gave the same 1,542 firing rows, outcomes included.
- Tick cost: attended wall time was +2.6% against the mean of two no-deal runs (budget 5%).
- Kill check: top template 3.05%, top-10 23.5%.
- Browser (1920×1080): `?spawn=encounter.barter_with_travelers` dealt four repertoire cards. With `&outcome=success_at_cost`, both new at-cost lines were in the archived chapter. The verdict read `no_aftermath_config`, which is correct: that template gets no new aftermath (plan clause 4).
- A cold critic pass on all ten templates found crit lines that claimed a lasting relationship or a future appointment with no state behind them. Those lines were rewritten before merge.
