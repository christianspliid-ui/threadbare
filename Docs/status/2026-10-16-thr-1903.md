# 2026-10-16 — THR-1903: carved places on the map

**Ticket:** [THR-1903](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1903). UI pillar (the map's place icons); Engine and Content N/A (no world change; the shapes are THR-1904's).

Every place on the map now draws its carved icon in the map's near-black ink: a capital the tower and flag, a city the gate, a lair the cave entrance, and THR-1879's picks for the rest. The hand-drawn silhouettes are gone from `locationIconRegistry.ts`, which now holds only each type's size class.

- **One builder.** `buildMapIconTexture` in `locationIconTextures.ts` rasterises any `iconShape()` result: outline pass, then fill, scaled from the shape's own viewBox, so a 512-grid carved shape and a 100-grid drawing fill the same box. Lenses II (THR-1898) reuses it for badges and battle sprites.
- **256 px textures** (was 128), for the carved detail at close zoom. Size classes and the lair sphere tints and cleared-lair fill are unchanged.
- **Every place subtype is in one of two lists.** `LOCATION_ICON_REGISTRY` draws it; `UNDRAWN_PLACE_TYPES` leaves it off on purpose, each with a reason: elder ruins (hidden delve sites until found), open wilderness, transit waypoints, farmland, trade-route nodes, and oasis and bridge (never drawn before; drawing them is a separate call). Leaving a new subtype out of both lists is a type error.
- **Unknown type → fallback.** A string that is no place subtype draws the empty-ring fallback at `small` and warns once, instead of nothing. `__DEBUG.getMapChrome().placeIcons.fallbacks` lists them.
- **Law 58.** `placeIcons.coverage.test.ts` sweeps every `LocationSubtype` (through `PLACE_ICON`'s keys, not the registry's own), checks each drawn type's carved shape, and pins Christian's eleven shapes. `extending.md` has the row.
- **Review gate** (two rounds). Round 1 found three problems in the first cut: it drew undiscovered elder ruins, it put fallback rings on transit waypoints, oases and bridges, and its coverage test only checked the registry against itself. All three are fixed. Round 2 made the fallback record describe the current map build. The cleared lair keeps the lair's shape with a muted fill, by THR-1879's design.
- **Found, not fixed here:** place-name labels already show for undrawn types, so the names of undiscovered elder ruins appear on the map (an earlier defect). Filed as [THR-1960](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1960).
- **Evidence.** `npm run gate` (head `e94fd892`): test, typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards and ui-debt all PASS; after the review fixes the touched suites (78 + 10 tests) and the typecheck ratchet (2782, unchanged) pass again. Playwright at 1920×1080 (k 6.7 and 13.5) and 2560×1440, `placeIcons.fallbacks: []`, and no console errors.
