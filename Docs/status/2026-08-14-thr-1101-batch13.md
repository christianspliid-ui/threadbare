## Register

*Knowledge that lives in other people, and charges admission.* Batch 12's divination sources
were wholly indifferent to being read — the sky, the stone, the seal. Every source here is
addressed to **someone**: a text written for initiates, a hermit who chooses whom to teach,
soldiers talking among themselves. So failure is never blankness. It is being handed the
version outsiders get — the doctrine word-perfect and no sense of what it is for, a
decorative border read flawlessly forty-one times, every account agreeing, which is the
trouble with them.

That premise carried an academic examination, a temple archive, two kinds of epigraphy, a
wilderness teacher, a pilgrimage and three registers of hearsay without fragmenting, which
**confirms batch 11's call to split `explore` by register rather than by count**: these nine
are audibly not the divination nine.

## The regression lock — retired by inversion, not deleted

The Law 43 vacuity guard read `withTokens.length > 0`, and its own comment named the moment
it should die: *"when it lands there this assertion goes red and the guard is retired
together with the corpus it guards."*

It now asserts the population is **zero**. That is strictly stronger than deletion: the old
bound could only catch the campaign finishing, while this one catches the mad-lib shape
being **reintroduced**, which is the only failure anyone has to worry about from here.

The narrower "no enriched field contains a raw word-pool token" sweep beside it was deleted —
unfalsifiable at zero population, and strictly subsumed by the any-shape sweep, since `{adj}`
is one shape of `\{[^}]*\}`. That deletion is the one test the suite loses (16,085 → 16,084).

**The resolver is not dead code and must not be pruned on this test's strength.**
`resolveWordPoolTokens` stays load-bearing for the corpora that still author in the token
shape — `narrative-content.ts` (377 token lines), `monster-faction-definitions.ts`,
`culture-content.ts`, `tavern-names.ts`, `army-words.ts`. Only the *encounter* corpus was
drained.

## THR-1107 reconciliation — and a gap in its predicate

Four pronoun-agreement defects were fixed here. Two are from [THR-1107](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1107)'s
enumerated 21, leaving **19**:

- `hermits_wisdom[1].scene` — *"she are"*
- `garrison_gossip[1].success` — *"she need"*

The other two are the same defect class and were **not** in that enumeration:

- `garrison_gossip[1].scene` — *"she actually want"*: `{they}` + **adverb** + bare verb. THR-1107
  explicitly classifies the adverb shape as a false positive (*"she privately"*), so its
  instrument cannot see this.
- `decipher_inscriptions.translate.failure` — *"She capture"*: the sentence leads with
  capitalised `{They}`.

**THR-1107's Done-when guard, written to the predicate as specified, would go green while the
class survives.** Recorded on that ticket.

## Impediment #573

The post-converter template **mirrors step prose onto `narrativeTemplates.*`** — step 0's
narrative becomes `.initiation`, the last step's afterimages become `.success`/`.failure` —
so 60 authored strings arrive on 87 surfaces. The probe's cross-template duplicate check
fired 27 phantom pairs, every one a template against *itself*, and its coverage assertion
read 87 against a source truth of 60.

It was caught **by** the anti-#572 assertion batch 12 required. A probe reporting only
"0 defects" would have shipped the phantom duplicates as a clean pass — which is the entire
argument for making a probe prove its own coverage before anyone trusts its verdict.
