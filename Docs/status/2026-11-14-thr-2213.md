# THR-2213 — The agency census ships

**Shipped 2026-11-14.** Thematic Pressure & Living World. Plan doc: `Docs/plans/2026-11-13-thr-2205-measure-whether-the-player-matters.md`.

- **`npm run census:agency`** promotes the design lane's proto. It plays the seeded route headless twice per seed: **hands-off** (every held step released with no card) and **played** (every affordable card played). Both leave forks to the mortal and cast nothing.
- **Modes.** One arm (`--seed N --arm none|greedy`); `--all --out-dir D` spawns the six arms (seeds 42, 99, 7) at most 4 at once with no wall-clock budget, then compares; `--compare` reprints the verdict from written files, and a missing arm or one stopped on `--budget-s` is "not measured". Arms run to t800 by default, the tick the headline is read at.
- **The verdict** (`scripts/agency-verdict.ts`, pure, unit-tested) compares five headline outcomes: which clock ended the age, the First's fate, the Apotheosis, Mandate progress ±0.10, Doom stage. It tests different, never better (Law 32).
- **Verdict on main @ 37954022:** the player mattered on **0 of 3** seeds (headline differences 0,0,0), the same as the plan's baseline. Cards were played (68 / 143 / 115) and the chapter mix moved (template overlap 0.73 / 0.66 / 0.95), but no headline outcome changed by t800.
- **Noise floor:** two hands-off seed-42 arms to t400 compare with 0 differences and chapter overlap 1.00.
- **Run time:** a t800 arm takes about 10–15 minutes with four running at once; launch `--all` in the background.
- Not wired into any gate, CI job or weekly review (pipeline freeze).
