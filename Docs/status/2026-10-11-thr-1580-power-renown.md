# 2026-10-11 — THR-1580 (slice 3): power renown reads the reach share

**Ticket:** [THR-1580](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1580) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-09-24-thr-1575-forecast-window.md` § Systems design 4 · multi-PR deferral, ticket stays open.

- **The reader.** `phaseReputationTraits` gives every actor `trait.reputation.power.renown` from its best tier across the eight reaches: tier ≥ 4 Noted, ≥ 6 Renowned, ≥ 8 Legendary. The trait gates The Renowned Duel and drives defer/recruit/avoid reactions. It is a standing label, not a d100 read. It read the pre-refit curve (midpoint 10, k 0.4), so every mortal past raw ~12 was Legendary.
- **Population.** Individuals are bimodal: most sit below raw 20 (p50 5, p90 10–12), and the protagonists sit at raw ≥ 40 (18 · 22 · 19 per world). Factions sit at raw 60–80 and are Legendary on every scale, so they do not move.
- **Measured** (`npm run measure:renown-scales`, every living actor, medium, tick 0, seeds 42 · 99 · 7). Individuals with any renown: pre-refit 26.4 · 14.0 · 14.0%, reach share 7.2 · 8.8 · 8.7%, dice 3.8 · 3.6 · 3.2%. Legendary: pre-refit 7.2 · 8.8 · 8.7%, reach share 3.8 · 3.5 · 3.2%, dice 3.6 · 3.5 · 2.9%.
- **Verdict: reach share.** Noted starts past raw 12, Renowned past 20, Legendary past 28. Above-ordinary mortals (raw 12–20) read Noted, and the protagonists read Legendary. The dice would leave the Noted rung empty, because it needs raw > 19.4 and almost nobody sits between 20 and 40. Tier thresholds are unchanged. `REACH_SHARE_FULL_RAW` and the three `REPUTATION_POWER_TIER_*` constants remain the levers.
- **Change.** `computeTier(computeReachShare(...))` replaces the pre-refit read. `renownLevelFromTier` is exported for the script and tests. One `TODO(THR-1580)` site is gone. `phaseReputationTraits.ts` is now a read site of `capability-thresholds-read-the-reach-share`. Four new tests cover the rung mapping and raw 10 → none, 15 → Noted (pre-refit read Legendary), 22 → Renowned, 30 → Legendary.
