# THR-2184 — While you were away

**Shipped 2026-11-06.** Warm playtest · round 3, slice 1 of THR-2173. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-11-06-thr-2173-while-you-were-away.md`.

1. **The recap.** When a `?warm=<ticks>` warm-up ends, the warm-up card stays up at the M tier and reads "While you were away": at most five lines (`WARM_RECAP_MAX_LINES`). The First's finished chapters come first, the newest two in the Aftermath's own `outcomeSentence`, the rest as "Kael finished three more chapters."; then "Kael is in the middle of …"; then the god's places that slipped ("Iron Glade slipped from Held to Touched.") or grew to Held or Sovereign. The empty case says "Nothing in your story changed while you were away." Kept by `WARM_RECAP_KEEP_ORDER`, shown in story order. The rival behind a slip is never named.
2. **Engine.** New pure view `src/engine/views/warmRecap.ts`: `snapshotWarmRecap` (dominion bands after the opening gifts settle, before tick one) and `buildWarmRecap` (archive filter, live chapter, band diff). No tick phase, no PRNG, no write.
3. **UI.** `useWarmStart` takes the snapshot through a new `onOpeningSettled` step, builds the recap at arrival (`buildArrivalRecap`, fail-soft: a missing snapshot falls back to chapter lines only, a throw closes the card as before), and returns `recap` / `dismissRecap`. The interrupt entry also holds while `warmRecapOpen`. `WarmStartOverlay` draws the recap: names as `EntityLink`s, band words with their `ui.dominion.<band>` hover, a muted next-step line, **Read it in the Ledger** and **Begin** (focused; Escape is Begin). The Ledger button lives in `GameView` (`handleWarmRecapOpenLedger`), so the R4 rule's chrome-opener list is unchanged.
4. **Time left.** Below three minutes the line steps in half minutes ("About 2½ minutes left." … "About half a minute left."), still rounding up.
5. **Done line.** `[warm-start] done` gains `recapLines` and `recapCut` (additive).
6. **Not exported:** the plan's note to export `formatList` from `phaseDominion.ts` was not needed — the recap's place list carries links, so it is built as segments (`WARM_RECAP_LIST_NAMES`, same three-names-then-"and others" shape).

7. **Review gate, round 1 (three findings, all fixed). These depart from the plan.**
   - **The band diff never ran.** The dominion ledger is written only inside a tick, so before a warm-up's first tick its `bands` are empty and no place line could ever appear. The recap now reads every place's band live through `dominionOf` over `getLocationNodes` (`readWarmRecapBands`, the same read the tick writes into the ledger), before and after.
   - **The recap is exclusive.** Under R4 a dialog co-renders with a Stage surface, so an encounter or a second dialog could open beside the recap while it said nothing waited. While `warmRecapOpen` (and the warm-up is not running), `resolveInterrupts` renders only `warm-start`; everything else is held and opens on Begin.
   - **The done line counts what waits.** It is still written at arrival, because round 2 found that the UX-review journey's `10-mid-game` moment waits for it without pressing Begin. With a recap up, `openInterruptsAtArrival` reads the surfaces held behind the card (`getHeldInterrupts`): the ones that open on Begin.
8. **Review gate, round 2 (three findings, all fixed).** The done line moved back to arrival, as above. That also removes the false count when the Ledger button is pressed. The `dominion-bands-feed-warm-recap` contract now names `dominionOf` as what the recap reads.
