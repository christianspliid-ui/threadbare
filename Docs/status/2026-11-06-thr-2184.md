**Shipped 2026-11-06.** Warm playtest · round 3, slice 1 of THR-2173. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-11-06-thr-2173-while-you-were-away.md`.

1. **The recap.** When a `?warm=<ticks>` warm-up ends, the warm-up card stays up at the M tier and reads "While you were away": at most five lines (`WARM_RECAP_MAX_LINES`). The First's finished chapters come first, the newest two in the Aftermath's own `outcomeSentence`, the rest as "Kael finished three more chapters."; then "Kael is in the middle of …"; then the god's places that slipped ("Iron Glade slipped from Held to Touched.") or grew to Held or Sovereign. 2. **Engine.** New pure view `src/engine/views/warmRecap.ts`: `snapshotWarmRecap` (dominion bands after the opening gifts settle, before tick one) and `buildWarmRecap` (archive filter, live chapter, band diff). No tick phase, no PRNG, no write.

The interrupt entry also holds while `warmRecapOpen`. `WarmStartOverlay` draws the recap: names as `EntityLink`s, band words with their `ui.dominion.<band>` hover, a muted next-step line, **Read it in the Ledger** and **Begin** (focused; Escape is Begin). The Ledger button lives in `GameView` (`handleWarmRecapOpenLedger`), so the R4 rule's chrome-opener list is unchanged.
4. **Time left.** Below three minutes the line steps in half minutes ("About 2½ minutes left." … "About half a minute left."), still rounding up.
5. **Done line.** `[warm-start] done` gains `recapLines` and `recapCut` (additive).
6. **Not exported:** the plan's note to export `formatList` from `phaseDominion.ts` was not needed — the recap's place list carries links, so it is built as segments (`WARM_RECAP_LIST_NAMES`, same three-names-then-"and others" shape).
