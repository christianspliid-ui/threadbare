# THR-1515 — every catalyst family has a member a hamlet accepts

*Deferred from [THR-1511](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1511), which anchored a completed
undertaking's catalyst on the work's own Location and measured the residue: on seeds 7, 42 and 99
every remaining catalyst wither had **both** the anchor and the feet at a hamlet or in the wild.
No member of any family in `UNDERTAKING_CELL_CATALYSTS` accepted `hamlet`, and `cell.create.location`
*founds* a hamlet (`UNDERTAKING_DEFAULT_LOCATION_SUBTYPE`), so the settlement the founder raised
rejected its own wake by construction. THR-1511's grooming block sanctioned this follow-up.*

## What shipped

**One existing member per family now lists `hamlet`**, chosen where the fiction already survives
the place: `bf.quest.lay_foundation` (a first foundation in a settlement the work founded),
`tg.quest.fence_goods` (a back-room fence needs no city), `mct.quest.market_survey` (a market day
read at a hamlet's stalls), `mc.quest.patrol` (a section of road), `crafting.quest.flawed_steel`
(a village forge), `cg.quest.break_up_brawl` (a brawl on a hamlet's one street). Content only —
no engine file changed.

**The other sanctioned shape — an ungated member — was declined.** `unifiedCandidates.ts`
offers any ungated `local` / `individual` template at every location to every mortal, so ungating
a guild errand would put it in the ambient pool everywhere. The gate stays; it just admits hamlets.

**`undertakingCellCatalysts.test.ts`** now asserts every family has an individual-performable
member for each of `hamlet`, `town`, `city` and `capital` (the same four every engine settlement
set names), and — new — that the founding cell's own product is accepted by its family, read off
`UNDERTAKING_DEFAULT_LOCATION_SUBTYPE` so a change to what is founded moves the test.

**`settlement` swept out of 18 encounter gates** (the ticket's "while here"). No location producer
writes that subtype, so it gated nothing but read as real to an author. In twelve templates it sat
beside real subtypes and was simply dropped. In six — the blinded oracle, the silent chamber, the
jury of the ruined, the oracle consulted, the star pilgrim, the pilgrim's offering — it was the only
settlement word, none of them declares a `settings` envelope, and it is now spelled `town`, `city`,
`capital`: those six become eligible in towns for the first time, which reads as the author's intent
rather than a widening.

## Measured

- **Seed 99 `cell.create.location`, both arms.** Before: `catalyst_seeded: fail — #fellowship_errand
  resolved empty at undertaking_catalyst — judged at anchor loc_hamlet_13_20_20 (hamlet), then feet
  loc_68 (fort); the family has no member either place accepts`. After: `catalyst_seeded: pass —
  #fellowship_errand resolved at undertaking_catalyst → bf.quest.lay_foundation … after 3 settle
  tick(s)`. Seed 5 lands the same; seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 miss the catalyst roll (0.65) and are
  `not_declared`, all five runs proved.
- **Seed 42 / medium / 200 ticks, after.** 12 catalyst seeds: 11 spawned, 1 withered, 0 pending.
  Six of the eleven were judged at a `hamlet` anchor (`tg.quest.blackmail_ledger`,
  `tg.quest.fence_goods` ×2, `crafting.quest.flawed_steel`, `mc.quest.guard_caravan`,
  `bf.quest.lay_foundation`). **Zero withers with a hamlet on either side.** The one wither had both
  anchor and feet at `wilderness`. Read off the seed-consumption ledger (`state.tickEvents`) and a
  per-tick ring harvest with zero evictions, never off the ring alone.

## Observed, not fixed

- **The wild.** A mortal in open country stands at a transient Location whose subtype is
  `wilderness`, which no catalyst family member accepts — the one residual wither above. It is
  closable without ungating: a member whose fiction is open country (`mc.quest.patrol` is the obvious
  one) lists `wilderness`. Left out because the ticket's predicate is the hamlet and the wild is one
  wither in twelve.
- **`ruins` for `#watch_errand`.** `cell.destroy.location` turns a settlement into `ruins`, and no
  Watch member accepts `ruins`, so the wake of a razing withers at the object the same way the
  founding did. Same shape, different subtype; not measured here.
- **More dead subtype words.** The sweep surfaced other values in `locationSubtypes` that
  `ALL_LOCATION_SUBTYPES` does not carry — `ruin` (the live word is `ruins`), `village` (`hamlet`),
  `fortress` (`fort` / `castle`), and `road`, `palace`, `academy`, `docks`, `mine`, `barracks`,
  `tavern`, `dungeon`, `battlefield`, `crossroads`, `border_crossing`. Some may be Place type ids
  the gate legitimately matches; a census against what the world writes is a design-session item,
  not a drive-by.
