## What shipped

- **The planner forecasts through the roll's scale path.** `forecastStepProbabilities` (`plannerForecast.ts`) goes through `forecastActionAtScale`; `estimateStepProbability` (`encounterScoring.ts`) through the new `scaledStepProbability` (`scaledForecast.ts`). Both apply the template's scale offset, the difficulty cap and the post-roll floor exactly as `resolveStepCore` does. The plan's case (capability 0.55, local, d 0.45) was planned at 0.10 and rolled at 0.65; it now plans at 0.65.
- **Cache entries carry `scale` and `stepFailBehaviors`** (`buildEntryUnified` and the faction-quest builder, via `stepFailBehaviorsOf`).
- **`PLANNER_DIFFICULTY_MULTIPLIERS_ENABLED = false`** (`agent-behavior-constants.ts`). The cache stores authored difficulty; the late-game ×1.3 and danger ×(1+0.5·danger) multipliers stay in code behind the switch.
- **Engagement forecast `F`** = P(the action ends in the success family): the product over steps of `1 − P(crit fail) − [fail_action]·P(plain fail)`, mirroring `advanceUnifiedAction`/`computeFinalActionOutcome`. It sits on `EncounterForecast.engagementForecast`, `ScoredCandidate.engagementForecast` and the `encounter_scoring` trace. `completionProb` is unchanged.
- **Growth-term units fixed.** `difficultyScaling` bands a 0–100 value and was handed 0–1, so every encounter offered the same growth credit. The term is now `estimateEncounterGrowthValue(entry, currentCap)`, exported and tested.
- **Interface row `planner-forecast-equals-roll`** (LIVE, verified by the parity test). `stepResolutionCore.contract.test.ts`'s resolver-importer pin drops `encounterScoring.ts`, which now reads the forecast through `scaledForecast.ts`.

## Evidence

- `plannerForecastParity.test.ts`: real cache entries over ten location kinds × three skill levels. That is **1,470 steps across local/regional/cosmic**, and on every step the planner P equals `previewStepProbability` to 9 decimals (the plan's bar is mean ≤ 0.02). The expected-utility path agrees within one d100 point. `F` equals the exact product on the fail_action + continue_weakened fixture and exceeds `completionProb`. Growth credit is distinct across five difficulty bands.
- Five older unit pins of the unscaled planner were updated to state the scale floor they now read. `GameView-momentCard.test.tsx` (heavy) had `MAX_TICKS` raised from 96 to 120: seed 42's decisions moved, and the first interrupt on its three followed works now lands at tick 102 (main: inside 96).
