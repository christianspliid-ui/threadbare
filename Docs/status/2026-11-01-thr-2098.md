# THR-2098 — the art-led Inspector pages (slice 2)

**Shipped 2026-11-01.** UI Visual Overhaul — Design System v1. Slice 1 dressed the person page; this slice applies the same rules to every other page kind: places, lands, factions, armies, relics, the god, content cards and records (plan `Docs/plans/2026-10-31-thr-2098-art-led-inspector-pages.md`).

**What changed.** At wide and Full every page now leads with its picture at the aside width, the name under it: a 16:9 hero for places, lands, factions, armies, relics and records, and the 3:4 portrait for people and the god. Docked keeps the 9rem thumbnail. A town's header now shows its own landscape: the page hands the tile's terrain to the existing art resolver, the one art path (Law 3). Before this, all 235 places in seed 42 drew the glyph. A lair's own art still wins. Every heading draws its carved icon, or is on the pick list for THR-2104 (`NO_IDEA_ROWS`, held by a registry-walk test). The fifteen label strips are gone. Standing, place of power, area facts, agenda, means, effect, duration and the event's callback read as sentences, each fact a linked or tooltipped term. Strongest reaches, ranks, reputations, essence, signatures, reaches and the Area's spheres are chips, and their bars drop. A faction that regards no one says "They hold no one in regard yet." The Mandate is the god page's one highlight, its name over "{trend}. {objective}"; its bar stays on the god rail. "How they hold her" is the faction page's one highlight. Relics at a place ("Relics here · N") and relics a faction holds ("Relics they hold · N") show as art tiles in the aside.

**Executor decisions.** Means reads as one clause per band ("They live in comfort", "They get by"), because "They are {band}" breaks on "Getting by". A loss key ("breakable") becomes a sentence ("It can be broken."); a loss written as words reads "It is lost when rest heals it." A town's subtype plate (town.png) does not beat its landscape; only lairs keep their own art, per decision 1. The faction highlight's headline is the faction's name.

**Evidence.** `artLedDressing.thr2098.test.ts` has 13 tests: place terrain, a lair's art, the registry walk, no panel among the fifteen rows, the sentences, the galleries, one highlight per page, and no numeral beyond the two named exemptions. `SubjectHeader.render.test.tsx` checks art tier `art` with terrain and none without, plus the docked thumbnail. `npm run gate` passes. Browser (bash-vite on :5247 + Playwright, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`): the town Black Crag-dawn draws `/concept-art/mountains.png` (`tier: art`); faction heraldry draws `art`; `iconFallbacks` is empty on every lab cell; no lab cell leaks a numeral or overflows sideways; the name sits at y=355–401 of 1080; the console shows 0 errors at 1920×1080 and 2560×1440.
