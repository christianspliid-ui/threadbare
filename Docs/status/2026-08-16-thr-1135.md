# THR-1135 — Stone's virtue word is `Careful`, and three vignettes stop borrowing Heart's meaning (2026-08-16)

**The defect was vocabulary, and the engine was right the whole time.** Christian opened Sevrin and saw the chips **Disloyal** and **Dependable** together. That pairing is mechanically legal — Heart (Sworn/Loyal ↔ Renegade/Disloyal) and Stone (Keeper/Dependable ↔ Destroyer/Reckless) are orthogonal axes and the emergence thresholds grant each independently — but "Dependable" in plain English means *people can rely on you*, which is an interpersonal-trust reading and therefore Heart's. Two words on two axes were pointing at one idea, so the sheet read as a contradiction on every surface that showed both. Creative-director decision: rename the **word** to `Careful`. `Careful ↔ Reckless` is a clean antonym pair with no trust connotation; the role name `Keeper` and the entire vice pole are untouched.

**One cell changed, because the registry is genuinely the single source.** `CANONICAL_AXES` in `src/types/axisRegistry.ts` is the only place the word is authored, so the chip name, the slider pole labels, the trait `description` ("their stone leanings have hardened into the Keeper's way — **careful** in how they choose") and the "has become X" event line all followed from a one-line edit. Confirmed against the live world rather than by reading imports: after `tick 30` on seed 42, `state.graph.getNode('trait.personality.stone.virtue')` returns `name: 'Careful'` with `id` unchanged and `subcategory: 'personality'` intact. **The id never moved** — trait ids are `trait.personality.{reach}.{pole}`, so this is display-only with no save or grant/release migration, and a test pins that explicitly so a future rename cannot quietly become one.

**The prose carried the same bleed as the label, so four lines were re-anchored too.** A word change alone would have left the Stone virtue *narrated* as reliability. The Stone flavor line was `Is the thing others lean on when it gives way.` — a lean-on-me image, Heart's meaning wearing Stone's name — now `Checks the beam before trusting weight to it.` Three of the nine Stone virtue origin vignettes encoded interpersonal trust or promise-keeping and were re-anchored on foresight and vigilance, **ids and magnitudes unchanged** so nothing about who draws them shifts:

| id | was | now |
|---|---|---|
| `origin.stone.virtue.held-a-key` | trusted to hold a secret or a key | `Kept the seed grain sealed and dry through two wet winters.` |
| `origin.stone.virtue.spare-key` | the one the street left their spare key with | `Checked every latch on the street twice at night, and found the one left open.` |
| `origin.stone.virtue.years-of-hardship` | stood by a promise through years of hardship | `Walked the flood wall every night of the wet season, and knew before anyone where it would give.` |

The other six (lamp, chores, dull-task, long-illness, relief-post, failing-farm) already read as steadiness and vigilance and fit `Careful` unchanged — they were left alone rather than rewritten for symmetry.

**Scope item 7 was checked and correctly produced no edit.** `prose-composer-playground.html` does contain the string "dependable", and it is not this word: the line is `SPHERE_LOCATION_PROSE.matter` — *"stone is solid and dependable, mineral wealth draws inhabitants"* — a Matter-sphere **location** description about literal stone as a building material. The playground carries no axis vocabulary at all (no `Keeper`, no `Reckless`, no `Destroyer`). The ticket's own body anticipated this class as "only incidental prose usage"; the scope line was conditional (*"if present"*) and the condition is not met. Historical plan docs (`Docs/plans/2026-06-29-*`) were likewise left recording the vocabulary as approved at the time, per the ticket.

**The evidence is a render assertion, and it was falsified before it was trusted.** `OverviewTabStoneVirtueWord.test.tsx` renders the real component and asserts both independent paths to the word — the emergent trait **chip** (whose fixture takes its name from the real `PERSONALITY_TRAIT_DEFINITIONS` node, so the test exercises registry → definition → display → DOM rather than restating a literal) and the moral-axis **pole label**, which `OverviewTab` reads straight off `CANONICAL_AXES` — plus the absence of "Dependable" anywhere on the sheet. Reverting the registry cell to `Dependable` fails all 5 assertions; restoring it passes all 5. This substitutes for the contractual 1920×1080 capture, which no unattended run can produce (impediment #546 — `preview_start` is refused with nobody present to approve it). It covers that the word reached the surface; it does not cover paint, overflow, or z-index, and no layout changed to put those at risk.
