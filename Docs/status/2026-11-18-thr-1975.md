# THR-1975 — A hand row shows the full card face on hover

**Shipped 2026-11-18.** UI Visual Overhaul — Design System v1. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-14-thr-1875-screen-frame.md` § F6 ("Hover opens a `HoverCard` with the full `CardFace`").

- **The change.** Hovering a god rail hand row lays the card's full `CardFace` beside the rail: picture, verb chip, reach, the paying sphere's tint, the framed par price, the name and the card's one line. It closes on leave and on Esc (a transient escape layer in the tooltip band). The row's name `Tooltip` is gone (Law 3); over the price, the price's own `ui.hand_price` hover answers and the face stands down, so one surface shows at a time.
- **The builder.** `src/components/Game/frame/handCardFace.ts` (`handCardFaceModel`) is the target-free `CardFaceModel`: it takes the Ascendant Beat's `templateToPreviewSlot` and lays the row's par price (`HandCardVM.parCost`, new), paying sphere and payable/reason over it, then runs the shared `actionCardModel`. No odds zone (no target, no roll). An unpayable card dims and says why. The effect line falls back to the description when a template has no effects prose.
- **The host.** `src/components/Game/frame/HandCardHover.tsx` portals the face, `inert` and `pointer-events: none`, right of the rail (left when the right has no room), its top clamped to the viewport (Law 33). Chosen over a `DetailPage` for templates: it adds the least new surface.
- **The test.** `src/components/Game/frame/__tests__/handCardHover.test.tsx` (11 cases): the builder's price, tint, odds and dim; the position clamp; open on hover, close on leave and Esc, no name tooltip, the face standing down over the price, fail-soft on an unknown id.
- **Browser.** Bash-Vite on port 5287 + Playwright at 1920×1080, `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`, three cards granted through `__DEBUG.grantUnlock`: hovering "Oneiric Sending" opens the face at x 328–538 beside the rail (right edge 320), one `role="tooltip"` on the page, the escape layer listed as "hand card face" at band 70, 0 console errors. Evidence: `Docs/evidence/thr-1975/hand-hover-1920.png`.
