# 2026-10-06 — THR-1744 (phase 1): the warm-start lever and the warm playtest mode

**Ticket:** [THR-1744](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1744) · **Plan:** `Docs/plans/2026-10-05-thr-1744-warm-playtest.md` · **Pillars:** Engine (one pure helper); Content N/A (harness text); UI (start lever + overlay).

## Why

Cold playtest rounds never get past the first ten minutes, so no tester has reached factions, undertakings or ambitions — the systems a headless check found weakest. A warm round starts the same no-knowledge testers a few seasons into a world with The First bonded.

## What shipped (phase 1 — code, harness, skill)

- **`?view=game&seeded&warm=<ticks>`** (works on the deployed build; no `__DEBUG`). `useWarmStart` advances the world through `runTicksSync` in chunks behind `WarmStartOverlay` ("The world moves on"), with The First on Lives on and interrupts held by the existing `interruptSuppressedUntilTick`. At the end it re-tiers the warm-up's undertaking moments to unacknowledged badges (`settleUndertakingMomentsAsBadges`), restores the attention mode, clears suppression, and logs one `[warm-start] done {…}` console line. Nothing is decided for the player.
- `runTicksSync` gains `{ markClock }` so the arrival keeps the "Time is still" prompt. `WarmStartOverlay` is an interrupt surface.
- The opening spine beat now waits while a journey vignette is pending, so two held decisions open one at a time (measured: warm=150 had both open at once before the change).
- Harness: `run-round.ps1 -Mode warm` / `run-player.ps1 -Mode warm`, `player-brief-warm.md`, warm keys + `coverageMarkers` in `config.json`, and a coverage step in `extract.mjs` (`--coverage-only`). Calibration on cold round 2 reproduces the plan's baseline (faction/undertaking/ambition `—` for all three, thread history reached by all three).
- `cold-playtest` skill gains `--warm`; the lane prompt mirror, registry row and `keep-work-flowing-cc` fold know the warm series; Linear label `warm-playtest`.

## Measured (dev build)

`warm=150`: 150 ticks in ~22 s, `firstModeRestored: pause`, `pendingInterruptsAtArrival: 0`, one decision waiting (`JourneyVignetteModal`). `WARM_START_CHUNK_TICKS` is 3, not the plan's 10: at 10 the page blocked ~2 s per chunk and Playwright screenshots timed out.
