# THR-1392 slice 4a — the verbs renamed, the registry redrawn on the catalogue, the grid

**2026-09-03 · engine + content + docs.** The undertaking model now speaks the world-object catalogue's words on both axes. Slice 4b (the migration of the 64 and the flag flip) follows; the retirement list below is the chat review the plan required before anything is deleted.

## The verbs (Christian, 2026-09-03)

**create · change · use · control · destroy · observe** — the four data verbs plus the two the game adds, ownership and yield. `change` splits like `control` does: **raise** one's own, **lower** another's (motive-gated, the hostile mirror). Two cells moved under the split: a blockade is a *lowering* of a route (it lifts), a smear a lowering of a standing; `destroy` now means the object as it was is gone (ruined, razed, disbanded, split, exposed). The old names (`found`, `improve`, `undo`, `survey`) are gone from the types, the constants, the verb line-sets (a new `change:lower` set), the cells, the resolver, the tests and the 59 ambition cell ids; the plain verb is the model's and the word the player reads is still the object's (a warband is *raised*, a masterwork *crafted*).

## The object types — fourteen catalogue kinds

`src/data/undertaking-objects.ts` registers Area, Location (seven classes; `create` founds the settlement class, `destroy` ruins settlement and stronghold), Place, Route, Faction, Company, Army, Network, Companion, Item, Power, Condition, Agreement (the mark class), Standing — never a code word. **42 live cells**: the 22 that shipped in slices 1–3, renamed, plus 20 redraw cells on operations the world already had but no undertaking reached: found a faction (`foundFaction`), raise and reinforce and stand down an army, roll up a network, recruit or send away a companion, cast a power (`activateSpell`, measured — a bestowed power that names no spell template is refused), cure a condition, dig for a secret, cultivate or smear or break a standing, trade along or toll a route, sabotage a Location, observe an area, a Location, a Place, a route, a faction. Every new cell is registered on the ambitions that would walk it (38 registrations across twelve ambitions).

## The grid — the map kept current (Christian's ask)

`npm run generate-undertaking-grid` renders **every world-object kind × every verb variant** to `Docs/canon/undertaking-grid.generated.md` and the served *Undertaking Grid* wiki page. Live cells come from the registry and nothing else; the curated half (`scripts/undertaking-grid-dispositions.ts`) holds a note per live cell (the op, what it does, the old templates it absorbs) and a disposition — *open* with the decision it waits on, or *not an object* with the reason — for every other cell. The generator fails by name on a live cell without a note, a non-live cell without a disposition, a disposition on a cell that shipped, or a kind with no place on the map; both outputs sit in `EXTERNAL_GENERATED_ARTIFACTS`, so the blocking freshness gate is the second lock. The catalogue's one-PR rule gained the fourth edit: a new kind arrives with its row on the grid. Today: 34 kinds × 8 variants, 42 live, 36 open, 17 kinds with a cell.

## The retirement list — the 64 authored templates

Classified by the operation each template actually ran, not by its fiction (`.cache/retire-list.ts`, seed-free):

- **60 absorbed by a cell** — 54 by the op they already called (the ten Place builds → `create × place`; the eight property changes → `change × location`; the seventeen intelligence writes → `observe`; the trade, warband, faction, mark and route ops → their cells) and six by intent where the template wrote nothing but the cell now does the thing the fiction promised (`burn_the_charts` and `destroy_masterwork` → `destroy × item`; `burn_the_mark` and `expose_mark` → `destroy × agreement`; `sever_network` → `destroy × network`; the six *maintain / guard / police / claim* stances → `control:claim × location`, the sustained mode).
- **4 retire outright** — `improve_masterwork` (change × item is an open cell; no op raises an item), `train_apprentice` (mentorship is its own initiative), `commission_quest` (the faction work-order phase's, not a mortal's work), `expose_cache` (prose only; a cache is not a kind).

Several court and merchant templates whose fiction was *influence* (`buy_influence`, `secure_office`, `negotiate_storage`, `extend_reach`) land in `observe × location` because intelligence was the only thing they ever wrote — the honest cell, and the reason `use × Location` (yield) and `use × Standing` (leverage) are open decisions on the grid rather than absorbed fictions.
