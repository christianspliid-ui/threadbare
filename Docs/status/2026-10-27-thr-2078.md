# THR-2078 — The Doom and Omen hints step aside for an open page

**Shipped 2026-10-27.** UI Visual Overhaul — Design System v1. Found by the first weekly UX review (no plan doc; a self-scoped bug).

**What changed.** A floating first-use caption (`src/components/shared/FirstUseCaption.tsx`, used only by the Doom and Omen top-bar items) now checks what lies under its four corners with `document.elementsFromPoint`, on the 400 ms re-read it already ran. It renders only while the map's root, now marked `data-first-use-caption-ground` (`src/components/HexMapV2/HexMapV2.tsx`), is underneath. The top bar it hangs from is skipped, because its top edge overlaps that row by design. With the Inspector, a Stage or a dialog over the spot, it hides without being dismissed, and it comes back when the surface closes. Fail-soft: with no `elementsFromPoint` (jsdom) or no marked ground, it shows as before. Laws 25, 35 and 53.

**Evidence.** `FirstUseCaption.test.tsx` has a new case: the caption hides over a covering surface, stays visible in the store, and returns over the ground. Browser, 1920×1080 at `?view=game&seeded&size=medium`: the Doom hint shows on the bare map; `openInInspector('faction','faction_0')` hides it (`doom` is still in `getFirstUseCaptions().visible`); closing the Inspector brings it back. No console errors. `npm run gate`: PASS (code track).
