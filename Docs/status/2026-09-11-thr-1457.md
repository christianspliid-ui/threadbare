## Written against the code, not the ticket body

Each entry was verified at its construct before a word was written, and three facts came
back different from — or absent from — the ticket's summary:

- **The Motive gate is opt-in**, an **unowned target is refused** (not allowed), and a
  type may **un-gate** one verb for a relation the actor holds (THR-1436, fails closed).
  None of the three is in the ticket body; all three are easy to assume backwards.
- **`hold`'s upkeep is a live gap**, not a documented behaviour — `neglectTicks` is
  incremented and never reset (THR-1287). The entry is seated on what the model *means*
  (renewed by working it) so it need not move when the reset lands.
- **`cast`'s floor is a second floor**, stacked after THR-571's, with distinguishable
  trace markers so *"the incapable scraped through"* and *"the god cannot fail"* are
  never read as one event.

**One executor's call, explicitly delegated:** *contested ambition* and *faction war* are
seated as **senses of Motive gate**, not headwords of their own — they exist only inside
the gate, where `[[Grudge]]` and `[[Rivalry]]` exist outside it too. The ticket asked to
"fix the two bare-prose motives into links"; since they are not headwords, the link that
actually helps is on the gate itself, so `[[Undertaking Verb]]`'s *motive-gated* now
resolves to the entry naming all four.

## A vacuous probe, caught

The first wikilink check ran `grep -qixF` per link — and `grep` **aborted** on every
invocation, so the `||` branch fired 28 times and reported all 28 links dangling. The
output looked like a real finding and was pure crash artifact. Re-run through a Node
probe: **4** genuinely dangling, of which one (`[[Motive Receipt]]`) resolved once its own
entry landed. The other three were mine and are fixed — `[[Standing]]` → `[[Reputation]]`,
`[[Divine Receipt]]` and `[[Outcome Band]]` to plain prose, since neither is a headword.
All 29 links this branch adds now resolve.
