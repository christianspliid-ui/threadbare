# THR-1254 — The Unfinished Rite: the corpus's first composed hand

**2026-08-26 · Encounter Experience · Content pillar**

`repertoire-deals-into-encounter-hand` is **🟢 LIVE**. It sat PARTIAL through two tickets for one
narrowing reason at a time — a thin play-profile corpus (THR-1247), then the fact that no
*shipped* template declared `ActionStep.deal` (THR-1248). What retires it is an encounter, not an
argument: **The Unfinished Rite** (`encounter.delve.the_unfinished_rite`), registered in both
catalog arrays, step 0 authoring two specials and declaring a fill.

A settlement is partway through a rite meant to end its own deaths; two of the six who began it
are already dead. The mortal must read the half-made working and rule on it — against a second
reader already on site, paid to say it will hold. One `veil` step at `fair`.

Design was fixed **before** the fiction (2026-08-24 ruling): `veil` is the thinnest reach (15 of
178), `sacred`/`arcane`/`rural` the thinnest setting classes, `delve` the thinnest family. The
hook was rolled after and the story written to fit. Binding draw `place` + `omen`, `place`
carrying persistence because `emit_omen` is print-only.

**The hand.** Two specials plus `deal: { count: 4, tags: ['insight','lore'] }`. Both are cards the
dealer structurally cannot mint: one binds `opposes: 'rival'` to a cast key only this scene
declares, the other grants `axiological_mark_apply` with `reach: 'veil'` — the spec § 3b case.
Measured, the same step composes a different hand per god — darkness/order draws Follow The Book
and Hide The Deed where force/matter draws Throw Full Weight, specials constant. The contract's
intent demonstrated rather than asserted.

**Three seams found by being first**, each fixed at source: the compiler **could not compile a
composed encounter at all** (#780 — `encounterPackageViolations` restated the whole-hand rules
and was blind to `deal`, rejecting two specials as "hand of 2, outside 4–8" while
`check:encounter` passed the same template; it now delegates to `checkComposedHand`); a dead
`core_curiosity` trait hook; and nine chips carrying `kind: 'status'`, not a chip kind.

**Evidence.** `check:encounter` clean, off the ratchet; `check:encounter-live` → `proved`; 1080
files / 17995 tests green; ratchet unchanged at 3010; build clean; 30-tick smoke to tick 30 with
384 agents. The composed hand is pinned against a repertoire built from a real sphere identity,
not a whole-library fixture, which would have asserted the fixture.
