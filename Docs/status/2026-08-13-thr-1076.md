# THR-1076 — the Codex stops speaking CRUD to the player

**Shipped 2026-08-13.** `mapDivineAction` / `mapMortalAction` / `mapTargetAction` in `codexRegistry.ts` piped `template.crudType` — an internal taxonomy for how a template mutates the graph — straight onto the player's browsable catalog of what a god can do. Law 14 forbids exactly this: *"No raw internal keys, ever… `snake_case` enums resolve through display vocabularies before render."*

## Three surfaces, not the two the ticket named

The ticket's Done-when named the subtitle and the `CRUD` detail row. Reading the panel **as composed** — which is what THR-1007 binds — found a third: `tags` carried `crudType` too, and `CodexDetailPanel` renders every tag as a visible chip that also feeds the search box. Same enum, same surface, smaller font.

| Surface | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Card subtitle | `Star · create` | `Star` |
| Detail row | `CRUD` → `create` | *(row removed)* |
| Tag chip | `create` | *(chip removed)* |

`mapDivineAction`'s subtitle was already sphere-based, so only two of the three mappers carried the subtitle form — worth noting because it means the leak was never uniform and a per-call-site fix would have looked complete after two edits.

## Deleted, not translated

Both the ticket and its filing comment preferred deletion, and the reasoning holds up: `update` covers everything from blessing a company to scorching a hex, so there is no honest single word for it. A vocabulary would have had to invent a distinction the axis does not carry. `crudType` stays on the template for the engine; it simply has no player-facing rendering, and the mappers now carry a comment saying so and why, so the next author is told rather than left to rediscover.

The `CRUD` **label** was the worse half of the defect. The enum is at least a real property of the action; `CRUD` is a database term used as a player-facing row heading.

## Guard

Two suites, deliberately at different layers, because either alone is a known trap.

1. **`codexPlayerVocabulary.test.ts`** — pins the invariant across the whole catalog (>150 action entries), not the three call sites, so a fourth mapper fails here instead of shipping the enum back. Sweeps subtitle, `details[].label` and `tags`; deliberately **not** `summary` / `technicalEffect`, which are authored prose where "creates a bond edge" is legitimate English rather than a leak. Guards the guard twice — non-empty entry population *and* non-empty `crudType` population, so the suite cannot read green by pinning nothing if the field ever disappears.
2. **`codexDetailPanelVocabulary.test.tsx`** — renders the real `CodexDetailPanel` against real catalog entries, one per mapper. Registry-level assertions prove the data is clean; they cannot prove the component does not reintroduce the enum on its own, which is the logic-layer-evidence-in-surface-clothes trap impediment #546 sharpened on THR-1035.

Falsified by reintroducing all three forms in `mapTargetAction`: exactly the three leak arms went red, naming the entries (`hex.bless_land: subtitle "Star · create" carries 'create'`), while the population guard and the blank-subtitle arm stayed green — correct, since they test different things. Restored with the inverse `Edit`, per impediment #549, rather than shell rewriting.

A fifth arm asserts the subtitle did not go **blank**. The fix is a deletion, so the risk it introduces is an empty card face rather than a leaking one, and that is the failure the deletion could plausibly cause.

## What was found and deliberately not fixed

The same UI-Laws pass on the composed panel found three **pre-existing** violations, filed as [THR-1103](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1103) rather than widened into per THR-1007: tag chips still render the raw reach key (`star` beside the resolved `Star` two rows below), `scale` renders raw (`regional`), and `Essence Cost` renders a bare numeral (`"3"`, Law 13). Measured, not inferred — `hex.bless_land` post-fix reads `TAGS: ["star","regional"]`.

Worth stating plainly so it does not read as an oversight: this change removed `crudType` from the tags array while leaving `reach` and `scale` there. That asymmetry is the scope line, not a miss.

## Verification

1002 files / 15,978 tests green; ratchet unchanged at 3447; `vite build` clean. No engine file touched, so no 30-tick smoke owed.

**Browser-verify substituted, pixel capture owed** — impediment #546, seventh occurrence. The route was decided before claiming rather than at verification, so it again cost no discovery time. Honest by the row's own scope test: the change is a pure deletion — one detail row, one tag chip, one subtitle suffix — so the failure classes only pixels catch (overflow, z-index, off-viewport paint) are structurally absent, since removing content from a vertical list strictly reduces layout pressure. The substitution is a render assertion on the real component, not a logic-layer stand-in.

**UI-Laws judgment** — Law 14: violation removed, all three surfaces (this is the ticket). Law 1: entries keep glyph, art and name; magnitudes untouched by this change. Law 13: no new numerals; one pre-existing (`Essence Cost`) filed as THR-1103. Law 17: no tooltip surface added or removed. Law 21: no entity links on this panel; unchanged. Law 37: N/A — the Codex is a catalog, not a multi-beat flow.
