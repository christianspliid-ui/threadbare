**A choice the god can't afford can no longer be played.** On the encounter veil, an authored choice that costs more than the paying sphere holds now dims and says "Not enough Mind essence" (naming the pool). It stays readable and selectable, but `Let fate decide` stays disabled while it is chosen. The commit handler now refuses the spend the same way a nudge hand does: the pool is untouched, the choice is not recorded, and a toast says the moment passed. Before this, the handler floored the pool at zero and applied the choice anyway, so a 5-essence choice could be bought for 1. The rule lives in `spendAuthoredChoiceEssence` (`encounter-stage/nudgeCommit.ts`). (THR-1720, 2026-10-04)
