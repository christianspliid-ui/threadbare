# THR-2102 — Art-led pass, slice 6: the ceremonies

**Shipped 2026-11-01.** UI Visual Overhaul — Design System v1. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-11-01-thr-2102-art-led-ceremonies.md`.

**Descriptors in the host (S6.1).** New `src/engine/views/ceremonyVisuals.ts`: `echoVisual` / `echoVisuals` (a legacy's face, knowledge-gated through `mortalFace`; a monument's place picture on its hex's terrain via `locationHex` + `landProseInputFor`; a relic's painting; a `card` echo has none; a lost origin keeps the designed tile under `echoDisplayName`), `godFaceVisual` (`ascendantPortraitSrc`, else the designed tile), `riteFaceVisual` (the shared resolver, ungated). `CeremonyHost` builds them once per harvest and per rite; the screens never read the graph for them.

**The harvest (S6.2).** It opens on the god's face in a ring at `--ceremony-medallion-w` (6rem at 1920, 7.5rem at 2560/3440). The title draws the `harvest` shape. Each echo card names the echo through `echoDisplayName` (decision 4), spells its spheres as words with their `sphere.<name>` tooltips and tints, and opens on its picture. A row of small pictures sits above the "Your age leaves behind" line, one per echo it names. The panel widens to `--ceremony-panel-max-w` (56rem), with cards at `HARVEST_ECHO_TILE_W` (11rem). The amber dot is gone. The header and leave-line tooltips are `block`, so "Cycle N complete" no longer shares the title's line and the line centres under its pictures.

**Kill criterion applied.** At 1920×1080 the triumphant fixture (five cards, three god's echoes) measured a 1145px panel with 16:9 echo bands, below the fold. Dropping the god's face to a chip was not enough (1089px). The echo picture is now a 40px chip beside the name (criterion 2), and the god's face could go back to full size: panel 47–1032px, button bottom 999px of 1080. At 2560×1440: button bottom 1240px of 1440.

**The end of the age (S6.3).** The ending heading draws `mandate` or `doom` (doom-kind shapes wait on THR-1908). The First's face is at `--ceremony-portrait-w` (7rem / 9rem; it was 72px).

**The rite (decision 5).** Both rite shapes draw the mortal through `EntityVisual`, so an unpainted mortal gets the designed tile, not a gradient smear or an empty disc. The opening face is at `--ceremony-portrait-w` (it was `min(220px, 18vw)`), keeps its radial mask, and keeps the `threading-rite-portrait` test id.

**Content.** `CEREMONY_HEADING_ICONS` in `view-content.ts`. The echo heading, the memory heading and the rite header have no fitting idea (`CEREMONY_NO_IDEA_HEADINGS`, for THR-2104). Meet The First and the start page are unchanged (decision 6).

**Debug.** `__DEBUG.getCeremonyDressing()` reads the open ceremony off its DOM: faces with role, tier and width; heading shapes; echo cards, cards with art; leave echoes and leave chips.
