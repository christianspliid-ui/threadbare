# THR-1082 — Consequence language: story-first aftermath chips

**Shipped 2026-08-12** · Plan doc: `Docs/plans/2026-08-12-thr-1082-consequence-language.md`

## What was wrong

Christian, reading The Unsafe Bridge's aftermath off a live screenshot: *"the consequences require a consequences icon language… writing out 'Vara's stone grew steadily' is not good enough — what does steadily even mean? how can a player use that word to gage anything"*. Two chips, three distinct defects:

- **TOLL** — *"The bridge spent something on this crossing that it will not get back."* No noun, no direction, no icon. "Something" is not a game state.
- **MARK** — *"Vara's Stone grew steadily."* The noun, and an adverb with no scale a reader can place against the adverb above it.

The load-bearing finding is that **nobody wrote the second one**. `growthSentence()` emits that exact shape for every capability growth in every encounter in the game, so no prose pass could ever have fixed it — and the engine *knew* the magnitude (a five-rung ladder) and threw it away at the surface. `steadily` was Law 13 being obeyed exactly as written: THR-1004 converted numerals to words and was right to. The law was a correct answer to "don't print `0.05`" and a wrong answer to "how much?".

## What shipped

**Engine.** `EncounterAftermathChange` gains five optional structured fields — `category` / `stateNoun` / `direction` / `magnitude` / `storyWeight` / `causeClause` — on a 278-importer type, all additive, so every pre-existing authored change renders unmodified. Every derived-sentence builder now returns `DerivedChange`: the sentence *plus* the structure it was built from. New `magnitudeBandIndex()` keeps the banding as an ascending rung index, flipping the arrays' highest-first declaration order once at the producer rather than at every consumer. The sentence is still built and still shipped, so THR-1004's numeral gate keeps its single address.

New `quintessence_shift` aftermath effect — Christian's "loss of confidence" shape. The engine has moved quintessence since TB-075 but only ever *itself*; nothing authored could. The applier does no arithmetic of its own, queuing the same `QuintessenceEvent` every other producer queues so clamping and dissolution stay in `phaseQuintessence`.

**UI.** Six display kinds fold onto four story categories — SCAR / BOND / BOON / PATH — at the adapter, wire `kind` untouched. **MARK dies**: a bucket named "everything else" can never be story-legible, so unknowns fold by polarity instead and there is deliberately no fifth bucket. New `DeltaCluster` shared primitive draws 1–3 triangles (`▲`/`▼`) or a gold `◆` for a way opening — the *same glyph family* as the encounter hand's setback marker, which was Christian's stated condition for approving chevrons, and deliberately **not** `OddsPips` (Law 10: pips mean effect on the odds everywhere). Chip anatomy is now icon tile → `CATEGORY · NOUN` tag → sentence → right-aligned cluster, with a first-contact legend persisted per Law 51.

Incidental drift renders **compact** — tag and cluster, no sentence — per Christian's 2026-08-10 ruling that it *"takes away from the encounter story"*. The adverb is demoted to the cluster's hover text, never destroyed.

**Docs.** Laws 13 amended, 15 rescoped, 31 clarified. The causality rule (cause → change, one sentence, the "bridge spent something" line quoted as the anti-example) landed in both authoring surfaces.

## What is outstanding

Substitution was **declined** rather than taken: #546's own scope test says a change that moves layout or stacking owes a checkpoint for an attended run, and this one adds an icon-tile column, a right-aligned cluster and a legend row. Nine jsdom render assertions on the real `EncounterVeil` cover the anatomy, and the PR is **held unarmed** pending the pixel pass.
