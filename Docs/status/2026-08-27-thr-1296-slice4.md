# THR-1296 slice 4 — the bind pass (2026-08-27)

**The binder now has a consumer.** Slices 1–3 shipped the registry, the scored board and
the mint valve each contract-tested and used by nothing. Slice 4 joins them into one pass
that runs immediately before every undertaking checkpoint and answers: who is in this
scene, and where does it play?

## What landed

- **`src/engine/binding/undertakingBindPass.ts`** — decide-in-binder / apply-in-lifecycle.
  Validates live bindings with the dual gone-test, consumes `rebindRequested`, then binds
  each slot the step wants: reuse, additive-only modify, or a queued mint. Location slots
  resolve to the stage rather than being scored — an undertaking already chose where it
  plays; what the row registers is the *persistence claim* on it, which is what makes a
  siege razing a bound stage loud instead of silent.
- **The schema seam** — `UndertakingCastSpec` + `StrategicActionTemplate.cast`, shipped
  deliberately unauthored. Doc 2 ([THR-1297](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1297))
  authors the rows; emptiness-pinning tests make its first row fail one on purpose.
- **Complication widening** — `complication` was produced by critical failure alone. A
  broken must-persist binding now reaches it too: the roll stands, but an advance is
  downgraded to advance-at-cost, or halts outright when the lost role was singular. The
  moment names the person — "loses Old Maerin" rather than the anonymous "hits serious
  trouble" — and fires exactly once per breakage (`lossReported`).
- **`awaiting_mint` deferral** — a slot waiting on a queued birth defers the checkpoint
  *without* charging the halt ratchet or the absence counter. Charging for the engine's own
  queue latency would read the mint budget as the actor's failure.
- **`isActorAtStage` resolver fix** — `resolveToParentLocation` climbs exactly one tier, so
  an actor nested two sublocations below their stage matched neither it nor its parent and
  read as *absent at every checkpoint forever* — a silent permanent stall. Both sides now
  compare through the recursive `resolveLocationToHex`. One-directional: deep-nested stages
  stop reading as absent; nothing that resolved before stops resolving.
- **Wiring the slice-1 seams to their first consumer** — the `removeNode` severance hook is
  registered front-of-tick, and both dissolution call sites consult the bound-stage hold.
  The two owed interface-map rows (`binder-decision-traced`, `binding-registry-reaper-hook`)
  landed with them.

## One bug the tests caught, worth recording

`reportLoss` first read the lost member's role off `graph.getNode(record.nodeId)` — a node
that is, by definition of "lost", already gone. `scarcity01(census, undefined)` scores
maximally scarce, so **every** node-removal loss would have read as singular and *halted*
its undertaking instead of downgrading it to at-cost. Fixed by snapshotting `boundRole` on
the ledger row at bind time: the ledger is what remembers there was a steward here.

## Three-mode evidence

`scripts/measure-binder-modes.ts` drives the real `resolveBinding` over real generated
worlds (seeds 42 + 99, 150 ticks, medium). A live-run trace read would be vacuous at this
slice — the pass fires only for templates declaring `cast`, and none do yet — so the harness
asks the question the kill criterion actually asks. It conditions on `rowsConsidered > 1`,
because a probe with nobody in range has only the mint row and minting there is the board
being *right*, not degenerate.

```
totals  reuse=85  modify=422  mint=2732  refused=0
scarce roles    (scarcity>=0.5, n=23):  reuse=8.5%  modify=44.1%  mint=47.4%
commodity roles (scarcity<0.5,  n=55):  reuse=0.2%  modify=0.0%   mint=99.8%
contested probes: 2221 — mint still won 77.2%

three-modes-occur: PASS
scarcity-steers:   PASS  (scarce→reuse, commodity→mint)
not-degenerate:    PASS
```

**Not retuned, and the reason is on the record.** The board is *bimodal*, not degenerate:
scarcity steers exactly as designed, and scarce roles produce a genuine three-way split.
But commodity roles mint ~100% of the time even with a role-matched local standing at the
stage — reuse needs a story-tie term ≥0.5 to overcome the mint row there. That is the
documented intent ("shared commodity cast reads as false coincidence") sitting very close to
a rule rather than a weight. The kill criterion is not tripped, and retuning now would be
tuning against a harness whose probes carry no authored identity requirements and no bonds —
both of which are doc 2's content and both of which move these numbers. Flagged for doc 2's
authoring pass instead, with the measurement as its starting reading.
