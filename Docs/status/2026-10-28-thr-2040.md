# THR-2040 — Hunger's stories are planted

**Shipped 2026-10-28.** M3: Dynamic Economy. A hungry town's people now live its hunger: on a town's second hungry day one resident is handed a hunger scene that fits the town (refugees, barter, the merchant's granaries, the exchange bell, mending nets…), and on the first day its granaries overflow one resident is handed a plenty scene (the harvest bounty, the market festival, the mill lease…). One per town every 10 days; the granary riot comes first. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-28-thr-2040-hunger-stories-planted.md` (the re-plan; the score lean of the first plan was measured flat and is not built).

**What changed.** New `src/engine/larderSeeds.ts` (`readLarder`, `pickLarderRow`, `plantLarderSeed`), called once per settlement from `unrestPressure.ts` `dailyPass` after the riot. It plants a `templateId` seed in the riot's shape (`sourceEncounterId: 'larder'`). Guards: a 10-day cooldown (`larderLastSeedTick`), a riot that day, a scarcity-arc move within the cooldown, no fitting row, no resident, and the kill switch. Every trigger day emits a `larder.seed` trace with its skip reason. Rows and constants live in `src/data/larder-seed-config.ts` (8 famine rows, 9 plenty rows). New `npm run census:larder`.

**Executor decisions.** The stable pick keys a private `mulberry32` on `src/lib/prng.ts` `hashString(town:tick)`. The raw hash barely moves between consecutive ticks, so on its own it would pick almost the same row in a town every time. No shared PRNG stream is drawn. A row whose template has no `locationSubtypes` gate fits every town, the same rule seeding's `gateAcceptsPlace` uses.

**Census** (`npm run census:larder`, seeds 42/99/7/1/2/3 × 360 ticks, medium, pooled; off = `setLarderSeedsEnabledOverride(false)`):

| | off | on |
|---|---|---|
| hungry spells (≥ 2 days) with a famine scene by someone in town | 10/48 (20.8%) | **35/47 (74.5%)** |
| glut runs with a plenty scene | 38/91 (41.8%) | **92/100 (92.0%)** |
| fed towns: famine / plenty share of firings | 4.9% / 3.3% | 5.0% / 3.3% |
| seeds planted / taken up by their target | — | 131 / 118 (90.1%) |

Every predicate passes: famine +53.7 points (needed ≥ 50% and +25), glut +50.2 (needed +15), fed towns within 0.1 point (needed ≤ 1), take-up 90% (needed ≥ 50%). Planted scenes spread over all 17 rows. The most frequent were festival_of_spheres (19), market_day_festival (19) and mill_lease_auction (17); plenty seeds outnumber famine seeds 97 to 34. Skips: 8 scarcity_arc, 3 cooldown, 6 no_resident.

**Review gate fixes.** The scarcity arc's hoarding seed now waits while a town's `larderLastFamineSeedTick` (famine plants only) is within the cooldown, and its phase trace names the withheld seed, so the guard holds both ways (`economicPower.ts`, one new test). The contract test's "one live template" check could never fail; it now compares definitions by content, since the registries hold equal copies by design.

**Evidence.** `larderSeeds.test.ts` (11) and `larderSeedConfig.contract.test.ts` (18). `npm run gate` verdict in the PR body.
