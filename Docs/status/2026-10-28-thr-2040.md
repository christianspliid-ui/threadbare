# THR-2040 — Hunger's stories are planted

**Shipped 2026-10-28.** M3: Dynamic Economy. A hungry town's people now live its hunger: on a town's second hungry day one resident is handed a hunger scene that fits the town (refugees, barter, the merchant's granaries, the exchange bell, mending nets…), and on the first day its granaries overflow one resident is handed a plenty scene (the harvest bounty, the market festival, the mill lease…). One per town every 10 days; the granary riot comes first. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-28-thr-2040-hunger-stories-planted.md` (the re-plan; the score lean of the first plan was measured flat and is not built).

**What changed.** New `src/engine/larderSeeds.ts` (`readLarder`, `pickLarderRow`, `plantLarderSeed`), called once per settlement from `unrestPressure.ts` `dailyPass` after the riot. It plants a `templateId` seed in the riot's shape (`sourceEncounterId: 'larder'`). Guards: a 10-day cooldown (`larderLastSeedTick`), a riot that day, a scarcity-arc move within the cooldown, no fitting row, no resident, and the kill switch. Every trigger day emits a `larder.seed` trace with its skip reason. Rows and constants live in `src/data/larder-seed-config.ts` (8 famine rows, 9 plenty rows). New `npm run census:larder`.

**Executor decisions.** The stable pick keys a private `mulberry32` on `src/lib/prng.ts` `hashString(town:tick)`. The raw hash barely moves between consecutive ticks, so on its own it would pick almost the same row in a town every time. No shared PRNG stream is drawn.

**Review gate (two findings, both fixed).** (1) The plan judged a row against any Place in the town, but the seed goes to one resident. A tavern-only row could go to a mortal standing in the market, wait, and wither. The planter now picks the resident first and keeps only rows that `templateAcceptsWhereTheyStand` accepts, the test seeding applies when the seed ripens. Take-up rose from 90% to 100%. Tavern-gated rows now plant only when the chosen resident is in a tavern. (2) A riot planted on hungry day 1 did not block the day-2 famine story. A riot within the cooldown now counts as `riot`. The riot itself still never yields to the larder, as the plan decides.

**Census** (`npm run census:larder`, seeds 42/99/7/1/2/3 × 360 ticks, medium, pooled; off = `setLarderSeedsEnabledOverride(false)`):

| | off | on |
|---|---|---|
| hungry spells (≥ 2 days) with a famine scene by someone in town | 10/48 (20.8%) | **31/44 (70.5%)** |
| glut runs with a plenty scene | 38/91 (41.8%) | **91/93 (97.8%)** |
| fed towns: famine / plenty share of firings | 4.9% / 3.3% | 4.9% / 3.6% |
| seeds planted / taken up by their target | — | 120 / 120 (100%) |

Every predicate passes. Famine +49.7 points (needed ≥ 50% and +25). Glut +56.0 points (needed +15). Fed towns within 0.3 points (needed ≤ 1). Take-up 100% (needed ≥ 50%). Planted scenes spread over 14 of the 17 rows. The most frequent were mill_lease_auction (26), festival_of_spheres (19) and market_day_festival (18). pickpocket, bell_at_the_exchange and the tavern drinking contest were never picked: each needs its resident standing in the right Place. Plenty seeds outnumber famine seeds 91 to 29. Skips: 8 scarcity_arc, 4 cooldown, 6 no_resident.

**Review gate fixes.** The row is judged where the chosen resident stands, so a Place-gated row never goes to a resident elsewhere. A riot seed within the cooldown blocks the larder, not only a riot that same day. The scarcity arc's hoarding seed waits while a town's `larderLastFamineSeedTick` (famine plants only) is within the cooldown, and its phase trace names the withheld seed (`economicPower.ts`). The contract test's "one live template" check now compares definitions by content.

**Evidence.** `larderSeeds.test.ts` (13) and `larderSeedConfig.contract.test.ts` (18). `npm run gate` verdict in the PR body.
