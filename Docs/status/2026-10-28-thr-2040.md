# THR-2040 — Hunger's stories are planted

**Shipped 2026-10-28.** M3: Dynamic Economy. A hungry town's people now live its hunger. On a town's second hungry day one resident is handed a hunger scene that fits the town (refugees, barter, the merchant's granaries, the exchange bell, mending nets…). Each run of overflowing granaries hands one resident a plenty scene (the harvest bounty, the market festival, the mill lease…), on the run's first day the guards allow. One per town every 10 days; the granary riot comes first. Plan: `Docs/plans/2026-10-28-thr-2040-hunger-stories-planted.md` (the re-plan; the score lean of the first plan was measured flat and is not built).

**What changed.** New `src/engine/larderSeeds.ts` (`readLarder`, `pickLarderRow`, `plantLarderSeed`), called once per settlement from `unrestPressure.ts` `dailyPass` after the riot. It plants a `templateId` seed in the riot's shape (`sourceEncounterId: 'larder'`). Guards: a 10-day cooldown (`larderLastSeedTick`), a riot seed within the cooldown, a scarcity-arc move within the cooldown, no fitting row, no resident, and the kill switch. Every famine trigger day, and the day a glut run opens, emits a `larder.seed` trace with its skip reason. Rows and constants live in `src/data/larder-seed-config.ts` (8 famine rows, 9 plenty rows). New `npm run census:larder`.

**Executor decisions.** The stable pick keys a private `mulberry32` on `src/lib/prng.ts` `hashString(town:tick)`. The raw hash barely moves between consecutive ticks, so on its own it would pick almost the same row in a town every time. No shared PRNG stream is drawn. A glut run tells one story, on its first day the guards allow (`larderGlutRunTold`), rather than only on its first day: a run whose first day fell in the cooldown would otherwise never be told. A town already glutted at its first larder read (a fresh world after its settling run, or an older save) gets a hash-staggered cooldown, so the world's open glut runs are told over the first 10 days instead of all on day one. An open run lasts while `foodSurplusDays` holds, so one day's balance under the floor does not re-open it.

**Review gate fixes.** The row is judged where the chosen resident stands (`templateAcceptsWhereTheyStand`), so a Place-gated row never goes to a resident elsewhere. A job-cast row (`protagonistRoles`, THR-1982) goes only to a resident of that trade; the seed path skips the draw's role-fit stage, so the planter applies it. A riot seed within the cooldown blocks the larder, not only a riot that same day. The scarcity arc's hoarding seed waits while a town's `larderLastFamineSeedTick` (famine plants only) is within the cooldown, and its phase trace names the withheld seed (`economicPower.ts`). The contract test's "one live template" check compares definitions by content.

| fed towns: famine / plenty share of firings | 5.0% / 3.3% | 4.5% / 3.1% |
| seeds planted / taken up by their target | — | 122 / 121 (99.2%) |

Every predicate passes. Famine +64.1 points (needed ≥ 50% and +25). Glut +49.4 points (needed +15). Fed towns within 0.5 points (needed ≤ 1). Take-up 99.2% (needed ≥ 50%). Planted scenes spread over 16 of the 17 rows; the most frequent were market_day_festival (18), mill_lease_auction (18) and festival_of_spheres (17). Plenty seeds outnumber famine seeds 85 to 37. Skips: 37 glut cooldown (mostly the staggered world-open runs waiting their day), 8 scarcity_arc, 5 no_resident, 1 famine cooldown.
