# 2026-10-17 — THR-1883: seizing a trade lane tolls the lane seized

**Ticket:** [THR-1883](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1883). Engine only; Content N/A (no words); UI N/A (no surface reads `controlledBy` directly yet).

- **The bug.** `control:seize` on a Route called `executeTaxTradeRoute`, which picks the busiest untolled lane at the caster's own durable location. A seizer standing at town A who took the B–C road put the toll on A's busiest lane and left B–C free. It only landed right when the seizer happened to stand at the lane's source (seed 99, t397).
- **The fix.** `tollTradeRouteEdge(graph, op, edgeId, controllerId)` (`src/engine/tradeRouteOps.ts`) stamps `controlledBy` and `taxRate` on one named `trades_with` edge. The seize verb passes the route identity's `routeEdgeId`. A missing or non-lane edge fails and writes nothing. The catalog `tax_trade_route` verb still acts from where the caster stands. Both share one `stampToll` writer.
- **Test.** `seizeTollsSeizedLane.test.ts`: the seizer at A, a busier untolled A–D lane, a B–C road. After the seize, B–C carries the toll and A–D does not. This test fails on the old code.
- **Evidence.** `npm run gate`: typecheck, build, encounter, god-cards, ui-debt, cli-smoke and test-heavy pass. `npm test` had 24,866 passes and 1 failure: `wanderingHealerAftermathProgression` hit its 5 s timeout under parallel load. That file is unrelated to this change, and run alone it passes in 842 ms.
