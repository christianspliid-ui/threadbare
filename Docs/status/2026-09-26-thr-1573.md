# 2026-09-26 — THR-1573: The Follow button says it keeps a mortal in close view

**Ticket:** [THR-1573](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1573) · **Project:** Thematic Pressure & Living World · **Plan:** the ticket body (a deferral from THR-1523) · **Pillars:** UI. Engine is N/A: THR-1523 already exempts followed mortals from stepping back out of the spotlight. Content is N/A: this is tooltip copy only.

## What shipped

The "Not following" and "Following" tooltips on the Follow toggle (`src/components/Game/FollowToggle.tsx`, `followToggleCopy`) now carry the clause *"and they stay among the mortals the world watches closely"*. Since THR-1523, an unwatched, idle builder can step back out of the spotlight, but a followed mortal never does. That makes Follow the player's lever for keeping a mortal in view, and until now the screen never said so. The clause uses game words only, with no tier name and no number (UI Law 13). The bond and muted tooltips are unchanged, because the step-back already exempts threaded mortals.

`FollowToggle.test.tsx` pins both strings and checks that neither leaks `spotlight` or `tier`.

## Evidence

Browser-verified on `?view=game&seeded&size=medium` at 1920×1080. The Journey tab of a mortal's sheet shows the new tooltip in both states, and `__DEBUG.getFollowedAgents().explicit` gains the mortal after pressing Follow.
