### 2026-10-05 — THR-1736: a mortal setting out for a meeting no longer starts work it cannot fit

**What was wrong.** When a mortal's promised meeting got close (the `departing` regime), the filter that drops encounters it has no time for priced the walk at one tick a hex. The regime's own slack prices the same walk on the road graph, so a few hexes costs about 13 ticks, not 4. Local work longer than the slack passed the filter. On seed 42, two mortals spent their whole departing window inside chains of local encounters and lost their meeting.

**Engine.** The departing drop now prices both legs (there, and on to the place) at the mortal's own road rate: `slack.travelTicks` over the hexes it covers (`appointmentTicksPerHex`, `departingTripOverruns` in `appointments.ts`). Local work is dropped exactly when it outlasts the slack. Kill switch `APPOINTMENT_DEPARTING_PRICED_TRAVEL`. `leaning` keeps the one-tick-a-hex proxy, because its discount is a lean, not a drop. A trip off the mortal's own hex never pays less than one tick a hex (`APPOINTMENT_OFF_PATH_MIN_TICKS_PER_HEX`), because a fast major road (about 0.4 a hex) would otherwise make detours cheaper than the old proxy did. The review gate found this; the floor did not bind on any of the twelve seeds, and the numbers below are re-measured with it. **Content / UI:** N/A. The player sees the result as meetings kept rather than broken.
