# Brainstorm — the ceremonies and the front door

## What the survey changed

The scope card names five surfaces. Reading them on `main` showed that two (Meet The First, the start page) already meet R1, and the rite meets it in looks but not in path. So the slice's weight sits on the two end-of-age screens, which open on words and, for the harvest, draw no art at all.

## Alternatives considered

- **Open the end of the age on the god's face, as the harvest does.** Rejected. The two screens play back to back, and the same face twice in a row is repetition, not emphasis. The end of the age is about how the age ended and what became of The First. The harvest is about what the god's age leaves. So each screen leads with its own subject.
- **Move The First above the ending at the end of the age.** Rejected. The ceremony was designed as ending → First → memories (THR-1381). Reordering it is a meaning change, not dressing.
- **Give the start page's menu carved shapes.** Rejected. Only Codex and Settings have an idea; New World and Credits would draw none. A menu where half the items have shapes reads as unfinished, and inventing shapes is THR-2104's job.
- **Show the card echo on the harvest with its card art.** Rejected for this slice. It is not listed today, and Law 53's spirit (dress, add nothing) holds for every slice in the programme.
- **A 40px picture beside each echo name instead of a 16:9 band.** Kept as the kill-criterion fallback. R3 asks for a band, and the measured layout (four 11rem cards a row in a 56rem panel) fits two rows at 1920×1080.
- **Pass `graph` into `HarvestScreen` and resolve there.** Rejected. Slice 1 and the ceremony host pattern keep the knowledge gate in one place: the host resolves and the component draws a descriptor.

## Tensions

- **Law 8 vs the harvest.** A legacy echo can be a mortal the god never came to know. The silhouette may feel odd at the end of an age. Kept: the gate is a law, and the echo's name still reads.
- **Decision independence.** Slices 3–5 made lane decisions within the last twelve hours. This slice draws on none of them. It reads the programme doc and the Laws only, so its veto window starts from its own decisions.

## Vision premises

- Non-negotiable #1, god not protagonist: the harvest opening on the god's face puts the god at the centre of the age's close.
- North star, the mortal as a person: The First's face, at a size that reads, beside their fate line.
