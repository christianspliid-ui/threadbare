# The dormant kinds II — rings and the plot — Brainstorm Companion

Companion to `Docs/plans/2026-09-07-thr-1430-dormant-kinds-rings-plot.md`. The decisions live on the wayfinder map [THR-1396](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1396) (the cells, the killing fork) and the Physical Conflict map's research ([THR-1261](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1261), the missing death API). This records the *how* that was weighed.

## Where the plan came from

The second half of the dormant-kinds band, split from the first because Networks and Mortal share no code with Powers and Conditions. The plot is the band's heaviest cell and the one Christian named a fork on 2026-09-03; THR-1397 resolved it in full, so the plan cites rather than decides. The one thing it corrects is the map's own systems table: the prototype counted `use × Network` as opening Stealth, and THR-1397's *no* to mortal surveillance feeding detection pressure means it does not. Said plainly in the plan rather than left to drift.

## Vision premises invoked

- **Mortal sovereignty** — the whole spine. A mortal kills a mortal on the world's own grudges; the god gets a warning and the levers it already has. The non-negotiable *binds the god, not one mortal against another* (THR-1397, verbatim).
- **The world remembers** — the dead stay as nodes; the retired assassinate card is retired *because* it deleted them.
- **Hyperconnectivity** — a ring is a multiplier on two verbs that now have readers (THR-1428); a plot's failure feeds the grievance lane; its success feeds nothing unless seen — the connection is the *seeing*, which is the grievance supply rule already in force.

## Alternatives considered

**A ring as a company with a flag vs a group kind that does not travel.** `GroupKind` already has `network`; the only thing missing is a writer and a movement exemption. A flag on a company would leave every group reader guessing. The kind is taken, with one filter on the movement phase.

**Run the ring as a new op vs reuse the two verbs' readers.** THR-1397 said the ring is *the multiplier on the two verbs already decided*. Reusing the observe reader and the mark op means a ring produces exactly what one mortal would, from farther away; a bespoke intelligence op would be the second familiarity system the readers plan refused to build.

**The plot's gate: the standard motive gate, or a narrower one.** The standard gate licenses seize and lower on a rivalry; THR-1397 said *never opportunism*. `holdsMotive` already splits `grudge` (injury provenance) from `rivalry` on the same edge family, so the narrower set costs one constant.

**Stages: three cells chained, or one cell with named checkpoints.** Three cells would put "the watching" and "the positioning" on the grid as verbs on a Mortal, which the grid says are not (observe × Mortal: *surveillance is observe on the kinds a mortal acts through*). One cell with three labelled checkpoints keeps the grid honest and the stages narrative.

**Death: delete, or retain and mark.** Decided on the map for the plot; the plan's contribution is the single helper. The first draft claimed all three existing writers retain the node; the intent judge found the lifecycle's own death path *deletes* (`agentLifecycle.ts:265-271`) — only band deaths and the aspect echo retain. So the helper carries a `retain` / `remove` mode and every writer keeps its current behaviour; whether *all* deaths should retain (which changes every node-absence reader) is recorded on the map as the next Agent-Lifecycle question rather than smuggled in here. The judge also found a second deleting card, `action.gold.commission-assassination`; it keeps its place as a god's card and stops deleting.

**The group phase and the network.** The first draft assumed cohesion and dissolution read `groupKind`; they do not — `phaseGroups` enumerates companies only, so a network would have been invisible to every sub-step, and "does not travel" would have gated a phase that never saw it. The enumeration is widened by kind and only movement is gated.

**The surface word.** *Ring* is not a UL term; the catalogue seats *Network*. Surfaces say Network; this document's title keeps *ring* as a gloss.

**The peril moment: a new god verb, or a warning plus existing levers.** A new intervention verb would be the fight-specific divine machinery the Physical Conflict charter ruled out for v1 and would put the god's hand *in* the killing. A warning with a grace window keeps the god's seat unchanged and makes wards and blessings matter.

**Vendetta on every kill, or only when seen.** THR-1383's rule already answers it; a clean kill that breeds an avenger would be the endless chain the grievance lane's own comment warns about. The exposure bands are where the story gets its avengers.
