# Brainstorm companion — The calling & the surfaces (THR-1299)

Plan doc: `Docs/plans/2026-09-02-thr-1299-calling-and-surfaces.md`. This records the alternatives weighed and the tensions carried, per design governance. Step-0 grill-me was **skipped with rationale**: every open decision was already resolved by the wayfinder map's decision tickets (THR-1279 + amendment, THR-1281 §7b, THR-1282 §6) and the review record's ratified rulings — the map process *was* the grilling, and this doc is the engineering of recorded verdicts. The genuinely open calls that remained (below) are calibration-class, which is agent-verdict ground.

## Alternatives considered and dismissed

- **Moments on the receipts vocabulary (`modal | toast`).** Dismissed. Ruling 2.1 pins moments to interrupt/badge with badges as the recovery route; a toast tier is a third thing — it acknowledges without recording, which re-opens the dismiss-spam hazard (review C1) and gives the badge family a member that self-destructs (Law 40 violation by construction). The receipts *idioms* (capped queue, idempotency, pure tiering fn) are copied; the vocabulary is not.
- **Follow state on the `thread` edge (a `followed` property, or removing the edge to unfollow).** Dismissed. `thread` carries court position with seven consumers (visibility depth, attend cost, tier matrix, curation…); overloading it means unfollow either destroys court state or silently means nothing. Separate GameState arrays keep player attention and court position as orthogonal axes, and make "followed by bond — mute?" honestly renderable.
- **A single `followState: Record<agentId, 'followed'|'muted'>` map instead of two arrays.** Considered; dismissed for save-compat symmetry with the shipped `followedAgentIds` (doc 1) — extending the shipped shape additively (NFP #6) beats migrating it for elegance.
- **Per-tick calling recompute with caching.** Dismissed on NFP #7 — the derivation only changes when its inputs change, and its inputs change at three enumerable write sites. Event-driven recompute also makes the hysteresis testable (each trigger is a controlled arm).
- **Calling stored in a new node type or edge.** Dismissed by the load-bearing rules' own test: a derived display label with no traversal consumers is node-internal data — a property, not a relationship.
- **Raising the chronicle threshold exception in `phaseNarrative` (an omen-style branch).** Dismissed in favor of a significance constant on the emitted event — one tunable number (NFP #1), zero orchestrator surface, and the chronicle's admission rule stays single.
- **The arc strip fed from the digest buffer.** Dismissed — 48-tick retention and background-encounter-only population make it a *recent activity* source, not a life arc. Persisted `StrategicHistoryEntry` + completed ambitions + christened works + calling changes are all durable state.
- **Building the named calendar.** Dismissed for v1 (the build-or-drop was this doc's call): a calendar is a whole new system serving one prose flourish; relative-time words already exist in the 12-tick-day vocabulary. Recorded as a veto invitation, not a silent drop.
- **Ascendant-bar freehold row.** Dismissed for v1 — Law 53 (HUD is a budget); the character sheet and arc panel are the canonical homes. `HooksBlock.tsx` names THR-1299 as owner, so the drop is recorded there too. Veto invited.
- **Auto-resolving moment cards in `beatDismissal`.** Dismissed — moment cards join the encounter-veil class of surfaces a verification run exists to observe.

## Tensions seen and carried

- **The interrupt arm is unreachable in every CLI world** (no thread edges → nobody followed → `presentation` never `'interrupt'`, THR-1293's measurement: 0 interrupts across 300 ticks × 2 seeds). Carried honestly: the Done-when gates on a *constructed* browser proof via debug follow levers, and organic CLI traces are observations only. This is the same discipline doc 4 applied to its aftermath chain.
- **The calling can be readable but inert** (review M1: "the sheet says Mender while he razes his third route"). Carried: the ambition term gets the heaviest weight because it is the input deeds actually move, and the chronicle-moment claim is gated on measured change rate with an explicit kill criterion (static title fallback) rather than shipped on hope.
- **The atomic presentation swap is real risk** — four live render sites plus persisted history records. Carried as a named non-additive move with its own slice, a legacy fallback map, and a fixture test; the alternative (dual-render both vocabularies) was rejected as the exact split-brain the review's Eng M1 warns about.
- **The dormant-thread predicate divergence** (orchestrator finding on this ticket, 2026-08-27): "is the player watching this agent?" had two answers — `courtPosition` for every attention predicate, bare edge-existence for `isFollowedAgent` — and the player action `thread.dormant` makes them disagree the moment doc 5 ships a consumer. Resolved as **option 1** (court-position-aware default-follow): it makes the action's own authored description true, it matches the ruling's default-follow population ("The First and retinue") which is a court-position set, and it retires the redundancy instead of commenting around it. Option 2 (dormant silences tugs but a life's work still interrupts) was a legitimate story position but loses on the one-gesture principle: the player's only "stop surfacing this person" verb must not be channel-inverted. A dormant or watched agent remains explicitly followable, which preserves the "follow any agent" north star without the accident.
- **Two vocabulary families now tier "how loudly does the game tell you"** (receipts `modal|toast`, moments `interrupt|badge|none`). Reconciled by scope, not unification: receipts answer *the god's own act*, moments answer *a watched mortal's act*. A future unification is possible but is not this doc's call; the reconciliation note in the substrate table keeps it discoverable.

## Vision premises leaned on

North star ("follow any agent's story and see a life, not a stat sheet") — the follow affordance is its first mechanical instrument. God-not-protagonist — attention is conferred, never control; the moment card's action slot offers Inspire/Sabotage (nudges), never a steering wheel. Prose-never-numbers — every new surface renders banded words; the one numeral class (none here) stays in traces.
