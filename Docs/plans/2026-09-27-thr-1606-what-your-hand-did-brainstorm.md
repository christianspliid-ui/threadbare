# What your hand did — brainstorm companion

The alternatives, tensions and Vision premises behind the consequence-and-readability plan (THR-1606, THR-1607).

## Vision premises invoked

- **North star:** "the intervention has to feel consequential in both directions." Today it is consequential in neither: the two starter casts write an empty payload.
- **Non-negotiable §3:** "all mechanics surface through prose, never numbers." Kept. The single numeral is the essence balance, under the exception Law 13 ratified for that counter on 2026-08-06.
- **Design tension (`03-design-tensions.md`):** essence costs "probably need to be more legible than we currently surface." That is this plan, answered inside the tension's own terms.
- **Taste profile:** "Foundation spheres are elder magic, discovered, not selected." This motivates folding them, not deleting them.

## The discovery that reshaped the plan

The ticket framed THR-1606 as a *visibility* problem ("after a cast the player never sees what changed"). Tracing it showed that for the two casts every tester used, **nothing changed**. The influence entry carries no payload the overlay reads. So a pure visibility fix would have been Law 56's forbidden move: a chip that names a change nothing wrote. The plan therefore opens with B2 (give the verbs real effects) and makes B1 soft-blocked on it.

## Alternatives considered

### What the two casts do (B2)

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| New effect types (`dream_vision`, `compulsion`) | Rejected: the influence pipeline is live end to end; two verbs just never fed it (NFP #6, and the substrate rule) |
| Dream plants an encounter seed (a prophetic dream that matures into a scene) | Attractive and very on-theme, but a bigger authoring load (seed content per reach) and a delayed consequence the round-2 tester may not connect to the cast. Recorded for THR-1644's ceremony work or a later "dreams" pass |
| **Dream deepens the mortal's own lean; compulsion pushes toward the god's named pole** | **Chosen:** immediate, visible on the sheet, and it reads true to each verb ("they dream of who they are" / "they are pushed"), with no god-side pole picker to design |
| Let the player pick the pole at cast time | Rejected for now: it adds a picker to a card that should be one click in the first ten minutes |

### Witness (B3)

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Stop offering delivery beats until playback exists | Honest but loses the most cinematic beat in the pool |
| **Build playback on the existing veil and debug spawn path** | **Chosen:** the machinery exists; the gap is one non-debug function and one skip |
| Keep today's silent aftermath | Rejected: it writes untrue consequences against the god (THR-1526's class) |

### Numbers (B4)

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Numbers and tooltips on everything (the veteran's literal ask) | Rejected: breaks Law 13 and non-negotiable §3 everywhere except the ratified pool exception |
| Words only, as today | Rejected: the bars did not move, so the words described nothing |
| **Numeral on the pool balance only; moving bars; pips and words elsewhere, each with a registry tooltip** | **Chosen:** it answers "what is my essence" and "what does doomed mean" (the ticket's two fixed-when tests) inside existing law |

### Forecast words on casts

- **Rename the tiers for casts.** Rejected: the forecast tier is a seated UL term (five words, pre-roll), and a second ladder would be a Law 10 split.
- **Hide the forecast on casts.** Rejected: "fated" on a difficulty-0 card is true information.
- **Chosen:** a cast-specific *tooltip* that says the word measures how cleanly the cast lands. The cast floor is a real rule, and the tooltip tells the truth about it.

## Tensions surfaced

1. **The veteran's "put a number on everything"** vs the prose-first Vision. Resolved by the existing Law 13 exception and by making every word explain itself. Recorded so a future session does not reopen it from the same playtest quote.
2. **Folding Foundation spheres** hides essence a god may hold. The fold opens automatically for a god whose identity holds one, and it is a disclosure, not a deletion.
3. **Compulsion's pole table** may not exist in data. The plan names the fallback (both verbs use "own lean") rather than inventing a table inside an execution slice.
