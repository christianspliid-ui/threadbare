# Brainstorm — the god rail and the top bar

## The tension

The art-led pass wants pictures everywhere. The rail and the top bar are the HUD, and Law 53 treats the HUD as a budget: anything always on screen must earn its place. The ticket resolves this itself — "dress only, add nothing" — so every option below was tested against one question: does it put a picture beside something already there, or does it add a thing?

## Alternatives considered

- **A larger god portrait (R1).** The mock's person page grew its portrait to 15rem. The rail is about 20rem wide and its job is the hand. A bigger face pushes the first card row down at 1080p. Rejected: the 68px haloed face already leads.
- **A bond strip for the god and The First (R4).** It would draw god face, link, First face at the top of the rail. That is a new always-on element, and the same strip already sits on The First's page. Rejected under Law 53.
- **A gold Mandate highlight in the rail (R5).** Slice 2 made the Mandate the god page's one highlight. A second gold line in the rail would compete with the page and with Law 31's one gold emphasis. Rejected.
- **Hook chips as art tiles (R3).** Relic art in the rail would grow every hook row to a picture band. Rejected for the HUD; the god page shows relics as tiles.
- **Near-miss icons for Reaches and Covenants** (for example `dominion` for Reaches, `bond` for Covenants). Each would make one shape mean two ideas (Law 9). Rejected; both go to Christian's pick, as slices 1 and 2 did.
- **Faces replacing the Needs-you row icon.** Cleaner, but the icon is how a row says what kind of thing waits. Kept the icon and put the face after it.
- **Seat line as a page link.** R6 and Law 21 would make the town name open its page. THR-1792 made it centre the map on purpose, because warm testers could not find their seat. Kept the map centring; added only the shape.

## Vision premises

- North star: the mortal has to feel like a person. Faces in the court do that at a glance.
- Non-negotiable #1: you are a god, not a protagonist. The rail still opens on the god's own face.
- Taste profile: prose-first, dark, one gold emphasis. No gold is added.
