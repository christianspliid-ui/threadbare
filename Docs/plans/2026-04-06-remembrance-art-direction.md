# Remembrance Flow — Art Direction Notes

**Date:** 2026-04-06
**Status:** Ready for batch generation
**Total images needed:** 38 unique (4 stirring + 12 origin + 10 drive + 12 hunger)

## Prompt Style Corrections (from user review)

**DO:**
- Start from a sense of **place** and **substance** — something real underneath the magic
- Atmospheric digital painting, refined rendering
- Dark fantasy palette from STYLE.md
- Threadbare magic threads clinging to, running through, or emerging from physical things
- Dissolved edges where the concrete meets the void
- Emotionally grounded — the feeling comes from recognizing something half-lost

**DON'T:**
- Heavy visible brushstrokes / "thick impasto oil paint" — too heavy-handed
- Pure abstract energy patterns with no focal point or grounding
- Starting from abstraction and adding magic — start from substance and dissolve it
- Fractal or mandala patterns floating in void — needs compositional form

## Per-Beat Direction

### Stirring (4 images)
Not pure gradients — evocative landscapes or textures with magic threaded through.
Each should feel like a place or moment you're glimpsing through a veil.

### Origin (12 images)
Real places seen through deep time. The library was the benchmark — recognizable architecture dissolving into atmospheric haze, faint magic at the knowledge-points. Each origin should evoke a specific mortal context: a hillside, a library, a battlefield, a throne room, a clinic, roads, a trading house, a temple, a courtroom, a dreamscape, a barricade, a workshop.

### Drive (10 images)
Real emotions anchored to physical things. The undying love face was perfect — not a portrait but the *feeling* of a face. Each drive should have a physical anchor: a face, a shattered crown, a question mark burned into darkness, a perfect geometric form, a dying culture's last artifact, a horizon, a creation taking shape, broken oath-stones, whispers from shadow, a fist closing on power.

### Hunger (12 images)
Cosmic but with a focal point that reads as *something divine*, not just energy. The convergence of threads should have form — a figure, a throne, a web with a spider, a breaking chain, a consuming void with teeth of light. Each hunger's sphere colors should dominate but be grounded by a recognizable divine silhouette or structure.

## Prompt Template (refined)

```
[Subject — a real place, object, or figure half-dissolved in darkness and magic.
2-3 narrative sentences describing what we almost-see. Physical substance first,
then the magic transforming it.]

[Composition — shot type, focal point, depth. Always have a clear focal point.]

[Magic — sphere colors from STYLE.md, form language, but threaded through the
physical subject, not floating in void. 10-25% coverage.]

Atmospheric dark fantasy digital painting. Refined rendering with subtle texture.
Dark world — environmental colors at 10-30% brightness. Magic threads intensely
bright and concentrated but clinging to surfaces. Deep atmospheric haze.

No text, no UI. No heavy visible brushstrokes.
```
