# Brainstorm — what an away lean should promise

Companion to [the plan](2026-11-07-thr-2189-an-away-lean-waits-for-the-reach.md). Measurements are from `scripts/crossroads-away-census.ts` on [`proto/thr-2189-away-lean-waits`](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/tree/proto/thr-2189-away-lean-waits).

## The diagnosis in one paragraph

The row offers *away* only on the snapshot's leader, and the snapshot already drops the road the mortal just took. So the leader is the mortal's *next* favourite, read off a board the encounter just taken is about to change. At the next decision that road is the mortal's unleaned first choice only about one time in three (`leanTurned` + `resisted` = 209 of 586 decided leans today). The rest of the time the mortal sets it aside without help, and the game says "as you leaned". The lean itself is strong: on the days it is tested it wins 75% of the time. The problem is *when* it is read, not what it does.

## Options weighed

| Option | Measured or argued | Taken? |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Away waits until the mortal reaches for the road** (the unleaned winner is the road) | Every follow is the lean's work (86/86); followed 74% of tested leans; 27% fade | **Yes** |
| B. Keep resolution, compute the away word from a measured base rate (≈ 26% turned) | Honest, but the player still pays for "sets it aside, as you leaned" when the lean did nothing (Law 36, Law 45 on the toast) | No — fixes the forecast, not the toast |
| C. Offer away only when the mortal is likely to repeat (a running work, a journey goal) | Works are the *worst* case: 1 / 27 turned today, 0 / 7 under A. Journeys are already compelled roads that override leans | No — the measurement points the other way; it became the "no away on a work" gate |
| D. Make away cheaper and say it "keeps a road off the next choice" | Truthful words, but still pays for nothing two times in three, just less | No |
| E. Retire away; leave only toward | Removes a god verb; changes what the lean is for | No — a meaning fork, and A makes away worth keeping |
| F. Under A, keep three forecast words from the snapshot | No signal: likely 69%, may 87%, not_enough 56% of tested leans; by lead bucket 43–67% | No — one conditional word instead |

## The Law 18 tension

Law 18 says a shown chance is computed by the same call as the roll. Today's away word *is* the same arithmetic — on the wrong board. The deciding board does not exist when the player pays. The only honest thing to show is the measured rate of the decision that will resolve it, phrased as conditional. This is recorded in the plan's Vision audit so a veto can name it, and the flag keeps the arithmetic one number away.

## Vision premises touched

- **Influence, not command** (non-negotiable #1, Law 9): unchanged. The lean halves one road's weight; 30 of 116 tested mortals took the road anyway.
- **Legibility vs mystery** (design tension #4): moves toward legibility. The fade line ("never reached for it again") keeps the world's own motion visible.
- **The small purse** (Law 36): a lean that resolves is now always the god's doing; one in four still buys nothing, as a toward lean can, with no refund.

## Would change the call

- Christian saying the away lean should be about *the next choice only*, a quick nudge, not a standing hand. Then option B (base-rate word plus a toast that says "Kael set it aside, as Kael would have") is the fallback.
- A build census where tested away leans follow below 60%: the word becomes "may".
