# Encounter Pipeline: The Debt Arbitration
> Scale: short | Slug: debt-arbitration | Pass: editorial
> Date: 2026-09-30 | Pipeline version: 2.0 (Factory v3 critic, batch expert-everyday-1 slot 1, THR-1678)

**Verdict: PASS WITH REVISIONS.** The design is sound: one decision (take the sure third or
call the arbitration), a real rival, an expert stake stated in money and standing, and a
knowledge payoff on both halves that turns a loss into leverage. Every trigger hit found
below is wording-level and has been fixed inline in `debt-arbitration-revised.md`. No
structural cut, no branch cut, no change of delivery mode.

## 1. Prose quality

Narrator mode holds. Every sentence of the opening is a stated fact, the one named person
per beat holds (`{cast:master}` in the opening and Watcher arm, `{cast:claimant}` in the
arbitration), and the dialogue-free register is plain game-master English. The defects were
in the stake and at the seams, not in the register.

- **P3 stated the procedure but not the price** (Doctrine v2 P3, trigger 25's "state the
  stake plainly"). The opening told the player that an arbitration exists and binds every
  claim, but never said what a creditor who loses it forfeits. Without that, "take the third"
  reads as simply worse, and the fork has no weight on the page.
  `[EDITORIAL REWRITE — applied]`
  > A noble with the largest claim wants the house's last pledge, the deed to its warehouses.
  > By the house's own rule, any creditor may call an arbitration. Once called, it runs to a
  > ruling, and a creditor who loses it forfeits the bill.

  Opening now 15 + 23 + 42 = **80 words** (at budget). Dropping "that binds every claim"
  also removes the opening→spine echo with the Vanguard spine's "read every claim".
- **A run-level contradiction.** Step 0's critical-success afterimage said the deed "alone
  covers every bill in full", and its carryover line repeated it. The Vanguard failure band
  then says "the deed did not stretch to their bill". A mortal who crits step 0 and loses
  the arbitration would read both. `[applied]` The afterimage now says the deed "is worth
  far more than the house admits", and the carryover line reads "They can prove the deed's
  true worth."
- **Vagueness lexicon, outcome field (trigger 15).** Vanguard critical_failure overview:
  "The bill is worth **nothing** now." `[applied]` → "{actor}'s bill is worthless now."
  Reaction intent "Say **nothing** outside the hall" (aftermath, outcome class) →
  "Say no word outside the hall about the noble's loans."
- **Seam echoes, afterimage → overview (trigger 22), on all ten bands** (verbatim or near it
  on six, close paraphrase on four). Every Watcher
  overview reopened on its afterimage's phrase ("a full third in good coin", "the strongroom
  ran dry before their turn", "a note on a house that has stopped paying its notes" —
  verbatim on critical_failure). Vanguard critical_failure repeated its afterimage nearly
  word for word ("ruled against … on every point and read the ruling aloud"); failure and
  success_at_cost reused "ranked the noble's claim first" and "the costs of the arbitration".
  `[applied]` Afterimages keep the step result. Overviews now carry what came of it: the
  books' reveal and the payment (Vanguard), and the house, the deed and the queue (Watcher).
- **Carryover factor lines echoing the afterimage they are keyed on (trigger 22).**
  "the pages the master chose" (step 0 failure afterimage → Vanguard failure line),
  "figures … false" (critical_failure afterimage → both arms' critical_failure lines),
  "covers every bill in full" (critical_success). `[applied]` Vanguard: "They argue from
  half the house's books." · "Their case rests on the master's lies." Watcher: "They expect
  far less coin than they are owed."
- **A card that introduced an unestablished target (Q8).** Stoke The Grievance's effect line
  ended "before its elders", but the elders first appear in the step-1 spine, after step 0's
  hand is dealt. `[applied]` → "They lean toward calling the arbitration." (the arbitration
  is in P3). Invoke The Rule named "their founders' procedure", a fact no prose carries.
  `[applied]` → "their own procedure" (keeps the card name's word "rule" out of its line).

The drafter's flag on "burn in their thoughts": kept. It is a kindled desire stated as the
god's act, the lawful register of the approved exemplar ("Fill them with blazing resolve"),
and it names what moves: the lean.

The drafter's flag on prose rule 7 (the bill): **passes.** The bill, the house and the deed
are scene-local inventions introduced as the reason for arrival and settled inside the
encounter. No graph relationship is asserted. Everything that persists (the favour, the
standing, the intelligence record, the prize) is minted through effects.

## 2. Branch seduction audit

The mortal decides (`courage_prudence`); the player never picks. The god's only levers on
the fork are the two step-0 specials with opposite pole leans.

- **Vanguard, call the arbitration.** Fantasy: kindling a grievance into a gamble against a
  noble. Why a god chooses it: full payment, public standing, and a chance to humble the
  household that emptied the bank. It protects the mortal's claim to be paid what they are
  owed. Seductive on every axis: the richest aftermath, the reveal, the reaction choice.
- **Watcher, collect the third.** Fantasy: counselling prudence and watching a careful
  mortal take the sure coin. Why a god chooses it: no risk to the bill, the master owes a
  favour. It protects what is certain. The arm is thinner (deal-only, no reactions), which is
  honest: this is the cheap legible exit of the Opt-in Complication shape, and its failure
  still costs standing (an expert seen leaving with paper).

Neither arm fails the seduction test. **KEEP 2.**

## 3. Branch count — KEEP 2 (one fork, two arms, each ending the encounter).

## 4. Scale discipline

Short, two beats. The stake is one bill and one town's regard, settled in an afternoon.
Expert difficulty comes from the rival and the numbers, not length. Matches.

## 5. Inspiration anchor honesty

- **hook.puzzle_gauntlet** earns its place: "once called, it runs to a ruling" is the
  founders' procedure that still works, and it is what makes the fork a commitment rather
  than a probe. The rolled `uncanny (own law)` die is read without magic, as the brief
  overrides.
- **hook.unlikely_alliance** survives as the "keep quiet" reaction: the rival becomes a
  debtor. Honest, if small.
- **Not a duplicate of The Counting-House Dispute** (`counting-house-dispute.package.json`).
  There the agent is a hired arbiter judging two houses, on gold → heart, with no rival
  across the table; here the agent is a creditor inside the arbitration, contesting a noble,
  gold → gold, with an opt-out arm. Both end with ledgers read and a `knowledge` chip; that
  thematic neighbour is acceptable for a gold family but worth watching across future gold
  batches.
- **Trigger 21 (card-type composition): differs.** Counting House's specials are untyped
  life / matter / time / mind leans; this encounter's are Kindled Ambition (spirit) +
  Whisper (mind) on step 0 and Stumble (chaos) + Signature (order) on the arbitration, with a
  deal-only Watcher arm. No shared composition.

## 6. Aftermath payoff

Actor-centred and named: the prize, the noble's name, the master's favour, the town's
regard. The strongest beat is the failure band: the books show the noble's household emptied
the bank, and the ruling stands anyway. That is a cool failure that hands the player a
lever.

Four fixes to the chip kit (§ 15):

- **Trigger 34.** The success reputation chip read "{location} will send its hardest money
  questions to {actor}", a promise of world behaviour nothing performs. `reputation_with`
  writes regard. `[applied]` → "The town trusts {actor}'s judgement with money more."
- **Trigger 32 / wiring.** The reputation nouns were written `reputation with {location}`.
  The canonical noun is `reputation with {target}`, anchored `$here`; the town is what the
  prose means here, so it is lawful under the brief's THR-1685 note. Chip details say
  "The town", not `{location}`, which can render a Place rather than the settlement (the
  counting-house precedent). `[applied]`
- **Trigger 33 / 35.** The knowledge chip "{actor} knows who borrowed the bank's money"
  retold the overview's reveal on every band. `[applied]` → "{actor} keeps the house's
  lending record." It states the state (a record held), not the fact the overview told.
- **Trigger 35.** The Watcher favour chip sat under an overview saying the master "would not
  forget it", the same fact twice. The Watcher scar carried the cause "Left with paper"
  under an overview that told it. `[applied]` The overview line is gone; the favour chip now
  carries the one cause the page otherwise lacks ("Spared the house an arbitration"), which
  also resolves the success_at_cost page, where "paid short in clipped coin" beside a bare
  "owes a favour" read as a contradiction. The scar cause is dropped.

## 7. Dilemma energy

Genuine. The sure third against a gamble with a named rival, decided by the mortal's
nerve, with the god leaning from both sides. The reaction pair is a real stance split:
leverage over a powerful debtor (silence as a favour) against public exposure that costs the
noble and pays the mortal only in being the one who told. The divine posture is legible:
a god who kindles grievances, or one who counsels coin.
