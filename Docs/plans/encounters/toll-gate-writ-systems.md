### Later-tense promises (prose rule 7b) and the place-and-time check

There is no `appointment` and none is owed: no sentence binds the mortal to a place by a time. Three sentences were found that overclaim against what the effects actually do, and were fixed in the final file:

1. **"{actor} is wanted there to swear to the reading"** (Seeker critical_success, success, success_at_cost). The only effect behind it is `agent_relocation away ≥4`, a seeded walk to *any* settlement at least four hexes off. Nothing sends the mortal to a county court, and the court is never reached. Changed to **"{actor} is sent for to swear to the reading"**, the editorial's own fallback. It claims a summons, which the departure performs, and names no place the walk is not bound for.
2. **"travelling away from {location} with the family"** (Sentinel PATH chip). The family is a scene-local object; no effect moves it, and the relocation does not pair the mortal with anyone. The chip asserted company the engine does not create. Changed to **"{actor} is travelling away from {location} now."**, the same line as the Seeker chip and the `assize-letter` shape. The Sentinel *overview* ("On the far side, {cast:traveller} told {actor}...") is a past-tense scene event, not a promise, and stands.
3. **"The court will want the maker as well"** (Seeker step-1 narrative). A future-tense claim with no effect behind it. Changed to the present tense, **"The court wants the maker as well."**, which states the charter's current demand and promises nothing.

The remaining forward sentences are backed: "the writ is now {actor}'s to answer for" is a present state of the charter, paid on the failure path by the `reputation_with` debit.

The pairings fit the library shapes (`nudge-card-library.ts`): Whisper "reveals a hidden factor" (the seam in the wax; the maker's door), Compulsion "an urge shaping the next decision" (the scorn removed), Stumble "physics turns against the opposition" (the maker's tremor), Boost (goodwill), Veil "same help, unwitnessed" (the carters' eyes).

### Item 8: composed hands

Composed steps skip the whole-hand checks at authoring (`nudgeHandChecklist.ts:237-259`); sphere spread and the ungated common floor are applied by `dealHand` as *preferences within budget* (`dealHand.ts:273-275, 374-391`), fail-soft. What the audit can verify is the authored half and the supply:

- **Authored spheres per step:** step 0 light + mind, Seeker spirit + chaos, Sentinel life + darkness: two distinct each, none duplicated across a step. `HAND_SPHERE_COVERAGE_MIN = 4` (`nudgeAuthoringConstants.ts:80`) therefore needs two further spheres from the four-card deal; `HAND_COMMON_OPTIONS_MIN = 1` (`:86`) is met by the dealer's forced "one ungated common card" pass.
- **Hand size:** 2 specials + deal 4 = 6, within `NUDGE_HAND_MAX`; `DEAL_MAX_AUTHORED_SPECIALS` respected.
- **Supply after type de-duplication** (the dealer skips a type an authored special already covers). Counting `PLAY_PROFILES` entries whose `contextTags` match the declared tags, minus the covered types: step 0 (`insight`, `craft`; Whisper and Compulsion excluded) leaves Insurance, Cache ×2, Omen, Stumble = 5 tagged cards; Seeker (`insight`, `shadow`; Whisper and Stumble excluded) leaves Veil ×2, Undertow, Compulsion ×2, Omen, Long Game = 7; Sentinel (`social`, `presence`; Boost and Veil excluded) leaves Favor ×2, Compulsion, Whisper, Kindled Ambition ×2, Long Game, Heavy Hand, Insurance, Mercy = 10. Each pool exceeds the fill of 4, and untagged cards backfill. The declared tags are all members of `DealContextTag` (`unifiedAction.ts:1898`).
- The spread actually dealt depends on the god's repertoire, which this audit cannot see. It is owed at `encounter:live-proof`, not here.

Image tags all resolve in `encounter-image-library.ts:628-639` and match their card's sphere: `generic.light` (light), `generic.focus` (mind), `generic.blessing` (spirit), `generic.luck` (chaos), `generic.warmth` (life), `generic.dark` (darkness).
