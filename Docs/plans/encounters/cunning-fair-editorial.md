## 1. Prose Quality

The draft is plain narrator-mode game prose. The widow's grief is kept in the right register: stated as fact ("no luck since her husband was buried", "she dreams of him each night"), never dwelt on. Cunning-folk practice is everyday work at a fair, with no spectacle. Defects found:

- **Dictionary words in the opening.** "asks for cunning-folk" and "The hearth will not draw" each need a second reading for many players (rule zero, trigger 29). A chimney that "will not draw" is trade idiom.
  `[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` openings: *"{actor} is in {location} on fair day when a widow asks for a dream reader."* / *"{actor} comes into {location} on fair day and hears a widow asking for a dream reader."* Spine: *"Her fire smokes, and she dreams of him each night."* The overviews follow: "her fire burns clean again", "Her fire still smokes".
- **"truly" ×3** (vague intensifier) in the spine, the initiation and the description. Replaced with "finds the cause" / "right".
- **P3 named "the town's trade" on a rural spawn.** The envelope includes `rural`, and the write is `reputation_with $here`. Rewritten as *"loses trade in {location}"*, which names the real place and matches the chip.
- **"sells a charm to lay him"**: "lay a ghost" is idiom. Now *"sells a charm to calm him"*. "from the next parish" became "a rival reader", which states the competitor role outright. The subject "the dead man" is kept so that "he" cannot be read as the rival.
- **The special's effect line leaked the answer (gate 8, checklist 8).** Borrow Her Sleep's *"so they read where the dead man is pointing"* used a fact (the pointing hand) that the scene never introduces and that *is* the solution. It is rewritten (see § 8, Q5/Q8).
- Opening word count after the rewrites: 77 urban, 78 rural (was 78/79 in the draft, and 87/86 after the first rewrite pass; trimmed).

Kept: "Whoever reads it wrong in front of the fair loses trade in {location}" is a stake stated as fact and a cost (Doctrine v2). It does not announce the ladder (trigger 25).
