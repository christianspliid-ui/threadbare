# Encounter Pipeline: The Inheritance Wake
> Scale: medium | Slug: inheritance-wake | Pass: editorial
> Date: 2026-10-01 | Pipeline version: 3.0 (Factory) | Batch: expert-everyday-3, slot 2 (THR-1680)

**Verdict: PASS WITH REVISIONS** (every edit applied in `inheritance-wake-revised.md`). Critic loop 1.

The design is sound and matches the brief's slot-2 row exactly: id, heart 0.60 → fork (heart 0.66 · gold 0.62), `rural`, `relationship` + `membership` wired on both arms. The fork sits on Heart's own axis, `loyalty_ambition`, which no shipped fork uses yet, and the scene is literally a question of loyalty: keep faith with the widow who asked, or set her wish aside for a deed. The god's step-0 lean has a price, because leaning toward the deed puts an expert peacemaker onto an off-reach Gold test.

The draft carried eight real defects. All are local; none is structural.

1. **A false premise in three overviews (path truth).** The design block says *why here* is `chance`: the agent comes to the farm and the widow asks. The Sworn failure overview said "The widow sent for a peacemaker", and both critical_failure overviews said "{actor} was sent for to keep the peace". Nobody sent for anyone. [REWRITE] "was asked to keep the peace".
2. **Trigger 34, a promise the engine never performs.** Both Renegade success overviews said the eldest "pays the other two their shares from every harvest". That binds later world behaviour (a payment each harvest) with no enacting effect. [REWRITE] The overview states the deed's content as a present fact: "The deed gives the eldest the whole farm and gives the other two a written share of its harvests."
3. **The Sworn narrative stated the goal as done.** "The farm stays whole under her while she lives, and all three children work it together", followed by "now refuses", reads as a contradiction on the first pass (trigger 29). [REWRITE] "{actor} backs the widow's wish: keep the farm whole under her, and have all three children work it."
4. **An ungrounded card (gate Q8).** Dim Old Grudges acts on "old slights", but no prose put a grudge on stage before the Renegade hand. [REWRITE] The Renegade narrative now says the youngest "has old grudges against the eldest".
5. **Scene prose on card faces (trigger 16).** Stoke Their Pride said "end the quarrel"; Weigh The Harvest said "each field's yield"; Dim Old Grudges said "each child's memory". Each read only in this encounter. [REWRITE] The faces are now generic: "settles things", "each sum", "each person's memory". Weigh The Harvest is renamed **Weigh Every Share** so the name does not carry the scene's crop either. Its effect line uses no word from the new name.
6. **Outcome-class vagueness (trigger 15).** The base overview "the land is settled one way or another" carries `way`, which is banned in the outcome class. [REWRITE] "The farmer's wake is over."
7. **One annotation clause.** "The widow did not get her wish, but every neighbour at the wake calls the split fair" matches `notButClause`. The encounter's budget is one, and this line does not need it. [REWRITE] Split into two sentences.
8. **Page repetition and a page conflict (trigger 35).**
   - Renegade critical_success: the afterimage says "{cast:youngest} signed first", and the Dim Old Grudges critical_success fragment said the same thing on the same band. [REWRITE] The fragment now reads "and the signing went quickly".
   - Renegade failure: the overview said "has neither the split nor the widow's trust", and the BOND loss chip said "{cast:widow} trusts {actor} less for setting her wish aside". That tells one fact twice. [REWRITE] The overview carries the expert cost ("set the widow's wish aside for a deed, and came back without one"); the chip carries only the state ("{cast:widow} trusts {actor} less.").
   - Sworn success_at_cost: "{cast:eldest} … has not forgiven the others for the night" sat over a BOND chip saying the eldest trusts {actor}. It is not a strict contradiction, but it reads as one. [REWRITE] "{cast:eldest} agreed only at dawn, and the whole wake heard the hard words first."
   - Sworn critical_success: "Everyone at the wake saw {actor} hold that family together" over a BOON chip "The village thinks well of {actor}'s work at the wake" paraphrases itself. [REWRITE] The chip reads "The village thinks better of {actor}."

Plus one missing support the package critic would have folded:

9. **The membership chip had no prose behind it.** Neither arm's overview mentioned the parish, so `a membership · the Temple of the Spheres` would have arrived from nowhere. A causeClause cannot carry it alone at fifteen words. [REWRITE] Sworn success overviews: "{cast:widow} has had {actor}'s name put on the parish roll." Renegade success overviews: "The parish priest witnessed the deed and put {actor}'s name on the parish roll." The chip drops its causeClause and states the state: "{actor} is a member of the Temple of the Spheres." No four-word run is shared with the overview.

## 1. Prose Quality

**Opening (P1 13 + spine 61 = 74).** The skeleton holds: arrival with `{location}`, the situation with its cost already paid (a death, a quarrel already started), and one stake of the `unmitigated_risk` shape ("If it comes to blows tonight, the family will not mend."). "who can calm a room" reads the draw (an expert Heart mortal), not history. That is the toll-gate-writ precedent ("who has a trained eye"). It is kept.

**The expert cost is now stated plainly** in both failure overviews, as the brief requires: "{actor} was asked because they can calm a room, and this room did not calm." (Sworn) and "{actor} set the widow's wish aside for a deed, and came back without one." (Renegade). Each says why failure costs more for someone this good.

**Step 0.** The afterimages are clean and test the reach (did the room calm). The critical_failure line ("The quarrel came to blows beside the coffin") makes the P3 stake land literally.

**Cards (post-fix).**
- Remember The Dead (time, lean +): "Fill the room with old memories of the one who died, so tempers cool. Grief for one shared loss pulls a family together." This states a mechanism and a lean. It is generic to any death scene.
- Stoke Their Pride (energy, lean −): "Fire up a wish to be the one who settles things, so they speak over the noise. Wanting credit, they care less for old wishes." Wanting credit is the ambition pole, and the lean follows from it.
- Soften A Hard Heart (life), Call The Promise (order), Weigh Every Share (matter), Dim Old Grudges (darkness). Every name opens on a lexicon verb (`soften`, `call`, `weigh`, `dim`, `remember`, `stoke`). No effect line repeats a content word from its name.

**Factor lines.** "Being Loyal, they know what a family owes its dead." / "Being Disloyal, they do not much care whose side wins." Both are trait-derived (trigger 23 passes) and ≤12 words.

## 2. Branch Seduction Audit

- **Sworn (keep the widow's terms).** *Fantasy:* filling a barn with the dead man's stories, then softening a hard heir and making an old promise weigh. *Why a god picks it:* a family kept whole, and a widow's gratitude carried to the parish. *Cost:* the eldest gives up custom's whole farm. A failure leaves the eldest blaming the mortal by name.
- **Renegade (a fair written split).** *Fantasy:* stoking a stranger's pride until they take charge, then setting the sums plain and dulling old grudges. *Why a god picks it:* the fair outcome by any outsider's measure, and the youngest's trust. *Cost:* the widow's wish is overruled, on an off-reach Gold test. A failure costs the widow's trust, which is the one person who asked.
- Asymmetry on four axes: reach (Heart against Gold), whose bond (eldest against youngest), who is let down on a loss (eldest against widow), and what it means (loyalty against fairness). Neither is plainly right. **KEEP 2.**

## 6. Aftermath Payoff

Actor-centred on both arms. A win leaves a bond with one named child, a place on the parish roll and the village's regard. A loss leaves the village thinking less of a peacemaker and one named person (the eldest, or the widow) trusting the mortal less. The wake ends warm on its success side, as the brief asks: the farmer is buried with the family at the grave.
