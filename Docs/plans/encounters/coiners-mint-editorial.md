## 1. Prose Quality

**Opening.** It is in narrator mode and follows the skeleton. P1 is the summons. P2 states the fact (light coin with the mint's mark, no one caught). P3 lands one stake shape, `unmitigated_risk`: if the assayer finds light coin, the mint shuts and its master hangs. The draft's count of 77 is right. There is no interior sensation, no camera work and no encoded fact. The investigation reveal (the apprentice, the strongroom, the furnace) is kept out of the opening, which is correct for this shape. P2 hints that the culprit is an insider ("the mint's own mark"), but that is what the mint-master would know, not a clue the investigation owns.

Weak spots, quoted:

- **Stir A Banked Fire acts on a hearth the prose never establishes (gate 8).** Neither the opening nor the step-0 spine mentions a furnace or a hearth.
  `[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` P2 sentence 2: *"No worker at the furnaces has been caught at it."* (+3 words; opening now 79/80.)
- **The Warm opposition rests on a fact the prose never states.** The Warm factor line reads *"slow to hand anyone to the hangman"*. The prose says the *mint-master* hangs if the coin is light. It never says the *coiner* hangs if caught. Without that, the drag on a Warm mortal (and the Harden A Kind Heart card) has no visible cause, and the dilemma loses its edge.
  `[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` The step-1 spine now states it: *"Whoever struck the false coin will hang for it."* That one sentence carries the own-trait opposition. It also makes the mercy-or-justice reaction a real choice.
- **Step-1 spine echoes the step-0 afterimages (trigger 22).** Success afterimage *"struck at night in the mint's strongroom, with dies hidden behind the furnace"* runs straight into the spine's *"The coiner works at night in the mint's strongroom, with a second pair of dies hidden there."* That is the same facts in the same sentence shape.
  `[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` The spine no longer names the room. It states the task and the stake: *"The assayer is due, and the coiner's dies are still inside the mint. {actor} must take them out without a hue and cry. Whoever struck the false coin will hang for it."* The afterimages carry the strongroom.
- **The spine contradicts step 0's critical failure.** The step-0 critical_failure afterimage *"hid the dies where no one could find them"* is followed (step 0 is `continue_weakened`) by a spine that sends {actor} to take those dies.
  `[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` *"They asked too openly. The coiner, the mint-master's own apprentice, heard every question."* That reads correctly whether the step ends the action or carries on. The carryover *"The coiner has been warned"* does the rest.
- **Hold The Door Shut's target is not grounded.** The spine named "the mint door", and the fragments rattle "the strongroom door".
  `[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` *"The night watch passes the mint every hour and tries its doors."*
- **"The apprentice" before introduction (narrator's checklist Q8).** Step-1 afterimages say *"the apprentice"*. On the step-0 success, success_at_cost and failure paths the apprentice has never been mentioned. Every step-1 afterimage now introduces them as *"the mint-master's apprentice"* on first mention. The critical_success afterimage works both ways: it confirms on the step-0 crit path and reveals on the others.
- **"They found it."** (step-0 critical_success afterimage) has a pronoun with no referent. Rewritten to state the find.

## 2. Branch Seduction Audit

Linear, so no fork exists and none is offered to the player. The seduction is in the hand.

- **Loosen A Tongue** (Whisper, mind, 2). It works through people: a porter says too much. Its failure shows the god's hand turning back on the mortal, as the porter repeats the questions.
- **Stir A Banked Fire** (Signature, energy, 2). It works through a sign: smoke where no fire should be. It answers a different question from the Whisper (a thing seen versus a thing said), and both point at the step's one question.
- **Hold The Door Shut** (Heavy Hand, force, 0 + detection). This is the batch's one Heavy Hand, as the brief allows. A god short on essence pays with being seen. The price is real and on one channel.
- **Harden A Kind Heart** (Trait card, Warm only, 0). It plays against the mortal's own nature. It is the encounter's opposition made playable, and it is the strongest card in the packet. It only reads once the spine says the coiner hangs (fixed above).

## 6b. Page read (THR-1474)

Pages assembled as overview → chips (scar · bond · boon · path) → reactions.

**critical_success (draft)**
> The assayer weighed the mint's coin and found it true. {cast:mintmaster} told the town council that {actor} saved the mint, and did not say from what.
> - BOND · reputation with {location}: {location} thinks well of {actor} now.
> - BOON · The Coiner's Dies: Taken from the coiner's anvil — The Coiner's Dies are in {actor}'s possessions now.
> > Keep the apprentice's name quiet / Name the apprentice to the council

- Repetition: none. "did not say from what" sets up the reaction rather than repeating it.
- Conflict: the boon cause *"Taken from the coiner's anvil"* is true only on the critical path. The table reuses the chips on success and success_at_cost, where the afterimages do not put the dies on an anvil. It is now *"Carried out of the strongroom"*, which is true on every success band.
- Noun: `reputation with {location}`. The `stateNoun` field does not enrich placeholders, so this ships as literal braces. The corpus form is `reputation with {target}` anchored to the counterparty (`$here`, `visualKind: 'location'`).

**success (draft)**: same chips. Clean after the boon cause fix.

**success_at_cost (draft)**
> The assayer found the coin true, but lamps were lit along the street that night. {location} talks of a thief at the mint, and {cast:mintmaster} still vouches for {actor}.
> - BOND · reputation: {location} thinks well of {actor} now.

- **Conflict (trigger 35).** The overview says the town talks of a thief at the mint. The chip says the town thinks well of {actor}. A reader cannot square these. The write is the same +0.05 (net +0.02 when step 0 failed). The fix is to give the cause to the chip: *"Vouched for by {cast:mintmaster} — {location} thinks a little better of {actor}."* Then cut "still vouches" from the overview so it is not told twice.
- **Seam echo (trigger 22).** *"lamps were lit along the street"* repeats the step-1 SAC afterimage *"lamps were lit along it"*. Cut from the overview.

**failure (draft)**
> The assayer found light coin in the mint's chests. The mint is shut, and {cast:mintmaster} is held for the crown's court. The town that sent for {actor} will not send again.
> - BOND · reputation: {location} thinks less of {actor} now.
> > Swear for the mint-master at court — "…the mint-master knew nothing…" / Name the apprentice to the court

- **Repetition (trigger 35).** "will not send again" and "thinks less of {actor}" tell one fact. The first is also a 7b promise (trigger 34). Rewritten as above.
- *"knew nothing"* in a reaction intent becomes *"did not know"*. Reaction intents are not in the spec's outcome field list, but this is the same indefinite the lexicon targets after the roll.

**critical_failure (draft)**
> The assayer found light coin, and the mint is shut. {location} has heard that {actor} was poking around the mint at night, and now calls them the coiner.
> - BOND · reputation: Named as the coiner — {location} thinks less of {actor} now.

- **Repetition (trigger 35).** The overview's *"now calls them the coiner"* and the chip's cause *"Named as the coiner"* are the same fact. The chip cause is cut.
- **Path conflict.** *"poking around the mint at night"* is false on the path where step 0's critical failure ends the action ("asked too openly", by day). It is rewritten path-neutral, and it carries the brief's "why it costs more for someone this good": *"{location} sent for {actor} as a master, and now calls them the coiner."*

**Every band (revised)** reads clean as one text: no fact told twice, no block contradicting another, and every chip sentence of 15 words or fewer.
