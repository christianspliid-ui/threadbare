## What every encounter gets (same contract as Batch 1)

1. **The full Composition Contract** — no exemptions (ruling 3). The census fact driving
   this batch: the WS5 set has **near-zero system touch** — no typed `kind:` effects at
   all, only bare step `reputationDelta`s. No items, no traits, no conditions, no seeds,
   no faction standing. The contract's systems quota (≥3) and persistent-consequence floor
   do the heavy lifting here.
2. **The prose re-register** — the THR-974 three moves, calibrated against the 10 rewrites
   in that ticket's resolution comment.
3. **Image tags** resolve against the live library.
4. **Concepts** per change (Law 2, THR-1033 rule).
5. **`libraryCardId` set** on every card matching a library member (corpus-wide the field
   has zero writers — see the border-perils brief's census).
