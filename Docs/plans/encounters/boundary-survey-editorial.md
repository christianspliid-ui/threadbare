## 1. Prose Quality

**Opening (P1 + step-0 spine).** Good. It follows the skeleton: arrival, costs already paid (the stones moved, the common is smaller), and one mystery with its rule, its opposition and its cost. Every fact is stated plainly. The recount is honest: 10 + 22 + 46 = **78**. No change.

**Step-1 spine.** Two defects:

- **The card depends on a fact the prose never states** (trigger 29, gate 8). Call Up The Oath says "so the reeve shows where the oak truly stood". That only makes sense if the reeve's first answer was wrong. The spine only says the reeve "shows {actor} where it stood". So the player has to infer a lie from a card face, and the Oath's success fragment ("walked back and pointed again") pays off a set-up that never happened. **[EDITORIAL REWRITE]** "{cast:reeve}, who leads the beating of the bounds each year and swears to them, shows {actor} where it stood, but a line from there misses the cross." This states the problem the step tests. It is a clue, not the answer: *who* moved the stones and *why* stay behind the roll.
- **"who swears to the bounds for the village every year"** leaves the custom unexplained, and it does not connect to "the beating of the bounds" two sentences later. The rewrite above links them. The last sentence becomes "at this year's beating of the bounds, in three days", so the reader sees one event, not two.
- **"watch anyone who walks it"**: "walk" is now used three times in the spine and the hand. **[EDITORIAL REWRITE]** "watch anyone who crosses it".

**Afterimages.**

- Step-1 failure: "…and no line was sworn." The failure SCAR's cause "No line sworn" then repeats it (§ 6b). **[EDITORIAL REWRITE]** "They could not make the spring and the cross agree on one line."
- Step-1 critical_failure: "They swore a line that gave the lord more of the common than the moved stones had." This makes the two paths into the band disagree (§ 6b, conflict). The step-0 path swears nothing. **[EDITORIAL REWRITE]** "They swore to the moved stones as the true line." Now both paths end with the survey siding with the moved stones, and it is still a different event from failure (no line sworn).
- Step-1 success_at_cost: "…after the lord's men turned them off the land once." The band overview told the same thing again ("the lord's men watched every step"). **[EDITORIAL REWRITE]** "They found the true line a day late, after measuring once from the wrong place." The afterimage now carries the lost day, and the lord's men move to the overview (§ 6b).

**Band fragments.**

- Quicken The Turf critical_success opened "By morning…" right after an afterimage that opens "By noon…". That is a repeated sentence shape across the seam (trigger 22). Success had the same after-the-fact ordering. **[EDITORIAL REWRITE]** CS "Overnight the grass had grown dark green in a ring wherever a stone had been lifted." · S "Overnight the grass had grown darker over every patch that had been dug."
- Call Up The Oath success, "{cast:reeve} walked back and pointed again", is appended after an afterimage in which the line has already been read out and the reeve has confessed. It reads backwards in time. **[EDITORIAL REWRITE]** S "{cast:reeve} had already gone back and shown them the oak's true place." · SAC "{cast:reeve} had shown them the oak's true place, and would not look at the village afterwards." F "…toward the oak's true place" is kept.
- Walk The Old Line critical_failure, "trusted the dream over the charter", is fine against the new afterimage. Critical_success moves to the past perfect for the same ordering reason.
- "truly" (Call Up The Oath effect line and fragments) is a `VAGUE_INTENSIFIERS` warning. Replaced with "the oak's true place".

**Aftermath overviews.** See § 6b. Critical_success and success are clean. Success_at_cost, failure and critical_failure are rewritten.

## 2. Branch Seduction Audit

Linear. There are no branches to seduce between. The seduction lives in the hand, which answers four different questions:

- **Quicken The Turf** (life): the ground tells the truth. This fantasy works on the land, not the people. It is the gentlest card.
- **Loose The Wind** (chaos, revised; see flag 5): the charter is forced into the open. This fantasy works on the opposition.
- **Call Up The Oath** (order): a neighbour is held to his word, and shamed in public for it. This is the card that says something about the god. Its success_at_cost fragment ("would not look at the village afterwards") puts a human price on it.
- **Walk The Old Line** (mind): a dream sent to the surveyor. This fantasy works on the mortal's own mind.

No card dominates. Price and delta track with each other (1/0.10 against 2/0.11–0.12).

## Rulings on the drafter's six flags

1. **`#territorial` on an encounter: lawful. Keep it.** It is seated in `src/data/content-tags.ts` on the `family` axis. `UnifiedActionTemplate.tags` asks authors for the `form` and `family` axes only (`reach`/`sphere` are projected and must not be restated). `contentTags.test.ts` requires seating and non-contradiction of projections, and places no per-kind restriction on family tags. The catalog lists no kind limit. Its bearers go from 3 to 4, with its first encounter bearer. The meaning ("ground held, claimed, or argued over") is exact.
2. **Card-type labels.**
   - **Whisper** (Quicken The Turf) is right: it reveals a hidden factor.
   - **Compulsion** (Walk The Old Line) is right: a dream-sent urge shaping the next decision.
   - **Favor** (Call Up The Oath) is wrong. The library's Favor "creates or calls in a favor owed" and runs on the favors host system. The shipped precedent (`the-garrisons-price.ts`, "Call the Debt") gates on `requiresFavor`. Holding a reeve to his yearly oath calls in no debt owed to the mortal or the god. It is relabelled **Signature (order)**: an order-keyed push whose sphere is the lever.
   - **Stumble** (the step-0 chaos card) was loose. Stumble is "physics turns against the scene's opposition", and the draft's card worked on the steward's nerve. The rewrite (flag 5) makes it a true Stumble.
   - Batch note: the brief caps `card.compulsion.signature.mind` at once across the batch. This is an authored mind Compulsion special, not that library card, but the batch should not stack a second mind Compulsion special on top of it.
3. **The kept sequel reusing bell-tower's `trait.condition.location.festival`: lawful. Keep it.** Location conditions are a shared catalog, and reuse is the point: masons-commission and the-beast-in-the-granary already wear it. A beating of the bounds that ends in a feast is the custom. The kept sequel's `rewardPool` (`#trade`, "the survey fee") had no fiction, because the parent says the assize already paid in kind. An unexplained second payment is a Law-56-inverse. The revised kept sequel names it as the lord's share of the fee, paid when the steward seals the survey.
4. **"the line stays unsettled": replace it.** On its own it is not a rule-31 breach (it is fiction state, not agent-world standing). But it sits in an outcome field next to a trigger-15 hit ("anything"), and it is a durable claim that no effect keeps true. **Revised** failure afterimage: "{cast:steward} left without agreeing to set the stone again, and thinks less of the surveyor who stayed away." This is backed by `bond_change` (−0.1/−0.12) and `reputation_with $cast:steward −0.04`. That is the bell-tower cracked sequel's exact shape. `narrativeTemplates.failure` is revised to match.
5. **"Crack The Certainty" naming the steward and the charter: not a trigger-16 breach. But the card fails trigger 17, and it is replaced.**
   - The spec's own line decides the naming question: *"An authored special still names its targets directly — that is what makes it a special."* The shipped bell tower's "Wake the lodge master's love for the tower" is the precedent.
   - The real defect is mechanism. "Knock the steward off balance, so every mark the charter names gets read out aloud" leaves open whether the knock is physical or mental, and gives no reason why an unsettled steward reads out *more*. The name ("Crack The Certainty") is also a mind verb on a chaos card.
   - **Revised:** `bounds.loose_the_wind`, **Loose The Wind** (Stumble, chaos, 1 essence, Δ 0.10, `generic.luck`): "Send a gust across the common as the steward unrolls the charter, so it blows open and every mark on it is read aloud." Its fragments are rewritten to match. Coverage (SAC/F/CF) is unchanged.
   - The draft's failure fragment ("rolled the charter up") also conflicted with the failure afterimage "had only the charter to go on". The new failure fragment ("the ink at the fold had faded past reading") does not.
6. **Known live-proof false negative: not an editorial matter.** This is the brief's impediment family #1111/#1113/#1118: the default live-proof run reports failure-band runs as missing success-side effects. Pass 3 and the live proof must take evidence from a seed sweep plus pinned bands (`?spawn=encounter.town.boundary_survey&outcome=<band>`), and read `getOutcomePinVerdict()` before trusting a band.
