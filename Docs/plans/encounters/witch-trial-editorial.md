## Verdict

**PASS WITH REVISIONS** (critic loop 1). Two trigger-class defects were found in the package as authored — a rule-34 promise (the assize, and a reaction that moves the mortal out of town) and a trigger-35 repetition (the *Name the miller* reaction re-telling the hidden-mark chip). Both are wording faults, not structure, and both are fixed in place in the package; no second loop is owed. The fixed package is the one judged below.

## 1. Prose quality

The encounter is strong. Narrator mode holds throughout: the opening and step-0 spine state the facts (stocks, pyre, the boy laid out for dead, the lane's belief) and the stake in one plain sentence. The truth (spoiled rye) is behind the step-1 bands, not front-loaded. Band fragments are concrete and every failure is interesting — *Uncover True Spirit* failing to show "a faint mark ... left by years of setting bones with old charms" is the best line in the hand: the god's sight landing truthfully and making things worse.

Weak passages, and the rewrites applied:

- Step 2 spine: "The sickness cares nothing for whom the town blames." restates step 1's "does not care what the town believes", and the next sentence already says it concretely. Jobless sentence (EDG Q2). `[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` cut.
- Step 0 critical_success afterimage: "By noon they were sure." directly after a sentence about the accused — "they" can read as actor + healer. `[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` "By noon {actor} was sure."

## 2. Branch seduction audit / 3. Branch count

Linear, branch count 0 — right for a read → read → rule spine. The mortal's value choice lives in the aftermath reactions (public truth vs discretion; solidarity vs self-preservation). **KEEP 0.**

## 4. Scale discipline

## 5. Inspiration anchor honesty

## 6. Aftermath payoff

The brief asked for the hardest failure in the batch; the author declined to burn the healer on the page because the cast node persists. That is the right call — the house and every remedy in it burned, the healer in chains, is hard and plain.
