## 1. Prose Quality

The design is strong: a fork that a real master has to make, against weather instead of a villain, with the abbot as pressure and not opposition. The prose is mostly in narrator mode, with five defects.

**Opening + spine is over budget.** The draft says about 84 words. A strict count gives 95 (P1 12 + P2 45 + P3 38) before the cast name expands. Over budget is a hard skeleton failure.

> The abbey has finished the stone vault over its new church. The vault still rests on its centering, the timber frame it was built on. The winter rains come next week. Wet timber swells, and a swollen frame can crack a new vault from below. / If the frame comes out this week, the church opens before winter. {cast:abbot}, the abbot, wants it out, and has told the chapter that {actor} answers for the vault. First {actor} must test whether the mortar has set.

`[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` (rural P1 / urban P1 · spine), 75 words (76 with "Anselm Hale" expanded):

> {actor} arrives at the abbey outside {location}, sent for as master builder.
> {actor} arrives at the abbey in {location}, sent for as master builder.
>
> The abbey has finished its new church's stone vault. It still rests on its centering, the timber frame beneath it. The winter rains come next week. Wet timber swells and can crack the vault.
>
> Abbot {cast:abbot} wants the frame out and the church open by winter. The abbot has told the monks {actor} answers for the vault. First {actor} must test the mortar.

This also gives P1 an arrival with a reason. The draft's "sent for by the abbey outside the village" told us the reason but not the role, and the Vanguard failure overview later says "a builder sent for by name" with nothing to anchor it. "Chapter" becomes "the monks" everywhere: it is a plainer word, and a game master would say it.

**Gendered cast wording.** The Vanguard step narrative says "puts **a man** at every wedge". The cast must never be gendered. Rewritten as "puts a mason at every wedge".

**Seam echoes (Q4b).** Read across each paragraph boundary:
- The spine says "can crack a new vault from below". The Watcher narrative says "or the frame will crack the vault from below". The same image and phrase cross the spine→step-1 seam. Rewritten: "or the frame will break the vault."
- The spine says "a swollen frame can crack". The Vanguard narrative says "the vault can crack and fall". The same "can crack" sentence shape appears on both sides of the seam. Rewritten: "the vault will split and fall into the nave."
- The Watcher narrative has "swelling timber" right after the spine's "Wet timber swells". Rewritten: "the rain will soak the frame and push it up against the new stone."
- The Watcher narrative says the frame stays in "tells the chapter … in front of the monks". That is one audience told twice. Collapsed to "tells the monks".

Carryover lines: "Most of the dry week is already gone." and "The rain reached the frame before the work began." "Pointing the crack" (Vanguard success_at_cost) is mason's jargon. ## 2. Branch Seduction Audit

The mortal picks the branch through `courage_prudence`, and the player never does. Both arms are earned.

- **Vanguard (strike now).** A mortal of nerve trusts their own test and their crew, and the abbot is waiting at the door. The god gets one day of even hands and a vault taking its own weight, which is a single, readable moment to nudge (bind the stones, pace the crew). It protects the town's winter in its church. The aftermath has the biggest swings: a fallen vault on the downside, a feast before the first rain on the upside.
- **Watcher (wait for spring).** A prudent mortal will not bet a vault on one dry week, and takes a season of wet nights instead. The god gets endurance against weather: stretch the dry spells, wring the timber. It protects the vault itself. The abbot calling it cowardice gives the arm its social cost, so waiting is not free. The decline arm stays priced in the master band, which is correct.
- Information asymmetry is honest. Step 0's two specials lean opposite poles (hasten the strike / reveal the crack), so the god's step-0 play feeds the fork without picking it.

## 3. Branch Count Assessment

`KEEP 2`. Opt-in Complication with one personality fork is the catalog shape, and a third arm (strike half, wait half) would only split the difference.

## 5. Inspiration Anchor Honesty

Honest. `hook.long_road` drifted into "the season is the road", and that gave the encounter its opposition (time) and the whole Watcher arm. The Great Building anchor gave the abbot's pressure without making the abbot a villain.

The effect line shares no word with the new name. Separately, "Hurry The Season" misnames its own card: the card does not hurry the season, it puts the rain in the mortal's mind and leans them toward the strike. Renamed to **Hasten The Strike**. Card ids are unchanged.

## 6. Aftermath Payoff

The tone row is met. Both arms end warm on every success-side band (the Festival on `$here` plus the abbot's trust). Failure costs the master's name, and nobody dies. In the draft, the overviews did the chips' work: every failure overview said the abbot blamed them, which the BOND chip also says. The rewrite gives the overview the event and the chip the state.

## 6b. Page read (every band, both arms)

The engine constraint applies: every success-side band shares the success chips, and every failure-side band shares the failure chips. Bands differ only in their overview prose.

## 7. Dilemma Energy

There is real tension, and both sides can be defended. Strike now, and the town gets its church while the master risks a fallen vault. Wait, and the vault is safer while the master is called a coward and the church stays shut. Courage and prudence are both shown as competence, which is the right reading for a master. The god's posture shows in which pole it feeds at step 0.
