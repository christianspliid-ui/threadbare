## 1. Prose Quality

**Opening.** The skeleton is right: arrival, then the costs already paid (tremor, failed iron, half fee), then one plea. The facts are stated, not encoded. Four defects:

- **Word count misreported.** The draft claims 72. An honest count of P1 plus the spine is **78** (P1 10, spine 68). That is still inside 80, but the self-audit, § 20 Q4 and the gate all carried the wrong number.
- **"If {actor} fails too, {location} will think less of both."** Two readings are needed to work out that "both" means the lodge and the mortal (trigger 29). The sentence also claims a regard loss for the *lodge* that no effect writes (a Law 56 / rule 31 edge). [EDITORIAL REWRITE] "If {actor} fails as well, {location} will think less of them."
- **One paragraph where the skeleton wants two.** The spine runs P2 (situation) and P3 (the ask) together. Split them.
- **"{cast:councillor} speaks for the council and asks…"** P1 has just said "sent for by the … council". [EDITORIAL REWRITE] "{cast:councillor} of the council asks {actor} to save the tower." This saves two words and one "council".

Revised opening: rural 78, urban 78 (P1 10 + P2 26 + P3 42).

**Step 1 spine.**

- **"the tower leans on them alone"** reads as "the tower leans *against* the props, with nothing else", which is not the fact meant (trigger 29). The fact is that the props are in. [EDITORIAL REWRITE] "By the second day the props are in."
- **Stir Old Pride acts on a hazard the scene never states.** The card says the master "calls out where the lodge set its iron ties". The step never says the ties are a danger to the cut. The Intervention Fantasy knows it ("knows where the iron is and has not said"), but the player is never told. [EDITORIAL REWRITE] Add: "Only the lodge knows where its iron ties sit inside the wall."
- **Stake over-claimed.** "If the new courses fail, the top of the tower comes down" makes plain failure and critical failure the same event (see below). [EDITORIAL REWRITE] "If the new courses give way, the top of the tower can come down." This is the well-sinking fix, applied to the same kind of sentence.

**Afterimages and bands.**

- **Failure and critical failure on step 1 are one event told twice.** Failure: "the bell chamber came down into the cleared street." Critical failure: "The top of the tower came down into the cleared street, bell and all." The bell chamber *is* the top of the tower, so the extreme band has nothing extra to say. [EDITORIAL REWRITE] failure: "The new courses would not take the weight, so the tower was propped again and left leaning." critical_failure: "The new courses gave way, and the top of the tower came down into the cleared street, bell and all."
- **Press The Wall: near_miss and failure say the same thing.** "sat still until the last course, then shifted as it went in" against "held still for the first courses and shifted on the last." [EDITORIAL REWRITE] failure: "The upper tower held still for the first courses, then shifted and cracked the new ones."
- **The arch is revealed twice.** Show The Hidden's success fragment ("lit the curve of a buried arch") renders beside the success afterimage ("a hidden arch inside the wall carries the bell"). The critical version does the same ("the whole buried arch" / "found the hidden arch"). The fragments now show *light reaching inside the wall*, and the afterimages keep the reveal. The critical_success and success_at_cost afterimages also used "the hidden arch" (definite) when nothing had introduced it. They now say "a hidden arch". The text now says "hidden arch" everywhere, never "buried" in some places and "hidden" in others.
- **Show The Hidden failure: "the dust hid the arch from them."** The fragment presumes an arch the band says they never found. [EDITORIAL REWRITE] "The low sun came through the crack, but the dust hung too thick to see inside."
- **Slow The Settling critical_failure echoes its afterimage.** "then opened all at once" is followed by "the crack opened wider". [EDITORIAL REWRITE] "The tower held still for an hour, then settled all at once."
- **Class scenery in outcome fields (trigger 18).** "the whole town can see it" (step 1 success_at_cost afterimage), "the town's stores" (three overviews) and "in front of the whole town" (failure overview) are false on every `rural` run. The kept sequel's "while the town watches" has the same problem. All now read `{location}` or drop the word.

**Aftermath overviews.** Four of five retold the afterimage directly above them. critical_success opened "The tower stands straighter than it has in years" straight after an afterimage ending "straighter than it has in years". success opened "The tower stands straight without its props" after "stood straight without its props". success_at_cost repeated the afterimage's sentence shape ("The tower stood …, but" → "The tower stands, but"). The success_at_cost overview was also **false on one path**: at-cost is reached by a step-0 failure plus a clean step-1 success, and on that path the afterimage says the tower "stood straight", while the overview said "not as straight as the council hoped". Each overview now carries what the ending *led to* (see § 6b).

## 2. Branch Seduction Audit

Linear, no branches. The choice lives in the hand. The two steps offer four distinct interference fantasies:

- **Show The Hidden** (light): make the tower legible, a god of revelation.
- **Slow The Settling** (time): buy the mason a day.
- **Stir Old Pride** (spirit, on the lodge's master): a god who works through a proud man's love for his own work. This is the best card in the encounter. It is the only one that uses the trespasser die, and its success_at_cost fragment (the master walks off) gives it a human shading.
- **Press The Wall** (force): brute help.

No card is weak. Show The Hidden was the one defect: its effect line named "the buried arch inside the wall", which is the answer step 0 exists to find. That spoiled the investigation gate and named a target the prose never established (gate 8). [EDITORIAL REWRITE] "Let the low sun shine through the crack, so they can see what carries the weight inside the wall."
