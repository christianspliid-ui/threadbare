## 1. Prose Quality

**Opening and spine.** The spine is good narrator-mode work. It states the facts and costs plainly (grain worthless, tithe in kind, cartload gone, road shut, blame named) and ends on a clear task. There is one seam echo at the opening→spine boundary. The opening says *"the lord's reeve counts the tithe"* and the next sentence says *"the lord took his tithe in kind"*, so the same lord/tithe image appears twice in two sentences.

`[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` Opening: *"{actor} arrives in {location} on tithe day."* The spine now introduces the lord, the reeve and the tithe once. Budget 7 + 67 = 74.

**Step-1 spines.** Both arms say the grain is in the elder's cellar and that half the houses are short of bread. Neither says the elder took it *for* them. The fork's whole moral weight rests on that link, and the reader has to infer it (trigger 29 territory: a load-bearing fact left encoded).

`[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` *"Half the houses are short of bread, and {cast:elder} took the grain to feed them."* This is applied to both arms.

**Three roads.** The overviews use "road" for three different things: the road out the reeve shut, "the carved road", and "the old roads" of the omen. One critical-success overview reads *"opened the road that morning … calls the carved road a sign that the old roads are opening"*, with three roads in two sentences. The step prose already calls the carved way "the passage". Revised so "road" always means the shut road and "passage" is the carved way on every player surface, including the hidden-mark label and the omen texts.

**Aphorism.** *"A sneak with a name is the easiest thief to believe in"* appears in two failure overviews. Doctrine v2 sets the irony budget to zero unless a character speaks the line, and the sentence adds nothing the band had not already said. Cut.

**Step-0 critical-failure afterimage.** *"The reeve's men caught them half out of the floor and told the whole village."* This contradicts step 1, which (under `continue_weakened`) goes on as though the passage were still secret. `[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` *"The reeve's men saw them go down through the floor, and now the whole watch is waiting."*

**Band fragment.** Lull near_miss: *"one woke at the wrong moment"* is vague after the roll (it names no event, and "the moment" is on the nominalised list). `[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` *"The watchmen dozed, but one woke and walked the barn with a lantern."*

## 2. Branch Seduction Audit

- **Confessor: catch the grain moving.** The fantasy is justice through the proper channel: clear the mortal's name by handing the reeve the real thief. The value protected is honesty and the lord's law. Its cost is the hungry houses and the elder's regard. The draft wrote the elder's bond loss but never said on the page that the village goes hungry. `[EDITORIAL REWRITE]` The Confessor success and critical-success overviews now say *"The hungry houses get none of the grain/of it."*, so the cost of the honest arm is told, not implied.
- **Puppeteer: return the grain unseen.** The fantasy is quiet mercy: undo the theft, shield the elder, keep the secret. The value protected is loyalty to the village over the lord. Its cost is risk, since a failure makes the mortal the thief with no one else named. It is paid off with the loaf and the hidden mark.
- The two arms are balanced. Each success helps a different person, and each failure costs the same name. Neither arm is a trap. KEEP both.
