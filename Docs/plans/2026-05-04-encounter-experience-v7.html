How prose-flexibility plays out

  • Short prose (this scene, 6-8 lines) — leans visible above-the-fold, no scroll needed.
  • Medium prose (15-20 lines) — leans push down ~150px; player scrolls slightly to commit.
  • Long prose (a ritual scene, a court intrigue with multiple speakers) — leans below the fold; the card scrolls within itself; a small "↓ leans below" affordance appears at the bottom of the visible region.
  • Encounter author writes naturally; the layout reflows. No fixed prose budget.
