What v5 commits to

  • Eira gets her own zone. Full left rail, 320 wide × 800 tall. Big portrait, identity, capability profile, items, vows, relationships to each cast member, recent moments that could echo. The protagonist is the centre of gravity of the encounter screen, not just the subject of its prose.
  • The panel is scene-filtered. Not her full character sheet. Only what matters here: items the encounter could touch, vows that could fire, faces she has met, moments adjacent to this place / this cast. Encounter authoring agent does the filtering at scene build time.
  • Capability shown qualitatively. Five-mote bands per sphere with text colour ("a steady arm", "she misses little", "her deepest thread"). No numbers, on-style.
  • Identification telegraphed in her zone. The vow is highlighted in pink with "ACTIVE NOW" — players see the protagonist invoking her established self before they choose. After the dice land, the same vow shows "HELD" — recognition closes the loop.
  • Cast moves to right rail. Compact strips with portrait + disposition + tags + sphere. They're still there but no longer competing with Eira for the player's primary attention.
  • Divine Court removed from encounter UI. It's a world-view concept. The cross-reference back to other threaded agents lives on the world view, not in the encounter scene.
  • Frame 2: registration happens IN her panel. New items materialise into her items section with gold glow and "+ NEW" badges. Her relationships update ("the trader is no longer a stranger"). Her recent-moments grows by one. Her state shifts from "steady, but reading the room" to "steady · she walked through". The change is visible where she lives, not as a separate notification panel.

What I'm asking your verdict on

  • Eira panel scope. Five sections: identity + state, capability, items+vows, relationships, recent moments. Right slice? Anything missing? Anything overkill?
  • Capability strips. Five-mote bands plus a one-line italic descriptor. Right balance of legibility and qualitative tone?
  • Relationships sub-panel. One line per cast member describing what they are to her ("a debt and a winter ago", "a stranger", "a face she has seen at council"). The right level of grounding? Or should it carry more weight?
  • Recent moments / could-echo. Two visible entries plus "+ 4 more". Useful? Or does it pull attention from the active beat?
  • Frame 2 registration in-panel. The "+ INTELLIGENCE NEW" / "+ FACTION TILT NEW" / "VOW HELD" / updated relationships / new biography moment — all crystallising visibly in Eira's zone. Right level of celebration? Or too gacha?
  • Right rail balance. Cast strips at top (288 high) + Ascendant hand (~520 high). Right ratio, or should cast be smaller and hand bigger (or vice versa)?
