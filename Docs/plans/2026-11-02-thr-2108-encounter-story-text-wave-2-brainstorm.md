# Brainstorm companion — encounter story text, wave 2 (THR-2108 / THR-2128)

Companion to `Docs/plans/2026-11-02-thr-2108-encounter-story-text-wave-2.md`. The alternatives weighed, and why each lost.

## How to slice

- **One slice of 23 (the ticket's literal scope).** Lost: slice 1 did ten rewrites and one completion in one executor run. This is 23 rewrites and ten completions. A run that dies halfway leaves a half-rewritten file on two append-only lists.
- **Three slices (11–20, 21–30, the P5 three).** Lost: the P5 three are 28 encounters met; a slice that small costs a full gate cycle for little. They share 2b's work type (completions), so they ride there.
- **Two slices (chosen).** 2a is light on completion work and carries slice 1's residue fix and the exemplar, so 2b's writer starts calibrated.

## The envelope rule

- **All eight classes everywhere (slice 1's wording).** Lost: it widens restricted templates. Slice 1's executor caught it on `market_day_festival`, and the plan's own kill criterion forbids it.
- **Narrow `locationTypes` to whole classes.** Lost: that shrinks placement, which is also a placement change.
- **Whole classes only, `locationTypes` kept, step-0 lead otherwise (chosen).** Placement provably cannot move: the union only gains subtypes already present.

## The exemplar

- **No board exemplar (slice 1's approach).** Lost: slice 1's critic and writer calibrated on factory-shaped text, and the residue was board-shaped (stake wording, critical-success brag lines). Both THR-2105 calibration suggestions ask for one.
- **Swollen Ford for everything.** Lost: it is a factory encounter with a dealt hand of authored cards; board templates are two-step linear entries.
- **`assess_holdings` (chosen).** It is the one slice-1 template the second cold read found nothing in. Plain, present tense, costs named in the scene.
- **Ask Christian to pick one.** Not needed: `Docs/exemplars.md` is maintained by agents (THR-1250 demoted two rows unattended). The choice is recorded with a veto line.

## Tensions noted

- The four migrated templates (decision 7) read writerly ("where the wall stops being the answer"). They are factory-era text, and the factory-habits ticket THR-2109 owns that voice. Folding them in here would double-own the fix.
- Ten `deal` declarations add a god's hand to encounters that had none. This is THR-1634's completion definition, not a new mechanic, and the hand is a pure fill.
