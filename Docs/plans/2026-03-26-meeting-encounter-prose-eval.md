# Meeting Encounter Prose Eval

**Purpose:** Walk through the complete meeting encounter six times — same base intent, different dilemma choices — to evaluate whether the Founding Gate tags emerge naturally from the story, and whether the emotional arc from meeting to implied Return outcome feels coherent.

**Base intent for all six paths:**
- Primary Reach: **Iron** (warfare, martial prowess)
- Secondary Reach: **Crown** (leadership, authority)
- Sphere: **Force** (cosmic energy of power, dominion, imposition of will)
- Flavor: young woman, dark-haired, tall, sharp-featured

**Candidate generated:** Axiological profile is random. Archetype: "Warlord-Sovereign" (Iron/Crown). The profile varies per path to illustrate how the dilemmas interact with who the character already is.

## Path 1: → Triumphant Return

**Gate tags needed:** `heroic_origin` or `sacrifice_seed + courage_shown`

### Step 1 — Intent

> You reach into the web of fate, searching for a thread of Iron and Crown — a soul with the potential to lead armies and rule. Your gaze falls on a border settlement where the Veil between settled lands and the wilds grows thin.

*Candidate revealed:*

> She is tall and sharp-featured, dark hair pulled back from a face that hasn't yet learned to hide what it feels. Her name is Kaela. She is seventeen, the eldest daughter of a garrison captain. She is courageous — perhaps recklessly so — and she has a stubborn sense of justice that the other children mistake for arrogance. She does not suffer fools. She does not back down.

*[Personality hints: courageous, just, stubborn — pointing toward high Courage, high Justice on axiological profile]*

### Step 2 — Dilemmas

**Dilemma 1 (Axiological — Mercy vs. Cruelty):**

> Raiders from beyond the Veil strike the settlement at dawn. Kaela's father rides out with the garrison. He does not come back. When the survivors drag his body through the gate, Kaela stands over him and does not cry. She is watching the treeline. The raiders left a wounded man behind — a boy, really, not much older than her. He is bleeding in the dirt outside the wall, calling for water.

**Choice A:** *She brings him water.* She kneels in the dirt beside the boy who helped kill her father, and she holds the cup to his lips. Something in her refuses to let suffering stand unanswered, even now. **[mercy reinforced, +0.2 mercy · tags: `heroic_origin`]**

**Choice B:** *She watches him die.* She stands on the wall and watches the light leave him, and she feels nothing but a cold clarity about what the world is. **[mercy reduced, +0.2 cruelty · tags: `ruthless_origin`]**

→ *Player picks A.* Tag earned: **`heroic_origin`**

**Dilemma 2 (Reach — Iron):**

> In the weeks after her father's death, the garrison falls to a new captain — a political appointee from the capital who has never held a sword in anger. He orders the walls sealed. No patrols, no retaliation, no risk. The settlement will simply endure. Kaela watches the raiders grow bolder. They burn a farmstead within sight of the walls.

**Choice A:** *She defies the captain.* She gathers the garrison's younger soldiers — the ones who remember her father — and leads a night raid on the enemy camp. It is brutal, clumsy, and she nearly dies, but they scatter the raiders for a season. **[iron +0.15, traits: `battle-tested` · tags: `courage_shown`]**

**Choice B:** *She endures and learns.* She bites her tongue, watches the captain's failures, and begins to understand how command works — not the sword part, but the politics. Who to flatter. Who to undermine. She will not make her move until she's certain. **[crown +0.1, iron +0.05, traits: `patient_strategist`]**

→ *Player picks A.* Tag earned: **`courage_shown`**

**Dilemma 3 (Domain — Force):**

> One night, alone on the wall, Kaela feels something she cannot name. The air thickens. Her hands tremble, not from cold but from a pressure inside her chest, as if her ribs are a cage for something vast. She sees — or imagines — a ripple in the dark, like heat haze, and for one heartbeat the world bends around her. A fox in the grass below the wall freezes, turns to look at her, and bolts.

**Choice A:** *She reaches for it.* She closes her eyes and pushes back against the pressure, and something inside her answers. A pulse of force cracks the stone beneath her feet. She stares at the fracture, breathing hard, and she smiles. **[force sphere locked, awareness: intuition · tags: `power_awakened`]**

**Choice B:** *She pulls away.* She clamps down on the feeling, walls it off, breathes until her hands stop shaking. Whatever that was, she doesn't trust it. She will master herself before she masters anything else. **[force sphere locked, +0.1 patience · tags: `discipline_seed`]**

→ *Player picks A.*

**Dilemma 4 (General — Graph):**

> Among the wounded from her night raid is a man named Torren — twice her age, a former mercenary who came to the settlement to drink himself to death. He watched her fight. He watched her nearly die. The next morning he is standing outside her door, sober for the first time in months. "You fight like your father," he says. "He was terrible too. I can fix that."

**Choice A:** *She accepts his training.* Torren becomes her arms-master, mentor, and the closest thing to a father she has left. He is brutal, honest, and completely loyal. **[graph: ally bond (mentor), traits: `disciplined_fighter` · tags: `mentor_bond`]**

**Choice B:** *She refuses. She'll learn her own way.* "I don't need fixing," she says, and closes the door. She trains alone, in the dark, inventing a fighting style that is all instinct and fury. **[iron +0.1, traits: `self-taught`, `wild_fighter` · tags: `lone_wolf`]**

→ *Player picks A.*

### Step 3 — The Spark

*The god chooses a trait from the god-given list:*

> **Available traits (filtered by Iron/Crown/Force):**
> - **Commanding Presence** — soldiers follow her instinctively; crowd morale shifts when she speaks
> - **Ironblood** — she recovers from wounds faster than anyone should; pain sharpens her focus
> - **Warcaller** — her battle-cry carries force; those who hear it feel either courage or dread

*Player picks Commanding Presence.*

> Something changes in Kaela the day she stands on the broken wall and calls the settlement to arms. The words that come from her mouth are not a seventeen-year-old's words. They are older, deeper, and they carry a weight that makes the garrison captain take a step back. The crowd listens. The crowd follows. She doesn't know where it came from — this voice, this certainty — but Torren knows. He saw the same thing in her father. It skipped a generation and came back sharper.

*Ambition emerges from the story:*

> She does not speak of revenge. She speaks of the border, and how no one should have to bury their father because a captain was afraid. **Her ambition: Conquer Territory** — she will push the border back and hold it, not because she was told to, but because she watched it fall.

### Step 4 — Confirm

> Kaela stands in the courtyard, Torren at her shoulder, the garrison watching. She is not yet what she will become. But the thread is woven. You feel her — bright, fierce, certain — and you know: this one will not break easily.
>
> **Do you claim her as your First?**

## Path 2: → Reluctant Savior

**Gate tags needed:** `loss_of_innocence` or `duty_over_desire`

### Step 1 — Intent

*Same setup. Same candidate revealed — but the axiological profile rolled differently:*

> Her name is Kaela. She is tall, sharp-featured, dark-haired. She is seventeen, the eldest daughter of a garrison captain. She is gentle in a way that surprises people who see only the height and the jaw — she tends the settlement's herb garden, she sings to her younger sister at night. She did not ask for any of this. She would rather the world left her alone.

*[Personality hints: gentle, reluctant, protective — high Mercy, high Patience, moderate Courage]*

### Step 2 — Dilemmas

**Dilemma 1 (Axiological — Courage vs. Cowardice):**

> The raiders come. Her father rides out. He does not come back. Kaela is the eldest. The settlement looks to her. She can feel their eyes — the soldiers, the families, the children — and every one of those gazes is a weight she did not choose. Her hands shake when she picks up her father's sword. It is too heavy for her.

**Choice A:** *She puts the sword down.* She doesn't want this. She never wanted this. She tells the garrison lieutenant to take command and retreats to the garden where things make sense. But that night, when her sister asks "are we safe?", she cannot answer. **[courage reduced, tags: `reluctance_seed`]**

**Choice B:** *She carries the sword anyway.* Not because she wants to. Not because she's brave. Because her sister is six years old and someone has to stand between her and the dark. The sword is too heavy, and she carries it anyway. **[courage +0.1, mercy unchanged, tags: `duty_over_desire`]**

→ *Player picks B.* Tag earned: **`duty_over_desire`**

**Dilemma 2 (Reach — Iron):**

> The lieutenant is competent but uninspired. He holds the walls. He does not push back. Kaela watches from the parapet as another farmstead burns, and she feels something she's never felt before — not rage, but a sick, heavy responsibility. If she does nothing, more people die. If she acts, she becomes something she doesn't want to be.

**Choice A:** *She leads the counterattack herself, hating every moment.* She vomits after her first kill. Torren — a retired mercenary in the settlement — finds her behind the stables, shaking, and says nothing. He just sits with her until she stops. **[iron +0.15, traits: `reluctant_warrior`, tags: `loss_of_innocence`]**

**Choice B:** *She organises the defence but refuses to fight.* She has a gift for logistics, for seeing where people should be. She puts the right soldiers in the right places and the raiders break against the wall. She never draws the sword. **[crown +0.15, tags: `strategic_mind`]**

→ *Player picks A.* Tag earned: **`loss_of_innocence`**

**Dilemma 3 (Domain — Force):**

> The pressure comes at night. The same sensation — something vast behind her ribs. But where a braver person might reach for it, Kaela flinches. The power feels like the violence she just committed — overwhelming, wrong, too big for a person who wanted to grow herbs.

**Choice A:** *She accepts it reluctantly, as she accepts everything — because someone must.* The force flows through her like grief, and she learns to use it the way she uses the sword: not because she wants to, but because the alternative is worse. **[force sphere locked, tags: `burden_accepted`]**

**Choice B:** *She tries to give it back.* She prays — to whatever god might listen — to take this from her. The pressure doesn't leave, but it quiets. It waits. It will be there when she's ready, whether or not she ever is. **[force sphere locked, awareness: unaware, tags: `power_deferred`]**

→ *Player picks A.*

**Dilemma 4 (General — Graph):**

> Her sister, Maret, is six. Maret draws pictures of their father every day — the same picture, the same pose, smiling in his armour. She gives every picture to Kaela. Kaela has forty-three of them now, folded carefully into a wooden box.

**Choice A:** *Kaela makes Maret a promise: "I will keep you safe. Always."* It is a vow she knows she cannot keep, and it chains her to the settlement more surely than any wall. But Maret stops crying at night. **[graph: bond (protected, sister), tags: `love_of_others`, `anchor_bond`]**

**Choice B:** *Kaela sends Maret away to relatives in the capital.* It breaks them both. But Kaela knows that what she's becoming — sword-carrier, wall-defender, killer — is not safe for a child to be near. **[tags: `sacrifice_seed`, `protector_from_distance`]**

→ *Player picks A.*

### Step 3 — The Spark

*Player picks from god-given traits:*
> - **Burden-Bearer** — she can carry more than anyone thinks possible; exhaustion touches her last
> - **Commanding Presence** — soldiers follow her instinctively
> - **Shield-Ward** — those near her in battle take fewer wounds; her awareness of danger extends beyond herself

*Player picks Shield-Ward.*

> It happens during the next raid. An arrow flies toward Torren's back and Kaela's hand moves before her mind does — not to catch it, but to *push*, and the air between them thickens, and the arrow skids sideways into the mud. She stares at her hand. Torren stares at her. "Don't tell anyone," she whispers. He nods. He doesn't tell anyone.

*Ambition:*

> She does not dream of conquest. She dreams of a wall that nothing can cross, a border that holds, a sister who grows up in a world where no one has to carry a sword they didn't choose. **Her ambition: Conquer Territory** — but not for glory. For the people behind the wall.

### Step 4 — Confirm

> Kaela stands on the wall at dusk, her father's sword leaning against the parapet because it is still too heavy to hold for long. She does not look like a hero. She looks tired, and young, and like she would rather be anywhere else. And she will not leave.
>
> **Do you claim her as your First?**

| `loss_of_innocence` | Reach: led the counterattack, vomited after her first kill |

**Eligible Return outcomes:** Reluctant Savior (loss_of_innocence ✓, duty_over_desire ✓), Bittersweet Sacrifice (sacrifice_seed ✗ but love_of_others is close — design question), Triumphant Return (heroic_origin ✗)

**Primary eligible: Reluctant Savior.** This character never wanted any of it. If the journey pushes her to the breaking point, she'll save everyone anyway — and hate that she had to.

## Path 3: → Loyal Ascension

**Gate tags needed:** `devotion_seed` or `faith_forged`

### Step 1 — Intent

> Her name is Kaela. She has always sensed things other people don't — a hum at the edge of hearing, a warmth in her chest when storms gather. Her father called it "the old blood" and told her to keep it quiet. She kept it quiet. She kept everything quiet. She is devout in a private, fierce way that she shows no one.

*[Personality hints: perceptive, devout, private — high Faith, moderate Courage]*

### Step 2 — Dilemmas

**Dilemma 1 (Axiological — Faith vs. Doubt):**

> The night her father dies, Kaela kneels in the settlement shrine and prays. She prays for hours, until her knees bleed on the stone floor. Nothing answers. The candles burn down. The silence is total. And then — at the very edge of her awareness — something stirs. Not a voice. A presence. A thread of attention, vast and patient, that brushes against her grief and holds it, just for a moment, the way a hand holds a wounded bird.

**Choice A:** *She reaches back.* She has never felt less alone. She doesn't understand what it is, but she offers her grief to it like a gift, and she feels the thread tighten — a connection forming that she knows, somehow, will never fully break. **[faith +0.3, tags: `devotion_seed`, `first_contact`]**

**Choice B:** *She flinches away.* Whatever that was, it's too big, too strange. She scrubs her face, blows out the candles, and goes back to the world of swords and walls. She will not kneel again. **[doubt +0.2, tags: `faith_rejected`]**

→ *Player picks A.* Tag earned: **`devotion_seed`**

**Dilemma 2 (Reach — Iron):**

> The raiders return. This time Kaela fights — not with her father's sword, which is too heavy, but with a spear she made herself. She is not strong, but something guides her — a whisper of timing, a pull at her wrist that tells her *now, step left, thrust*. She kills two men and saves the gate. Afterward she kneels in the mud and thanks whatever spoke through her hands.

**Choice A:** *She begins to fight as an act of prayer.* Every battle is a communion. She does not rage; she listens. The presence guides her strikes, and she becomes something the settlement has never seen: a warrior-priestess who moves like water and hits like stone. **[iron +0.1, faith +0.1, tags: `faith_forged`, traits: `divine_conduit`]**

**Choice B:** *She separates the fighting from the faith.* The presence helped her once; she's grateful. But she will not make violence sacred. She trains with Torren like everyone else, and she prays alone at night, and the two parts of her life do not touch. **[iron +0.15, tags: `discipline_seed`]**

→ *Player picks A.* Tag earned: **`faith_forged`**

**Dilemma 3 (Domain — Force):**

> The force manifests differently for Kaela. Where another might feel pressure, she feels a warmth — a golden thread running from her sternum to some place above and behind the sky. When she pulls on it, the air shimmers, and wounds close, and broken things mend. It is not her power. She knows that with absolute certainty. It is borrowed. It is a gift. And gifts have a source.

**Choice A:** *She asks the source what it wants.* Not aloud — in the silence of the shrine, on bleeding knees, with her eyes closed. She gets no words. But she gets an image: a web of golden threads spreading across the land, connecting people to people to something greater. She understands. **[force sphere locked, awareness: faith, tags: `divine_purpose`]**

**Choice B:** *She uses the gift but doesn't ask questions.* The power heals. That's enough. Why it came, who sent it, what it wants — these are questions for later, or never. **[force sphere locked, awareness: intuition, tags: `pragmatic_faith`]**

→ *Player picks A.*

**Dilemma 4 (General — Graph):**

> A travelling smith arrives at the settlement — a quiet, grizzled woman who says little and works iron like it's clay. She sees Kaela's spear and shakes her head. "That's a stake, not a weapon." Over three days, she reforges it into something beautiful: a glaive with a blade like a crescent moon, the haft wrapped in leather that feels warm to the touch. She refuses payment. "Someone told me to come here," she says. "I don't know who."

**Choice A:** *Kaela accepts the weapon as a sign.* She names it. She carries it like a sacred object. It becomes part of how people know her — the woman with the moon-glaive who fights like she's praying. **[graph: legendary weapon (moon_glaive), tags: `divine_gift`]**

**Choice B:** *Kaela thanks the smith and sets the glaive aside.* She fights with her own spear. She will not be defined by gifts from strangers, no matter how beautiful. **[tags: `self_reliance`]**

→ *Player picks A.*

### Step 3 — The Spark

*Player picks:*
> - **Godsense** — she can feel the thread between her and the divine presence; she knows when she is acting in alignment with its purpose, and when she is drifting

*Player picks Godsense.*

> It begins as a warmth when she does the right thing and a chill when she doesn't. But it deepens. She starts to sense the threads on other people — faint, gossamer, most of them frayed or broken. She is the only one whose thread hums with tension, with life, with attention. She doesn't know the word "First" yet. She doesn't know that she is the brightest point in a web that spans the world. But she knows — with the certainty of the devout — that she is not alone, and that she was made for something.

*Ambition:*

> She prays for guidance and receives, for the first time, something like a word. Not spoken — felt, the way you feel sunlight. *Spread.* She understands. **Her ambition: Spread the Faith** — not through conquest, but through the web of threads. She will find the broken connections and mend them.

### Step 4 — Confirm

> Kaela kneels in the shrine, the moon-glaive across her knees, and she is smiling. She is the most dangerous kind of believer: one who has evidence.
>
> **Do you claim her as your First?**

## Path 4: → Bittersweet Sacrifice

**Gate tags needed:** `sacrifice_seed` or `love_of_others`

### Step 1 — Intent

> Her name is Kaela. She is the eldest of four. Her father trained her to fight because the border demanded it, but she trained herself to cook, to bandage, to sing her siblings through nightmares. She is strong, and she is soft, and she does not see a contradiction.

*[Personality hints: nurturing, strong, selfless — high Mercy, high Loyalty]*

### Step 2 — Dilemmas

**Dilemma 1 (Axiological — Loyalty vs. Betrayal):**

> The raiders take prisoners this time — including Kaela's youngest brother, Davan, who is nine. The garrison captain offers a trade: the prisoners for the settlement's winter grain stores. Without the grain, people will starve before spring. The captain refuses the trade. "We cannot feed the dead," he says.

**Choice A:** *She steals the grain and makes the trade herself.* She breaks into the stores at night, loads a cart, and rides to the raider camp alone. Davan comes home. The settlement starves, but not badly enough to kill — Kaela rations what's left with a precision that borders on cruelty. Half the settlement hates her. Davan is alive. **[loyalty +0.3, tags: `sacrifice_seed`, `love_of_others`]**

**Choice B:** *She obeys the captain.* Davan is not returned. She carries the grief like a stone in her chest, and she never questions authority again — because if she starts, she will never stop, and the settlement needs her steady. **[loyalty -0.1, patience +0.2, tags: `duty_over_desire`, `loss_endured`]**

→ *Player picks A.* Tags earned: **`sacrifice_seed`**, **`love_of_others`**

**Dilemma 2 (Reach — Iron):**

> The raiders retaliate for the grain theft. Kaela fights on the wall with a ferocity that surprises everyone, including herself. But when the fighting ends and she looks at the wounded — raiders and defenders alike — she moves to help them all. Torren grabs her arm. "You can't heal the enemy." She looks at him. "They're bleeding."

**Choice A:** *She heals everyone.* Raider and defender. She earns the hatred of the hardliners and the bewildered respect of the prisoners. One raider, a woman named Ashka, refuses to leave after her wounds heal. She stays. She becomes the first defector. **[mercy +0.1, iron +0.05, graph: ally bond (former enemy), tags: `compassion_in_war`]**

**Choice B:** *She heals only her own.* Torren is right. Compassion has limits, and she already spent half the settlement's food on a rescue mission. She walks past the raider wounded, and she doesn't look down. **[iron +0.1, tags: `hard_choices`]**

→ *Player picks A.*

**Dilemma 3 (Domain — Force):**

> The force manifests as warmth in her hands. She discovers she can push it into other people — not to harm, but to mend. A broken arm knits in hours instead of weeks. A fever breaks at her touch. It costs her. After each healing, she is hollow, shaking, empty. It takes from her body what it gives to theirs.

**Choice A:** *She heals until it nearly kills her.* She collapses after mending a child's shattered leg. Torren finds her grey-faced and barely breathing. "You can't pour from an empty cup," he says. She says: "Watch me." **[force sphere locked, tags: `self_sacrifice`, traits: `healer_at_cost`]**

**Choice B:** *She learns her limits and respects them.* Three healings a day. No more. She makes a list. She prioritises. It is agonising to say "not today" to someone in pain, but she will be useless to everyone if she burns out. **[force sphere locked, +0.1 patience, tags: `discipline_seed`]**

→ *Player picks A.*

**Dilemma 4 (General — Graph):**

> Ashka, the raider defector, tells Kaela about the raider settlements beyond the border. They're not monsters. They're starving. The soil is dead, the game is gone, and they raid because there is nothing else. Ashka's own children are there. Kaela looks at the wall she's been defending and sees it differently: not a shield, but a line drawn between people who eat and people who don't.

**Choice A:** *She proposes a supply run beyond the border.* Not a raid. Not a trade. A gift. It is insane and dangerous and half the settlement calls her a traitor. But she loads a cart with seed grain and medicinal herbs, and she and Ashka ride out together. **[graph: faction bond (raider settlement), tags: `bridge_builder`, `compassion_extended`]**

**Choice B:** *She fortifies the wall and focuses on her own people.* She's already given too much. The raiders have their own problems. **[tags: `protector`, `hard_boundary`]**

→ *Player picks A.*

### Step 3 — The Spark

*Player picks:*
> - **Heartstring** — she feels the pain of those around her; she cannot ignore suffering, and those she bonds with heal faster in her presence

*Player picks Heartstring.*

> She is standing in the raider settlement, surrounded by gaunt faces and crying children, and she can feel all of them — every hunger, every wound, every grief — like threads connected to her ribs. The force flows out of her before she decides to let it. Ashka catches her when she staggers. "Why do you do this?" Ashka asks. Kaela looks at Ashka's children, who are eating the seed grain with their hands. "Because it hurts more not to."

*Ambition:*

> She cannot conquer hunger with a sword. She cannot mend the border with a wall. But she can build something — a bridge, a trade route, a reason for the two sides to stop killing each other. **Her ambition: Dominate Regional Trade** — but trade as a form of peace, not profit. A web of dependency that makes war too expensive.

### Step 4 — Confirm

> Kaela sits by a fire in the raider camp, Ashka beside her, two peoples' children playing together in the dirt. She has given away the settlement's surplus, her own strength, and most of her youth. She looks exhausted and entirely alive.
>
> **Do you claim her as your First?**

| `love_of_others` | Axiological: same choice — prioritised family over settlement |
| `compassion_in_war` | Reach: healed enemies alongside allies |
| `self_sacrifice` | Domain: healed until it nearly killed her |

**Eligible Return outcomes:** Bittersweet Sacrifice (sacrifice_seed ✓, love_of_others ✓). This person gives and gives and gives. The Return will ask: what is left of her when she's given everything? The answer will be beautiful and terrible.

## Path 5: → Betrayal & Fall

**Gate tags needed:** `ruthless_origin` or `vengeance_seed` or `pride_seed`

### Step 1 — Intent

> Her name is Kaela. She is ambitious in a way that the settlement cannot contain. She has always known she was better than this place — smarter, faster, more. Her father saw it and it worried him. He was right to worry.

*[Personality hints: ambitious, proud, impatient — high Pride (Justice→Tyranny axis), low Patience]*

### Step 2 — Dilemmas

**Dilemma 1 (Axiological — Justice vs. Tyranny):**

> Her father dies in the raid. The garrison captain — the political appointee — takes command and immediately begins consolidating power. He hoards food, arms his favourites, and makes it clear that loyalty to him is the price of survival. Kaela watches this and feels not outrage but recognition. She understands him perfectly. Power fills a vacuum. He simply got there first.

**Choice A:** *She takes power from him.* Not through force — through politics. She turns his own favourites against him, one by one, with promises and leverage and a cold intelligence that frightens even her. Within a month, the captain answers to her, though he doesn't know it yet. She is seventeen and she rules the settlement from behind a curtain. **[justice -0.2 (toward tyranny), crown +0.15, tags: `pride_seed`, `political_ascent`]**

**Choice B:** *She allies with him openly.* She offers her sword and her father's name, and in exchange, she wants a seat at the table. It is a partnership of convenience, and she knows exactly how it will end — but not yet. **[crown +0.1, tags: `pragmatic_alliance`]**

→ *Player picks A.* Tag earned: **`pride_seed`**

**Dilemma 2 (Reach — Iron):**

> The raiders come again. Kaela doesn't fight on the wall — she sends the captain and his men. From the watchtower, she observes the battle like a chess game. Two soldiers die because the captain positioned them badly. Kaela noted the weakness an hour before the attack and said nothing. She needed the captain to fail publicly.

### Does the same base intent (Iron/Crown/Force) produce six genuinely different characters?

Yes. The reaches and sphere stay the same, but the axiological profile, the dilemma choices, and the traits create completely different people. The *martial* part is shared — they all fight — but *why* they fight, *who* they are while fighting, and *what they become* diverge dramatically.

### Emotional resonance?

| Path | Emotional hook |
|---|---|
| 1 | You're building a hero. It feels good. |
| 2 | You're forcing someone to carry a burden they didn't choose. It hurts. |
| 3 | You're bonding with your creation. It's intimate. |
| 4 | You're watching someone give too much. You know how this ends. |
| 5 | You're complicit. You're building a monster and you know it. |
| 6 | You're witnessing something transcendent. It's awe. |

### Founding Gates concern

Paths 1 and 2 both use `sacrifice_seed` in the table I proposed earlier — Path 1 for Triumphant Return (via courage_shown) and Path 4 for Bittersweet Sacrifice. But in practice, the tag mapping works: Path 1 earns heroic_origin + courage_shown (no sacrifice_seed), while Path 4 earns sacrifice_seed + love_of_others. **The tags are distinct enough to separate the outcomes.**

The bigger question: should each path lock in **exactly one** primary Return outcome, or leave 2-3 eligible? Currently each path strongly signals one outcome, but a looser gating (2-3 eligible, journey determines which) would give the journey more narrative room.
