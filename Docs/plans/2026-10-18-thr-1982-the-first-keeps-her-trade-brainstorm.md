# Brainstorm companion — The First keeps her trade

## The tension

The meeting is written as a person (a face, an epithet, a short vignette); the chapter engine reads a stat block (Reaches, capability, motivations, traits). Round 4 testers love the meeting and lose the person one chapter later. The question the ticket asked: what identity does The First carry out of the meeting, and how strongly should it steer her chapters?

## Options considered

**A. Use the calling.** The game already has a player-facing identity title, the calling ("Called Mender"). Rejected as the carrier: UL says it is *derived, never stored as a stat*, and recomputes when life changes. Writing the meeting's trade into it would either fight the derivation or be overwritten by it at the first ambition. The calling is the right word for "what the world calls her now"; it is the wrong place for "what she was when you found her". Law 43 (one concept per thing) cuts the other way only if they were one fact; they are two.

**B. Derive the role from the vignette's Reaches.** Cheapest — the 56-role grid gives every reach pair exactly one role. Rejected after measurement: the empath (veil/heart) maps to *bard*, the cartographer (eye/stone) to *clerk*, the martyr (heart/star) to *herald*. That reproduces the recast the ticket is about, with the engine's authority behind it.

**C. Authored trade per vignette (taken).** 24 rows of content, the role the epithet names. It costs a mild mechanical pull: the healer role leans gold/star while the empath's capability is veil/heart, so role affinity nudges her toward chapters she is less skilled at. The forecast window (mortals take 50–65% attempts, THR-1575) already removes the ones she would fail, so the pull is on flavour, not odds. NFP 5 (narrative over mechanical perfection) decides it.

**D. Make role affinity a hard filter.** Rejected: it would remove most of the chapters a First can draw (role affinity is reach-based, and a First's own two Reaches often differ from her trade's pair — the empath's veil/heart against the healer's gold/star). The ticket asked for a floor.

**E. Rewrite the five job-cast chapters as situations** ("the archive she keeps"). Rejected for this pass: those chapters are regional set pieces whose premise *is* the job — "a person who has spent forty years learning to tell a true document from a false one" is the reason the restraint branch works. A generic rewrite loses the scene's engine (Law 40, game before fiction: the job is the structure). A gate keeps the scene intact and true wherever it runs.

**F. Gate the five chapters (taken), fail-open for roleless mortals.** Every worldgen mortal without a role keeps today's draws. Each gate list names a role some First can start with, so none of the five becomes unreachable for The First.

**G. Show the whole epithet on the sheet.** Tempting (it is the exact text the player chose by), but the epithet is a three-clause portrait line written for the picker ("healer who feels too much, cracked hands, kind eyes"), and the sheet's identity block is a column of one-line facts. The trade word is the fact the testers missed; the rest is mood. Held back; a later pass can add the epithet as the sheet's subtitle if round 5 asks for it.

## What would change the call

- Christian: "the trade should change when her life does" → drop the sheet line and fold the trade into the calling's seed instead.
- Christian: "don't lock chapters away from The First" → drop `protagonistRoles` and rewrite the five chapters' prose (option E) instead.
- The ordinary-scene half (the Fellowship's "{name} is given iron stock" class, the round-4 "smithing guild applicant") is not waited on: it is filed now as [THR-1983](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1983). If round 5 still sees a *story chapter* recast, the gate census was too narrow.
- Binder: The First is already castable in other mortals' scenes on main (no threaded exclusion, roleless actors cast mid-band). Excluding her was considered and left out: it changes casting for every run and is a separate call.

## Vision premises touched

- "The story becomes yours" — the player's chosen person persists. Served.
- The god influences, never commands (Law 9) — the trade is who she was, not an order. Untouched.
- Emergence over authorship — the trade weighs her draws; it never scripts them.
