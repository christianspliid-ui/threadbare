How v6 reads

  • Left zone — who she is. Big portrait. Identity. Capability. Items + active vow. Recent moments that could echo. Five clear sections, each with breath.
  • Center zone, top — what is happening. Place painting (left), prose with callback note (right), three lean cards along the bottom of the card.
  • Center zone, bottom — relevant nodes. Three cast tiles in a horizontal strip. Each shows portrait, name, sphere/role, scene-disposition (italic, hover for tooltip), "to Eira:" relationship, and tags. Cast lives here once and only once — no redundancy with the protagonist panel.
  • Right zone, top — your hand. Three compact Ascendant cards, each one row tall: icon + name + one-line description + cost. "+1 NEW" badge floats above the header. "+ N more" disclosure below.
  • Right zone, bottom — state of the scene. Threads in play (legends with weights), factions present (chips), place conditions (text), conditions on Eira (currently "none active"). All slots fill from the encounter graph; empty slots show as dashed placeholders.
  • Bottom bar — pacing & commit. Quintessence orbs (left). Center hint + scene-pacing dots showing beats 1-4. Watch-only on the right. The bar is now load-bearing, not sparse.

How the containers stay elastic

  • Items list: "2 of 5 items relevant here" — count visible; the panel scrolls or the encounter author selects which items surface.
  • Recent moments: 1–N entries, with "+ N more" overflow.
  • Cast tiles: 0–N. Three shown here; in a tavern brawl scene there could be six tiles in two rows; in a solo dilemma encounter, zero (just Eira and the moment). Layout flows.
  • Hand: 1–N playable + dimmed. Three visible by default; the "+ N more" expands.
  • Threads in play: 0–N labelled lines. The encounter author lists what threads are active; the rendering reflows.
  • Factions present: 0–N chips.
  • Conditions on Eira: 0–N (currently "none active"; in a cursed scene, could show wound, exhaustion, doubt, marked-by-rival, etc.).
  • Place conditions: free-text from the place node, varies by location.
