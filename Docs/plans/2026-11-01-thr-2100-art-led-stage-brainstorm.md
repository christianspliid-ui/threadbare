# Brainstorm — the Stage moments

## The tension

The Stage is where a mortal's moment plays, and the prose is the point. The art-led pass wants a face and pictures on every surface. The question for each option: does it put the mortal in front of the player without pushing the prose or the choices down?

## Alternatives considered

- **A face in the context line** ("[face] Maren Ashfold at Millbrook"). Cheapest: one shared component. Rejected: a chip-sized face is the problem R1 names ("at a size that reads").
- **Each surface keeps its own portrait size.** Rejected: three sizes for one face across six moments breaks Law 5's three picture sizes and Law 3's one path.
- **The ruin's landscape on the new-power dilemma.** The ruin is the place, and `resolveEntityVisual` can draw a landscape. Rejected: the ruin is already lit on the map beside the panel, and the moment is about what the delver brought out.
- **Keep the encounter's 28px chip beside the name as well as the left-column face.** Rejected: two faces of one person in one panel.
- **Near-miss icons** (`reveal` for a whisper, `curse` for *Corrupt*, `trust` for *At stake*). Each would make one shape mean two ideas (Law 9). Rejected; they go to Christian's pick, as in slices 1–3.
- **Carved shapes on the moment card's tone tiles** (▲ ▼ ✦). Rejected: those tiles say gain or loss, not a concept, and the chip word already names the concept.
- **A gold highlight (R5) for the encounter's stakes line.** Rejected: the Veil already has its gold, and Law 37 keeps one chrome per flow.

## Vision premises

- North star: the mortal has to feel like a person. A face on every moment does that before a word is read.
- Non-negotiable #3: mechanics through prose. No prose is replaced; pictures sit beside it.
- Taste profile: prose-first, dark, one gold emphasis. No gold is added.
