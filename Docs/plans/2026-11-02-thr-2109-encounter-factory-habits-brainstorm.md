## How to slice

- **One slice (the ticket's literal scope).** Lost: about 117 effect lines, the band prose of seven board templates, and P8 sites across 51 town files is roughly 300 rewritten lines in 60 files. Wave 2's split exists because slice 1's load is about what one build run finishes.
- **Two slices (effect lines + P3, then P8).** Lost: the seven migrated templates' band prose is dense with P3 lines ("The hand stayed steady. The ground under the north side did not."). Together with 117 effect lines in one run, the cold read would be judging two jobs at once.
- **Three slices (chosen).** a is the source fix plus the mechanical sweep, judged by predicates. b is one cold-read job on one file. c is one cold-read job on the town corpus. b and c touch disjoint files.

## Strength tags: trim or rewrite

- **Trim the tag, keep the rest.** Lost: many lines are a tag plus a mood sentence ("Strong help. The metal gives an honest account of itself."). Trimming leaves a mood line, which the communication pivot also bans.
- **Rewrite the whole line as a spell (chosen).** Matches Christian's 2026-08-25 "rewrite from scratch" ruling and the spec's "say what the god does".
