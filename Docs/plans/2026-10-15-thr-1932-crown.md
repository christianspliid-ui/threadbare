# The crown — THR-1932

*Realms win other factions' fealty by protection, coin or force, take a share of what they earn, and lose them when they cannot pay, cannot defend, or are outbid. A realm that holds two or more oaths is a Crown.*

## Why this is load-bearing

Christian set the kingdom economy's order on the [Mortal economy map](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1863): purse → crown → war aims. His words on the crown: *"the crown goes to those who can convince other factions of fealty and tax rights."* [The faction purse](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1931) (plan [`2026-10-15-thr-1931-faction-purse.md`](2026-10-15-thr-1931-faction-purse.md), Ready for Dev, not built) gives every faction coin from its land and makes armies cost coin. Without the crown, that coin only ever buys soldiers: no faction can turn wealth into power over another faction except by conquest, and nobody rules anybody. Today there is **no relationship of fealty in the game at all**. No edge type, property or content effect is named for an oath, vassal, liege or fealty (§ Substrate inventory). The two encounters that are *about* bending factions to a cause (`encounter.faction_unification`, `reputation.iron.warlords_tribute`) change reputation only.

This plan adds one directed edge, `sworn_to` (vassal → liege). Every behaviour hangs off that edge and the purse: how an oath is won, the tax it carries, a daily loyalty pass, the breaks and rebellions, the derived Crown title, the Crown's outline on the map, and the faction-sheet lines.

**How-level choices this plan made** (the decision left them open; reasons in the [brainstorm companion](2026-10-15-thr-1932-crown-brainstorm.md)):

1. Only a realm that holds at least one town courts.
2. `seek_fealty` is a real faction action in the existing action table; the daily loyalty pass is its own small phase after the purse.
3. A vassal pays its own soldiers before the crown's share.
4. A refusal cools feeling a little and makes no declared rival; a break cools it hard.
5. The re-swear cooldown lives on the pair's existing `relates_to` edge.
6. "A town just taken" is read off a new `takenFrom` property on the conquering `controls` edge.
7. Loyalty is shown with the reputation words, and an oath breaks exactly when it falls to *Distrusted*.
8. Player-facing words are **fealty** and **Crown**, never *sworn* (that word is already a rung on the realm court ladder).
9. The crown's outline is drawn only when a *realm* is sworn.
10. No royal charter in v1.

## Engine pillar

### Systems design

New module `src/engine/crown.ts` (the phase and executor) with pure helpers in `src/engine/crown/` (`offer.ts`, `loyalty.ts`, `eligibility.ts`, `crownView.ts`). Constants in `src/data/crown-constants.ts`, taken from the prototype file.

**1. Who may swear, and who may court** (`crown/eligibility.ts`).

- **May swear:** any faction except:
  - those whose `factionType` is in `NEVER_SWEAR_FACTION_TYPES` (`['criminal', 'monster']`; `types/faction.ts:102-108`);
  - those whose `factionDefId` is in `NEVER_SWEAR_DEF_IDS` (`thieves_guild`, `underking_court`);
  - those whose `guildType` is in `NEVER_SWEAR_GUILD_TYPES` (`thieves`);
  - excluded purse factions (`PURSE_EXCLUDED_FACTION_TYPES`).

  This is the decision's list: realms, town guilds, guilds and orders, temples and mercenary companies swear; thieves and monster lairs never do.
- **May court:** a faction whose `factionClass === 'realm'` that holds at least one settlement (`controls`) and has no `sworn_to` edge of its own.
- **One layer (Christian's ruling):**
  - a faction with an outgoing `sworn_to` edge cannot court;
  - a faction with any incoming `sworn_to` edge cannot swear;
  - a faction holds at most one outgoing `sworn_to` at a time.
- **Candidates of a realm R:** every swear-eligible faction, not R, with a hall, holding or `located_at` member inside R's projected hexes or in one of R's towns, plus every realm whose hexes touch R's (hex distance ≤ 1). Built once per pay day from the cached realm projection and cached on the runtime for that day.

**2. Winning an oath — `seek_fealty`** (a faction action; executor `executeSeekFealty`). Template: `treasuryCost 0`, `minMembers 0` (realms lose members, [THR-1918](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1918)), `cooldownTicks` `SEEK_FEALTY_COOLDOWN_TICKS` 12 (superseded by THR-1961: `cooldownTicks` 0, and `can_court` also requires no courtship yet in the calendar day `floor(tick / SEEK_FEALTY_WINDOW_TICKS)`), prerequisite `{ type: 'can_court' }` (new kind in `checkPrerequisite`: §1's court test plus at least one candidate). The weight is `SEEK_FEALTY_WEIGHT`, tuned so a courting realm picks it about once a day (the prototype's rate; § Done when predicate 6). On execute, for realm R:

1. **Coercion first** — the coerce pool is every candidate C, not on cooldown with R, where either:
   - an active siege battle whose attacker army is R's has run `≥ OATH_COERCE_MIN_SIEGE_TICKS` ticks on a settlement C controls; or
   - a `controls` edge from R carries `via:'conquest'`, `takenFrom: C`, and `establishedTick` within `OATH_COERCE_WINDOW_DAYS × TICKS_PER_DAY`.

   If the pool is non-empty, R takes the pool member with the highest day income (ties by id), and C swears **coerced**. No score is needed: Christian ruled force allowed.
2. **Else persuasion or purchase.** For each candidate C not on cooldown with R:
   - **Persuaded** if `offerScore(C, R, giftShare = 0) ≥ OATH_ACCEPT_THRESHOLD`.
   - **Bought** if `offerScore(C, R, giftShare = affordableGift(C, R)) ≥ OATH_ACCEPT_THRESHOLD`.
3. Of all accepting candidates, R takes the one with the highest day income, persuaded before bought on a tie, then by id.
4. A bought oath moves `giftShare × OATH_GIFT_DAYS × income(C)` from R to C through `applyTreasuryDelta`.
5. **No acceptance** → a refusal (§ 6) for the best-scoring candidate only.

**Offer score** (`crown/offer.ts`, pure), the decision's four inputs:

```
protection = threat > 0 ? strength(R) / (strength(R) + threat) : OATH_NO_THREAT_PROTECTION
  threat   = strongest army within OATH_THREAT_RADIUS_HEXES of any of C's holdings or halls,
             not C's, not R's, not a fellow vassal of R's, not C's ally
gift       = giftShare ∈ [0,1]; affordableGift = min(1, max(0, wealth(R) − OATH_GIFT_RESERVE) / (OATH_GIFT_DAYS × income(C)))
standing   = +1 ally (relates_to isAlliance) · −1 rival (isRival) or at war (basis 'war') or hostile_to either way · else 0
pride      = min(OATH_PRIDE_CAP, income(C) / max(0.01, fullIncome(R)))
score      = OATH_PROTECTION_W·protection + OATH_GIFT_W·gift + OATH_STANDING_W·standing − OATH_PRIDE_W·pride
```

`income(X)` = X's own day income from `purseLastDay` (all five sources plus guild tax in, minus guild tax out). `fullIncome(X)` adds X's fealty income in. Army strength reads the existing army strength field the prototype used, summed over X's armies. Every input is deterministic.

**3. What an oath writes.** One `sworn_to` edge C → R:

```ts
interface SwornToEdgeProperties {
  kind: 'persuaded' | 'bought' | 'coerced';
  taxShare: number;          // FEALTY_TAX_SHARE at swearing
  loyalty: number;           // 0–1, LOYALTY_START[kind]
  swornTick: number;
  giftPaid: number;          // 0 unless bought
  lastLoyaltyDay: { tick: number; deltas: { reason: LoyaltyReason; amount: number }[] } | null;
  recentLosses: { tick: number; reason: LoyaltyReason; amount: number }[]; // trimmed to OATH_BREAK_REASON_WINDOW_DAYS each pass
}
type LoyaltyReason = 'liege_wealthy' | 'liege_strained' | 'liege_destitute' | 'holding_lost' | 'outgrown' | 'rival_offer';
```

**4. The tax.** New inline phase **`crown`**, run by the orchestrator directly after `faction_purse` on the same daily gate, in its own try/catch. For each `sworn_to` edge in edge-id order:

- `due = taxShare × max(0, income(C))`;
- `paid = min(due, wealth(C))`, moved C → R through `applyTreasuryDelta`.

The vassal has already paid its soldiers in `faction_purse`, so a lord feeds his own spears before the crown takes its share. Nothing goes below 0, and there is no debt (the purse's rule). `PurseDay` gains `fealtyOut` and `fealtyIn`, written back onto both `purseLastDay` records by this phase.

**The tax holder of a town does not change.** The decision puts fealty *on top of* the 20% landholder cut. So the purse's `taxHolderOf` stays as written. The purse plan's executor note (*"the crown will change who the tax holder is"*) is superseded by this paragraph, and the build PR edits that note.

**5. Loyalty, daily, in the same `crown` phase** (before the tax), per oath:

| Condition (read today) | Change |
|---|---|
| Liege's Means is Wealthy or Magnate | `+LOYALTY_RISE_WEALTHY` (0.01) |
| Liege's Means is Getting by | `−LOYALTY_FALL_STRAINED` (0.005) |
| Liege's Means is Destitute | `−LOYALTY_FALL_DESTITUTE` (0.01) |
| Each settlement C lost since the last pass (a `controls` edge `takenFrom: C`, `establishedTick` > last pass) | `−LOYALTY_FAIL_DEFENCE` (0.15) each |
| `income(C) > fullIncome(R)` | `−LOYALTY_FALL_OUTGROWN` (0.02) |
| Some other court-eligible realm R2 that counts C a candidate has `offerScore(C, R2, 0) > offerScore(C, R, 0) + OATH_RIVAL_MARGIN` | `−LOYALTY_FALL_RIVAL_OFFER` (0.02) |

The decision's working Means names map one-to-one onto the purse's five shown words: Flush/Overflowing → Wealthy/Magnate, Strained → Getting by, Bankrupt → Destitute (purse plan § Resolution 3). Clamp to 0–1, and write `lastLoyaltyDay`.

**6. Breaking, refusing, rebelling.**

- **Break:** `loyalty < OATH_BREAK_THRESHOLD` (0.2), or either end is gone. The edge is removed. The pair's `relates_to` edge (minted if missing, both directions) takes:
  - `sentiment += OATH_BREAK_SENTIMENT_DELTA` (−0.3);
  - `oathBrokenTick = tick`.

  The pair may not swear again while `tick − oathBrokenTick < OATH_RESWEAR_COOLDOWN_DAYS × TICKS_PER_DAY`. The break reason is the largest summed loss family in the edge's `recentLosses` (the last `OATH_BREAK_REASON_WINDOW_DAYS`), kept on `oathBrokenReason`.
- **Rebellion:** a breaking vassal that is a realm and earns `≥ REBELLION_INCOME_RATIO × fullIncome(R)` rebels. It writes `hostile_to` both ways with `{ reason: 'rebellion', sinceTick }`, which [war aims](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1933) rule 1 reads as an enemy.
- **Refusal:** the refused candidate's `relates_to` toward R takes `sentiment += OATH_REFUSAL_SENTIMENT_DELTA` (−0.02), floored at `OATH_REFUSAL_SENTIMENT_FLOOR` (−0.25). That stays above the rivalry threshold of −0.3, so refusals alone never make rivals. The decision's *"rival tie"* on a refusal is read as a cooling, and the hard tie is kept for breaks (brainstorm § 4).

**7. The Crown is derived, never stored.** `isCrown(R)` = R has `≥ CROWN_MIN_OATHS` incoming `sworn_to` edges. `crown/crownView.ts` exports pure helpers:

- `isCrown`;
- `vassalsOf`;
- `liegeOf`;
- `protectedHoldingsOf(R)` — R's own settlements plus every settlement controlled by a faction sworn to R. This is the read [war aims'](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1933) defence-first rule uses.
- `crownHexes(R)` — the union of R's projected hexes and its sworn *realms'* projected hexes.

The ruler is `getFactionLeaderId(R)`.

**8. Rollback.** `CROWN_ENABLED = false` does four things:

- removes `seek_fealty` from selection (the prerequisite returns false);
- skips the `crown` phase;
- stops writing `takenFrom`;
- makes `crownView` return empty.

Existing `sworn_to` edges in a save are ignored by every reader.

### Resolution logic

1. **Coercion over persuasion** — a coercible candidate is taken before any courtship that day (the prototype's order).
2. **Pick among acceptors** — highest day income first; persuaded before bought on a tie; then faction id.
3. **Loyalty before tax** — an oath that breaks today pays no tax today.
4. **Break reason** — the largest summed negative family in `recentLosses` (`OATH_BREAK_REASON_WINDOW_DAYS`); ties in table order.
5. **Means words** — read from the purse's `meansBand`; never recomputed.

### PRNG callouts

None new. `seek_fealty` is chosen by the existing `selectWeighted` draw inside `processFaction` (`phaseFactionActions.ts:815`), so it consumes no new stream. Everything inside the executor and the phase is deterministic.

### Player-facing display

**Faction sheet (`FactionSheet.tsx`):**

1. **Under Leadership — the Crown line**, only when `isCrown`: a **Crown** chip (registry tooltip `ui.crown.title`) and the sentence "*<ruler name> wears the crown; nine factions owe it fealty.*" With no resolvable ruler: "*Nine factions owe it fealty.*" The count is spelled out in words, never a digit. The words *king* and *queen* are never used (a gender guess). *Kingdom* is never used: it is retired on player surfaces (UL Realm).
2. **New Fealty section**, placed after *Relations* (`:474`):
   - **On a vassal:** "*Owes fealty to the Hold of Witness Skyfield for a fortnight, bought with silver.*" ("won by protection" / "bought with silver" / "forced by siege"), then the standing line — the word from `getReputationWord(loyalty)`, then how the oath was won, then one trend clause from `lastLoyaltyDay` naming the largest reason. When the word is *Unknown* (the band just above the break), the line says so in plain words: "*one step from breaking*". Rendered for a forced vassal: "*Its standing with its liege: Unknown, one step from breaking — it swore under siege, and its loyalty is falling because its liege cannot pay its soldiers.*" For a bought vassal: "*Its standing with its liege: Respected — it was bought with silver, and its loyalty is rising because its liege is rich.*" The word *Distrusted* never shows on a live oath: at Distrusted the oath has already broken.
   - **On a liege:** "*Holds the fealty of:*" and a list of up to 8 vassals, each with its standing word. The overflow is "*and three more*" (spelled out), which opens the full list in the same section (no scroll at 1920×1080: the list folds, Law 33).
   - **Neither:** the section is not rendered (Law 56: no empty chrome).
3. **The Purse panel** (purse plan) gains one sentence when `fealtyOut > 0`: "*It pays a share of all it earns to the Hold of Witness Skyfield.*" On a liege with `fealtyIn > 0`, the income sentence may name "*the fealty of its vassals*" as one of its two top sources.

### Visual presence (HexMapV2)

**The crown's outline** (`scene/BorderMesh.ts`): when `crownHexes(R)` includes at least one sworn realm's hexes, a second, outer line is drawn around the union, in R's border ink, at `CROWN_OUTLINE_WIDTH` and `CROWN_OUTLINE_OPACITY`. Realm borders inside it stay as they are. A Crown whose vassals are all guilds draws no outline, because its land is its own border. This is the decision's *"the crown's border is the union of its sworn realms' borders"*. The outline rides the existing border layer, so it shows on the plain map exactly as realm borders do. It adds no lens and no new plain-map signifier ([THR-1830](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1830) grammar). If [realm borders in the holder's ink](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1902) has landed, the outline uses that ink; otherwise it uses the current realm border colour.

**Hex tooltip** on a hex inside a crown outline adds one clause to the realm line: "*Held by the Sovereignty of Stone-Set Hollow, which owes fealty to the Hold of Witness Skyfield, a Crown.*"

## Kill criteria

- If no crown forms by day 45 on any of seeds 42/99/7 with the purse live, retune first (`OATH_ACCEPT_THRESHOLD`, `OATH_GIFT_RESERVE`, `SEEK_FEALTY_WEIGHT`). If no tuning passes, flip `CROWN_ENABLED` off and reopen [the crown decision](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1917) (its *Would change the call*: no crown by day 40).
- If one realm holds more than 75% of all swear-eligible factions by day 90 on two of three seeds, the gift is too cheap. Raise `OATH_GIFT_RESERVE` or `OATH_GIFT_DAYS`; do not cap oaths.
- If oath breaks per 90 days exceed oaths sworn, loyalty falls too fast. Halve the `LOYALTY_FALL_*` constants before anything else.
