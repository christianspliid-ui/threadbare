# Brainstorm companion — Doom's last stage is a stand

Companion to [the plan](2026-11-19-thr-2247-dooms-last-stage-is-a-stand.md). It records the options weighed and the tensions behind each decision.

## The shared premise

The ticket asked three things: what pushes the clock besides time, what the last stage offers the god, and how to measure "something to decide". Each option was tested on two questions. *Does it give the player a move they can see, at the place the threat is?* *Does it stay inside Law 31 (Doom is never bought back) and Law 36 (the purse stays small)?*

## What the measurement changed

The ticket expected "about 145 clock ticks from other pushes, complications write `currentTick` directly". The census confirmed the size (171–206 clock ticks after the warm-up, 23–29% of the movement) and found it is **all** failures: two complications, about 650–740 of them a run. They were spread across 41–57 mortals at 47–74 places, and the First caused 1–2.5%. That ruled out anything that answers one push at a time and pointed at a rule about places.

It also found two things the ticket did not know:
- The Return fires on the exact tick the last stage begins (four runs of four). The ticket treated "after the Return" and "the last stage" as two stretches. They are one.
- The purse rises through the last stage in every played run, and the hands-off run sits at the cap. "A last use for the purse" is not a nice-to-have: today the purse has no use at all there.

## Question 1 — the pushes

1. **Tended ground starves the Doom, and Rebuke reaches a doom-fed place (chosen).** One rule covers every push, and it reuses tending and Rebuke. It also gives Dominion a second job, which Law 37 asks of home ground.
2. **A card per push.** Rejected: hundreds of plays a run, each worth 0.25 of a clock tick.
3. **Stay also slows the failures' pushes.** The 2026-11-16 decision weighed this and rejected it as a new reach for the card. The ticket asked not to reopen it without new numbers. There are new numbers (the pushes are a quarter of the clock), but the place answer covers the same pushes without changing Stay, so it stays closed.
4. **Fewer feeding complications (tune the data).** Rejected as the answer: it makes the clock slower for everyone and gives the player nothing to do. It stays a tuning lever if the kill criterion fires.
5. **Show every push as a toast.** Rejected: about three a day is a storm (UI Law 49) of events the player did not ask for (Law 15). The panel lists them, and the daily alert names one.

Tension: Rebuke's name says "rival". The UL proposal ([THR-2210](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2210)) already defines it as "a card and a way of tending a place". Rebuking the Doom where it fed fits that definition.

## Question 2 — the last stage

1. **The stand over the god's ground (chosen):** a notice line, a daily alert, and the Doom panel list. Every move is a Rebuke or a tending, paid from the same purse as the Mandate's chapters.
2. **A new last-stage card ("hold the line" over all ground).** Rejected for now: the ticket asked to reuse before adding. It is the next lever if the predicate still fails with the alert live.
3. **Unspent essence becomes something at the age's end** (a monument, a tilt to the next world). Rejected here: it reaches across runs (Law 39, "the world remembers") and changes what a run is worth. That is Christian's call. The plan instead gives the purse a use during the stand.
4. **A faster Doom in the last stage, so the stand bites.** Rejected: it would undo [a well-played age](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2245)'s measured win rate while its veto window is open, and it punishes the hands-off player for nothing they did.
5. **A second journey after the Return.** Reserved for Christian (THR-2204). The plan works either way.

## Question 3 — the predicate

Decisions offered per in-game day, last stage against before it, in the played arm. It is a ratio, so a seed with a busy middle is not held to the same absolute number as a quiet one. 0.5 is a first threshold: today's best seed is 0.26. The alert counts at most once a day, because it names a place and a card. One alert a day could clear the bar by itself, so the predicate has a second half: the played arm must actually cast a doom Rebuke on half the alert days. An alert counts only when Rebuke is affordable. The census also reports clock ticks absorbed, and the kill criterion reads whether the age's end moved.

## Vision premises

- The world does not wait (Law 13): mortals keep failing on their own, and the Doom keeps feeding.
- The god answers where it stands (Law 37): home ground starves the Doom.
- Neither clock is bought (Laws 30 and 31): the purse buys held ground, never progress back.
