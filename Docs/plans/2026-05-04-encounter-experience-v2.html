What this v2 commits to

  • Cards spine, with a graph inset. A beat is still a card; the graph on the right shows which threads are in play right now and what kind of relation each one is — TESTS, BEHIND HER · HIDING, WATCHES, CARRIES, HOLDS, STANDS AT. This is C keeping A honest.
  • Place painting in every active beat. Atmospheric scene at the top of the card so the player can see the gate, the dusk, the queue, the lanterns, the captain's silhouette. The thin gold thread crossing the sky is the only magic visible — concentrated, networked, on-style.
  • Cast portraits with weight. Each cast member has a portrait, a one-line role, a disposition line, tags, and the sphere they answer to (Iron / Eye / Heart). Reminds the player who's in the room.
  • Concrete prose. "Veiren plants his bootheel in front of Eira and stops." Dialogue, action, dust. Halren coughs from three places back. The lyric note (italic) is reserved for the dialogue line and one or two strikes per beat.
  • Lean choices as directorial notes. "Make her step up." "Make her look past him." "Make her step aside." Each says what she does, then what it costs, then what the consequence shape is. No flowery euphemism.
  • "Watch only" right-edge panel — opt-out remains free; the default. The hint underneath: "the world stays uncertain either way."

What I'm asking your verdict on

  • Prose tone. Did I land the "less flowery, more short-story" target? Or is it still drifting? If it's still off, point me to one line that nails it and one that doesn't and I'll calibrate.
  • Lean phrasing. "Make her step up / look past him / step aside" — does that feel right? Or should it be even more practical (e.g. "Step forward · IRON · small breath" with no narrator framing at all)?
  • Graph inset. Does seeing labelled relations alongside the card give the clarity you wanted from C? Or is it cluttered? It can be collapsed to a strip with edge labels only if it's competing too hard with the prose.
  • Place painting fidelity. SVG-painterly is doing a job here, but for a real "movie in the head" we likely want generated concept art slotted into this frame. If yes, I'll run an image-generation pass next.
  • Cast portrait fidelity. Same question — gradient silhouettes are placeholder; do we want generated portraits for the named cast?

Drop or evolve? If this v2 is roughly the right shape, I'll write the long-form design plan against it (Engine / Content / UI pillars, lean-primitive vocabulary, change vocabulary, content-author handbook). If it's still off, tell me where to push and I'll iterate the wireframe before anything else.
