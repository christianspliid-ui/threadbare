Christian named both verbs in the THR-74 grill (Q8, verbatim: "influencing reforming and splitup
is also great candidates for ascendant actions") and directed them to the backlog. With THR-74's

## First-pass framing I considered

"Two new mechanisms: a reunion system and a schism system." The pre-flight killed that framing in
minutes: everything both verbs need already exists (disbanded persistence, formation scan,
cohesion deltas, leave decisions, the `blessedUntilTick` window pattern) or is already
commissioned to THR-74's remainder (convergence pull, fray pool). The honest shape is two
timed-window properties and three multiplier reads — catalog expansion that genuinely rides
substrate, the thing the skill exists to verify.

## Alternatives considered

**A. Reunite as an authored "Reunion" encounter the god casts directly.** Rejected — commands the
mortals instead of tilting them; also duplicates Seeking Companions' formation-moment machinery.
The verb bends probability (convergence + compatibility); the mortals still walk the road.

**B. Sunder as instant dissolution.** Rejected — a kill-switch, not a god-verb; skips the drama
(fray scenes, The Parting) that is the company system's whole payoff; violates soft-power.

**C. Sunder targeting a specific member ("corrupt the weakest link").** Deferred — member-scale
targeting is a richer verb but needs betrayal-arc content (THR-733's slow-burn betrayal) to pay
off; v1 targets the company and lets the systemic drama pick the member. Revisit after THR-733.

**D. Bless/Sunder mutual cancellation.** Rejected — resolving the collision mechanically erases
the most interesting state (two divine wills pulling one fellowship apart/together). Independent
multipliers; the tug-of-war is the story.

**E. New trace types for windows.** Rejected — existing aggregate + event traces cover it; the
Omit-collapse quirk makes union edits cost more than the passthrough field is worth.

**F. Ship now, before THR-74's remainder.** Rejected — Reunite without convergence machinery and
Sunder without the fray pool would be property writes nothing reads: wiring to a dead pipeline.
The blocked-by stays.

## Decision

## Tensions surfaced

- **Catalog growth vs. surface pressure:** the 2026-05-04 retirement of fixed verbs makes every
  new global template suspect. Both verbs pass the surface-shape check the same way Bless does —
  focused-target institutional casts with no host encounter — and the pre-flight records it.
- **Power vs. drama:** Sunder could trivially be "win button vs bands." The multiplier-window
  shape keeps it slow and visible — enemies get scenes, not deletion.

## Vision premises this plan leans on

- **Probability, never command.** This plan's version: windows bias formation math and leave
  rolls; no mortal is moved or removed by fiat.
- **The graph is the world's memory.** This plan's version: a disbanded roster is not dead data —
  the god can act on grief.
- **Failure is plot.** This plan's version: a lapsed Reunite window closes as a chronicle beat;
  Sunder's success is measured in authored drama scenes fired.
