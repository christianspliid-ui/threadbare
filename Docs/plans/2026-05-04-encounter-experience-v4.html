What v4 adds. Frame 1 (active beat) gains: a callback note when a marked moment is invoked (closes the long-term-investment loop), an identification banner that telegraphs when she's acting from her established traits, failure-as-complication preview on every lean card (failure as a story, not a punishment), Ascendant deck growth indicators (deck count, "new card" badge, rare-card pulse), and a change-shape hint showing what kind of change each lean tilts toward. Frame 2 (outcome → aftermath) is new: shows the dice moment landing, prose with the causal echo highlighted in gold ("because you sharpened her sight…"), the change registering visually as artifacts materialising in the items rail with an audio cue, the aftermath card flipping with three artifacts crystallising, a deck event ("+1 to your hand"), and an identification beat ("Eira held her vow").

What I'd like your verdict on

  • The causal echo treatment. Gold italic "Because you sharpened her sight" at the head of the outcome prose. Earns its weight, or too didactic? If too on-the-nose, we can lean it lighter ("Her gaze flicks past Veiren — your sight has done its work").
  • The five-band outcome ladder visible bottom-left. A small at-a-glance "where the threads landed" indicator after the roll. Useful, or breaks the "no numbers" principle? It uses no numbers — but it does name the band.
  • The identification banner ("Holding her vow / She held her vow"). A subtle telegraph that telegraphs twice (once before, once after). Right amount of "yes, that's her now" recognition? Or too explicit?
  • The Ascendant deck growth indicators. "+1 NEW" badge, deck count, rare-card pulse. Right level of MTG/deck-builder accent? Or pulls toward gacha aesthetic?
  • The aftermath card layout. One card, three artifacts crystallising as cards into the rail, one new thread teased. Right shape for the registration moment? Too busy? Too sparse?
  • Causal chain visibility. The callback note in Frame 1 ("she invokes the night Veiren tested her last winter") demonstrates the deferred-payoff loop. If this lands, it's the most powerful long-term hook in the design. Are we showing it the right way?
