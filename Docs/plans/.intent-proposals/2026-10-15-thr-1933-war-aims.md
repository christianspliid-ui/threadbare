## scope (what this plan does)

Two slices. **S1 (this ticket builds it):** one shared enemy rule (`isEnemyFaction`) read by army targeting, the battle start and the siege start; defence first (an army marches to a threatened own-or-sworn holding within 8 hexes); value targeting over in-reach enemy towns using the purse's income helpers, with the staple bonus live and the monopoly bonuses behind one seam returning 0; a daily re-aim inside `army_movement`; ambitions' `targetNodeId` written; the army sheet's reason and hold lines; traces and debug accessors; rollback flag. **S2 (filed at handoff):** the regional monopoly rule, scarcity rent in the purse, the monopoly bonuses live, the `embargo` action (a stock-balance cut on embargoed towns plus a lane mark for the map, because lanes carry no goods into stocks on main), the embargo and monopoly chronicle, `resource_acquisition`'s target, the Consortium id map, the Trade lens hover lines (after map lenses II) and the monopoly chip (after the purse panel).

## scope (what this plan does NOT do — explicit non-goals)

- Does not decide **whether** factions go to war: who is an enemy stays with rivalry, war sentiment and rebellion (decision rule 1).
- Does not add `intercept` (chasing an army); defenders hold the threatened town.
- Does not change `areFactionsHostile` or its callers (auras, spells, undertaking motives).
- No royal charter (the crown's, later); no monopoly as diplomatic weight or rival-god magnet (decision: not yet specified).
- No god card, no essence change; the god's stake is THR-1935.
- No mercenary hiring (THR-1939).
- Does not build the purse, the crown, the Trade lens or the Purse panel — it reads or fills them.
- No number on any player surface.
