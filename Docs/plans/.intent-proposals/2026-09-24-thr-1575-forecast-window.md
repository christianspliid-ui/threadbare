## scope (what this plan does)

The plan re-designs how odds form and how autonomous mortals choose what to attempt, in five slices:

- **S1 (gauge, no behaviour change):** a measurement script and per-proficiency-band KPIs, including retry-after-failure, failure-streak and attempted-difficulty-trend, plus a heavy-lane invariant test.
- **S2 (forecast = roll):** the planner forecasts with the same scale adjustment and difficulty the roll uses. It gains a success-family forecast that mirrors the runtime's outcome aggregation, and the growth-term units bug is fixed.
- **S3 (dice re-fit):**
  - capability curve midpoint 10 → 30, k 0.4 → 0.08;
  - P = odds-at-par + gain × (capability − difficulty) + modifiers;
  - scale floors retired, and the difficulty-cap that encodes the old formula switched off;
  - non-dice capability readers kept on a named pre-refit reader.
- **S4 (forecast window):** a fit multiplier that makes mortals engage challenges they forecast at 50–65%, refuse those far above them, and find those beneath them unattractive. Personality shifts the window slightly. A capped setback shift eases the window after consecutive failures. Failed templates get a longer cooldown. The outgrowth filter is retired by switch.
- **S5 (words):** the character sheet reads the same capability curve as the encounter skill line.

The rulebook (§7 and the quick reference) is rewritten in the same PR to state the rule in Christian's terms, marked `[DESIGN]`.

## scope (what this plan does NOT do — explicit non-goals)

- It authors no new higher-difficulty content (encounters, monsters, undertakings). By Christian's ruling that comes later; S1's coverage report is its brief, and a Todo follow-up is filed after S4.
- It rewrites no authored difficulty values. They are re-read as "proficiency demanded".
- It does not scale difficulty to the actor (rejected: it makes skill meaningless).
- It does not re-fit sight, gates, tier readers or growth. Awareness hops, mentorship tiers, renown, social leverage, threshold gates and capability growth stay on the pre-refit curve; a Deferral re-fits them one at a time.
- It does not change the five-band ladder, riders, nudge card magnitudes, the player-cast floor (THR-728), or crit rules.
- It does not change the at-cost texture band (Christian's July ruling). It measures it and escalates if it moves.
- It does not fix THR-1535 (standing modifiers missing from the roll). That ticket is mutex and preferably lands first.
- It does not change mastery-trait decay. That is time-based, not failure-based, and is reported separately.
- It adds no new node or edge type, and no field on `UnifiedAction`.

## kill criteria

- **S3 (dice, choices unchanged):** total success outside 0.45–0.72, `crit_failure_rate` > 0.15, `failure_story_rate` < 0.90, or idle rate up more than 5 points. Stop and report; never restore floors to hit them.
- **S4:**
  - the level-success invariant fails for a covered band;
  - total success outside 0.50–0.65;
  - retry-after-failure above 0.10;
  - failure-streak p95 above 4;
  - attempted-difficulty trend ≤ 0;
  - template variety KPIs out of threshold;
  - at-cost share outside 0.30–0.70.

  Any of these: stop. The at-cost case escalates to Christian.
- **Fights:** the fight-block calibration's own kill criterion.
- **Whole design:** if S4's invariant cannot be met for journeyman/novice bands without restoring floors or scaling difficulty to the actor, the premise ("enough content exists at lower levels") is wrong, and the plan returns to design.

## author notes for the judge

- **The numbers are starting values** from a counterfactual on 3,100 recorded rolls, which holds today's choices fixed. Real success after S4 depends on content coverage, so the invariant is scoped to bands with ≥30 engagements and the gap is reported, not hidden.
- **Where I am least certain:**
  - whether novice-band content is sufficient for the lowest-skill spotlight mortals, since refusals may push idle up (guarded by the idle KPI);
  - whether the setback shift and failure cooldown are enough against retry loops, given the familiarity discount already exists (guarded by the retry KPI).
- **The difficulty-cap switch** in `applyScaleDifficultyAdjust` is easy to miss. Leaving it on silently recreates a ~0.61 floor under the new formula. The plan calls it out as load-bearing.
- **The pre-refit reader is a containment choice, not a claim that those readers are right.** Tiers are saturated today too. Re-fitting them in the same release would make cause and effect unreadable.
- **THR-1535** (items and conditions missing from the roll) is Todo and awaiting Christian's go-ahead. The two are mutex, and the plan prefers 1535 first but does not hard-block on it.
