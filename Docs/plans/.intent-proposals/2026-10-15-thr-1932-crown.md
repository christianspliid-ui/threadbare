## scope (what this plan does)

The plan specifies:

- **The oath edge:** one new directed edge `sworn_to` (vassal faction → liege realm), fully designed: properties, single writer module, readers, tick participation, traces.
- **Winning an oath:** a new faction action `seek_fealty` in the existing action table, so a realm with at least one town wins oaths by coercion (a 3-day siege, or a town taken in the last 5 days), persuasion (offer score ≥ 0.85) or purchase (a gift from its purse).
- **The `crown` phase:** a daily inline phase after `faction_purse` that moves the oaths' daily loyalty, takes the 0.3 fealty tax after the vassal has paid its soldiers, breaks oaths below 0.2 and triggers rebellion for a big realm.
- **The derived Crown:** a Crown title for a realm with ≥ 2 oaths, and an exported `protectedHoldingsOf` read for war aims' defence rule.
- **Conquest record:** a `takenFrom` property on conquest edges.
- **Player-facing surfaces:** five chronicle triggers; the faction sheet's Crown line, Fealty section and one Purse sentence; a crown outline on the map when a realm is sworn; one hex-tooltip clause; debug accessors.
- **Two filed tickets:** a UL proposal (THR-1943, Crown and Fealty) and a deferral for encounter writers (THR-1944).
