The ticket under design ([THR-2069](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2069)) has this Done-when: "a plan doc in `Docs/plans/` decides add-salt vs re-pick the good, with all three pillars, and its implementation ticket is filed (Ready for Dev at handoff)."

## scope (what this plan does)

The plan adds salt as a nineteenth good, classed strategic. It sits on steppe, plateau, the three desert terrains and the coast, and comes with resource prose and tier prose. The plan also authors the salt-stock half of the ford scene's outcome rows (both `smugglers_ford` variants), on success bands only. `smuggle_goods` gets no salt row: its salt is sold outside the town. It does this using the existing `economy_shift` `stock` path. No new code path, node, edge, phase or UI component is added. The existing readers pick salt up as they do any good: seeding, stock tiers, purse yield, road cargo, regional monopoly, embargo, Reveal the Vein and the Codex.

## scope (what this plan does NOT do — explicit non-goals)

- It does not change The Salt Monopoly scene. That scene is about a duke's licence, not the regional monopoly.
- It does not change how regional monopolies form. None of any good formed in 30 days on three seeds, and that baseline is out of scope.
- It does not fix the scarcity chronicle's hunger wording or capitalised good names for non-food goods. That is filed as its own bug.
- It does not touch `world-model.json` (CMS-only and stale), `DEPOSIT_YIELD_CHANNEL`, `STOCK_DEPOSIT_GOODS` or guild place affinity.
- It does not add salt to mountains or hills.
- It does not add a per-place presence chance to resource seeding.
- It does not cover bounty collection or the mill-lease row. Both stay on THR-1954.

## impact_class

Reversible. Data rows and content tables. The new terrain rows shift the seeded resource stream, so every seed's world changes slightly. That shift is deterministic and stated in the plan.
