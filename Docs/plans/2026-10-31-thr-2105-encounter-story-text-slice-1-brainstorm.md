# Brainstorm companion — encounter story text, slice 1

The plan: [2026-10-31-thr-2105-encounter-story-text-slice-1.md](2026-10-31-thr-2105-encounter-story-text-slice-1.md). The evidence: [the audit](../audits/2026-10-31-encounter-story-text-quality.md).

## How the slice was chosen

The ticket offered two shapes: a rewrite wave, or authoring-chain changes. The audit's demand table decided the order. The old board is 74.5% of what the player meets, and nothing new is written into it, so a rewrite is its source fix. The factory corpus is 13.3% and mostly holds the doctrine. Its habits are narrower and get a chain fix in their own ticket.

The slice takes the ten most-met board templates (22.6%), not twenty (37.6%). Twenty templates with eight openings each is 160 arrival lines plus the rewrite. That is past what one executor run reads back carefully, and the cold read in the Done-when is the quality gate. Wave 2 takes the next twenty once wave 1 shows the calibration holds (kill criterion 1).

The exemplar and pronoun fixes ride along. They are small and they sit at the source of two patterns the factory copies. Leaving them for the chain ticket would let every factory run until then copy them again.

## Options weighed and not taken

- **A board-specific exemplar first.** Cheaper to calibrate, but nothing in the doctrine says board text differs, and the doctrine's exemplars plus the audit's good lines are a fair bar. Kept as kill criterion 1's fallback.
- **Openings only for the classes each template plausibly fires in.** That is THR-1222's honest-envelope move. It changes placement, so demand shifts under the slice and Done-when 5 cannot hold. Rejected for this slice.
- **Naming the role figures** ("the trader" → a cast member). The doctrine prefers named people. On the board that needs cast bindings across 168 templates, and prose may not invent names. Rejected for now; it is a structural ticket of its own if Christian wants it.
- **Fixing the writing checks alongside.** Out of scope by the ticket: THR-2106 decides them with Christian.

## The fork left for Christian

Factory cards on a choice step often end with a sentence that says which way the card leans ("It argues for the honest way."). The doctrine says the effect line is "what the god does" and bans "meta-commentary about effort or odds", but it does not say whether a card may name the choice it favours. Three ways to write `smugglers_ford` · Count The Fine:

- **A — keep the lean sentence:** "Set the full price of being caught plainly before them, in coin and in name. It argues for the honest way."
- **B — the effect only; the lean is shown by the card's fork marker, not words:** "Set the full price of being caught plainly before them, in coin and in name."
- **C — fold the lean into the effect:** "Make them see the full price of being caught, so the honest way looks cheaper."

No slice changes these lines until Christian picks, so silence keeps A.
