✅ **THR-2048 — a place held after success** (2026-10-27, M3: Dynamic Economy). The forge a mortal builds and the claim they stake are theirs afterwards: a new `hold_place` aftermath effect raises the Place (or takes up an unheld one) as the actor's freehold, one to a town or wild place. When someone else already holds it, the actor is paid for the work instead. Forge construction, the prospecting expedition and prospect the seam carry it.
