✅ **THR-2091 — A hex card's sphere push measures distance on the map's own grid** (2026-10-30, Thematic Pressure & Living World). The resolver that picks the place a hex push lands on now measures on the map's odd-q grid, not odd-r, so diagonal places are the right distance away. Details: `Docs/status/2026-10-30-thr-2091.md`.
