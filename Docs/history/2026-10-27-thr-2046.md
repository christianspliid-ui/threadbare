✅ **THR-2046 — a road's encounters write to that road** (2026-10-27, M3: Dynamic Economy). A route-event seed carries its lane onto its action, so the ambush, the toll dispute and the embargo write the road they happened on. The new `lane_threat` / `lane_toll` quantities let a lost caravan threaten its road, a broken ambush clear it, and a struck toll go to the town's faction (else the mortal), with an *A toll is set* receipt row.
