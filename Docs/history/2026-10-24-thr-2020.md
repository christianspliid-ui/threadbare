✅ **THR-2020 — A liege pays for its vassal's raise** (2026-10-24, M3: Dynamic Economy). A sworn free company or realm that keeps a standing army, fields none, and is refused a raise only by its purse now gets one paid by its liege: the raise plus a first day, from what the liege holds above its wage reserve, at most once per 5 days. The armed-day share does not reach half on seed 42; the reason is in `Docs/status/2026-10-24-thr-2020.md`.
