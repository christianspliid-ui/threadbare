✅ **THR-2031 — a relic left lying can be taken up** (2026-10-27, Dominion — Player Power Progression). Seekers and wanderers can make *Take up a relic left lying* their work. When it completes, a past god's relic no longer lies at its place: it is theirs, it leans with its bearer, and the world says so in its own line. Details: `Docs/status/2026-10-27-thr-2031.md`.
