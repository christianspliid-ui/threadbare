✅ **THR-2088 — Inspire now changes what a mortal decides** (2026-10-28, Thematic Pressure & Living World). Breath of Purpose carries an `own_lean` value-drift rule (`INSPIRE_VALUE_DRIFT` 0.10), so the "Inspired" mortal leans harder on the question the god's reach asks. It also gains receipt lines, and its card text no longer promises a flame that never fades. Details: `Docs/status/2026-10-28-thr-2088.md`.
