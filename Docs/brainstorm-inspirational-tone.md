# Brainstorm: Inspirational Tone & Source Catalogue

## Sources
- **Malazan Book of the Fallen** (Steven Erikson) — primary tonal influence
- **Mystara / Known World** (classic D&D BECMI setting) — primary structural/cultural influence

## Target Audience
- Middle-aged nerds who like fantasy books and games
- Grew up with BECMI-era D&D, read Erikson in their 20s-30s
- Want fantasy that respects their intelligence while hitting nostalgia buttons

## Core Tonal Principle: "Nostalgic form, adult substance"

The *shape* of events echoes classic D&D adventure beats (dragon threatens frontier, ancient tomb holds secrets, empire marches). The *meaning* underneath is Malazan — morally complex, historically layered, consequences ripple.

## Blend Decisions (Agreed)

### 1. Ascendancy as burden, not promotion
- Mystara shape: real achievement, earned through play, feels like progression
- Malazan substance: every step costs something human, power isolates, the World-Soul's resonance fragments are scar tissue from previous ascendants
- Player earns it but also *loses* something with each gain

### 2. Mystara's cultural mosaic, Malazan's cultural contradictions
- Archetypes are Mystara-shaped: recognizable, distinct, evocative (the magocracy, the steppe nomads, the trading republic, the theocracy)
- Individuals within them are Malazan-shaped: contradictory, surprising, human
- No monolithic cultures — every society has dissenters, wounds, people who don't fit

### 3. Convergence as the god-game's set-piece
- Malazan's Convergences (power draws power into explosive confrontation) = what a god-game *is*
- Player interventions create ripples → rival gods respond → mortal agents caught in crossfire → factions collide at one hex
- Dressed in Mystara's geographic specificity — location shapes the event's flavor

### 4. Deep time, revealed in fragments
- System knows everything (world-model graph has full history)
- Player discovers Malazan-style: ruins, cultural memories, contradictory legends, agents saying things that don't match the official story
- Progressive disclosure system already built for this

### 5. Wonder layered over grief
- Mystara mood: "wow, look at this amazing place!"
- Malazan mood: "everything beautiful is built on something terrible"
- Blend: player feels wonder first (nostalgia hit), then discovers the weight underneath
- A golden city should be gorgeous — and three ticks later, you realize whose bones are in the foundation
