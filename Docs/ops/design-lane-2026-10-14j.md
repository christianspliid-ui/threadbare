## Decided for you

- [The god's stake in the mortal economy](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1871/the-gods-stake-in-the-mortal-economy-what-a-god-gains-from-a-thriving) — **your god earns from the economy through its worshippers, not through coin.** A hungry town on your ground prays to you, and you have about six days to answer. Answer it (Bless the Harvest, Open the Markets) and your hold on that place grows. If the town then recovers, its people become a faithful congregation: a small essence source that thrives while the town is fed and withers when it starves. If you ignore the prayer, the rival god answers instead and goes after that town first. Help given to a hungry town off your ground widens your ground a little but wins no congregation. There is no tithe and no fear to collect; Blight hurts through hunger and unrest. Three new cards join the omen market: *Fortune's Favour* and *Ruin* make one mortal richer or poorer, and *Bless the Holding* makes a held place pay its owner more for a season. Claim Resource now pays by what it claims, and Open the Markets' promised thieves' guild gets built or the promise is removed. *To veto, say:* **"the god should be paid by the coin, not the prayers"**, **"every hungry town should pray to me"**, or **"a god should profit from fear"**.
