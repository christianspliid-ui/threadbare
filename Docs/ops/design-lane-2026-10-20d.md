## Decided for you

- [A beast cannot be a bound scene actor](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1274/no-non-human-cast-primitive-a-beast-cannot-be-a-bound-scene-actor): the game gets **no separate creature type**. A beast that a mortal can fight, curse in the middle of a scene and meet again is **a lair's monster**, and that already works. Any other animal (the bear in the granary, a wolf pack, a herd) stays **scenery**: it lives in the story text, in the wounds it leaves and in the state of the place. When a scene needs someone to work against, it gives a person who speaks for the danger, like the drover who tracks the pack. Nothing changes in play. There is nothing to build, so the ticket only needs your Done click. *Veto:* "animals should live in the world".
