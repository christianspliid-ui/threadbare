## Decided for you

- [The world's own drift fills the Mandate with no god](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2081): **the Mandate now has a plan: it counts only what your own hand raised.** A god that only watches sees its Mandate sit at New. A god that works its spheres sees it fill, and slip back when a rival raids ground the god built. Three calls inside the plan:
  - **Rivals take back only what you built.** If every rival raid on your spheres anywhere counted, a god would start some runs hundreds of points in the hole before acting. *Veto:* "rivals wearing my spheres down anywhere should cost me".
  - **The target is a fixed amount of your own work** (80 for the first sphere, 20 for the second), the same in every world. A god that spends everything on it finishes a little past the halfway point of doom. *Veto:* "a bigger world should need a bigger Mandate".
  - **It ships before energy cards exist.** No card a god can cast carries energy. So Kindle, Wander and Illuminate gods can raise energy only slowly, mostly through their bond with the land, and their age will end by doom until those cards are made ([filed as its own ticket](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2086/energy-has-no-card-a-god-can-cast-kindle-wander-and-illuminate-cannot)). Their Mandate page says so in words. Today every Mandate fills by itself. *Veto:* "hold the Mandate change until energy cards exist".
