## Decided for you

- [The world's own drift fills the Mandate with no god](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2081): **only growth the god causes counts toward the Mandate.** The world keeps founding places and filling with newborn mortals, and every new one adds to every sphere's total, so the Mandate filled with nobody playing. Two of four test worlds ended their age that way. From now on, Mandate progress counts what the god's own hand puts into its spheres: deeds, holds, claims, answered prayers. Rivals and doom wearing those spheres down still pulls it back, so it stays a contest. *Veto:* "the world drifting toward my spheres is my doing" or "a mortal acting on my nudge should count".
