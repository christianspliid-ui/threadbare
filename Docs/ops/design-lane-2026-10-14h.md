## Decided for you

- [Undertakings in the economy](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1873/undertakings-in-the-economy-what-founding-building-and-trading-should) — **what mortals build now stays built and pays.**
  - **Founding a town:** the founder's faction rules and taxes it, and the founder keeps a freehold. A founder with no town-holding faction rules it alone.
  - **Raising a building:** it is the town's next building, picked by [the settlement economy](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1923/plan-doc-the-settlement-economy-towns-grow-from-surplus-build-to-their) from what its land can support, not a workshop every time. The builder holds it.
  - **Trade:** a trade run pays the trader by what the road carries, and whoever opens a road collects its toll.
  - **Ambitions:** each now asks for things its own undertakings can make. Today, Found Anew wants an estate and Rebuild from Ashes wants a camp, and neither undertaking can build one. Dominate Regional Trade now also counts roads held and a Wealthy purse.
  - **Monopolies:** a monopoly is a faction's goal, never one mortal's.

  *To veto, say:* **"a founder should be a lord in their own right"**, **"mortals shouldn't get rich from trade runs"**, or **"let mortals build any building they like"**.
