## Noted, not filed
- Essence shows three ways on the opening screens: numbers for 8 pools on the god rail, words for 12 spheres (including Chaos, Order, Light and Darkness) on the god's page, and "128 essence" in Read the Threads. The player cannot reconcile them. This is a design question, close to complaint class PC-1. Hold it until THR-2080 lets the mid-game frames show whether it persists.
- The First's thread row shows initials ("KT") instead of his portrait and truncates his name with room to spare (Laws 3, 57).
- Read the Threads is on cooldown at the very start of a game, behind a gold primary-looking button (Law 25: disabled needs to read as disabled, with its reason).
- The Doom record prints bare counts ("Counter-omens 0", "Doom debt 0"), and its key: value table is the pattern Law 16 calls unfinished (Laws 13, 16).
- The first encounter's dialog leaves most of its area empty while the prose stays small.
