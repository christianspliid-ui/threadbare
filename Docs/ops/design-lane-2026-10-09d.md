## Decided for you

- [The opposing dominion as a force](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1763/the-opposing-dominion-as-a-force-who-pushes-back-how-fast-turf-erodes) — how your god's turf is lost. Three things push back:
  - **Neglect.** A place nobody tends (no Claim, not your seat, none of your threaded mortals standing there) slowly fades. It drops a band in about three weeks.
  - **Rival gods.** They now raid your ground with the spheres opposite yours, aimed at your untended places first. The more ground you hold, the more often they come.
  - **Doom.** It scorches everyone's ground as it does today.

  Tending is the defence: one Claim or one threaded mortal in a place keeps it. You can lose everything you took, but never your seat. A lost band is a chronicle line and a mark on the map, never a pause. *To veto, say:* **"losing ground should be able to drive me from my home"**, **"untended turf shouldn't fade on its own"** or **"doom should aim at my turf"**.
