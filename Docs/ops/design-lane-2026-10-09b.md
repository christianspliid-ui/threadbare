## Decided for you

- [What the band buys](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1761/what-the-band-buys-cost-effect-strength-thread-yield-and-source-income) — what your turf is worth in play.
  - **Home turf is a bonus, never a requirement.** On neutral ground everything costs and pays what it does today.
  - **On your own ground** cards cost less (up to a quarter off), casts land more often, and your mortals and wellsprings pay more (up to half again).
  - **On hostile ground** cards cost half again as much, casts land less often, and a wellspring there pays nothing.
  - **Your mortals now pay back.** A threaded mortal earns more than it costs to keep, and earns more as they rise.
  - *To veto, say:* **"a wellspring should only pay on ground I hold"** or **"turf should change the size of what I do, not just the odds"**.
