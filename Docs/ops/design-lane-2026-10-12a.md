## Decided for you

- [Find anything](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1832/find-anything-what-the-god-can-search-for-and-what-a-result-does) — Ctrl+K (or /) opens a search box under the top bar.
  - **What it finds:** places, people, factions, armies, artifacts, regions, the cards in your hand, and Codex entries. It only finds what you have already seen, so it never names a stranger or a hidden treasure.
  - **What picking a result does:** a place or person flies the map there and opens it in the side panel, a card arms ready to cast, and a Codex entry opens the Codex at that page.
  - **Empty box and misses:** an empty box shows your recent picks and your own people. A miss says "Nothing you know of by that name", whether or not the thing exists.
  - **Time:** searching never pauses the game.
  - *To veto, say:* **"search should find anything in the world"**, **"only find people I know well"** or **"pause the game while I search"**.
