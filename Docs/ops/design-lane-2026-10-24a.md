## Decided for you

- [Three player texts promise that mandates slow the doom clock](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2004/three-player-texts-promise-that-mandates-slow-the-doom-clock-they-only): **the words change, not the rules.** Completing a mandate does not slow the doom and does not give essence. Four texts said it did: the Mandates tooltip, the Culmination stage tooltip, the Divine Essence tooltip, and The Seat beat. They will now say what really happens. A mandate checkpoint you keep well softens the doom's next blow, and finishing a mandate fulfils it before the doom closes. This matches the doom lines that were fixed last week. The two clocks stay rivals that draw on one purse. Nothing changes in play except the words. *Veto:* "a mandate should hold the doom back" or "mandates should feed the purse". That makes it a rules change instead.
