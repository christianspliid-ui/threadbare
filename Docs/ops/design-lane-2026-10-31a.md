## Decided for you

- [Spirit, life and entropy get a cheap place card](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2090/spirit-life-and-entropy-get-a-cheap-place-card-and-doom-wears-a-life). Doom's rising tide still darkens the whole world, but it no longer wipes a god's Mandate. Doom's own works and rivals still take back what the god built. Without this, a life god (Gather) could never fill its Mandate: 1.2 of 87 points, against 30 with the change. Kindle's life half was in the same state. Veto line: "doom should wear a life god down everywhere".
- Same ticket: three cheap town cards at 2 essence.
  - *Tend the Sick* (life) heals a town.
  - *Let the Damp In* (entropy) slowly sickens one.
  - *Keep the Vigil* (spirit) calms unrest by mourning the dead.

  *Stoke the Forges* drops from 3 to 2 essence, so every Hunger's sphere now has a cheap town card.
