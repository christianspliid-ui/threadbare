## Decided for you

- [The crown plan](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1932/plan-doc-the-crown-oaths-of-fealty-and-tax-how-a-faction-wins-them) — **only a realm that holds a town can win oaths; guilds and temples swear, they never collect.** ([plan](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/Docs/plans/2026-10-15-thr-1932-crown.md), [why each choice](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/Docs/plans/2026-10-15-thr-1932-crown-brainstorm.md)) *Veto:* **"let rich guilds hold oaths too"**.
- Same plan — **a faction that turns a realm down only cools toward it a little; it does not become a rival, and it is not news.** Breaking an oath is what makes enemies and reaches the Chronicle. (The prototype saw 250 refusals in 90 days.) *Veto:* **"every snub should be news"**.
- Same plan — **a sworn lord pays its own soldiers first, then the crown takes its share** of what is left. *Veto:* **"the crown's share comes off the top"**.
- Same plan — **loyalty uses the usual standing words**, and an oath breaks the moment it falls to *Distrusted*. A forced vassal reads "*Unknown, one step from breaking — it swore under siege*". *Veto:* **"use Wary / Friendly for oaths instead"**.
- Same plan — **the word on screen is "fealty", never "sworn"** (that is already a court rank), and never "king", "queen" or "kingdom". A Crown is written "*the Hold of Witness Skyfield, a Crown*". *Veto:* **"call them kings"**.
- Same plan — **the crown's outer border is drawn only when a whole realm has sworn to it**; a crown of guilds keeps its own border. *Veto:* **"always draw the crown's line"**.
- Same plan — **no royal charters yet**: nothing in the game could honour one. *Veto:* **"let crowns grant charters now"**.
