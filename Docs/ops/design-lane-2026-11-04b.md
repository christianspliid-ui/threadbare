## Decided for you

These are for [The Comet at the Turning, the Page Beneath the Saint and Apotheosis, rewritten in plain words](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2134/novel-prose-story-beats-slice-b-the-comet-at-the-turning-the-page). Its first rewrite stopped on 2 November because the writer had no rule for four kinds of mistake. These are the four rules:

- **When a mortal wins at a cost, the mortal pays.** The result line says what *they* gave up: strength, nerve, what they would have learned. It never says the god paid essence. That is how the game works: a win at a cost drains the mortal's quintessence and halves what they learn. The god pays for a card when it is played, whatever happens. Three Apotheosis lines said the god paid, and they change.
- **The ending paragraph tells what happened; the small result tag says what changed.** They stop saying the same thing twice. The paragraph shows the moment (who acted, what they did, who saw) and leaves the result to the tag. This follows your 15-word tag ruling of 12 September, that a tag must not repeat the ending.
- **A mortal's pronouns come from the mortal.** Lines that name the mortal use the game's pronoun tokens, so a mortal is never wrongly called "they". Fifteen lines across the three encounters change.
- **A failed ending gets its own words.** Three endings read like a win even when the mortal lost: the comet read aloud and failing, the page brought out and failing, the page buried and badly failing. Each now gets its own failure ending. These endings only reuse result tags the encounter already has, so no new effect enters the game.

If the second rewrite still fails its cold read, each encounter ships or stays as it is on its own merits. There is no third round.

Say "veto <title>" to reverse any of these. Nobody builds on them before 02:30 UTC tomorrow (5 November).
