### The find: a precondition that was met without its deliverable

[THR-1405](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1405/task-join-the-catalogue-to-the-systems-inventory-owningsystem-values) is a `wayfinder:task` whose own 2026-09-03 comment set its closing condition in one sentence: *"This ticket stays open as the map-side tracker until THR-1407 lands, at which point the third grid view becomes generable."*

Three inputs present, nothing joining them. Deliverables (1), (2) and (3) of THR-1405 are discharged; (4) — the subsystem × verb view with its LIVE-TOUCHED / OPEN-ONLY / UNTOUCHED status and reader column — is not, and had no executor home.

**This is a class worth naming, because a sweep would have got it wrong in the safe-looking direction.** A ticket whose stated precondition is met reads as finished. Here the precondition was a *proxy* for the deliverable, and the proxy cleared while the deliverable did not — so a lane matching on the sentence would have closed THR-1405 today and dropped the view on the floor, which is the failure the sentence was written to prevent, one step later. The general shape: **a closing condition phrased as another ticket's state is only as good as that ticket's scope, and scope drifts between filing and shipping.**
