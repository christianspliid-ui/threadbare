Threadbearer — Opening & Action Availability

How a divine action becomes available to the player, and when. This is the knob-board for the opening experience — the system we tune to make the game start good and fun.

Reflects the Ascendant Beats — Divine Cadence design (shipped per Linear, 2026-06-28). Companion to the canonical doc Docs/plans/2026-06-28-opening-and-action-availability.md and the visual catalog action-catalog.html.

1The two axes

Does the action exist?

The catalog — ~114 ascendant templates today, plus the Ascendant Beats verbs. Grows slowly. See action-catalog.html.

Is it available right now?

The availability system — floor → unlocks → gates → surfaces. This is where the opening is tuned.

2The floor — turn 1

A fresh run shows Move  Investiture  — and nothing else.

Every hex verb, agent intervention, and AscendantBar Self/Rare action is hidden until unlocked. The legacy intervention wheel was retired (THR-501); agent interventions now flow through the unlock-gated divine.* templates. STARTER_ACTION_IDS = [move, investiture]; runUnlockedActionIds grows from there as beats grant.

5The unlock model — three buckets

Within-run only today (per-account meta deferred → THR-480). runUnlockedActionIds starts at the floor and the unlock_action aftermath pushes IDs in as beats resolve.

Generic — always (the floor): move, investiture
Unlockable-generic — earned via beats, any ascendant: invest.thread_actor, imbue, consecrate, bestow, anoint
Reach-gated — earned via beats and within your two domains: reach-specific investment cards

6The two-domain lock

Every ascendant has exactly one primary + one secondary domain (reach), fixed for the whole run. No third domain is ever reachable — a card needing one is hidden the entire game, never shown even as aspiration. Spheres are the orthogonal axis (also primary+secondary, fixed at chargen). Expression-card magic is flavored by these two axes.

eyeheartstarveilshadowironstonegoldlife

Of the nine reaches, a given run uses exactly two. The rest are dark for that game.

9What we iterate on (answer by playtest, not on paper)

· Does turn 1 = 2 cards feel empowering or empty? (Candidate: fire Beat 0 on turn 0–1 so bind-thread + observe arrive immediately.)

· Is the spine cadence too slow/fast for a first session?

· Does the pool kick in at the right moment, with the right texture?

· Reach-gated cards: surfaced as aspiration, or invisible until earned? (Currently invisible.)

· Do Self/Rare innate actions belong in the opening at all? (Currently gated.)

The point of making availability data-driven is that we answer these by feel, fast.
