## Current Focus

**A god's power is rooted in the land their people work (THR-618, 2026-07-28).** The mortal economy and the divine economy sat on the same map and never touched — the P4 extraction checkpoint proved it (`essenceSources.ts` imported nothing from `resourceEconomy.ts`), and that same evidence was the diagnosis. A **typed** essence source now drifts its sanctity each tick from the stock tiers of the goods at its host location that share its Sphere: a Spirit shrine lives off its valley's pearls, a Matter nexus off its ore. Two asymmetries carry the design. Upward drift stops at a ceiling deliberately **below** the flowering threshold, so a rich valley readies a source and only a Sanctify cast flowers it — without that bound, a well-sited shrine would flower unattended and the Build verb would be decoration. Withering has **no** floor above zero and stacks with the contested drain in the same tick: the world takes away more than it gives, which is what makes tending and defending a source worth attention. Untyped (migrated, unbuilt) sources are skipped by construction, preserving THR-611's contract that a save which has done nothing keeps exactly its old income. No new phase, node type, edge type, or income channel — one signed number per source per tick, inside the `controls` walk that was already running, reading the same coarse tier the Livelihood line renders so what the player is shown and what the divine economy acts on cannot drift apart.

**The cap that was documented for years and never existed (THR-827, 2026-07-28).** `ResolutionInput.actionModifiers` has read `// capped at ±0.20` since Phase 2, and `computeResolutionThreshold` never clamped it — only the *result*, to [0.05, 0.95]. THR-821 filed the residue as a two-way question: stale comment, or real cap wanting enforcement. **It is stale, and the sharper finding is that it was never true** — not merely overtaken by the nudge model. `resolutionModifiers.ts` bounds each *contributor* and has never bounded their sum: sphere ±0.10, equipment 0.15, terrain ±0.10, traits 0.10, effects **0.30** — 0.75 from the bounded sources alone, before the unbounded divine-intervention and `modify_rules` terms, and `EFFECT_MODIFIER_CAP` on its own already exceeds the claimed total. That distinction decided the fix: "enforce the documented cap" looked like the conservative reading and was the destructive one, because it would have imposed a **new** constraint on shipped content while appearing to restore an old one — truncating equipment/effect stacking and making cards 3–8 of an authored hand buy nothing, the exact decorative-hand pathology THR-821's own finding exists to prevent. What shipped is the honest contract on both `actionModifiers` and `ResolutionModifiers.total`, documented at the computation, plus **`NUDGE_HAND_MAX_TOTAL_DELTA` (0.70)** in the authoring rubric — since nothing bounds hand strength at runtime, that file now holds the only real bound, warn-level and director-tunable, asserted against every exemplar hand and explicitly **not** a runtime clamp.
