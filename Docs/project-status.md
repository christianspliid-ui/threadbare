**Every encounter ended the same way regardless of how it ended, and no amount of content skill could have fixed it (THR-969, 2026-08-02).** Christian, chat review of the vertical-slice aftermath: *"all the stuff in the original prototype we made is not there."* `BranchAwareAftermathConfig` keyed aftermath variants on the `choiceId` of one step and on nothing else — and because most linear templates ship `variants: {}`, a **choice-less encounter could only ever render its single `fallback`**. So `encounter.slice.unsafe_bridge` produced byte-identical prose, chips, and reaction buttons whether the traveler crossed clean or went into the river. This was a structural gap, not a content one: the mockup's defining feature was **inexpressible by construction**, and a content pass against it would have failed no matter how well written. `AftermathVariant.byOutcome` now maps a `UnifiedActionOutcome` to per-band overrides of `overview` / `changes` / `reactionPrompt` / `reactions`, authorable on a choice-keyed variant *or* on the `fallback`, so the two axes compose: resolution is **choice → outcome band → base variant → fallback**, the band layering onto whichever variant the choice already picked, field by field, so a band may restate only what actually differs. **The band keys on the seven-value `UnifiedActionOutcome`** — deliberately not the five-band `EncounterOutcomeBand` (a trace type), the six-value `StepOutcome`, or `OutcomeBand` from `outcomeConsequences.ts`, each of which type-checks while being the wrong domain, which is the trap `StepNudge.bandProse` already documents. **The duplicate resolver was the real hazard.** The engine's aftermath assembly and the encounter-stage adapter each carried their *own* copy of the choice lookup, so threading the outcome through one and not the other would have left the UI rendering the un-banded ending the engine had already resolved past — invisibly, since both are green. Both now call one shared `resolveAftermathVariant`. Additive throughout (NFP #6): a band-less config resolves to the **identical object** it always did, pinned by an identity (`toBe`) assertion rather than deep equality, and an unauthored band or absent outcome degrades to the un-banded variant rather than throwing. Content re-authoring of the five slice aftermaths is THR-973, which was filed blocked on this and is now unblocked; the consequence-chip surface is THR-971.
