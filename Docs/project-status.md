**Items answer back when they are used (THR-719, 2026-07-25).** The last dead half of the item substrate is closed. `attachmentTriggers.ts` was a complete, tested on-use resolver with **zero production importers** — a sword whose tooltip said it snapped on a critical failure had never once snapped. All nine authored `onUseTriggers` blocks ported verbatim across both catalogs; the field, its three types, the resolver and its suite are gone, and the tooltip/detail-view read path now shows what actually fires. **A latent bug caught in the port:** `decayConditions` counts down `ticksRemaining`, not the `durationTicks` that `apply_condition` writes — granting on the wrong field would have minted conditions that never expire, the same silent-death class the ticket exists to end. **Your castings can land crooked (THR-728, 2026-07-25).** A player cast auto-succeeded before any capability, difficulty or shaper was consulted, so the Divine Receipt (THR-727) could only ever report a foregone conclusion — and the authored difficulties on **84 of 138** ascendant-castable templates were computed and thrown away. Casts now roll the same sigmoid→d100 ladder mortals do, with a **safety floor** (user verdict, chat 2026-07-24): a losing roll is caught at `success_at_cost`, so the working always lands but the world can charge for the holding; `setback`/`catastrophe` stay unreachable by construction, and the upside (clean, surge) stays fully earned. Zero-difficulty templates — every `divine.*` soul-verb, the `invest.*` signatures, observation — remain guaranteed as a thematic statement. Push and resist stay mortal. **The plan's one grey zone turned out to be the whole feature:** `computeCapability(graph, ascendantId, reach)` returns **0.018** for every god, because the ascendant node carries `domainAffinities` (2–5, a reach *ranking* weight) and never `domainCapabilities` (10–40, the raw scale the sigmoid consumes) — read literally, 94% of casts land on one band (400-seed measurement), which would have shipped a *flatter* receipt than the auto-success it replaced. The player path now maps affinity onto the raw scale (`ascendantCastRawBonus` → new `computeCapabilityWithRawBonus`), deliberately **not** folded into `computeRawScore` so THR-613's Deepening tier-crossings keep the score they were tuned against (pinned by a test).
