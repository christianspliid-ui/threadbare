**The ambition files stop claiming a vocabulary the game does not have (THR-813, 2026-07-27).** The residue THR-808 tracked rather than absorbed: 21 authored trait refs that no producer mints, all of them in `ambition-templates.ts`. The ticket asked for a decision *per class*, and the decision came from the UL rather than from taste — `Docs/ubiquitous-language/Traits.md` § Trait Ref already rules that **a ref naming a trait nothing creates is an authoring defect, not a missing engine feature**, and two of its corollaries close the routes that looked open. Minting needs a *category*, and a category is a lifecycle contract that names its producer, so "just add a definition" was never a route — adopting a producer is, and none of the ten contracts covers a biographical selection key. The phantom-grant route is canon-illegal for identity: a bare grant key has no display name, and THR-789's canon is that an object's traits are always visible in its interface once known, so the "stated cost" THR-808 recorded is a disqualification, not a trade-off. That retires the 13 `noLiveCounterpart` selection concepts (`pacifist`, `noble_blood`, `apostate`, …) across 14 sites. **Removal is behaviour-identical by construction, not by luck:** `ambitionSelection.ts` tests `agent.traits.includes(ref)`, so a dead blocker never blocked (permissive) and a dead boost never scored — and every *boosting* site retained a live key, so no ambition lost its trait signal. **The class the ticket expected to discharge is the one that could not be, and the reason is worth more than the fix would have been.** The three abandonment flags (`accepted_exile`, `homeless_wanderer`, `made_peace_with_the_land`) each name a *loss*, and abandonment is evaluated from an ambition's first tick with no grace period, ahead of milestones. The obvious repoints — zero `loyalty` bonds, outside the homeland, not having moved — are all true for a typical agent *at assignment*, and none of the three ambitions gates on them the way `ambition_dominate_trade`'s `gold ≥ 0.4` floor gates its `gold < 0.2` trigger. So every repoint abandons on tick one: THR-812's inverted-risk failure in abandonment form, and strictly worse than the dead ref, since these ambitions currently never *abandon* whereas a misfiring trigger means they never *run*.
