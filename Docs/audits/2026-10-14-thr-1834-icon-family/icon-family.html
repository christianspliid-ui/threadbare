Places: carved, so they read at a glance

Proposed after your note. Today's place drawings share one layered style, so at map size a capital, a castle and a temple are hard to tell apart. The carved set gives each kind of place its own outline: columns for a temple, a cart for a mine, tents for a camp, a cave mouth for a lair. The same icon is used on the map and in every panel.

How the icons behave

These follow the existing UI Laws, so they need no new decision from you.

14 px floor
18 px in a chip
24 px in a list
34 px with no label
Blessing· LifeCurse· SpiritBattle· ForceOmen· MindWaiting· Time

One idea, one icon

Each meaning gets its own shape. No glyph stands for two things again (Laws 9 and 10).

Filled, never outlined

Content icons are solid silhouettes in one colour. Sphere colour tints them; the art itself is never repainted.

Named sizes

14, 18, 24 and 34 px. Never below 14 px. An icon with no word beside it is drawn at 34 px (Law 11).

Words where it matters

Top-bar buttons always carry their word. Elsewhere a new icon gets a tooltip and a legend the first time the player meets it (Law 12).

No emoji

Emoji and symbol characters can only go down. A build check counts them, together with the other UI debt.

Carries to Unity

Every icon is a single flat shape, so it exports cleanly to a sprite atlas.

Places keep their size

On the map a capital or city still draws larger than a town, and a town larger than a shrine. The new outline adds to the size, it doesn't replace it.

Icons in gold: game-icons.net by Lorc, Delapouite and contributors, CC BY 3.0, recoloured. Line icons: lucide, ISC licence. Place, sphere and reach drawings: the game's own.
