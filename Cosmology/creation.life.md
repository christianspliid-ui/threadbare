# Life

> The sphere of biology, growth, healing, reproduction, and fertility. Life is the organizing principle that animates flesh, drives the flourishing of plants and animals, enables regeneration, and perpetuates itself across generations. It also encompasses disease, decay, and the dark side of biological processes.
