# Entropy

> The sphere of decay, dissolution, randomness, and chaos. Entropy is the tendency of order to break down, of all things to tend toward disorder. It governs decay and rot, the randomness of chance, probability and luck, and the chaotic magic that defies prediction. Entropy is not evil but rather the natural tendency of the universe toward greater freedom and disorder.
