# Chaos

> The primordial underpinning of all existence—raw potential, formless and boundless. Chaos is not evil or destructive, but rather the infinite wellspring from which all order, structure, and being emerge. It is the silence before creation, the void pregnant with possibility, the ground upon which the binary of Darkness and Light first manifests.
