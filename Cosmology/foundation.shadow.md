# Shadow

> The emergent sphere born from the interplay of Darkness and Light—neither pure darkness nor pure light, but the zone of twilight, ambiguity, and transformation. Shadow is the trickster principle: deception and truth entwined, the half-revealed, the liminal space. Shadows shift with the angle of light, representing adaptability, cunning, and the wisdom found in paradox. Shadow is not a third equal sphere like Darkness and Light, but rather the dynamic interaction between them.
