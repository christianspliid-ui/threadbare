# Darkness

> The principle of absence, void, and mystery. Darkness is not malevolence but rather the unknown, the hidden, the unexplored. It represents concealment, privacy, rest, and the unconscious depths. Where Darkness reigns, mysteries abound and secrets are kept. It is the complement to Light, neither superior nor inferior.
