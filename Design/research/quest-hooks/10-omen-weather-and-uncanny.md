# 10 — Omen, Weather and the Uncanny

The world behaving wrongly. Authoring note: the discipline here is that the strangeness must have a *cost or a consequence someone is already dealing with*. "A strange thing is happening" is a mood. "A strange thing is happening and the harvest depends on it and nobody will say so" is a hook. Roughly a third of this file is weather and season, which published lists almost entirely ignore and which is the most reliable source of village-scale pressure in the game.
