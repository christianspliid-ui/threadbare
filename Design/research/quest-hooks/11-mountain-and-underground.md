# 11 — Mountain and Underground

Stone, depth, cold, mines, passes. Authoring note: the reliable engine here is that a mine or a pass is a *shared dependency* — one hole in the ground feeds four villages, one pass is the only way through for a season. That makes every problem instantly regional rather than personal, which is where the faction and economy systems have something to bite on.
