# 05 — Veil and the Arcane

Magic, divination, ritual, and leakage. Authoring note: the strong hooks here are about magic as *infrastructure and labour* — who maintains it, who can afford it, who is ruined when it stops — rather than magic as spectacle. A wizard doing a wonder is a set piece. A town whose entire economy depends on one failing ward is a situation.
