# 07 — War and Borderland

Frontier, siege, campaign, and aftermath. Authoring note: battles themselves are poor hooks — they resolve too fast and too loudly for a god who nudges. What works is the *week before*, the *supply problem*, and the *four years after*, where decisions are still open and people are still choosing.
