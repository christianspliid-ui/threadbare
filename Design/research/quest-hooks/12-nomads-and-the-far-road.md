# 12 — Nomads and the Far Road

Migration, herds, caravans, exile, and people whose home is a route rather than a place. This file exists because the corpus needed a block where `static` is the exception rather than the rule — the whole file is built on the observation that a travelling population carries its problems with it, which means the same hook can fire in four different hexes across a season.
