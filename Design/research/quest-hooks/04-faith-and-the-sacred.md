# 04 — Faith and the Sacred

Temples, shrines, belief, heresy, and the machinery of religion. Note for authoring: in a game where gods are real and the player is one, the interesting religious hooks are almost never "is the god real" — they are about the *institution*, the *gap between what people were told and what happened*, and what a person does when their faith becomes inconvenient.
