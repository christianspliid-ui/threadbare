# 08 — Guild, Trade and Gold

Craft, contracts, money, patronage. Authoring note: this file is where the `enrich` / `repay` / `dispose` / `conceal` split from the objective axis does the most work. A hook about someone getting rich is thin; a hook about someone who has money they cannot spend, cannot return, and cannot explain is a situation with a clock on it.
