# 06 — Ruins and the Buried

The past surfacing. Authoring note: a ruin is only interesting when somebody currently alive needs something from it — stone for a wall, a claim, a body, a lie confirmed. "There is an old place and it is spooky" is scenery. "The quarry is closed and the only cut stone within thirty miles is in the dead city" is a situation.
