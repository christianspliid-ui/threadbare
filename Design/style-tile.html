Threadbare

Dark world. Hidden magic. Threads that break through.
The world is dark, weathered, and ancient. Behind everything runs a vast invisible web of magic — but it is hidden, behind the veil. You only see magic where it breaks through: at concentrated nodes, fracture points, and moments of power. It appears as thin luminous threads, bright points, and networked filaments — never as diffuse ambient glow.

The World — Dark, Desaturated

Value range: 10–40% brightness. Nothing in the environment is bright. Overcast, twilight, or torchlit — never midday sun.

Magic Colors — Concentrated, Threadlike, Networked

Magic is the ONLY source of strong color. It occupies 5–15% of image area. 70–100% brightness but concentrated into thin threads and bright points. Narrow glow on immediately adjacent surfaces only.

Text Rules

The ONLY text ever allowed in art is the game title "Threadbare" — warm gold serif, glowing, matching the thread aesthetic.
Most images should have NO text at all. Never labels, captions, UI text, or descriptive text.

Art Style

Medium & Technique

  • Painterly oil painting — visible brushstrokes, textural, not smooth digital
  • Dark chiaroscuro — magic IS the light source
  • Atmospheric depth — figures emerge from shadow and haze
  • NOT photorealistic — stylized, evocative, dreamlike-dark

Perspective

  • God's-eye view — world/landscape scenes (this is a god-game)
  • Epic wide shot — cosmic/divine scenes (scale matters)
  • Mid-distance — character/faction scenes (environment context)

References (priority order)

  1. Diablo 3 / Path of Exile — radiant spell effects against dungeon darkness
  2. Elden Ring / Dark Souls — dark ancient landscapes, golden divine light
  3. Malazan covers (Simonetti) — epic oil-painted dark fantasy, atmospheric depth
  4. Endless Legend concept art — painterly, warm/cool contrast, strategic scale
  5. WoW raid environments — neon magic against dark caverns, theatrical lighting

Lighting Rules

  • Primary light = magic threads and nodes
  • Magic light is narrow — immediately adjacent surfaces only
  • Secondary = fire, torches, celestial bodies
  • Ambient = very low, deep twilight or underground gloom
  • No bright daylight scenes — even daytime is overcast

Thematic Subjects

The Unmaking

Terrain fractures into void-black chasms. Magic threads — all Creation Sphere colors — tear free from the dying world, rising like luminous nerves pulled from dark flesh. Ghostly silhouettes ascend. The sky splits.

The World-Soul

A luminous cosmic sphere pulsing with interwoven threads of all eight Creation Sphere colors. Orbits in darkness. Surrounded by faint echoes of past worlds. The brightest object in any scene.

The Ascendant

A divine figure forming from converging magic threads. Not fully solid — partially translucent, luminous, woven from light. Sphere colors reflect the player's choices. Always emerging from or standing above the dark world.

Echoes

Ghostly remnants of previous worlds. Faded, semi-transparent, rendered in desaturated versions of their original sphere colors. Monuments, heroes, relics drifting like afterimages. Less vivid than active magic.

Rival Gods

Dark divine figures with their own sphere-colored emanations. More imposing, alien, threatening. Their magic is as vivid as the player's but with different color combinations. Often in opposition or looming over territory.

Exclusions — Always Apply

No UI elements or HUD overlays No modern elements No cartoon or anime style No photorealistic rendering No bright daylight or sunny scenes No pastel colors anywhere No text labels or captions (only "Threadbare") No excessive detail that flattens painterly quality No ambient magic glow or diffuse color wash No static geometric lines for magic (must be organic)

Prompt Construction Pattern

Six layers, always included:

1. Subject What the scene depicts 2. World tone "dark fantasy oil painting, charcoal and deep shadow, ancient weathered world" 3. Magic spec Name the specific sphere colors as glowing/radiant threads 4. Lighting "magic provides primary illumination, deep chiaroscuro, no bright daylight" 5. Style anchors "painterly brushstrokes, atmospheric depth, Diablo-like magic, Elden Ring grandeur" 6. Exclusions "no UI, no modern, no cartoon, no photorealistic, no daylight, no pastel, no text"

Example Prompt

[Subject description]. Dark fantasy oil painting style.
Ancient weathered world in deep shadow and charcoal tones, 85-95% of the image is dark.
Thin luminous [sphere color] magic threads break through the darkness
at concentrated points — organic, alive, swirling like luminous dust,
curling like ink in dark water, flickering like flame along thin veins and nodes.
The threads are not rigid — they pulse, drift, and breathe.
Narrow colored glow only on immediately adjacent dark surfaces.
Painterly brushstrokes, atmospheric depth, dramatic chiaroscuro.
No ambient magic glow, no diffuse color wash — magic is always concentrated
into thin organic threads, bright nodes, and swirling particles.
No UI elements, no text, no modern elements, no cartoon style,
no photorealistic, no bright daylight, no pastel colors, no static geometric lines.
