Domain System Exploration — How Actors Express Power Across All Scales

Each domain represents a reach of an actor's influence into reality. Poetic single-word names work as in-world language: "She has deep reach in Shadow" or "His Iron is unmatched." Every domain spans from individual action to cosmic force.

Species Domain Profiles — "Nature" Without a Nature Domain

Instead of a 10th "Nature" domain, every species of being carries innate domain affinities as origin traits. A forest's "nature power" isn't a separate force — it's the collective Stone, Veil, and Heart of the treants, fey, and wildlife that live there. Nature emerges from what inhabits a place, not from the place itself.

Each species below shows its innate domain bias — the starting shape before individual traits, culture, and history reshape it. Bright bars = strong affinity. Dim bars = possible but uncommon. No bar = alien to this being's nature.

The Proof — "Nature" Emerges From Inhabitants

A forest hex doesn't have a "Nature: 85%" stat. Instead, the beings that inhabit it collectively create a regional domain profile. When the player zooms out, what they see is the aggregate of everything living there. This is more interesting than a nature domain because it changes dynamically — burn the forest, scatter the fey, and the hex's profile shifts.

The Key Insight

A "nature domain" would be a static label. But species domain profiles give us something better: dynamic, emergent nature. Burn the Whisperwood and scatter its fey — the hex's Veil collapses, its Eye drops, its Heart fractures. What was once a magical forest becomes a scarred wasteland with a profile heavy in Stone and Flesh (the surviving beasts) but hollow in everything else. That's narrative depth. A nature domain can't do this.

Design Implications

For Origin Traits
Species domain profiles ARE origin trait clusters. A new dwarf actor spawns with "Forgeborn," "Stone-Sense," "Iron Constitution" — traits that naturally compute to high Iron/Gold/Stone. No stat initialization needed. The species profile is emergent, not stored.
For Outliers & Exceptions
An elf blacksmith is interesting because their species bias is Eye/Veil/Flesh, not Gold. Their Gold trait ("Artisan of Starsilver") stands out against the species default. The profile makes exceptions visible and narratively meaningful.
For Hybrid Beings
Half-elf? Blend the human flat profile with the elven peaked profile. The result: moderate everywhere with slight Eye/Veil lift. Mechanically elegant, narratively resonant — the half-elf is pulled between two natures.
For Player Strategy
The player-god sees species profiles on their divine overview. "This region has high Veil because of the fey population" is strategic information. Displacing species reshapes regional capability — a tool for the Weaver of Fate.
