Recommended Strategy

Tier 1 — Convert to SVG now (smaller than PNG, clear win): Ocean, Mountain, Volcanic, Tundra v2, Bog v2, Swamp v2, all overlay icons. These tiles have clean geometric shapes that vectorize beautifully.

Tier 2 — Acceptable as SVG (1.1–1.85× larger, but gain infinite scalability): Jungle v2, Open Grassland v2, Badlands, Castle overlay. Worth it if scalability matters more than bandwidth.

Tier 3 — Keep as PNG (3–4× larger as SVG, not worth it): Dense Forest, Deciduous Forest, Taiga. These have too many small shapes — even the flat-graphic v2 prompts didn't simplify them enough for efficient vectorization. For these, stick with PNGs and use CSS clip-path for the hex shape.

Key insight: The V2 flat-graphic prompts dramatically improved jungle, swamp, tundra, and bog (all went from "unvectorizable" to reasonable), but forests and taiga remain stubbornly complex. The number of distinct shape boundaries matters more than texture detail.
