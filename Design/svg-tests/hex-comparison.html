Verdict: Mixed — great for clean tiles, bad for dense patterns

Tiles with clean geometric shapes (mountain, ocean, overlays) vectorize beautifully — 80-86% smaller and infinitely scalable. Tiles with dense organic patterns (deciduous forest, dense forest) explode in size because every leaf becomes dozens of bezier paths.

Recommended approach: Convert the ~15 clean/geometric tiles to SVG, keep the ~12 dense/organic tiles as PNG. Total cost: ~15 credits (you have 43 remaining). Or: redesign the dense tiles to be more graphic (fewer, larger shapes) and then vectorize everything.

Watch out: Deciduous Forest / Dense Forest

These tiles have hundreds of tiny leaf shapes. Each one becomes many SVG paths. The 1.5 MB SVG will also render slower than the 583 KB PNG in the browser. Either keep these as PNG or redesign them with larger, simpler canopy shapes.
