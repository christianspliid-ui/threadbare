The Storm-Born Reach
A plateau above the world, where the Children of the Shifting Dry Canyons endure beneath Iron Covenant banners
⛰ The Land

A high flat expanse above the world, wind-scoured and remote. The plateau drops away on all sides into dry canyons whose walls show the strata of ages — red clay over pale chalk over dark stone that no one has named. The air is thin and restless, carrying dust from the east and silence from everywhere else.

The land offers little comfort but much endurance. Wind-blasted stone breaks through the thin topsoil everywhere, and the few rivers that survive the climb run fast and cold through narrow gorges. What grows here grows tough — scrub grasses, thorn-bark, and the pale lichen that the canyon-folk boil into dye.

✦ The Soul

Energy crackles through the high stone — not as fire, but as the relentless static charge that builds before storms break over the plateau. Lightning strikes are frequent, and the canyon-folk say the rock remembers each bolt. At dusk, thin golden threads pulse in the exposed quartz veins where forces older than the sky concentrate into points of brilliant, restless light.

Beneath the dominant charge, faint Time pools in the canyon depths. The strata in the cliff walls glow faintly orange at certain hours — concentric ripples of temporal residue, overlapping echoes from an age when something here measured the turning of the heavens. The threads are thin and concentrated, visible only at the fracture points where old power seeps through.

⚑ The People

The Children of the Shifting Dry Canyons claim this wind-scoured height — a people shaped by patience and territorial pride, who mark their boundaries in hammered iron and sun-bleached bone. Their ways lean chaotic: fluid hierarchies, personal honor over written law, and a deep distrust of any structure that outlasts the generation that built it. They venerate Energy, reading omens in the lightning and forge-fire alike.

Yet it is The Iron Covenant that holds the gates. Their banners mark every approach to the single town on this plateau.

🏚 The Forge of Sorrow Town · 1 soul
A settlement built around necessity — chimneys black with use, streets worn into grooves by generations of the same heavy boots. The forge at its heart has not gone dark in living memory.

The Covenant's grip is administrative, not cultural; they tax the pass roads and maintain a garrison, but the people's loyalties run deeper than any banner.

◇ The Ruins

Before the Canyon people came, this plateau belonged to the Star-Readers — a people of chaos and celestial obsession who charted the heavens from this high, clear vantage. Their name survives in the region itself: the locals call this expanse the Storm-Born Reach, a corruption of a Star-Reader phrase that meant "the place where sky touches stone."

Shattered observatories still dot the plateau's eastern edge, their great lenses cracked, star-charts etched into stone floors now open to the sky. At certain hours the Time-residue in the canyon walls aligns with scoring on these observatory floors, and the charts glow faintly — a dead culture's instruments still measuring a sky that has moved on without them.

They read the heavens until the heavens read them back.

The eastern observatories have not been fully explored. Something in the lowest level still draws power.
A Star-Reader star-chart, if reconstructed, might reveal patterns the current sky no longer shows.
