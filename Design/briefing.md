- [An oath from tribute](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2051/plan-doc-an-oath-from-tribute-the-house-that-pays-tribute-in-a-crowns): four calls inside [the plan](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/Docs/plans/2026-10-26-thr-2051-oath-from-tribute.md#decisions). Each can be vetoed on its own until Tuesday 09:49. *— from [the design lane](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/ops/Docs/ops/design-lane-2026-10-26b.md)*
  - **The mortal's own guild or company swears to the crown over the hall.** When a mortal wins The Tribute Exchange, their own guild, company or order (never a whole realm) swears fealty to the realm that holds that town. If a temple order holds the town and has sworn to a realm, it is that realm. *Veto:* "only a town a realm holds directly counts".
  - **Every win swears; a loss swears nothing.** A clean win, a win and a costly win all swear. A loss still costs the tribute. *Veto:* "a costly win should pay without the oath".
  - **A tribute that can bind nobody is still paid.** If the mortal has no guild that may swear, or no crown stands over the town, the coin is spent and nothing is sworn. *Veto:* "a tribute that buys no oath should be refunded".
  - **The scene comes up as often as today.** It does not lean toward towns under a crown. So the oath is rare: on three test worlds, 2 of 32 tribute scenes had both a guild and a crown. *Veto:* "the tribute scene should only come up under a crown".
- [A place held after success](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2048/plan-doc-a-place-held-after-success-the-forge-a-mortal-builds-the-mill): four calls inside [the plan](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/Docs/plans/2026-10-26-thr-2048-place-held-after-success.md#decisions). Each can be vetoed on its own until Tuesday 06:45. *— from [the design lane](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/ops/Docs/ops/design-lane-2026-10-26a.md)*
  - **The forge a mortal builds is theirs.** When a forge-building scene succeeds, the town gets a forge and the builder holds it. It pays them a little every day, as any freehold does. The forge is not one of the town's own buildings. *Veto:* "the smiths, not the builder, should hold the forge".
  - **One forge per town, and one claim per place.** If a forge already stands and nobody holds it, the builder takes it. If someone holds it, the builder is paid for the work instead. A prospector holds the place's mine the same way, or stakes one if there is none. A held mine stays even when it is worked out.
  - **A scene never takes a holding away.** A failed stake gains nothing, but it never loses a claim the mortal already holds. *Veto:* "a failed stake should lose the claim they had".
  - **The mill lease stays the fellowship's.** The scene wins the lease for the millers' fellowship, and the mortal is paid a fee. So no one gets the mill yet. That row goes back on [the later-rows list](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-1954). *Veto:* "the mortal who wins the lease should hold the mill".
- [A relic left lying in the world can now be taken up](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2031/nothing-can-take-up-an-unclaimed-artifact-a-seeded-relic-lies-at-its): three calls inside [the plan](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/Docs/plans/2026-10-26-thr-2031-take-up-a-lying-relic.md#decisions).   - **A relic is taken up as a mortal's work, not in a scene.** A mortal makes "take up a relic left lying" their undertaking, and when it completes they hold the relic. *Veto:* "it should be a scene the god can watch".
  - **The work is done from wherever the mortal stands,** like founding a town. The journey is the work; distance only ranks the targets. *Veto:* "a mortal must stand at the place to pick it up".
  - **Seekers and wanderers want it.** It is the payoff of their treasure hunt. Only a relic that lies somewhere can be taken up.
- [A road's encounters write to that road](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2046/plan-doc-a-roads-encounters-write-to-that-road-the-ambush-the-toll): four calls inside [the plan](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/Docs/plans/2026-10-25-thr-2046-encounter-lane-writes.md). Each can be vetoed on its own. *— from [the design lane](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/ops/Docs/ops/design-lane-2026-10-25h.md)*
  - **A road event writes its own road.** When bandits, an embargo or a toll quarrel is stirred up on one road, the result lands on that road. Today it lands on the town's busiest road, which is the wrong road about half the time. *Veto:* "an encounter should always pick the town's busiest road".
  - **Encounters can turn a road red, and clear it.** Beating off an ambush clears the road. A lost caravan escort, or a critical failure on a caravan or pirate raid, threatens it for about two days. A blockade is never lifted by a mortal's scene. *Veto:* "a lost caravan should not turn its road red".
  - **A struck toll goes to the faction that holds the town.** If no faction holds it, the mortal who struck it keeps it. A scene never takes a toll away from a mortal. *Veto:* "the mortal who strikes a toll should keep it".
  - **A lost ambush only thins the road.** Only a critical failure also costs the town some prosperity, because ambushes are lost 36 to 57 times a month on two test worlds. *Veto:* "every failed ambush should hurt the town".
- [A faction's commission pays the member who takes it up](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2042/plan-doc-a-factions-commission-pays-the-member-who-takes-it-up-the): three calls inside [the plan](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/Docs/plans/2026-10-25-thr-2042-faction-commission-pay.md). The first of its members **who makes choices** to finish any of its work is paid, and their Means rises one step. The aftermath says "— a commission their faction paid for." Unseen members still earn only standing. *Veto:* "any member, seen or unseen, can take a commission".
  - **The pay is one Means step for the 5 coin posted.** This is the border levy in reverse. *Veto:* "a commission should pay more than that".
  - **An errand nobody takes gives its coin back** to the faction's purse when it expires. Today that coin simply vanishes. *Veto:* "a lapsed commission's coin is spent anyway".
- [Famine and glut draws](https://linear.app/threadbare/issue/THR-2040/plan-doc-famine-and-glut-draws-a-hungry-town-tells-hungers-stories-a): three calls inside [the plan](https://github.com/christianspliid-ui/threadbare/blob/main/Docs/plans/2026-10-25-thr-2040-famine-and-glut-draws.md). A town with a little spare food is the normal state of a settled town, so it does not count. *Veto:* "any town with spare food is in glut".
