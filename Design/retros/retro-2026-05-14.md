## Workflow observations

- **The retro→issue→ship pipeline is real and fast — it just isn't visible to the *next* retro.** The mechanism works (THR-420–425 prove it). What is missing is the read-back: a retro has no reliable step to check "what did last week's experiments become?" This run got the story backwards for exactly that reason. The fix is small and is Experiment #0.
- **My own board-scan method was the bug.** Querying the Continuous Improvement project by `Ready for Dev`, `Ready for Codex`, `In Dev`, `Implementation Planning`, `In Design`, `Todo`, `Backlog`, `Idea` — and *not* `Done` — produced a confident, wrong "nothing was filed" conclusion. Done is where shipped work goes; omitting it from a status scan is a methodology error, not a data error. Worth an `impediments.md` entry under `process-friction`.
- **`Done`-state issues also explain "ghost" work.** THR-420/421/422 were assigned to "Christian Spliid" and Done within hours — the executor automation lanes are landing retro fixes between retros without anything surfacing in chat. That is the system working; the retro just needs to look in the right place.
- **Recurrences still dominate, but several clusters are now closing.** #114/#121/#122/#127 (cross-executor collisions) → THR-421. #112 → THR-423. #126 → THR-424. #118/#124/#125 → THR-420. #131 (in-repo half) → THR-422. The impediment stream is recurrence-heavy *because the fixes are landing* — the open recurrences (#132 confirm, #133, #134-outside-repo) are the residue, not the bulk.
- **Off-cadence firing dilutes signal and raises the error rate.** A 3-day window means the retro is mostly reading *other sessions'* recent output rather than its own period — which is exactly the situation where a stale-data scan produces a wrong narrative. Aligning to a clean weekly Monday slot would reduce both problems.
- **Shipping velocity remains healthy** and the workflow-improvement lane is now *also* moving (5 process fixes in 3 days). The 2026-05-11 retro's "Cowork ships features fine but not its own process fixes" observation no longer holds for issues that get filed.
