### Root cause (investigated 2026-07-04, logged as impediment #154 count-bump)

**The mounted workspace has outgrown the sync layer.** The Cowork sandbox mounts the entire repo folder, which now weighs **~20+ GB / ~320k files** — dominated by `.claude/worktrees`: **12.45 GB / 121,212 files across 27 CC worktrees accumulated since 2026-06-23** (each carrying its own `node_modules`), plus `.venv` (5 GB/35k), `.git` (2.1 GB/20k), `node_modules` (667 MB), and ~1.1 GB of `dist`/`public`/`Assets`/`Design`. Under this load the sync layer (a) delivers **partial transfers** — `.git/config` truncated at 2,040 of 2,570 bytes across multiple re-syncs, `package.json` at 9,647 bytes (impediment #154's original sighting); (b) serves **mixed-vintage stale mirrors** — sandbox `.git/config` dated 07-02 while sibling files differ in vintage; (c) **times out** on sandbox tree scans; and (d) **leaks FUSE debris back onto the host** (`.fuse_hidden*` files in `.git/`, skill dirs, and every worktree).

**Why now:** worktree accumulation started exactly at the 06-23 single-executor consolidation, and the planned mitigation — THR-410's weekly worktree-orphan scan — was never registered because `create_scheduled_task` is blocked inside scheduled sessions (impediments #130/#133).
