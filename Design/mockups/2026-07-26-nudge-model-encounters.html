god of MIND + ENTROPY
A MISSIONThe Ferry's granary count came up short at the autumn moot. Kael carried the purse and the errand downriver.

The grain factor Oswin Pell keeps his ledger open on the counter the way other men keep dogs — teeth showing. Kael needs four wagons of winter grain for Halden's Ferry, and Pell's asking price would empty the village that eats it.

III · The Long Way Back

quintessence rebuilding · slow by design
THE GOD'S HANDKael did not choose this road. The god set it under Kael's feet, and Kael — broken — did not argue.

A broken mortal cannot be commanded whole. The rebuilding encounters are theirs to walk — a few roads per Reach, each giving quintessence back slowly. The god picks the road and keeps the weather kind.
