# Phase 3.1: Stencil-Based Organic Coastline - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-03-22
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Fix Phase 3's unmet success criterion: "Coastal hexes show their inland biome color with an organic shoreline cutting through (not hex-edge aligned)." The organic coastline must clip LAND hex edges to an organic contour boundary. Water hexes show their normal water colors. Per-hex terrain colors must be preserved — no flat overlay colors.

Previous approaches that FAILED and must NOT be retried:
- Ocean mask with 65+ land contour holes → earcut triangulation silently fails at this complexity
- Land contour fill overlay at z > 0 → covers terrain with flat green-brown despite z-ordering
- Depth band fill shapes with land holes → earcut fails again
- depthTest tricks → GPU-dependent, not portable (works on some GPUs, not others)
- Coastal hex scaling (shrinking coastal hexes) → creates white/colored gaps

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Approach: WebGL Stencil Buffer
- Use the GPU stencil buffer to clip land hexes to the organic contour boundary
- This is the standard game industry technique for this exact problem
- No earcut holes needed — the stencil contour is a simple filled shape per loop
- No z-ordering tricks needed — stencil operates at the pixel level regardless of depth

### Architecture: Split InstancedMesh
- Split `createHexFillMesh` into TWO InstancedMeshes:
  - **Land mesh** — only land hexes (non-water, non-lake), stencil-tested: only renders where stencil is set (inside organic contour)
  - **Water mesh** — only water + lake hexes, rendered normally (no stencil test)
- Both meshes use the same hex geometry and per-instance colors
- Callers receive both meshes (or a container Group)

### Stencil Rendering Pipeline
- **Pass 0 (stencil write):** Render land contour shapes (from `computeCoastline().loops`) as invisible geometry that writes stencilRef=1 to the stencil buffer. Use `colorWrite: false, depthWrite: false, stencilWrite: true`.
- **Pass 1 (land hexes):** Render land InstancedMesh with `stencilFunc: EqualStencilFunc, stencilRef: 1`. Only pixels inside the organic contour pass — land hex edges are clipped to the organic boundary.
- **Pass 2 (water hexes):** Render water InstancedMesh normally (no stencil test). Water hexes show their full hexagonal shape with water colors.
- **Pass 3+ (other layers):** Grid lines, signifiers, rivers, etc. render as before on top.

### Contour Threshold Tuning
- The land contour threshold (currently 0.35) determines WHERE the organic boundary sits
- For stencil clipping, the contour should pass through the OUTER EDGES of the first land hex row
- This means land hexes at the coast get their edges clipped — the visible organic boundary IS the coastline
- May need to lower threshold slightly (e.g., 0.30) so the contour extends past land hex edges into water territory, ensuring full coverage of land hex interiors

### Lake Handling
- Lake hexes render in the water InstancedMesh (below stencil) — they show their hexagonal lake-blue fills
- Lake contour shapes (from `computeCoastline().lakeLoops`) can optionally be rendered as organic lake fills on top of lake hexes (same filled-shape approach that works for simple shapes)
- Lake fills are simple shapes (no holes needed) so earcut handles them fine

### Water Depth Bands
- Shallows band (from `shallowLoops`) rendered as filled shapes on top of water hexes
- These are in the WATER area only (outside the land contour) so they can't cover terrain
- Gives organic depth gradient: deep_ocean hex fill → ocean band → shallows band → organic land edge

### Render Order
- Stencil write pass: renderOrder -1 (before everything)
- Land hexes: renderOrder 0 (HEX_FILL)
- Water hexes: renderOrder 0 (HEX_FILL)
- Water depth bands: renderOrder 1 (COASTLINE)
- Grid lines: renderOrder 2 (GRID)
- Everything else: unchanged

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact stencil buffer configuration values (stencilPass, stencilFail, stencilZFail ops)
- Whether to use a single Group return or separate mesh returns from createHexFillMesh
- Contour decimation strategy (MAX_LOOP_POINTS for earcut-safe stencil shapes)
- Whether to keep the updateHexColors function signature or adapt it for split meshes

</decisions>
