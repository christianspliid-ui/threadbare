# Phase m2.5 Plan 03: Monster Encounter Templates and Army Attrition Summary

Monster encounter content created, registered in encounter pool via fallback chain, integrated into Adventuring Guild quests, and monster territory attrition wired into army movement — making monsters tactically relevant.

## What Was Built

### Task 1: Monster Encounter Templates

Created `src/data/monster-encounter-content.ts` with 5 encounter templates following the exact `EncounterTemplate` interface:

| ID | locationTypes | threatRating | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `monster.hunt.minor` | `['lair', 'wilderness']` | `easy` | Agent clears minor lair via Adventuring Guild quest. 2-step: scout + drive out. |
| `monster.hunt.named_elite` | `['lair']` | `hard` | Named beast confrontation. 3-step: approach, confront, final reckoning. |
| `monster.encounter.ambush` | `['wilderness']` | `moderate` | Generic wilderness predator ambush. 2-step: sense danger + repel. |
| `monster.encounter.lair_defense` | `['lair']` | `hard` | Army enters lair, triggers defenders. 2-step: formation + drive through horde. |
| `monster.encounter.horde_raid` | `['hamlet','town','city','capital']` | `deadly` | Monster army raids settlement. 3-step: organize defense + repel waves. |

Registered `getMonsterEncounterById` into the `getAnyEncounterById` fallback chain in `encounter-content.ts`. Added `monster.hunt.minor` and `monster.hunt.named_elite` to Adventuring Guild `questTemplateIds` in `faction-definitions.ts`.

### Task 2: Monster Territory Attrition

Added to `src/engine/armyAttrition.ts`:
- `MONSTER_TERRITORY_ATTRITION = 1.2` — additive Quintessence loss per tick
- `hasActiveLairAtHex()` — queries `location` nodes at army hex for `locationSubtype === 'lair'`
- Wired into `phaseArmyAttrition` as `attritionSources.monsterTerritory`

Behavior: armies in hexes with active lairs lose additional 1.2 Q/tick. Cleared lairs (`cleared_lair`) do not trigger. One check per army per tick regardless of lair count.
