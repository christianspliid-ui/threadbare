## Magic (34 pages)

- [[Air Magic]] — The manipulation of wind, breath, and atmospheric pressure. Air mages summon gales, breathe underwater, or speak across vast distances through the wind.
- [[Animal Magic]] — The communion with and control of beasts. Animal mages call creatures to aid them, command animal swarms, or understand animal thoughts.
- [[Ascension & Transcendence Magic]] — The elevation of consciousness and the pursuit of higher forms of being. Ascetics use magic to transcend limitation, achieve enlightenment, or touch divinity.
- [[Binding & Restriction Magic]] — The confinement and restriction of movement, action, or will. Binders paralyze foes, trap spirits, or lock doors with unbreakable seals.
- [[Blood Magic]] — The use of blood and vital essence as a magical focus. Blood mages use vitality as a reagent, create binding pacts, or empower spells with life force.
- [[Chaos Magic & Wild Sorcery]] — The channeling of pure, unpredictable power without structure. Chaos mages embrace randomness, cast spells with unpredictable effects, or tap into the void itself.
- [[Chronomancy & Time Magic]] — The direct manipulation of temporal flow. Chronomancers slow or accelerate time, glimpse alternate timelines, or create localized time distortions.
- [[Corruption & Dark Binding]] — The tainting and degradation of matter, flesh, or will. Corruptors spread blight, twist creatures, or bind victims to their will through dark pacts.
- [[Demonology & Infernal Pacts]] — The summoning, binding, and negotiation with demonic entities. Demonologists gain power through pacts, command infernal servants, and traffic in forbidden knowledge.
- [[Divination & Prophecy]] — The seeking of hidden knowledge and glimpses of the future. Diviners read signs, scry through crystals, or receive visions of what is to come.
- [[Dream Magic]] — The navigation and manipulation of dreams and the dreamscape. Dream mages enter sleep, reshape dreams, or pull objects from dream into waking.
- [[Druidism & Shapeshifting]] — The transformation of the body and harmony with nature's forms. Druids shapeshift into animals, merge their essence with nature, and walk between civilized and wild worlds.
- [[Earth Magic]] — The command of stone, soil, and mineral matter. Earth mages raise stone walls, shake the ground, or transmute soil into fertile loam.
- [[Enchantment & Artificing]] — The infusion of objects with magical properties. Enchanters imbue weapons with power, create magical artifacts, or bind spirits into items.
- [[Fire Magic]] — The control and manipulation of flame, heat, and combustion. Fire mages wield infernos, kindle sacred flames, or snuff out fires at will. They often embody passion, rage, or purification.
- [[Healing Magic]] — The mending of wounds, curing of disease, and restoration of vitality. Healers channel life force to knit flesh, purge toxins, and restore wholeness.
- [[Holy & Divine Magic]] — The channeling of divine light, sacred power, and celestial authority. Holy mages smite the wicked, grant blessings, and wield the power of the gods.
- [[Ice Magic]] — The subtractive control of thermal energy, freezing moisture and slowing molecular motion. Ice mages create frozen shields, glaciers, and flash-freeze their foes.
- [[Illusion Magic]] — The crafting of false sensory experiences. Illusionists deceive the eye and mind, create convincing phantasms, and twist perception of reality.
- [[Lightning Magic]] — The channeling of electrical energy and atmospheric discharge. Lightning mages call down storms, ride bolts through the air, or burn foes with raw electricity.
- [[Luck & Probability Magic]] — The bending of chance and probability. Luck mages tilt fortune in their favor, manipulate odds, or invoke the randomness of chaos itself.
- [[Necromancy]] — The binding and command of the dead. Necromancers raise undead servants, commune with departed spirits, and tap the power of death itself.
- [[Plant Magic]] — The fostering and command of flora. Plant mages accelerate growth, manipulate vines and roots, or infuse plants with magical properties.
- [[Poison & Toxin Magic]] — The crafting and deployment of toxins, venom, and harmful biological agents. Poison mages create deadly concoctions, accelerate rot, or infect with magical disease.
- [[Psionics & Mind Control]] — The direct manipulation of consciousness and thought. Psions read minds, implant suggestions, control bodies, or shatter sanity with psychic force.
- [[Restoration & Redemption]] — The reversal of corruption, healing of ancient wounds, and restoration of wholeness. Restorers mend curses, purge corruption, and restore balance.
- [[Rune Magic & Symbology]] — The inscription and activation of runes for magical effects. Rune mages inscribe symbols, bind power into writing, or activate pre-prepared enchantments.
- [[Shamanism & Soul Magic]] — The communion with spirits, ancestors, and the spiritual realm. Shamans journey between worlds, commune with spirit guides, and channel ancestral wisdom.
- [[Summoning & Conjuration]] — The calling forth of creatures and entities from afar or from other realms. Summoners conjure elementals, call distant allies, or pull beings from the void.
- [[Teleportation & Spatial Magic]] — The manipulation of space itself, enabling instant travel or warping of distance. Teleporters blink across battlefields, create portals, or fold space itself.
- [[Transmutation & Alchemy]] — The transformation of one substance into another. Alchemists turn lead to gold, poison to medicine, or rearrange matter at the molecular level.
- [[Warding & Abjuration]] — The creation of protective barriers and the banishment of hostile forces. Warders shield allies, banish monsters, or create sanctified ground.
- [[Water Magic]] — The domination of liquid water in all its forms. Water mages part seas, create whirlpools, heal with purifying water, or drown enemies in torrents.
- [[Weather Magic]] — The manipulation of storms, precipitation, and atmospheric phenomena. Weather mages summon rain or drought, call lightning, or create localized tempests.

## Terrain (23 pages)

- [[Badlands]] — A landscape of eroded ridges, gullies, and colorful stratified rock. Little vegetation survives here; heat shimmers off the stone. An alien, austere beauty pervades.
- [[Bog & Fen]] — A cold, waterlogged biome where sphagnum moss dominates. Sparse vegetation, dark water, and a sense of desolation pervade. Strange lights dance over the water at night.
- [[Coastal]] — Shorelines, beaches, and seaside regions
- [[Coastal Shallows]] — Where the ocean meets the land—shallow waters, sandbars, and rocky outcrops. Seagulls cry overhead, and the sound of waves breaking on shore is ever-present.
- [[Deciduous Forest]] — A forest of broad-leafed trees that lose their leaves seasonally. Rich undergrowth, dappled light, and the songs of countless birds characterize this temperate biome.
- [[Dense Forest]] — A thick, primordial forest with towering trees and dense canopy. Sunlight barely penetrates the gloom; the air is thick with moisture and life. Home to ancient creatures and hidden peoples.
- [[Desert]] — Vast, arid expanses of sand or stone where water is precious. Extreme heat by day, freezing cold by night. Oases are treasured; trade routes cut across the dunes.
- [[Farmland]] — Cultivated land bearing crops and domesticated life. Plowed fields, irrigation channels, and scattered farmsteads mark human presence. A place of order imposed upon nature.
- [[Glacier & Ice]] — Vast sheets of ancient ice covering land and sea. Crevasses hide deadly falls; the ice creaks and groans as it moves. A place of stark beauty and existential cold.
- [[Grassland & Plains]] — Rolling or flat expanses of grass and low vegetation. The horizon stretches endlessly, wind whispers through the grasses, and the sky dominates the landscape. Perfect for cavalry and nomadic peoples.
- [[Hills]] — Gently rolling terrain with moderate elevation changes. Valleys nestle between slopes; forests cling to hillsides. A biome of transition and layered views.
- [[Jungle & Tropical Forest]] — A riotous explosion of life in hot, wet lands. Towering canopy, violent competition, and year-round growth create a green chaos. Danger and wonder lurk behind every frond.
- [[Lake]] — A body of freshwater surrounded by land. Lakes provide sustenance, beauty, and mystery. Some are serene and clear; others are murky and deep, harboring secrets.
- [[Mountains]] — Towering peaks that pierce the sky. Snow caps summits, thin air makes breathing difficult, and views stretch for a hundred miles. Home to hermits, dwarves, and ancient dragons.
- [[Ocean]] — The vast open sea—deep, mysterious, and dangerous. Crashing waves, salt spray, and the infinite horizon characterize this biome. Home to maritime trade routes, leviathans, and the unknowable depths.
- [[Plateau]] — Elevated terrain with relatively flat expanses at high elevation. Dramatic cliffs drop away; the wind is relentless. A place of stark beauty and exposed geology.
- [[River]] — Flowing freshwater that carves through the land. Rivers are highways of commerce, sources of life, and often form natural barriers or meeting places.
- [[Savanna]] — Grassland with scattered trees, often in warm regions. The dry season browns the grasses; the wet season brings explosive growth. Large herds of grazing animals roam here.
- [[Steppe]] — Semi-arid grassland with extreme temperature swings. Sparse vegetation, strong winds, and vast horizons define this hardy biome. Home to nomadic herders and tough peoples.
- [[Swamp & Marsh]] — Waterlogged terrain where water and land blur together. Cattails, lily pads, and murky channels characterize this biome. Insects swarm; the air reeks of decay and life.
- [[Taiga & Boreal Forest]] — The northernmost forest, dominated by coniferous trees like spruce and pine. Long, harsh winters and brief summers define this biome. Solitude and resilience are its hallmarks.
- [[Tundra]] — Frozen wasteland where permafrost underlies sparse, hardy vegetation. Brutal cold defines this biome. Few humans survive here; those who do are tough beyond measure.
- [[Volcanic]] — Landscapes shaped by volcanic activity. Lava flows, ash fields, and hot springs dot the terrain. The earth rumbles with primal power; danger and opportunity intertwine.
