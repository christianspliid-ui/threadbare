Divine Premonition — Modal Design

Two modal variants: Whisper (dreamlike) and Compulsion (forceful)

Whisper Modal
A Stirring in the Thread
That night, Serafina dreamed of iron gates rusted shut and a hammer that sang when struck. She woke with calloused palms and the taste of forge-smoke on her tongue.
You sense three currents pulling at your mortal:
Seek the path of Iron
Her hands remember the weight of a blade — 1 essence
Rest. Recover. You are not yet whole.
Her quintessence wanes — the body needs stillness — 1 essence
Your destiny lies elsewhere
She has walked this road too long unchanged — 2 essence
Let the dream fade
Compulsion Modal
The God's Will
Serafina stood at the crossroads, three paths stretching before her. A certainty rose in her chest — not her own. The shrine on the ridge. She could not say why, but she must go there.
She weighs her options. You may tip the scales:
The Shrine of Broken Oaths
Iron · Threat 3
A reckoning with old promises — 4 essence
The Merchant's Gambit
Gold · Threat 1
Coin and cleverness in the market square — 3 essence
The Whispering Grove
Veil · Threat 2
Old secrets stir beneath the canopy — 3 essence
Release your hold — let her choose

Key differences

  • Color: Whisper is cool purple/violet (dreamlike). Compulsion is warm red/amber (forceful).
  • Header: "A Stirring in the Thread" vs "The God's Will"
  • Vignette: Whisper prose is subconscious (dreams, omens). Compulsion prose is present-tense, urgent.
  • Options: Whisper shows vague directional nudges. Compulsion shows actual encounter names with reach + threat.
  • Dismiss: "Let the dream fade" vs "Release your hold — let her choose"
  • Cost: Whisper 1-2 essence. Compulsion 3-5 essence.
