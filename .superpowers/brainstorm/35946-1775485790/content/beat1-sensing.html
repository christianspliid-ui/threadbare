Beat 1: The Sensing

The god reaches out through threads of fate and glimpses 3 mortals at pivotal moments

Opening

Full-screen dark. Prose fades in, colored by the Ascendant's Hunger:

Opening Prose (Gatherer example)

You reach out through the web of fates,
feeling for a thread that hums with purpose.
Three lives flicker at the edge of your sight.

Candidate Presentation

3 mortals appear as full-bleed image cards with prose vignettes. No stats, no reaches, no spheres — just a scene from their life that hints at who they are.

[concept art: a young woman haggling fiercely
in a lantern-lit market at dusk]

A. Triptych Layout

All 3 candidates visible at once as side-by-side portrait cards. Each has a large image (top 60%) and prose vignette (bottom 40%). Player clicks to select. Similar to Remembrance's OriginBeat fragment layout but with larger images.

Example prose: "She counts coins like a general counts soldiers — each one a battle won. The merchants twice her age have learned not to underestimate the girl with ink-stained fingers and a ledger she guards like scripture."
[concept art: scattered images at different depths,
like memories surfacing from dark water]

B. Spatial/Scattered Layout

Images float at different positions and scales on a dark field, like the Remembrance StirringBeat. Click to focus one (it centers and enlarges, others blur). Click again to confirm. Prose appears below the focused image. More dreamlike and god-like.

Interaction: First click = focus (image centers, prose fades in). Second click = choose. Other images dim and blur.
[concept art: a single figure fills the screen,
their story told in a cinematic moment]

C. Sequential Reveal

One mortal shown at a time, full-screen with cinematic image treatment. Swipe/arrow to see next. Each gets maximum visual impact and prose space. After seeing all 3, player picks from thumbnail strip at bottom.

Trade-off: Maximum impact per candidate, but can't compare side-by-side. Takes longer.

Behind the Scenes

The Ascendant's Hunger and sphere alignment filter which 3 mortals surface from the candidate pool. A Gatherer god sees people at moments of connection/isolation. A Witness god sees people at moments of hidden truth. Each mortal's hidden stats (reach capabilities, axiological profile) are derived from their narrative context — the merchant girl has high Gold, the soldier has high Iron. The player never sees numbers.
