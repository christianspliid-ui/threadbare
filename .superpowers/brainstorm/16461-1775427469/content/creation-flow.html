REMEMBRANCE 1

The Origin

"Where did you come from?" — Who you were + when you were mortal.

"You remember a throne room where your word was law. The silk banners still hang in the great hall of Tessara."

Recent ruler → living faction connections

"You remember a throne room, empty now. Its pillars are swallowed by roots. No one remembers the name you held."

Ancient ruler → ruins layer connections

Seeds: mortal archetype, time-since-ascension, culture/faction affinity, domain leanings

↓
REMEMBRANCE 2

The Drive

"What couldn't you let go of?" — The obsession that fueled ascension.

"You remember a face. Always the same face. You would have unmade the world to see it again."

Undying love → shapes what hungers are offered

"You remember counting. Always counting. Lives, coins, days. Never enough."

Obsessive accumulation → different hunger options

Seeds: personality axis, sphere affinity direction, narrows hunger options. Choices filtered by Origin.

↓
REMEMBRANCE 3

The Transformation

"How did you become this?" — The collision with divine power.

Agency shifts here. The power is showing what it wants from you. You're not choosing freely — you're negotiating.

Step A: The Hunger reveals itself

2–3 hungers offered, filtered by Origin + Drive. These are the distorted echoes of who you were.

You hunger to Gather

"Your love became need. You would gather every soul under your wings, whether they wish it or not."

You hunger to Preserve

"Your love became fear. You would freeze the world in amber before letting anything be lost again."

Step B: The Shape crystallizes (minor notch)

Court geometry defaults from the Hunger but player can nudge. "The power settles into a pattern…"

◆ Circle (default)
◆ Web

Step C: The Resonance locks in (reveal, no choice)

Sphere alignment is the consequence. "Life and Spirit pour through you. This is what the universe made you."

Output: Hunger (mandate/victory), Court geometry, Sphere alignment, Domain affinities, Personality profile
