Reaches, Spheres & Quintessence

Two questions govern every act of a god: what are you doing? and what is fuelling it? The first answer is a Reach. The second is a Sphere. They are separate axes, and the whole texture of the world lives in how they combine.

Two axes, not oneEverything a god does is described by a Reach and coloured by a Sphere. Neither contains the other.

When you act on the world, two independent things are always true at once. There is what you are doing — pressing a mortal toward courage, buying loyalty, hiding a truth, breaking a wall. That is a Reach. And there is what the act is made of — the cosmic energy it draws on, which decides how it feels and what it costs. That is a Sphere.

There are Eight Reaches and Twelve Spheres, and they are orthogonal: any Reach can be powered by any Sphere. The same gesture in two different Spheres is not the same gesture. This is the engine that makes the world feel large — eight things to do, twelve ways to mean them.

Quintessence is not a ninth Reach

A third idea rides alongside the two axes: Quintessence — how present, central, and hard-to-erase a being is in the story. It is a meta-property, not a Reach and not a Sphere. It is covered at the end of this page, and it matters more than its quiet nature suggests.

The Eight Reaches — what you doEight domains of action. A god favours two of them for a whole run; a mortal can grow along any.

A Reach is a category of doing. It says nothing about good or evil, magic or muscle — only about the kind of pressure being applied to the world.

ReachThe domain it covers
IronStrength, force, martial action, direct confrontation
GoldWealth, influence, social capital, patronage, trade
ShadowStealth, deception, sabotage, covert leverage, hidden action
VeilMagic, divination, supernatural perception, ritual
HeartEmotion, loyalty, persuasion, personal bonds
EyeKnowledge, observation, judgment, intelligence
StoneEndurance, craftsmanship, building, preservation
StarTravel, fate, faith, navigation, survival, long distances

Your remembered identity settles on one primary and one secondary Reach, permanent for the run — the actions a god can ever take come from those two domains. Mortals below you are not bound this way; a peasant, a general, and a spymaster differ mostly in which Reaches they have grown, and how far.

There is no ninth Reach

An earlier design had a "Flesh" Reach for the body and survival. It is gone — its ground was split among the eight above (healing folded into Gold, athletics into Iron, the body's craft into Stone, survival into Star) and its old job of "are you fading out of the story?" became Quintessence. If you meet "Flesh" or "nine Reaches" anywhere, that source is stale.

The Twelve Spheres — what fuels itTwelve cosmic energies in two families. They colour a Reach's action rather than changing what it is.

A Sphere is the material of an act — the flavour of energy it runs on. The Spheres come in two families.

The Foundation four — the elder energies

The Foundation Spheres are the world's oldest, rawest cosmic axes: Chaos ↔ Order and Light ↔ Darkness. They govern the deep grain of reality — change against stability, revelation against concealment. A god does not walk in already aligned to these; the Foundation energies are elder magic, met in the world — in ruins, relics, and old places of power — rather than chosen at your remembrance.

Chaos↔Order
Change, disruption, entropy-as-creation — against stability, law, and structure.
Light↔Darkness
Illumination, truth, clarity — against mystery, shadow, and concealment.

The Creation eight — the working energies

The Creation Spheres are the everyday energies a god shapes the living world with. They fall into four opposed pairs — each Sphere has one true opposite that resists it and one neighbour that reinforces it.

Force↔Mind
Physical power and momentum — against thought, intelligence, and will.
Matter↔Time
Substance and crafting — against duration, memory, and causality.
Energy↔Spirit
Vitality, fire, kinetic power — against soul, essence, and divine nature.
Life↔Entropy
Growth, biology, healing — against decay, dissolution, and transformation.

The world you play in is not evenly lit by all twelve. Each world is seeded with its own cosmology — a weighting that makes some Spheres abundant and others rare — so a world thick with Spirit and Mind plays nothing like one drowning in Entropy.

Orthogonality in playSame Reach, different Sphere, different story. This is the point of keeping the axes separate.

Because a Reach never contains a Sphere, the same action reads completely differently depending on what fuels it. Take one Reach — Heart, the domain of loyalty and bonds — and change only the Sphere:

Heart, fuelled by Light

Devotion offered openly — a bond of trust the mortal understands and would name aloud. Loyalty as clarity.

Heart, fuelled by Darkness

The same bond, but half-hidden — an attachment they feel and cannot quite explain. Loyalty as spell.

Neither is "the real Heart action" — both are. Eight Reaches times twelve Spheres is the combinatorial space the whole content library draws from, and why two gods who both "work through the Heart" can feel like different religions.

The moral axis of each ReachEvery Reach runs between a virtue and a vice. Which way a character leans is drawn by their choices, not fixed at birth.

Each Reach carries a built-in moral dimension — a pair of archetypes at opposite ends of the same domain. The virtue pole and the vice pole are two ways of being powerful in the same Reach, and a mortal's arc tilts them along it as they act. An Iron soul is somewhere on the line between Protector and Conqueror; where they end up is a story you can lean on.

ReachVirtue poleVice pole
IronProtector · braveConqueror · power-hungry
GoldPatron · generousExtractor · greedy
ShadowBroker · fairManipulator · scheming
VeilWeaver · patientUnraveller · impatient
HeartSworn · loyalRenegade · disloyal
EyeSeer · perceptiveInquisitor · judgemental
StoneKeeper · dependableDestroyer · reckless
StarBeacon · guidingWrecker · misleading

The pull is gentle and directional — a Gold merchant who keeps choosing to hoard drifts toward Extractor; one who keeps giving drifts toward Patron. You do not set a mortal's morality directly. You lean on the choices, and the axis does the rest.

How deep a Reach runs — Domain CapabilityWithin a Reach, capability is a climbing ladder of named tiers, not a raw number the game shows you.

Having a Reach is not the same as being formidable in it. Every actor — mortal or god — has a Domain Capability in each Reach: how far along it they have grown. Capability is what the game checks when it decides whether an action is even possible; a deep Iron warrior can attempt things a soft one cannot.

You never see a bare score. Capability surfaces as a word that climbs as the actor grows — a ten-rung ladder unique to each Reach. Iron, for example, runs:

Frail› Soft› Sturdy› Trained› Steeled› Tempered› Fearsome› Dread› Ruinous› Cataclysmic

Gold climbs from Destitute to Imperial; Veil from Mundane to Mythic; every Reach has its own vocabulary. Mortals grow their capability by doing — succeeding at encounter steps in a Reach deepens it. A god's power to act, likewise, grows within its two chosen domains rather than spreading thin across all eight.

Quintessence — how present you are in the storyA quiet meter of centrality and self. High Quintessence is sovereign and hard to move; low Quintessence is threadbare and at risk of being written out.

Quintessence is the odd one out — not what you do (a Reach), not what fuels it (a Sphere), but how strongly you exist in the story at all. It measures a being's integrity of self and centrality to the tale being told.

  • High Quintessence — sovereign, confident, fully present. Hard to manipulate, hard to reduce, central to the story. The world writes them in.
  • Low Quintessence — thinning, threadbare, easier to bend through indirect pressure. At risk of dropping out of the story — through death, transformation, or simply becoming a symbol or tool rather than a self.

It is a narrative concept, not a health bar. A character does not need a sword to their throat to be in danger — a proud noble who has been slowly hollowed out, made into other people's instrument, is losing Quintessence just as surely as one who is dying. When it reaches nothing, the being passes out of the story in whatever way fits: a person dies or transforms, a place falls to ruins. This is the abstract, story-first replacement for the old body-and-survival "Flesh" idea — the same slot, a wholly different meaning.

Why this matters to a god

A threadbare mortal is easier to lean on — indirect nudges bite deeper when a self is thinning. Threadbare-ness is both a vulnerability you can exploit in others and a fate you can rescue a favoured mortal from.

Implementation status, key surfaces, and canon pointers for the cosmology.

The Eight Reaches IMPL

  • Source of truth is the ReachDomain union in src/types/traits.ts: iron | gold | shadow | veil | heart | eye | stone | star. Iterated as REACH_DOMAINS.
  • Flesh was the deprecated 9th Reach, removed in TB-075 (2026-03-28) and its content migrated (healing → Gold, athletics → Iron, body-craft → Stone, survival → Star). Do not reintroduce reach: 'flesh' in content data — it is stale. A lingering "all 9 domains" comment in computeFullProfile is a doc artifact; the union is eight.
  • A god's permanent two-domain pair is the top-ranked entries in the ascendant's domainAffinities; the requiresReach action gate (THR-503) enforces set-membership so out-of-domain cards never surface. See the run-lifecycle (W1) and divine-actions (W4) pages.

The Twelve Spheres & opposition IMPL

  • SPHERE_NAMES (12) partitions into Foundation (chaos, order, light, darkness) and Creation (force, matter, energy, life, mind, spirit, time, entropy).
  • Opposition and ally maps live in src/engine/cosmology.ts: SPHERE_OPPOSITES / SPHERE_OPPOSITIONS (foundation pairs at max 5/5 opposition; creation pairs force↔mind, matter↔time, energy↔spirit, life↔entropy) and SPHERE_ALLIES (chaos↔darkness, order↔light; creation quadrant bridges force↔matter, energy↔life, mind↔spirit, time↔entropy). These feed the resolution modifier pipeline.
  • Per-world weighting is a CosmologyProfile seeded at worldgen (stored on GameState.cosmology); presets in COSMOLOGY_PRESETS (balanced, elemental_dominance, living_world, mystic_realm, entropic, material). createBalancedCosmology / normalizeCosmology / adjustSphere keep weights summing to 1.

Foundation-as-elder-magic framing DESIGN

  • The player-layer claim that the Foundation four are met in the world rather than chosen at remembrance is a design framing of the cosmology, not a hard-coded chargen lock in a single constant. The seeded dev identity aligns to Creation spheres (DEV_ASCENDANT_IDENTITY, hunger.witness / mind+spirit), consistent with the framing. Treat the "never at chargen" line as intended texture; there is no foundationLocked flag to point at.

Archetype-pair moral axes IMPL

  • The virtue/vice poles per Reach are the 2026-06-29 revision recorded in Docs/canon/cosmology.md (supersedes the 2026-03-28 brainstorm names). Wiring spec: Docs/plans/2026-06-29-archetype-virtue-vice-layer.md. Note two relocations — Manipulator moved Veil → Shadow, Seer moved Veil → Eye — so content referencing archetype names by Reach must be checked against the canon table.
  • Behavioural words (brave, greedy, loyal, …) are deliberately plain for in-game readability; moral drift tilts an agent along its Reach's axis (agents page W10).

Domain Capability & the word ladder IMPL

  • NARRATIVE_LEXICON (src/types/traits.ts) is the 10-rung label ladder, one array per Reach. src/engine/domainCapability.ts computes a raw score → sigmoid capability (SIGMOID_MIDPOINT = 10, SIGMOID_K = 0.4) → tier → label; computeTier indexes the lexicon.
  • Growth is encounter-driven and deterministic — src/engine/capabilityGrowth.ts: BASE_ENCOUNTER_GROWTH = 0.5, PROMOTION_ELIGIBLE_MULTIPLIER = 2.0, DIMINISHING_RETURNS_FACTOR = 0.7, FAILURE_GROWTH_FRACTION = 0.2 (constants re-exported from src/data/agent-behavior-constants.ts). No PRNG.

Quintessence IMPL

  • A derived meta-property on actor and location nodes, not a Reach. Constants in src/types/quintessence.ts: thresholds STRAINED 0.50 / WEAKENED 0.25 / CRITICAL 0.10 / DISSOLUTION 0.0, QUINTESSENCE_PASSIVE_REGEN = 0.002/tick, defaults 1.0, erosion amounts for overchannel (0.05) and encounter failure (0.03).
  • src/engine/phaseQuintessence.ts processes erosion/recovery events, applies passive regen, and runs zero-state dissolution (ZERO_STATE_RULES: actor → death_or_transformation, location → ruins_or_void). Phase order: after phaseSpherePressure, before phaseSphereAggregation.
  • Framing is user-verdicted (2026-05-05): centrality/threadbare-ness, an abstract successor to Flesh's survival framing — see canon. The UL Quintessence entry is authoritative for the definition (the older "vitality/life force" gloss in the Cosmology shard's Quintessence line is flagged stale in canon).

Sphere aggregation into the World-Soul IMPL

  • src/engine/phaseSphereAggregation.ts sums every entity's sphereAffinity into the global state.worldSoul.aggregate (weighted: hex-scale locations 1.0, agents 0.2, factions 0.1) and mirrors the normalized form into FundamentState.sphereWeights. This is the sphere-side link from the living world to the World-Soul (twilight page W17).
