What awaits

A world that lives

Threadbearer is not a game with a world attached. It is a world with a game discovered inside it.

✦

Every run tells a different story

Your divine mandate defines what kind of god you are this time. Build a trade empire. Shepherd a mortal to ascension. Unite warring kingdoms. Each playthrough is a unique myth written by the collision of your choices and a world that reacts to them.

✷

Systems that surprise each other

The economy reacts to war. War reacts to cosmic balance. Cosmic balance reacts to your divine interventions. No system exists in isolation — every thread you pull sends vibrations through the entire tapestry.

✵

Stories, not spreadsheets

You'll never see a stat block. Your agents have character, not numbers. A merchant isn't "Gold: 7" — she's "a shrewd trader whose ambition outpaces her caution." The world speaks in prose. You read stories, not data.

✹

The clock is always ticking

The Doom Clock counts down to the Unmaking — the end of the current age. As it advances, the world frays: resources wither, armies converge, ancient debts come due. You must complete your mandate before everything you've built is unmade.

Divine Power

Shape the world through will alone

You don't move units or click buttons. You are a god. Dream prophecies into mortal minds. Inspire heroes. Curse the land. Forge artifacts of terrible power. Shift the cosmic balance between Life and Entropy, Order and Chaos.

Your influence flows through the agents you've chosen — mortals threaded to your will. Your First, the hero whose journey defines your reign. Your retinue, the faithful who carry your purpose into the world.

43 divine actions across four narrative layers: Land, Soul, People, and Ruins. Eight intervention types from subtle dreams to devastating afflictions.
A Living World

Civilizations that grow, trade, and fall

Settlements rise from hamlets to cities. Markets open. Trade routes thread between capitals. Guilds form, compete, and scheme. Prosperity flows where commerce thrives — and collapses where war arrives.

Every settlement has character. A mining town in the mountains feels different from a port city on the coast. Not because we scripted it, but because the simulation produces it.

Dynamic prosperity, trade routes with decay, economic sublocations (mines, markets, harbors), faction-driven guilds, and settlement promotion from hamlet to metropolis.

War & Ruin

Empires fall. Cities burn.

Armies march across the hex map, led by faction champions with war goals of their own. Sieges grind against city walls. When a settlement is sacked, its markets close, its people flee, and what was once a thriving city becomes ruins for future adventurers to explore.

Rival gods field their own armies. Ancient monsters roam the borderlands. The world is beautiful and dangerous in equal measure.

Faction-driven warfare, siege mechanics, location destruction and demotion, refugee generation, monster encounters scaled by province danger, and divine intervention in battle.
Mortal Lives

Guide heroes through their own stories

Every mortal has values, ambitions, and a cooperation strategy shaped by their experiences. They don't follow orders — they have their own axiological profiles that determine how they respond to your divine influence.

Meet your First: the mortal whose journey defines your reign. Witness their origin through dilemmas you choose. Watch them grow from uncertain wanderer to legendary champion — or tragic cautionary tale.

19 narrative archetypes, axiological motivation engine, hero's journey arcs tied to the doom clock, familiarity-gated revelation, and prose-driven character sheets that unfold as you learn about each mortal.

Under the Hood

A simulation with real depth

Threadbearer's world is built on interconnected systems that produce emergent behavior. Nothing is scripted. Everything is deterministic.

Seeded Determinism

Every world is reproducible. Share a seed with a friend and play the same world. The same choices produce the same outcomes — but different choices unfold entirely new histories.

Graph-Native World

Every entity, relationship, and effect is a node or edge in a living graph. Armies, alliances, trade routes, curses — all the same data structure, all queryable, all traceable.

Hex Map with Terrain Generation

Procedurally generated worlds with realistic climate zones, river systems, biome distribution, and organic coastlines. Mountains form ranges. Rivers flow downhill. Deserts form in rain shadows.

Encounter System

64+ encounter templates across 10 types and 20 location subtypes. Multi-step narratives with cultural overlays, difficulty tiers, and outcomes that ripple through the world.

Faction Ecology

Guilds, cults, and factions form organically, recruit agents, assign quests, and compete for territory and influence. Join encounters, rank promotions, and inter-faction social dynamics.

The Doom Clock

Seven doom archetypes — The Breach, The Convergence, The Failing, The Sundering — each with five-stage thematic escalation that changes how the world feels as the end approaches.
