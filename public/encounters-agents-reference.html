Quick Reference Glossary

Reach
One of eight activity domains (Iron, Gold, Shadow, Veil, Heart, Eye, Stone, Star). What you do.
Sphere
One of eight cosmic energies (Force, Matter, Energy, Life, Mind, Spirit, Time, Entropy). What fuels it.
Domain Capability
A 0–1 score per Reach, computed from traits + attachments via a sigmoid curve. The agent's "stat sheet."
Fate Forecast
The predicted outcome tier (Doomed → Fated) shown before an encounter step resolves. Based on the full resolution formula.
Attachment
Anything an agent carries, suffers, or is bound by. Six categories: possessions, conditions, blessings/curses, bestowed powers, agreements, retainers.
Rarity Tier
Four levels of narrative weight: Mundane (grey), Storied (copper), Mythic (indigo), Legendary (gold). Scales modifiers and rewards.
Quintessence
An actor's life force (current/max). Depletes from failure and erosion. At zero: dissolution (death or dispersal).
Threat Rating
Encounter difficulty band: Trivial, Easy, Moderate, Hard, Deadly. The threat stage still exists in the pipeline, but the live tune currently disables it by default.
Encounter Step
One test within a multi-step encounter (2–4 steps). Each step has its own Reach, difficulty, and duration.
Divine Thread
A bond between the player-god and an agent. Tiers 0–4 (Unbonded → Aspect). Higher tiers = cheaper intervention, deeper influence.
Influence Essence
The player's divine resource. Generated from controlled locations. Spent on interventions, nudges, and divine actions.
Decision Pipeline
Live encounter chooser: merge static + dynamic candidates, run the filter pipeline, apply cooldown/exhaustion, score survivors, then start, move, act remotely, or idle.
