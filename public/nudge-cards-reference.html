The Nudge Card Library

Every choice the god can play into a mortal's moment — the types, the odds language, and the room left to grow

The odds pip vocabulary DESIGN

Approved 2026-07-30. Magnitude to the nearest ~5%: five pips per color tier, tier colors matching the attachment-rarity ladder, pip shape changes with tier so magnitude survives colorblindness — and shape escalation doubles as rarity feel. Stored values are raw numbers; the tiering is purely display (recorded on THR-885).

How generic cards become specific — the binding model

  • Text slots — effect and flavor strings carry typed placeholders ({condition}, {host}, {target}) filled at deal time from the live encounter, through the same resolution as {cast:*} placeholders.
  • Target selectors — a card declares how to find its target, never the target itself: the agent's worst active condition, the owner of the place they're staying, the opposition slot in the scene's cast. Binding failure = the card is not dealt, so dealing is self-grounding: no Mend card where nothing needs mending.
  • Ascendant scaling — sphere discounts and (later) progression bonuses bend the effective numbers; the pips render the result. Same card, three pips for one god and four for another.

Hand-building doctrine

  • Hands are fully authored at encounter design time — 4–6 cards per step, picked from this library. No runtime generic deck; runtime only filters (trait, group, favor-availability, target binding).
  • No two cards in a hand answer the same question — at most one Boost per cost tier.
  • No two encounters in a family repeat a type composition — variety is audited by the coverage matrix (THR-884), not hoped for.
  • Supporting content ships with the card — a card granting an item, trait, ambition, or omen is only legal when that content exists; enforced by the grant-liveness build gate.

Open design space — where the repertoire grows OPEN

Christian, 2026-07-30: "there is a lot of magic in expanding the repertoire and connecting it to the roguelike progression and the ascendant type." Direction agreed, design pending — candidates below are hooks into systems that already exist, listed here so iteration happens on this page.

  • Cards as roguelike unlocks — the ascendant progression system already grants action cards at milestones; nudge-card unlocks can ride the same machinery. Earning "Gambit" at a milestone teaches the mechanic exactly when the player is ready for it.
  • God-earned traits unlock cards — Traits wave 3 (THR-791) plans traits minted from how you play; an #oathkeeper god drawing oath-flavored cards is the natural join.
  • Ascendant type as deck identity — the Sphere-Governed Ascendant direction (THR-870, parked) wants the god's spheres to govern power. Signature cards are that direction's first live surface: per-sphere signature sets, off-sphere cards locked, in-sphere upgraded. When THR-870 wakes, this page holds its card-side design.
  • Card upgrades across a run — the same library card at deeper pip tiers as alignment strengthens; visible growth with zero new content.
  • Run-to-run persistence — the World-Soul carries echoes between runs; a card earned in a triumphant age surfacing in the next run's starting repertoire is the roguelite loop stated in one sentence.
