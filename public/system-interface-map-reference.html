Systems & Interfaces

The world is a web of systems feeding systems. The richness of a run lives in these arrows — and when one is quietly severed, a feature leaks out of the game without any test noticing.

The world at a glance Every box is a living subsystem — one box for every row of the generated systems inventory, plus the run-start generators. Arrows are what one system does to another, at design level.

verified live leaked — broken, repair ticketed partial — works, but a stated gap orientation only — not yet audited

Thin gray arrows are orientation, not verified contracts — each gets promoted (or corrected) as its subsystem is audited. The two audited systems are drawn in color. Far fewer arrows exist than in the real game: only the strongest couplings are drawn, and unlisted ones are absence of audit, not absence of connection.

Why this map exists Tests assert that a system writes its data; almost nothing asserts that another system still reads it. That gap is where five attachment contracts quietly died while every test stayed green. The audited truth lives in Docs/canon/interface-map.md; this page is its picture.

Spotlight — Attachments Audited 2026-07-23. The engine is alive (items are granted, held, displayed) — but half its outbound promises were severed.

  • Encounters ⟶ Attachments — victories and disasters grant items, blessings, wounds, debtsLIVE
  • Attachment ⟶ Action resolution — what you carry shapes the dice when you actLIVE
  • Attachment ⟶ Traits while held — a treasure map makes you a ruin-seekerLIVE
  • Attachment ⟶ Character sheet — the Attachments tab shows what an agent carriesLIVE
  • Attachment ⟶ Capability tiers — a legendary blade should make its bearer visibly mightierLIVE Restored 2026-07-24 (THR-718). Items carry a stat_contribution effect on the effects[] substrate; collectStatContributions feeds an additive per-Reach term into computeRawScore's possesses/bonded_to walk, through the same sigmoid as traits. A dot-scale magnitude indicator sits beside the capability tier on the character sheet.
  • Attachment ⟶ On-use consequences — swords that shatter, curses that bite on a critical failureLIVE Closed 2026-07-25 (THR-719). The orphaned attachmentTriggers.ts resolver and the onUseTriggers field it read are deleted, not adopted — wiring them would have built a second resolution path beside effects[]. On-use behavior is now the production-wired action_trigger primitive, extended with outcome-band events (a blade snaps on a critical failure, not any loss), a seeded probability guard, item-behavior payloads (condition_grant, condition_remove, self_remove), and authored prose that reaches the aftermath feed. All nine authored triggers ported; checkAndFireActionTriggers fires them from resolution, the orchestrator, and movement, and applyActionTriggerPayloads applies them to the graph.
  • Attachment ⟶ Granted abilities — "this item grants cavalry_charge"REPAIRING · THR-737 THR-722 (2026-07-24) retired the possession-edge grants[] property that carried this since worldgen and moved its authored abilities — cavalry_charge, rapid_retreat, intimidate, dark_ferocity — onto the effects[] substrate as trait_grant, joining the ~20 catalog entries already there. Still leaked, because the leak moved rather than closed: the resolver collects granted traits correctly, but hasGrantedTrait has no production caller, so an item promising intimidate still changes no decision. THR-737 wires the consumer.
  • Attachment ⟶ Activated powers — deliberately invoking an artifact's abilityPARKED · THR-720 A real future feature, explicitly parked rather than quietly lost.
  • Tier advancement ⟶ item strength — items grow with their bearers, but currently pump a stat nothing readsPARTIAL · THR-723

Spotlight — Ambitions Audited 2026-07-23. Deeply alive in the engine — mortals want things and act on it — and, since THR-721, the player can read it.

  • Worldgen & births ⟶ Ambitions — mortals arrive wanting things (hundreds pursued by mid-run)LIVE
  • Ambitions ⟶ Encounter choice — a mortal chasing mastery seeks different scenes than one chasing revengeLIVE
  • Ambitions ⟶ Milestones & completion — drives progress, complete, or get abandoned, with story beatsLIVE
  • Ambitions ⟶ Foreshadowing — motive receipts explain why someone actedLIVE
  • World events ⟶ Ambitions — a sacked town mints avengers and refugees; the deed becomes their desire (THR-726)LIVE
  • Minted ambition ⟶ Foreshadowing — the receipt names the origin event: "vengeance for the blighted fields" (THR-726)LIVE
  • Faction ambitions ⟶ Faction action — guilds and orders pursue agendas of their ownLIVE
  • Ambitions ⟶ The player's eye — the character sheet reveals a mortal's primary drive at first meaningful knowledge (THR-721)LIVE THR-721 (2026-07-24) lowered the primary-ambition gate — one meaningful exposure, or recognition-level knowledge, now surfaces the drive (was: two interactions or "known"). Secondary ambitions stay gated deeper.
  • Completed ambitions ⟶ The Chronicle — a readable history of who an agent became (THR-721)LIVE The Chronicle's "Completed Ambitions" section, a placeholder since it was drawn, now lists fulfilled ambitions newest-first from resolved pursues edges.

Spotlight — Player casts Audited 2026-07-25 (audit-on-touch, THR-728). The god's own actions finally run on the world's resolution machinery instead of beside it.

  • Authored step difficulty ⟶ Player resolution — the price a content author wrote into a card is finally chargedLIVE Restored 2026-07-25 (THR-728). 84 of 138 ascendant-castable templates carry authored difficulties (0.1–0.65); the player branch of resolveUncontestedStep discarded every one of them behind an unconditional auto-success early-return. That return is now gated behind PLAYER_CAST_VARIANCE_ENABLED and casts fall through to the shared capability-vs-difficulty path. targetActions.ts reads the same field to render the card's risk line, so the number the author wrote is what the player is warned about.
  • Player resolution ⟶ Divine Receipt — the receipt reports how a cast landed, not merely that it didLIVE THR-727 built the six-band receipt; until THR-728 only two bands were reachable for a player, because no roll happened. All six frame lines were pre-authored and needed no change — the bands simply began to vary. A player cast is floored at success-at-cost, so setback and catastrophe stay unreachable by construction.
  • Ascendant affinities ⟶ Cast capability — the god's innate aptitude reaches the rollLIVE Found and closed in the same pass (THR-728). The ascendant node carries domainAffinities (2–5, a reach ranking weight); mortals carry domainCapabilities (10–40) on the raw scale the capability sigmoid consumes. Read literally, every god rolled at capability 0.02 and 94% of casts landed on one band — measured over 400 seeds. The player path now maps affinity onto the raw scale via ascendantCastRawBonus + computeCapabilityWithRawBonus. Deliberately not folded into computeRawScore, so THR-613's Deepening tier-crossings keep reading the score they were tuned against; a test pins that separation.

How this map stays true A hand-drawn diagram is a lie waiting to happen. This one is designed to become a projection.

Source-of-truth chain. The contract registry (scripts/interface-contracts.ts, arriving with the interface-map framework ticket) is the single source of truth. The canon page Docs/canon/interface-map.md holds the audited tables and the binding design protocol. This page is the human-readable projection — when the registry lands, its generator takes over the badges here, and a severed contract turns the build red before it can quietly rot this picture.

Box membership is a rule, not a judgment call. The first version of this diagram silently dropped nine systems — Culture, Ruins, Stealth, Reputation, Personality, Strategic Projects, Secrets & Favors, Essence, Omens — proving the drift pattern on the drift-prevention page itself. The rule now: one box per row of the generated systems inventory (Docs/canon/systems-inventory.md, regenerated from the engine), plus the run-start generators (worldgen, encounter seeding) and the cross-cutting capability math. If an inventory row has no box here, that is a bug in this page. Membership was swept against the inventory, the pre-Linear backlog history, and the full Linear archive (ARC team, ~240 done issues) on 2026-07-23.

Audited vs. orientation. Only Attachments and Ambitions have been contract-audited so far. Everything drawn in thin gray is orientation — a reasonable sketch of the coupling, not a verified claim. Each future design session that touches a gray system writes its real contract table (audit-on-touch), and a weekly sweep works through the cold ones.

What your feedback looks like. This map is a design instrument, not just a debugging aid. "That arrow should exist and doesn't" is a feature directive. "That system is an island" is a richness gap — the game's depth lives in coupling, so a box with few arrows is usually the next place to invest. And "I remember this working" pointed at a green arrow is exactly the drift report that catches what automation can't.
